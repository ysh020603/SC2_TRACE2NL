# N008 — Technology/Economy/Upgrade Development with Ground Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and a ground-leaning opponent posture. Continue developing technology and economy while strengthening your ground army, maintaining flexibility for adaptation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are heavy, production is moderate, and your army composition is unknown but likely ground-oriented. You have invested in infantry upgrades and stimpack, and are producing Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy investment in technology and upgrades, with Marauder production and infantry upgrades. This aligns with a macro-oriented, ground-based strategy. The presence of SiegeTank suggests a potential transition to a more defensive or siege-oriented play.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that your economy is indeed heavy and you have sufficient income to support continued tech and production.
- Verify that your opponent's ground posture is still valid; if they show signs of air tech, adjust accordingly.
- Ensure that your production facilities are sufficient to support your army growth.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing technology and economy while strengthening your ground army, maintaining flexibility for adaptation.

The trajectory shows a focus on technology and upgrades (Stimpack, ShieldWall, infantry weapons/armor) alongside Marauder production, indicating a commitment to a bio-based ground army. The opponent's ground posture suggests that investing in ground upgrades and Marauders is appropriate, but you must remain alert to possible tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong mid-game position with upgraded bio forces and a solid economy, ready to respond to opponent tech choices.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If you are not converting your economic and technological advantage into army strength, re-evaluate your production and upgrade priorities. Also, if you repeatedly fail to adapt to opponent's composition, recheck your scouting and decision-making process.
