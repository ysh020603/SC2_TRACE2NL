## Opening Strategy

A strategic template emphasizing technology, economy, and upgrades, with a ground-leaning army composition and moderate production. The opening prioritizes developing a robust tech and economic base while maintaining flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

Develop a technology / economy / upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
