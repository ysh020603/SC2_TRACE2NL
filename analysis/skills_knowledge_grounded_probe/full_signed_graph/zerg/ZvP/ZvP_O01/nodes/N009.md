# N009 — Late Midgame Ground Macro vs Heavy Air Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In late midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy economy and production faces opponent heavy air posture with heavy economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground army while maintaining defense and increasing economy. Continue production and technology investment. Maintain air direction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Phoenix, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include Zergling, Queen, Overseer. Economy and expansion are heavy. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues (CreepTumor, Drone, Overlord, Roach, EvolutionChamber, Extractor, Zergling, Queen, SporeCrawler, Hydralisk) indicate a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. Opponent cues (Zealot, Stalker, Phoenix, Oracle) suggest an air-heavy composition. The policy signature (strengthen_ground, increase economy, continue production) aligns with this evidence.

## Applicability Checks

- Check own economy and production status: are resources sufficient for continued production?
- Check opponent air presence: are Phoenix or Oracle actively harassing?
- Check own anti-air capabilities: are SporeCrawlers and Queens in place?
- Check own army composition: is ground army strong enough to deter or engage?
- Check for supply blocks or resource bank issues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while maintaining defense and increasing economy. Continue production and technology investment. Maintain air direction.

Trajectory evidence indicates a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. Opponent air posture suggests need for anti-air defense and ground army strength. Knowledge supports ground units like Hydralisk and Roach countering ground units, while Queen and SporeCrawler provide anti-air. Maintain economy to support continued production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with anti-air support, while continuing economy and expansion.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resource-to-army conversion failure is detected, recheck economy and production balance. Ensure army composition matches opponent threats. If feedback is not changing repeated failed posture, re-evaluate strategic direction.
