# N011 — ZvP Midgame Ground-Macro Posture with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and production on both sides; opponent shows a ground-leaning army with heavy tech; own forces are ground-oriented with moderate air presence and heavy defense. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and expansion development; maintain production and defense with safety checks.

## When This Applies

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and moderate technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Human trajectories frequently include Mutalisk, Corruptor, and ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1, indicating a common air transition in this matchup. The presence of Zergling and Queen suggests a ground backbone. This supports a mixed air-ground approach.

## Applicability Checks

- Check if you have a Spire and Lair to produce Mutalisk and Corruptor.
- Check if you have sufficient gas income to support air units and upgrades.
- Check if your economy can sustain continued Drone production while teching.
- Check if your opponent has a significant Stalker count that could threaten your air units.
- Check if your Overlords are positioned safely to avoid being sniped.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and expansion development; maintain production and defense with safety checks.

Trajectory evidence shows a shift toward air units (Mutalisk, Corruptor) and air upgrades (ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1). Supported relations indicate that Mutalisk synergizes with ground forces (Zergling, Queen) and Corruptor, while ZergFlyerWeaponsLevel1 boosts air units. Opponent ground units (Zealot, Stalker, Sentry) are countered by Zergling and Mutalisk, but Stalker can counter Mutalisk and Overlord, so caution is needed.
