# N012 — Late Midgame Ground-Macro Posture with Heavy Economy and Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In late midgame, both sides exhibit heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence suggests a ground-oriented Protoss army with possible heavy technology. Own forces are ground-based with heavy air presence and upgrades. Trajectory shows continued expansion, drone production, and air unit investment. Continue developing economy and production while strengthening air forces. Maintain defensive structures and creep spread. Use harassment and map control to pressure the opponent. Avoid overcommitting to air without sufficient ground support.

Air units and upgrades are being produced, indicating a transition toward air dominance. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.

- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory shows a heavy economy and production posture with air investment. The opponent's ground-heavy composition can be countered by air units, but Stalkers and Sentries pose threats. Maintaining a balanced ground-air army and continuing to expand ensures long-term advantage.

## Applicability Checks

- Check that you have at least one Spire and Lair to produce Mutalisks and Corruptors.
- Verify that you have sufficient gas income to support air unit production and upgrades.
- Ensure that your ground army (Zerglings, Queens) is sufficient to defend against ground pushes while you build air forces.
- Confirm that you have adequate overlord supply to avoid supply blocks.
- Check that your economy can sustain continued drone production and expansion.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and production while strengthening air forces. Maintain defensive structures and creep spread. Use harassment and map control to pressure the opponent. Avoid overcommitting to air without sufficient ground support.
