# PvT_O03 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O03
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

This opening establishes a heavy technology and economy foundation with moderate production, aiming for a ground-leaning army composition. It is a strategic template that prioritizes flexible adaptation based on live scouting and opponent actions, rather than a rigid build order.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology posture on both sides; opponent ground-leaning with possible pressure.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, and technology while maintaining defense and expansion. Continue air direction.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [NEGATIVE] N002 — Technology/Economy Overextension with Insufficient Ground Defense

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame state where both sides show heavy economy and technology investment, but own defense posture is light or uncertain while opponent may apply pressure.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid overextending on economy or technology without adequate ground defense, and avoid neglecting scouting or detection against potential harassment.

**Read for details:** `N002`
