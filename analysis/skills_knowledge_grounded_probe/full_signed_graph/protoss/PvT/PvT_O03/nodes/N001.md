# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology posture on both sides; opponent ground-leaning with possible pressure. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, and technology while maintaining defense and expansion. Continue air direction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Hellion. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain. Representative units include Zealot and Stalker.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Human-Trajectory Interpretation

Trajectory cues (Probe, Pylon, Stalker, Gateway, Assimilator, Zealot, RoboticsFacility, TwilightCouncil, WarpGateResearch, Immortal) indicate a standard tech/economy opening with ground units and tech structures.

## Applicability Checks

- Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Verify that enemy air presence remains light or uncertain; if air presence becomes significant, re-evaluate.
- Confirm that own defense posture is adequate; if defense is light, consider defensive structures or units.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, production, and technology while maintaining defense and expansion. Continue air direction.

Human trajectories show a high frequency of this node, indicating a common development phase. The knowledge base supports ground unit interactions: Zealot counters Marine before a currently reachable knowledge-verified option, Stalker counters Hellion, and Zealot synergizes with Immortal. Heavy economy and technology investment suggest preparing for midgame transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a robust ground army with technology upgrades and production capacity, while maintaining economy and expansion.

## Stop / Repair / Recheck

If resources accumulate without army growth, recheck production and tech progression. If repeated failed posture, adjust strategy based on live observations.
