# N012 — Early Game Balanced Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, and their economy and technology are heavy. Your own economy and production are moderate. Strengthen your ground army and continue increasing your economy and technology. Maintain your defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines. Their economy and technology are heavy, and their production is moderate. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are moderate, with no clear army composition yet. You have some defensive structures.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue increasing your economy and technology. Maintain your defensive structures.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential push. Building a strong ground army and maintaining economy will help you defend and counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's intentions become clearer.
