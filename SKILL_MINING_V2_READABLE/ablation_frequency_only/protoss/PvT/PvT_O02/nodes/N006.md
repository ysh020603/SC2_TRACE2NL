# N006 — Early Game Defensive Stance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent shows a Reaper, and their economy and technology are heavy. Your own defense is heavy, but production is light. Increase your defense and economy while continuing to develop technology. Maintain production and strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper. Their economy and technology are heavy, and their production is moderate. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your defense is heavy, but your production is light. You have invested in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy while continuing to develop technology. Maintain production and strengthen your ground army.

The Reaper suggests early harassment. A heavy defense will protect your economy while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a solid economy and tech advantage.
