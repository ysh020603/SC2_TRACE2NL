# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Response

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own economy and production are moderate. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for a potential ground push. Continue increasing your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines. Their production and technology are heavy, and their exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are moderate, with no clear army composition yet. You have some defensive structures.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to prepare for a potential ground push. Continue increasing your economy and technology.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential early push. Building a ground army and maintaining economy will help you defend and counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's intentions become clearer.
