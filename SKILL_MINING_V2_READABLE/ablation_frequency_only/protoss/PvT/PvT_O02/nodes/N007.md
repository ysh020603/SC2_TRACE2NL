## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 420-540 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own economy and defense are heavy. Strengthen your ground army and continue increasing your economy and technology. Maintain your defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Their production and technology are heavy, and their exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and defense are heavy, with a moderate production. You have Zealots in your army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue increasing your economy and technology. Maintain your defensive structures.

The opponent's heavy ground army suggests a potential push. Building a strong ground army and maintaining economy will help you defend and counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's intentions become clearer.
