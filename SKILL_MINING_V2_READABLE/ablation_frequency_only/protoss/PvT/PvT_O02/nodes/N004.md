# N004 — Late-Midgame Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the opponent's posture remains unknown, but your own economy and production are still light or uncertain. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and increase production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production and technology are light or uncertain, and their exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain, with no clear army composition yet. You have the flexibility to choose a direction.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and increase production.

With limited information, it is prudent to build a solid foundation. Focusing on economy and technology allows you to adapt to whatever the opponent reveals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's intentions become clearer.
