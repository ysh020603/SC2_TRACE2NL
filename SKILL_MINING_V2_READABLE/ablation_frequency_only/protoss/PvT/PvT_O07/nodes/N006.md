# N006 — Early-Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, enemy ground posture is likely with Marine cues. Own posture is unknown but defense is heavy. Maintain a defensive posture, increase economy and tech, and continue production while scouting for enemy intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground posture is possible with Marine presence. Production and tech are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with heavy economy and tech, but production is moderate. Defense is heavy, expansion is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a defensive posture, increase economy and tech, and continue production while scouting for enemy intentions.

Heavy defense suggests a reactive playstyle; focusing on economy and tech can lead to a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a tech and economy lead.
