# N007 — Early-Midgame Flexible Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, enemy posture is unknown with moderate economy and tech. Own posture is unknown but economy and tech are heavy. Continue developing economy and tech, increase production, and maintain flexibility for any enemy composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy posture is unclear, but economy and tech are moderate. No reliable combat-unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with heavy economy and tech, production is moderate. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and tech, increase production, and maintain flexibility for any enemy composition.

With limited enemy information, a flexible approach allows adaptation to whatever the opponent does.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame with a strong economy and tech, ready to adapt.
