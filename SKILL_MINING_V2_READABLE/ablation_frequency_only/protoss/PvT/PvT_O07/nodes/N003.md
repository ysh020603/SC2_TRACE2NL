# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, enemy posture is unknown but tech is heavy. Own posture is also unknown with heavy tech investment. Continue developing economy and tech, increase production, and maintain a defensive posture while scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but tech investment appears heavy. Possible Marine/Reaper cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with heavy economy and tech, but production is moderate. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and tech, increase production, and maintain a defensive posture while scouting.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base without committing to a specific army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a tech advantage.
