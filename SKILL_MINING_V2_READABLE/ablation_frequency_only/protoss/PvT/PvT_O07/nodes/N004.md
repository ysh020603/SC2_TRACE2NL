# N004 — Early Game Ground Readiness

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, enemy shows a ground posture with Marine cues. Own posture is unknown but economy is heavy. Strengthen ground forces, increase production, and consider expanding to support a longer game.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground posture is likely with Marine presence. Production and tech appear heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with heavy economy and tech, but production is moderate. Expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase production, and consider expanding to support a longer game.

Enemy ground presence suggests early pressure is possible; having a defensive ground force is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a safe early game with a solid economy and a modest ground army.
