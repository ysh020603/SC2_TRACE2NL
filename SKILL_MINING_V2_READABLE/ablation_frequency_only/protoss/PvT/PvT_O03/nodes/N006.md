# N006 — Early Game Defensive Stance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Marines, and your own army is Zealot/Stalker. Your defense is heavy, but expansion is uncertain. Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Avoid overextending.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, indicating possible early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a defensive posture. Production is moderate, and you have Zealots and Stalkers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Avoid overextending.

A defensive stance is appropriate when the opponent's intentions are unclear. Heavy economy and tech allow for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a tech advantage.
