# N002 — Early Game Tech Investment

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent is unknown but possibly has a Reaper. Your own army is Stalker-based, with heavy technology investment. Continue investing in technology and economy while building a flexible ground force. Maintain scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but a Reaper may be present. Their production is moderate, and technology is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army composition is unclear. Production is moderate, and defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue investing in technology and economy while building a flexible ground force. Maintain scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

Early game uncertainty calls for a balanced approach that supports tech without sacrificing economy. Stalkers provide mobility and scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a clearer game plan once the opponent's strategy is revealed.
