## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Your own army includes Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals. Continue strengthening your ground army with tech units and upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion.

- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army with tech units and upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion.

The opponent's composition requires a mix of anti-armor and support units. Your tech advantage can be leveraged.
