# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, both sides show heavy economy and production, with the opponent possibly fielding Reapers, Hellions, or Widow Mines. Your own forces are Zealot/Stalker based. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Reapers, Hellions, or Widow Mines. Their production and technology are heavy, and they may be preparing pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army of Zealots and Stalkers. Technology is heavy, but defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion and economy.

The opponent's ground-oriented cues and heavy production suggest a potential early-midgame push. Investing in ground forces and tech keeps you competitive without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground composition with support from tech units like Immortals or Sentries.
