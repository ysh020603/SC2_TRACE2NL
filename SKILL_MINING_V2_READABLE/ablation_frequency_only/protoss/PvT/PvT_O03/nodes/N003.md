# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 480 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Marines and Medivacs, and your own army is Zealot/Stalker. Both have heavy economies. Strengthen your ground army and increase production. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi to counter bio.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Medivacs, indicating a bio-based army. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Zealots and Stalkers. Defense is light, but you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase production. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi to counter bio.

The opponent's bio army is strong in the midgame, so you need a mix of anti-light and anti-armor units. Heavy economy supports a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground composition with tech support.
