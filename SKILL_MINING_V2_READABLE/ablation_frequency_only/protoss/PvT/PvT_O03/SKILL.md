# PvT_O03 Technology/Economy/Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O03
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: Technology/Economy/Production
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production, aiming for a flexible midgame transition.

Develop a technology/economy/production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, both sides show heavy economy and production, with the opponent possibly fielding Reapers, Hellions, or Widow Mines. Your own forces are Zealot/Stalker based.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion and economy.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Early Game Tech Investment

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent is unknown but possibly has a Reaper. Your own army is Stalker-based, with heavy technology investment.

**Direction:**  
Continue investing in technology and economy while building a flexible ground force. Maintain scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [FREQUENT] N003 — Midgame Ground Macro

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 480 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Marines and Medivacs, and your own army is Zealot/Stalker. Both have heavy economies.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army and increase production. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi to counter bio.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [FREQUENT] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 600 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Your own army includes Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army with tech units and upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [FREQUENT] N005 — Early Game Ground Pressure

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Marines, and your own army is Zealot-based. Both have heavy economies.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army and increase production. Consider adding Stalkers for ranged support.

**Read for details:** `N005`

---

### [FREQUENT] N006 — Early Game Defensive Stance

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Marines, and your own army is Zealot/Stalker. Your defense is heavy, but expansion is uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Avoid overextending.

**Read for details:** `N006`
