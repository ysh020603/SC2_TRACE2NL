# PvT_O01 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes heavy technology investment, a strong economy, and expansion while keeping production moderate early on. It aims to develop a versatile foundation that can adapt to the opponent's actions.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Technology and Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game, around 3 minutes, with both sides having heavy economy and technology investment, but production is moderate and army composition is unclear.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a ground-based army for safety.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Early-Mid Game Ground Army Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 6 minutes, both sides have heavy economy and technology, and you have a ground army with Stalkers. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army and continue expanding your economy and tech.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [FREQUENT] N003 — Midgame Army and Tech Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 7-9 minutes, you have a ground army with Stalkers and Warp Prisms, and the opponent has a ground posture with Marines and Marauders.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production and tech.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [FREQUENT] N004 — Early Game Ground Army Response

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game, around 3 minutes, opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, and you have no clear army yet.

**Direction:**  
Start building a ground army to defend against potential early pressure.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [FREQUENT] N005 — Late Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 10-12 minutes, you have a strong ground army with Stalkers, Warp Prisms, Observers, and Immortals. Opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding tech units like Colossi or High Templar.

**Read for details:** `N005`
