# N004 — Early Game Ground Army Response

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, and you have no clear army yet. Start building a ground army to defend against potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and tech are developing, but you have no army yet. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Start building a ground army to defend against potential early pressure.

The opponent's early Marines could indicate an aggressive build, so you need some army to be safe.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a standard macro game with a solid ground army.
