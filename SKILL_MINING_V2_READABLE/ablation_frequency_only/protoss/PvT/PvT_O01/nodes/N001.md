# N001 — Early Game Technology and Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with both sides having heavy economy and technology investment, but production is moderate and army composition is unclear. Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a ground-based army for safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are developing well, but your army is not yet formed. Production is moderate, and you have no clear army composition yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a ground-based army for safety.

This opening prioritizes long-term economic and tech advantages. Early army strength is not the focus, but you need some defense against potential early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a solid ground army composition while maintaining your tech lead.
