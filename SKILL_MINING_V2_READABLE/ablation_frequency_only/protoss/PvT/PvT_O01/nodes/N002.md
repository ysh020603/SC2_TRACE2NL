# N002 — Early-Mid Game Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 6 minutes, both sides have heavy economy and technology, and you have a ground army with Stalkers. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. Strengthen your ground army and continue expanding your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Stalkers, and your economy and tech are strong. Production is heavy, and you are ready to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue expanding your economy and tech.

With a ground army in place, you can safely expand and tech up. The opponent's ground posture suggests a macro game, so you can focus on outscaling.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more advanced army composition with Warp Prisms and possibly tech units like Immortals.
