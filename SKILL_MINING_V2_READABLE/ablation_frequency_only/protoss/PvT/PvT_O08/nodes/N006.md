# N006 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 6 minutes, the opponent has a ground posture with Marines, and you have a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers. Your defense is light, and you are in the early-midgame. Increase your defense posture while continuing to develop your ground army and technology. Maintain your current expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots and Stalkers. Your technology is heavy, but your defense is light, and you are in the early-midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense posture while continuing to develop your ground army and technology. Maintain your current expansion and production.

Your light defense posture is a risk if the opponent applies pressure. Increasing defense will help stabilize your position while you continue to develop.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more stable position with improved defense, while continuing to develop your ground forces and technology.
