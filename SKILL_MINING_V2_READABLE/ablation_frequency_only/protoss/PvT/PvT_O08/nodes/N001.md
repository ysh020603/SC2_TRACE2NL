# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 5 minutes, both sides have a heavy economy and production, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Marines, Reapers) and you having a ground army with Stalkers. Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Stalkers. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early-midgame phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a focus on ground combat. Strengthening your ground forces and technology will help you match their composition and prepare for potential engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more robust ground composition with additional units like Sentries and Immortals, while keeping an eye on the opponent's tech path.
