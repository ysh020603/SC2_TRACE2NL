# N002 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the opponent's posture is unknown (possibly Marines), and you have a ground army with Zealots. Both have heavy economies and technology. Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Zealots. Your production is moderate, and you are in the early game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

With the opponent's posture unknown, it is prudent to continue your planned development while keeping flexibility to adapt. Strengthening your ground forces and technology provides a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more defined army composition as scouting reveals the opponent's intentions, while maintaining a strong economy and technology base.
