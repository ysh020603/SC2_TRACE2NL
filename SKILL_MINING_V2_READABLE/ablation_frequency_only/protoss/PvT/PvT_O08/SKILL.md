# PvT_O08 Technology/Economy/Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O08
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: Technology/Economy/Production
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while maintaining a moderate production base. The early game focuses on developing a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers, transitioning into a heavier ground composition with Sentries and Immortals in the midgame. The opening is flexible, allowing for adaptation to air-oriented play if needed.

Develop a technology/economy/production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 5 minutes, both sides have a heavy economy and production, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Marines, Reapers) and you having a ground army with Stalkers.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.
