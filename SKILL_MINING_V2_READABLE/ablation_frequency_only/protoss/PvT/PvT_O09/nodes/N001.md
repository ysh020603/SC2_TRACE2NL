# N001 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with own posture unknown-oriented, moderate production, heavy tech, and light expansion. Opponent posture is uncertain, possibly with Reaper or Marine cues, moderate production, heavy tech. Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a light defense. Increase production and consider expanding to support your tech-heavy build.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain; you may have seen a Reaper or Marine, but their overall posture is unknown. They appear to have moderate production and heavy technology investment, but their expansion and pressure posture are unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Zealot and are investing heavily in technology and economy, but your army composition and expansion status are not yet clear. Production is moderate, and you have not committed to a specific army style.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a light defense. Increase production and consider expanding to support your tech-heavy build.

This opening prioritizes long-term economic and tech advantages. By staying flexible and not over-committing to early army units, you can transition into a strong mid-game composition based on what you scout.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined ground-based army with Stalkers and Observers, while maintaining heavy tech and economy. Aim to secure a second expansion.
