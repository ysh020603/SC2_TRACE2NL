# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 420-540 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production, tech, economy, and expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army, focusing on units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or Colossus. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding air support if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. They have heavy production and tech, and are likely preparing for a push or contain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. Your economy and tech are heavy, and you have multiple expansions. Production is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, focusing on units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or Colossus. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding air support if needed.

Siege Tanks are a core threat; Immortals and Colossus are effective. Your heavy economy allows for a larger army and tech upgrades. Observers provide crucial vision.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-midgame army with Colossus or High Templar, and possibly a fleet of Void Rays or Carriers if the opponent goes heavy on air.
