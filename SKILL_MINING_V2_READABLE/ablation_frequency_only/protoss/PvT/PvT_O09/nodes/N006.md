# N006 — Early Midgame Defensive Ground Army

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 300 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production, tech, economy, and moderate expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines, heavy production and tech. Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding Observers for detection and Immortals for tanky units. Increase your expansion to support further production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. They have heavy production and tech, and may be planning a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Your economy and tech are heavy, and you have a moderate expansion. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding Observers for detection and Immortals for tanky units. Increase your expansion to support further production.

Widow Mines require detection, and Sentries can provide defensive force fields. Your heavy economy allows for a strong defense and transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more aggressive mid-game army with Immortals or Colossus, while maintaining a solid defense.
