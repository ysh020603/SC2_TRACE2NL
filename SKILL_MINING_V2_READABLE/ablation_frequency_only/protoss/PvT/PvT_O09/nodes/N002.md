# N002 — Mid-Early Game Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 300-360 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Marine/Reaper cues, heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead. Consider adding Sentries or Immortals based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and tech. They are likely expanding and may be preparing for a mid-game push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Observers. Your economy and tech are heavy, and you have expanded. Production is heavy, allowing for continuous unit reinforcement.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead. Consider adding Sentries or Immortals based on scouting.

With a strong economy and tech, you can out-produce the opponent. Observers provide vision to avoid surprises. This is a safe development phase to build a decisive advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a mid-game army with Siege Tank counters like Immortals or Colossus, while maintaining heavy economy and tech.
