# N004 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with own posture unknown-oriented, moderate production, heavy tech, light expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Marine cues, heavy production and tech. Increase production and consider a defensive structure or unit to hold off early pressure. Continue tech and economy development, and prepare to expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, heavy production and tech. They may be planning an early push or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Zealot and are focusing on tech and economy. Your production is moderate, and you have not expanded yet. Your defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and consider a defensive structure or unit to hold off early pressure. Continue tech and economy development, and prepare to expand.

The opponent's heavy production suggests potential early aggression. A defensive investment can buy time for your tech to pay off.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more stable ground army and secure an expansion, while maintaining tech and economy.
