# PvT_O09 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O09
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production. The early game is characterized by a Zealot-based ground presence and a focus on tech structures, with the intention of transitioning into a robust mid-game army. The opening is flexible, allowing for adaptation to Terran pressure or greedy play.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180 seconds) with own posture unknown-oriented, moderate production, heavy tech, and light expansion. Opponent posture is uncertain, possibly with Reaper or Marine cues, moderate production, heavy tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a light defense. Increase production and consider expanding to support your tech-heavy build.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Mid-Early Game Ground Army Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 300-360 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Marine/Reaper cues, heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead. Consider adding Sentries or Immortals based on scouting.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [FREQUENT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 420-540 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production, tech, economy, and expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army, focusing on units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or Colossus. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding air support if needed.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [FREQUENT] N004 — Early Game Defensive Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180 seconds) with own posture unknown-oriented, moderate production, heavy tech, light expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Marine cues, heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Increase production and consider a defensive structure or unit to hold off early pressure. Continue tech and economy development, and prepare to expand.

**Read for details:** `N004`

### [FREQUENT] N005 — Late Midgame Ground Army with Warp Prism

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 600 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production, tech, economy, and expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding Colossus or High Templar for area damage. Use Warp Prisms for mobility and harassment. Maintain your economy and tech lead.

**Read for details:** `N005`

### [FREQUENT] N006 — Early Midgame Defensive Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 300 seconds, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production, tech, economy, and moderate expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines, heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding Observers for detection and Immortals for tanky units. Increase your expansion to support further production.
