# PvT_O05 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy economy and technology investment while maintaining a flexible posture. Early game focuses on developing infrastructure and tech, with a gradual shift toward a ground-oriented army composition in the midgame. The opening is designed to support multiple transition paths, including ground-based compositions with Colossus or High Templar, or a mixed air-ground approach with Phoenix.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers, and heavy production and technology. The opponent is also ground-oriented with Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines, with heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and tech. Maintain your expansion and economy. Consider adding Immortals or Colossus to counter potential Marine-heavy compositions.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, you have an unknown army posture with Zealots, and the opponent is unknown with a possible Reaper. Both have heavy economy and tech, but production is moderate.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech. Consider adding a Stargate or Twilight Council to open up tech options. Keep scouting to determine the opponent's direction.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [FREQUENT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 420 seconds, you have a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals, and the opponent has a similar ground composition with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs. Both have heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals or Colossus to counter the bio composition. Increase your production and tech, and consider adding a warp prism for mobility.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [FREQUENT] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Army with Colossus

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 600 seconds, you have a ground army with Colossus, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries, and the opponent has a ground composition with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs. Both have heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Colossus or transitioning to High Templar for storm. Maintain your economy and tech, and consider adding a third or fourth base.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [FREQUENT] N005 — Early Game Ground Tech with Marine Pressure

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, you have an unknown army posture with Zealots, and the opponent has a ground posture with Marines. Both have heavy economy and tech, but the opponent's production is heavy.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech, but be prepared for early pressure. Consider adding a shield battery or a few more Zealots for defense. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

**Read for details:** `N005`
