## When This Applies

- The opponent is also in the early game, with a possible Reaper scout. Their tech is heavy, but their army composition is unclear. They may be going for a fast expand or a tech rush.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a focus on economy and tech. You have a Zealot or two, but your army is not yet defined. Your production is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech. Consider adding a Stargate or Twilight Council to open up tech options. Keep scouting to determine the opponent's direction.

This opening prioritizes a strong economy and tech foundation, which will allow you to outscale the opponent in the midgame. Early army is minimal, so you need to rely on defensive structures and positioning.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
