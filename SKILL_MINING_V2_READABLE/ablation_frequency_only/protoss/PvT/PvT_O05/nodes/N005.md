# N005 — Early Game Ground Tech with Marine Pressure

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have an unknown army posture with Zealots, and the opponent has a ground posture with Marines. Both have heavy economy and tech, but the opponent's production is heavy. Continue developing your economy and tech, but be prepared for early pressure. Consider adding a shield battery or a few more Zealots for defense. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, indicating a possible early pressure or a fast expand. Their production is heavy, so they may be planning a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a focus on economy and tech. You have a Zealot or two, but your army is not yet defined. Your production is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech, but be prepared for early pressure. Consider adding a shield battery or a few more Zealots for defense. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

Your tech and economy are strong, but you need to survive early pressure. A shield battery and a few Zealots can hold off a Marine push while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent's build, whether that be ground-based or tech-heavy.
