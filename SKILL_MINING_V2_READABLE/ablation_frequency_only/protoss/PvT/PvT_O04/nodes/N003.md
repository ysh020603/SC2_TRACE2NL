# N003 — Early-Mid Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame around 5-6 minutes, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, opponent shows Marine/Reaper and heavy production. Strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase economy and production, increase technology and upgrades, develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is moderate. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase economy and production, increase technology and upgrades, develop with safety checks.

With both sides heavy on ground tech, developing a strong ground army while maintaining defense is essential. Heavy production allows for flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized composition based on opponent's tech choices.
