# N004 — Early-Mid Tech Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame around 4-5 minutes, own posture unknown-oriented with heavy tech and defense, opponent shows Reaper and moderate production. Strengthen ground army, increase defense and economy, maintain expansion, continue production and technology, stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is light or uncertain. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase defense and economy, maintain expansion, continue production and technology, stabilize then develop.

Tech-heavy with uncertain expansion, stabilizing defense and economy is key. Ground army strengthening counters potential Reaper harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once opponent's intentions are clearer.
