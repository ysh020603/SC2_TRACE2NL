# N006 — Late-Mid Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame around 10-12 minutes, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, opponent shows Marine and heavy production. Strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase economy and production, increase technology and upgrades, develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase economy and production, increase technology and upgrades, develop with safety checks.

Both sides are heavily invested in ground. Maintaining a strong ground army with tech advantages is key to not falling behind.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can break siege lines, possibly with disruptors or air support.
