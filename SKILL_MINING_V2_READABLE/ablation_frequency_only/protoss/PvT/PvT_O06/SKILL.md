# PvT_O06 Technology/Economy/Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O06
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: Technology / Economy / Production
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A heavy-economy, heavy-technology, heavy-production opening that develops a broadly ground-oriented posture while retaining flexibility to shift toward air or tech units based on live observation.

Develop a technology/economy/production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, both sides show heavy economy, production, and technology, with the opponent likely fielding Marines and Reapers, while you have Stalkers.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and economy, increasing production and technology while maintaining expansion.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Midgame Ground Tech Push

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 420-480 seconds, both sides have heavy economy and tech, with the opponent adding Marauders, and you have Zealots and Stalkers.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army, increase production and technology, and consider adding support units like Sentries or Immortals.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [FREQUENT] N003 — Early Game Tech Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent's posture is unknown, with possible Reaper, while you have heavy tech and moderate production.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your technology and economy, while increasing production and scouting to determine the opponent's intent.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [FREQUENT] N004 — Early Game Ground Read

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Reaper, while your own posture is still unknown.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your technology and economy, while increasing production and scouting to confirm the opponent's ground intent.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [FREQUENT] N005 — Late Midgame Ground Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 600-720 seconds, the opponent has a ground army with Medivacs, and you have High Templars and Sentries, indicating a tech-heavy ground composition.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and technology, adding upgrades and support units, while maintaining economy and expansion.

**Read for details:** `N005`
