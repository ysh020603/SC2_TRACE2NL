# N003 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent's posture is unknown, with possible Reaper, while you have heavy tech and moderate production. Continue developing your technology and economy, while increasing production and scouting to determine the opponent's intent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, tech heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and technology, but production is moderate and expansion is light or uncertain. Army composition is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy, while increasing production and scouting to determine the opponent's intent.

This frequent path supports a macro-oriented approach, leveraging your heavy economy and tech to outscale the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy and tech base while gaining scouting information.
