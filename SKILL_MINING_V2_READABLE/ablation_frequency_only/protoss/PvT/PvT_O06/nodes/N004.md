# N004 — Early Game Ground Read

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Reaper, while your own posture is still unknown. Continue developing your technology and economy, while increasing production and scouting to confirm the opponent's ground intent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, tech heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and technology, but production is moderate and expansion is light or uncertain. Army composition is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy, while increasing production and scouting to confirm the opponent's ground intent.

This frequent path supports a macro-oriented approach, leveraging your heavy economy and tech to outscale the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy and tech base while confirming the opponent's composition.
