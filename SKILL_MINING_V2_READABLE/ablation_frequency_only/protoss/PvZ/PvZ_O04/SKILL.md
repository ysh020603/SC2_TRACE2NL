# PvZ_O04 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O04
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production. The early game focuses on establishing a strong economic base and teching up, with the army composition leaning ground-oriented but retaining flexibility to transition to air if scouting information suggests it.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, you are establishing your economy and technology infrastructure. You have a Zealot as a representative unit, and your opponent is likely to have a Queen, indicating a standard Zerg opening.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Consider expanding to secure your economy and maintain a defensive posture.
