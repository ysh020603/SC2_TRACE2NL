## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You have a mixed army with Void Rays, indicating a transition to air. The opponent has Zerglings and Queens, with a ground posture. Continue to strengthen your air presence and increase your technology. Consider adding more Void Rays or other air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, and technology is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a mixed army with Void Rays. Your defense is moderate, and technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air presence and increase your technology. Consider adding more Void Rays or other air units.

Void Rays can be very effective against Zerg ground armies, especially if they lack anti-air. Your tech advantage allows you to produce them.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
