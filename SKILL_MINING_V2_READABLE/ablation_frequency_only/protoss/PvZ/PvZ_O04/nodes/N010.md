# N010 — Mid-Game Ground Army with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You have a ground army with Zealots, and a heavy defense. The opponent has Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a strong ground presence. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase your production. Consider adding more advanced units like Stalkers or Immortals.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army of Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Their production is moderate, and technology is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a ground-oriented army with Zealots. Your defense is heavy, and technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase your production. Consider adding more advanced units like Stalkers or Immortals.

With a heavy defense, you can safely expand and tech up. Your ground army can be strengthened to take map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a larger, more upgraded ground army and consider adding air support if the opponent transitions to air.
