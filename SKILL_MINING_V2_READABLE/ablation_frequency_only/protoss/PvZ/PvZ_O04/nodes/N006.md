# N006 — Early Mid-Game Ground Army with Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You are in the early mid-game with a ground army of Stalkers and Sentries, and a heavy technology investment. The opponent has Zerglings and Queens. Continue to increase your economy and production while strengthening your ground army. Consider expanding to secure your economy and adding more tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a heavy production and technology. Your expansion is light or uncertain, but you have a solid ground army core.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your economy and production while strengthening your ground army. Consider expanding to secure your economy and adding more tech.

With a tech advantage, you can produce more advanced units. By expanding and increasing production, you can outscale the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a larger ground army with more advanced units and secure additional expansions.
