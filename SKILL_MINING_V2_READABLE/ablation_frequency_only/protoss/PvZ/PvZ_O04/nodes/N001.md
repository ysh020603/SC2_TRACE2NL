# N001 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, you are establishing your economy and technology infrastructure. You have a Zealot as a representative unit, and your opponent is likely to have a Queen, indicating a standard Zerg opening. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Consider expanding to secure your economy and maintain a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a ground macro posture with a heavy economy and expansion. They have a Queen as a representative unit, and their production and technology are moderate or light. Their pressure posture is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology focus, but your expansion is light or uncertain. Your army is ground-oriented with a Zealot, and production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Consider expanding to secure your economy and maintain a defensive posture.

This opening prioritizes long-term economic and technological advantages. By focusing on economy and tech, you can outscale the opponent in the mid-game, but you need to ensure you have enough defense to survive any early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with more production and a solid expansion. Aim to have a healthy economy and tech lead by the early mid-game.
