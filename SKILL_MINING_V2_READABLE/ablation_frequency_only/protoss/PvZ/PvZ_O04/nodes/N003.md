# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Army

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You are in the early game with a heavy technology focus, but your army composition is unknown. You have a Zealot, but your overall army direction is not yet defined. The opponent has a Queen. Focus on developing your technology and economy while scouting to determine your army direction. Consider building a defensive structure or units to protect against potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is on a ground macro posture with a heavy economy and expansion. They have a Queen, and their production is moderate. Technology is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a heavy technology investment. Your expansion is heavy, but your army domain and style are unknown. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your technology and economy while scouting to determine your army direction. Consider building a defensive structure or units to protect against potential early pressure.

With an unknown army, it's crucial to gather information and adapt. Your heavy tech investment gives you flexibility, but you need to decide on a composition based on the opponent's actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Determine your army direction based on scouting and transition to a solid composition, whether ground or air, while maintaining your tech and economy lead.
