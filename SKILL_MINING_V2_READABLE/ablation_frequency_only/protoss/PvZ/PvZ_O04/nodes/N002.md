# N002 — Early Mid-Game Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You have transitioned into the early mid-game with a solid economy and production. Your army now includes Zealots and Stalkers, while the opponent has Zerglings and Queens. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and technology development, and consider adding more gateways or tech structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is still on a ground macro posture with a heavy economy and expansion. They have Zerglings and Queens, and their production is moderate. Their technology is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army of Zealots and Stalkers. Your expansion is heavy, and technology is heavy. You are in a good position to increase your army strength.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and technology development, and consider adding more gateways or tech structures.

With a strong economy, you can afford to invest in a larger army and more production. This will help you maintain map control and prepare for potential mid-game engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a larger, more versatile ground army with additional tech (e.g., Sentries, Immortals) and ensure your production can sustain it.
