# N004 — Mid-Game Ground Army with Tech Advantage

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the mid-game, you have a strong ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. The opponent has Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a heavy ground presence. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintaining your economy. Consider adding more tech upgrades or expanding your army with additional units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army of Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a strong defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army that includes advanced units like Immortals and Observers. Your defense is moderate, and technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintaining your economy. Consider adding more tech upgrades or expanding your army with additional units.

Your tech advantage allows you to field a more powerful army. By continuing to invest in tech and production, you can maintain an edge in engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a larger, more upgraded ground army and consider adding air support if the opponent transitions to air.
