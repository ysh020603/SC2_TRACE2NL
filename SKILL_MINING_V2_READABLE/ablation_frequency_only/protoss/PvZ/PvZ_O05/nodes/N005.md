# N005 — Early-Midgame Economy and Production Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture unknown but defense and tech heavy, opponent ground posture with light tech. Increase production and economy while maintaining defense. Continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, and their tech level appears light or uncertain. They may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unclear, but you have a heavy defense and tech investment. Your economy and expansion are both heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and economy while maintaining defense. Continue tech development.

With a solid defensive foundation, you can afford to expand your economy and production. This sets up a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong economy and production base to support a midgame army.
