# N004 — Midgame Ground Army and Tech Push

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame, own army is ground-based with heavy production and tech, opponent ground posture with moderate tech. Strengthen your ground forces and increase defense. Continue tech development and maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is heavy, and their tech level appears moderate. They may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Carriers. Your production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces and increase defense. Continue tech development and maintain economy.

With a strong ground army and tech advantage, you can apply pressure or defend against the Zerg's ground forces. Carriers provide a powerful late-game option.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army while preparing for potential air threats.
