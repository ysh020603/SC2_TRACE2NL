# N001 — Early Defense and Tech Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown but defense and tech heavy, opponent ground posture with light tech. Strengthen your ground forces while increasing defense and economy. Continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, and their tech level appears light or uncertain. They may be preparing for early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unclear, but you have invested heavily in defense and technology. Production is moderate, and you are prioritizing a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces while increasing defense and economy. Continue tech development.

Given the opponent's possible early pressure, a defensive posture with tech investment allows you to stabilize and then develop. Ground forces are a safe response to a ground-oriented opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a solid economy and tech advantage.
