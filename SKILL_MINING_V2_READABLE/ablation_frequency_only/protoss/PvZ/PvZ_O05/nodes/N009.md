# N009 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army is air-based with heavy production and tech, opponent ground posture with light tech. Increase air forces and continue tech development. Maintain defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, and their tech level appears light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-based army with Oracles. Your production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces and continue tech development. Maintain defense and economy.

An early air transition with Oracles can provide harassment and map control, while your tech advantage allows for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army to transition into the midgame.
