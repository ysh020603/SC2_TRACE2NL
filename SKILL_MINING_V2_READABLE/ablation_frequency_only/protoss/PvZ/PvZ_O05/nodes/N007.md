# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Army with Mixed Air

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own army is ground-based with heavy production and tech, opponent ground posture with heavy tech. Maintain ground forces and continue tech development. Consider adding more air units for flexibility.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Their production and tech are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Phoenixes. Your production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground forces and continue tech development. Consider adding more air units for flexibility.

A mixed army with Phoenixes provides anti-air and harassment capabilities, while ground forces remain strong against the Zerg's ground composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a flexible army that can adapt to the opponent's tech choices.
