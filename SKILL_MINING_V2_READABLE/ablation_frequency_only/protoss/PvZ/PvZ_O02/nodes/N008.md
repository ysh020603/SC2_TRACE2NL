# N008 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, you have developed a moderate economy and heavy technology, while the opponent shows a ground macro posture. Strengthen your ground army and continue developing your economy and technology. Maintain safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed cues may include Zergling and Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue developing your economy and technology. Maintain safety checks.

With the opponent ground-oriented, it is important to build a solid ground army while maintaining economic and technological advantages.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing your ground army and economy, and prepare for potential engagements.
