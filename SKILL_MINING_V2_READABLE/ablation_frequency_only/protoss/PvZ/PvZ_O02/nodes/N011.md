## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent's ground posture is more developed, with Zergling, Queen, and Overseer cues, indicating a heavy ground macro strategy. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Consider preparing for a ground-based opponent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed cues may include Zergling, Queen, and Overseer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is still broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and technology. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Consider preparing for a ground-based opponent.

The opponent's heavy ground posture suggests a potential large ground army. Maintaining flexibility while developing is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
