# N010 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early in the game, you have a moderate economy and heavy technology, while the opponent shows a ground macro posture. Increase your economy and production while continuing to develop technology. Strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed cues may include Zergling and Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and heavy technology investment. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production while continuing to develop technology. Strengthen your ground army.

With a strong economy and technology, you can outpace the opponent in the long run. Building a ground army helps defend against potential aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing your economy and technology while preparing to respond to ground threats.
