Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Consider preparing for a ground-based opponent.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential early pressure or macro game. Maintaining flexibility while developing is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
