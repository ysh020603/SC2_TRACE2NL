# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own development is in early stages. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Avoid over-committing to any single strategy until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light_or_uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and technology investment. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Avoid over-committing to any single strategy until more information is available.

Early game information is limited, so it is prudent to develop economically and technologically while remaining flexible to adapt to the opponent's actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing your economy and technology while scouting for opponent information.
