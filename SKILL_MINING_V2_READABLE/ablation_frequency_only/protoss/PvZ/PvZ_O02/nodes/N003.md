# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Entering the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, but your own development continues. Maintain current development path, continuing to build economy and technology while keeping safety checks active.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence remains consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light_or_uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is still broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and technology. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, continuing to build economy and technology while keeping safety checks active.

With limited information, it is still prudent to develop broadly and avoid risky commitments.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing your economy and technology, and prepare to transition to a more concrete strategy once the opponent's posture is clearer.
