# N004 — Early Game Expansion Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own posture unknown but heavy tech, opponent ground posture with Queen, own expansion posture light/uncertain. Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, continue production, increase technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, continue production, increase technology and upgrades.

With a light/uncertain expansion, focusing on economy and expansion is key to catching up. The opponent's ground posture suggests potential pressure, so maintain defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
