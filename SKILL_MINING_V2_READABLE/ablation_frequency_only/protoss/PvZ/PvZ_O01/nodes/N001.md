# N001 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and technology investment, with production ramping up. Your army composition is not yet fully defined, but you have some Stalkers and Sentries. The opponent is likely on a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, showing moderate production and light technology. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and production, and increase your technology investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, technology investment is light or uncertain, and they have a heavy economy and expansion. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology posture. Your production is heavy, and you have some ground units (Stalkers, Sentries) but your army style is still flexible. Your expansion is heavy, indicating a strong economic base.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and production, and increase your technology investment.

This node represents a common path where you are building a strong economic and technological foundation. The opponent's ground posture suggests that a ground-based army with support from tech units can be effective. Continuing to develop while keeping safety checks is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more defined army composition, either ground or air, based on further scouting. Aim to increase production and technology to support your chosen direction.
