# N009 — Midgame Air Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 420-540 seconds, you have an air-oriented army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Phoenixes. The opponent has a ground macro posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, showing moderate production and technology. Continue strengthening your air army and increase your air production. Maintain your economy and technology development. Consider adding more air units like Phoenixes or Oracles.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground macro posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Their production is moderate, technology is moderate, and they have a heavy economy and expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with an air-oriented army style. Your production is heavy, and you have a heavy economy and technology investment. Your expansion is heavy, and you have a mix of air and ground units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and increase your air production. Maintain your economy and technology development. Consider adding more air units like Phoenixes or Oracles.

This node represents a common path where you are building an air army. The opponent's ground posture suggests that an air army can be effective, especially if they lack anti-air. Continuing to develop while keeping safety checks is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful air army with support from ground units. Aim to maintain a strong economy and tech base.
