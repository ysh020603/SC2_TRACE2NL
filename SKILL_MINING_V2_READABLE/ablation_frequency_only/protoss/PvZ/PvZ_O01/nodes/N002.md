# N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy technology investment and moderate production, with an unknown army style. The opponent is on a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, showing moderate production and light technology. Increase your economy and expansion while continuing your technology development. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, and consider increasing upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, technology is light or uncertain, and they have a heavy economy and expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy technology investment and moderate production. Your economy is heavy, but your expansion is light or uncertain. Your army style is unknown, and you have no representative units yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion while continuing your technology development. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, and consider increasing upgrades.

This node reflects a common early game where you are investing heavily in technology. The opponent's ground posture suggests that a ground army will be needed, but you have flexibility. Building a strong economy and tech base will pay off later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and expand your production. Aim to have a solid economy and tech base by the midgame.
