# N004 — Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. The opponent is on a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, showing moderate production and light technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your expansion. Maintain your production and technology development. Consider adding upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, technology is light or uncertain, and they have a heavy economy and expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented army style. Your production is heavy, and you have a heavy economy and technology investment. Your expansion is light or uncertain, but you have some ground units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your expansion. Maintain your production and technology development. Consider adding upgrades.

This node represents a common path where you are committing to a ground army. The opponent's ground posture suggests that a ground-based army with tech support can be effective. Continuing to develop while keeping safety checks is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful ground army with support from tech units like Immortals or Colossi. Aim to maintain a strong economy and tech base.
