# N003 — Early Game Tech and Economy with Heavy Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production and a heavy expansion. The opponent is on a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, showing moderate production and light technology. Maintain your expansion and increase production. Continue developing your technology and strengthen your ground army. Keep an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, technology is light or uncertain, and they have a heavy economy and expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology posture. Your production is moderate, and you have a heavy expansion, indicating a strong economic base. Your army style is unknown, and you have no representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your expansion and increase production. Continue developing your technology and strengthen your ground army. Keep an eye on defense.

This node represents a common path where you are building a strong economy and tech base. The opponent's ground posture suggests that a ground army will be needed, but you have flexibility. Maintaining your expansion while increasing production is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and increase production to support your economy. Aim to have a solid tech base by the midgame.
