# PvZ_O01 Technology / Economy / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / upgrade opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology and economy development while keeping production moderate and army composition flexible. The early game focuses on establishing a strong economic and technological foundation, with the option to transition into either a ground or air-oriented army based on scouting information.

Develop a technology / economy / upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240-300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and technology investment, with production ramping up. Your army composition is not yet fully defined, but you have some Stalkers and Sentries. The opponent is likely on a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, showing moderate production and light technology.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and production, and increase your technology investment.
