# PvP_O03 Technology / Defense / Economy

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O03
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / defense / economy opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss versus Protoss opening that emphasizes early technology and defensive stability while building a strong economy. The opening is flexible, allowing transitions into either ground or air compositions based on scouting information.

Develop a technology / defense / economy posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: moderate
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Tech and Defense

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game, around 3-4 minutes, with limited information about the opponent. Both players are investing heavily in technology, and your own defense is strong.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a strong defense. Avoid over-committing to a specific army composition until more information is available.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Mid-Game Ground Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 5-6 minutes, the opponent is showing a ground-based army with Zealots, and you have a strong economy and defense.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army to counter the opponent's ground forces. Continue to increase your economy and technology, but prioritize ground units and upgrades.
