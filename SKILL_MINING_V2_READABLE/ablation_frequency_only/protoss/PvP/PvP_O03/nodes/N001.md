# N001 — Early Game Tech and Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, around 3-4 minutes, with limited information about the opponent. Both players are investing heavily in technology, and your own defense is strong. Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a strong defense. Avoid over-committing to a specific army composition until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is also investing heavily in technology, with a moderate economy and light defense. Their army composition is unknown, but they may be preparing for a tech-based strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defensive posture and are investing in technology. Your economy is moderate, and you have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a strong defense. Avoid over-committing to a specific army composition until more information is available.

In the early game, information is limited, and over-committing can be punished. A balanced approach with a focus on technology and defense allows you to adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the mid-game with a solid economy and technology base, ready to choose a composition based on the opponent's actions.
