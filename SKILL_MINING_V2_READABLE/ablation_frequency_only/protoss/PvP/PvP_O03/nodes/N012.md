# N012 — Early Mid-Game Air Mirror

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 6-7 minutes, the opponent has an air-based army with Oracles, and you have an air-based army with Oracles. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize air units and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an air-based army with Oracles, and their economy and expansion are strong. They may be preparing for an air push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense, with a moderate air presence. Your army includes Oracles, and you are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize air units and upgrades.

In an air mirror, having a stronger air army and better upgrades can give you an advantage. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to out-produce the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army capable of controlling the map and threatening the opponent's bases, while maintaining a solid defense.
