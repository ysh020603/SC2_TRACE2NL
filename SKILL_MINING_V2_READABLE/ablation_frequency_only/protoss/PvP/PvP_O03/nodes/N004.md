# N004 — Mid-Game Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 7-8 minutes, the opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots and Stalkers, and you have already invested in air units like Void Rays. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize air units and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots and Stalkers, and their economy and expansion are strong. They may be preparing for a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense, with a moderate air presence. Your army includes Void Rays, and you are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize air units and upgrades.

Since you have already invested in air units, continuing to strengthen your air army can give you an advantage over the opponent's ground forces, especially if they lack anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army capable of controlling the map and threatening the opponent's bases, while maintaining a solid defense.
