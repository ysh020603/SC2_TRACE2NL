# N008 — Late Mid-Game Ground Army

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 10-12 minutes, the opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, and you have a ground-based army with Zealots. Strengthen your ground army to counter the opponent's ground forces. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize ground units and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, and their economy and expansion are strong. They may be preparing for a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense, with a ground-based army. Your army includes Zealots, and you are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to counter the opponent's ground forces. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize ground units and upgrades.

Since both you and the opponent are ground-based, a strong ground army with appropriate units (e.g., Immortals to counter Stalkers) can give you an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army capable of defending against the opponent's push, while continuing to expand and tech up.
