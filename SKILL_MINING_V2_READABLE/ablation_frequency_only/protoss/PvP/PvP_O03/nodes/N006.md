# N006 — Late Mid-Game Air Superiority

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 10-12 minutes, the opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, and you have a strong air army with Void Rays. Continue to increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Maintain a strong defense and continue to develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, and their economy and expansion are strong. They may be preparing for a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense, with a heavy air presence. Your army includes Void Rays, and you are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Maintain a strong defense and continue to develop your economy and technology.

With a strong air army, you can control the map and threaten the opponent's bases, while the opponent's ground army may struggle to deal with your air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air superiority and use it to pressure the opponent, while continuing to expand and tech up.
