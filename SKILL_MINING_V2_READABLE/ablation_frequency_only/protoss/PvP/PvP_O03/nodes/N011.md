# N011 — Mid-Game Ground Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 9-10 minutes, the opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and defense. You have a ground-based army with Zealots, Warp Prisms, Immortals, and Oracles. Continue to develop your ground army and technology, while maintaining a strong defense. Avoid making a large army commitment until you have more information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and defense, but their army composition is unknown. They may be teching up for a specific strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense, with a ground-based army. Your army includes Zealots, Warp Prisms, Immortals, and Oracles, and you are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your ground army and technology, while maintaining a strong defense. Avoid making a large army commitment until you have more information.

With limited information, it is safer to focus on economy and technology, which gives you flexibility to respond to the opponent's strategy later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the late mid-game with a strong economy and technology base, ready to choose a composition based on the opponent's actions.
