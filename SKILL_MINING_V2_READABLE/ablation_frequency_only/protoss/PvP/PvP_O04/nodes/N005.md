# N005 — Early-Mid Air Transition by Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 300-360 seconds, opponent shows air presence with Oracles, while you are ground-oriented. Maintain ground strength but consider adding anti-air or transitioning to a mixed composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech, and possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength but consider adding anti-air or transitioning to a mixed composition.

Oracles can harass and provide vision; you need to defend against air threats while maintaining your economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Adapt to opponent's air presence while maintaining a strong economy.
