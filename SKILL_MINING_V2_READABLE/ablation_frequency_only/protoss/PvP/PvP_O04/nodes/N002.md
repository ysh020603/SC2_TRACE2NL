# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 180 seconds, you have heavy tech investment but opponent's army composition is unknown. Stabilize your economy and production, continue technology development, and prepare to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown army posture, moderate production, heavy tech, and light expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Stalker and heavy technology investment, with moderate production and economy. Expansion is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your economy and production, continue technology development, and prepare to adapt based on scouting.

Early game uncertainty requires a balanced approach to avoid being caught off-guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with clear scouting information.
