# N006 — Midgame Air Dominance by Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 420-540 seconds, opponent has heavy air presence with Void Rays and Oracles, while you are ground-oriented. Maintain ground strength but invest in anti-air units and possibly transition to a mixed or air composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy air posture with Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Stalkers and Sentries, heavy production and tech, and multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength but invest in anti-air units and possibly transition to a mixed or air composition.

Void Rays are powerful against ground; you need to counter with anti-air or air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a composition that can handle heavy air while maintaining economy.
