# N011 — Midgame Air vs Air

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 420-540 seconds, both sides have heavy air presence with Void Rays and Oracles. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces, while maintaining economy and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy air posture with Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers, heavy production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces, while maintaining economy and defense.

The matchup is air vs air; you need to out-produce and out-tech your opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
