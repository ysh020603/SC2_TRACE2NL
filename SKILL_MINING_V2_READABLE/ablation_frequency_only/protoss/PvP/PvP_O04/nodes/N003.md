# N003 — Early Game Tech with Stalker Cue

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 180 seconds, you have heavy tech and a Stalker, while opponent shows a Stalker cue but unknown posture. Continue developing economy and technology, strengthen ground forces, and maintain flexibility.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Stalker cue, moderate production, heavy tech, and unknown army posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Stalker and heavy technology, moderate production, and heavy economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology, strengthen ground forces, and maintain flexibility.

The Stalker cue suggests possible early aggression, so maintaining a defensive ground core is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a safe early-midgame with a solid ground army.
