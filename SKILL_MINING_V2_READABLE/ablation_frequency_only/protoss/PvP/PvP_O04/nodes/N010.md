# N010 — Early-Mid Air Transition with Mixed Own

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 300 seconds, opponent shows air presence with Oracles, while you have a mixed army. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces, while maintaining economy and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a mixed army with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech, and multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces, while maintaining economy and defense.

Your mixed composition allows you to match the opponent's air presence.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a strong mixed army with air superiority.
