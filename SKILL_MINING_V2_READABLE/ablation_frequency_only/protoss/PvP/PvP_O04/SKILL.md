# PvP_O04 Technology/Economy/Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O04
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: Technology / Economy / Upgrade
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy development with moderate production, aiming for a flexible midgame posture.

Develop a technology/economy/upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 240-300 seconds, both sides show heavy economy and technology, with ground-oriented armies and possible pressure.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansions, maintain defense, and continue technology development.
