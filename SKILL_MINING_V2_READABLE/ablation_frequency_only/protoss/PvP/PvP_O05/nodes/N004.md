# N004 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 4 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production and an unknown army posture. The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a possible ground posture. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining moderate production. Keep your army options open and prepare to adapt to the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a possible ground posture and Zealots. Their production is moderate, and their defense is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and an unknown army posture. Your expansion is heavy, and you have some Stalkers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining moderate production. Keep your army options open and prepare to adapt to the opponent's composition.

This approach allows you to build a strong economic and technological base, which can be leveraged to produce a powerful army later, while remaining flexible.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a strong economy and technology, ready to choose a strategic direction based on the opponent's actions.
