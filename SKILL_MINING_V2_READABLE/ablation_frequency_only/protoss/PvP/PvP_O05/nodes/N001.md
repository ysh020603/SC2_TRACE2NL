## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 4 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production and a possible ground army. The opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and technology, but their exact composition is unclear. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to have a ground posture with heavy production and technology, and a moderate expansion. They may have Stalkers, but their exact army composition and hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and a possible ground army. Your expansion is heavy, but your defense is light or uncertain. You have some Stalkers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows aggression.

This approach allows you to leverage your economic and technological advantages to build a strong midgame army, while keeping options open to transition to air or other compositions if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a solid midgame position with a strong ground army and continued economic growth, while maintaining flexibility to adapt to the opponent's composition.
