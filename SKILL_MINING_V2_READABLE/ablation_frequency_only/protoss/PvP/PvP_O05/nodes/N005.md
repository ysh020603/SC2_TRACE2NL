# N005 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 6 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology, with a moderate air presence and an air-oriented army. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Continue investing in your air army and increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and expansion, and consider adding more production to support your air forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and technology, and possible Zealots. Their expansion is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology, and an air-oriented army with Stalkers and Phoenixes. Your expansion is heavy, and your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue investing in your air army and increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and expansion, and consider adding more production to support your air forces.

An air transition can give you a strategic advantage if the opponent is heavily ground-oriented, as it allows you to harass and control the map while avoiding their ground army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army that can pressure the opponent and control the map, while maintaining a solid economy and expansion.
