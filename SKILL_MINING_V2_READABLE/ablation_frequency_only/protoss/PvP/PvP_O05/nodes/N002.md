# N002 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production and an unknown army posture. The opponent also has a heavy economy and technology, but their army posture is unknown. Focus on developing your technology and economy while maintaining a moderate production. Keep your options open for either ground or air transitions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production and an unknown army posture. They may have Zealots, but their exact composition is unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and an unknown army posture. Your expansion is heavy, but your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your technology and economy while maintaining a moderate production. Keep your options open for either ground or air transitions.

In the early game, it is important to establish a strong economic and technological foundation without committing to a specific army composition, allowing you to react to the opponent's choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a solid economy and technology, ready to choose a strategic direction based on the opponent's actions.
