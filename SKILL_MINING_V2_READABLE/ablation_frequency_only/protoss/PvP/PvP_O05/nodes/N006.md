# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground vs Air Adaptation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 5 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army. The opponent shows a moderate air presence and an air-oriented posture with possible Oracles. Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology, and be ready to adapt to the opponent's air threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a moderate air presence and an air-oriented posture with Stalkers and Oracles. Their production and technology are heavy, and they may be preparing for air pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology, and a ground army with Stalkers. Your expansion is heavy, and your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology, and be ready to adapt to the opponent's air threats.

Since the opponent has an air presence, it is important to have some anti-air units or structures to defend against potential harass or attacks, while still developing your own strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a balanced army that can handle both ground and air threats, while maintaining a strong economy and technology.
