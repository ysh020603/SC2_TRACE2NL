# N007 — Midgame Air Army Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 8 minutes, you have a heavy economy, production, and technology, with a heavy air presence and an air-oriented army. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Continue investing in your air army and increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and expansion, and consider adding more production to support your air forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and technology, and possible Zealots. Their expansion is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is air-oriented with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your expansion and defense are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue investing in your air army and increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and expansion, and consider adding more production to support your air forces.

A strong air army can give you a significant advantage in the midgame, allowing you to harass the opponent's economy and avoid their ground army, while your own economy and technology support the transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful air army that can control the map and pressure the opponent, while maintaining a solid economy and expansion.
