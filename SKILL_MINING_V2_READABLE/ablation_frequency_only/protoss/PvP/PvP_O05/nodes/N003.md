## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense, and consider adding more production if needed.

With a strong economy and technology, you can afford to build a powerful ground army to match the opponent's likely composition, while keeping the option to transition to air if necessary.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
