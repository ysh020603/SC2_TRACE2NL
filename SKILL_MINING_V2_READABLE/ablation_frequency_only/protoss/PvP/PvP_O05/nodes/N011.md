# N011 — Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 9 minutes, you have a heavy economy, production, and technology, with a ground army. The opponent has a mixed posture with heavy production and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense, and consider adding more production if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a mixed posture with heavy production and technology, and their exact composition is unclear. Their expansion is heavy, and they may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Stalkers. Your expansion and defense are moderate to heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain your current expansion and defense, and consider adding more production if needed.

With a strong economy and technology, you can build a powerful ground army to match the opponent's likely composition, while keeping the option to transition to air if necessary.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong midgame army and economy, ready to transition to a lategame composition based on the opponent's tech choices.
