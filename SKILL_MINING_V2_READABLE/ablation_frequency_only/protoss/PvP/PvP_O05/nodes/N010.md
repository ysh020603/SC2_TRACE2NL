# N010 — Midgame Ground Army Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 7 minutes, you have a heavy economy, production, and technology, with a ground army. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Stabilize your position by strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Continue developing your economy and technology, and consider adding defensive structures if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and technology, and possible Zealots. Their expansion is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zealots and Sentries. Your expansion and defense are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Continue developing your economy and technology, and consider adding defensive structures if needed.

With a heavy economy and technology, you can afford to build a strong ground army to match the opponent's likely composition, while ensuring your defenses are solid.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong midgame army and economy, ready to transition to a lategame composition based on the opponent's tech choices.
