# PvP_O05 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss versus Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy development with a moderate production base, aiming to establish a strong midgame position while remaining adaptable to opponent actions.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 4 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production and a possible ground army. The opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and technology, but their exact composition is unclear.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows aggression.
