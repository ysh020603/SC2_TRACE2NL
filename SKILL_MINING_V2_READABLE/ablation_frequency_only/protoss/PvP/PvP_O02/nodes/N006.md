# N006 — Early Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zealots. Your own posture is moderate with heavy technology. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Their production is moderate, technology is heavy, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your defense is moderate, economy is moderate, and technology is heavy. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology.

The opponent's ground posture suggests they may be building a Zealot-based army. Strengthening your ground forces helps you defend against potential aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army to defend and potentially counter-attack.
