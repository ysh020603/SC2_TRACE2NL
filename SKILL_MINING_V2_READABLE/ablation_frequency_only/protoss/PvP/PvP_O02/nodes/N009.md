# N009 — Midgame Maintain Current Path

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zealots, but your own posture is still light or uncertain. Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Their production is heavy, technology is heavy, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks.

Despite the opponent's ground posture, you have not seen enough to justify a major shift. Maintaining flexibility allows you to adapt later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing until you have a clearer picture of the opponent's plan.
