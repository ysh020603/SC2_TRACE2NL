# N008 — Early Midgame Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent's posture is unknown, but their economy and expansion appear heavy. Your own posture is defensive with heavy technology. Stabilize your position by increasing defense and economy, while continuing technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their economy is heavy, expansion is heavy, production is moderate, and technology is heavy. Their posture is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your defense is heavy, economy is moderate, and technology is heavy. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing defense and economy, while continuing technology development.

The opponent's heavy economy and expansion suggest they are planning a macro-oriented game. Stabilizing your defense and economy allows you to compete in the long run.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid foundation to transition into a midgame strategy.
