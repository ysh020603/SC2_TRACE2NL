# N010 — Midgame Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zealots. Your own posture is defensive with heavy technology and moderate economy. Stabilize your position by increasing defense and economy, while continuing technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Their production is heavy, technology is heavy, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your defense is heavy, economy is moderate, and technology is heavy. You have an expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing defense and economy, while continuing technology development.

The opponent's heavy ground army suggests they may be planning an aggressive push. Stabilizing your defense and economy allows you to withstand it.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid defense and economy to transition into a midgame strategy.
