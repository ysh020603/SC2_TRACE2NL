# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own development is just beginning. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks in mind.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and technology are light or uncertain, and their posture is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You are in the early game with no confirmed combat units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks in mind.

With no clear threat, it is safe to develop your economy and technology. Maintaining flexibility allows you to adapt to whatever the opponent reveals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing until you have more information about the opponent's intentions.
