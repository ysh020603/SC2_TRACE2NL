# N003 — Early Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own development is light or uncertain. Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and technology are light or uncertain, and their posture is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are light or uncertain. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks.

With no clear threat, it is safe to continue developing. Maintaining flexibility allows you to adapt to the opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing until you have more information about the opponent's intentions.
