## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 420-540 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army, while the opponent has an air posture with Void Rays. Maintain your ground army and strengthen anti-air capabilities. Continue production and tech progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Void Ray sightings. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a strong economy. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production and technology, and a light defense posture. Your economy is heavy, and you have multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and strengthen anti-air capabilities. Continue production and tech progression.

The opponent's air army poses a significant threat. Building anti-air units and maintaining a strong ground force will allow you to defend and potentially counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
