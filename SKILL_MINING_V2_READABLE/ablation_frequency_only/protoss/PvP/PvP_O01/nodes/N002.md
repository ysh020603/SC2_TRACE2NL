# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy technology investment but your army composition is not yet defined. The opponent's posture is also unknown. Increase production and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, with no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production appears moderate, but technology investment is heavy. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy technology focus, moderate production, and an undefined army composition. Your economy is heavy, but your expansion count is uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline.

With both players investing heavily in tech, the game is likely to be decided by tech advantages. Building a solid ground core while teching up provides flexibility to adapt to the opponent's eventual composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state with a defined army composition and a stronger economy.
