# N009 — Early-Midgame Ground vs Air

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army, while the opponent has transitioned to an air posture with Void Rays. Maintain your ground army and consider adding anti-air capabilities. Continue production and tech progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Void Ray sightings. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a strong economy. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production and technology, and a light defense posture. Your economy is heavy, and you have multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and consider adding anti-air capabilities. Continue production and tech progression.

The opponent's air transition requires you to adapt. Maintaining a strong ground army while adding anti-air units will help you defend against Void Rays and potentially punish their tech investment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state with a balanced army that can handle both ground and air threats.
