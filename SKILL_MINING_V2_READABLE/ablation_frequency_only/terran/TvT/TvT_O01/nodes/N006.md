# N006 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 480-540 seconds, both players have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. The opponent has Siege Tanks and Marines, and their defense is moderate. Maintain your ground army and economy. Consider increasing your air presence for mobility. Continue developing your technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Your defense is moderate, and you are in the midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy. Consider increasing your air presence for mobility. Continue developing your technology.

Maintaining a strong ground army while adding air support can provide flexibility. A heavy economy allows for this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air components.
