# N002 — Early Game Ground Macro with Moderate Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and technology, but moderate production. The opponent is likely to have a ground posture with Reapers, but their production is heavy. Increase your production to match the opponent's heavy production. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Reapers. Their production appears heavy, and they have a heavy economy and technology. Their defense is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground-oriented army including Marines. Your production is moderate, so you may need to increase it to keep up with the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production to match the opponent's heavy production. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Consider expanding further.

In the early game, keeping production on par with the opponent is crucial to avoid falling behind. A heavy economy supports this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced production and economy, preparing for the midgame.
