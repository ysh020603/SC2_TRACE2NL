# N009 — Early Game Ground Macro with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground-oriented army. The opponent's posture is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and technology. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain your production and consider expanding. Maintain scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's posture. They have a heavy economy and technology, but their production is moderate. No reliable combat-unit cues are available.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Reapers. Your production is heavy, and you are in the early game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain your production and consider expanding. Maintain scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

With an unknown opponent, it is important to maintain a solid economy and production while gathering information. A ground army is a safe default.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more informed strategy once you have scouted the opponent.
