## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. The opponent's posture is ground, but their exact composition is uncertain. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain your defense and consider increasing production. Continue scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Reapers. Your defense is heavy, and you are in the early-midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain your defense and consider increasing production. Continue scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

With an uncertain opponent, maintaining a strong defense and economy is important. A ground army is a safe default.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more informed strategy once you have scouted the opponent.
