# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 480-540 seconds, both players have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Siege Tanks and Marines. The opponent may have a moderate air presence. Increase your air presence and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Consider adding more Medivacs or transitioning to air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. Their air presence is moderate, and they have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs. Your air presence is moderate, and you are in the midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Consider adding more Medivacs or transitioning to air units.

As the game progresses, having a moderate air presence can provide mobility and support. Increasing technology can unlock stronger units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air components, preparing for the late game.
