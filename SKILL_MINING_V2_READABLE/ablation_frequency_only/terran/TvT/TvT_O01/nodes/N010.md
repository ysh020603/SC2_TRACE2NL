# N010 — Early Game Ground Macro with Moderate Economy

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a moderate economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. The opponent has a ground posture with Reapers, and their production is heavy. Increase your economy and production to match the opponent. Continue developing your ground army. Consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Reapers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and production, with a ground army including Marines. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production to match the opponent. Continue developing your ground army. Consider expanding.

In the early game, keeping up with the opponent's economy and production is crucial to avoid falling behind.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced economy and production, preparing for the midgame.
