# N008 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, both players have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. The opponent has Siege Tanks and Marauders, and their air presence is moderate. Increase your air presence and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Consider adding more Medivacs or transitioning to air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marauders. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs. Your air presence is moderate, and you are in the late-midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Consider adding more Medivacs or transitioning to air units.

As the game progresses, having a moderate air presence can provide mobility and support. Increasing technology can unlock stronger units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air components, preparing for the late game.
