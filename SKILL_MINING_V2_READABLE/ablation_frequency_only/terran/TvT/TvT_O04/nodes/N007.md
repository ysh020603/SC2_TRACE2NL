# N007 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the game is in the early phase with both players focusing on economy, but the opponent shows a Marauder, indicating a possible tech path. Focus on stabilizing your economy and production while scouting to determine the opponent's exact build.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Marauder, with heavy technology investment, but production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, but your army composition is unknown, with only a Reaper seen. Production is moderate, and technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your economy and production while scouting to determine the opponent's exact build.

This node represents a common early game state where the opponent may be teching. Stabilizing your own position is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a solid game plan once the opponent's strategy is clearer.
