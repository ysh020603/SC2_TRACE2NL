# N006 — Late Midgame Ground with Air Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the game is in the late midgame with both players having heavy economies and ground armies, but the opponent shows signs of air tech. Maintain your ground army while preparing for potential air threats by adding anti-air capabilities.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Banshees, indicating air tech investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. Your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while preparing for potential air threats by adding anti-air capabilities.

This node represents a common late midgame state where the opponent may be transitioning to air. Preparing for that is important.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air threats.
