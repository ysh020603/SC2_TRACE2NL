# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Consolidation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, both sides have established a heavy economy and are transitioning into a ground-oriented army composition. Continue developing your ground forces and economy, while maintaining defensive awareness for potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a similar ground posture with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and technology, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Reapers. Your technology is also heavy, indicating investment in upgrades or advanced structures.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground forces and economy, while maintaining defensive awareness for potential pressure.

This node represents a common, stable development point where both players are investing heavily in macro. Maintaining your own development while scouting for deviations is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a strong economy and a versatile ground army.
