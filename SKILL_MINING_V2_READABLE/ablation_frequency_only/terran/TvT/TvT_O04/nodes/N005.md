# N005 — Late Midgame Ground and Air Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 600-720 seconds, the game has reached the late midgame with both players having heavy economies and ground armies, and air presence is moderate. Continue to strengthen your ground forces while expanding your air capabilities to gain an edge.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a similar ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, and moderate air presence.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Your technology is heavy, and you are expanding your air forces.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground forces while expanding your air capabilities to gain an edge.

This node represents a common late midgame state where both players are building up their armies. A balanced approach can be effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a powerful army composition that can take engagements favorably.
