# N003 — Midgame Ground and Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 480-540 seconds, the game has reached the midgame with both players having heavy economies and ground armies, but air presence is increasing. Continue strengthening your ground forces while gradually adding air units to gain an advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a similar ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, and moderate air presence.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Your technology is heavy, and you are starting to invest in air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground forces while gradually adding air units to gain an advantage.

This node represents a common midgame state where both players are transitioning to more advanced compositions. Investing in air can provide flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced ground-air composition to handle various threats.
