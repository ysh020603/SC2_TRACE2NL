# N002 — Early Game Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the game is in the early phase with both players focusing on economy and technology, but army composition is not yet clear. Focus on expanding your economy and tech, while scouting to determine the opponent's exact build.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, heavy production and technology, but exact details are unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, but your army composition is unknown, with only a Reaper seen. Production and technology are moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding your economy and tech, while scouting to determine the opponent's exact build.

This is a common early game state where information is limited. Prioritizing macro while gathering intel is a safe approach.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a clearer game plan once the opponent's composition is revealed.
