# N002 — Early Midgame Ground Macro with Moderate Enemy Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, enemy production appears moderate while your own is heavy. Both have heavy economy and tech, ground-oriented armies. Increase production to leverage your production advantage, continue tech, and maintain economy growth. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows ground posture with Marines, but production is moderate, suggesting a possible tech or economy focus rather than mass army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy production, economy, and tech, with a ground army. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production to leverage your production advantage, continue tech, and maintain economy growth. Stabilize then develop.

With higher production, you can out-produce the enemy if they are investing elsewhere, but need to stabilize defenses first.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a production lead and transition to a stronger army composition, possibly with tech units.
