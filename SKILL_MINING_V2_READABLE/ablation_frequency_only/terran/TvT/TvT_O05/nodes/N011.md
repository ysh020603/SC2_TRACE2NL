# N011 — Early Game Ground Posture with Marauder

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, enemy shows a ground posture with heavy production, while you have a Marauder and unknown posture. Increase economy, increase production, and continue tech. Strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Marines, heavy production and tech, suggesting a macro ground build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and tech, but expansion is light or uncertain. Production is moderate, army unknown with a Marauder.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, increase production, and continue tech. Strengthen ground forces.

With a Marauder, you have a slight ground advantage, but need to expand and produce more to secure it.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with a solid economy.
