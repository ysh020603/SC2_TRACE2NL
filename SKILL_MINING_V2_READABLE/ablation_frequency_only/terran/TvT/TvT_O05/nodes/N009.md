# N009 — Early Game Ground Posture with Heavy Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, enemy shows a ground posture with heavy production and tech, while your own posture is unknown. Increase economy, continue tech, and strengthen ground forces. Maintain expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Marines, heavy production and tech, suggesting an aggressive or macro ground build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and expansion, but army and defense are unknown. Production is moderate, tech heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, continue tech, and strengthen ground forces. Maintain expansion and production.

Enemy is ground-focused, so you need to prepare a ground defense while continuing to develop.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a solid ground army with a strong economy.
