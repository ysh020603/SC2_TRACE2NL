## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment with moderate production, aiming for a flexible ground-oriented posture. It is a strategic template, not a fixed build order, and should be adapted based on live observations.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240-300 seconds, both sides show heavy economy and expansion, with ground-oriented armies and heavy technology investment. Enemy production may be heavy or moderate, and air presence is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Maintain ground strength, increase economy, continue technology, and keep production steady. Maintain defense and expansion, with a tempo of develop with safety checks.

**Read for details:** `N001`
