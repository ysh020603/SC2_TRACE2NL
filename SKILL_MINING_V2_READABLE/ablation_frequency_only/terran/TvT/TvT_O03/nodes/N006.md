# N006 — Midgame Tech and Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420-540s) with heavy economy, production, and technology. Opponent is ground-oriented with heavy production and possible pressure. Increase air presence and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion, and continue tech upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy, and defense is moderate. You have Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion, and continue tech upgrades.

As the game progresses, adding Medivacs provides mobility and healing, while tech upgrades improve army efficiency. This prepares for mid-game engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mobile composition with Medivacs and possibly Banshees or Ravens.
