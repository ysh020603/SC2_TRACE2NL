# N011 — Late-Midgame Air and Ground Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with heavy economy, production, and technology. Opponent is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy defense. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and expansion, and continue tech upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy, and defense is moderate. You have Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and expansion, and continue tech upgrades.

Adding air units can provide a strategic advantage, especially if the opponent lacks anti-air. Widow Mines provide ground defense while transitioning.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more air-oriented composition with Banshees or Ravens.
