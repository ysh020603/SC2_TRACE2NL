# N010 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 360s) with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and moderate expansion. Opponent is ground-oriented with heavy production. Maintain current army path and economy. Keep production and tech as is, but be ready to adapt.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Economy is heavy, but expansion is moderate. Defense is moderate. You have Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and economy. Keep production and tech as is, but be ready to adapt.

With moderate production and expansion, stabilizing is important to avoid falling behind. Maintaining current path allows for flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground composition as tech allows.
