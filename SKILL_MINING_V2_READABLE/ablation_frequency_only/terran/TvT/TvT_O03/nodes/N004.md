# N004 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-360s) with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and moderate defense. Opponent is ground-oriented with heavy production. Increase production to strengthen your ground army while maintaining economy and tech. Keep defense moderate to handle potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy, and defense is moderate. You have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production to strengthen your ground army while maintaining economy and tech. Keep defense moderate to handle potential pressure.

With moderate production, you need to ramp up to match the opponent's heavy production. Increasing production now ensures you can field a competitive army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
