# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Reinforcement

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-360s) with heavy economy, production, and technology. Opponent is ground-oriented with heavy production and possible pressure. Strengthen ground forces and maintain economy. Continue production and technology development, but keep an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy, but defense is light or uncertain. You have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces and maintain economy. Continue production and technology development, but keep an eye on defense.

With both sides investing heavily in tech and economy, a solid ground army is needed to deter aggression and set up for mid-game engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced composition with Siege Tanks or Medivacs as tech allows.
