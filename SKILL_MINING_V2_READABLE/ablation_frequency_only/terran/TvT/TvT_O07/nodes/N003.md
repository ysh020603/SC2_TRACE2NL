# N003 — Early Game Ground Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent has shown a ground posture with Marines, while you are still in an unknown posture. Both have heavy economies and technology. Strengthen your ground army and increase your defenses. Continue to expand and tech up, but be ready to adapt if the opponent commits to a specific composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, indicating a potential bio or mech transition. Their production is moderate, but their technology is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your production is moderate. Your army is small, and you have not expanded much.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your defenses. Continue to expand and tech up, but be ready to adapt if the opponent commits to a specific composition.

Since the opponent is ground-oriented, you need to prepare a ground counter. Your heavy economy allows you to outproduce them if you invest in the right units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army that can counter the opponent's composition while maintaining your economy.
