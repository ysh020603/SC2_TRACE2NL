# N009 — Late-Mid Game Army Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 600-720 seconds, both sides have a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. The opponent has Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs, while you have Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions. Increase your economy and expand further to support a larger army. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air support if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs, indicating a bio-mech composition. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expand further to support a larger army. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air support if needed.

The opponent has a strong composition, so you need to outscale them economically. By expanding and increasing production, you can field a larger army and win the late game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a large, powerful army that can overwhelm the opponent in the late game.
