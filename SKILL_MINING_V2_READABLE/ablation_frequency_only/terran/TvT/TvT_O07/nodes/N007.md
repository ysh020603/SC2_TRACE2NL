# N007 — Mid-Game Tech and Army Balance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, both sides have a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. The opponent has Marines, and you have Marines and Reapers. Maintain your current army composition while continuing to tech up. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other support units to strengthen your army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, indicating a bio or mech composition. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Reapers. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army composition while continuing to tech up. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other support units to strengthen your army.

Your current army is versatile, but you need to tech up to counter the opponent's potential composition. By balancing tech and army, you can adapt to the opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a mid-game position with a balanced army and tech that can handle various threats.
