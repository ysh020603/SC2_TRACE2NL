# N005 — Mid-Game Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, both sides have a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. The opponent has Marines, and you have a moderate defense. Stabilize your position by maintaining your defenses and continuing to develop your economy and technology. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, indicating a bio or mech composition. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. Your defenses are moderate, and you have a solid army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by maintaining your defenses and continuing to develop your economy and technology. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential engagements.

With both sides macroing, the game is likely to be decided by army composition and upgrades. By stabilizing, you can avoid costly mistakes and reach a strong mid-game position.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable mid-game with a strong economy, upgraded army, and clear tech path.
