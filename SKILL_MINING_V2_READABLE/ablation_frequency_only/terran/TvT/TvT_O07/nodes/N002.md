# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with unknown army compositions. The opponent has heavy production and technology, but their expansion is only moderate. Continue teching up and increasing your economy. Consider adding production structures to support a future army. Maintain a defensive posture to avoid early game losses.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have heavy production and technology. Their expansion is moderate, suggesting they are focusing on tech rather than early aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but your production is only moderate. Your army is small, and you have not yet expanded aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching up and increasing your economy. Consider adding production structures to support a future army. Maintain a defensive posture to avoid early game losses.

This opening prioritizes technology and economy to gain a mid-game advantage. By staying defensive, you can avoid early game setbacks and leverage your tech lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a solid early-mid game with a clear tech advantage and a growing economy.
