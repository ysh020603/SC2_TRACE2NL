# N001 — Early-Mid Macro Consolidation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, both sides have transitioned to a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and production. The opponent's army composition is not fully scouted, but their production and technology are heavy. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defenses and consider adding a defensive structure if you suspect aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture, but exact unit composition is unknown. They have heavy production and technology, indicating a macro-oriented approach. Their defenses are light, and they may be vulnerable to harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. Your army is small but you have the infrastructure to expand and tech up. Defenses are light, so you are vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defenses and consider adding a defensive structure if you suspect aggression.

This opening is designed to outscale the opponent through superior economy and technology. By focusing on macro, you can reach a stronger mid-game army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable mid-game position with a strong economy, upgraded units, and a clear tech path.
