# N004 — Early Game Expansion Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and expansion. Your own posture is unknown with moderate production. Focus on expanding and increasing your production. Continue teching up, but also start building a defensive army to protect your expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, indicating a macro-oriented approach. Their army composition is unknown, but they have moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your production is moderate. You have not expanded much, and your army is small.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding and increasing your production. Continue teching up, but also start building a defensive army to protect your expansions.

The opponent is expanding, so you need to keep up economically. By expanding and increasing production, you can match their economy and prepare for a long game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong economy with multiple bases and a production capacity to support a large army.
