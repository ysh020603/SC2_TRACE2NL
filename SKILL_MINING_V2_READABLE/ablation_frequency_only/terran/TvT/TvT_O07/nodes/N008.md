# N008 — Mid-Game Defensive Posture

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 480-540 seconds, both sides have a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. The opponent has Marines and Hellions, while you have Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders. Maintain your defensive posture and continue to develop your economy. Consider adding upgrades and expanding to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground army with Marines and Hellions, indicating a bio or mech composition. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture and continue to develop your economy. Consider adding upgrades and expanding to support a larger army.

Your defensive posture allows you to safely develop your economy and tech. By maintaining this, you can outscale the opponent in the late game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with a strong economy and a powerful army.
