# TvT_O07 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O07
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production. The strategic posture is ground-leaning with a focus on developing a robust macro foundation before committing to aggressive actions.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Mid Macro Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240-300 seconds, both sides have transitioned to a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and production. The opponent's army composition is not fully scouted, but their production and technology are heavy.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defenses and consider adding a defensive structure if you suspect aggression.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [FREQUENT] N002 — Early Game Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with unknown army compositions. The opponent has heavy production and technology, but their expansion is only moderate.

**Direction:**  
Continue teching up and increasing your economy. Consider adding production structures to support a future army. Maintain a defensive posture to avoid early game losses.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [FREQUENT] N003 — Early Game Ground Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent has shown a ground posture with Marines, while you are still in an unknown posture. Both have heavy economies and technology.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army and increase your defenses. Continue to expand and tech up, but be ready to adapt if the opponent commits to a specific composition.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [FREQUENT] N004 — Early Game Expansion Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, the opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and expansion. Your own posture is unknown with moderate production.

**Direction:**  
Focus on expanding and increasing your production. Continue teching up, but also start building a defensive army to protect your expansions.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [FREQUENT] N005 — Mid-Game Stabilization

Stabilize your position by maintaining your defenses and continuing to develop your economy and technology. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential engagements.
