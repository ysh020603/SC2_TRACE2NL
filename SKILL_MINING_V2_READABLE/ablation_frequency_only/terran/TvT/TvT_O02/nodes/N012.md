# N012 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent has a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own army is ground-based with Marines, and you have a moderate economy. Strengthen your ground army by producing more Marines and possibly adding Marauders or Siege Tanks. Continue to expand your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. They may be planning a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is ground-based with Marines, and you have a moderate economy and heavy production. You are in a good position to strengthen your ground forces.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army by producing more Marines and possibly adding Marauders or Siege Tanks. Continue to expand your economy.

With a heavy economy, you can afford a larger army. Strengthening your ground forces will help you win engagements and control the map.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful ground army that can take map control and pressure the opponent.
