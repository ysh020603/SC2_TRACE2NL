# N009 — Early-Midgame Ground Posture with Heavy Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent has a ground posture with Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own posture is unknown, but you have heavy production and technology. Strengthen your ground defense and consider producing a few units to fend off potential harassment. Continue to develop your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. They may be planning a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, but you have heavy production and technology investment. You have not yet produced many combat units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground defense and consider producing a few units to fend off potential harassment. Continue to develop your economy.

A Reaper can cause economic damage if left unchecked. Producing a few units or building defensive structures can mitigate this risk.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Defend against early harassment while maintaining your economic development.
