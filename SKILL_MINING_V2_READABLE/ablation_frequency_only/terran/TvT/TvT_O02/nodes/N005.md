# N005 — Early Game Reaper Harassment Response

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early in the game, you spot a Reaper from the opponent, indicating a possible harassment or early pressure. Your own economy is moderate, and you have invested in technology. Strengthen your ground defense and consider producing a few units to fend off potential harassment. Continue to develop your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a Reaper, suggesting a possible early harassment. Their production is moderate, and technology is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have heavy technology investment. You have not yet produced combat units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground defense and consider producing a few units to fend off potential harassment. Continue to develop your economy.

A Reaper can cause economic damage if left unchecked. Producing a few units or building defensive structures can mitigate this risk.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Defend against early harassment while maintaining your economic development.
