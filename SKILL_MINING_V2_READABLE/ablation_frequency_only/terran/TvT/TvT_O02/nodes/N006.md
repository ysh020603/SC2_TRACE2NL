# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Posture Adaptation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own posture is still unknown, but you have a moderate economy. Maintain your current development path, but start to prepare for a potential ground engagement. Consider producing units that counter Reapers, such as Marines or Marauders.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. They may be planning a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is light or uncertain, but you have a moderate production and technology investment. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but start to prepare for a potential ground engagement. Consider producing units that counter Reapers, such as Marines or Marauders.

The opponent's heavy economy and production suggest they are building a strong army. Preparing a ground force can help you defend or counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced ground army while maintaining economic growth.
