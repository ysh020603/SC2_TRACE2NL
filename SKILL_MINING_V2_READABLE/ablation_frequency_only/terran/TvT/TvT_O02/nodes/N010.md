# N010 — Early-Midgame Reaper Harassment with Moderate Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent has a ground posture with Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy technology. Continue to develop your economy and technology. Consider producing more units to support your Reaper harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Reapers, and their economy and production are heavy. They may be planning a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have heavy technology investment. You have produced a Reaper for harassment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology. Consider producing more units to support your Reaper harassment.

With a Reaper, you can harass the opponent's economy while you build up your own. This can give you an economic advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use your Reaper to gain information and economic advantage while building a solid foundation.
