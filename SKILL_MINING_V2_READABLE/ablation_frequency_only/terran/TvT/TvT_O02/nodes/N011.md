# N011 — Late-Midgame Ground Posture with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, and their economy and production are heavy. Your own posture is unknown, but you have a light economy. Maintain your current development path, but start to prepare for a potential ground engagement. Consider producing units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Marauders or air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, and their economy and production are heavy. They have a strong ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain, and you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but start to prepare for a potential ground engagement. Consider producing units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Marauders or air units.

The opponent's heavy economy and production suggest they are building a strong army. Preparing a ground force can help you defend or counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
