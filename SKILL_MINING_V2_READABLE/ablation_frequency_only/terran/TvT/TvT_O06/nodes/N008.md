# N008 — Midgame Ground with Ghost Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420s) with own heavy production and technology, including Siege Tank, Marine, Ghost, Marauder. Opponent shows heavy production and technology with Marine cues. Maintain production and technology while strengthening ground forces. Consider adding air support if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with moderate defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Marine.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with light or uncertain air presence, heavy defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Siege Tank, Marine, Ghost, Marauder.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain production and technology while strengthening ground forces. Consider adding air support if needed.

Ghosts provide utility with EMP and snipe, useful in TvT. Maintaining a strong ground core with Siege Tanks and bio is solid. Continuing tech ensures access to upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mobile composition with Medivacs or add Vikings for air control.
