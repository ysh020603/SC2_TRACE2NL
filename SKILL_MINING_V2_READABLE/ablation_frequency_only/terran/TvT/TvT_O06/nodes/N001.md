# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with both sides showing ground-oriented macro postures, heavy technology investment, and moderate production. Opponent may have Reaper or Marine cues. Strengthen ground forces while continuing economy and technology development. Maintain defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with light or uncertain air presence, heavy economy, heavy expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Reaper.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while continuing economy and technology development. Maintain defense and expansion.

Both sides are investing heavily in tech and economy. Maintaining a solid ground core with defensive structures allows safe scaling into midgame without overcommitting to a specific composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground composition (e.g., Marine-Tank or bio) while continuing to expand and tech.
