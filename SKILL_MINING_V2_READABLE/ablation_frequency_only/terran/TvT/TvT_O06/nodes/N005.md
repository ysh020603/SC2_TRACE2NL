# N005 — Early Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own posture unknown but moderate production and heavy tech. Opponent shows heavy production and technology with Marine cues. Stabilize the early game by increasing production and continuing technology development while maintaining defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy economy, moderate expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Marine.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Unknown army domain with heavy defense, heavy economy, moderate expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize the early game by increasing production and continuing technology development while maintaining defense.

With an undefined own posture, focusing on a solid economy and tech base allows flexibility to adapt to opponent's moves. Increasing production ensures a capable defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined ground composition once opponent's strategy is clearer.
