## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240s) with both sides having heavy production and technology. Own and opponent both show ground-oriented postures with Marine cues. Maintain production and economy while continuing technology development. Strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Marine.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain production and economy while continuing technology development. Strengthen ground forces.

Both sides are evenly matched in production and tech. Maintaining a steady production cycle and expanding economy ensures a strong midgame transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
