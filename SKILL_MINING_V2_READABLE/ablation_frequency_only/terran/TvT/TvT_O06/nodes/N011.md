# N011 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480s) with own heavy production and technology, including Siege Tank, Marine, Marauder, Hellion. Opponent shows Siege Tank, Reaper, Battlecruiser. Increase air presence and technology while continuing ground army strengthening and economy growth.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with moderate air presence, moderate defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative cues include Siege Tank, Reaper, Battlecruiser.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with moderate air presence, heavy defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Siege Tank, Marine, Marauder, Hellion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and technology while continuing ground army strengthening and economy growth.

Opponent showing Battlecruiser indicates a potential air transition. Adding air units (e.g., Vikings) and tech (e.g., Raven) provides counterplay and flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed composition with strong ground core and air support.
