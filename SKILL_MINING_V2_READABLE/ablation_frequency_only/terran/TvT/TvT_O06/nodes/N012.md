# N012 — Midgame Maintain Current Path

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 540s) with own heavy production and technology, including Siege Tank and Marine. Opponent shows Marine, Marauder, Hellion, WidowMine. Maintain current army path and production while continuing technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with light or uncertain air presence, moderate defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative cues include Marine, Marauder, Hellion, WidowMine.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with light or uncertain air presence, heavy defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Siege Tank, Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and production while continuing technology development.

With a solid Siege Tank and Marine composition, maintaining production and tech is sufficient. WidowMines can be countered with detection and positioning.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing into late-midgame with a strong ground army.
