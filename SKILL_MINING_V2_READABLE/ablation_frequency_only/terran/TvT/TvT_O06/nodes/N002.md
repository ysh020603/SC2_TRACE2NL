# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240s) with both sides having heavy economy and technology, and opponent showing heavy production. Own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production. Increase economy and expansion while continuing ground army strengthening and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy economy, heavy expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Reaper.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, heavy expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Marine and Reaper.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion while continuing ground army strengthening and technology development.

With both sides heavily teched, expanding and increasing production allows for a stronger midgame army. Maintaining a defensive posture mitigates risk of early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive stance or a specific composition based on scouting information.
