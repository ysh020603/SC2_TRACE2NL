# TvZ_O06 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O06
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that emphasizes heavy technology investment and economic expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The early game focuses on building a strong infrastructure and tech base, with production ramping up through the midgame. The opponent is typically a Zerg player who is also expanding heavily, with a ground-focused army composition.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Tech and Economy Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, you are establishing your economy and technology infrastructure. You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production. Your army is ground-oriented, with Marines as a representative unit. The opponent is also expanding heavily, with a ground posture and light or uncertain air presence.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a strong ground defense. Increase your production and expansion to support your tech-heavy build.

**Read for details:** `N001`
