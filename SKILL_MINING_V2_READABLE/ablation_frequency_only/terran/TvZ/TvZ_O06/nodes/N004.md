# N004 — Midgame Tech and Army Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You are in the midgame with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production is heavy, and your army includes Siege Tanks and Marines. The opponent's technology investment is now heavy, and they have a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your technology. Maintain your economy and production, and consider expanding further if safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. They have a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their technology investment is heavy. Their production is heavy, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks and Marines, and your defense is heavy. You are expanding and increasing your economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your technology. Maintain your economy and production, and consider expanding further if safe.

With both players having heavy tech, the game is likely to be decided by army composition and positioning. Your Siege Tanks provide a strong defensive backbone, and continued tech will give you an edge.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late-midgame with a strong army and advanced technology.
