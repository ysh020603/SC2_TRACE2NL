# N002 — Early-Midgame Production Ramp

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production has increased to heavy, and your army is still ground-oriented with Marines. The opponent remains on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, but their technology investment is still light or uncertain. Continue increasing your production and economy while maintaining your tech development. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. They have a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their technology investment is light or uncertain. Their production is moderate, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Marines, and your defense is heavy. You are expanding and increasing your economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing your production and economy while maintaining your tech development. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential engagements.

With a heavy economy and tech, you can afford to ramp up production. This will give you a stronger army to defend against pressure and set up for midgame transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a solid army composition and continued tech progression.
