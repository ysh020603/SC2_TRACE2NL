# N009 — Early Game Tech and Economy Foundation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, you are establishing your economy and technology infrastructure. You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production. Your army is ground-oriented with Reapers. The opponent is also expanding heavily, with a ground posture and light or uncertain air presence. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a strong ground defense. Increase your production and expansion to support your tech-heavy build.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion. They have a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their technology investment is light or uncertain. Their production is moderate, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production is moderate, and your army is ground-oriented with Reapers. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a strong ground defense. Increase your production and expansion to support your tech-heavy build.

The early game is about building a solid foundation. Your heavy tech investment will pay off later, but you need to ensure you have enough economy and production to support it. Maintaining a strong defense is crucial to avoid early game pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a stronger production capacity and continued tech development.
