# N003 — Midgame Tech and Army Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production is heavy, and your army now includes Siege Tanks and Marines. The opponent's technology investment has increased to moderate, and they still have a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your technology. Maintain your economy and production, and consider expanding further if safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. They have a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their technology investment is moderate. Their production is moderate, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks and Marines, and your defense is heavy. You are expanding and increasing your economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your technology. Maintain your economy and production, and consider expanding further if safe.

With Siege Tanks, you have a strong defensive and offensive capability. Increasing tech will give you access to better units and upgrades, which is crucial for the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a strong ground army and advanced technology.
