# N010 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground army with Marines and Reapers; opponent has Zerglings and Queens, moderate tech. Stabilize your position, increase production and economy, continue tech. Strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy and production, technology heavy. Army has Marines and Reapers. Defense light, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position, increase production and economy, continue tech. Strengthen ground army.

Stabilizing ensures you can defend against early pressure while building a strong economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Reach a stable midgame with a solid economy and army.
