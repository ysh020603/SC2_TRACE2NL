# N003 — Expansion and Production Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, own ground posture with Reapers, heavy production and tech; opponent ground-oriented with moderate production. Increase expansion and production, continue technology. Strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent appears ground-oriented with moderate production and light tech. No combat units seen yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy and production, technology heavy. Army has Reapers. Expansion is light or uncertain, so you plan to increase it.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and production, continue technology. Strengthen ground army.

Expanding early secures a long-term economy advantage. Reapers provide map control and scouting.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
