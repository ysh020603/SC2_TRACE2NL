# N001 — Early Ground Macro Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, own ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology, opponent appears ground-oriented with moderate production. Continue developing your ground army and economy, maintaining current expansion and defense. Increase economy and continue technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. No reliable combat-unit cues observed yet. Expansion is heavy, but pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with technology investment. Your army is ground-focused with Marines and Reapers. Defense is light or uncertain, and expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, maintaining current expansion and defense. Increase economy and continue technology.

Establish a strong macro foundation while keeping options open. Heavy production and technology support a flexible transition to either more aggression or further defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a solid economy and production, ready to adapt to opponent's tech choices.
