- Opponent shows ground posture with Queens, possibly Zerglings. Production moderate, technology light or uncertain. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, continue economy and technology. Maintain defense and expansion.

Siege Tanks provide defensive stability and map control. Continuing tech and economy prepares for midgame transitions.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
