# N004 — Balanced Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, own ground posture with Marines, moderate economy, heavy production and tech; opponent ground-oriented. Maintain expansion and production, increase economy, continue tech. Strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground-oriented with moderate production and light tech. No combat units seen.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Economy is moderate, but production and tech are heavy. Army has Marines. Expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain expansion and production, increase economy, continue tech. Strengthen ground army.

Balanced development keeps options open. Heavy production and tech allow for quick adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a solid economy and production.
