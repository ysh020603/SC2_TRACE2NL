# N008 — Early Game Aggressive Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground posture with moderate production and heavy technology. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with moderate production. Increase production and expansion, strengthen ground forces, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is consistent with a ground posture, likely Zergling and Queen. Production moderate, technology light or uncertain. Economy and expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. Army includes Marines and Reapers. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion, strengthen ground forces, and continue technology development.

Aggressively expand to secure an economic lead while maintaining a strong ground army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and technology posture as the game progresses.
