# N005 — Midgame Tech and Army Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 480 seconds, you have a heavy ground posture with Siege Tanks and Hellions. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with heavy production and technology. Increase technology, maintain production, strengthen ground forces, and continue economy development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is consistent with a ground posture, likely Zergling, Hydralisk, and Queen. Production heavy, technology heavy. Economy and expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy, production, and technology. Army includes Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology, maintain production, strengthen ground forces, and continue economy development.

Maintain technological edge and adapt to enemy's heavy ground composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more versatile composition with Medivacs and possibly Banshees.
