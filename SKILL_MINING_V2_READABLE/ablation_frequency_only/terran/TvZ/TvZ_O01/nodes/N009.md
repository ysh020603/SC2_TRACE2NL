# N009 — Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 420 seconds, you have a heavy ground posture with moderate production and technology. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with heavy production. Stabilize your position, increase production, maintain defense, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is consistent with a ground posture, likely Zergling and Queen. Production heavy, technology moderate. Economy and expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy, production, and technology. Army includes Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position, increase production, maintain defense, and continue technology development.

Ensure a solid defensive position before expanding further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once stabilized.
