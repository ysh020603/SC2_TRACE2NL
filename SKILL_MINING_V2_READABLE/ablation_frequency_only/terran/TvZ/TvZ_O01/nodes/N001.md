# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a heavy ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology. Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with moderate production and light technology. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansion, maintain defense and air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is consistent with a ground posture, likely Zergling and Queen. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain. Economy and expansion are heavy. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Marines and Reapers. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansion, maintain defense and air presence.

Maintain economic and technological lead while building a strong ground army to prepare for potential engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with Siege Tanks and Marauders as the game progresses.
