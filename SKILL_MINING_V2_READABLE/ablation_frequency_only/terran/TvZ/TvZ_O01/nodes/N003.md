# N003 — Midgame Tech and Ground Strength

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy ground posture with heavy production and technology. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with moderate production. Increase technology, maintain production, strengthen ground forces, and continue economy development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is consistent with a ground posture, likely Zergling and Queen. Production moderate, technology light or uncertain. Economy and expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy, production, and technology. Army includes Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology, maintain production, strengthen ground forces, and continue economy development.

Leverage technological advantage to gain an edge in midgame engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.
