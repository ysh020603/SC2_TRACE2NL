# N001 — Early-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-360 seconds, both own and opponent postures are largely unknown, with light or uncertain production and technology. The opponent may have a ground macro posture with Zerglings, but this is not confirmed. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Avoid over-committing to any single strategy until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology. You have not committed to a specific army composition or expansion plan.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Avoid over-committing to any single strategy until more information is available.

With both sides uncertain, it is prudent to keep options open and avoid risky investments. Maintaining a balanced economy and production allows adaptation to whatever the opponent reveals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and technology while scouting to identify the opponent's actual strategy.
