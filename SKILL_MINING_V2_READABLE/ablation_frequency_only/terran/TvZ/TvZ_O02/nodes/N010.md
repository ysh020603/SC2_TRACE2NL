# N010 — Early-Midgame Ground Strengthening with Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-360 seconds, the opponent shows a ground macro posture with Zerglings, while your own posture is unknown but with moderate production, heavy technology investment, and a heavy expansion. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy. Maintain your expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have expanded heavily and are investing in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy. Maintain your expansion.

Your heavy expansion gives you an economic advantage, but you need to build a ground army to defend against potential Zergling pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and maintain your economic lead to transition into the midgame.
