# N008 — Early Game Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent shows a ground macro posture with Zerglings, while your own posture is unknown but with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Strengthen your ground army and increase production to prepare for potential aggression. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have not yet built a significant army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase production to prepare for potential aggression. Continue technology development.

The opponent's Zergling presence suggests a possible early pressure, so building a solid ground defense is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and maintain a solid economy to transition into the midgame.
