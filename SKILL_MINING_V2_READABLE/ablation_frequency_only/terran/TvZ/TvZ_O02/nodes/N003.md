# N003 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both own and opponent postures are largely unknown, with light or uncertain production and technology. This is the initial development phase. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on establishing a solid economy and production foundation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition or expansion plan.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on establishing a solid economy and production foundation.

Early game uncertainty calls for a balanced approach that can adapt to any opponent strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop economy and technology while scouting to identify the opponent's early intentions.
