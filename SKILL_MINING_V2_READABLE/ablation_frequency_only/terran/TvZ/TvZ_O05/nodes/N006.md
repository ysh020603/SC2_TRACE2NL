## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground-oriented with heavy production, opponent ground-macro with Zerglings. Stabilize defenses, increase production and economy, maintain expansions.

## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.

- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize defenses, increase production and economy, maintain expansions.

Zergling pressure is possible; stabilizing with defensive units and economy ensures safety while building toward a stronger midgame.
