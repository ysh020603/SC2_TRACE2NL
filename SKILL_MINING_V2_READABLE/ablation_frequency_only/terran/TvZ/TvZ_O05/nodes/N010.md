# N010 — Early Tech Investment

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, own unknown-oriented with moderate production, opponent ground-macro. Maintain ground army strength, increase economy, continue production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength, increase economy, continue production and tech.

Investing in tech early provides long-term benefits; maintaining a moderate army ensures safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a ground-oriented army with heavy production and tech by early-midgame.
