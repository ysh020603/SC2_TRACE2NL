FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240s) with own ground posture, heavy tech, and moderate defense. Opponent ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Stabilize defense, increase production, and continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings and Queens, indicating a ground army with moderate production and light tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, heavy tech, and heavy production. Economy is heavy, but defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize defense, increase production, and continue tech.

With moderate defense, you need to ensure safety before expanding further. Increasing production and tech prepares for mid-game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state with stronger defense and production, maintaining tech.
