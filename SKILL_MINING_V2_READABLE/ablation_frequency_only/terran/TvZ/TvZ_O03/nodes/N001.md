# N001 — Early Tech Focus with Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own posture unknown-oriented, heavy tech investment, and light expansion. Opponent appears ground-oriented with heavy economy and light tech. Increase economy and expansion while continuing technology development. Strengthen ground forces for safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy economy and expansion, but light tech and defense. No reliable combat units observed yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unclear, but you have heavy technology investment and moderate production. Economy is heavy, but expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion while continuing technology development. Strengthen ground forces for safety.

With heavy tech investment, you need a solid economy to support it. Expanding now leverages your tech advantage while maintaining defensive capability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state with stronger economy and expansion, maintaining tech lead.
