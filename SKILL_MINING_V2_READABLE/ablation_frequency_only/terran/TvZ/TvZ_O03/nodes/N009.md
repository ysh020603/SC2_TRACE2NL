FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with own ground posture featuring Siege Tanks and Marines. Opponent has Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Increase defense, continue tech, and expand economy.

- Opponent has Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, indicating a mix of ground and air with heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. Heavy tech and production, heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, continue tech, and expand economy.

With Mutalisks present, you need to bolster defense. Continuing tech and economy prepares for a longer game.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
