# N002 — Early Tech with Marine Presence

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own posture unknown but Marines present, heavy tech, and heavy expansion. Opponent ground-oriented with heavy economy. Continue economy and tech development, maintain expansion, and strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent appears ground-oriented with heavy economy and expansion, but light tech and defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Marines as representative units, heavy tech investment, and a solid economy with heavy expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue economy and tech development, maintain expansion, and strengthen ground forces.

With Marines and heavy tech, you can safely expand and tech up. Maintaining expansion supports long-term economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong economy and tech while building a ground army.
