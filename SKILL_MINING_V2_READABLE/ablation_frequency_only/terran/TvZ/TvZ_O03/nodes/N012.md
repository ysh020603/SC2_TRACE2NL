FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with own ground posture featuring Ghosts, Hellions, and Widow Mines. Opponent has Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Increase production, continue tech, and maintain expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, indicating a mix of ground and air with heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Ghosts, Hellions, and Widow Mines. Heavy tech and production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production, continue tech, and maintain expansion.

With Ghosts and Widow Mines, you have strong anti-ground and anti-air potential. Increasing production and tech solidifies your position.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
