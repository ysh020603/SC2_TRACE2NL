# N003 — Mid-Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240s) with own ground posture, heavy tech, and heavy expansion. Opponent ground-oriented with heavy economy. Increase economy and expansion, continue tech, and strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent remains ground-oriented with heavy economy and expansion, but light tech and defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Marines, heavy tech, and heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, continue tech, and strengthen ground forces.

With a solid ground base and heavy tech, you can expand further and tech up to gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state with even stronger economy and tech, maintaining ground army.
