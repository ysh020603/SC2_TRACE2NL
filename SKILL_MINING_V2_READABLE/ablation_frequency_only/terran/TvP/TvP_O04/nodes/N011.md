# N011 — Early Game Defensive Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a Reaper and are focusing on economy and technology, while the opponent is showing a defensive posture with heavy defense. Continue to develop your economy and technology, and consider scouting to gain information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is unknown in army composition but shows heavy defense and economy. No reliable combat-unit cues yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper and are developing your economy and technology. Your production is moderate, and your army is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology, and consider scouting to gain information.

Early game is about establishing a strong foundation. You want to maximize your economy and tech while staying safe.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition once you have more information.
