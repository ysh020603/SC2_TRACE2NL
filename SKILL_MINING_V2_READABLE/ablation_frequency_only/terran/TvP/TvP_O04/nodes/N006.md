# N006 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, and the opponent is showing an air-oriented composition with Stalkers and Oracles. Increase your air presence to counter the opponent's air threats, while continuing to strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is air-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production, and heavy technology. They may be planning an air harass or transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your air presence is light or uncertain, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence to counter the opponent's air threats, while continuing to strengthen your ground army.

The opponent's air composition, especially Oracles, can be dangerous. You need anti-air capabilities and detection.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air capabilities.
