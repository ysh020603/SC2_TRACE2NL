# N002 — Midgame Ground Push Preparation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a strong ground army with Marines and Reapers, and the opponent is showing a ground-oriented composition with Stalkers. Strengthen your ground army and consider increasing your air presence to counter potential tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is ground-oriented with Stalkers, heavy production, and heavy technology. They may be preparing for a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your defense is moderate, and your economy is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and consider increasing your air presence to counter potential tech switches.

The opponent's ground focus suggests a potential ground engagement, so you want to be strong on the ground while also preparing for air threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army composition with both ground and air capabilities.
