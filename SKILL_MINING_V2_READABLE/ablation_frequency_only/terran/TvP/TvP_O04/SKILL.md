# TvP_O04 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvP_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Protoss
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on establishing a strong economy and technology base while maintaining flexibility. It is not a fixed build order but a strategic template that emphasizes heavy economy, moderate production, and heavy technology investment.

Develop a expansion / economy / technology posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology, while the opponent is still unknown but shows signs of heavy economy and technology.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining a defensive posture and increasing your expansion.
