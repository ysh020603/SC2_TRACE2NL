# N009 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides have heavy technology investment, but your production is heavy while economy is moderate. Opponent's posture is unknown. Focus on technology development while increasing economy and maintaining production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, but production is heavy and technology is heavy. Army composition is unclear.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on technology development while increasing economy and maintaining production.

Heavy technology investment suggests a tech-focused strategy. Increasing economy will support future production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a tech lead and a solid economy for midgame.
