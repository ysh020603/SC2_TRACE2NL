# N007 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent's posture is unknown, but you have a ground army. Your economy is heavy, but expansion is light or uncertain. Increase production and defense to stabilize, while continuing economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground army is developing (Marines), with moderate production and heavy technology. Defense is light, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and defense to stabilize, while continuing economy and technology development.

With an uncertain opponent, stabilizing with more production and defense reduces risk of early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid foundation for midgame transitions.
