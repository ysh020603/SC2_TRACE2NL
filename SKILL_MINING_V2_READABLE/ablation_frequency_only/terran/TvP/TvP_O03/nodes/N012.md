# N012 — Early Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, the opponent has a defensive posture with unknown army composition, while you have a ground army. Your production is heavy. Stabilize your position by increasing production and defense, while continuing economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground army is developing (Marines), with heavy production and technology. Defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing production and defense, while continuing economy and technology development.

The opponent's defensive posture suggests they may be preparing a tech or timing attack. Stabilizing with more production and defense reduces vulnerability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid defense and production capacity to withstand potential pressure.
