# N003 — Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, both sides have heavy ground armies (Marines vs Stalkers) and heavy economies. Defense is moderate on your side. Maintain ground army strength and continue technology development. Keep expanding but avoid overextending.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground army is strong (Marines), with heavy production and technology. Defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength and continue technology development. Keep expanding but avoid overextending.

In a ground vs ground matchup, maintaining a strong core army and tech upgrades is crucial. Expanding provides resources for sustained production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame army with upgrades (e.g., stim, combat shields) and a solid economy.
