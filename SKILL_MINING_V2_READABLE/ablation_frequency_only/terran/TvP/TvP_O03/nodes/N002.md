# N002 — Early Game Tech/Economy Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides are in early game with heavy economies and technology, but production is moderate. Opponent's posture is unknown, with Zealot cues possible. Increase economy and production while continuing technology development. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, but production is moderate and army composition is unclear. You are expanding and investing in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and production while continuing technology development. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech lead. Moderate production suggests focusing on tech and economy before massing army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build up production and army to a level that can respond to opponent's first major tech choice.
