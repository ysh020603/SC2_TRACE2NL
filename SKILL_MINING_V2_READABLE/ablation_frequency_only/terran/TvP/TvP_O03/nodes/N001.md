# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, both sides have heavy economies and production, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Zealot cues) and your own ground forces (Marines) developing. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain current technology and production levels.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are heavy, with a ground-oriented army (Marines) and heavy technology investment. Defense is light or uncertain, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain current technology and production levels.

With both sides investing heavily in economy and technology, maintaining a solid ground core while expanding provides a stable foundation for midgame transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger ground army composition with additional production and expansions, ready to respond to opponent's tech choices.
