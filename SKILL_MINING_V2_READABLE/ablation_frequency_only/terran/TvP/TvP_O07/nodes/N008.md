## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, own ground posture, opponent unknown with heavy defense, possible tech. Continue ground development, increase economy, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture, heavy defense, moderate production, heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansions heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue ground development, increase economy, maintain defense.

Opponent's heavy defense suggests a macro-oriented game, so focus on economy and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
