# N009 — Early Game Unknown Opponent with Moderate Economy

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, own ground posture, opponent unknown with moderate economy, possible tech. Continue tech development, increase economy, maintain ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture, moderate economy, moderate production, heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with moderate production, heavy tech, economy and expansions heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue tech development, increase economy, maintain ground forces.

Tech advantage can be leveraged later, but need to scout for opponent's direction.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong tech base while scouting opponent's intentions.
