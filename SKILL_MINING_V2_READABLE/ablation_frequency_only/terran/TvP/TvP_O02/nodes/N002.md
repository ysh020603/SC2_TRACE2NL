# N002 — Early-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

As the game transitions into the early-midgame, both sides still have limited information. The opponent's posture remains unknown, and your own posture is still developing. This node represents a continuation of the development phase. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Continue to focus on economy and technology, but be prepared to adapt if the opponent reveals their strategy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology investment. You are still in the process of building up your economy and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Continue to focus on economy and technology, but be prepared to adapt if the opponent reveals their strategy.

The early-midgame is a critical period where you need to balance development with the potential for an opponent push. Maintaining a flexible approach allows you to respond to threats without sacrificing your economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more concrete army composition as the opponent's strategy becomes clearer, likely toward a ground-oriented force.
