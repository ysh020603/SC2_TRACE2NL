## Node Type

FREQUENT

Your own posture is also ground-oriented with marines, so you need to strengthen your ground forces to counter the opponent. Strengthen your ground forces to counter the opponent's Zealot/Sentry composition. Continue to increase your economy and production to support a larger army.

- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces to counter the opponent's Zealot/Sentry composition. Continue to increase your economy and production to support a larger army.

The opponent's ground-heavy composition requires a strong ground response. Strengthening your ground forces allows you to engage effectively.
