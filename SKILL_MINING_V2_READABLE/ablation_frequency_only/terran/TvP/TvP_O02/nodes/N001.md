# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides show limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own posture is also uncertain. This node represents the initial phase where you are establishing your economy and technology. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Focus on economy and technology without overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology investment. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Focus on economy and technology without overcommitting to a specific army composition.

Early game information is limited, so it is prudent to develop a solid foundation before making aggressive moves. This approach preserves flexibility to adapt to the opponent's strategy as it becomes clearer.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once the opponent's strategy becomes clearer, either toward a ground-oriented army or a more tech-heavy approach.
