# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

By the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have had time to develop your economy and technology. This node represents a continued focus on development while maintaining safety. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Continue to build up your economy and technology, but be ready to transition to a more aggressive stance if the opponent shows weakness.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology investment. You have a solid foundation but have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Continue to build up your economy and technology, but be ready to transition to a more aggressive stance if the opponent shows weakness.

The midgame is a time to solidify your position and prepare for the late game. Maintaining a flexible approach allows you to adapt to the opponent's strategy while building a strong economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more concrete army composition as the opponent's strategy becomes clearer, likely toward a ground-oriented force with siege tanks and marines.
