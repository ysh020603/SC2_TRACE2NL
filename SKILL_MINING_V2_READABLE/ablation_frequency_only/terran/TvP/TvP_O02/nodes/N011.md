# N011 — Early Game Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

The opponent's posture is unknown, but your own posture is more defined with moderate economy and heavy expansion. This node represents a need to strengthen your ground forces while continuing to develop. Strengthen your ground forces while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Focus on increasing your production and technology to support a stronger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and moderate technology investment. You have a solid economy but need to strengthen your ground forces.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Focus on increasing your production and technology to support a stronger army.

With a solid economy, you can afford to invest in a stronger ground army. Strengthening your ground forces prepares you for potential engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition while maintaining your economy.
