# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Response

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

The opponent has revealed a ground-oriented posture with Zealots and Sentries, indicating a heavy investment in ground forces. Your own posture is still unknown, but you need to respond to the opponent's composition. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Consider strengthening your ground forces to counter the opponent's Zealot/Sentry composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Sentry. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology investment. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Consider strengthening your ground forces to counter the opponent's Zealot/Sentry composition.

The opponent's ground-heavy composition suggests a potential push. Strengthening your ground forces can help you defend against it while maintaining your economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a ground-oriented army composition with marines and possibly marauders to counter the opponent's Zealots and Sentries.
