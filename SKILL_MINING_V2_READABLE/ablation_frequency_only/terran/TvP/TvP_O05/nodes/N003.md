# N003 — Early Game Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with moderate production and heavy tech. Opponent's posture is unknown but economy is heavy. Stabilize your economy and production while continuing tech development. Maintain a defensive posture to scout opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about opponent's army composition, but economy and tech appear heavy. No reliable combat-unit cues yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders, moderate production, heavy tech, and are expanding. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your economy and production while continuing tech development. Maintain a defensive posture to scout opponent's intentions.

Early game uncertainty calls for a safe, macro-oriented approach. Building a solid economy and tech base allows flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army once opponent's intentions are clearer.
