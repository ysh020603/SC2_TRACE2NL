# N012 — Early-Mid Ground Macro vs Air Posture

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, you have a ground army with moderate defense, production, and tech. Opponent is air-oriented with Oracle. Maintain your ground army and defense while continuing to increase economy. Keep production and tech steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Zealot and Oracle. Production and tech appear heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders, moderate defense, heavy production, and tech. Economy and expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and defense while continuing to increase economy. Keep production and tech steady.

A balanced approach keeps you safe against potential pressure while allowing economic growth. Maintaining tech ensures you can adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid mid-game composition with Siege Tanks or Medivacs.
