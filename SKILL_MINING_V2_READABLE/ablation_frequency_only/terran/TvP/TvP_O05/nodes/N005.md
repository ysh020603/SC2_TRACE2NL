# N005 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 420-540 seconds, you have a ground army with heavy defense, production, and tech. Opponent is ground-oriented with Colossus/Zealot/Oracle. Increase your air presence and technology to counter potential Colossus or Oracle threats. Continue strengthening ground army and economy.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology to counter potential Colossus or Oracle threats. Continue strengthening ground army and economy.

Colossus and Oracle can be dangerous, so adding air units like Vikings or Medivacs provides counterplay. Maintaining economy supports tech transitions.
