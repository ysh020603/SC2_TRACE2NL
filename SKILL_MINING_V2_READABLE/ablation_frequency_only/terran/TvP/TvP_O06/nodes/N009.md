Strengthen your ground army and consider increasing your air presence. Continue your economy and technology development.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and consider increasing your air presence. Continue your economy and technology development.

With a strong ground composition and Medivac support, you can sustain long engagements. Increasing air presence can give you more flexibility against potential tech switches.
