# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Macro with Siege Tank and Reaper

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks and Reapers, heavy economy and production, and the opponent is likely on a ground macro posture with heavy economy and production. Strengthen your ground army while maintaining your economy and production. Continue technology development and consider increasing your economy further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy economy and production, but no specific combat units have been reliably observed. Their defense appears light or uncertain, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Reapers, heavy economy and production, and are in the early midgame. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you have potential for pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while maintaining your economy and production. Continue technology development and consider increasing your economy further.

With a strong ground core and heavy macro, you can leverage your production advantage to outlast the opponent. Maintaining economy and technology ensures you can transition to a stronger composition later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful ground army with additional support units, while keeping your economy and production heavy.
