# N002 — Early Game Ground Macro with Marine

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Marines, heavy economy and production, and the opponent's posture is unknown but with heavy economy and technology. Strengthen your ground army and continue your economy and production development. Maintain your technology progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's army composition, but they have heavy economy and technology. Their defense is light or uncertain, and their pressure potential is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, heavy economy and production, and are in the early game. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you have potential for pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue your economy and production development. Maintain your technology progression.

With a solid marine core and heavy macro, you can safely expand your economy and tech, preparing for a strong midgame. The opponent's unknown posture means you should keep scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more diverse ground army with additional units like Marauders or Siege Tanks, while maintaining your economy.
