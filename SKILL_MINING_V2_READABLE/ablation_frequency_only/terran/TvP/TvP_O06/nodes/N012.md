# N012 — Early Midgame Ground Macro with Marine and Widow Mine vs Oracle

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Widow Mines, heavy economy and production, and the opponent is on an air posture with an Oracle. Strengthen your ground army and consider increasing your air presence. Continue your economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with an Oracle. They have heavy economy and production, and their defense is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Widow Mines, heavy economy and production, and are in the early midgame. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you have potential for pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and consider increasing your air presence. Continue your economy and technology development.

With an Oracle, the opponent may harass your economy. Strengthening your ground army and adding anti-air can help defend against Oracle harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with additional air support, while maintaining your economy and production.
