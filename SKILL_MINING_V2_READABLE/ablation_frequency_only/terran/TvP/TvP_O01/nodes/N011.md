# N011 — Midgame vs Heavy Air

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 540 seconds, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Widow Mines. The opponent has a heavy air army with Stalkers and Void Rays. Increase anti-air capabilities by adding more Marines, Thors, or Vikings. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy air posture with Stalkers and Void Rays. Production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Siege Tanks, Marines, and Widow Mines, heavy production and technology. Defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase anti-air capabilities by adding more Marines, Thors, or Vikings. Continue expanding and teching.

The opponent's air army is a significant threat, so you need to prioritize anti-air to defend and eventually counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong anti-air composition to neutralize the opponent's air advantage.
