At around 240 seconds, you have a ground army with heavy production. The opponent is unknown but teching heavily, with a Zealot seen. Continue developing your technology and production while maintaining a light defense. Strengthen ground forces as scouting reveals more.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but a Zealot is seen. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper and are focusing on heavy production and technology. Defense is light, but you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and production while maintaining a light defense. Strengthen ground forces as scouting reveals more.

Maintaining a flexible tech path allows you to adapt to the opponent's composition once it becomes clear.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Transition into a defined ground or air composition based on scouting information.
