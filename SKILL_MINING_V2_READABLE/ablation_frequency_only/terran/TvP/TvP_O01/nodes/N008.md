# N008 — Midgame Defensive Ground

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 540 seconds, you have a heavy ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. The opponent is ground-based with Zealots and Stalkers. Maintain your defensive ground posture while increasing economy and technology. Continue producing Siege Tanks and Marines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and technology. Defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive ground posture while increasing economy and technology. Continue producing Siege Tanks and Marines.

Siege Tanks provide strong defense against ground pushes, allowing you to safely expand and tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid defensive line and prepare for a midgame push or transition.
