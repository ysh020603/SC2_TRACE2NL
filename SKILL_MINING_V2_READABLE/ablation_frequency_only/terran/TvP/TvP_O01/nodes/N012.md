# N012 — Early-Midgame vs Air Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. The opponent is transitioning to air with Stalkers and Void Rays. Maintain your ground army while starting to add anti-air units like Marines and Thors. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Stalkers and Void Rays. Production and technology appear heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and technology. Defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while starting to add anti-air units like Marines and Thors. Continue expanding and teching.

Void Rays are strong against ground units, so you need anti-air to defend and eventually counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army with sufficient anti-air to handle the opponent's air composition.
