FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have an unknown army posture but heavy technology and economy. The opponent is also unknown but likely teching heavily. Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a light defense. Strengthen ground forces as scouting reveals more.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but a Zealot is seen. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper and are focusing on technology and economy. Production is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a light defense. Strengthen ground forces as scouting reveals more.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech lead without overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a defined ground or air composition based on scouting information.
