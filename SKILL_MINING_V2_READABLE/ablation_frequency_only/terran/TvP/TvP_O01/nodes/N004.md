# N004 — Early-Midgame Heavy Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a ground army with heavy production and technology. The opponent is also ground-based with Zealots and Stalkers. Increase production and defense while continuing to expand. Strengthen ground forces to prepare for potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper and are focusing on heavy production and technology. Defense is light, but you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and defense while continuing to expand. Strengthen ground forces to prepare for potential pressure.

Heavy production allows you to outproduce the opponent and maintain army strength, while defense ensures safety against early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a larger ground army and establish a solid defensive position.
