## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, maintain economy and production, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is expected.

With both players on similar ground compositions, maintaining a strong economy while building a larger army can set up a timing advantage. Defense should be improved to avoid being caught off guard.
