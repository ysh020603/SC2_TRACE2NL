# N008 — Early-Midgame Tech Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At 300 seconds, both players have Zerglings and Queens, with heavy economy and expansion. Opponent has moderate defense and heavy tech, own has light defense and moderate tech. Production is moderate. Increase technology and economy, maintain production and defense, and prepare for potential tech-based transitions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Zergling and Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings and Queens, with a heavy economy and expansion. Defense is light or uncertain, production is moderate, tech is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology and economy, maintain production and defense, and prepare for potential tech-based transitions.

With opponent investing heavily in tech, you need to keep pace to avoid being out-teched. Increasing tech can open up options like roaches or mutalisks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a solid tech base and economy, ready to transition to advanced units.
