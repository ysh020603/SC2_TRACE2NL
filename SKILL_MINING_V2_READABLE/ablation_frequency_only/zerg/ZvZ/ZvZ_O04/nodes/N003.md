# N003 — Early Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has a moderate production and light or uncertain technology, with a ground posture. Your own posture is similar, but with a heavier defense. The opponent's defense is moderate, and pressure is possible. Maintain your ground army and defense, increase your economy, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, but their defense is moderate. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy and defense, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and defense, increase your economy, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

In the early midgame, it is important to balance economy and defense. The opponent's moderate production and light tech suggest they may be vulnerable to pressure, but your own defense should be solid to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a strong economy and a solid ground army, ready to adapt to the opponent's tech choices.
