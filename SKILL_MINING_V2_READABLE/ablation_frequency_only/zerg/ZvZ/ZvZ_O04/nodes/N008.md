# N008 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early midgame, both players have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. The opponent's defense is moderate, and pressure is possible. Your defense is heavy. Maintain your ground army and defense, increase your economy and production, and continue technology development. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and their defense is moderate. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy, expansion, and defense. Your production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and defense, increase your economy and production, and continue technology development. Stabilize then develop.

Both players are on similar footing, so it is important to maintain your economy and defense while increasing production. Your heavy defense gives you an edge in safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a strong economy and a solid ground army, ready to adapt to the opponent's tech choices.
