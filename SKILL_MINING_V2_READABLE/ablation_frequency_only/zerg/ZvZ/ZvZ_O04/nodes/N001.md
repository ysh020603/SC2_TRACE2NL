# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players are in the early game with a ground-oriented macro posture. The opponent is likely to have Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. Your own posture is similar, with a heavy economy and defense. Maintain your ground army and defense, increase your economy, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be in a ground macro posture, with representative cues of Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, technology is light or uncertain, and they have a heavy economy and expansion. Their defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and defense, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Your army is ground-oriented, and you have no confirmed air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and defense, increase your economy, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

In the early game, it is important to secure your economy and defend against potential early pressure. Maintaining a solid ground army and defense while expanding your economy sets a strong foundation for the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a strong economy and a solid ground army, ready to adapt to the opponent's tech choices.
