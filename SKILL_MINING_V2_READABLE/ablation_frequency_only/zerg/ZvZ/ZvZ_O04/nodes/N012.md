# N012 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the late midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground posture with Roaches and Overseers, and heavy technology. Your own posture has a heavy air presence with Mutalisks, and you have heavy production and technology. The opponent's defense is heavy, and pressure is possible. Increase your air presence and economy, continue production and technology development. Maintain your ground army and defense. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and their defense is heavy. They have Roaches and Overseers, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late midgame with a heavy economy, production, and defense. Your technology is heavy, and your army has a heavy air presence with Mutalisks, along with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and economy, continue production and technology development. Maintain your ground army and defense. Develop with safety checks.

Your air presence is a key advantage, but you need to continue developing your economy and tech to support it. The opponent's heavy ground army requires you to maintain a solid defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late game with a powerful air-ground composition, ready to adapt to the opponent's final composition.
