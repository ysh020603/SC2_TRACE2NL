# N007 — Early Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has a moderate production and light or uncertain technology, with a ground posture including Roaches. Your own posture is similar, but with a heavier defense. The opponent's defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible. Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, but their defense is light or uncertain. They may have Roaches in addition to Zerglings and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy and defense, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

The opponent's light defense may present an opportunity, but your priority is to stabilize and develop. Your heavy defense protects you while you catch up in economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a stronger economy and a solid defense, ready to compete with the opponent's macro.
