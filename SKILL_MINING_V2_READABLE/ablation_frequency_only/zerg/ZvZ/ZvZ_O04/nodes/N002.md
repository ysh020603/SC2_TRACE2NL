# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, both players have a heavy economy and production, with moderate technology. The opponent still shows a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and possibly Roaches. Your own posture is similar, with a heavy ground army. Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and defense, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with heavy production and moderate technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and their defense is heavy. Pressure is possible, and they may have Roaches in addition to Zerglings and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy, production, and defense. Your technology is moderate, and your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and defense, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

In the midgame, maintaining a strong economy and ground army is crucial to withstand potential attacks and to transition into a stronger composition. Keeping production and technology steady allows for flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late midgame with a robust ground army and a strong economy, ready to adapt to the opponent's final composition.
