# N004 — Early Game Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early game, your economy is moderate and your expansion is moderate, while the opponent has a heavy economy and expansion. The opponent's defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible. Your defense is heavy. Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. Their defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible. They are in a ground macro posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy and expansion, but a heavy defense. Your production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

Your economy is behind the opponent's, so it is important to catch up while maintaining a strong defense. The opponent's light defense may present an opportunity, but your priority is to stabilize and develop.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early midgame with a stronger economy and a solid defense, ready to compete with the opponent's macro.
