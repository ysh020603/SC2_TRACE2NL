# N009 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy production and heavy technology, with a ground posture. Your own posture is similar, but with moderate production and moderate technology. The opponent's defense is moderate, and pressure is possible. Increase your economy, expansion, production, and technology. Maintain your ground army and defense. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and their defense is moderate. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy and defense, but moderate production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy, expansion, production, and technology. Maintain your ground army and defense. Develop with safety checks.

The opponent has a stronger production and tech, so it is important to catch up while maintaining a solid defense. Your heavy economy allows you to invest in production and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late midgame with a stronger production and tech, ready to compete with the opponent's macro.
