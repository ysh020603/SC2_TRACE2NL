# N005 — Late Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both players have a heavy economy, production, and defense. The opponent has moderate technology and a ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your own technology is heavy, and your army is ground-oriented. Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and defense, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with heavy production and moderate technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and their defense is heavy. They may have Roaches in addition to Zerglings and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late midgame with a heavy economy, production, and defense. Your technology is heavy, and your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and defense, and keep production and technology steady. Develop with safety checks.

In the late midgame, maintaining a strong ground army and economy is essential to prepare for the late game. Your heavy technology gives you an advantage, but you should continue to build a robust army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late game with a powerful ground army and a strong economy, ready to adapt to the opponent's final composition.
