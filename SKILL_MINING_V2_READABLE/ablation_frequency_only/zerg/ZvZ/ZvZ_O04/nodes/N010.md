# N010 — Late Midgame Ground Defense with Air Presence

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the late midgame, the opponent has a heavy air presence, with Mutalisks, and a ground posture. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Roaches, and you have heavy production and technology. The opponent's defense is heavy, and pressure is possible. Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy air presence, with Mutalisks, and a ground posture. They have heavy production and moderate technology, with a heavy economy and expansion. Their defense is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late midgame with a heavy economy, production, and defense. Your technology is heavy, and your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. You have no confirmed air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

The opponent's air presence requires you to strengthen your defense and possibly transition to anti-air. Your heavy technology allows you to invest in anti-air units or tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late game with a balanced army that can handle both ground and air threats.
