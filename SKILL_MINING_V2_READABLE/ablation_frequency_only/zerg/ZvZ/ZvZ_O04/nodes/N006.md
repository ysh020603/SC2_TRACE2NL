# N006 — Midgame Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy production and heavy technology, with a ground posture including Roaches. Your own posture is similar, but with a heavier defense. The opponent's defense is moderate, and pressure is possible. Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in a ground macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and their defense is moderate. They may have Roaches in addition to Zerglings and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy, production, and defense. Your technology is heavy, and your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology development. Stabilize then develop.

The opponent has a strong economy and tech, so it is important to stabilize your defense and continue developing. Your heavy technology allows you to match their tech, but you need to ensure your army is strong enough.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late midgame with a strong economy and a solid ground army, ready to compete with the opponent's macro.
