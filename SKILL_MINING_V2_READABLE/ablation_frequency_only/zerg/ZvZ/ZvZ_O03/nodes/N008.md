# N008 — Early Game Defensive Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with a defensive posture and moderate expansion. Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a Queen, moderate production, and light technology, with heavy defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, moderate expansion, and moderate technology. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology.

A defensive posture helps secure your expansion while you develop.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive ground army once economy is stable.
