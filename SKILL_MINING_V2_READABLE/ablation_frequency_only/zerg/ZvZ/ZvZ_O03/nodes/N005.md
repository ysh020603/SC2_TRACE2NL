# N005 — Late-Midgame Heavy Ground

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, both players have heavy production and technology, ground-focused. Increase economy and continue technology, while maintaining ground army strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, and heavy technology. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and continue technology, while maintaining ground army strength.

With heavy economy, you can afford tech switches or upgrades to gain an edge.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition or tech switch if needed.
