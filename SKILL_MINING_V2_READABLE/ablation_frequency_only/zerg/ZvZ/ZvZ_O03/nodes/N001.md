# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with both players on ground posture, heavy economy, and moderate production. Strengthen ground army while increasing economy and continuing technology. Maintain current expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a similar ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. No special threats detected.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, and moderate technology. Defense is light, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while increasing economy and continuing technology. Maintain current expansion and production.

In ZvZ, early ground army is crucial for defense and potential pressure. Heavy economy supports future tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more developed ground army with Roaches if needed, while keeping economy strong.
