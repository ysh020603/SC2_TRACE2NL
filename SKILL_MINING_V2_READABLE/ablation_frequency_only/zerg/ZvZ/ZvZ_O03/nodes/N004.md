# N004 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 4-5 minutes, with a slightly different early game path, still ground-focused. Stabilize your position by increasing economy and production, while continuing technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a similar ground posture, but technology is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, and moderate technology. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing economy and production, while continuing technology.

Ensuring a stable economy and production allows you to respond to opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more developed ground army with Roaches.
