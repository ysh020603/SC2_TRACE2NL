# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 7-8 minutes, both players have moderate to heavy production and technology, still ground. Continue strengthening ground army, maintain economy and production, and keep technology steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and moderate technology. No air presence.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a mix of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy economy, and moderate to heavy technology. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army, maintain economy and production, and keep technology steady.

A strong ground army is essential for map control and defending expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to heavier ground units like Roaches or prepare for tech switch if needed.
