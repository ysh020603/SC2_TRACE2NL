# N007 — Late-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, with a heavy ground army and heavy economy. Increase economy and production, continue technology, and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a similar heavy ground posture with Roaches and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy and technology. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and production, continue technology, and maintain defense.

Stabilizing your economy and production allows for tech switches or upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition or tech switch.
