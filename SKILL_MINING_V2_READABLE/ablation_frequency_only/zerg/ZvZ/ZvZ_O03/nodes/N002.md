# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 5-6 minutes, both players have moderate production and technology, still ground-focused. Continue strengthening ground army, maintain economy and production, and keep technology steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is similar, with moderate production and technology, ground posture, and no air presence.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, and moderate technology. Defense is light to moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army, maintain economy and production, and keep technology steady.

Maintaining a balanced ground force allows flexibility to respond to tech switches or pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for midgame by adding Roaches or other ground units as needed.
