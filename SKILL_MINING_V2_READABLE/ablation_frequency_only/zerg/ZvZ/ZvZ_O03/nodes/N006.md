# N006 — Midgame Roach Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 9 minutes, with a heavier ground army and Roaches appearing. Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Roaches and Queens, heavy production, moderate technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy economy and technology. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology.

Roach-heavy compositions require strong defense and economy to sustain.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced ground army or prepare for air defense.
