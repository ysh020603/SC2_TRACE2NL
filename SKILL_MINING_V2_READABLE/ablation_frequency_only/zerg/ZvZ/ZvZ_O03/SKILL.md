# ZvZ_O03 Economy / Technology / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O03
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented posture. The early game focuses on heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and technology. As the game progresses, production and technology become heavier, and the army remains ground-focused with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while expanding, keeping ground army strength as a safety net. Adapt to opponent's tech choices, especially potential air transitions.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on live scouting and opponent's actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
