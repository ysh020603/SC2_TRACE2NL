# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, both players are on ground macro posture with heavy economy and expansion. Opponent shows light or uncertain air presence, possible pressure. Maintain ground army strength, increase economy, and keep production and technology steady. Focus on safe development with periodic checks for aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. Representative cues may include Queen. Pressure is possible, so keep some defensive capability.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a ground-oriented macro posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Economy and expansion are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, so be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength, increase economy, and keep production and technology steady. Focus on safe development with periodic checks for aggression.

This is a standard early macro opening. Prioritizing economy and expansion while maintaining a ground army allows you to stay flexible and respond to opponent's tech or pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into midgame with a solid economy and a ground army capable of defending or attacking.
