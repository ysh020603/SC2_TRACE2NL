# N002 — Midgame Ground Reinforcement

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economy and production, with ground armies. Opponent shows moderate defense and possible pressure. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and continue technology upgrades. Maintain production and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zergling and Queen, heavy production and technology. Defense is moderate, pressure possible. Exact counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. Production and technology are heavy. Economy is strong, but defense is moderate, so be ready to reinforce.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and continue technology upgrades. Maintain production and defense.

With both players on heavy ground, maintaining a strong army and economy is key. Upgrades and tech can provide an edge.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a decisive ground advantage or transition to a tech that counters opponent's composition.
