# N004 — Late Midgame Ground and Tech Push

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame, both players have heavy ground armies with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. Economy and production are heavy, tech heavy. Increase economy and continue tech upgrades. Maintain ground army strength and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a similar heavy ground army with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. Heavy production and tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. Production and tech are heavy. Defense is heavy, so you are well protected.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and continue tech upgrades. Maintain ground army strength and defense.

With both players at high tech and production, upgrades and economy are key to gaining an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a decisive advantage in upgrades or economy to transition into a winning composition.
