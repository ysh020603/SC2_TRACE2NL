# N012 — Early Game Production Increase

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, opponent has a ground posture with Zergling and Queen. You have a ground macro posture with moderate production and light or uncertain tech. Increase production and economy, continue tech. Focus on building a stronger ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a ground macro posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Economy is heavy, but defense is light, so be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and economy, continue tech. Focus on building a stronger ground army.

With a heavy economy, investing in production can lead to a strong early midgame. Maintaining a ground army ensures safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach early midgame with a larger ground army and solid economy.
