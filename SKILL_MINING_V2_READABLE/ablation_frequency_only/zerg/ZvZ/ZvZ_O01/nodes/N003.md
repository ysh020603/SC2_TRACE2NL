# N003 — Early Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame, both players have ground armies with Zergling and Queen. Economy is heavy, production moderate, tech light or uncertain. Continue developing ground forces, maintain economy and production, and keep tech steady. Focus on safe expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a similar ground posture with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Economy is heavy, but defense is light, so be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing ground forces, maintain economy and production, and keep tech steady. Focus on safe expansion.

This is a balanced early midgame state. Maintaining parity while looking for opportunities to gain an edge is important.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a solid economy and a ground army ready to tech or expand.
