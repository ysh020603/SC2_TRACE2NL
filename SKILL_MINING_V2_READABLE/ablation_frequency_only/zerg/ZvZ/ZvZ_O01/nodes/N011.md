# N011 — Early Midgame Defensive Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Zergling and Queen. You have a ground army with Zergling and Queen, but your tech is heavy. Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech. Focus on stabilizing while teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and heavy tech. Defense is moderate, so you need to increase defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech. Focus on stabilizing while teching.

With heavy tech, you may be investing in upgrades or tech structures. Ensuring defense is solid allows you to tech safely.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a solid defense and advanced tech.
