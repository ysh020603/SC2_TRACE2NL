# N005 — Early Game Defensive Stabilization

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, opponent shows ground posture with Queen, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Your defense is light, so you need to stabilize. Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech. Focus on stabilizing before expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Queen, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a ground macro posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Economy is heavy, but defense is light, so you need to increase defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech. Focus on stabilizing before expanding further.

With light defense, it's important to stabilize to avoid being caught off guard. Building a defensive buffer allows safe development.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early midgame with a solid defense and economy.
