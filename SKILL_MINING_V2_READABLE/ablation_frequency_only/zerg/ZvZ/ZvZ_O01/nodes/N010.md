# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. You have a heavy ground army but also air presence with Mutalisk. Increase air strength and economy, continue tech. Maintain ground defense and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. Heavy production and tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zergling, Mutalisk, and Queen. Air presence is heavy, production is heavy, tech is moderate. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air strength and economy, continue tech. Maintain ground defense and production.

Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage if opponent is ground-focused. Maintaining a strong economy supports this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air superiority while maintaining a solid ground defense.
