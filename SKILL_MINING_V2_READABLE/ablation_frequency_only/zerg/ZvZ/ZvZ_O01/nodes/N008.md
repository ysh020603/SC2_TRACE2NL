# N008 — Late Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, Roach, and Queen. You have a ground army with Zergling, Roach, and Queen, but no air. Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech. Consider adding anti-air or air units to counter opponent's Mutalisk.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, Roach, and Queen. Heavy production and tech. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zergling, Roach, and Queen. Production and tech are heavy. Defense is heavy, but you lack air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech. Consider adding anti-air or air units to counter opponent's Mutalisk.

With opponent having Mutalisk, you need to prepare anti-air or transition to air yourself. Strengthening defense while teching is crucial.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a counter to opponent's air or transition to air yourself.
