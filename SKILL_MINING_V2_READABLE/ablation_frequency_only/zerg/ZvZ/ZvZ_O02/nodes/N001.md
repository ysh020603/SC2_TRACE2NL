# N001 — Early Game Development with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own posture is also uncertain. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on economy and expansion while keeping options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on economy and expansion while keeping options open.

With limited information, it is prudent to develop a solid foundation without committing to a specific aggressive or tech-heavy strategy. This allows adaptation based on future observations.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and infrastructure while scouting for opponent intentions.
