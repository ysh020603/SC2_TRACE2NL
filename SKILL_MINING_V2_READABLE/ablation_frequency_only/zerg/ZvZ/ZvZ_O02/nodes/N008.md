# N008 — Early Game Ground Mirror with Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early in the game, you are adopting a ground macro posture with a moderate economy, while the opponent has a heavy economy and expansion. Strengthen ground army and increase economy. Maintain current defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and increase economy. Maintain current defense and expansion.

The opponent is focusing on economy, so you can either match their economy or prepare a timing attack. Strengthening your ground army gives you options.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Balance economy and army to be ready for any opponent move.
