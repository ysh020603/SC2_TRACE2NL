# N002 — Early-Midgame Development with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, the opponent's posture remains unknown, and your own posture is still developing. Continue maintaining current development path with safety checks. Keep expanding and building a versatile army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining current development path with safety checks. Keep expanding and building a versatile army.

Maintaining a balanced approach allows you to respond to a variety of opponent strategies without being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for midgame by solidifying your economy and army structure.
