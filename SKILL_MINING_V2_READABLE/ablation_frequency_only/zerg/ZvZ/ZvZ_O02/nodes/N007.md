# N007 — Early Game Ground Mirror with Defense Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early in the game, both you and the opponent are adopting ground macro postures. You have Zerglings, and the opponent has Queens. Strengthen ground army and increase defense. Continue expanding and developing economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and increase defense. Continue expanding and developing economy.

In a mirror matchup, it is important to have a strong ground army to defend against potential aggression and to be ready to transition into a more aggressive stance if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army and defense while maintaining a strong economy.
