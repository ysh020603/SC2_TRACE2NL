# N009 — Early-Midgame Ground Mirror with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, both you and the opponent have heavy economies and ground armies. You have Zerglings and Queens, while the opponent has Zerglings. Strengthen ground army and continue developing economy. Maintain current defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and continue developing economy. Maintain current defense and expansion.

With both players having strong economies, it is important to have a strong army to defend and potentially take map control. Continuing to develop your economy will allow you to outlast the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to gain an advantage.
