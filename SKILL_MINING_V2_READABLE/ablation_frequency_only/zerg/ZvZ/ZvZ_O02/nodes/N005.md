# N005 — Early Game vs Ground Macro Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early in the game, you observe the opponent adopting a ground macro posture, with heavy economy and expansion, and possible pressure. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Consider building a ground army to match the opponent's potential aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Consider building a ground army to match the opponent's potential aggression.

The opponent's ground macro posture suggests they may be aiming for a strong economy and a ground army. Preparing a ground defense can help counter any early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
