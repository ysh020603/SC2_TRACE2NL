## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on developing a robust economy and expanding while maintaining a ground-oriented army composition. It is flexible and can adapt to various opponent strategies.

Develop a strong economy and expand while building a ground-based army, preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
