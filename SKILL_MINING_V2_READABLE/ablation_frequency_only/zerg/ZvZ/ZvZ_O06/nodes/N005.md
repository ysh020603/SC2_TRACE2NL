# N005 — Early-Midgame Expansion Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-300 seconds) with both players on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Focus on expanding and increasing your economy. Maintain production and technology, and consider adding more production structures to support future army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings and Queens observed. Their economy and expansion appear heavy, production moderate, and technology light or uncertain. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented macro posture. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. You have Zerglings and Queens as representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding and increasing your economy. Maintain production and technology, and consider adding more production structures to support future army.

This node appears in a context where you have a strong economy lead. Expanding further and increasing production will allow you to out-macro the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a strong economy and production base to support a larger army later.
