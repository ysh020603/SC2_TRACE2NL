# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with both players on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and expansion. Strengthen your ground army composition while keeping production and technology at current levels.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings and Queens observed. Their economy and expansion appear heavy, production moderate, and technology light or uncertain. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented macro posture. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. You have Zerglings and Queens as representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and expansion. Strengthen your ground army composition while keeping production and technology at current levels.

Both players are in a similar macro-oriented state. Committing to a heavy tech or air switch now could leave you vulnerable to a ground timing attack. Maintaining a balanced ground army and economy allows you to respond to opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing your economy and ground army, while keeping scouting active to detect any tech or army shifts.
