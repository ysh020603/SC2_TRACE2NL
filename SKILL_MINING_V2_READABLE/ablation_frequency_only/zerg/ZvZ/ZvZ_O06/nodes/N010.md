# N010 — Late-Midgame Air Harassment

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600 seconds) with you having a heavy air presence, while the opponent remains on a ground macro posture with heavy production and technology. Continue to use your air force for harassment and map control. Maintain economy and expansion, and consider increasing technology for air upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens observed. Their production appears heavy, technology heavy, and economy heavy. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a ground-oriented macro posture, but you have a heavy air presence. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is heavy, and technology is moderate. You have Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers as representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to use your air force for harassment and map control. Maintain economy and expansion, and consider increasing technology for air upgrades.

Your Mutalisk force can be very effective against a ground-heavy opponent, especially if you can harass their economy and force them to split their army.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air superiority while continuing to expand and tech up.
