# ZvT_O03 Economy / Technology / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O03
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes a heavy economy, technology, and expansion posture while maintaining a moderate production base. It is a flexible macro-oriented approach that seeks to outgrow the opponent through superior economy and tech, with the option to transition into either ground or air compositions based on scouting information.

Develop a strong economy and technology foundation while expanding safely, preserving flexibility to adapt to the opponent's composition and pressure.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on live scouting and opponent actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Economy and Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 3 minutes) with both players focusing on economy and technology, minimal combat units observed.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology, maintain a defensive posture, and prepare to scout for the opponent's tech path.
