# N007 — Early Midgame Aggressive Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 5-6 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army with Marines and Reapers. You have a moderate production and technology. Increase your expansion rate and production to match the opponent's heavy production. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider tech to counter Reapers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. The opponent has heavy production and technology, likely preparing for a bio push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and technology. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion rate and production to match the opponent's heavy production. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider tech to counter Reapers.

The opponent's bio army with Reapers can be aggressive. Expanding and increasing production will give you the resources to overwhelm.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base, increase production structures, and tech towards units that can handle bio (e.g., Banelings, Roaches).
