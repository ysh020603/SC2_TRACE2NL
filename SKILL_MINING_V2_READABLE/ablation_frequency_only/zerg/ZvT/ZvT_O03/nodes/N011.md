# N011 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Battlecruisers. You have a heavy economy and production, with air presence. Increase your air presence and continue developing your ground army. Consider tech to counter Battlecruisers (e.g., Corruptors, Vipers).

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Battlecruisers. The opponent has heavy production and technology, suggesting a mech or sky transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, indicating a transition to air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue developing your ground army. Consider tech to counter Battlecruisers (e.g., Corruptors, Vipers).

The opponent's composition with Battlecruisers requires air superiority. Transitioning to air gives you the mobility and firepower to counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with air and ground components, secure additional expansions, and prepare for a decisive engagement.
