# N005 — Early Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 5-6 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army with Marines. You have a moderate production and technology. Continue developing your ground army, expand, and maintain a defensive posture. Consider adding Roaches or Banelings to counter Marines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Marines. The opponent has heavy production and technology, likely preparing for a bio push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and technology. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army, expand, and maintain a defensive posture. Consider adding Roaches or Banelings to counter Marines.

The opponent's Marine-heavy army is strong in the early midgame. You need a solid ground force to hold your bases and potentially counterattack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army with upgrades, secure additional expansions, and prepare for a potential engagement.
