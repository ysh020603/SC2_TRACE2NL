# N010 — Midgame Tech and Army Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs. You have a moderate production and heavy technology. Increase your technology and production to counter the opponent's bio army. Consider transitioning to a more advanced ground composition (e.g., Roaches, Hydralisks) or tech to air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs. The opponent has heavy production and technology, likely preparing for a bio push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your technology and production to counter the opponent's bio army. Consider transitioning to a more advanced ground composition (e.g., Roaches, Hydralisks) or tech to air.

The opponent's bio army with Medivacs is strong in the midgame. You need a tech advantage to hold your bases and potentially counterattack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong army with tech upgrades, secure additional expansions, and prepare for a potential engagement.
