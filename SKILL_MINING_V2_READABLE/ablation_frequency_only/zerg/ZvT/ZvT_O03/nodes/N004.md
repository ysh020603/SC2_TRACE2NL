# N004 — Late Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors. You have a heavy economy and production. Maintain your ground army strength, continue expanding, and consider tech transitions to counter mech (e.g., Brood Lords, Vipers).

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors. The opponent has heavy production and technology, suggesting a mech-heavy composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army strength, continue expanding, and consider tech transitions to counter mech (e.g., Brood Lords, Vipers).

The opponent's mech composition is powerful in the late midgame. You need a strong economy and tech to break through their defenses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a tech advantage (e.g., Hive tech) and a large army to take a decisive engagement or outmaneuver the opponent.
