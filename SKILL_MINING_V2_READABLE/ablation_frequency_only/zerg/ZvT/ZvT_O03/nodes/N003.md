# N003 — Midgame Aggressive Expansion and Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 9 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. You have a heavy economy and production. Increase your production and expansion rate to match the opponent's heavy production. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider tech to break siege lines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. The opponent has heavy production and technology, suggesting a mech or bio-mech composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and expansion rate to match the opponent's heavy production. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider tech to break siege lines.

The opponent's composition with Siege Tanks requires a strong economy to outproduce and outmaneuver. Expanding and increasing production will give you the resources to overwhelm.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third and fourth base, increase production structures, and tech towards units that can handle mech (e.g., Roach/Ravager, Swarm Hosts).
