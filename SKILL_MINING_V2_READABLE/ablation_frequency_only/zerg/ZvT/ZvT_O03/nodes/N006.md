# N006 — Early Game Expansion and Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with the opponent showing a ground army with Marines. You have a moderate production and technology. Continue expanding and developing your technology. Maintain a defensive posture and prepare to scout for the opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Marines. The opponent has moderate production and heavy technology investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with no significant combat units yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and developing your technology. Maintain a defensive posture and prepare to scout for the opponent's tech path.

This opening aims to leverage economic and technological advantages. Early aggression is not necessary; focus on safe expansion and tech progression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a solid economy, tech, and a defensive army capable of responding to the opponent's first major push.
