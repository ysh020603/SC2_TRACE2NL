## Summary

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Battlecruiser cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy, and air presence is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, continue expanding, and maintain ground defense.

Mutalisks provide harassment and map control, but require careful micro. Having a mix of air and ground forces can be effective.
