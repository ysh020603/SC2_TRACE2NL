# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground Dominance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At late-midgame (around 600 seconds), opponent still relies on ground forces with Siege Tanks. Own army is strong ground with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and consider tech upgrades to break siege lines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Hellion cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and consider tech upgrades to break siege lines.

Maintaining a strong economy and tech advantage allows for multiple tech paths. Ground army can be supplemented with spellcasters or air if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to late game with a versatile army and strong economy.
