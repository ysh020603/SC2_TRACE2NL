# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At early game (around 180 seconds), both sides are developing ground-oriented macro postures. Opponent shows possible early Reaper or Marine presence, while own forces are limited to Queens. Maintain current development, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while keeping an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Marine and Reaper cues. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, but defense is light. Possible early pressure from opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while keeping an eye on defense.

Early game is about establishing a solid economy and production base. Since opponent's tech is heavy, investing in tech later may be necessary, but for now, focus on macro and safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into early-midgame with a stronger ground army and more bases.
