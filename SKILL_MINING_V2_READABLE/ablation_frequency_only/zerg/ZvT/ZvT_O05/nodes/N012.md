# N012 — Early Game Defensive Preparation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At early game (around 180 seconds), opponent shows Reaper presence, indicating possible early harassment. Own army is minimal, but defense is heavy. Maintain defense, increase economy, and consider adding units to repel Reaper harass.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Reaper cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense, increase economy, and consider adding units to repel Reaper harass.

Reapers can be annoying early on. Having a few Zerglings or Queens can deter harassment while you focus on macro.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to early-midgame with a stable economy and defense.
