# N003 — Midgame Tech and Production Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At early-midgame (around 360 seconds), own tech has increased to heavy, and production is moderate. Opponent shows Hellion presence, indicating possible harassment. Increase technology and production, continue expanding, and strengthen ground forces to handle potential Hellion harass.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Marine, Reaper, and Hellion cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology and production, continue expanding, and strengthen ground forces to handle potential Hellion harass.

With heavy tech, you can start transitioning to more advanced units. Maintaining economy and production is key to supporting a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a robust tech tree and production capacity.
