# N011 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At early-midgame (around 300 seconds), opponent shows Siege Tanks and Medivacs, indicating a possible timing push. Own army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens. Increase production and expansion, strengthen ground forces, and consider defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tank, Marine, and Medivac cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion, strengthen ground forces, and consider defensive structures.

Siege Tanks and Medivacs suggest a timing attack. Preparing defenses and having a strong economy to support reinforcements is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
