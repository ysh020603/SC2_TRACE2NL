# N007 — Late-Midgame Anti-Air Preparation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At late-midgame (around 600 seconds), opponent still shows Battlecruiser cues. Own army is ground-based with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening ground forces but also invest in anti-air units or upgrades to counter potential Battlecruisers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Battlecruiser cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces but also invest in anti-air units or upgrades to counter potential Battlecruisers.

As the game progresses, Battlecruisers become more likely. Having a mix of anti-air and ground forces provides flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced army capable of handling both ground and air threats.
