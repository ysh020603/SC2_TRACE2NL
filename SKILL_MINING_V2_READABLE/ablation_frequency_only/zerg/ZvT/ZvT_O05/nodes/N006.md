# N006 — Midgame Tech Adaptation to Battlecruiser

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At midgame (around 540 seconds), opponent shows Battlecruiser cues, indicating possible air transition. Own army is ground-based with heavy production and tech. Maintain ground strength but consider adding anti-air units or tech to handle potential Battlecruisers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Battlecruiser cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength but consider adding anti-air units or tech to handle potential Battlecruisers.

Battlecruisers are expensive and slow, but can be deadly if unopposed. Having some anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks, Mutalisks, or Corruptors) is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army with anti-air capability.
