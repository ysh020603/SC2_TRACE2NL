## Summary

At midgame (around 540 seconds), opponent has Siege Tanks and heavy production. Own army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and defense is heavy. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and consider tech upgrades to break siege lines.

## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Hellion cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and consider tech upgrades to break siege lines.

Siege Tanks require either overwhelming numbers or tech solutions like banelings or vipers. Maintaining economy allows for tech switches.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
