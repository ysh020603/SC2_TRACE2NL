# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Reinforcement

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At early-midgame (around 240-300 seconds), opponent's production has increased, and own ground forces are still developing. Possible pressure from Terran ground units. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is felt.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Marine and Reaper cues. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, but defense is light. Possible early pressure from opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is felt.

As opponent's production ramps up, it's important to match army size while maintaining economic lead. Tech investment can be delayed until opponent's composition is clearer.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a solid ground army and tech infrastructure.
