# ZvT_O05 Expansion / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O05
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / production opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes early expansion and economy development while maintaining moderate production and light technology investment. The strategic goal is to build a strong economic foundation and flexible production capacity, allowing adaptation based on scouting information.

Develop a expansion / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Game Ground Macro Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At early game (around 180 seconds), both sides are developing ground-oriented macro postures. Opponent shows possible early Reaper or Marine presence, while own forces are limited to Queens.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while keeping an eye on defense.
