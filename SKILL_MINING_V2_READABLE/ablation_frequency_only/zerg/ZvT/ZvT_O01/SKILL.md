# ZvT_O01 Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O01
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on heavy economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game emphasizes Zerglings and Queens, with technology investment initially light but increasing through the midgame. The strategic goal is to develop a strong economy and expansion lead while preserving flexibility to adapt to Terran ground or air transitions.

Develop a heavy economy and expansion lead while building a ground-oriented army, with the flexibility to transition to air (e.g., Mutalisks) if the opponent commits to a ground posture or if map control favors harassment.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of the opponent's composition and pressure.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
