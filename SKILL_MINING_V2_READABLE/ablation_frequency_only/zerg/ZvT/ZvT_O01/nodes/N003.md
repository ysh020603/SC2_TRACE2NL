# N003 — Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 360s) with own ground posture and moderate production, but heavy tech investment. Continue tech investment, increase production, and maintain expansion. Consider adding tech units like Hydralisks or preparing for air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, production heavy, tech heavy, and pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and heavy tech. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue tech investment, increase production, and maintain expansion. Consider adding tech units like Hydralisks or preparing for air transition.

Heavy tech allows for a stronger midgame army and flexibility to counter opponent's composition. Maintaining economy supports this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame with a versatile army and tech advantage, ready to respond to opponent's moves.
