# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-300s) with own ground posture and opponent likely ground (Marine/Reaper) with heavy production. Increase economy and expansion, continue ground army development, and consider tech upgrades. Maintain scouting to detect tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, production heavy, tech heavy, and pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Queens, moderate production, and light tech. Economy and expansion are heavy, and you are looking to increase both.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, continue ground army development, and consider tech upgrades. Maintain scouting to detect tech switches.

With a heavy economy, you can afford to expand and tech while maintaining a defensive ground army. This sets up for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame position with a strong economy, multiple bases, and a tech advantage.
