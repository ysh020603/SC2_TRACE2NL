# N007 — Late Midgame Siege Tank Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 600s) with own ground posture and heavy defense, opponent showing Siege Tanks. Maintain a strong defensive posture, continue economy, and consider tech for breaking siege lines (e.g., Vipers or Brood Lords).

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, production heavy, tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, and heavy tech. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a strong defensive posture, continue economy, and consider tech for breaking siege lines (e.g., Vipers or Brood Lords).

Siege Tanks are strong defensively, so you need to avoid costly engagements and tech to counter them.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with tech to break Terran defenses and a strong economy.
