# N005 — Early Game Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own ground posture and opponent unknown (no reliable combat-unit cue). Maintain a safe economy and ground army, but keep scouting to determine opponent's plan. Avoid risky investments.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, with no reliable combat-unit cue. Production moderate, tech heavy, and pressure unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light tech. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a safe economy and ground army, but keep scouting to determine opponent's plan. Avoid risky investments.

With unknown opponent, it's important to stay flexible and not over-commit. A solid economy and defensive ground army provide safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gain information about opponent's composition and transition to a more informed strategy.
