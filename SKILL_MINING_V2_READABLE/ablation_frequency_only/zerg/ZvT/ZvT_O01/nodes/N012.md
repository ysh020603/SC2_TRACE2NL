# N012 — Midgame Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480s) with own ground posture and heavy production, opponent ground with Medivacs. Continue expanding, increase production, and consider tech for anti-air or anti-armor.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs, production heavy, tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, and moderate tech. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding, increase production, and consider tech for anti-air or anti-armor.

With a heavy economy, you can afford to expand and tech while maintaining a large army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame with a strong economy and a versatile army.
