# N011 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 600s) with own air presence (Mutalisks) and heavy defense, opponent ground with Medivacs. Increase air presence, continue economy, and consider upgrades for Mutalisks. Use air for harassment and map control.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, production heavy, tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, heavy production, and heavy tech. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, continue economy, and consider upgrades for Mutalisks. Use air for harassment and map control.

Mutalisks provide mobility and harassment, forcing the opponent to split attention. This can create opportunities.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with air superiority and a strong economy.
