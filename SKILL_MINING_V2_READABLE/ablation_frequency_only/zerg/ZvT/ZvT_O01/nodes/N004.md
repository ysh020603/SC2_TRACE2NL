# N004 — Midgame Production Increase

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300s) with own ground posture and moderate production, opponent showing Hellions. Increase production to match opponent's army size, continue ground army development, and consider tech for anti-armor.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, production heavy, tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light tech. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production to match opponent's army size, continue ground army development, and consider tech for anti-armor.

Hellions can be dangerous to workers, so having a larger army and better tech (e.g., Banelings) can mitigate pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame with a strong ground army and production capacity to sustain it.
