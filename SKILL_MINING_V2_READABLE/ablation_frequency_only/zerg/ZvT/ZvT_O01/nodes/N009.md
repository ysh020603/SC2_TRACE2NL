# N009 — Late Midgame Medivac Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 600s) with own ground posture and heavy defense, opponent showing Medivacs. Maintain defense against drops, continue economy, and consider anti-air tech (e.g., Hydralisks or Mutalisks).

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, production heavy, tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, and heavy tech. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense against drops, continue economy, and consider anti-air tech (e.g., Hydralisks or Mutalisks).

Medivacs enable drops and bio mobility, so you need anti-air and map vision to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with anti-air capabilities and a strong economy.
