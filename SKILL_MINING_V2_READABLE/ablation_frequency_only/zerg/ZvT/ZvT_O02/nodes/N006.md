# N006 — Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent continues with a ground-based Marine composition, and you have a moderate defense. Maintain your current army path, but focus on strengthening your ground forces and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, heavy economy, and heavy production. They may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is still unknown, but you have the ability to strengthen your ground army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path, but focus on strengthening your ground forces and defense.

A strong ground defense will help you survive any early pushes and set up for a counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army that can defend and potentially transition to a more aggressive stance.
