# N003 — Midgame Strategic Pivot

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

By the midgame, you have enough information to start making more concrete decisions about your army composition. Maintain your current development path, but start considering which tech paths to pursue based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture remains unknown, but you may have seen some cues. They could be going for a ground or air composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you have the capacity to expand into a more specific build.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but start considering which tech paths to pursue based on scouting.

Midgame is a critical point where you need to balance economy, tech, and army. Staying flexible allows you to adapt to the opponent's choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a solid army composition that can handle the opponent's likely strategies.
