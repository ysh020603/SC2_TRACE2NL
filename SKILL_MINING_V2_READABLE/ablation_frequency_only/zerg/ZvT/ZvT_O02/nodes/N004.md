# N004 — Late-Midgame Execution

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, you should have a clear direction and be executing your strategy. Maintain your current army path and continue to develop your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you may have seen some units. They could be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you have the infrastructure to support a strong army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue to develop your economy and tech.

At this stage, consistency is key. You need to ensure your army is strong enough to defend against potential attacks while continuing to grow.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a point where you can start transitioning to a late-game composition if needed.
