# N009 — Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, you have a ground-based army with Zerglings and Queens, and the opponent is also ground-based. Increase your production and continue to strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, heavy economy, and heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground posture with heavy economy and expansion. You are producing Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and continue to strengthen your ground army.

A larger ground army will allow you to defend against the opponent's pushes and potentially take map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a large enough army to defend and potentially counter-attack.
