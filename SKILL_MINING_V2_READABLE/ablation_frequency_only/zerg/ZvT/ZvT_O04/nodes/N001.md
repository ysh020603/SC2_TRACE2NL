# N001 — Early Economy Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and technology. Continue developing economy and tech, but add safety checks. Maintain ground army strength and consider early defense structures if pressure is detected.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but may show Marines. Their economy and expansion are heavy, technology is heavy, but production is moderate. No clear pressure yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with moderate production, likely with Queens and Zerglings.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and tech, but add safety checks. Maintain ground army strength and consider early defense structures if pressure is detected.

The early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech lead. Since opponent is also heavy on economy, you need to match their macro while preparing for potential aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army and more production as the game progresses.
