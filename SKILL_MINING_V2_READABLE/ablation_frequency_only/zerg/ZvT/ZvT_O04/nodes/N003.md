# N003 — Early Game Defensive Preparation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and technology. Maintain economy and expansion, but add defensive structures (e.g., Spine Crawlers) if pressure is expected. Strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines. Their production is heavy, technology is heavy, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with moderate production, likely with Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and expansion, but add defensive structures (e.g., Spine Crawlers) if pressure is expected. Strengthen ground army.

The opponent has heavy production and may be planning an early push. Preparing defense can prevent economic damage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army and more production as the game progresses.
