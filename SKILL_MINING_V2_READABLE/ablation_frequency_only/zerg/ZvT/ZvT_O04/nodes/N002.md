# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame, around 4-5 minutes, with heavy economy and expansion, but still light defense and technology. Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and continue technology development. Consider adding more production structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and possibly Ghosts. Their production is heavy, technology is heavy, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with moderate production, likely with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and continue technology development. Consider adding more production structures.

The opponent is investing heavily in ground forces and tech. You need to match their army strength while maintaining your economy lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army composition with better tech (e.g., Roaches, Lurkers) as the game progresses.
