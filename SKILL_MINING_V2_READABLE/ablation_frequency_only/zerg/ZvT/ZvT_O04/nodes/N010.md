# N010 — Midgame Tech and Army Balance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame, around 9 minutes, with heavy economy, expansion, production, and moderate technology. Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and consider adding anti-air if needed. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Ravens. Their production is heavy, technology is heavy, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, production, and moderate technology. Your army is ground-oriented with heavy production, likely with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and consider adding anti-air if needed. Continue technology development.

The opponent has a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Ravens. You need to maintain your economy lead and tech advantage to break their defenses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more diverse army composition with better tech (e.g., Brood Lords, Vipers) as the game progresses.
