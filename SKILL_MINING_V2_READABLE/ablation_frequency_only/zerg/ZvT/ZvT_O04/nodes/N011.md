# N011 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame, around 10 minutes, with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Increase air presence and upgrades, continue economy and expansion, and maintain ground army strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Thors, and Ravens. Their production is heavy, technology is heavy, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with heavy production, likely with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and upgrades, continue economy and expansion, and maintain ground army strength.

The opponent has a strong ground army with Thors and Ravens. You need to transition to air (Mutalisks) to counter their ground forces.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more diverse army composition with better tech (e.g., Brood Lords, Vipers) as the game progresses.
