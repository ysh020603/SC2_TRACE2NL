# ZvT_O04 Economy / Upgrade / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O04
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / upgrade / expansion opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on heavy economy, upgrades, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game is characterized by moderate production and light technology, transitioning to heavy production and technology in the midgame.

Develop a strong economy and tech base while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation. Aim to outscale the Terran opponent through superior economy and upgrades.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on scouting and opponent actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
