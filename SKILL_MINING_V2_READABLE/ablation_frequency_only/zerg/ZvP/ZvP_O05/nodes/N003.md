# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. The opponent is unknown but has heavy defense and moderate production, with heavy technology. Continue developing your economy and army, while maintaining defensive structures. Consider adding tech structures to enable a transition to Roaches or other units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, technology is light or uncertain, and you are in a defensive posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and army, while maintaining defensive structures. Consider adding tech structures to enable a transition to Roaches or other units.

With an unknown opponent, it is prudent to focus on economy and defense while maintaining flexibility. Heavy economy allows for later tech switches or army production as needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame army composition based on scouting information, whether that be Roach-based, Hydralisk, or tech into spellcasters.
