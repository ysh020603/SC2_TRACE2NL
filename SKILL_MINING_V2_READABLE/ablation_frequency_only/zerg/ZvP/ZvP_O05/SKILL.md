# ZvP_O05 Economy / Expansion / Defense

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O05
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / defense opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening that prioritizes economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Early game focuses on heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. The army is ground-oriented, typically Zerglings and Queens, with a possible transition to Roaches or other tech later. The opponent is often unknown early, but may show ground or air leanings as the game progresses.

Develop a strong economy and expansion lead while staying safe with defensive structures and units. Maintain flexibility to adapt to opponent's tech or army composition, and transition into a midgame army that can punish overextension or defend against aggression.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on scouting and live game state.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
