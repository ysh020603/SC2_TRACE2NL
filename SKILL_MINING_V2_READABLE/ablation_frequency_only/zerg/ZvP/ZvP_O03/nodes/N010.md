# N010 — Midgame Air Transition Response

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 8 minutes) with opponent showing Phoenixes, indicating an air army. Your economy is heavy. Consider adding anti-air units like Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Maintain economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has an air army with Phoenixes, heavy production and technology. They are likely going for a stargate-based army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider adding anti-air units like Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Maintain economy and tech.

Phoenixes are strong against ground, so you need anti-air. Hydralisks are a good choice, and Mutalisks can also fight them.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with anti-air capabilities, and prepare for a potential air battle.
