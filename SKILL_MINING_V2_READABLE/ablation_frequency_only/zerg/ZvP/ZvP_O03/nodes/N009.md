# N009 — Late Midgame Tech and Army Maintenance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) with opponent showing Zealots and Carriers, indicating a tech switch to air. Your economy is heavy. Maintain your ground army while considering anti-air options like Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Keep economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Carriers, heavy production and technology. They are transitioning to air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and moderate technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while considering anti-air options like Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Keep economy and tech.

Carriers are a late-game threat, so you need anti-air. Hydralisks are a solid choice, and Mutalisks can harass.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with anti-air capabilities, and prepare for a late-game engagement.
