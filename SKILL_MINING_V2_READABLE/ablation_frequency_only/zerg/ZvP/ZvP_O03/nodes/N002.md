# N002 — Early Midgame Tech and Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 4 minutes) with opponent showing Stalker, indicating a possible tech path. Your economy remains heavy. Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy. Consider adding Roaches or teching towards Lair for more options.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology. They have shown a Stalker, suggesting a gateway-based army or tech transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy is heavy, expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy. Consider adding Roaches or teching towards Lair for more options.

Stalkers are versatile and can be part of many Protoss builds. Strengthening your ground army with Roaches provides a solid core that can handle gateway units and gives you a foundation for later tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army composition (e.g., Roach-Zergling) and prepare to tech towards Lair for upgrades or tech units.
