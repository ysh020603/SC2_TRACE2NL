# N005 — Midgame Tech and Production Increase

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 6 minutes) with opponent showing Zealots and Sentries, indicating a ground army. Your economy is heavy. Increase production and technology. Consider adding Roaches and teching to Lair for upgrades or tech units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Sentries, heavy production and technology. They are likely going for a gateway-based army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology. Consider adding Roaches and teching to Lair for upgrades or tech units.

To keep up with the opponent's heavy production, you need to increase your own. Roaches are a good addition to your army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stronger army and tech, and prepare for a potential third base.
