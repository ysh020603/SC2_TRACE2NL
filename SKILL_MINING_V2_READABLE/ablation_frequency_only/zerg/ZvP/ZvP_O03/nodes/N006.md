# N006 — Midgame Army Maintenance and Tech

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 8 minutes) with opponent showing Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prism. Your economy is heavy. Maintain your army and tech, consider adding Roaches or Mutalisks for mobility. Keep economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prism, heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production and moderate technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your army and tech, consider adding Roaches or Mutalisks for mobility. Keep economy and expansion.

Warp Prism indicates potential harass, so having a mobile response is useful. Mutalisks can also harass and provide map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile army and tech, and prepare for late-game transitions.
