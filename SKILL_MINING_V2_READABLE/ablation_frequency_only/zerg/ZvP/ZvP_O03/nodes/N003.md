# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 5 minutes) with opponent showing Zealots and Sentries, indicating a ground-based army. Your economy is heavy. Strengthen your ground army with Roaches and consider getting upgrades. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Sentries, heavy production and technology. They are likely going for a gateway-based army with force fields.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army with Roaches and consider getting upgrades. Maintain economy and expansion.

Zealots and Sentries are strong in the early midgame, but Roaches can tank damage and deal with them effectively. Upgrades will help in the long run.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a Roach-Zergling army with upgrades, and prepare for a potential third base or tech switch.
