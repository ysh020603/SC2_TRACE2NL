# N011 — Early Midgame Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 4 minutes) with opponent showing Zealots, indicating a ground army. Your economy is heavy. Increase production and expansion, consider adding Roaches. Maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, heavy production and technology. They are likely going for a gateway-based army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion, consider adding Roaches. Maintain economy.

To match the opponent's heavy production, you need to increase your own. Roaches are a good addition to your army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stronger army and tech, and prepare for a potential third base.
