# N001 — Early Economy and Expansion Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with both players on heavy economy and expansion. Opponent's tech is heavy but army composition is unknown. Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining a light defense. Focus on macro and scouting to identify opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. No reliable combat unit cues yet, so their army composition is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining a light defense. Focus on macro and scouting to identify opponent's tech path.

With both players on heavy economy, the game is likely to be macro-oriented. Investing in economy and expansions now sets up a strong mid-game. Maintaining a light defense with Zerglings and Queens is sufficient to deter early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy with multiple bases, then transition to a more defined army composition based on scouting.
