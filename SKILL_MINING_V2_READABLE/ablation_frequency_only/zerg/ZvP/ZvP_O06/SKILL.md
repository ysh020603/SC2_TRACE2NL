# ZvP_O06 Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O06
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening that prioritizes economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game focuses on Zerglings and Queens, with a transition to heavier ground compositions and technology as the game progresses.

Develop a strong economy and expansion lead while building a versatile ground army. Maintain flexibility to adapt to Protoss tech choices, whether they go ground, air, or tech-heavy.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on scouting and live game state.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
