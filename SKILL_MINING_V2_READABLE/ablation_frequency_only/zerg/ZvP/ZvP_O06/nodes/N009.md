# N009 — Late-Midgame Tech and Army

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 600 seconds, opponent has a ground army with High Templars and Phoenixes. Your army is heavy. Continue strengthening army and tech. Consider adding units that can deal with High Templars, such as Corruptors or Vipers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Zealots, High Templars, Sentries, and Phoenixes. They have strong spellcasters and air support.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy ground army with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, heavy production, heavy technology. Defense heavy, economy heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening army and tech. Consider adding units that can deal with High Templars, such as Corruptors or Vipers.

High Templars can storm your army, so you need to either kill them quickly or have spellcasters of your own.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced composition with spellcasters or anti-air, and continue teching.
