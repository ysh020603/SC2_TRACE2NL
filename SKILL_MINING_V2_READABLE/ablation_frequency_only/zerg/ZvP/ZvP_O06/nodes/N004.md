# N004 — Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 360 seconds, opponent still on ground with Zealots and Stalkers. Your defense is moderate, tech is moderate. Increase technology investment to get access to better units and upgrades. Continue expanding and strengthening army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Zealots and Stalkers, production heavy, technology heavy. They may be teching to something like Immortals or Colossus.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, moderate technology. Defense is moderate, economy heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology investment to get access to better units and upgrades. Continue expanding and strengthening army.

Investing in tech now gives you long-term advantages. Upgrades and better units will be crucial in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a tech-heavy ground army with units like Roaches, Hydralisks, or Lair tech.
