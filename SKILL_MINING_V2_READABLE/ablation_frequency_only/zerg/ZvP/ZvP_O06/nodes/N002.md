# N002 — Early-Midgame Tech and Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 240-300 seconds, opponent shows Zealot, indicating a possible ground or tech transition. Your economy remains heavy. Strengthen ground army and continue economy. Consider adding Roaches or teching towards Lair for more options.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Zealots, production appears heavy, technology heavy. They may be going for a ground push or teching to something like Immortals or Colossus.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and continue economy. Consider adding Roaches or teching towards Lair for more options.

Zealots are a basic ground unit; opponent is likely building a ground army. Strengthening your ground forces and teching gives you flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground composition, possibly with Roaches, and prepare for a potential midgame engagement.
