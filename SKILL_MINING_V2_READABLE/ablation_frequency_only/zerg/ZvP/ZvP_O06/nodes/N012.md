# N012 — Midgame Air Harass Response

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 360 seconds, opponent has Phoenixes, Warp Prisms, and Oracles. Your defense is heavy. Maintain defense and consider adding anti-air units or tech. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Phoenixes, Warp Prisms, and Oracles, indicating a heavy air presence and possible harass.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, moderate technology. Defense heavy, economy heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense and consider adding anti-air units or tech. Continue expanding and teching.

Warp Prisms can drop units, and Oracles can harass. You need to be prepared for multi-pronged attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with anti-air and mobility, such as Hydralisks or Mutalisks.
