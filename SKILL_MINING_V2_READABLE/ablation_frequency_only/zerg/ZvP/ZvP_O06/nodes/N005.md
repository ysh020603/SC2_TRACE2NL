# N005 — Midgame Heavy Ground Army

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 480-540 seconds, opponent has a strong ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. Your army is heavy. Continue strengthening ground army and tech. Consider adding units that counter Immortals, such as Roaches or Hydralisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a powerful ground army with Immortals, which are strong against armored units. They are likely to push soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, heavy technology. Defense is heavy, economy heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army and tech. Consider adding units that counter Immortals, such as Roaches or Hydralisks.

Immortals are strong against armored, so you need units that are not armored or have high DPS. Roaches and Hydralisks are good choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed ground army with Roaches, Hydralisks, and possibly Lurkers or other tech.
