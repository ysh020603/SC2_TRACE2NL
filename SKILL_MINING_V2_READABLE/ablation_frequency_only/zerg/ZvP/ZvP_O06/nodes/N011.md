# N011 — Early-Midgame Air Defense

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 300-360 seconds, opponent has Phoenixes and Oracles. Your defense is light. Increase defense and consider adding anti-air units such as Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers. Continue economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has Phoenixes and Oracles, indicating an air-based harass or transition. They may be going for a stargate build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light technology. Defense light, economy heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and consider adding anti-air units such as Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers. Continue economy.

Phoenixes can harass workers and pick off units. You need anti-air to defend and deter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defensive posture with anti-air, while continuing to develop economy.
