- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- Ground-oriented macro posture with Zergling, Roach, and Queen presence. Economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion while continuing ground army development. Consider increasing production.

The opponent's heavy production suggests a macro game. Expanding your economy and production will help you keep up.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
