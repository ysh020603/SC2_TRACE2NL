# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 240 seconds, you have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, while opponent's army is still unknown but they are teching heavily. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to expand and tech. Maintain a defensive posture and scout for potential tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues. They have heavy economy and technology, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to expand and tech. Maintain a defensive posture and scout for potential tech switches.

Building a solid ground army provides a safe base to transition from, while scouting helps you prepare for potential air or tech threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized army composition based on opponent's tech choice, such as adding anti-air if needed.
