# N005 — Early-Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 300 seconds, opponent has a ground army with Stalkers, and you have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. You are both expanding. Maintain a defensive posture while continuing to expand and tech. Focus on scouting to detect any tech switches or aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Stalker. They have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a defensive posture while continuing to expand and tech. Focus on scouting to detect any tech switches or aggression.

With opponent on ground, you can safely develop, but you need to be prepared for potential pressure. Maintaining a defensive stance allows you to react to threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive stance once you have a solid economy and army, or if you scout an opportunity.
