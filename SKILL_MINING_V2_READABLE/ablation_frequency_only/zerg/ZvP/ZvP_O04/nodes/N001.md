# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game, around 180 seconds, with both players focusing on economy and expansion. Opponent's army composition is unknown, but they are investing heavily in technology. Continue developing your economy and production while maintaining a defensive posture. Focus on scouting to determine opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues observed. They appear to have heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and light technology. Your economy and expansion are heavy, and you have light defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production while maintaining a defensive posture. Focus on scouting to determine opponent's tech path.

Early game is about establishing a strong foundation. Maintaining a balanced economy and production allows you to adapt to whatever the opponent commits to.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition based on scouting information, while continuing to expand and tech up.
