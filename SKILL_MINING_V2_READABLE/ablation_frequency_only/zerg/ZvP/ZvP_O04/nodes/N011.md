# N011 — Early Game Defensive Expansion

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Around 180 seconds, opponent's army is unknown, but they have heavy defense. You have a ground-oriented army with moderate production. Continue expanding and teching while maintaining a defensive posture. Scout to determine opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues. They have heavy economy and technology, with moderate production and heavy defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and teching while maintaining a defensive posture. Scout to determine opponent's tech path.

With opponent's army unknown, it's important to build a solid foundation and be ready to adapt. Expanding and teching gives you flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition based on scouting information.
