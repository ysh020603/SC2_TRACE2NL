# N002 — Midgame Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the midgame, both players have had time to develop their economies and armies. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but your own posture is also uncertain. Maintain your current development path, but start to consider potential transitions based on scouting information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production and technology are light or uncertain. They could be transitioning to any strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition. You need to start making decisions about your midgame strategy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but start to consider potential transitions based on scouting information.

In the midgame, you need to start preparing for the opponent's potential strategies while maintaining your own economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition that can handle the opponent's likely strategies.
