# N010 — Early-Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, both you and the opponent have ground-based armies. The opponent has a heavy ground composition with Stalkers. Strengthen your ground army and continue developing your economy. Consider adding units that counter the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy ground composition with Stalkers. The opponent's production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Zerglings and Queens. Your economy is heavy, and your production is moderate. You need to strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue developing your economy. Consider adding units that counter the opponent's composition.

The opponent has a strong ground army, so you need to match or counter it. Roaches and Zerglings can be effective, but you may need to add more specialized units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a ground army that can hold against the opponent's heavy composition and potentially counter-attack.
