# N009 — Midgame Ground Opponent

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

In the midgame, you have scouted a ground-based opponent with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. The opponent's production is heavy, and their technology is heavy. Maintain your current development path, but start to prepare for a potential ground-based attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. The opponent's production is heavy, and their technology is heavy. They may be preparing for a ground-based push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain. You have no confirmed army composition yet. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but start to prepare for a potential ground-based attack.

Knowing the opponent is ground-based allows you to prepare appropriate defenses and tech choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a ground army that can hold against the opponent's Zealot-based composition.
