# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players are in the early game with limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own posture is also uncertain. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and expansion. Keep your options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production and technology are light or uncertain. They could be going for any strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain. You have no confirmed army composition yet. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and expansion. Keep your options open.

In the early game, it is important to not commit to a specific strategy until you have more information about the opponent's intentions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a solid economy and a flexible army composition.
