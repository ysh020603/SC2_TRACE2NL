# N002 — Mid-Game Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Strengthen ground army and increase production. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider adding tech structures to support army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and increase production. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider adding tech structures to support army.

The frequent pattern indicates a need to bolster ground forces against a ground-based Protoss army while maintaining economic lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid mid-game army composition with better tech.
