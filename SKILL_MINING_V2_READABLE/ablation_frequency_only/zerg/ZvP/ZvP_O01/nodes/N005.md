# N005 — Early-Midgame Tech and Production Increase

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 6 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and moderate technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Increase technology and production. Continue expanding and developing economy. Strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Production heavy, technology heavy, economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and moderate technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology and production. Continue expanding and developing economy. Strengthen ground army.

The frequent pattern shows a need to advance tech and production to keep up with opponent's heavy production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid mid-game army composition with better tech.
