# N010 — Early Game Expansion and Production Increase

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent posture uncertain, possibly heavy defense. Increase production and continue expanding. Maintain ground army strength. Consider scouting to clarify opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain; no reliable combat-unit cues. Production moderate, technology heavy, economy moderate, expansion moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and continue expanding. Maintain ground army strength. Consider scouting to clarify opponent's intentions.

The frequent pattern shows a focus on expansion and production during early game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined early-midgame posture.
