# N008 — Midgame Ground Army and Tech Maintenance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and moderate technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Maintain ground army and technology. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider adding more production if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Production heavy, technology heavy, economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and moderate technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army and technology. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider adding more production if needed.

The frequent pattern shows a need to maintain current strengths while keeping up with opponent's heavy production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition with better tech.
