# N006 — Early Game Expansion and Upgrade Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Zealots. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider starting upgrades. Maintain ground army strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Production moderate, technology heavy, economy heavy, expansion moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider starting upgrades. Maintain ground army strength.

The frequent pattern shows a focus on expansion and upgrades during early game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined early-midgame posture.
