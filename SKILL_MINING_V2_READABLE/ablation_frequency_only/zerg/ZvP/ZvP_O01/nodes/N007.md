# N007 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Continue expanding and increasing production. Maintain ground army strength. Consider scouting to clarify opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Production heavy, technology heavy, economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing production. Maintain ground army strength. Consider scouting to clarify opponent's intentions.

The frequent pattern shows a focus on expansion and production during early-midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined mid-game posture based on scouting information.
