# N001 — Early Economy and Expansion Focus

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with own ground-oriented posture, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent posture is uncertain, possibly heavy economy and expansion. Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining ground army strength. Keep production steady and consider scouting to clarify opponent intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain; no reliable combat-unit cues. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, and economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, but defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining ground army strength. Keep production steady and consider scouting to clarify opponent intentions.

The frequent pattern shows a focus on economic growth and expansion during early game, with safety checks to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined mid-game posture based on scouting information.
