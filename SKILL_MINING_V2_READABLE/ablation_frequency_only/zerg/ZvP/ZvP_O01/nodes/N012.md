# N012 — Late-Midgame Ground Army and Tech Maintenance

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 10 minutes) with own ground army, heavy production, and heavy technology. Opponent shows ground posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Maintain ground army and technology. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider scouting to clarify opponent's army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Production heavy, technology heavy, economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, heavy production, and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army and technology. Continue expanding and developing economy. Consider scouting to clarify opponent's army composition.

The frequent pattern shows a need to maintain current strengths while keeping up with opponent's heavy production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition with better tech.
