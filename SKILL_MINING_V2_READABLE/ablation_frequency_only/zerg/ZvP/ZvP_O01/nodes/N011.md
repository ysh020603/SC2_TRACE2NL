# N011 — Midgame Air Defense Preparation

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and heavy technology. Opponent shows air posture with Phoenixes and Oracles. Prepare anti-air capabilities while maintaining ground army. Continue developing technology and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Zealots, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Production heavy, technology heavy, economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, moderate production, and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Prepare anti-air capabilities while maintaining ground army. Continue developing technology and economy.

The frequent pattern shows a need to address opponent's air presence while maintaining macro.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with anti-air capabilities.
