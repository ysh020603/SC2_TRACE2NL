# N004 — Midgame Ground Army and Tech Development

## Node Type

FREQUENT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with own ground army, moderate production, and heavy technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Continue strengthening ground army and increasing technology. Maintain defense and economy. Consider adding more production if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Production heavy, technology heavy, economy/expansion heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, moderate production, and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army and increasing technology. Maintain defense and economy. Consider adding more production if needed.

The frequent pattern indicates a need to develop tech and army to match opponent's ground forces.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition with better tech.
