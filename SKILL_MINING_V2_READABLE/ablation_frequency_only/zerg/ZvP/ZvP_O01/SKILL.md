# ZvP_O01 Economy / Expansion / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O01
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / production opening
- Method: Frequency Only

## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on building a strong economy and expanding while maintaining moderate production. It is a flexible macro-oriented approach that can adapt to various Protoss strategies.

Develop a robust economy and expansion lead while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [FREQUENT] N001 — Early Economy and Expansion Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 3 minutes) with own ground-oriented posture, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent posture is uncertain, possibly heavy economy and expansion.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining ground army strength. Keep production steady and consider scouting to clarify opponent intentions.
