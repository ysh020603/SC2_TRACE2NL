# N003 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both you and the opponent have unknown army compositions, but both are investing heavily in economy and technology. Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defensive posture. Use your Reaper for scouting to gather information about the opponent's strategy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's army is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and technology. They are expanding and have moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production. Your army is small, with a Reaper for scouting. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defensive posture. Use your Reaper for scouting to gather information about the opponent's strategy.

This early game focus on economy and tech sets up a strong midgame, and scouting helps you adapt to the opponent's choices.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Early Game: Don't Overcommit to Tech

**When this applies:** Early game, both sides unknown compositions, heavy economy and tech investment, around 180 seconds.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to tech at the expense of army production and neglecting scouting.

**Correction:** Focus on developing economy and technology while maintaining a light defensive posture. Use your Reaper for scouting to gather information.

**Why:** This sets up a strong midgame, and scouting helps you adapt to the opponent's choices.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have more scouting information or if the opponent shows signs of an early attack.
