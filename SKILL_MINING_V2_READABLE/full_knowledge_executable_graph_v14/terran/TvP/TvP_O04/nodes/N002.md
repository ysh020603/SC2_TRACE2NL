# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, and the opponent is also ground-oriented with Stalkers. Both have heavy economies and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your technology investment. Consider adding air units to complement your ground forces. Maintain your expansion and production.

The opponent is ground-oriented, so a strong ground army is essential. Adding air units can provide flexibility and counter potential air transitions.
