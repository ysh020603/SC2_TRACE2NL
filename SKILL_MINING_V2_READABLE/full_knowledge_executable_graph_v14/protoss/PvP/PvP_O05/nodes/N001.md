## Node Type

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both sides having heavy economy and technology, but own army composition still unclear. Opponent shows a ground posture with Stalker cues. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your current expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalker cues. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they have moderate expansion. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are heavy, but your army composition is not yet defined. You have moderate production and are expanding well. Representative units include Stalker.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your current expansion and production.

With both sides investing heavily in tech and economy, a solid ground core provides safety while you transition to a more defined composition. This approach keeps options open for either a ground or air switch later.
