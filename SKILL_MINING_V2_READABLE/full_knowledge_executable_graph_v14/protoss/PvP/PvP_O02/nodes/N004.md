## Summary

In the midgame, both sides have had time to develop. Your own posture remains uncertain, but the opponent's is also still unclear. Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition.

In the midgame, the game can swing based on tech choices. By staying flexible, you can counter the opponent's composition once it becomes clear.
