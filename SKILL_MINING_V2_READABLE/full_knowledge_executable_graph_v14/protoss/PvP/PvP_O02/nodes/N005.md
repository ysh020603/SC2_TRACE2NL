# N005 — Early Game Stabilization and Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, you have a heavy defense posture and moderate economy, while the opponent has a heavy economy and moderate production. This suggests a potential tech or economic race. Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense while continuing to develop your economy and technology. This is a stabilize-then-develop approach.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues, but their economy is heavy and production is moderate. They may be teching or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have invested in defense and have a moderate economy. Your production is light or uncertain, but your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense while continuing to develop your economy and technology. This is a stabilize-then-develop approach.

Given the opponent's heavy economy, they may be planning a tech-heavy composition. By strengthening your ground army and defense, you can hold off any early pressure while you develop your own tech.
