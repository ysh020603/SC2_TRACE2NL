# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. Your own production and tech are uncertain, and the opponent's posture is similarly unclear. Maintain current development path with a focus on economy and technology. Keep production flexible to adapt to incoming information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and tech are also light or uncertain, indicating a possible economic or tech-focused opening.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your early game is characterized by light or uncertain production and technology. You have not committed to a specific army composition yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with a focus on economy and technology. Keep production flexible to adapt to incoming information.

In the early game, committing too early to a specific army composition can be punished. A balanced development approach allows you to respond to the opponent's tech or aggression once it becomes visible.
