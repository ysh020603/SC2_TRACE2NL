## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Balanced development under uncertainty

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 300-360 seconds, with heavy defense and moderate economy, while the opponent's posture is unknown.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to offense before your defense is solid, risking vulnerability when the opponent's intentions are unclear.

**Correction:** Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

**Why:** With limited information, balanced development keeps options open. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid base for any transition.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you observe more of the opponent's army composition or production structures, or when you reach a new tech milestone.
