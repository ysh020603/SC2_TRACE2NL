# N001 — Early Game Uncertainty

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own forces are still developing. Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and scouting. Avoid overcommitting to any single strategy until you have more information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's posture is unknown, with light or uncertain production and technology. They could be going for an economic build, an aggressive rush, or something in between.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your forces are in the early stages of development, with no clear army composition yet. Production and technology are light or uncertain, and you have not committed to a specific strategy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and scouting. Avoid overcommitting to any single strategy until you have more information.

In the early game, information is scarce. By keeping your options open and developing your economy, you can adapt to whatever the opponent does without falling behind.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined strategy once you have scouted the opponent's intentions. If the opponent is passive, continue expanding; if they are aggressive, prepare defenses.
