## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid overextending against a heavy ground Terran

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 240-360 game time, with a solid economy and expansion, and enemy intelligence indicating a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, possibly Marines and Reapers.

**Mistake:** Tempting to strengthen your ground army and increase production, economy, and expansion aggressively, but this can lead to engaging Siege Tanks head-on without proper tech or positioning, or over-expanding without sufficient army to defend.

**Correction:** Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your expansion and production.

**Why:** A strong economy and technology base will allow you to outproduce the Terran in the midgame. Ground army strength is important to defend against potential pressure.

**Recheck when:** Recheck if the opponent shows signs of air transitions or heavy pressure.
