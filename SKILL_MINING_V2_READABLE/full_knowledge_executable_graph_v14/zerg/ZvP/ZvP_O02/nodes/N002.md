# N002 — Early-Midgame Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240-360 seconds, both sides are still in early-midgame with limited information. Enemy posture is unknown, and your own development is flexible. Maintain current development path, keep scouting, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues; production and tech are uncertain. They could be teching or expanding quietly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and tech are still developing; no strong army composition has been committed. You have the flexibility to choose a direction.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, keep scouting, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

With limited information, committing early could be punished. Maintaining flexibility allows you to react to the enemy's actual composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and tech while gathering more information to transition into a counter composition.
