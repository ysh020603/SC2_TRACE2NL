# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, Oracles). Your own army is ground-oriented with Zealots. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture and consider adding more tech (e.g., High Templar) for spell support.

### Own cues

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture and consider adding more tech (e.g., High Templar) for spell support.

A ground army with Zealots can be effective, but adding tech like High Templar can provide spell support and counter enemy compositions. Maintaining a solid economy allows for further tech and expansion.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Ground vs Ground with Oracles: Avoid Ignoring Spell Support

**When this applies:** Late-midgame (10-12 minutes), opponent shows ground posture (Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, Oracles), your army is ground-oriented with Zealots.

**Mistake:** Tempting to continue pure ground production without adding spell casters, missing opportunities to counter enemy composition.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening ground army and economy, but add tech like High Templar for spell support and maintain defensive posture.

**Why:** Zealots alone can be countered; High Templar provide spell support and improve composition. Economy allows further tech and expansion.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if opponent transitions to air or if composition needs adaptation.
