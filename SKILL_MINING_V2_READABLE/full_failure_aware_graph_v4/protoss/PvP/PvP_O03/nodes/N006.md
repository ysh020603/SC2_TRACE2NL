# N006 — Late-Midgame Air Integration

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, Oracles). Your own army is ground-oriented but has added Void Rays. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue increasing your economy and maintain a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Zealot, Stalker, Immortal, and Oracle. Their production appears heavy, technology investment heavy, and economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, defense is heavy, and technology investment is heavy. Production is heavy, and your army is ground-oriented with Zealots, Void Rays, and Immortals.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue increasing your economy and maintain a defensive posture.

Adding Void Rays to a ground army provides flexibility and can counter enemy ground forces effectively. This mixed composition can be difficult for the opponent to handle.
