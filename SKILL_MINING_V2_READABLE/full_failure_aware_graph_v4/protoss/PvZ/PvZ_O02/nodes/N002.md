# N002 — Early-Midgame: Continue Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game progresses into the early-midgame with still limited information. The opponent remains unknown, and your own posture is still developing. Maintain current army path, keep production and technology development steady, and prepare to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, keep production and technology development steady, and prepare to adapt based on scouting.

With no information on the opponent's intentions, committing to a specific build is risky. Maintaining flexibility allows you to respond to whatever the opponent reveals.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify powered production and prerequisites before adding tech or combat-unit entries.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a clear direction based on the first scouting information.
