# PvZ_O05 Technology / Defense / Economy

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / defense / economy opening
- Method: Failure-Aware Full V4

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology investment and a defensive posture while building a solid economy. Early game is characterized by moderate production and an unknown army composition, with a focus on teching up and expanding safely.

Develop a technology / defense / economy posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: moderate
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## V4 Failure-Aware Execution Guardrails

Apply these checks before following any strategic direction or matchup-specific lesson.

### G01 — Keep the queue executable

- Rebuild the ordered queue from the live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites.
- Put prerequisite structures before dependent add-ons, technology, upgrades, or units; omit actions whose parent will still be unavailable.
- Prefer a short queue that can begin now. Do not let repeated workers, supply providers, or future tech hide the immediate army-production action.

### G02 — Supply is just-in-time, not a spending plan

- Count completed, pending, and already queued supply together. Add one provider when free supply is at or below 4, or two only when several active production queues will consume the space immediately.
- Never add three or more supply providers in one decision. If supply is already comfortable, spend on workers, production, combat units, or required technology instead.

### G03 — Convert the bank into fighting capacity

- If the combined mineral and gas bank is at least 750 while production is idle or army supply is low, prioritize currently executable production structures and combat units before optional expansion or technology.
- Around 05:00, aim for at least two usable unit-production sources and roughly 10 army supply. Around 06:00, if army supply is below 15, pause optional greed and restore continuous army production.
- When predicted army advantage is OverwhelmingDisadvantage or an owned zone is threatened, army, counters, detection, and production take priority over expansion and nonessential technology until the live comparison improves.

### G04 — Balance workers against survival

- Keep worker production moving toward current base saturation, but do not queue many workers ahead of a missing production facility or urgent defensive units.
- Recheck worker count, ideal workers, income, army supply, production queues, and threat flags every cycle; replace the old queue when the bottleneck changes.

### G05 — Protoss production interpretation

- Scale Gateways or other unit-producing tech only after power, prerequisites, and production capacity are executable.

## V4 Matchup-Specific Corrections

### R01 — Maintain Tech and Defense Tempo

**When:** Early game (before 5:00) with heavy technology investment, moderate production, and heavy defense posture. Own army composition unknown. Enemy shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light technology.

**Correction:** Prioritize completing a Cybernetics Core and a defensive structure (e.g., Shield Battery or Photon Cannon) if prerequisites are met. Queue 2-3 Stalkers or Zealots from Gateways, ensuring Warp Gate research is started if available. Keep worker production continuous toward 2-base saturation (about 32-40 workers). If bank exceeds 400 minerals and 200 gas, add a Gateway or Forge to convert resources into production capacity. Do not expand beyond 2 bases until army supply is at least 15 and production is active.

**Recheck:** At next decision cycle, verify that Cybernetics Core is completed or under construction, at least one defensive structure exists, and Gateway count is at least 2. Ensure worker count is at least 26 by 4:00 and 32 by 5:00. If bank is high, confirm production is not idle.

### R02 — Counter Ground Swarm with Tech and Defense

**When:** Midgame (after 5:00) when enemy intelligence shows a ground-heavy composition with Zerglings, Roaches, or Hydralisks, and moderate to heavy production. Own army is ground-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech.

**Correction:** If enemy has Roaches or Hydralisks, prioritize Immortal or Colossus tech (e.g., Robotics Facility and Robotics Bay) if prerequisites are met. Add 1-2 Shield Batteries at natural and third base if threatened. Queue Zealots and Stalkers to maintain a mixed ground army. If enemy has Mutalisks (air threat), add 2-3 Phoenixes or a Stalker-heavy composition with Blink. Keep worker count at 3-base saturation (about 48-54 workers) and expand to a third base only if army supply is above 15 and production is active. Convert excess bank (over 1000 minerals/gas) into additional Gateways or tech structures.

**Recheck:** At next decision cycle, confirm that appropriate tech structures (Robotics Facility or Stargate) are completed or under construction. Verify army supply is at least 30 by 6:00 and 45 by 8:00. Ensure at least 2 defensive structures at each base if threat flags are high.

### R03 — Recover from Low Army and High Bank

**When:** Any time when army supply is below 15, bank exceeds 1000 minerals and 500 gas, or predicted advantage is OverwhelmingDisadvantage. Also if owned bases are threatened and production is insufficient.

**Correction:** Immediately queue units from all available Gateways, prioritizing Zealots and Stalkers. If Warp Gate is researched, warp in a round of units at a proxy pylon near the threatened base. If production is idle, add Gateways (up to 4-6 total) to convert bank into army. Do not expand or tech further until army supply is at least 30. If enemy has air units, include Stalkers or Phoenixes. If enemy has ground swarm, include Colossi or Immortals if tech is available. Keep workers at current base saturation; do not transfer workers to new bases.

**Recheck:** At next decision cycle, verify that all Gateways are producing or have queued units. Confirm army supply has increased by at least 10 from the previous cycle. If bank remains high, add more production. Reassess threat level and adjust composition accordingly.

## Contrastive Lessons

Use these mistake-to-correction pairs only when the live situation matches.

### L01 — Avoid over-committing to air or neglecting defense

**When:** Early game, own army composition unknown, heavy technology investment, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light technology.

**Mistake → correction:** Tempting to over-commit to air or neglect defense, or expand too greedily without scouting. → Strengthen ground forces, increase defense, continue economy growth, and increase technology. Maintain air and expansion.

**Why:** With an unknown own army and possible early Zergling pressure, prioritizing defense and ground strength while teching up is a safe way to transition into a stronger midgame.

**Read for full checks:** `N001`

### L02 — Avoid being too passive; seek map control

**When:** Early-midgame, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech.

**Mistake → correction:** Tempting to be too passive, missing opportunities to gain map control or punish over-expansion. → Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

**Why:** As the game progresses, stabilizing your defense and economy while continuing tech development prepares you for a strong midgame transition.

**Read for full checks:** `N003`

### L03 — Avoid neglecting air defense if opponent techs to Mutalisks

**When:** Midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, heavy production and tech.

**Mistake → correction:** Tempting to neglect air defense if opponent techs to Mutalisks; also risk over-expanding. → Strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

**Why:** With a defined ground army, focusing on strengthening it while maintaining a solid economy is key to matching the opponent's heavy production.

**Read for full checks:** `N004`
