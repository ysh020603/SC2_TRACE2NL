# N003 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with heavy production and tech. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. Maintain your defensive posture while increasing your production and tech. Continue to expand to support your growing army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Terran ground army with Marines and Reapers. Their production is heavy, and they are also investing in technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-oriented, and you are investing heavily in production and technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture while increasing your production and tech. Continue to expand to support your growing army.

A strong economy and tech advantage will allow you to outproduce the opponent in the mid-game. Your defensive structures buy you time.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid over-extending with expansions or tech

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with heavy production and tech. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers.

**Mistake:** Over-extending with expansions or tech that leaves you vulnerable to early pressure.

**Correction:** Maintain your defensive posture while increasing your production and tech. Continue to expand to support your growing army.

**Why:** A strong economy and tech advantage will allow you to outproduce the opponent in the mid-game. Your defensive structures buy you time.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's tech path or if they attempt a timing attack.
