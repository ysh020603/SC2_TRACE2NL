# N005 — Early Game Ground Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders, and you have a Stalker but need to build your army. Continue to develop your economy and technology, while building a ground army to defend against potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders, moderate production, and heavy technology. They may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army is unknown. You have a Stalker, and you need to decide on your tech path and army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology, while building a ground army to defend against potential pressure.

The opponent's ground army is developing, and you need to have a defensive force ready. Stalkers are versatile and can transition to other tech.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify powered production and prerequisites before adding tech or combat-unit entries.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a solid ground army and a clear tech direction, ready to adapt to the opponent's strategy.
