# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, with a heavy economy and production, facing a Terran ground posture with heavy tech. Increase your economy and production, continue strengthening your ground army, and consider expanding to secure more resources. Maintain your current defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, and their production and tech are heavy. They have a moderate defense and heavy expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and you have a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers. Your expansion is light or uncertain, but you have the resources to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production, continue strengthening your ground army, and consider expanding to secure more resources. Maintain your current defense.

Investing in tech and economy now will give you a strong midgame. The Terran's heavy tech suggests they will have a strong army, so you need to match it.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid over-extending economy without defense

**When this applies:** Early midgame, heavy economy and production, facing Terran ground with heavy tech.

**Mistake:** Over-extending economy without proper defense, leaving you vulnerable to harassment like Reapers.

**Correction:** Increase economy and production, continue strengthening ground army, and consider expanding while maintaining current defense.

**Why:** Investing in tech and economy now gives a strong midgame, and you need to match the Terran's heavy tech.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have secured a third base or detect a change in Terran strategy.
