# N004 — Early Game Tech and Economy Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, with a heavy economy and moderate production, facing a Terran with unknown posture but heavy tech. Focus on increasing your economy and production, while continuing to develop your technology. Maintain your current expansion and prepare for the midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with a Marauder, and their production is moderate but tech is heavy. Their defense and expansion are light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and you have a Zealot. Your expansion is light or uncertain, but you have the potential to expand.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your economy and production, while continuing to develop your technology. Maintain your current expansion and prepare for the midgame.

Building a strong economy and tech foundation early will pay off in the midgame. The Terran's heavy tech suggests they will have strong units, so you need to be prepared.
