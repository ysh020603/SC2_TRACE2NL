# N006 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy defense and moderate economy, while the opponent shows a heavy economy and expansion with a Reaper cue. You need to stabilize and develop. Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper cue, with heavy economy and expansion. Production is moderate, technology is heavy. The opponent may be planning a fast expand or a Reaper harass.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is light or uncertain, technology is heavy, and defense is heavy. Your economy is moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

The opponent's heavy economy and expansion suggest a macro-oriented play. You need to stabilize your defense while developing your own economy and tech.
