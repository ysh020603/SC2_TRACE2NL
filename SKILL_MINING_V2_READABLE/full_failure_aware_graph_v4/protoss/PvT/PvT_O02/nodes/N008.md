- Enemy Intelligence shows a Marine cue, with heavy economy and expansion. Production is moderate, technology is heavy. The opponent may be planning a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is light or uncertain, technology is heavy, and defense is heavy. Your economy is moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground attack. You need to stabilize your defense while developing your own economy and tech.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Stabilize before pushing

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 240 seconds, with heavy defense and moderate economy, while the opponent shows a ground posture with Marine cues.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to offense before your defense is solid, potentially leaving you vulnerable to a ground attack.

**Correction:** Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

**Why:** The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground attack. Stabilizing your defense while developing your economy and tech is prudent.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or if they apply pressure.
