# N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Strength

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600-720 game time, you have a heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors, indicating a strong ground army. Maintain your ground army strength while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider adding more anti-air if the opponent's air presence increases.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army strength while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider adding more anti-air if the opponent's air presence increases.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need a robust ground force to counter. Your Mutalisks provide harassment and map control.
