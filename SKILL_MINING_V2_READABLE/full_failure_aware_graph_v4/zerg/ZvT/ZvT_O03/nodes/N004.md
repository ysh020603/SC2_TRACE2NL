# N004 — Midgame Ground Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420-540 game time, you have a heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Marines and Hellions, indicating potential harassment or early pressure. Maintain your ground army and continue developing your technology. Be prepared to defend against Hellion run-bys or Marine pushes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Marines and Hellions. Exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, giving you a solid ground defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue developing your technology. Be prepared to defend against Hellion run-bys or Marine pushes.

Hellions can be dangerous to workers, so having Roaches and Queens helps defend. Continuing tech will give you more options.
