# N007 — Early Midgame Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, opponent ground posture with Marine/Reaper/Hellion, own Zergling/Queen. Own tech moderate. Strengthen ground, increase economy, continue expansions and production, increase tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with Marine, Reaper, Hellion cues. Heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy, continue expansions and production, increase tech.

As you transition to midgame, you need to start teching to counter opponent's composition. Maintaining economy is key.
