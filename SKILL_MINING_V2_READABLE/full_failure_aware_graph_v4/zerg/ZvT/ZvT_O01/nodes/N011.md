# N011 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, opponent ground posture with Marine, own Zergling/Mutalisk/Queen. Own air presence heavy. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and expansions, increase upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with Marine cues. Heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens. Heavy production and tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and expansions, increase upgrades.

Transitioning to air with Mutalisks can provide map control and harassment. Continue macro to support this.
