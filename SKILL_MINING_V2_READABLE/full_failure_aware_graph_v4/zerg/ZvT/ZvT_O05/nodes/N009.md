# N009 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, consider transitioning to a Mutalisk-based army to harass and control the map. Increase your air presence and use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with moderate technology. Your army includes zerglings, Mutalisks, and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.

Mutalisks are effective against Terran ground armies and can force the opponent to invest in anti-air.
