# N006 — Midgame Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, consider transitioning to a more advanced army composition based on the opponent's tech. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air units if the opponent transitions to Battlecruisers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army includes Hydralisks, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a tech investment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air units if the opponent transitions to Battlecruisers.

The opponent's Battlecruiser presence suggests a potential air transition, so preparing anti-air is wise.
