# N011 — Early-Midgame Tech Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, focus on expanding and teching up to prepare for the midgame. Increase your expansion and production while investing in technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate production, with light technology. Your army consists of zerglings and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion and production while investing in technology.

A strong economy and tech base will allow you to transition into a powerful midgame army.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid over-expanding without defense

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, when you have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and light tech, and the opponent is also ground-oriented with heavy production and tech.

**Mistake:** Focusing solely on expanding and teching up while neglecting army production and defense, leaving you vulnerable to enemy aggression.

**Correction:** Increase your expansion and production while investing in technology, but ensure you maintain a defensive force to protect your bases.

**Why:** A strong economy and tech base will allow you to transition into a powerful midgame army, but only if you survive the early-midgame pressure.

**Recheck when:** Reassess when you have a strong economy and the opponent's tech direction is clear.
