# N009 — Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420 seconds, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Keep your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers and Immortals, heavy production and tech, and heavy defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. Your army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Keep your defense.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need a solid ground composition. Your economy supports further investment.

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
