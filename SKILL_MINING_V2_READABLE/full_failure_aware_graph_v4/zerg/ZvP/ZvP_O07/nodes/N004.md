# N004 — Early-Midgame Economy and Tech Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, but light production and tech. The opponent's posture is unknown, but they have heavy economy and tech. Increase your economy and tech, while maintaining your production and expansion. Keep a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but they have heavy economy and tech, with moderate production. They may be teching up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, but light production and tech. Your army is small, with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech, while maintaining your production and expansion. Keep a defensive posture.

Your economy is strong, so you can invest in tech. The opponent's unknown posture means you should be cautious.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid blind teching without scouting

**When this applies:** Early midgame, around 240 seconds, when you have a heavy economy and expansion but light production and tech, and opponent's posture is unknown.

**Mistake:** Increasing economy and tech without scouting, potentially leaving you unprepared for the opponent's strategy and neglecting army production.

**Correction:** Increase your economy and tech, while maintaining your production and expansion. Keep a defensive posture.

**Why:** Your economy is strong, so you can invest in tech. The opponent's unknown posture means you should be cautious.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have more scouting information.
