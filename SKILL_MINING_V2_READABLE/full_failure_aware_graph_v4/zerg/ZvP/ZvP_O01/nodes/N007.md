## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 6 minutes, with enemy showing Phoenixes and Oracles, indicating an air commitment. Maintain your ground army while preparing anti-air. Consider adding Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers for defense. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a moderate air presence with Phoenixes and Oracles, and is producing heavily. They may be planning harass or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Your production is moderate, and you are starting to tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while preparing anti-air. Consider adding Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers for defense. Continue expanding and teching.

Phoenix and Oracle can harass workers and pick off units. Having anti-air options protects your economy and army.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 6 minutes, when enemy shows Phoenixes and Oracles.

**Correction:** Maintain ground army while adding anti-air like Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers. Continue expanding and teching.
