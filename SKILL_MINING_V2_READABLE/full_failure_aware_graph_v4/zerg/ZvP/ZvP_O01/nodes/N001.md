# N001 — Early Economy and Queen Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with limited enemy information and your own economy expanding. Continue expanding and increasing economy while maintaining a defensive ground posture. Keep producing Queens for defense and creep spread.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is likely expanding and teching, but their army composition is unclear. They may have Zealots and Stalkers, but no confirmed heavy commitment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy with Queens and possibly Zerglings. Your production is moderate and tech is light. You are vulnerable to early pressure but have the foundation for growth.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy while maintaining a defensive ground posture. Keep producing Queens for defense and creep spread.

A strong economy allows you to outproduce the Protoss later. Queens provide defense against early harass and support creep for faster expansion.
