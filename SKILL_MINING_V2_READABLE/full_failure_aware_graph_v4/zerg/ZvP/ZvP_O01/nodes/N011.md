# N011 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, around 8-9 minutes, with enemy having a ground army and you are transitioning to air. Continue building Mutalisks and upgrading them. Use them for harassment and to control the map. Maintain a ground army for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. They are producing heavily and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Your tech is moderate, and you are investing in air upgrades.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building Mutalisks and upgrading them. Use them for harassment and to control the map. Maintain a ground army for defense.

Mutalisks provide mobility and harassment, forcing the enemy to split their army. This can give you an economic advantage.

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.

- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a strong air force that can harass and a ground army to defend.
