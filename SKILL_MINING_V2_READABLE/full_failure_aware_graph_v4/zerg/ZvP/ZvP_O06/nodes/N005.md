# N005 — Mid-Game Tech and Army Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 7 minutes, opponent has a ground army with Immortals. You have a heavy defense and are investing in tech. Continue teching and increase your army production. Consider adding units that counter Immortals, such as Roaches or Hydralisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. They are likely to push soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a heavy defense. You are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and increase your army production. Consider adding units that counter Immortals, such as Roaches or Hydralisks.

Immortals are strong against armored units, so you need a mix of units. Your tech investment will provide upgrades to help in the fight.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Counter Immortal-heavy ground armies

**When this applies:** Midgame, around 7 minutes, when opponent has a ground army with Immortals and you have heavy defense and tech investment.

**Mistake:** Staying on a small ground army and continuing to tech without increasing production, leaving you vulnerable to being overwhelmed.

**Correction:** Continue teching and increase army production. Add units that counter Immortals, such as Roaches or Hydralisks.

**Why:** Immortals are strong against armored units, so you need a mix of units. Your tech investment will provide upgrades to help in the fight.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when your tech is complete or when the opponent attacks.
