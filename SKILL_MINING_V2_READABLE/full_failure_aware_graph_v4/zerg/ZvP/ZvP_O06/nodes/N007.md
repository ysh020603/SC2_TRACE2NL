# N007 — Air Transition Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 8 minutes, opponent transitions to an air-based army with Phoenixes and Warp Prisms. You have a heavy ground defense. Add anti-air units such as Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Consider building Spore Crawlers for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has an air-heavy army with Sentries, Phoenixes, Warp Prisms, and Immortals. They may be planning harass or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a heavy ground defense. You are producing heavily and have heavy tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Add anti-air units such as Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Consider building Spore Crawlers for defense.

The opponent's air army requires anti-air capabilities. Your strong economy can support a tech switch to counter.
