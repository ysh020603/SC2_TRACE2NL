# N010 — Late-Midgame Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. You have a heavy air presence with Mutalisks, Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and heavy production and moderate technology. Increase your air army and continue harassing the opponent. Maintain your ground army for defense. Consider increasing your technology to get better air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. They may be preparing to attack or expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and heavy production. You have Mutalisks, Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and your technology is moderate. Your defense is heavy, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air army and continue harassing the opponent. Maintain your ground army for defense. Consider increasing your technology to get better air units.

With the opponent going heavy on ground, using Mutalisks to harass can disrupt their economy and force them to react. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to sustain a large air force. Overseers provide detection and scouting.
