# N006 — Early-Midgame Aggressive Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and moderate production and light technology. You have a light ground posture with Queens, and moderate production and light technology. Increase your expansion and production to get ahead economically. Continue developing your ground army and technology. Keep your defense light but be ready to react.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and moderate production and light technology. They may be preparing to attack or expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and moderate production. You have Queens, and your technology is light. Your defense is light, so you need to be cautious of aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion and production to get ahead economically. Continue developing your ground army and technology. Keep your defense light but be ready to react.

With the opponent having a moderate army, you can afford to expand aggressively to gain an economic lead. Increasing production allows you to build a larger army later. Maintaining a light defense is acceptable if you scout well.
