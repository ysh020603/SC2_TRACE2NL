# N001 — Early Game Economy and Safety

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players are in a ground macro posture with heavy economy and expansion, light technology, and moderate production. The opponent is likely to have Zerglings and Queens, with possible early pressure. Maintain your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Keep your defense light but be ready to react to early pressure. Continue producing Queens and Zerglings to maintain safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be in a similar ground macro posture with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light technology. They may have Zerglings and Queens, and could apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and moderate production. You have Zerglings and Queens, and your technology is light. Your defense is light, so you need to be cautious of early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Keep your defense light but be ready to react to early pressure. Continue producing Queens and Zerglings to maintain safety.

In ZvZ, early economy is crucial, but you need some army to defend against early Zergling rushes. Queens provide defense and creep spread, while Zerglings offer map presence and scouting. Maintaining a heavy economy sets up a strong midgame.
