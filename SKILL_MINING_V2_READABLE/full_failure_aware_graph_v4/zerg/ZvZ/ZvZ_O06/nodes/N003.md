# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. You have a moderate ground army with Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens, and moderate technology. Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's composition. Continue developing your economy and production. Maintain your defense and consider increasing your technology to get better units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. They may be preparing to attack or expand further.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and moderate production. You have Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens, and your technology is moderate. Your defense is moderate, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's composition. Continue developing your economy and production. Maintain your defense and consider increasing your technology to get better units.

With the opponent going heavy on ground, you need to have a strong ground army to defend and potentially counter-attack. Hydralisks are good against Roaches, and Zerglings provide mobility. Keeping your economy heavy allows you to sustain a large army.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Match opponent's heavy ground with tech and economy

**When this applies:** Midgame, opponent has heavy ground (Zergling, Roach, Queen) with heavy production and tech; you have moderate ground (Zergling, Hydralisk, Queen) and moderate tech.

**Mistake:** Over-investing in a single unit composition without scouting, risking a surprise switch to air or different ground.

**Correction:** Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's composition, continue developing economy and production, and consider increasing technology for better units.

**Why:** A strong ground army defends and counters, while economy sustains it. Hydralisks counter Roaches, Zerglings provide mobility.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when your army is comparable and economy is good. If opponent transitions to air, add anti-air or switch to air.
