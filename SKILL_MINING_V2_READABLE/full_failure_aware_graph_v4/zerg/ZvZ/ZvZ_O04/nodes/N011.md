# N011 — Late Midgame Air and Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, you have air presence with Mutalisk cues, and the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy tech. Continue to increase your air presence and economy, maintain your production and tech, and strengthen both your air and ground armies.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen, and Overseer cues. Their production is heavy, tech heavy, and they are likely to have a strong ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your army includes Mutalisks, Roaches, and Zerglings, giving you a mixed force.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your air presence and economy, maintain your production and tech, and strengthen both your air and ground armies.

A mixed army of Mutalisks and ground units is versatile and can handle various threats. Your heavy economy allows you to sustain this composition.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify larvae, Overlords, bases, and worker-versus-army larva allocation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for a late-game transition, possibly to a more powerful air composition like Brood Lords or a ground composition like Ultralisks.
