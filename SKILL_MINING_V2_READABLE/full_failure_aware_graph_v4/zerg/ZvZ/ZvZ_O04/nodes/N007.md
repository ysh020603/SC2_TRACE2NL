# N007 — Early Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, you have a heavy economy and heavy expansion, while the opponent has a moderate economy. You can afford to be aggressive. Increase your production and economy, continue your expansion, and strengthen your ground army. Consider applying pressure if you have an advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their economy is moderate, production moderate, and tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy expansion, and heavy defense. Your ground army is strong and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and economy, continue your expansion, and strengthen your ground army. Consider applying pressure if you have an advantage.

You have an economic lead, so you can afford to invest in production and army to pressure the opponent or secure a stronger position.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify larvae, Overlords, bases, and worker-versus-army larva allocation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain your economic lead and transition to a stronger army composition.
