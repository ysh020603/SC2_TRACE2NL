# N009 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, the opponent shows air presence with Mutalisk cues, while you have a ground army. You need to adapt. Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and consider adding anti-air units or transitioning to air yourself.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, and Queen cues. Their air presence is heavy, and they are likely to use Mutalisks for harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your ground army is strong with Zergling, Roach, and Queen, but you lack air defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and consider adding anti-air units or transitioning to air yourself.

Mutalisks can be very effective against a ground-only army. You need to either get anti-air units like Hydralisks or transition to your own air force to counter.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify larvae, Overlords, bases, and worker-versus-army larva allocation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a response to the opponent's air presence while maintaining your economy.
