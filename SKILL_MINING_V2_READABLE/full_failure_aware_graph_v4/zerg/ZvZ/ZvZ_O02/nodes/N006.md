# N006 — Balanced Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have scouted the opponent and detected a ground-based posture, but your own economy is moderate. You need to balance army and economy development. Maintain a balanced approach, developing your economy while also strengthening your ground army. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Queens as representative units. The opponent's economy and expansion appear heavy, suggesting a macro-oriented strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with moderate production, but your economy is still developing. You have not committed to a specific army composition, but you are ready to adapt.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced approach, developing your economy while also strengthening your ground army. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

Since the opponent is focusing on economy, you need to keep up economically while also building a defensive army to deter any potential attacks. A balanced approach allows you to adapt to the opponent's actions.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid falling behind in economy

**When this applies:** Early game, opponent has ground posture with Queen, your economy is moderate, and you have light tech.

**Mistake:** Making unnecessary units that could be drones, falling behind economically.

**Correction:** Maintain a balanced approach: develop economy while strengthening ground army. Focus on scouting to gain information.

**Why:** Opponent focuses on economy; you need to keep up economically while building a defensive army to deter attacks. Balanced approach adapts to opponent's actions.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have more information about opponent's army composition or when they make a move. Exit when you have a clear strategic direction.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if the opponent remains passive, or continue developing if they are also macroing. Be ready to adapt if the opponent switches to air or tech.
