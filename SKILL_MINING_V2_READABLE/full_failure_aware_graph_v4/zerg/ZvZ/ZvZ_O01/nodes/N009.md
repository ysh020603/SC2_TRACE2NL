# N009 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground with light air, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech. Zergling, Roach, Queen cues. Increase air, economy, continue tech, maintain production, strengthen air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Zergling, Roach, Queen cues, ground posture with light air, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in midgame with ground macro posture, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech. Defense heavy, expansion heavy. You have air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air, economy, continue tech, maintain production, strengthen air.

If you have air presence and opponent is ground-heavy, transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage.
