# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground army with heavy production/tech, opponent heavy ground with possible air. Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue tech and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy heavy ground army with Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen, heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Hellions, Banshees.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue tech and production.

Maintain ground dominance while adding air flexibility to counter potential tech switches.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Add air flexibility while maintaining ground

**When this applies:** Midgame, own ground army with heavy production and tech, opponent heavy ground with possible air.

**Mistake:** Over-investing in air without proper scouting, or neglecting upgrades.

**Correction:** Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue tech and production.

**Why:** Maintain ground dominance while adding air flexibility to counter potential tech switches.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when opponent tech or air presence changes.
