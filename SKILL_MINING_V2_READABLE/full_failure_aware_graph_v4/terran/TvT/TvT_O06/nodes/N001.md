# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with both players having a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and heavy technology investment. Opponent shows light or uncertain air presence and possible Reaper cues. Continue developing your ground forces and economy while maintaining defensive structures. Increase economy and continue technology upgrades. Keep production steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is moderate, technology is heavy. Representative units include Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground forces and economy while maintaining defensive structures. Increase economy and continue technology upgrades. Keep production steady.

The opponent is likely investing in technology and economy, so matching their macro while maintaining a solid defense is key. Strengthening ground forces prepares for potential early pressure or a transition to midgame.
