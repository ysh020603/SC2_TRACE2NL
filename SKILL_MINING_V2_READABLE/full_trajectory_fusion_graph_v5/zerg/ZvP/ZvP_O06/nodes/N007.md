# N007 — Air Transition Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 8 minutes, opponent transitions to an air-based army with Phoenixes and Warp Prisms. You have a heavy ground defense. Add anti-air units such as Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Consider building Spore Crawlers for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has an air-heavy army with Sentries, Phoenixes, Warp Prisms, and Immortals. They may be planning harass or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a heavy ground defense. You are producing heavily and have heavy tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Add anti-air units such as Hydralisks or Mutalisks. Consider building Spore Crawlers for defense.

The opponent's air army requires anti-air capabilities. Your strong economy can support a tech switch to counter.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, around 7-8 minutes, with a heavy ground defense and tech investment. Opponent may have a ground army with Immortals or transition to air.

**Strategic target:** Continue teching and increase army production. Add units that counter the opponent's composition, such as Roaches or Hydralisks. If opponent transitions to air, add anti-air units like Hydralisks or Mutalisks and consider Spore Crawlers.

**Veto:** Do not stay on a small ground army without increasing production, leaving you vulnerable to being overwhelmed.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when your tech is complete or when the opponent attacks or shows a tech switch.
