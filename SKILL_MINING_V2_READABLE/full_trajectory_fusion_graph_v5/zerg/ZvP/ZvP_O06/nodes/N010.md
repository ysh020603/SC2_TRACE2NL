# N010 — Late Game Carrier Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, opponent transitions to Carriers. You have a heavy ground defense and production. Add anti-air units such as Hydralisks, Corruptors, or Mutalisks. Consider building Spore Crawlers for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Carriers. They are likely to attack with a strong air force.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a heavy ground defense. You are producing heavily and have heavy tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Add anti-air units such as Hydralisks, Corruptors, or Mutalisks. Consider building Spore Crawlers for defense.

Carriers are a powerful air unit, so you need a strong anti-air response. Your economy can support a tech switch.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late midgame, around 10 minutes, with a heavy ground defense and production. Opponent may have a ground army with Observers and Immortals or transition to Carriers.

**Strategic target:** Maintain your army and continue producing. Consider adding upgrades and tech units like Lurkers or Brood Lords. If opponent transitions to Carriers, add anti-air units such as Hydralisks, Corruptors, or Mutalisks and consider Spore Crawlers.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air if opponent transitions to air, leaving you vulnerable to air harassment.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a large army or when the opponent attacks or shows a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a mixed army with anti-air to counter the Carrier threat.
