# N004 — Maintain and Adapt

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 6 minutes, opponent has a ground army. Your economy is strong, but your tech is light. Maintain your current production and defense. Consider adding a few tech structures to prepare for the mid-game.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and heavy production. They are likely to pressure soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a moderate defense. Your tech is light, and you are maintaining your current production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current production and defense. Consider adding a few tech structures to prepare for the mid-game.

Maintaining a stable position allows you to react to the opponent's moves. A light tech investment can be a stepping stone to a stronger army.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early midgame, around 3-5 minutes, with a heavy economy and expansion posture. Opponent is unknown or light, with heavy tech investment.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing your economy and expansion. Maintain a ground army core and keep scouting to detect tech or air transitions.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting or overcommit to a single tech path while opponent's tech is unknown.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a clear read on the opponent's tech path or when you are ready to start your tech transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Keep your economy and army balanced, then transition to a tech choice when you have a clear read.
