# N008 — Early Game Expansion and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with a heavy economy and expansion. Opponent is unknown, but tech-heavy. Continue expanding and teching. Maintain a ground army for safety and scout for tech choices.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but they are investing heavily in technology. No clear army composition yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, but production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and teching. Maintain a ground army for safety and scout for tech choices.

A strong economy and tech lead will give you an advantage in the mid-game. Ground army provides defense against early pressure.
