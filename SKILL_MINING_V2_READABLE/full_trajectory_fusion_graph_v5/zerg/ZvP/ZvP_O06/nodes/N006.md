# N006 — Mid-Game Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 8 minutes, opponent has a ground army with Observers and Immortals. You have a heavy defense and are producing heavily. Consolidate your army and continue producing. Consider adding Hydralisks or Lurkers for anti-ground damage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. They are likely to attack soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a heavy defense. You are producing heavily and have moderate tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consolidate your army and continue producing. Consider adding Hydralisks or Lurkers for anti-ground damage.

The opponent's army is strong, so you need a well-rounded composition. Your heavy production will allow you to outnumber them.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, around 7-8 minutes, with a heavy ground defense and tech investment. Opponent may have a ground army with Immortals or transition to air.

**Strategic target:** Continue teching and increase army production. Add units that counter the opponent's composition, such as Roaches or Hydralisks. If opponent transitions to air, add anti-air units like Hydralisks or Mutalisks and consider Spore Crawlers.

**Veto:** Do not stay on a small ground army without increasing production, leaving you vulnerable to being overwhelmed.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when your tech is complete or when the opponent attacks or shows a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a large, well-upgraded army to counter the opponent's composition.
