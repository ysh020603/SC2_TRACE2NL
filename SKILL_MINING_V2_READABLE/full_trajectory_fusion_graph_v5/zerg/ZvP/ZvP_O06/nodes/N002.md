## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 5 minutes, opponent shows a ground-based army with Zealots and Stalkers. Your economy is strong, and you are expanding. Increase your ground army production and continue teching. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks to counter the ground composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and heavy production. They are likely to apply pressure soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and are expanding. Your army is still ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, but you need to strengthen it to match the opponent's production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your ground army production and continue teching. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks to counter the ground composition.

The opponent's heavy ground army requires a stronger defensive force. Your economy can support a larger army, so it's time to invest in production.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early midgame, around 3-5 minutes, with a heavy economy and expansion posture. Opponent is unknown or light, with heavy tech investment.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing your economy and expansion. Maintain a ground army core and keep scouting to detect tech or air transitions.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting or overcommit to a single tech path while opponent's tech is unknown.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a clear read on the opponent's tech path or when you are ready to start your tech transition.
