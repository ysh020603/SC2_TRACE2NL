# N006 — Early-Midgame Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, enemy shows a heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. Your own army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens. Increase your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and keep scouting to identify the enemy's plan.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is heavily invested in economy and tech, possibly preparing for a tech-heavy composition or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and a ground army core. You can expand further and tech up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and keep scouting to identify the enemy's plan.

Matching the enemy's economy and tech is important to avoid falling behind, while maintaining a defensive ground army.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 240-360 seconds, both sides are still in early-midgame with limited information. Enemy posture is unknown, and your own development is flexible.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current development path, keep scouting, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition without scouting information.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you gain more scouting information about the enemy's army composition or tech path.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
