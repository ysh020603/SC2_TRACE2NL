# N012 — Early-Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate tech. The opponent has an air posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Oracles, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate tech. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and you have a moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

The opponent's air threat requires a response, so you need to prepare anti-air while maintaining your economy.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 300 seconds, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, opponent ground posture with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and production, strengthen ground army, maintain expansion, keep an eye on potential pressure.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting or army production while teching; avoid blind tech without information.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when new opponent units are seen or if a tech switch is detected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air, while maintaining your economy.
