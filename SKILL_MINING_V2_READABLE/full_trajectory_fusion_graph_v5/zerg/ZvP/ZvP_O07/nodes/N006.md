# N006 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Maintain your economy, production, and tech, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Keep your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, High Templars, Observers, and Archons, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. Your army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy, production, and tech, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Keep your defense.

The opponent's army is strong, so you need a solid ground composition. Your economy supports continued investment.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 600 seconds, heavy economy, production, and expansion, heavy tech, opponent ground posture with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Maintain economy, production, and tech, continue strengthening ground army, keep defense.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air if opponent transitions to air; avoid overcommitting to ground without scouting.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if air units or a tech switch are seen.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army with better tech, while maintaining your economy.
