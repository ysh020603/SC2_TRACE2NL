# N008 — Midgame Air Pressure and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 540 seconds, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. The opponent has an air posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech, and heavy defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. Your army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

The opponent's air threat requires a response, so you need to prepare anti-air while maintaining your economy.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid neglecting ground defense against air-heavy opponent

**When this applies:** Midgame, around 540 seconds, when you have a heavy economy, production, expansion, and tech, and opponent has an air posture with heavy production and tech.

**Mistake:** Continuing to strengthen ground army and expand without adding anti-air, leaving you vulnerable to the opponent's air units.

**Correction:** Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

**Why:** The opponent's air threat requires a response, so you need to prepare anti-air while maintaining your economy.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's air composition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 480 seconds, heavy economy, production, and expansion, moderate tech, opponent ground posture with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground army, continue developing economy and tech, maintain defense.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground without anti-air if opponent shows air units; avoid neglecting defense.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if air units or a tech switch are seen.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air, while maintaining your economy.
