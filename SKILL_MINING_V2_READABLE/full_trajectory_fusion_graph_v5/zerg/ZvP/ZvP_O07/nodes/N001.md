# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light tech. The opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Continue developing your economy and production, strengthen your ground army, and maintain your expansion. Keep an eye on potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and a moderate defense. They are likely expanding and teching up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, and a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light, but you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production, strengthen your ground army, and maintain your expansion. Keep an eye on potential pressure.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to invest in army and tech. The opponent's ground posture means you should prepare for a ground engagement.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 300 seconds, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, opponent ground posture with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and production, strengthen ground army, maintain expansion, keep an eye on potential pressure.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting or army production while teching; avoid blind tech without information.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when new opponent units are seen or if a tech switch is detected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with more tech, while maintaining your economy.
