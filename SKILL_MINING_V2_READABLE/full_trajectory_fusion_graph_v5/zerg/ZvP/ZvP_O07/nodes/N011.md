# N011 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy tech. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your economy and tech, while continuing to develop your production and expansion. Strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy tech. Your army includes Roaches and Queens, and you have a moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech, while continuing to develop your production and expansion. Strengthen your ground army.

Your tech is heavy, so you can leverage it to gain an advantage. The opponent's ground posture requires a strong ground response.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 300 seconds, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, opponent ground posture with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and production, strengthen ground army, maintain expansion, keep an eye on potential pressure.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting or army production while teching; avoid blind tech without information.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when new opponent units are seen or if a tech switch is detected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more tech-heavy army while maintaining your economy.
