# N005 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, around 7-8 minutes, with enemy having a ground army including Sentries. Consolidate your ground army and tech. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks for durability. Maintain defense and continue expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. They are producing heavily and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate production. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you are starting to tech up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consolidate your ground army and tech. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks for durability. Maintain defense and continue expanding.

A balanced ground army with tech support can handle the enemy's composition. Keeping your economy strong ensures you can sustain losses.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, around 7-9 minutes, with enemy having a heavy ground army including Sentries and Observers, or transitioning to air.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army and tech, adding Roaches or Hydralisks for anti-armor. Maintain map control and prepare for a decisive battle. If enemy transitions to air, consider adding anti-air or transitioning to Mutalisks.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air if enemy shows air units; avoid overextending without map control, and do not ignore economy while building army.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a significant army advantage, when enemy makes a tech switch, or when you have a sufficient air force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a strong, well-rounded army ready to defend or attack.
