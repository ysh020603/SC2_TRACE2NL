# N012 — Late-Mid Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with enemy having a ground army and you have a strong air force. Continue using Mutalisks to harass expansions and force the enemy to react. Maintain a strong ground army for defense. Consider adding Corruptors if the enemy transitions to air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. They are producing heavily and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Your tech is heavy, and you are using air for harassment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue using Mutalisks to harass expansions and force the enemy to react. Maintain a strong ground army for defense. Consider adding Corruptors if the enemy transitions to air.

Mutalisk harassment can disrupt the enemy's economy and force them to make inefficient decisions. This gives you a strategic advantage.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with enemy having heavy ground or air presence, and you have a strong economy.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army and tech, adding Roaches or Hydralisks for anti-armor. If enemy has heavy air, transition to air units like Mutalisks or Corruptors to counter. Maintain map control and prepare for a decisive battle.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single composition without scouting; avoid neglecting anti-air if enemy air is present, and do not ignore defense while teching.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a significant army advantage, when enemy makes a tech switch, or when you have a sufficient air force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air superiority and economic advantage while preparing for a final push.
