# N004 — Early-Mid Expansion and Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with enemy showing a ground army and you are expanding. Continue expanding and increasing production. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and possibly Roaches. Maintain scouting to detect any tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and is producing heavily. They may be planning an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding further. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing production. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and possibly Roaches. Maintain scouting to detect any tech switches.

Expanding gives you a long-term economic advantage. A strong ground army deters aggression and allows you to take map control.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, around 4-6 minutes, with enemy showing ground units (Zealots, Stalkers) or unknown posture, and you are expanding.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army with Zerglings and possibly Roaches, continue expanding and increasing production, and maintain scouting to detect tech switches. If enemy shows air (Phoenix/Oracle), prepare anti-air with Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single composition without scouting; avoid neglecting anti-air if enemy air is detected, and do not ignore defense while expanding.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see enemy tech buildings, a large army movement, or more air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base and have a solid army to defend it.
