# N012 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro vs Air Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and a heavy economy. The opponent shows an air posture with Oracles. Maintain your current production and economy, and continue developing your ground army. Keep your technology at current levels.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Oracles. They have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground army, heavy economy, and moderate production. Your technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current production and economy, and continue developing your ground army. Keep your technology at current levels.

The opponent's air presence is light, so you can focus on your ground army and economy while preparing for potential air threats.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 240-360 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and a heavy economy. The opponent may show a ground posture with Zealots and Sentries, or an unknown posture with heavy economy and expansions.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army while increasing your economy. Maintain your current expansion and production, and continue technology development.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting for air transitions or heavy pressure while focusing on economy.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if the opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger ground army and economy, with flexibility to tech into anti-air if needed.
