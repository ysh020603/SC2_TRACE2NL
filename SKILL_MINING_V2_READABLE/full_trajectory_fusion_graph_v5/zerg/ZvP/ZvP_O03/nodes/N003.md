- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. They have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong ground army, heavy economy, and heavy production and technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and technology, continue expanding and producing. Strengthen your ground army further.

The opponent's composition with Warp Prisms suggests potential for harass or warp-in tactics. A strong economy and tech will allow you to outproduce and tech into answers.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 420-540 seconds, you have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and technology. The opponent may show a ground posture with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms, or an air posture with Void Rays and Oracles.

**Strategic target:** Increase your economy and technology, continue expanding and producing. Strengthen your ground army further.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground-only forces without preparing anti-air if the opponent shows air units.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if the opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
