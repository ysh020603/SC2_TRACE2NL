# N001 — Early Economy and Ground Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 180 seconds, with both players expanding and investing in economy. Opponent's tech is heavy but army composition is unknown. Continue developing your economy and ground forces. Maintain production and consider increasing technology to keep pace with the opponent's heavy tech investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is also expanding heavily with a heavy economy. Their production is moderate, but technology investment is heavy. No combat units have been reliably observed, so their army composition is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-oriented with moderate production and light technology. Defense is light, but you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and ground forces. Maintain production and consider increasing technology to keep pace with the opponent's heavy tech investment.

The opponent's heavy tech investment suggests they may be teching to a specific composition. By maintaining a strong economy and ground army, you can adapt to whatever they produce while staying ahead economically.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's tech direction becomes clearer.
