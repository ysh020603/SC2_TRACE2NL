# N007 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame around 360 seconds, with the opponent showing an air presence including Phoenixes and Oracles. You have a ground army and heavy technology. Continue to invest in technology and production. Consider adding anti-air units and expanding further to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a moderate air presence with Phoenixes and Oracles, supported by Stalkers. Their production is heavy and technology is heavy, indicating an air-oriented strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and you are expanding. You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and heavy technology. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to invest in technology and production. Consider adding anti-air units and expanding further to support a larger army.

With heavy technology, you can access advanced units to counter the opponent's air. Expanding and increasing production will give you the resources to field a strong army.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame around 240 seconds, with both players still expanding. You have Zerglings and Queens, while the opponent remains unknown.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army and continue economic growth. Maintain production and consider adding more tech to match the opponent's heavy investment.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single unit composition or neglect scouting, leaving you unprepared for the opponent's heavy tech investment.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when the opponent's first combat units are seen or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a tech-heavy army that can counter the opponent's air and ground forces.
