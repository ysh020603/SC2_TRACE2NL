## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

- Enemy Intelligence shows air posture with Stalkers, Sentries, Phoenixes, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings, Infestors, Queens, Overseers, heavy economy and tech.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and tech, consider adding anti-air units.

Phoenixes can harass, so anti-air is needed to defend.
