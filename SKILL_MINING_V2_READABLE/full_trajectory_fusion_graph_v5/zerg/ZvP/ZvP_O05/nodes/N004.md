## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and defense, continue ground development.

Queens provide defense and creep, supporting a safe expansion.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and ground forces, increase production and expansions.

**Veto:** Do not over-commit to a specific tech path or army composition without more information, neglecting scouting or defensive positioning.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when opponent's first combat units appear or when tech advantage is established.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
