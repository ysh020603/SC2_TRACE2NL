# N010 — Midgame Ground vs Ground with Medivac

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground posture with SiegeTank/Marine/Reaper/Medivac, own Zergling/Queen. Own tech moderate. Strengthen ground, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper, Medivac cues. Heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, moderate tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

Medivac presence indicates potential drops or bio pushes. Maintain a strong ground army and consider anti-air.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, opponent ground posture (SiegeTank/Marine/Reaper/Marauder), own Zergling/Hydralisk/Queen/Overseer.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech without maintaining army size; keep scouting for air transitions.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
