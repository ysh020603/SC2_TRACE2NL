# N009 — Late Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, opponent ground posture with Marine, own Zergling/Queen. Both heavy economy and tech. Strengthen ground, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with Marine cues. Heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production and tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

Maintain a solid defense while continuing to macro. Opponent may be preparing a push.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late midgame, opponent ground posture (SiegeTank/Marine/Reaper/Hellion), own Zergling/Queen, both heavy economy and tech.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue expansions and production, increase tech.

**Veto:** Do not neglect defense while teching; ensure scouting to avoid being caught off guard.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when opponent tech or army composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition, possibly with Brood Lords or Ultralisks.
