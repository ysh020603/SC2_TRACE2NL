# N002 — Standard Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, similar to N001 but with slightly different expansion timing. Opponent ground posture, own Zergling/Queen. Strengthen ground, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with Marine/Reaper cues, moderate production, heavy tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

This is a common path where you maintain a balanced macro approach. Keeping expansions and tech steady supports a strong midgame.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Balanced Macro with Ground Focus

**When this applies:** Early game, opponent ground posture (Marine/Reaper), own Zergling/Queen, moderate production and light tech.

**Mistake:** Being too passive and letting the Terran dictate the pace, neglecting army production while expanding.

**Correction:** Strengthen ground army, increase economy, maintain expansions and production, continue tech.

**Why:** Maintaining a balanced macro approach supports a strong midgame.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when opponent tech or army composition changes.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, opponent ground posture (Marine/Reaper) or unknown, own Zergling/Queen, economy heavy.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue tech, maintain defense.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to expansions without maintaining army production and scouting for early pressure.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when opponent shows air presence or tech switch, or when you reach a new tech tier.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition, possibly adding tech units.
