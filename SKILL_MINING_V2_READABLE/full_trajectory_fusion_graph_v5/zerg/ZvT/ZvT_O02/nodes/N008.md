# N008 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent reveals a ground posture with heavy production and economy. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy economy and expansion. Strengthen your ground army, increase economy and expansion, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with representative cue Marine. The opponent's production and economy are heavy, and expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, expansion is heavy, and production is moderate. You have a ground-oriented army with Zergling and Queen cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase economy and expansion, and continue technology development.

The opponent is investing heavily in ground forces, so it is wise to strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and expansion.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Balance tech and army production

**When this applies:** Midgame, opponent has heavy ground production (e.g., Marines) and you have a strong economy but moderate production.

**Mistake:** Neglecting army production while teching, or over-expanding if an attack is imminent.

**Correction:** Strengthen your ground army, increase economy and expansion, and continue technology development.

**Why:** The opponent is investing heavily in ground forces, so you need a strong army to defend while maintaining your economy.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or expansion.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own economy and production remain light or uncertain. If the opponent reveals a ground posture with heavy production and economy, consider strengthening your ground army.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current development path with safety checks. Continue focusing on economy and expansion. If the opponent reveals a ground posture, consider strengthening your ground army.

**Veto:** Do not over-expand without sufficient army to defend, especially if the opponent shows heavy ground production.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and production, and prepare to transition to a more concrete strategy once the opponent's posture is revealed.
