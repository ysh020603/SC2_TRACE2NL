# N003 — Midgame Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own economy and production remain light or uncertain. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Continue focusing on economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production, technology, and expansion remain light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are still light or uncertain. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Continue focusing on economy and expansion.

The opponent's intentions are still unclear, so it is prudent to continue building a strong economy and production capacity while remaining flexible.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own economy and production remain light or uncertain. If the opponent reveals a ground posture with heavy production and economy, consider strengthening your ground army.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current development path with safety checks. Continue focusing on economy and expansion. If the opponent reveals a ground posture, consider strengthening your ground army.

**Veto:** Do not over-expand without sufficient army to defend, especially if the opponent shows heavy ground production.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and production, and prepare to transition to a more concrete strategy once the opponent's posture is revealed.
