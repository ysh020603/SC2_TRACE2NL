# N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Strength

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600-720 game time, you have a heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors, indicating a strong ground army. Maintain your ground army strength while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider adding more anti-air if the opponent's air presence increases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors. Air presence is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, giving you some air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army strength while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider adding more anti-air if the opponent's air presence increases.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need a robust ground force to counter. Your Mutalisks provide harassment and map control.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, around 600-720 game time, with a heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors, indicating a strong ground army.

**Strategic target:** Maintain your ground army strength while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider adding more anti-air if the opponent's air presence increases.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground forces without considering potential air transitions, or neglect anti-air if the opponent adds air units.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if the opponent transitions to heavy air or shows signs of a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for late-game engagements with a balanced army and strong economy.
