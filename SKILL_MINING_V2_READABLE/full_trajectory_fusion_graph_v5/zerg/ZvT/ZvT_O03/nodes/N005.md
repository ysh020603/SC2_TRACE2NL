# N005 — Early Game Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180-240 game time, you have a heavy economy and are expanding. Enemy intelligence is uncertain, with no clear combat units observed, but production and technology appear heavy. Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue developing your technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, with no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology appear heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army is minimal, with only Queens for defense, and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue developing your technology.

A strong economy early will pay off later. Since the opponent's intentions are unclear, it's safe to focus on macro.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid overcommitting to army when enemy is unknown

**When this applies:** Early game, around 180-240 game time, with a heavy economy and expansion, and enemy intelligence uncertain with no clear combat units observed, but production and technology appear heavy.

**Mistake:** Tempting to strengthen ground army and increase production, economy, and expansion, but this can overcommit to army production early, slowing your economy, and neglect scouting for early pressure or all-ins.

**Correction:** Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue developing your technology.

**Why:** A strong economy early will pay off later. Since the opponent's intentions are unclear, it's safe to focus on macro.

**Recheck when:** Recheck as soon as more enemy information is available.
