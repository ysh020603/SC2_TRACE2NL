- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues include Marines and Reapers, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens, and your production and technology are moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your expansion and production.

A strong economy and technology base will allow you to outproduce the Terran in the midgame. Ground army strength is important to defend against potential pressure.
