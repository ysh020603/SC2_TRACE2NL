# N009 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, consider transitioning to a Mutalisk-based army to harass and control the map. Increase your air presence and use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with moderate technology. Your army includes zerglings, Mutalisks, and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.

Mutalisks are effective against Terran ground armies and can force the opponent to invest in anti-air.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Entering the midgame, focus on increasing technology and production to match the opponent's heavy investment.

**Strategic target:** Increase technology and production to match opponent. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army. Consider adding Hydralisks or Roaches to counter ground forces.

**Veto:** Do not over-expand without defense; avoid neglecting army production while teching up.

**Live recheck:** Reassess when you have a solid army composition and the opponent's tech direction is clear.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a Mutalisk-based army that can harass and control the map.
