# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, continue expanding and increasing production. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production. Maintain your expansion rate and start investing more in technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's ground posture is confirmed with Marines and Reapers, and their production has increased to heavy. Technology remains heavy, suggesting possible tech units like Siege Tanks or Medivacs.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a moderate production of zerglings and queens. Technology is still light, but you are starting to invest more.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production. Maintain your expansion rate and start investing more in technology.

Matching the opponent's production while maintaining a strong economy sets you up for a powerful midgame. Technology investment now pays off later.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** As the game progresses into the early-midgame, continue expanding and increasing production.

**Strategic target:** Continue to strengthen ground army and increase production. Maintain expansion rate and start investing more in technology.

**Veto:** Do not over-invest in tech at the expense of army size and scouting; avoid passivity that lets Terran dictate pace.

**Live recheck:** Reassess when you have a strong army and the opponent's tech choices are more apparent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army and prepare for potential tech transitions.
