# N006 — Midgame Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, consider transitioning to a more advanced army composition based on the opponent's tech. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air units if the opponent transitions to Battlecruisers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army includes Hydralisks, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a tech investment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air units if the opponent transitions to Battlecruisers.

The opponent's Battlecruiser presence suggests a potential air transition, so preparing anti-air is wise.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Entering the midgame, focus on increasing technology and production to match the opponent's heavy investment.

**Strategic target:** Increase technology and production to match opponent. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army. Consider adding Hydralisks or Roaches to counter ground forces.

**Veto:** Do not over-expand without defense; avoid neglecting army production while teching up.

**Live recheck:** Reassess when you have a solid army composition and the opponent's tech direction is clear.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile army that can handle both ground and air threats.
