# N005 — Midgame Army Composition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, focus on building a balanced army composition to counter the opponent's ground forces. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding units like Hydralisks or Roaches to counter the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army includes zerglings and queens, and you are well expanded.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and consider adding units like Hydralisks or Roaches to counter the opponent's composition.

A diverse army composition is key to dealing with Terran ground forces, especially Siege Tanks and Medivac support.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Entering the midgame, focus on increasing technology and production to match the opponent's heavy investment.

**Strategic target:** Increase technology and production to match opponent. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army. Consider adding Hydralisks or Roaches to counter ground forces.

**Veto:** Do not over-expand without defense; avoid neglecting army production while teching up.

**Live recheck:** Reassess when you have a solid army composition and the opponent's tech direction is clear.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army that can engage the opponent effectively.
