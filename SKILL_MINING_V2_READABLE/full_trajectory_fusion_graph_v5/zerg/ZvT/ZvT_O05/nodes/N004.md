# N004 — Late-Midgame Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, focus on strengthening your army and maintaining your economy. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase technology. Maintain your expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their production and technology are heavy, indicating a powerful army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army consists of zerglings and queens, and you are well expanded.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase technology. Maintain your expansion and production.

A strong army is necessary to counter the opponent's heavy ground forces. Technology upgrades will give you an edge in engagements.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the late-midgame, focus on strengthening your army and maintaining your economy.

**Strategic target:** Continue to strengthen ground army and increase technology. Maintain expansion and production. Consider Hydralisk-based army and upgrades.

**Veto:** Do not over-invest in tech at the expense of army size; avoid neglecting scouting and defense.

**Live recheck:** Reassess when you have a strong army and the opponent's composition is known.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful army capable of taking engagements.
