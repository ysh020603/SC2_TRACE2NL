## Summary

At around 6 minutes, both players are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented posture. Enemy intelligence suggests a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Your own forces are similarly ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you have a strong economy and expansion. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain your current production and technology levels, while keeping an eye on potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy intelligence indicates a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. They have a heavy economy and expansion, suggesting a macro-oriented approach.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. You are in a safe development phase, but should be prepared for potential pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain your current production and technology levels, while keeping an eye on potential threats.

Both players are in a similar macro-oriented state. Continuing to develop your economy and ground forces will keep you competitive and allow you to respond to enemy moves.
