# N005 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, the game is in the late-midgame. Enemy intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your own army has transitioned to include Mutalisks, indicating an air component. Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your air army. Maintain your economy and production to support the transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and heavy technology. Their army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers. You have a mix of ground and air forces.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your air army. Maintain your economy and production to support the transition.

Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage if the enemy is heavily ground-based. Mutalisks can harass and provide map control.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, enemy ground posture with heavy production and moderate-to-heavy tech, your army may include air units.

**Strategic target:** Consolidate ground army and continue upgrades; if air transitioned, increase air presence and maintain economy/production.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air defense if enemy has air; avoid overcommitting to one composition without scouting.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if enemy tech switches to air or if a large engagement is imminent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a strong air-based army while maintaining a solid ground defense.
