# N008 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. You have a heavy air presence with Mutalisks, Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and heavy production and moderate technology. Increase your air army and continue developing your economy. Maintain your ground army for defense. Consider increasing your technology to get better air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. They may be preparing to attack or expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and heavy production. You have Mutalisks, Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and your technology is moderate. Your defense is moderate, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air army and continue developing your economy. Maintain your ground army for defense. Consider increasing your technology to get better air units.

With the opponent going heavy on ground, transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage. Mutalisks are mobile and can harass, while also being effective against ground units. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to sustain a large air force.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. You have a moderate ground army with Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens, and moderate technology.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's composition. Continue developing your economy and production. Maintain your defense and consider increasing your technology to get better units.

**Veto:** Do not over-invest in a single unit composition without scouting; maintain flexibility to adapt to opponent's tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when your army is comparable to the opponent's and you have a good economy. If the opponent transitions to air, consider adding anti-air or switching to air yourself.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late-midgame with a strong air army and heavy production, ready to either continue air or transition back to ground if needed.
