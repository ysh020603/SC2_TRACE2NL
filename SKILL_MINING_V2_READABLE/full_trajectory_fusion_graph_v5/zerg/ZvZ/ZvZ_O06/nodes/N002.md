- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and moderate production. Your technology is now moderate, and you have Zerglings and Queens. Your defense is light, so you need to be cautious of aggression.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production. Maintain your ground army and consider increasing your technology. Keep your defense light but be ready to react to pressure.

As the game progresses, you need to start teching to stay ahead. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to afford tech structures and units. Keeping a moderate army ensures you can defend while you tech.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Balance economy and tech with defense

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, both players are on ground with moderate production and tech, and you have a strong economy.

**Mistake:** Focusing solely on economy and neglecting army production, leaving you vulnerable to a sudden attack.

**Correction:** Continue developing economy and production, maintain a moderate ground army, and start teching up. Keep defense light but be ready to react to pressure.

**Why:** Teching early keeps you ahead, while a moderate army deters attacks and allows safe tech investment.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have a few tech structures started and economy is still strong. If opponent goes air, prepare anti-air.
