# N010 — Late-Midgame Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. You have a heavy air presence with Mutalisks, Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and heavy production and moderate technology. Increase your air army and continue harassing the opponent. Maintain your ground army for defense. Consider increasing your technology to get better air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. They may be preparing to attack or expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and heavy production. You have Mutalisks, Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and your technology is moderate. Your defense is heavy, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air army and continue harassing the opponent. Maintain your ground army for defense. Consider increasing your technology to get better air units.

With the opponent going heavy on ground, using Mutalisks to harass can disrupt their economy and force them to react. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to sustain a large air force. Overseers provide detection and scouting.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the late-midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and moderate technology. You have a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and heavy production and moderate technology.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your heavy production. Consider increasing your technology to get better units like Roaches or Hydralisks. Keep your defense strong.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air if the opponent transitions to air; maintain a balanced composition to avoid being caught off guard.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a strong army and economy. If the opponent transitions to air, consider adding anti-air or switching to air yourself.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late game with a strong air army and heavy production, ready to either continue air or transition back to ground if needed.
