# N003 — Aggressive Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Opponent Queen cues, pressure possible. Increase economy, expansion, and production, continue tech, strengthen ground.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Queen cues, ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Defense light, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, expansion, and production, continue tech, strengthen ground.

With both sides on similar paths, pushing economy and expansion can give you a macro lead if you can defend against potential pressure.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Opponent shows Queen cues, pressure possible.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue tech, maintain defense and expansion.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to economy at the expense of early defense; ensure sufficient army to respond to pressure.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy and larger ground army.
