# N010 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent ground with light air, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech. Zergling, Queen cues. Increase air, economy, continue tech, maintain production, strengthen air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Zergling, Queen cues, ground posture with light air, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in late-midgame with ground macro posture, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech. Defense heavy, expansion heavy. You have air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air, economy, continue tech, maintain production, strengthen air.

With air presence and opponent ground-heavy, continuing to build air can maintain your advantage.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, opponent ground with heavy air presence, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech. Zergling, Mutalisk, Roach, Queen cues.

**Strategic target:** Increase defense, economy, continue production and tech, strengthen ground.

**Veto:** Do not ignore air threats; ensure anti-air capabilities while maintaining ground strength.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if opponent commits to air or ground.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger air force while maintaining economy.
