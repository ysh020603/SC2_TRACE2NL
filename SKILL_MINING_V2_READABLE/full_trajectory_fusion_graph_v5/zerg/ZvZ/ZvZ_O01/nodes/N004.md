# N004 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both sides ground, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech. Zergling and Queen cues. Maintain ground strength, economy, production, tech, and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Zergling and Queen cues, ground posture, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in midgame with ground macro posture, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech. Defense moderate, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength, economy, production, tech, and expansion.

In a mirror matchup, maintaining a strong ground army and economy is key to staying even and ready for tech transitions.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, both sides ground, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech. Zergling and Queen cues.

**Strategic target:** Maintain ground strength, economy, production, tech, and expansion.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech or air without confirming opponent's air presence; keep ground defense solid.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if opponent techs to air or shows heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to late-midgame with a strong ground army.
