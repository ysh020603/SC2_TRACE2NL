# N005 — Ground-Oriented Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have scouted the opponent and detected a ground-based posture, with cues such as Queens. The opponent appears to be focusing on economy and expansion. Strengthen your ground army and increase your defenses. Continue expanding your economy to match the opponent's growth.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Queens as representative units. The opponent's economy and expansion appear heavy, suggesting a macro-oriented strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings, and your production is moderate. Your economy is still developing, but you are in a good position to expand.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your defenses. Continue expanding your economy to match the opponent's growth.

Since the opponent is focusing on economy, you need to keep up economically while also building a defensive army to deter any potential attacks. A ground army is appropriate given the opponent's ground posture.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At the start of the game, both players have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own forces are still developing.

**Strategic target:** Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and scouting. Avoid overcommitting to any single strategy until you have more information.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single strategy without scouting information; avoid neglecting economy or scouting.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have more scouting information, such as seeing the opponent's first units or expansions. Exit this node when you have a clearer picture of the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if the opponent remains passive, or continue developing if they are also macroing. Be ready to adapt if the opponent switches to air or tech.
