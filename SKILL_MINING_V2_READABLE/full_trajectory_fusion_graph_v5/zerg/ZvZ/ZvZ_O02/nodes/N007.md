# N007 — Midgame Ground Confrontation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-mid game, you have scouted the opponent and detected a ground-based posture with Zerglings. The opponent's economy and expansion are heavy, indicating a macro-oriented strategy. Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider expanding further to secure a long-term advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings as representative units. The opponent's economy and expansion appear heavy, suggesting a macro-oriented strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, and your economy is heavy. You are in a strong position to expand further or prepare for a confrontation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider expanding further to secure a long-term advantage.

Since both you and the opponent are focusing on economy and ground forces, the game may come down to who has the better economy and army composition. By continuing to develop, you can gain an advantage.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid premature tech investment

**When this applies:** Early-mid game, opponent has ground posture with Zerglings, heavy economy, and you have moderate production and light tech.

**Mistake:** Investing in unnecessary technology that delays army production.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy. Consider expanding further to secure long-term advantage.

**Why:** Both sides focus on economy and ground forces; the better economy and army composition will prevail.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have more information about opponent's army composition or when they make a move. Exit when you have a clear strategic direction.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** As the game progresses into the early-mid game, you still have limited information about the opponent, but your own economy and production are starting to take shape.

**Strategic target:** Continue to develop your economy and production while keeping your army composition flexible. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

**Veto:** Do not neglect scouting or overcommit to a single army composition without information.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have more scouting information or when the opponent's actions become clearer. Exit this node when you have a defined strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if you have an army advantage, or continue developing if the game is even. Be ready to adapt if the opponent switches to air or tech.
