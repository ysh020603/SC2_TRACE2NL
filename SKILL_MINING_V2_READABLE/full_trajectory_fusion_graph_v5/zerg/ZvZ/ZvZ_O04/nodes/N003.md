# N003 — Early Defense and Economy Boost

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, you have a moderate economy and moderate expansion, while the opponent has a heavy economy. You need to stabilize and develop. Increase your economy and defense, continue your expansion, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize before pushing out.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their economy is heavy, production moderate, and tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate and your expansion is moderate, but your defense is heavy. You have a ground army and moderate production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and defense, continue your expansion, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize before pushing out.

You are slightly behind in economy, so you need to catch up while ensuring you are safe from early pressure. A heavy defense buys time to expand.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, both players are on a ground macro posture with heavy economies. Opponent shows light or uncertain tech and moderate production.

**Strategic target:** Maintain your ground army and economy, strengthen your ground forces, and keep your defense solid. Continue developing with safety checks.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to economy at the expense of scouting or defense; avoid neglecting safety checks for early attacks.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see the opponent's tech or production increase, or if you scout an early attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach an equal or better economy while maintaining a solid defense.
