# N005 — Midgame Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, you have a heavy economy and heavy production, but the opponent shows heavy tech and a ground posture with Roach cues. Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize and prepare for a potential engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, and Queen cues. Their production is heavy, tech heavy, and they are likely to have a strong army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your ground army is strong and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize and prepare for a potential engagement.

The opponent has heavy tech, so you need to ensure your army composition can handle theirs. Increasing defense and economy gives you the resources to tech up and outproduce them.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, both players have heavy economies and heavy production. Opponent shows moderate tech and ground posture.

**Strategic target:** Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and production, and keep your defense up. Consider adding tech when safe.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech without sufficient army; avoid neglecting scouting for tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see the opponent's tech or army composition change, or if you plan to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you can match or counter the opponent's tech and army.
