# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, you have air presence with Mutalisk cues, while the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy tech. Increase your air presence and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your air army. Use Mutalisks for harassment while building a ground defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen, and Overseer cues. Their production is heavy, tech heavy, and they are likely to have a strong ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and moderate tech. Your army includes Mutalisks, giving you air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your air army. Use Mutalisks for harassment while building a ground defense.

Mutalisks can harass the opponent's economy and force them to react. Your heavy economy supports this playstyle, and you can transition to a more powerful air composition later.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, both players have heavy economies and heavy production. Opponent shows moderate tech and ground posture.

**Strategic target:** Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and production, and keep your defense up. Consider adding tech when safe.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech without sufficient army; avoid neglecting scouting for tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see the opponent's tech or army composition change, or if you plan to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air superiority while building a solid ground army to defend.
