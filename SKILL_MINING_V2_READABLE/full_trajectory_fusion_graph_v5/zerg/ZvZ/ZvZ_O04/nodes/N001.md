# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players are on a ground macro posture with heavy economies. Opponent shows light or uncertain tech and moderate production. Maintain your ground army and economy, strengthen your ground forces, and keep your defense solid. Continue developing with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their economy is heavy, production moderate, and tech light or uncertain. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and a heavy defense posture, with moderate production and light tech. Your army is ground-oriented and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy, strengthen your ground forces, and keep your defense solid. Continue developing with safety checks.

A heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent if they commit to an early attack. Maintaining a strong defense while expanding ensures you can safely reach the midgame.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, both players are on a ground macro posture with heavy economies. Opponent shows light or uncertain tech and moderate production.

**Strategic target:** Maintain your ground army and economy, strengthen your ground forces, and keep your defense solid. Continue developing with safety checks.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to economy at the expense of scouting or defense; avoid neglecting safety checks for early attacks.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see the opponent's tech or production increase, or if you scout an early attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a strong economy and a solid ground army, ready to adapt.
