# N009 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, the opponent shows air presence with Mutalisk cues, while you have a ground army. You need to adapt. Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and consider adding anti-air units or transitioning to air yourself.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, and Queen cues. Their air presence is heavy, and they are likely to use Mutalisks for harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your ground army is strong with Zergling, Roach, and Queen, but you lack air defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and consider adding anti-air units or transitioning to air yourself.

Mutalisks can be very effective against a ground-only army. You need to either get anti-air units like Hydralisks or transition to your own air force to counter.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late midgame, both players have heavy economies and heavy production. Opponent shows moderate tech and ground posture with Roach cues.

**Strategic target:** Maintain your ground army and economy, continue to strengthen your ground forces, and consider adding upgrades or tech to gain an edge.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech without sufficient army; avoid neglecting scouting for tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see the opponent's tech or army composition change, or if you plan to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a response to the opponent's air presence while maintaining your economy.
