# N006 — Early Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, the opponent has an air-oriented army with Phoenixes and Oracles, while you have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. Consider adding anti-air units such as Marines, Thors, or Vikings to counter the opponent's air presence. Maintain your ground army and continue expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an air army with Phoenixes and Oracles, indicating a possible air harass or transition. They have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Your defense is moderate, and you have light air presence. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider adding anti-air units such as Marines, Thors, or Vikings to counter the opponent's air presence. Maintain your ground army and continue expanding.

The opponent's air units can be a threat, so you need to have a response. Adding anti-air units will help defend against harass and potential air attacks.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Ground vs Air: Don't Ignore Anti-Air

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, opponent air-oriented with Phoenixes and Oracles, you have ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, around 360 seconds.

**Mistake:** Neglecting your ground army entirely or over-investing in anti-air without a clear threat.

**Correction:** Add anti-air units such as Marines, Thors, or Vikings to counter the opponent's air presence. Maintain your ground army and continue expanding.

**Why:** The opponent's air units can be a threat, so you need a response. Adding anti-air units helps defend against harass and potential air attacks.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions back to ground or if you face a large push.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, while the opponent is still unknown but likely teching heavily.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground army. Maintain defensive posture and keep expanding.

**Veto:** Do not overextend economy without reinforcing your army, assuming the enemy is passive.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have more scouting information about the opponent's army composition or if you detect an aggressive push.
