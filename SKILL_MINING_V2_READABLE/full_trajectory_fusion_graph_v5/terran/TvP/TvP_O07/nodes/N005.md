### L01 — Anti-Air Gap vs Void Rays

**When this applies:** Midgame, around 420 seconds, with a heavy ground army and economy, and enemy scouting shows Zealots and Void Rays.

**Mistake:** Sticking to a pure ground composition without anti-air, leaving your army vulnerable to Void Rays.

**Correction:** Maintain your ground army and continue expanding, but add anti-air units like Marines or Thors to counter potential Void Rays.

**Why:** Your ground army is strong, but Void Rays threaten it. Adding anti-air ensures you can handle mixed compositions.

**Recheck when:** Recheck if the opponent commits heavily to air or if you need to adjust your composition.
