# N006 — Early-Midgame vs Air Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy ground army with Marines. Your economy and tech are heavy. The opponent shows an air posture with Zealots and Oracles, heavy production. Maintain your ground army but start preparing anti-air. Consider adding Marines and possibly Thors or Vikings. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is transitioning to an air posture with Oracles. They have heavy production and tech. Their defense is light.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a strong economy and tech. Your army is ground-based with Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start preparing anti-air. Consider adding Marines and possibly Thors or Vikings. Continue expanding and teching.

Oracles can harass, but your economy is strong. By adding anti-air, you can defend against air threats while maintaining a ground core.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Overreacting to Oracles

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 300 seconds, with a heavy ground army and economy, and enemy scouting shows Zealots and Oracles.

**Mistake:** Over-investing in anti-air or abandoning your ground army in response to Oracle harassment.

**Correction:** Maintain your ground army but start preparing anti-air by adding Marines and possibly Thors or Vikings, while continuing to expand and tech.

**Why:** Oracles can harass, but your economy is strong. Adding anti-air defends against air threats without sacrificing your ground core.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you see more air units (e.g., Phoenix, Void Rays) or if the opponent switches back to ground.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 240 seconds, you have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech investment. Your army is ground-oriented with Reapers. The opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, moderate production, and heavy tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and tech. Strengthen ground army composition. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army. Maintain defensive positioning while scouting for tech switches.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a tech switch or abandon ground defense based on incomplete enemy information.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you see a tech switch (e.g., Phoenix, Oracle, or Colossus) or when the opponent's army size becomes threatening.
