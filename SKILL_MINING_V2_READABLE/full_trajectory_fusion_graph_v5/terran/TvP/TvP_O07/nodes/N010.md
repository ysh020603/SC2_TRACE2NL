DEFAULT

## Summary

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue to expand. Consider adding more anti-air units (Thors, Vikings) to counter the air threat. Keep teching.

The opponent's air army is strong, but your ground army is solid. By adding anti-air, you can handle their composition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 600 seconds, you have a heavy ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. Your economy and tech are heavy. The opponent has a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals, and heavy production.

**Strategic target:** Focus on stabilizing your position. Increase defensive structures and maintain army strength. Continue economic growth but prioritize safety. Consider adding more Medivacs for sustain.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to expansion or tech while neglecting defense and army safety.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have a decisive army advantage or if the opponent makes a tech switch.
