# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, opponent posture unknown but heavy economy and tech. Continue heavy production and technology, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while maintaining expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment; no reliable combat-unit cues yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with heavy production and technology investment; representative units include Marines. Economy and expansion are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue heavy production and technology, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while maintaining expansion.

A heavy tech/economy opening benefits from uninterrupted development; maintaining a strong ground core provides safety against early pressure.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid neglecting army production while teching

**When this applies:** Early game, own ground macro with heavy production and tech, opponent posture unknown but heavy economy and tech.

**Mistake:** Focusing solely on economy and technology, neglecting army production, leaving you vulnerable to early timing attacks.

**Correction:** Continue heavy production and technology, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while maintaining expansion.

**Why:** A heavy tech/economy opening benefits from uninterrupted development; maintaining a strong ground core provides safety against early pressure.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when opponent's first combat units are scouted or when transitioning to midgame.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, own ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, opponent posture unknown but heavy economy and tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue heavy production and technology, strengthen ground forces, and increase economy while maintaining expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a solid early-midgame position with a strong ground army and tech lead.
