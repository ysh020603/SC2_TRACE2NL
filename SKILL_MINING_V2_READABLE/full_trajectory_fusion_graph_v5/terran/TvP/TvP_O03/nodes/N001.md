# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides are in early game with unknown army compositions. You have a heavy economy and technology focus, with moderate production and a light defense posture. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Increase your expansion and production to support a strong midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are developing a heavy economy and technology base, with moderate production. Your army is not yet defined, but you have a Marine as a representative unit. Your expansion is heavy, indicating a macro-oriented start.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Increase your expansion and production to support a strong midgame.

A heavy economy and technology investment sets up a strong midgame, allowing you to transition into a powerful army composition. Maintaining a light defense is sufficient early on, as the opponent's posture is unknown.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
