# N009 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, but you are transitioning to an air-oriented army. Your air presence is moderate, and you have Banshees and Battlecruisers as representative units. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Colossus, Zealot, Stalker, Sentry. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, but you are adding air units like Banshees and Battlecruisers. Your economy is heavy, and your expansion is heavy. Your defense posture is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

Transitioning to air can give you an advantage if the opponent is heavily ground-based. Banshees and Battlecruisers provide strong air-to-ground and air-to-air capabilities.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the late-midgame, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Your defense posture is heavy, and you have Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs as representative units.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy and production. Consider adding Vikings to counter Colossus if the opponent has them.

**Veto:** Do not neglect ground defense while transitioning to air; ensure you have sufficient anti-air and ground presence.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's late-game tech units, such as High Templar or Carriers.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a strong air army with Banshees and Battlecruisers, while maintaining a solid ground defense.
