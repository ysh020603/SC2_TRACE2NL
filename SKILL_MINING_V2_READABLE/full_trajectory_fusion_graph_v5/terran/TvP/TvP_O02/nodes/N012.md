# N012 — Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame: The opponent has a ground posture with Zealots, heavy economy, production, and technology. Your own ground forces include Marines. Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders or Siege Tanks to counter Zealots.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Production and technology are heavy, and economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground posture is well-developed with heavy production and technology. You have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders or Siege Tanks to counter Zealots.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need to ensure your ground forces can handle them. Marauders are effective against Zealots, and Siege Tanks provide good area denial.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Entering the midgame, the situation remains unclear. The opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own development is still light or uncertain.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition or neglect safety checks while balancing production and technology.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you spot enemy units or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain a strong economy to support it.
