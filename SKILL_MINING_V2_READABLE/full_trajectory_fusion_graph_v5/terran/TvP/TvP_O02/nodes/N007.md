# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame: Both sides have a ground posture. The opponent has Zealots and Stalkers, heavy economy, production, and technology. Your own ground forces include Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines. Strengthen your ground forces further. Consider adding Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter the opponent's Zealot and Stalker composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Production and technology are heavy, and economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground posture is well-developed with heavy production and technology. You have Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces further. Consider adding Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter the opponent's Zealot and Stalker composition.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need to ensure your ground forces can handle them. Siege Tanks provide good area denial, and Marauders are effective against Zealots.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Counter Ground Composition

**When this applies:** Late-midgame when both sides have ground posture, opponent has Zealots and Stalkers with heavy economy, production, and technology, and your ground forces include Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines.

**Mistake:** Neglecting upgrades or tech that could give the opponent an advantage in the ground engagement.

**Correction:** Strengthen your ground forces further, considering adding Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter Zealots and Stalkers.

**Why:** The opponent's ground army is strong; Siege Tanks provide area denial and Marauders are effective against Zealots.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or when you are ready to push.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the late-midgame, the situation remains unclear. The opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own development is still light or uncertain.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition or neglect safety checks while balancing production and technology.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you spot enemy units or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
