# N009 — Early Game Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game: The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your own economy is moderate, and expansion is heavy. Increase your economy and production to match the opponent's heavy economy. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues, but economy and expansion are heavy, production moderate, and technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, expansion is heavy, but production is light or uncertain. You have no reliable combat-unit cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production to match the opponent's heavy economy. Continue technology development.

The opponent is investing heavily in economy and technology, suggesting a macro-oriented game. Matching their economy and production will keep you competitive.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At the start of the game, both sides are in an early phase with limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own production and technology are light or uncertain.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current development path with a focus on economy and technology. Keep production flexible and avoid committing to a specific army composition.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition or neglect safety checks while developing economy and technology.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you observe enemy units or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your economy and production, then continue developing towards a strong mid-game position.
