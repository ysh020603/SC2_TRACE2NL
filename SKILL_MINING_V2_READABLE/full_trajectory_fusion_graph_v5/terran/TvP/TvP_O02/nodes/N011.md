## Summary

Early-midgame: The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your own economy is moderate, and expansion is heavy. Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders to counter Zealots.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Zealots, but the overall posture is unknown. Economy and expansion are heavy, production moderate, and technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, expansion is heavy, and production is moderate. You have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders to counter Zealots.

The opponent's Zealots suggest a potential ground threat. Strengthening your ground forces can help defend against this while you continue to develop.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** As the game progresses into the early-mid game, the situation remains largely unknown. Both sides are still developing, with no significant combat-unit cues.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition or neglect safety checks while balancing production and technology.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you spot enemy units or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
