# N011 — Late-Mid Ground vs Air (Void Ray) - Defensive

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and tech, including Marines. Enemy has an air posture with Stalker, Sentry, and Void Ray cues. Increase your defensive capabilities against air, such as adding Thors or Marines. Continue developing your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has an air posture with heavy production and tech, including Stalker, Sentry, and Void Ray. Their defense is heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, including Marines. Defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defensive capabilities against air, such as adding Thors or Marines. Continue developing your ground army.

Void Rays are strong against ground units. Adding anti-air units is essential to defend against enemy air composition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame around 600 seconds with ground-oriented posture, heavy production and tech, moderate air presence, and enemy ground cues (Zealot/Stalker/Sentry/Immortal).

**Strategic target:** Increase air presence while continuing to strengthen ground army; maintain defense and tech progression.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to air at the expense of ground defense or economy.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when enemy tech direction changes or when a decisive army composition advantage is achieved.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
