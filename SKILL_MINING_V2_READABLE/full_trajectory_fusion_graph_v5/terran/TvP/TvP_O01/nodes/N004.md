# N004 — Late-Mid Ground vs Ground with Air Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and tech, and moderate air presence. Enemy is ground-oriented with Zealot/Stalker/Sentry/Immortal cues. Increase your air presence while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain defense and tech progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with heavy production and tech, including Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, and Immortal cues. Their defense is moderate, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground macro posture with heavy production, heavy tech, and moderate air presence. Defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain defense and tech progression.

Enemy ground composition includes Immortals, which are strong against armored ground. Adding air units gives you a way to deal with them and provides flexibility.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame around 600 seconds with ground-oriented posture, heavy production and tech, moderate air presence, and enemy ground cues (Zealot/Stalker/Sentry/Immortal).

**Strategic target:** Increase air presence while continuing to strengthen ground army; maintain defense and tech progression.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to air at the expense of ground defense or economy.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when enemy tech direction changes or when a decisive army composition advantage is achieved.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a mixed ground-air army that can handle enemy ground composition and potential air transitions.
