- Enemy has an air posture with heavy production and tech, including Stalker and Void Ray. Their defense is moderate, and they are expanding.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defensive capabilities against air, such as adding Marines or Thors. Continue developing your ground army.

Void Rays are strong against ground units. Adding anti-air units is essential to defend against enemy air composition.
