## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, the opponent has a ground posture with Colossi, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Increase your defense and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Colossi, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

The opponent's composition includes Colossi, which can be dangerous to your ground army. A heavier defense and continued development will help you withstand potential pushes.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
