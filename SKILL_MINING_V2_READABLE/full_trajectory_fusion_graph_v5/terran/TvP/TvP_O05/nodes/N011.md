# N011 — Early Game Unknown Posture with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and technology. You have a heavy economy and production. Continue developing your economy and technology, while maintaining a flexible army composition. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's posture, but they have a heavy economy and technology. They may have no reliable combat-unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, but your army composition is unknown. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, while maintaining a flexible army composition. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline.

With limited information, it is safe to focus on macro and technology, while preparing for a ground-oriented response.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 3 minutes, opponent's army composition is unknown but they have heavy economy and technology; you have heavy economy and production.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition; strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

**Veto:** Do not over-extend economy without sufficient army or neglect scouting to identify opponent's tech path.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you gain more scouting information about the opponent's army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's posture becomes clearer.
