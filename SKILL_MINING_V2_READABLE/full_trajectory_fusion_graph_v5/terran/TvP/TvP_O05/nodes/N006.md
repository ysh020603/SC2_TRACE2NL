# N006 — Mid-Game Air Transition by Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 5 minutes, the opponent has transitioned to an air posture with Oracles and Zealots. You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders. Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Zealots and Oracles. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology.

The opponent's air presence requires you to prepare for potential Oracle harassment or a transition to a more air-heavy composition.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid neglecting anti-air against air transition

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 5 minutes, opponent has transitioned to air with Oracles/Zealots, you have ground army with Marines/Marauders.

**Mistake:** Neglecting your ground army in favor of anti-air, or being caught off guard by Oracle harassment.

**Correction:** Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology.

**Why:** The opponent's air presence requires you to prepare for potential Oracle harassment or a transition to a more air-heavy composition.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent commits more heavily to air or if they switch back to ground.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 4 minutes, both sides have heavy economy and production; opponent likely ground-oriented with Zealots and Stalkers; you have Marines and Marauders.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening ground army while increasing economy and expansions; maintain defense and technology development.

**Veto:** Do not over-commit to a single attack without scouting or neglect anti-air if opponent transitions to air.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of an air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air threats, such as adding Marines and possibly Vikings.
