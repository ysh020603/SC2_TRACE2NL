# N007 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, the opponent has a ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. Your own posture is unknown and underdeveloped. Maintain your current development path, but be aware that the opponent is ahead. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but be aware that the opponent is ahead. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter the opponent's composition.

The opponent has a strong ground army, so you need to catch up. Maintaining your development while preparing a defense is the safest option.

**Mistake:** Engaging the opponent's army without a plan, or neglecting your economy while trying to catch up.

**Correction:** Maintain your current development path, but be aware that the opponent is ahead. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter the opponent's composition.

**Why:** The opponent has a strong ground army, so you need to catch up. Maintaining development while preparing a defense is the safest option.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have a stronger army or when you have more information about the opponent's intentions.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** As the game progresses into the early-midgame, the opponent's posture remains unknown, but you have had time to develop your economy and technology.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep your options open.

**Veto:** Do not neglect your economy while building an army, or overcommit to a single unit composition without adaptability.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's first combat units or when you have a better read on their strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
