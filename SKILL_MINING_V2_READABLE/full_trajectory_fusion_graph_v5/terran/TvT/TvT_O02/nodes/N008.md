# N008 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, the opponent has a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Your own posture is unknown and underdeveloped. Maintain your current development path, but be aware that you are behind. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, but be aware that you are behind. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter the opponent's composition.

The opponent has a strong ground army, so you need to catch up. Maintaining your development while preparing a defense is the safest option.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have had time to build up your economy and technology.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep your options open.

**Veto:** Do not neglect your economy while building an army, or overcommit to a single unit composition without adaptability.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's first combat units or when you have a better read on their strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Catch up in economy and technology, and build a army capable of defending against the opponent's push.
