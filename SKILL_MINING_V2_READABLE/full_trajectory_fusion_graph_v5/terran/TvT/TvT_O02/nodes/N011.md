# N011 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, the opponent has a ground army with Marines and Reapers, and a heavy economy. You have a similar ground composition. Continue strengthening your ground forces and increasing your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and technology, and a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Marines, and a heavy economy and production. Your technology is also heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground forces and increasing your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

Both players are on a similar ground macro path. Strengthening your army and economy will help you gain an advantage in the midgame.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** As the game progresses into the early-midgame, the opponent's posture remains unknown, but you have had time to develop your economy and technology.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep your options open.

**Veto:** Do not neglect your economy while building an army, or overcommit to a single unit composition without adaptability.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's first combat units or when you have a better read on their strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong midgame position with a powerful ground army and a robust economy.
