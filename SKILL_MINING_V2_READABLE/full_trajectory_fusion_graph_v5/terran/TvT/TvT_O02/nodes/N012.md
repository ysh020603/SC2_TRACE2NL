# N012 — Midgame Transition to Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, the opponent has a ground army with Marines and Reapers. You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs, and you are considering adding air units. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air forces. Consider adding Vikings or Banshees to gain an advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and technology, and a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air forces. Consider adding Vikings or Banshees to gain an advantage.

The opponent is heavily invested in ground forces. Adding air units can give you a tactical advantage and force the opponent to react.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, the opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have had time to build up your economy and technology.

**Strategic target:** Continue maintaining your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep your options open.

**Veto:** Do not neglect your economy while building an army, or overcommit to a single unit composition without adaptability.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's first combat units or when you have a better read on their strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army with a strong air component to pressure the opponent.
