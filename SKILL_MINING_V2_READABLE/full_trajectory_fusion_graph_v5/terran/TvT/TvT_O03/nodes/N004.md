# N004 — Early Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech; opponent has heavy defense and heavy economy. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and maintaining production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and maintaining production.

Build a safe economic and tech lead while preparing for potential early aggression.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Unknown vs Ground: Avoid Passive Defense

**When this applies:** Early game, own posture unknown with moderate production and heavy tech; opponent ground with heavy defense and economy.

**Mistake:** Staying too defensive for too long, neglecting expansion and map control, and failing to scout for tech switches.

**Correction:** Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and maintaining production.

**Why:** Build a safe economic and tech lead while preparing for potential early aggression.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when more enemy information is available or when ready to apply pressure.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, own posture unknown-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech investment; opponent appears ground-oriented with light defense and heavy economy.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and continuing technology development.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single tech path without scouting, neglecting defense against possible aggression.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when more enemy units are scouted or when transitioning to midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive ground posture once tech advantages are secured.
