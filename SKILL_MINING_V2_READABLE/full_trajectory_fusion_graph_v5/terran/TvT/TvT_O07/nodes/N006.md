# N006 — Late-Midgame Ground to Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 600 seconds, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. The opponent shows a ground army with marines and marauders, while your own forces are also ground-based. Begin transitioning to an air-oriented composition, increasing your air presence and technology. Continue to strengthen your ground forces as a base.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Marauder, Hellion. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army posture and heavy production and technology. Your defense is heavy, and you have the potential to apply pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Begin transitioning to an air-oriented composition, increasing your air presence and technology. Continue to strengthen your ground forces as a base.

Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage if the opponent is heavily invested in ground forces. Air units can provide mobility and harassment options.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 600 seconds, both players have ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology; opponent has moderate air presence with medivacs, your air is light.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening ground forces while adding air support or anti-air to counter medivacs; maintain defensive posture and economy.

**Veto:** Do not neglect air defense; avoid overcommitting to air without maintaining ground core.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if opponent commits heavily to air or if defensive positioning needs adjustment; check anti-air capabilities and medivac count.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes air units such as banshees or medivacs, while maintaining a strong economy.
