# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground with Air Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 600 seconds, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. The opponent has a moderate air presence with medivacs, while your own air presence is light or uncertain. Continue strengthening your ground forces while considering adding air support to counter the opponent's medivacs. Maintain your defensive posture and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Marauder, Medivac. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army posture and heavy production and technology. Your defense is heavy, and you have the potential to apply pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground forces while considering adding air support to counter the opponent's medivacs. Maintain your defensive posture and economy.

The opponent's medivacs provide mobility and healing, so adding some air units or anti-air capabilities can help you deal with drops and sustain in engagements.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid neglecting air defense

**When this applies:** Late midgame, around 600 seconds, when both players have ground-oriented macro postures with heavy production and technology, and the opponent has medivacs while your air presence is light.

**Mistake:** Neglecting air defense, leaving you vulnerable to Medivac drops and healing.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening ground forces while adding air support or anti-air capabilities to counter medivacs. Maintain defensive posture and economy.

**Why:** The opponent's medivacs provide mobility and healing, so adding air units or anti-air helps deal with drops and sustain in engagements.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent commits heavily to air or if defensive positioning needs adjustment.
