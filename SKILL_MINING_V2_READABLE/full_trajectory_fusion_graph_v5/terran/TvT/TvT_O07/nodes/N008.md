DEFAULT

## Summary

The opponent shows a ground army with marines and reapers, while your own forces include siege tanks and medivacs. Increase your air presence and technology, while maintaining a strong ground core. Continue to expand and develop your economy.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology, while maintaining a strong ground core. Continue to expand and develop your economy.

Adding air units like medivacs can provide mobility and healing, giving you an advantage in engagements and harassment.
