# N010 — Early Game Tech Focus with Defensive Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 180 seconds, both players have an unknown army posture, but with heavy technology investment. The opponent's production is moderate, while your own production is also moderate. Focus on increasing your production and technology, while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue to expand your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate expansion, with an unknown army posture and moderate production. Your technology is heavy, and you have a Marine and Reaper as representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your production and technology, while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue to expand your economy.

With the opponent's army unknown, it is important to build a solid foundation and be ready to adapt. Increasing production allows you to field a larger army once you know the opponent's composition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 180 seconds, both players have an unknown army posture with heavy technology investment; opponent production is heavy while yours is moderate.

**Strategic target:** Increase economy and expansion while continuing technology development; strengthen ground forces as a baseline and maintain a defensive posture.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a large army before enemy plan is known; avoid neglecting economy or tech for premature aggression.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate once more scouting information about opponent's army composition is available; check current resources, supply, and production queues.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground posture as you gain more information about the opponent's strategy.
