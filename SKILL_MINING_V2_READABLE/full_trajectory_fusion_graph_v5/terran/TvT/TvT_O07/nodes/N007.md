## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by ensuring your defenses are solid, then continue developing your economy and technology. Increase your production to support a stronger army.

With the opponent having a similar ground posture, stabilizing first allows you to avoid early game losses and then outscale your opponent in the midgame.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 240-300 seconds, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology; opponent shows ground army with possible pressure.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground forces; maintain defensive posture and consider increasing expansion and production.

**Veto:** Do not neglect defensive positioning while expanding; avoid overextending without scouting for air transitions or large pushes.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if opponent shows signs of air transition or significant army push; check current army strength and production capacity.
