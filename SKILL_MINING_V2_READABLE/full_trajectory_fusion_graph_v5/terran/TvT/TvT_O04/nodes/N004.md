# N004 — Early Game Ground Orientation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Reapers, while the opponent's army composition is unknown but they have a heavy economy. Continue developing your ground army and economy, while scouting to identify the opponent's plan.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, but their army composition is unknown. They may be planning a variety of strategies.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines and Reapers. Your defense is light, and you are ready to expand.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, while scouting to identify the opponent's plan.

A ground-oriented army is flexible and can transition to many compositions. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to adapt quickly.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 180 seconds, the opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines. Your own army composition is still unknown.

**Strategic target:** Focus on expanding your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition without scouting information; avoid neglecting defensive readiness while focusing on economy.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's build or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized army based on scouting, while continuing to expand.
