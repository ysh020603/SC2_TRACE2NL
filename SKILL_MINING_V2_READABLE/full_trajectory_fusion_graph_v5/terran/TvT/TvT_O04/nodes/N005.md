# N005 — Late Midgame Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, both players have a heavy ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, and a moderate air presence. The opponent has a heavy defense. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase your air presence, and maintain your economy to support a long fight.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a similar composition, with a heavy defense and a strong ground army. They are likely to be preparing for a decisive engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a strong ground army including Siege Tanks and Marines. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase your air presence, and maintain your economy to support a long fight.

With both players having strong ground armies, adding air units and maintaining a robust economy gives you an edge in the late game.
