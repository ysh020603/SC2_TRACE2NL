# N008 — Early Game Macro Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, the opponent's posture is unknown, but you have a ground-oriented army with Marines. Your economy is moderate, and you are expanding. Focus on macro, expanding and teching while maintaining a defensive posture. Scout to gain information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues. Production appears moderate, technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and are expanding, with heavy production and technology investment. Your army consists of Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on macro, expanding and teching while maintaining a defensive posture. Scout to gain information.

A strong economy and technology base will allow you to outproduce the opponent in the midgame.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 3 minutes, opponent posture unknown, ground-oriented army with Marines, heavy economy and expanding.

**Strategic target:** Establish a strong macro foundation: continue expanding and teching while maintaining a defensive posture. If enemy Reapers are seen, apply light harassment with your own Reapers while continuing macro.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to harassment or expansion without scouting; avoid neglecting defensive positioning.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's first units or when you reach a new expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more active midgame with a stronger army composition.
