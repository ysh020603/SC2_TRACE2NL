# N006 — Early Midgame Defensive Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 6 minutes) with opponent showing a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, and you have Siege Tanks and Marines. Increase defense and expansion. Continue production and tech, and increase economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is heavy, technology is heavy. Representative units include Siege Tanks and Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and expansion. Continue production and tech, and increase economy.

With Siege Tanks, you can defend expansions effectively. Expanding and increasing economy will give you a long-term advantage, while maintaining defense prevents any early aggression.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early midgame (~5-6 min) with ground-oriented posture, heavy production and tech, possible Marine/Reaper cues.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground army with Siege Tanks for defense; increase economy and production; continue tech; consider expansion if safe.

**Veto:** Do not push into fortified positions without Siege Tank support; avoid neglecting defense while expanding.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have a clear read on the opponent's composition or if they attempt an aggressive move.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a strong economy through safe expansions, supported by Siege Tanks.
