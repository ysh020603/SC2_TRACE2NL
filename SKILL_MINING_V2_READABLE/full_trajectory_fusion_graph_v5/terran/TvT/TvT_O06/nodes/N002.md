# N002 — Early Game Heavy Production with Moderate Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with opponent showing a ground posture and possible Reaper cues. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy technology, but economy is moderate. Increase your economy to catch up to your heavy production and technology. Maintain production and continue technology upgrades. Keep defense solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, moderate economy, and moderate expansion. Production is heavy, technology is heavy. Representative units include Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy to catch up to your heavy production and technology. Maintain production and continue technology upgrades. Keep defense solid.

Your production is ahead, but economy lags. Boosting economy will sustain your production and tech investments, ensuring you don't fall behind in the long run.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game (~3 min) with ground-oriented posture, heavy economy and tech, possible Reaper cues.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing ground forces and economy while maintaining defensive structures; increase economy and continue technology upgrades; keep production steady.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single tech path or neglect scouting for early pressure; avoid excessive production without economy support.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or if they apply pressure; adjust production and tech accordingly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a balanced economy and production to support a strong midgame transition.
