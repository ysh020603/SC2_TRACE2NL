# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground with Reapers

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent shows Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers, indicating a ground tech path with harassment potential. Your own posture is ground with heavy production. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production and technology. Consider adding Reapers for map control or scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper. Production heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production and technology. Consider adding Reapers for map control or scouting.

Reapers can provide early harassment and scouting, helping you react to the opponent's moves. Maintaining a strong economy supports a larger army later.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, Siege Tanks, Marauders, or Reapers. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain or increase production. Continue technology development. Consider adding Siege Tanks or Reapers based on opponent cues.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to air or neglect ground defense while focusing solely on tech.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when opponent's air presence or army composition changes, or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use Reapers for map control while building a solid ground army.
