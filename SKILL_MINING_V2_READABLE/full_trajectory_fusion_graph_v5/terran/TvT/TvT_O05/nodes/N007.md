# N007 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Your own posture has moderate air presence, indicating a possible air transition. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and expansion. Continue technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Marine, Marauder. Production heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with moderate air presence. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is moderate, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and expansion. Continue technology and upgrades.

Transitioning to air can give you an advantage if the opponent is ground-heavy. Banshees or Vikings can harass and control the map.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and possibly Battlecruisers. Your own posture is ground with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Maintain ground strength, increase economy, and continue technology. Consider adding air units or upgrades to counter potential Battlecruisers.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground without preparing for a potential air transition to Battlecruisers.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you confirm the opponent's air transition or when your army composition is ready.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air force while maintaining a solid ground defense.
