# N002 — Developing Ground with Siege Tanks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent shows Siege Tanks and Marauders, indicating a heavy ground tech path. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production and technology. Consider adding Siege Tanks to your composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include SiegeTank, Marauder. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production and technology. Consider adding Siege Tanks to your composition.

Siege Tanks provide strong defensive and offensive capabilities in TvT. Matching the opponent's tech keeps you even while you build a lead.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, Siege Tanks, Marauders, or Reapers. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain or increase production. Continue technology development. Consider adding Siege Tanks or Reapers based on opponent cues.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to air or neglect ground defense while focusing solely on tech.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when opponent's air presence or army composition changes, or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid ground army with Siege Tank support and continue expanding.
