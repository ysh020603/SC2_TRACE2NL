# N004 — Early Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 4 minutes, you have a ground army with Marines, while the opponent has Zerglings and Queens, with light tech. Stabilize your position by increasing production and maintaining defense, while continuing tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, but defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing production and maintaining defense, while continuing tech development.

The opponent's light tech suggests they are not rushing to a specific threat, so you can safely focus on macro and tech. Increasing production ensures you can respond to any pressure.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 4 minutes, you have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy tech, while the opponent appears to be on a ground macro path with light or uncertain air.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain current defense and air posture.

**Veto:** Do not neglect defense or overextend expansions without scouting for early pressure or tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of a tech switch or heavy air presence, or if you face early pressure that requires defensive adjustments.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a strong economy and tech advantage.
