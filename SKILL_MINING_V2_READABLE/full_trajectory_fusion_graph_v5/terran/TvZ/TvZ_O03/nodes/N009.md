# N009 — Midgame Army Maintenance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 8 minutes, you have a ground army with Marines, while the opponent has Zerglings and Queens. Maintain your current army path and defense, while keeping economy and tech development steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy and expansions are heavy, and defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and defense, while keeping economy and tech development steady.

Your current composition is solid against the opponent's ground army. Maintaining your path avoids unnecessary risks and allows you to build a strong economy.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 8 minutes, you have a heavy ground army with Ghosts, Hellions, and Medivacs, while the opponent has a heavy ground composition with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.

**Strategic target:** Increase your air presence and technology while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain defense and increase economy and expansions.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground-only forces without anti-air, leaving you vulnerable to Mutalisk harassment.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent commits to a specific tech path or if you face a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
