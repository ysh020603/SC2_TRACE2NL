# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 4 minutes, you have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy tech, while the opponent appears to be on a ground macro path with light or uncertain air. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain current defense and air posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Marines, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Your economy and expansions are heavy, but your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain current defense and air posture.

Your heavy tech investment gives you an advantage in the midgame, so you can afford to expand and build a strong economy. The opponent's light tech suggests they are not rushing to a specific threat, so you can develop safely.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 4 minutes, you have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy tech, while the opponent appears to be on a ground macro path with light or uncertain air.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain current defense and air posture.

**Veto:** Do not neglect defense or overextend expansions without scouting for early pressure or tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of a tech switch or heavy air presence, or if you face early pressure that requires defensive adjustments.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a solid ground army, a strong economy, and the tech to transition into whatever composition the opponent's scouting reveals.
