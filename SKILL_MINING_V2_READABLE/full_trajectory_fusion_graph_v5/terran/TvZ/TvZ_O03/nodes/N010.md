# N010 — Early Game Defense and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, you have an unknown army posture, while the opponent has Zerglings and Queens. Increase defense and economy, while continuing production and tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, and defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, while continuing production and tech development.

The opponent's early Zerglings could pose a threat, so investing in defense is prudent. Your heavy tech gives you a long-term advantage.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 3 minutes, you have an unknown army posture, while the opponent has a Queen and light tech.

**Strategic target:** Increase production and economy, while maintaining defense and continuing tech development.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to army production before your heavy tech investment pays off, delaying your economy and tech progression.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of aggression or tech progression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early midgame with a solid defense and economy.
