# N009 — Midgame Stabilization with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 7 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Enemy has a ground army with Zergling and Queen, heavy production and moderate tech. Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Increase production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Zergling and Queen. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and tech. Your army is ground-oriented with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Increase production.

With a solid economy and production, you can afford to stabilize and tech up. This prepares you for a strong midgame push.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 7-9 minutes, heavy economy and production, Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, enemy ground posture with Zergling/Queen, heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening ground army and increase technology; maintain economy and defense.

**Veto:** Do not over-invest in air without scouting or neglect upgrades.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if enemy shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a Siege Tank-based army for stronger engagements.
