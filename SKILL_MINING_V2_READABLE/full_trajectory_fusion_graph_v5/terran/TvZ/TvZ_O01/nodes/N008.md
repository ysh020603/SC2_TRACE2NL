# N008 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and moderate production, with Marines and Reapers. Enemy has no reliable combat-unit cues, but is likely on a ground macro posture. Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Increase production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and moderate production, but no confirmed combat units. They are likely expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate production, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Increase production.

With no confirmed enemy units, it's safe to focus on economy and tech. Increasing production prepares you for any aggression.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Around 3 minutes, heavy economy, moderate production, Reaper scouting, enemy ground posture with Zergling/Queen, moderate production, light tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground army; maintain current production.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to early aggression or neglect scouting for tech switches.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if enemy shows early pressure or tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined ground army composition.
