# N004 — Early-Mid Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground army with heavy production/tech, opponent ground with light air. Stabilize then develop, increase production, maintain ground strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground-oriented with Zergling and Queen, heavy economy, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Marines and Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize then develop, increase production, maintain ground strength.

Ensure safety before expanding further, leveraging tech advantage.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Stabilize before expanding

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, own ground army with heavy production and tech, opponent ground with light air.

**Mistake:** Risky aggression or overexpansion without sufficient army strength or defense.

**Correction:** Stabilize then develop, increase production, maintain ground strength.

**Why:** Ensure safety before expanding further, leveraging tech advantage.
