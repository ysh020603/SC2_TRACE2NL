# N007 — Early Game Reaper Harass

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own moderate production and heavy tech, using Reapers. Opponent ground macro with light defense. Use your Reaper to harass the opponent's economy and scout. Continue to develop your economy and tech, and consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, but their defense is light, making them vulnerable to harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper for harassment, with moderate production and heavy tech. Economy and expansions are heavy, defenses are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use your Reaper to harass the opponent's economy and scout. Continue to develop your economy and tech, and consider expanding.

Reaper harassment can disrupt the opponent's economy and force reactions, while you continue to macro behind it.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game (around 180s) with own ground-oriented macro posture, heavy economy and tech, moderate production. Opponent appears as a ground macro Zerg with light tech and moderate production.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and consider expanding further.

**Veto:** Do not overextend your economy without sufficient army to defend, or neglect scouting for tech switches or all-ins.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have a significant army advantage or when scouting reveals a change in opponent's posture (e.g., tech switch or aggression).

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger army while maintaining pressure on the opponent.
