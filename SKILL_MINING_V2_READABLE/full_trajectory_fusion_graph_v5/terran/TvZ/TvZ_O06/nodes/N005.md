# N005 — Midgame Air Transition and Ground Core

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420-480s) with own ground army but moderate air presence, including Battlecruisers. Opponent heavy production and tech, ground-oriented. Continue to strengthen your ground army while also developing your air presence. Increase production and tech, and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has heavy production and tech, still fielding Zerglings and Queens, but with heavier investment. They are likely preparing for a stronger army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Reapers, Hellions, and Battlecruisers, indicating a mix of harassment and heavy units. Production and tech are heavy, economy strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army while also developing your air presence. Increase production and tech, and consider expanding further.

Having a mix of ground and air units can give you flexibility against Zerg's ground-based army. Battlecruisers provide a powerful late-game option.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame (around 420-480s) with own heavy ground army, heavy production and tech. Opponent has heavy production and tech, still ground-oriented.

**Strategic target:** Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase tech. Maintain production and consider adding more expansions if needed. Keep defenses strong.

**Veto:** Do not overextend without scouting for all-ins or tech switches, and do not neglect anti-air if opponent shows air units.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have a decisive army advantage or when opponent's composition changes significantly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more balanced army with strong air support, while maintaining a solid ground core.
