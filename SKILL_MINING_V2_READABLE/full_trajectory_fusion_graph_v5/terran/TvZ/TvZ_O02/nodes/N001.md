# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both sides have limited information. Opponent posture is unknown, own posture is also uncertain. Focus on establishing a solid foundation. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Keep production, economy, technology, and expansion directions steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Keep production, economy, technology, and expansion directions steady.

With limited information, it is prudent to avoid risky commitments and focus on a stable economy and technology base. This allows adaptation once more information is available.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, both sides have limited information. Opponent posture is unknown, own posture is also uncertain. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current development path with safety checks. Keep production, economy, technology, and expansion directions steady. If opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling, strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue production and technology.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to heavy technology or expansion without maintaining sufficient army production to defend against early Zergling pressure.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when more information about opponent's posture becomes available, or when a significant event occurs (e.g., opponent attack, expansion).

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing while gathering information. Be ready to adjust based on scouting.
