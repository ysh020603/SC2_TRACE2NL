# N011 — Early Midgame Ground Posture with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen. Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy defense. This is a signal to start leaning towards a ground-oriented response. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, increase production, continue technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, increase production, continue technology.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground-based army. Maintaining a flexible development allows you to respond appropriately without overcommitting.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early midgame, still limited information. Both sides maintain unknown postures. Continue safe development.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current development path with safety checks. Keep production, economy, technology, and expansion directions steady. If opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen, strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue production and technology.

**Veto:** Do not neglect army production while expanding or teching; ensure a solid ground core to handle potential Zergling aggression.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when more information about opponent's posture becomes available, or when a significant event occurs (e.g., opponent attack, expansion).

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing while gathering information. Be ready to adjust based on scouting.
