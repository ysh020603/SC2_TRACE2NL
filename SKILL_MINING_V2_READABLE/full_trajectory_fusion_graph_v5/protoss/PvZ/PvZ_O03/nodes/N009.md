# N009 — Midgame Air Army with Phoenix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own army air-oriented with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Corruptors, and Queens. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Consider adding Phoenix to counter Mutalisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a mixed ground/air army with Mutalisks and Corruptors, indicating a tech switch. They have heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Oracles and some ground support. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Consider adding Phoenix to counter Mutalisks.

Phoenix are strong against Mutalisks, and Oracles provide utility. An air army can control the skies.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to Void Rays if the opponent has many Corruptors, and do not neglect ground defense.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
