# N005 — Early Game Expansion Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, economy heavy, expansion light_or_uncertain. Opponent ground-based with light air, moderate production. Increase expansion and continue developing technology. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows a ground posture with light air presence, moderate production, and light tech. Queens are representative.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, but you are focusing on economy and technology. Expansion is not yet heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and continue developing technology. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

Expanding early can secure a long-term economic advantage. Technology investment provides options for later.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, own army composition unknown, economy heavy, technology heavy, production moderate. Opponent shows ground posture with light air presence, moderate production, light tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline, but keep options open for air transition.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a specific army composition before scouting the opponent's tech path, and do not neglect defense while focusing on economy.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's tech path or when your army composition becomes defined.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach an early-midgame position with a solid economy and a defined army direction.
