# N007 — Air Transition with Oracles

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army air-oriented with Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground-based with light air, moderate production. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue developing technology and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent remains ground-based with light air presence, moderate production, and light tech. Queens are representative.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have committed to an air army with Oracles, indicating a tech-heavy approach. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue developing technology and economy.

Oracles provide harassment and scouting, and can transition into Phoenix or Void Ray compositions. Air can be strong against ground-based Zerg.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Balance air commitment with ground defense

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, own army air-oriented with Oracles, heavy production and tech, opponent ground-based with light air.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to air while neglecting ground defense, or failing to adapt if the opponent shows anti-air.

**Correction:** Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue developing technology and economy, but ensure ground defense is not neglected.

**Why:** Oracles provide harassment and scouting, and can transition into Phoenix or Void Ray compositions. Air can be strong against ground-based Zerg, but ground defense remains essential.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to anti-air.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground-based, light air, moderate production.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing ground forces and technology while maintaining safety checks. Increase production and economy.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to air while neglecting ground defense, and do not fail to adapt if the opponent shows anti-air.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or tech.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
