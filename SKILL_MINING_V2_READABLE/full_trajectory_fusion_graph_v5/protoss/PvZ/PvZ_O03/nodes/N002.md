# N002 — Ground Army Stabilization

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army is ground-oriented with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Opponent remains ground-based with light air, moderate production. Stabilize your ground army and continue increasing production and technology. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent still shows a ground posture with light air presence, moderate production, and light tech. Queens are representative.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have committed to a ground army with Stalkers as a core unit. Production and technology are heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your ground army and continue increasing production and technology. Maintain economy and expansion.

A ground army with Stalkers provides a solid foundation against Zerg ground forces. Heavy production and tech allow for transitions if needed.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground-based, light air, moderate production.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing ground forces and technology while maintaining safety checks. Increase production and economy.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to air while neglecting ground defense, and do not fail to adapt if the opponent shows anti-air.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame position with a strong ground army and the ability to tech into additional units.
