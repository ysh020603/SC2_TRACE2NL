# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech. Strengthen ground forces, increase production and tech, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is ground-based with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Economy and defense are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase production and tech, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

With a defined ground army, focusing on strengthening it and increasing production can put pressure on the opponent while maintaining a safe economy.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech.

**Strategic target:** Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

**Veto:** Do not be too passive; avoid missing opportunities to gain map control or punish over-expansion.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when your army composition becomes clear or if the opponent shows signs of tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army to pressure the opponent and secure map control.
