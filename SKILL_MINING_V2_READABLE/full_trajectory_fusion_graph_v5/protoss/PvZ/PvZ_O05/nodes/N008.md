# N008 — Late-Midgame Air Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own army air-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, moderate tech. Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, moderate tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is air-based with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Economy and defense are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

With a strong air army, focusing on air dominance and tech can give you a significant advantage against a ground-based opponent.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
