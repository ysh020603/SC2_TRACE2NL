# N010 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own army ground-oriented with Zealots, Stalkers, and Carriers, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech. Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is ground-based with Zealots, Stalkers, and Carriers, heavy production and tech. Economy and defense are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

With a mixed ground army, focusing on defense and economy while continuing tech development prepares you for a strong late-game.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, own army air-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, moderate tech.

**Strategic target:** Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air defense if opponent techs to anti-air; avoid over-expanding.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of anti-air tech or if your air army is not effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable late-game with a strong army and economy.
