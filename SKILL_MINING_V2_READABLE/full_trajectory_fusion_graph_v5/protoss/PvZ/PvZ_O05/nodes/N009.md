# N009 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army air-oriented with Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech. Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is air-based with Oracles, heavy production and tech. Economy and defense are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

With an air-oriented army, focusing on air strength and tech can give you an advantage against a ground-based opponent.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech.

**Strategic target:** Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

**Veto:** Do not be too passive; avoid missing opportunities to gain map control or punish over-expansion.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when your army composition becomes clear or if the opponent shows signs of tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army to pressure the opponent and secure map control.
