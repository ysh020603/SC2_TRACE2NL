## Contrastive Lessons

Use these mistake-to-correction pairs only when the live situation matches.

### L01 — Avoid over-committing to air or neglecting defense

**When:** Early game, own army composition unknown, heavy technology investment, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light technology.

**Mistake → correction:** Tempting to over-commit to air or neglect defense, or expand too greedily without scouting. → Strengthen ground forces, increase defense, continue economy growth, and increase technology. Maintain air and expansion.

**Why:** With an unknown own army and possible early Zergling pressure, prioritizing defense and ground strength while teching up is a safe way to transition into a stronger midgame.

**Read for full checks:** `N001`

### L02 — Avoid being too passive; seek map control

**When:** Early-midgame, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech.

**Mistake → correction:** Tempting to be too passive, missing opportunities to gain map control or punish over-expansion. → Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

**Why:** As the game progresses, stabilizing your defense and economy while continuing tech development prepares you for a strong midgame transition.

**Read for full checks:** `N003`

### L03 — Avoid neglecting air defense if opponent techs to Mutalisks

**When:** Midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, heavy production and tech.

**Mistake → correction:** Tempting to neglect air defense if opponent techs to Mutalisks; also risk over-expanding. → Strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

**Why:** With a defined ground army, focusing on strengthening it while maintaining a solid economy is key to matching the opponent's heavy production.

**Read for full checks:** `N004`
