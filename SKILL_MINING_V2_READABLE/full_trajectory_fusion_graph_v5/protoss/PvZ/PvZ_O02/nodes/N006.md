# N006 — Early Game: Economy and Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Your own posture is unknown but with heavy technology investment and heavy expansion. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and production, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and production, and continue technology development.

The opponent's ground posture suggests potential early pressure. Investing in economy and tech while maintaining production provides a solid foundation.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At the start of the game, both sides are largely unknown. No reliable combat-unit cues have been observed from the opponent, and your own posture is also undefined.

**Strategic target:** Maintain current army path, keep production and technology development steady, and prepare to adapt based on scouting. If the opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, and continue technology development.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to technology while neglecting army size or defense, especially if the opponent shows a ground posture.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when the opponent's first units or buildings are scouted, or when your own tech/expansion choices are ready to be made. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stable ground army and tech advantage to transition into the midgame.
