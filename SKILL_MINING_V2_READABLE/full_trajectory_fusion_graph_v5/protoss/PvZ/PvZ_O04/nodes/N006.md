# N006 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own air posture with Phoenixes, opponent ground with Zerglings, Queens, Overseers, both heavy tech. Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground posture with Zerglings, Queens, Overseers, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy production and tech, air army with Phoenixes, heavy economy, moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

Transition to air to exploit the opponent's ground-focused army and gain map control.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame, own ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, Immortals, opponent ground with Zerglings, Queens, Overseers, both heavy tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and tech, consider upgrades.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air or overcommit to ground without scouting for air transitions; avoid ignoring defensive upgrades.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when opponent tech or army composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
