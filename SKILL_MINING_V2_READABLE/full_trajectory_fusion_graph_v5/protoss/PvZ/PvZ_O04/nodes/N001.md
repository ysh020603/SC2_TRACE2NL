# N001 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown or ground-oriented, opponent ground posture with light tech, both heavy economy. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Queens, moderate production, light tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, army composition unclear or early Zealot presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansions.

Establish a solid economic and technological foundation to outscale the opponent's early ground-oriented play.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, own posture unknown or ground-oriented, opponent ground posture with light tech, both heavy economy.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansions.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech or economy at the expense of scouting and defensive readiness; avoid neglecting ground defense while expanding.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when more enemy units are scouted or when transitioning to mid-game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground or mixed army as the game progresses.
