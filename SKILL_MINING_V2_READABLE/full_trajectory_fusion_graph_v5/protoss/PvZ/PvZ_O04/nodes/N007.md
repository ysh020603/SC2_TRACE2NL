### Own cues

- Heavy economy and tech, moderate production, ground army with Zealots, heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansions, continue production and tech, maintain defense.

Build a safe economic and tech foundation while preparing for potential early pressure.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game, own posture unknown or ground-oriented, opponent ground posture with light tech, both heavy economy.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and technology, maintain defense and expansions.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech or economy at the expense of scouting and defensive readiness; avoid neglecting ground defense while expanding.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when more enemy units are scouted or when transitioning to mid-game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Transition to a more aggressive or tech-heavy composition as the game progresses.
