The opponent's army is ground-heavy, and air units like Phoenixes can provide excellent harassment and map control. Your heavy economy and technology allow you to invest in a strong air force.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame (around 7-9 minutes) with a well-established ground army and heavy economy/technology. Opponent has heavier ground presence with Roaches and Overseers. If enemy shows air transition, branch to air-oriented development.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening ground army and maintain economic/tech advantage. Consider adding Immortals or Colossi to counter Roach-heavy composition. **Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground-only composition without scouting enemy tech transitions, and do not neglect defense while teching.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when you have information about the opponent's tech transitions or when you are ready to launch a major attack.
