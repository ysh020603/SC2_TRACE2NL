# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 480-540 seconds, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Medivacs, and you have a solid economy but need to strengthen your army. Increase your production and technology to strengthen your ground army, while maintaining your economy and expansion lead.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Medivacs, heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, but your army is still light on defense. You have Zealots and Stalkers, but need more units and tech to match the opponent's army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and technology to strengthen your ground army, while maintaining your economy and expansion lead.

The opponent's army is growing, and you need to keep up in army strength to avoid being overwhelmed. Investing in tech like Immortals or Colossi will give you an edge in the midgame.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 480-540 seconds, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Medivacs, and you have a solid economy but need to strengthen your army.

**Veto:** Do not overextend production and tech without ensuring your army composition can handle the opponent's ground forces, especially with Medivac support.

**Live recheck:** Recheck if the opponent shows signs of a large push or if you scout a tech switch. Adjust your production and tech priorities accordingly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army with tech support, ready to take favorable engagements or defend against pushes.
