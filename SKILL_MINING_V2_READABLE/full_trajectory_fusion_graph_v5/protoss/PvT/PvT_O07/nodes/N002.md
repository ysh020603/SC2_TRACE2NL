# N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both you and the opponent have unknown army compositions, but you have a heavy economy and technology investment. The opponent shows possible Reapers and Marines. Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Consider scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but possible Marine and Reaper cues suggest early aggression. Their economy is heavy, technology is heavy, and production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, but you have a heavy economy and technology. Your production is moderate, and your expansion is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Consider scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

Early game is about establishing a strong foundation. Heavy technology investment can pay off later, but you need to be aware of potential early pressure.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid blind teching without scouting

**When this applies:** Early game around 180 seconds, when you have heavy economy and technology investment but unknown enemy composition, with possible Reapers and Marines.

**Mistake:** Assuming the enemy's build without scouting and neglecting defense while teching.

**Correction:** Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Scout to clarify the opponent's intentions.

**Why:** Early game is about establishing a strong foundation. Heavy technology investment can pay off later, but you need to be aware of potential early pressure.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or if you face early pressure.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 180 seconds, with heavy economy and technology investment, and unknown enemy composition possibly including Marines and Reapers.

**Strategic target:** Increase production and technology while maintaining a safe economy; scout to clarify enemy intentions.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to technology without scouting or neglect defense against possible early pressure.

**Live recheck:** Recheck when more information about enemy army composition is available or if early pressure is detected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a clearer army composition based on scouting, while continuing to grow your economy and technology.
