# N004 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 4-5 minutes, with heavy tech investment and moderate production. Opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers. Continue to invest in technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Terran ground posture with Reapers. Their production is moderate, but their tech investment is heavy, indicating a possible tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, but your army composition is not yet defined. You are heavily investing in technology, and your defense is solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to invest in technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

Heavy tech investment gives you flexibility to counter the opponent's eventual composition. A defensive posture prevents early game losses.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid over-committing to expansions or air units

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 4-5 minutes, with heavy tech investment and moderate production. Opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to expansions or air units before confirming the opponent's intentions, leaving you exposed to early aggression.

**Correction:** Continue to invest in technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

**Why:** Heavy tech investment gives you flexibility to counter the opponent's eventual composition. A defensive posture prevents early game losses.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army or if they apply pressure.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with heavy production and tech. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers.

**Strategic target:** Maintain defensive posture while increasing production and tech. Continue to expand to support growing army.

**Veto:** Do not over-extend with expansions or tech that leaves you vulnerable to early pressure.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's tech path or if they attempt a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have identified the opponent's tech path.
