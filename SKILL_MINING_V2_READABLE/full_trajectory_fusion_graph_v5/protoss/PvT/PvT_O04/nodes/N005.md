# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground/Air Hybrid

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with a heavy ground army and some air support. Opponent has a heavy ground army with Marines. Continue to strengthen your ground army while adding air support to deal with potential threats. Maintain your defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Terran ground army with Marines. Their production is heavy, and they are heavily invested in technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Void Rays. Your economy and tech are strong, and you have multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army while adding air support to deal with potential threats. Maintain your defensive posture.

A mix of ground and air units gives you flexibility to handle various Terran compositions. Void Rays can be effective against massed Marines.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with a heavy ground army and some air support. Opponent has a heavy ground army with Marines.

**Strategic target:** Continue to strengthen ground army while adding air support to deal with potential threats. Maintain defensive posture.

**Veto:** Do not over-commit to air units before confirming opponent's intentions, leaving you exposed to early aggression.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have a clear army advantage or if the opponent switches to a more air-heavy composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if you have an advantage, or continue to develop if the game is even.
