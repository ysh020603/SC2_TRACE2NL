# N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you are in the early game with heavy economy and tech investment, but your army composition is unclear. Enemy shows a Reaper, indicating possible early harassment. Focus on developing your economy and technology while preparing to defend against potential early pressure. Strengthen your ground forces as you transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper, suggesting early harassment potential. Their economy and tech are heavy, but their production is moderate. Their army posture is unknown, and they may be expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production is moderate, and your army composition is not yet defined. Your expansion is light or uncertain, and you have minimal defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while preparing to defend against potential early pressure. Strengthen your ground forces as you transition.

A heavy tech and economy start can give you a long-term advantage, but you need to be safe against early Reaper harassment. Building a solid foundation now will pay off later.

**When this applies:** Early game, around 3 minutes, with heavy economy and tech investment, enemy shows Reaper indicating possible early harassment.

**Mistake:** Assuming the enemy's build without scouting and neglecting defense while teching, leaving yourself vulnerable to early pressure.

**Correction:** Focus on developing economy and technology while preparing to defend against potential early pressure. Strengthen ground forces as you transition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game around 3 minutes with heavy economy and tech investment, army composition unclear; enemy shows Reaper or Marine indicating possible early harassment or push.

**Strategic target:** Develop economy and technology while strengthening ground forces and preparing defense against early pressure.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech and economy without ensuring defensive capability against early harassment.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when more information about enemy army composition and intentions is available.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the early-midgame with a clear army direction and a solid economy.
