# N006 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy defense and moderate economy, while the opponent shows a heavy economy and expansion with a Reaper cue. You need to stabilize and develop. Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper cue, with heavy economy and expansion. Production is moderate, technology is heavy. The opponent may be planning a fast expand or a Reaper harass.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is light or uncertain, technology is heavy, and defense is heavy. Your economy is moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

The opponent's heavy economy and expansion suggest a macro-oriented play. You need to stabilize your defense while developing your own economy and tech.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with minimal information. Your own posture is light or uncertain, and the opponent's is also unclear. The focus is on establishing a solid economy and tech base.

**Strategic target:** Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to offense before your defense is solid, especially if the opponent shows a Reaper cue.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you observe more of the opponent's army composition or production structures, or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable state where you can transition to a more aggressive or macro-oriented plan.
