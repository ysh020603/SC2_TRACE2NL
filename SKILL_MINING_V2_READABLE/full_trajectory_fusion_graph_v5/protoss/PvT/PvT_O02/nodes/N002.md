# N002 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with minimal information. Your own posture is light or uncertain, and the opponent's is also unclear. The focus is on establishing a solid economy and tech base. Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology appear light or uncertain. The opponent's posture is unknown, with no clear pressure or expansion signals.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production and technology are light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition. Economy is light or uncertain, and expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

With limited information, it is safe to focus on a balanced development that keeps options open. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid base for any transition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with minimal information. Your own posture is light or uncertain, and the opponent's is also unclear. The focus is on establishing a solid economy and tech base.

**Strategic target:** Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to offense before your defense is solid, especially if the opponent shows a Reaper cue.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you observe more of the opponent's army composition or production structures, or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a clearer read on the opponent's intentions and can adapt your composition accordingly.
