# N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Reinforcement

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, with a heavy economy and production, facing a Terran ground posture with heavy tech and moderate air presence. Continue strengthening your ground army and technology, while maintaining your economy and production. Keep your defense solid and consider adding more tech units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Their production and tech are heavy, and they have a heavy defense and expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Observers. Your defense is heavy, indicating you have prepared for potential attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and technology, while maintaining your economy and production. Keep your defense solid and consider adding more tech units.

The Terran's heavy ground army requires a strong response. Your heavy economy allows you to invest in both army and tech, giving you an advantage in the late game.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid passive ground buildup

**When this applies:** Late midgame, heavy economy and production, facing Terran ground with heavy tech and moderate air.

**Mistake:** Passively strengthening ground and tech without pushing or scouting, allowing the enemy to amass a large force.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening ground army and tech, but maintain defense and consider adding tech units to prepare for a decisive engagement.

**Why:** Your heavy economy supports both army and tech, giving you an advantage if you stay active and ready.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see a clear Terran tech switch or when you are ready to push.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Late-midgame, heavy economy and production, facing a Terran ground posture with heavy tech and moderate air presence.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening ground army and technology, maintain economy and production, keep defense solid, and consider adding more tech units.

**Veto:** Do not neglect defense while over-investing in tech or economy, leaving you vulnerable to harassment or a decisive enemy push.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see a clear Terran tech switch or when you are ready to push for a decisive engagement.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition that can handle both ground and potential air threats.
