# N001 — Midgame Ground Macro Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Reaching the midgame with a heavy economy and production, facing a Terran ground posture with heavy tech. Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion count and keep a watchful eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Their production and tech are heavy, and they have a heavy defense and expansion posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are heavy, with a ground-oriented army including Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. Defense is light or uncertain, but you have the capacity to strengthen it.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology, while increasing production and economy. Maintain your current expansion count and keep a watchful eye on defense.

Your heavy economy and tech allow you to out-scale the Terran if you avoid early losses. Strengthening your ground forces and tech will prepare you for the midgame clash.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Reaching the midgame with a heavy economy and production, facing a Terran ground posture with heavy tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing ground army and technology, increase production and economy, maintain current expansion count, and keep defense watchful.

**Veto:** Do not passively strengthen ground and tech without pushing or scouting, allowing the enemy to amass a large force.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see a shift in Terran composition (e.g., more air units) or when you are ready to take a more aggressive stance.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground composition with Colossus or Immortal support, and ensure your production can sustain a large army.
