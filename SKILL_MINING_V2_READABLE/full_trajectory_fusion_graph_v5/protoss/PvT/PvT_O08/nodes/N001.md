# N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (~3 min) with own ground-oriented macro posture, moderate production, heavy tech; opponent shows ground posture with heavy production and economy, possible pressure. Continue developing your technology and economy while strengthening your ground army. Increase production and tech, maintain defense, and keep expansion options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Marines. Production and economy appear heavy, technology heavy, but exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with Zealots, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Economy is heavy but expansion is uncertain. Defense is light, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while strengthening your ground army. Increase production and tech, maintain defense, and keep expansion options open.

This early phase favors a macro-oriented approach that builds a strong tech/economy base, enabling flexible responses to opponent's ground-heavy posture.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early game (~3 min) with own ground-oriented macro posture, moderate production, heavy tech; opponent shows ground posture with heavy production and economy, possible pressure, or unknown posture with Marines, heavy economy, moderate production, heavy tech.

**Strategic target:** Continue developing technology and economy while strengthening a flexible ground army; increase production and tech, maintain defense, and keep expansion options open.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to tech or economy at the expense of scouting and defensive readiness; avoid neglecting army production if pressure is imminent.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when opponent's composition or pressure becomes clearer, or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a reinforced ground army and continued tech/economy growth.
