# N011 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers, and their economy is heavy. You have a moderate economy and heavy tech, but your expansion is uncertain. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Their economy is heavy, and they have expanded heavily. Their production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have not committed to a specific army composition. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture.

The opponent's mixed posture suggests they may be vulnerable to air attacks. By transitioning to an air army, you can exploit their lack of anti-air units.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, both sides have had time to develop. Your own posture remains uncertain, but the opponent's is also still unclear.

**Strategic target:** Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition. If the opponent shows a ground posture, strengthen your ground army and consider increasing production and upgrades.

**Veto:** Do not overextend economy without adequate defense; avoid neglecting air defense if the opponent has mixed forces.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's main army composition or when you are ready to push out. If the opponent is passive, consider expanding or teching further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined air army composition, possibly with Void Rays or Phoenixes, to counter the opponent's ground forces.
