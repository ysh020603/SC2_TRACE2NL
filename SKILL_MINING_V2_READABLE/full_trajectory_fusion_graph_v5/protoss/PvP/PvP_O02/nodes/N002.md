# N002 — Late Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both sides have had time to develop. Your own posture remains uncertain, but the opponent's is also still unclear. Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and tech remain light or uncertain, suggesting they may be playing a macro-oriented game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production and tech are still light or uncertain, but you have had time to build up. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition.

In the late midgame, the game can swing based on tech choices. By staying flexible, you can counter the opponent's composition once it becomes clear.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the late midgame, both sides have had time to develop. Your own posture remains uncertain, but the opponent's is also still unclear.

**Strategic target:** Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition. If the opponent has a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals, strengthen your ground army and continue developing economy and tech.

**Veto:** Do not overextend economy without adequate defense; avoid neglecting scouting due to unknown composition.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's main army composition or when you are ready to push out. If the opponent is passive, consider expanding or teching further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition, either ground or air, based on the opponent's observed units and your own tech advantages.
