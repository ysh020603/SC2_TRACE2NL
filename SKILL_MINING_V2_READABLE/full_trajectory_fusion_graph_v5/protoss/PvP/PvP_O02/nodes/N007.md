# N007 — Midgame Ground Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a ground posture with Zealots, and their economy and production are heavy. You have a moderate economy and heavy tech, but your expansion is heavy. Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense while continuing to develop your economy and technology. This is a stabilize-then-develop approach.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Their economy and production are heavy, and they have expanded heavily. They may be preparing a large ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, but you have expanded heavily. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense while continuing to develop your economy and technology. This is a stabilize-then-develop approach.

The opponent's heavy economy and production suggest they are building a large army. By strengthening your ground forces and defense, you can hold off any attack while you develop your own tech.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** In the midgame, both sides have had time to develop. Your own posture remains uncertain, but the opponent's is also still unclear.

**Strategic target:** Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to respond to the opponent's eventual composition. If the opponent shows a ground posture, strengthen your ground army and consider increasing production and upgrades.

**Veto:** Do not overextend economy without adequate defense; avoid neglecting air defense if the opponent has mixed forces.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's main army composition or when you are ready to push out. If the opponent is passive, consider expanding or teching further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition, possibly with Stalkers or Immortals, to counter the opponent's Zealots.
