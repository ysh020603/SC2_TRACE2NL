# N005 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, both players are in early game with unknown army postures, but you have Zealots and Stalkers. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a developing ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

With the opponent's posture unknown, building a solid ground foundation and economy gives you flexibility to adapt.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 3 minutes, both players are in early game with unknown army postures, but you have Zealots and Stalkers.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single tech path without scouting, and do not neglect defense against possible pressure.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you gain more information about the opponent's army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong ground army and economy, ready to transition based on scouting information.
