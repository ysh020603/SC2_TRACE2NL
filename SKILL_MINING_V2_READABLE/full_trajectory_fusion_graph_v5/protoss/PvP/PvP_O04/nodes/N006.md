# N006 — Early-Mid Air Mirror

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 6 minutes, both players have air-oriented armies with Phoenixes and Oracles, and heavy economies. Maintain air presence and strengthen ground forces as a complement. Continue economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Phoenix. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Oracles, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain air presence and strengthen ground forces as a complement. Continue economy and technology development.

The opponent is air-focused, so maintaining air superiority while adding ground support can give you an edge in engagements.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 4 minutes, both players are in early-midgame with heavy economy and technology, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalker cues.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and continue production.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single ground tech path without scouting, and do not neglect defense against possible pressure.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when the opponent's army composition or posture changes, or when you reach a new tech tier.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced air-ground army with a strong economy, ready for midgame.
