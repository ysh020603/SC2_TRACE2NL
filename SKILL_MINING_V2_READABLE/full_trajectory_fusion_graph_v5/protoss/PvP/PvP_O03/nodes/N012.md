DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 4-5 minutes, with the opponent's posture still unknown. Your own army is undefined but you have a Stalker. Increase your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but remain flexible to transition to air if scouting suggests.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues. Their economy appears heavy, technology investment heavy, and production moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, defense is heavy, and technology investment is heavy. Production is moderate, and your army is undefined but includes a Stalker.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but remain flexible to transition to air if scouting suggests.

With limited information, it's important to build a solid foundation. A defensive, technology-focused approach allows you to adapt to the opponent's composition once it becomes clearer.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Zealots observed). Your own army is still undefined, but you have a heavy economy and defense.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's ground-oriented composition. Continue increasing your economy and technology, and maintain a defensive posture.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to ground forces without ensuring anti-air capability; avoid neglecting scouting for potential air transitions.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if you gain information about their tech path.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a stable economy, a defensive structure, and the technology infrastructure to support either a ground or air transition.
