# N006 — Midgame Ground Army vs Mixed Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have a ground army with Stalkers, while the opponent shows a mixed posture with moderate air presence. Strengthen your ground army while considering adding some anti-air capabilities. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a mixed posture with moderate air presence. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, backed by heavy production and technology. Your economy and expansion are strong, and you have moderate defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while considering adding some anti-air capabilities. Maintain your economy and expansion.

The opponent's mixed posture means you need to be prepared for both ground and air threats. A strong ground core with some anti-air support provides flexibility.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame phase where both sides have heavy ground armies and heavy tech. Own army includes Stalkers and Immortals, while opponent shows Zealot cues.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

**Veto:** Avoid being too passive when opponent has heavy ground and detection, allowing them to build up for a timing attack.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of transitioning to air or if you face a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army that can handle both ground and air threats.
