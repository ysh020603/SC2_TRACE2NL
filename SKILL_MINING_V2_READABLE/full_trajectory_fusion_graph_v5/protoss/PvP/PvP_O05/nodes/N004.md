# N004 — Midgame Air Transition with Phoenix and Oracle

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have transitioned to an air-oriented army with Phoenix and Oracle, while the opponent remains ground-focused with Zealots. Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your air army. Maintain your economy and expansion, and keep up production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Zealot cues. They have heavy production and technology, but their air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy air army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your economy, production, and technology are all heavy, and you have strong defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your air army. Maintain your economy and expansion, and keep up production.

Air units like Phoenix and Oracle can harass and control the map, while the opponent's ground army may struggle to deal with a strong air force. Your heavy economy supports this transition.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame phase where both sides have heavy ground armies and heavy tech. Own army includes Stalkers and Immortals, while opponent shows Zealot cues.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

**Veto:** Avoid being too passive when opponent has heavy ground and detection, allowing them to build up for a timing attack.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of transitioning to air or if you face a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a dominant air force while maintaining a solid ground defense to protect your bases.
