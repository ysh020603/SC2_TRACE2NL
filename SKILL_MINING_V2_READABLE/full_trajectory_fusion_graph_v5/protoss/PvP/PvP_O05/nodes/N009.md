# N009 — Midgame Air Transition with Oracle Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have an air army with Phoenix and Oracle, while the opponent shows an air posture with Oracle cues. Maintain your air army and consider adding more Phoenixes or Oracles. Continue developing your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air posture with Zealot, Stalker, and Oracle cues. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate air army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your economy, production, and technology are all heavy, and you have moderate defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your air army and consider adding more Phoenixes or Oracles. Continue developing your economy and technology.

Both sides are investing in air, so you need to maintain air superiority. Phoenixes are good for harassing, while Oracles provide utility.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** Midgame phase where both sides have heavy ground armies and heavy tech. Own army includes Stalkers and Immortals, while opponent shows Zealot cues.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

**Veto:** Avoid being too passive when opponent has heavy ground and detection, allowing them to build up for a timing attack.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of transitioning to air or if you face a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air force while maintaining a solid ground defense.
