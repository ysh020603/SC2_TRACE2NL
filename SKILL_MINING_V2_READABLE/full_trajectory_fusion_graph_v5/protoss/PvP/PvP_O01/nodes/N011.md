DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, both sides show an air posture with Void Rays. You have a mixed army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles. Continue developing your air army, focusing on Void Rays and supporting units. Maintain a strong economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air posture with Void Rays, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy with multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your air army, focusing on Void Rays and supporting units. Maintain a strong economy and tech.

In an air mirror, whoever has more Void Rays and better upgrades will win. Your economy supports a strong air force.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 240-360 seconds, you have a ground army with Zealots, while the opponent may show ground (Zealots/Stalkers) or air (Void Rays). Production and tech are heavy.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army and increase economy. If the opponent shows air, add anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Maintain scouting to identify the opponent's tech path.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single ground unit type without support; avoid neglecting anti-air if the opponent shows air.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's army composition or if they move out.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
