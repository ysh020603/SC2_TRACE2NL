## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building your air army, focusing on Carriers and supporting units. Maintain a strong economy and tech.

Carriers are powerful in the late game. Your air army can outmaneuver the opponent's ground forces.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 420-540 seconds, the opponent may show ground (Zealots, Sentries, Warp Prisms) or air (Void Rays). You have a ground army with Zealots and a strong economy.

**Strategic target:** Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. If the opponent uses Warp Prisms, add Sentries for defense and detection. If the opponent has Void Rays, prioritize anti-air units and consider transitioning to air.

**Veto:** Do not neglect anti-air if the opponent has air; avoid staying passive against a massing air army.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if you need to respond to harass.
