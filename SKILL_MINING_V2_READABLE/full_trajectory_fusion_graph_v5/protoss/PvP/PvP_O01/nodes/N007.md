# N007 — Early-Mid Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, the opponent shows an air posture with Void Rays, while you have a ground army with Zealots. Your economy is strong. Consider transitioning to include anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Continue developing your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air posture with Void Rays, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy. They may be preparing an air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy with multiple expansions. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider transitioning to include anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Continue developing your economy and tech.

Void Rays are strong against ground units. Adding anti-air will help defend against an air attack and maintain map control.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Early-Midgame Air Threat with Void Rays

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, opponent shows air posture with Void Rays. You have a ground army with Zealots and a strong economy.

**Mistake:** Staying pure ground without anti-air, or over-committing to air without proper tech.

**Correction:** Transition to include anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Continue developing your economy and tech.

**Why:** Void Rays are strong against ground units. Adding anti-air will help defend against an air attack and maintain map control.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions back to ground or if you need to commit to air.

## V5 Phase-Conditioned Trajectory Policy

**Applies when:** At around 240-360 seconds, you have a ground army with Zealots, while the opponent may show ground (Zealots/Stalkers) or air (Void Rays). Production and tech are heavy.

**Strategic target:** Strengthen your ground army and increase economy. If the opponent shows air, add anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Maintain scouting to identify the opponent's tech path.

**Veto:** Do not overcommit to a single ground unit type without support; avoid neglecting anti-air if the opponent shows air.

**Live recheck:** Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's army composition or if they move out.
