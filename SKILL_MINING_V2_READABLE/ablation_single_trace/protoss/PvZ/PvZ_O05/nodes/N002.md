# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, transition to a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers while maintaining heavy economy and technology investment. Maintain current army path and continue developing economy and technology; keep defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Possible pressure from Zerg; maintain scouting to detect tech switches or mass units.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with ground army composition, heavy defense and economy, moderate production, heavy technology. Representative units: Zealot, Stalker.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue developing economy and technology; keep defensive posture.

A ground-based army with heavy technology support provides a solid core that can adapt to various Zerg strategies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Incorporate air units like Carriers to diversify the army and counter potential Zerg air or ground compositions.
