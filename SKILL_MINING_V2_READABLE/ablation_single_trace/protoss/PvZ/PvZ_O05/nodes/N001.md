# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and technology base while maintaining defensive readiness. Maintain current development path with safety checks; continue heavy technology investment and moderate production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No specific opponent information available; maintain scouting to detect early pressure or all-ins.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with unknown army composition, heavy defense and economy posture, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Representative unit: Zealot.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks; continue heavy technology investment and moderate production.

Establish a strong foundation for later transitions while staying safe against potential early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers as technology progresses.
