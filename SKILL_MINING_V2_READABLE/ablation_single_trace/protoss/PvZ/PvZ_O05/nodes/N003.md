# N003 — Midgame Mixed Army

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, integrate air units like Carriers into the ground army to create a mixed composition with heavy technology support. Maintain current army path and continue developing economy and technology; keep defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg may have a mix of ground and air units; maintain scouting to detect tech switches or mass expansions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame with mixed army composition (ground and air), heavy defense and economy, moderate production, heavy technology. Representative units: Zealot, Stalker, Carrier.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue developing economy and technology; keep defensive posture.

A mixed army with Carriers provides strong pushing power and can handle a variety of Zerg compositions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to strengthen the army composition and prepare for late-game transitions if needed.
