## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes a balanced macro posture with heavy technology investment, moderate production, and a defensive stance. It transitions from an early unknown army composition to a ground-oriented force, eventually incorporating air units like Carriers in the midgame.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
