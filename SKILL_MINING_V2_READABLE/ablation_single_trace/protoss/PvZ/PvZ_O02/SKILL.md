# PvZ_O02 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O02
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A balanced Protoss macro opening against Zerg, focusing on steady economy and production development while remaining flexible to adapt based on scouting. The opening does not commit to a specific army composition or tech path early, instead aiming to reach a stable midgame with options open.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: light_or_uncertain
- Production: light_or_uncertain
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, you have limited information about the opponent's intentions. Your own economy, production, and technology are all in early development stages.

**Direction:**  
Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and production while keeping safety checks in mind. Avoid overcommitting to any single strategy until you have more information.
