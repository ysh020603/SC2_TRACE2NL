# N003 — Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, you have more information about the opponent's strategy, but your own posture is still light or uncertain. You need to consolidate your position and prepare for the midgame conflicts. Maintain a balanced development path, but start to solidify your army composition and tech choices. Continue to develop with safety checks, ensuring you have defenses against potential attacks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's strategy is becoming clearer. They may be massing a specific unit composition, expanding heavily, or preparing a timing attack. Use your scouting to identify their key threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a light or uncertain economy, production, and technology. Your army composition is still unknown, but you have a ground-leaning tendency. Your posture remains broadly unknown-oriented, so you need to check your current structures and army strength.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, but start to solidify your army composition and tech choices. Continue to develop with safety checks, ensuring you have defenses against potential attacks.

In the midgame, you need to be ready for engagements. A balanced approach with a ground-leaning army gives you flexibility to counter the opponent's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the late-midgame with a stable economy, a defined army composition, and the ability to transition to a lategame strategy.
