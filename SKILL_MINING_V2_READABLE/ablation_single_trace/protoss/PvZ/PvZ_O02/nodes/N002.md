# N002 — Early-Midgame Adaptation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, you still have limited information about the opponent, but you may have some scouting data. Your own economy and production remain light or uncertain. Maintain a balanced development path, but start to lean towards a ground-based army composition. Keep your options open for tech switches if needed. Continue to develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's strategy is still not fully clear. They might be preparing a timing attack, expanding aggressively, or teching to a specific unit composition. Continue scouting to identify their intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a light or uncertain economy, production, and technology. Your army composition is still unknown, and you have not committed to a specific strategy. Your posture remains broadly unknown-oriented, so you need to continue checking your current status.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, but start to lean towards a ground-based army composition. Keep your options open for tech switches if needed. Continue to develop with safety checks.

At this stage, you can start to shape your army composition based on the opponent's likely strategy. A ground-leaning approach is versatile and can transition to air if necessary.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a solid economy and a flexible army composition, ready to respond to the opponent's midgame threats.
