# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, you have limited information about the opponent's intentions. Your own economy, production, and technology are all in early development stages. Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and production while keeping safety checks in mind. Avoid overcommitting to any single strategy until you have more information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's actions are not yet clear. They may be expanding, teching, or preparing an early attack. You should scout to gather information about their base and unit composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a light or uncertain economy, production, and technology. Your army composition and style are unknown, and you have not committed to a specific defensive or offensive posture. Your overall posture is broadly unknown-oriented, so you need to check your current structures, queues, and resources to make informed decisions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and production while keeping safety checks in mind. Avoid overcommitting to any single strategy until you have more information.

In the early game, information is limited, and overcommitting can be punished. A balanced approach allows you to respond to a variety of opponent strategies while building a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a stable economy and production, ready to adapt based on scouting information.
