# N004 — Late-Midgame Preparation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the late-midgame, you have a good understanding of the opponent's strategy, and you need to prepare for the transition to the lategame. Your own posture is still light or uncertain, but you have a foundation to build upon. Maintain a balanced development path, but start to make concrete plans for the lategame. Consider transitioning to a composition that can counter the opponent's likely lategame army. Continue to develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's strategy is now clear. They may be preparing for a lategame composition, such as brood lords or ultralisks, or they may be planning a final push. Use your scouting to anticipate their next move.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a light or uncertain economy, production, and technology. Your army composition is still unknown, but you have a ground-leaning tendency. Your posture remains broadly unknown-oriented, so you need to check your current status and plan for the future.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, but start to make concrete plans for the lategame. Consider transitioning to a composition that can counter the opponent's likely lategame army. Continue to develop with safety checks.

In the late-midgame, you need to set up for the lategame. A balanced approach with a ground-leaning army can be adapted to include air units or spellcasters as needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the lategame with a strong economy, a well-defined army composition, and the ability to execute your chosen strategy.
