## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes a heavy economy and technology investment while keeping production moderate early on. The army composition is initially undefined, then shifts to a ground-based force with Stalkers, and later incorporates Warp Prisms, Observers, Immortals, and Dark Templars. The overall posture remains defensive and macro-oriented, with a focus on expanding and teching up.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning
