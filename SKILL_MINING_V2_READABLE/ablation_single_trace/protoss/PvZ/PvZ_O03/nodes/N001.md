# N001 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, the focus is on establishing a strong economy and technology base while keeping production moderate. Maintain the current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's strategy or army composition at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and no clear army composition yet. Defense is light, and expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

A heavy economy and technology investment provides long-term flexibility and allows for a strong mid-game transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition as technology completes and scouting information becomes available.
