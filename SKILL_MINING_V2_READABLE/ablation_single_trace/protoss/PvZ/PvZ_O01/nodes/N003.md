# N003 — Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, consolidate the army composition and production to match the opponent's strategy. Maintain the current army path and production, but be ready to adapt based on scouting. Continue to expand and tech up while keeping a strong defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is still not fully known, but the possibility of pressure or aggression is present.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, production, technology, and expansion. The army is ground-based with Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, and there is a moderate air presence. Defensive posture is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current army path and production, but be ready to adapt based on scouting. Continue to expand and tech up while keeping a strong defense.

The combination of Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles provides a strong ground-based army with support capabilities. Heavy production and economy allow for sustained pressure or defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-game composition or a timing attack based on the opponent's weaknesses.
