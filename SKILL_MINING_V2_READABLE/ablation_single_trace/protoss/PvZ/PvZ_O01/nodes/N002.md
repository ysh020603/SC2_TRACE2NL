# N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, maintain the economic and technological lead while starting to build a ground-based army. Continue to maintain the current path, focusing on expanding the economy and technology while producing Stalkers for defense. Keep production moderate to allow for flexibility.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is still unknown, but the possibility of pressure is emerging.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, technology, and expansion, with a heavy defensive posture. Production is moderate, and the army is starting to include Stalkers, indicating a ground-oriented direction.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to maintain the current path, focusing on expanding the economy and technology while producing Stalkers for defense. Keep production moderate to allow for flexibility.

The heavy economy and technology investment will pay off in the midgame, and Stalkers provide a versatile defensive unit that can also be used for harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a full midgame posture with heavier production and a more defined army composition.
