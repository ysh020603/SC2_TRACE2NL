# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economic and technological foundation. Maintain current economic and technology development while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No specific information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light defensive posture. Army composition is unknown, and there is no significant air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current economic and technology development while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

A balanced start allows for flexibility in responding to the opponent's actions. Heavy economy and technology investment set up a strong midgame, while moderate production ensures some defensive capability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame by continuing to expand and tech up, while starting to build a defensive army.
