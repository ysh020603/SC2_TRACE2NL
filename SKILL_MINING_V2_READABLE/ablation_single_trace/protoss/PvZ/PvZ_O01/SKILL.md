# PvZ_O01 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A flexible Protoss opening that prioritizes a balanced economy and technology development while keeping defensive options open. Early game focuses on establishing a solid economic base and teching up, with a gradual transition to a ground-based army composition featuring Stalkers and later Immortals and Oracles.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on scouting and live game state.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economic and technological foundation.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current economic and technology development while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
As the game progresses into the early-midgame, maintain the economic and technological lead while starting to build a ground-based army.

**Direction:**  
Continue to maintain the current path, focusing on expanding the economy and technology while producing Stalkers for defense. Keep production moderate to allow for flexibility.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
In the midgame, consolidate the army composition and production to match the opponent's strategy.

**Direction:**  
Maintain the current army path and production, but be ready to adapt based on scouting. Continue to expand and tech up while keeping a strong defense.

**Read for details:** `N003`
