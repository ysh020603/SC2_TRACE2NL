# N003 — Midgame Ground Army

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

With a solid economy and technology, focus on building a ground-based army with heavy production and defense. Maintain current army path, continuing to build up the ground army and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's strategy or composition at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame with a ground-based army composition including Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Heavy economy, production, expansion, and technology. Defense posture is heavy, and pressure posture is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, continuing to build up the ground army and defense.

A strong ground army with heavy production and defense provides a solid foundation for midgame engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
