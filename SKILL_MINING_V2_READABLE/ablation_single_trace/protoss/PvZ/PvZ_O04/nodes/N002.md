# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, increase production and expansion to support a growing economy and technology. Maintain current development path, continuing to build up economy, production, and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's strategy or composition at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with heavy economy, production, expansion, and technology. Army composition is still undefined but starting to include Stalkers alongside Zealots. Defense posture is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, continuing to build up economy, production, and technology.

A strong economy and production base will enable a flexible response to the opponent's strategy later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined ground-based army composition with heavy production.
