# N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, begin to solidify defense and prepare for potential pressure. Maintain current path, but increase defensive readiness and continue tech investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Still limited information, but potential for early pressure exists.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with heavy economy, expansion, and tech. Defense is now heavy, production is moderate, and army is still unknown but starting to form.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current path, but increase defensive readiness and continue tech investment.

Heavy defense and tech provide a safety net against early aggression while setting up for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a ground-oriented army with heavy production.
