# N001 — Early Game Technology Focus

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate production and heavy technology investment, but army composition and posture are still undefined. Maintain current development path while keeping options open. Continue investing in economy and technology, but avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is developing heavily, but your army composition and defensive posture are not yet established. You have a moderate production capacity and are investing heavily in technology. Your representative unit is a Stalker, indicating a potential ground path, but you have not committed to a specific army style.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping options open. Continue investing in economy and technology, but avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

Early game is a period of uncertainty. By focusing on economy and technology, you build a strong foundation that can support multiple strategic directions later. This approach minimizes risk while preserving flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground-based army composition as you gain more information and your technology advantages come online.
