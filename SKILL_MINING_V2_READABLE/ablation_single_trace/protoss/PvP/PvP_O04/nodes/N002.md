# N002 — Ground Macro Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Transition to early-midgame with a clear ground-oriented army style and a heavy expansion posture. Continue developing your ground army and economy while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep an eye out for opportunities to apply pressure, but do not overextend.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is now ground-based with a macro-oriented style, and you have expanded heavily. Your economy remains strong, and you continue to invest in technology. Your production is moderate, and you have a possible pressure posture, meaning you might be able to apply pressure if the opportunity arises.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep an eye out for opportunities to apply pressure, but do not overextend.

With a solid economy and technology lead, you can afford to build a strong ground army. Maintaining a defensive posture reduces risk while you continue to scale up. The possible pressure posture indicates that you may be able to threaten the opponent, but only if you have sufficient information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production capacity and a more defined army composition as you approach the midgame.
