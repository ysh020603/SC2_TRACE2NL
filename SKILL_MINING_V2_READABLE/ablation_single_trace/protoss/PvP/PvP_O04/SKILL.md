### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Heavy Production

**Trigger situation:**

**Direction:**  
Maintain your current army composition and production, but be prepared to adapt based on the opponent's actions. Consider using your Sentries for scouting or defensive purposes, and use your Immortals to counter any heavy ground units the opponent might have.
