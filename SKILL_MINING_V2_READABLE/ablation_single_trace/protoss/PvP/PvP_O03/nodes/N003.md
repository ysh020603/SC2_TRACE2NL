# N003 — Midgame Army Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame with heavy production, heavy technology, defense heavy, economy moderate, representative units include Zealot. Maintain current army path while continuing to develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is still unclear; maintain scouting to detect tech switches or massing armies.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path while continuing to develop.

With heavy production and tech, you can now field a credible army while still having flexibility to adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition based on scouting information.
