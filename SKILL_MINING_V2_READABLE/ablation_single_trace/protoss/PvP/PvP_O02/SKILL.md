## Opening Strategy

A balanced macro-oriented Protoss opening in the PvP matchup, designed to establish a stable economy and production foundation while remaining flexible to adapt based on scouting information. The opening avoids committing heavily to early aggression or tech, instead focusing on a well-rounded build that can transition into various mid-game strategies.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. The opening is designed to be adaptable, with decisions made based on scouting and opponent actions.
