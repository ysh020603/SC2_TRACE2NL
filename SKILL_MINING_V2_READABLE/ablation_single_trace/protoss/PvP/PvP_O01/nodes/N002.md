# N002 — Early Midgame Ground Army Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game enters the early midgame, the expansion posture becomes heavy, and a Stalker is produced, signaling a transition toward a ground-based army. Continue developing the ground army while maintaining the heavy economy and technology investment. Keep production moderate and avoid overextending defensively.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Still no reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early midgame with heavy economy, technology, and expansion. Production is moderate, and the army is starting to form with a Stalker as a representative unit. Defense remains light, and air presence is still uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing the ground army while maintaining the heavy economy and technology investment. Keep production moderate and avoid overextending defensively.

The heavy economy and technology base supports a strong transition into a ground army. Maintaining moderate production allows for flexibility in responding to the opponent's actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition, possibly including Zealots and Stalkers, while continuing to expand and tech up.
