# N001 — Early Game Heavy Tech Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a strong economy and technology base while keeping production moderate and defense light. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to defense or army composition until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light defense. Army composition is unknown, and there is no significant air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to defense or army composition until more information is available.

A heavy technology investment provides long-term flexibility, allowing adaptation to the opponent's strategy once it becomes clearer. Moderate production ensures a stable economy without sacrificing too much early game presence.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and expansion posture as the game progresses, while continuing to develop economy and technology.
