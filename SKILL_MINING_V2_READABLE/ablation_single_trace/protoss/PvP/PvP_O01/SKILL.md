# PvP_O01 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A balanced Protoss opening that prioritizes economy and technology while keeping production moderate and defense light. It aims to reach a heavy economy and technology posture by the early midgame, with a possible transition to a ground-based army.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Heavy Tech Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on establishing a strong economy and technology base while keeping production moderate and defense light.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to defense or army composition until more information is available.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [TRACE] N002 — Early Midgame Ground Army Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
As the game enters the early midgame, the expansion posture becomes heavy, and a Stalker is produced, signaling a transition toward a ground-based army.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing the ground army while maintaining the heavy economy and technology investment. Keep production moderate and avoid overextending defensively.

**Read for details:** `N002`
