# N001 — Early Game Balanced Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the early game, you have a heavy technology investment and a heavy defensive posture, but your production and expansion are light or uncertain. Your army composition is unknown, and you have no confirmed air presence. Maintain your current development path with a focus on economy and technology, while keeping your defensive posture strong. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition or expansion until you have more information about the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Your opponent is Terran, but you have no confirmed information about their army composition, tech choices, or expansion plans. They could be pursuing a variety of strategies, so you must remain alert and gather information through scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy focus on technology and defense. Your economy is also heavy, but your production and expansion are light or uncertain. Your army is not yet defined, and you have no confirmed air units. Your overall posture is still developing, so you need to check your current structures, queues, and resources to make precise decisions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path with a focus on economy and technology, while keeping your defensive posture strong. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition or expansion until you have more information about the opponent's intentions.

By maintaining a balanced approach, you preserve the flexibility to react to whatever the Terran player is doing. Heavy technology investment can lead to powerful mid-game options, while a strong economy supports future production. Keeping defense heavy reduces vulnerability to early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

As you move into the early-mid game, aim to increase your production capacity and begin to define your army composition based on what you have observed from the opponent. Continue to develop your technology and economy, but start to shift toward a more balanced posture.
