# PvT_O04 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O04
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A flexible early-game Protoss opening that emphasizes a balanced economy and technology investment while keeping production light and defensive options open. The strategy is designed to adapt based on live scouting rather than committing to a rigid build.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: light_or_uncertain
- Production: light_or_uncertain
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Balanced Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the early game, you have a heavy technology investment and a heavy defensive posture, but your production and expansion are light or uncertain. Your army composition is unknown, and you have no confirmed air presence.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your current development path with a focus on economy and technology, while keeping your defensive posture strong. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition or expansion until you have more information about the opponent's intentions.

**Read for details:** `N001`
