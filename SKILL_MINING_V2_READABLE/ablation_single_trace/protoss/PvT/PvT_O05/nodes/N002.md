## Summary

Early-midgame phase with still unknown army composition, but production is increasing and air presence is becoming moderate. Continue maintaining your development path, but start to prepare for potential transitions based on what you observe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the Terran opponent's composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology remain heavy, and production is ramping up. You have Zealots and possibly Oracles, indicating a mix of ground and air capabilities.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your development path, but start to prepare for potential transitions based on what you observe.

As the game progresses, you need to be ready to shift your army composition. Maintaining a balanced approach allows you to adapt to the opponent's tech choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile army core that can transition into either ground or air dominance.
