# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early game phase with unknown army composition and light defense, but heavy economy and technology investment. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the Terran opponent's composition or intentions at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are developing strongly, but your army composition and defensive posture are not yet clear. Production is moderate, and you have a Zealot as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production steady.

Early game is about establishing a solid foundation. Heavy economy and technology investment will pay off later, but you need to be ready to adapt if the opponent shows aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing while gathering information about the opponent's strategy.
