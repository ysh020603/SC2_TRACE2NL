# PvT_O05 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A flexible Protoss opening that prioritizes economy and technology while keeping production moderate. It transitions from an unknown early posture into a ground-heavy midgame with heavy production and defense.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**

**Direction:**

**Read for details:** `N003`

### [TRACE] N004 — Late-Midgame Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame phase with a powerful ground army including Colossi, heavy production, and heavy defense.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your army and continue to develop. Consider pushing or expanding if you have an advantage, but keep your defenses strong.
