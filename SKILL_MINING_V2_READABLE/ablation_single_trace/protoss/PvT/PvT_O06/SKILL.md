# PvT_O06 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O06
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes a heavy economy and heavy technology investment while keeping production moderate early on. The army composition is initially undefined, then transitions into a ground-oriented force with Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals. Expansion activity remains light until the late midgame, when it becomes heavier. The overall posture is defensive and macro-focused, with a medium confidence level.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Technology Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, the focus is on establishing a strong economy and technology base. Production is moderate, and the army composition is not yet defined.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your current economic and technology development path. Continue producing workers and investing in technology structures. Keep your options open for army composition.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Midgame Transition to Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
As the game progresses into the early midgame, you begin to define your army composition. Production becomes heavy, and you start producing ground units.

**Direction:**  
Continue producing a ground-based army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Use Observers for vision and to detect hidden threats. Maintain your economic and technology development.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
In the midgame, your army composition is now well-defined with a mix of Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Production is heavy, and you are maintaining a strong economy.

**Direction:**  
Continue producing your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more expansions to support your production. Use Observers to scout and detect incoming threats.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [TRACE] N004 — Late Midgame Expansion and Stability

**Trigger situation:**  
In the late midgame, you have secured additional expansions and your economy is strong. Your army is well-developed and you are in a stable position.

**Direction:**  
Continue to produce units and maintain your economy. Consider adding more production structures if needed. Use your Observers to keep vision on the map and detect any opponent movements.

**Read for details:** `N004`
