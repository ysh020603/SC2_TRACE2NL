# N003 — Midgame Army Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, your army composition is now well-defined with a mix of Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Production is heavy, and you are maintaining a strong economy. Continue producing your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more expansions to support your production. Use Observers to scout and detect incoming threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is Terran, and you may have scouted their composition. They could be preparing for a timing attack or expanding. Be aware of potential drops or harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Your defense is still light or uncertain, but you have a possible pressure posture. You have not expanded yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue producing your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more expansions to support your production. Use Observers to scout and detect incoming threats.

A well-rounded ground army can handle most Terran strategies. Expanding will give you the resources to sustain a long game. Observers provide valuable information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive stance if you have an army advantage, or continue to macro if the game is even.
