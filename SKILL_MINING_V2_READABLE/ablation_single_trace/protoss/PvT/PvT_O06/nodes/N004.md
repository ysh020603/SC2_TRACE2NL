# N004 — Late Midgame Expansion and Stability

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the late midgame, you have secured additional expansions and your economy is strong. Your army is well-developed and you are in a stable position. Continue to produce units and maintain your economy. Consider adding more production structures if needed. Use your Observers to keep vision on the map and detect any opponent movements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is Terran, and they may be preparing for a late-game push or transitioning to a more advanced composition. You need to be ready for anything.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late midgame with a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. You have expanded, and your defense is still light or uncertain, but you have a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to produce units and maintain your economy. Consider adding more production structures if needed. Use your Observers to keep vision on the map and detect any opponent movements.

With a strong economy and a solid army, you are in a good position to transition into the late game. Maintaining vision and production will allow you to respond to any threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition that can counter the opponent's final strategy.
