# N002 — Midgame Transition to Ground Army

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early midgame, you begin to define your army composition. Production becomes heavy, and you start producing ground units. Continue producing a ground-based army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Use Observers for vision and to detect hidden threats. Maintain your economic and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is Terran, and you may have some information about their composition. They could be preparing for a push or expanding themselves. Keep an eye on their production and army movements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is starting to form, with Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals appearing. Your defense is still light or uncertain, and you have not expanded yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue producing a ground-based army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Use Observers for vision and to detect hidden threats. Maintain your economic and technology development.

A ground army with these units provides a solid core that can handle most Terran compositions. Observers give you crucial information and detection. Heavy production ensures you can reinforce quickly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if the opponent is passive, or prepare defenses if they are aggressive.
