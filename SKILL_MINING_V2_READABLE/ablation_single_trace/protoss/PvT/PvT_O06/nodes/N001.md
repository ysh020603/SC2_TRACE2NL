# N001 — Early Game Technology Focus

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, the focus is on establishing a strong economy and technology base. Production is moderate, and the army composition is not yet defined. Maintain your current economic and technology development path. Continue producing workers and investing in technology structures. Keep your options open for army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is Terran, but their specific actions are not yet known. They may be following any standard opening, so it is important to gather information.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and heavy technology investment. Production is moderate, and your army composition is unknown. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you have not expanded yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current economic and technology development path. Continue producing workers and investing in technology structures. Keep your options open for army composition.

A heavy economy and technology investment will give you a strong foundation for the midgame. By not committing to a specific army composition early, you can adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent's intentions.
