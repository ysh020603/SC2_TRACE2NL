# N004 — Air Army and Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Your army is now air-oriented with Void Rays, and you are in the late-midgame. Maintain your air army and continue to expand your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- You have limited information about the opponent, but you are aware of a possible pressure from them.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology is heavy, production is moderate, and defense is light. Your army is air-oriented with Stalkers, Sentries, and Void Rays, and you have a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your air army and continue to expand your economy and technology.

An air army with Void Rays can be very powerful, but it requires a strong economy to sustain. Heavy technology investment supports upgrades and transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army again if the opponent's composition demands it, or continue to strengthen your air army.
