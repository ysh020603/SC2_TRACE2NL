## Summary

- You have limited information about the opponent, but you are aware of a possible pressure from them.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology is heavy, production is moderate, and defense is light. Your army is mixed with Stalkers and Void Rays, and you have a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your mixed army composition and continue to develop your economy and technology.

A mixed army provides flexibility against various opponent compositions. Heavy economy and technology support continued production and upgrades.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
