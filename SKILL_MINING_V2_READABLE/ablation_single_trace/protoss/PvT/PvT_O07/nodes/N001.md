# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, your army composition is not yet defined, and you are focusing on economy and technology. Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology is heavy, production is moderate, and defense is light. Your army composition is unknown, and you have no representative units yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

Early game is about establishing a solid foundation. Heavy economy and technology investment set up for later transitions, while moderate production keeps options open.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a ground-oriented army with Stalkers as you approach the early-midgame.
