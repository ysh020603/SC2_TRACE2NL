# PvT_O08 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O08
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A balanced macro-oriented opening that prioritizes economy and technology while keeping production moderate early, then scaling up. It transitions from an unknown army composition to a ground-based force with heavy tech investment.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Economy and Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game with moderate production and heavy tech investment, economy heavy, expansion light.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current economy and technology development, keep production steady, and avoid overcommitting to army composition.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Scaling Production and Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame with heavy production, heavy economy, and heavy expansion, still heavy tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue scaling production and expansion while maintaining tech development. Keep army composition flexible.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Ground Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame with a ground-based army, heavy production, heavy economy, heavy expansion, and heavy defense.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your ground army and continue to develop economy and tech. Be ready to defend against possible pressure.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [TRACE] N004 — Advanced Ground Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame with a ground army including Immortals, heavy production, economy, expansion, and defense.

**Direction:**  
Continue to develop your ground army and tech. Consider adding Dark Templar for harassment or to counter specific compositions.

**Read for details:** `N004`
