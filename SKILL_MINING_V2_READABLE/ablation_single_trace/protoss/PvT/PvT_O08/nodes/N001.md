# N001 — Early Economy and Tech Focus

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early game with moderate production and heavy tech investment, economy heavy, expansion light. Maintain current economy and technology development, keep production steady, and avoid overcommitting to army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the opponent's composition or strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology investment is heavy, but production is only moderate. Army composition is unclear, with a Zealot as a representative unit. Defense is light, and expansion is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current economy and technology development, keep production steady, and avoid overcommitting to army composition.

Early game is about building a solid foundation. Heavy tech investment sets up for later options, while moderate production keeps you safe without overextending.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and expansion posture while continuing tech development.
