# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and technology foundation. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the opponent's strategy or army composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and no clear army composition yet. Defense is light, and expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

A balanced macro opening allows flexibility to adapt to the opponent's actions without committing to a specific army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a clear army direction.
