# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economic and technological base. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping defensive options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's strategy or composition at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Army composition is not yet defined, but a zealot is present as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping defensive options open.

A balanced macro approach allows for flexibility in responding to the opponent's actions later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined ground-oriented army with additional production.
