# N003 — Midgame Power Spike

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, the army composition is well-established with a mix of core and advanced units. Maintain the current army path and continue to expand and tech up, while keeping an eye on defensive needs.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's strategy is still not fully known, but pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame with a heavy ground army, including zealots, stalkers, observers, and immortals. Economy and production are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current army path and continue to expand and tech up, while keeping an eye on defensive needs.

A well-rounded ground army with support units can handle a variety of threats and set up for a strong mid-game push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards colossi for additional splash damage and a stronger army composition.
