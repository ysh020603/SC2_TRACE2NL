# N002 — Ground Army Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, the army composition becomes more defined with ground units. Continue developing the ground army and economy while maintaining defensive readiness.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's strategy is still unclear, but there is a possibility of pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, and heavy technology investment. Representative units include zealots and stalkers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing the ground army and economy while maintaining defensive readiness.

A strong ground army with technological support provides a solid foundation for mid-game engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Expand further and add more advanced units like observers and immortals.
