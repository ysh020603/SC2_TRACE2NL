# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economic and technological foundation. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the opponent's actions or composition yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and expansion are heavy, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Your army composition is not yet defined, but you have a Marine representative. Your defense is light or uncertain, and your pressure posture is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate.

Establishing a strong economy and tech base early allows for flexible responses to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a ground-oriented army with a heavier defense posture as the game progresses.
