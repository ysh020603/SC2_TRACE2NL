# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, your army becomes ground-oriented and your defense posture strengthens. Continue maintaining your current army path and defensive posture, while keeping an eye on potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Still no confirmed information about the opponent's composition or strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and expansion remain heavy, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with a macro posture, and your defense is heavy. Pressure is possible, but not confirmed.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current army path and defensive posture, while keeping an eye on potential pressure.

A solid ground army with heavy defense provides a stable foundation to respond to Zerg's potential tech switches or aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for midgame transitions based on observed opponent behavior, such as adding air units or tech if needed.
