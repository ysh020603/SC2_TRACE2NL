# TvZ_O06 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O06
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that develops a heavy economy and technology base while maintaining a defensive posture. Early game focuses on Marines and infrastructure, transitioning to a ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks and support units by midgame. The opening is flexible, allowing adaptation based on scouting information.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and infrastructure. The opponent's strategy is unknown, so maintain a defensive posture while scouting.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep production moderate and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
As the game enters the early-midgame, the army begins to take shape with a ground focus. The opponent may start applying pressure, so maintain a defensive posture while continuing to expand.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing the ground army and maintain a defensive posture. Keep production and technology progressing, and consider adding Siege Tanks for defense.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Siege Tank Core

**Trigger situation:**  
In the midgame, the army is built around Siege Tanks and Marines, providing a strong defensive and positional advantage. Production is heavy, and technology continues to advance.

**Direction:**  
Continue producing Siege Tanks and Marines, and consider adding support units like Ravens or Thors for detection and anti-air. Maintain a defensive posture while pushing out when advantageous.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [TRACE] N004 — Late-Midgame Diversification

**Trigger situation:**  
In the late-midgame, the army becomes more diverse with Thors, Ravens, and possibly air units. The game is approaching a critical point where a decisive engagement or transition to late game occurs.

**Direction:**  
Maintain a balanced army composition with strong ground and anti-air capabilities. Continue expanding and teching, and prepare for a potential late-game transition. Use Ravens for detection and interference.

**Read for details:** `N004`
