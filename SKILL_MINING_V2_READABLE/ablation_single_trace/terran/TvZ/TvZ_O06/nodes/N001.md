# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and infrastructure. The opponent's strategy is unknown, so maintain a defensive posture while scouting. Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep production moderate and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No information about the opponent's strategy is available at this stage. Expect the possibility of early aggression or economic play.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with a heavy economy and technology investment. Production is moderate, and the army is not yet defined. Representative units include Marines. Defense is heavy, indicating a focus on safety.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep production moderate and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

A balanced macro approach allows flexibility to respond to the opponent's actions. Heavy economy and technology investment set up for a strong midgame, while defense prevents early game losses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition as the game progresses, while continuing to expand and tech up.
