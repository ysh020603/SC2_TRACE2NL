# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game enters the early-midgame, the army begins to take shape with a ground focus. The opponent may start applying pressure, so maintain a defensive posture while continuing to expand. Continue developing the ground army and maintain a defensive posture. Keep production and technology progressing, and consider adding Siege Tanks for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be preparing for aggression or expanding. Their composition is not fully known, but expect potential pressure from Zerg units such as Zerglings or Roaches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Marines and Reapers. Production is moderate, and technology investment remains heavy. Defense and economy are both heavy, indicating a strong foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing the ground army and maintain a defensive posture. Keep production and technology progressing, and consider adding Siege Tanks for defense.

A ground army with Marines and Reapers provides early map presence and scouting. Heavy defense and economy ensure safety while transitioning to a stronger midgame composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and more advanced ground composition, including Siege Tanks, to handle midgame engagements.
