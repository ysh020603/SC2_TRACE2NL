## Summary

In the midgame, the army is built around Siege Tanks and Marines, providing a strong defensive and positional advantage. Production is heavy, and technology continues to advance. Continue producing Siege Tanks and Marines, and consider adding support units like Ravens or Thors for detection and anti-air. Maintain a defensive posture while pushing out when advantageous.
