# N004 — Late-Midgame Diversification

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the army becomes more diverse with Thors, Ravens, and possibly air units. The game is approaching a critical point where a decisive engagement or transition to late game occurs. Maintain a balanced army composition with strong ground and anti-air capabilities. Continue expanding and teching, and prepare for a potential late-game transition. Use Ravens for detection and interference.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent likely has a strong Zerg army with a mix of ground and air units. Expect potential tech switches or massed units.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Thors, and Ravens. Production is heavy, and technology is heavy. Air presence is moderate, indicating some investment in air units. Defense and economy are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced army composition with strong ground and anti-air capabilities. Continue expanding and teching, and prepare for a potential late-game transition. Use Ravens for detection and interference.

A diverse army with Thors and Ravens provides strong anti-air and utility. Heavy economy and production allow for sustaining a large army and transitioning to late-game units if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game army with potentially Battlecruisers or other high-tech units, while maintaining a strong economy and defense.
