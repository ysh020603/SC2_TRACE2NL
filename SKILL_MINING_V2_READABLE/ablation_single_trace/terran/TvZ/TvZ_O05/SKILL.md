## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture with early Reaper and Marine production, transitioning into a heavier ground army with Siege Tanks by the early midgame. The economy and expansion are prioritized, with heavy technology investment.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
