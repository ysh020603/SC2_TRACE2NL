# N002 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As you enter the early midgame, your posture remains ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Maintain your current army path, economy, expansion, production, technology, and defense. Continue developing with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent may be expanding or preparing a tech switch. Their exact composition is still unclear, so you need to keep scouting and be ready to adapt.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You still have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, and now Siege Tanks are appearing. Your economy and expansion remain heavy, and production is increasing. Defense is still light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path, economy, expansion, production, technology, and defense. Continue developing with safety checks.

The early midgame is a critical transition period. Maintaining a balanced macro posture while adding Siege Tanks gives you a solid ground army that can defend and apply pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the midgame with a stronger ground army and a robust economy, ready to respond to the opponent's tech choices.
