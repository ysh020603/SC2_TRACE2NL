# N003 — Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, the army becomes more rounded with Medivacs for mobility and healing, while production and tech remain heavy. Maintain the current path, focusing on army composition and upgrades while keeping defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg may have a mix of ground and air units, possibly including Mutalisks or Banelings. Exact composition is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current path, focusing on army composition and upgrades while keeping defensive structures.

Medivacs add sustain and mobility, allowing for better map control and multi-pronged pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for late-midgame by adding Marauders and increasing air presence.
