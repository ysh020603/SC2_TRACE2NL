# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and early tech while keeping a light defensive posture. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping a light defensive presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent is likely to be expanding and may apply early pressure. Exact composition is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with a ground-oriented army, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy, but defense is light. Representative units include Marines and Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping a light defensive presence.

A heavy economy and tech investment sets up for a strong midgame, while light defense is sufficient if scouting indicates no immediate threat.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and more robust army composition as the game progresses.
