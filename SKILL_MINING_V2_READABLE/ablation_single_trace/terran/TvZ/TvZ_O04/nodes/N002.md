# N002 — Early-Mid Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game enters the early-mid phase, production becomes heavier and Siege Tanks are added to the army. Maintain the current path, continuing to build up production and tech while keeping an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg may be transitioning to a more tech-heavy composition or preparing a timing attack. Exact composition is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, and heavy technology. Economy and expansion remain heavy. Representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current path, continuing to build up production and tech while keeping an eye on defense.

Siege Tanks provide strong defensive and positional strength, supporting a macro-oriented approach.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue expanding and teching while preparing for potential midgame engagements.
