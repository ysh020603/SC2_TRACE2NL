# N002 — Ground Macro Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame with a ground-oriented army composition (Marine, Reaper) and moderate production, with possible pressure from the opponent. Maintain current ground army path and continue developing economy and technology, while keeping an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; be aware of possible pressure and scout to confirm.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented army composition, including Marines and Reapers. Your economy and expansion posture are heavy, technology investment is heavy, and production is moderate. Your defense posture is light or uncertain, and pressure posture is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current ground army path and continue developing economy and technology, while keeping an eye on defense.

A ground-oriented army with heavy tech support provides a solid foundation for midgame transitions, and maintaining economy allows for flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production posture and stronger defense as the game progresses.
