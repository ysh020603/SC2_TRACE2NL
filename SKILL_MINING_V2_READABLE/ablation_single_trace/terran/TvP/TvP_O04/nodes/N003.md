# N003 — Midgame Heavy Production

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame with a ground-oriented army (Marine, Reaper, Siege Tank) and heavy production, with possible pressure from the opponent. Maintain current ground army path and continue heavy production and technology investment, while keeping defense strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; maintain awareness of possible pressure and scout for tech switches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented army composition, including Marines, Reapers, and Siege Tanks. Your economy and expansion posture are heavy, technology investment is heavy, and production is heavy. Your defense posture is heavy, and pressure posture is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current ground army path and continue heavy production and technology investment, while keeping defense strong.

A heavy ground army with siege tanks provides strong defensive and offensive capabilities, and heavy production supports sustained engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more versatile army composition if the opponent shows air or heavy spellcaster usage.
