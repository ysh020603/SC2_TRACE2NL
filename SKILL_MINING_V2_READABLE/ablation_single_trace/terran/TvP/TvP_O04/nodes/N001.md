# N001 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early game with a Reaper representative unit and heavy technology investment, but army composition and pressure posture are still unclear. Maintain current development path with a focus on technology and economy, while keeping production moderate and staying flexible.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available at this stage; rely on scouting to infer their intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a Reaper as a representative unit. Your economy and expansion posture are heavy, technology investment is heavy, but production is only moderate. Your army domain and style are unknown, and your defense posture is light or uncertain. Your pressure posture is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with a focus on technology and economy, while keeping production moderate and staying flexible.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base without committing to a specific army composition, allowing adaptation based on scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground-oriented army composition as scouting reveals opponent's strategy.
