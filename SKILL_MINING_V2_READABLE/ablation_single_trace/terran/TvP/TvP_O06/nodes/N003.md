# N003 — Midgame Heavy Production and Air Integration

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, maintain heavy production and technology while integrating air units. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may have a mix of ground and air units; their composition is still uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame with ground-oriented army, heavy defense, heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, Banshees, and Widow Mines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

Leverage heavy production to field a versatile army that can respond to various threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to late-midgame with continued heavy production and potential for Battlecruisers.
