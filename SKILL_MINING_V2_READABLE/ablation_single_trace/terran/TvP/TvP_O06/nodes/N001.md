# N001 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and early ground forces. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Protoss; early scouting may reveal their opening, but their exact composition is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with a ground-oriented army, moderate production, heavy economy and technology investment, and light defense. Representative units include Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

Establish a strong economic and technological foundation to support future transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to early-midgame with heavier defense and production.
