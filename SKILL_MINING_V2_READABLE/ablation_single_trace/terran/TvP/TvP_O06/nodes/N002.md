# N002 — Early-Midgame Defense and Production Ramp

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, increase defense and production while maintaining heavy economy and technology. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing for aggression or expanding; their exact tech path is unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with ground-oriented army, heavy defense, heavy economy and expansion, moderate to heavy production, and heavy technology. Representative units include Marines and Widow Mines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

Strengthen defensive capabilities to deter potential pressure while continuing to grow the economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to midgame with heavier production and air presence.
