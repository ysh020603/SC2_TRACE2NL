# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, with limited information about the opponent's intentions. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and expansion while keeping production and tech balanced.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the Protoss opponent's build or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with moderate economy and production, heavy expansion posture, but army composition and defense are uncertain. Technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and expansion while keeping production and tech balanced.

Establish a solid economic and technological foundation to support future transitions, without committing to a specific army composition prematurely.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to a more defined mid-game posture with clearer army direction.
