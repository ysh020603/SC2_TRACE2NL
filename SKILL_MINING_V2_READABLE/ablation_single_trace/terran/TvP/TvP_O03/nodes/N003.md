# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Entering the midgame with a clear ground-based army composition and heavy economy. Maintain the current ground-macro path, focusing on army upgrades and tech transitions to support the composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Protoss opponent may be preparing for a timing attack or tech switch, but details are unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-based with Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines. Defense is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current ground-macro path, focusing on army upgrades and tech transitions to support the composition.

A heavy ground army with defensive structures can hold most midgame threats while allowing for tech upgrades to improve army efficiency.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for late-midgame by adding air support or additional tech units.
