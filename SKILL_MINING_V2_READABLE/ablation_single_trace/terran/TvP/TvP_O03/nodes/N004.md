# N004 — Late-Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the late-midgame, with a strong ground army and moderate air presence. Continue maintaining the current path, but consider adding more air units or tech to counter potential Protoss air or spellcasters.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Protoss opponent may be transitioning to a late-game composition, but their exact plan is still uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, production, and technology. Army includes Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Defense is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining the current path, but consider adding more air units or tech to counter potential Protoss air or spellcasters.

With a solid ground core, adding Medivacs and potentially Vikings or Liberators can provide mobility and support, while Siege Tanks anchor the defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for the late game by diversifying the army and maintaining a strong economy.
