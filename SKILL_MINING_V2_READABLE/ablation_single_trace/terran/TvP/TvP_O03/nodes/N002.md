# N002 — Early-Mid Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-mid phase, with some scouting information gathered. Continue maintaining the current path, but start considering defensive upgrades and army composition shifts.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Still limited information, but the opponent may be preparing for pressure or tech play.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, technology is heavy. Army composition is still unknown, but Marines are present. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining the current path, but start considering defensive upgrades and army composition shifts.

With heavy tech investment, it's important to ensure the army can defend against potential early pressure while continuing to expand.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Move towards a ground-based army composition with stronger defensive capabilities.
