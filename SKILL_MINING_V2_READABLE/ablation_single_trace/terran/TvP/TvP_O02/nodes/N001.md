# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, with limited information about the opponent's intentions, the focus is on establishing a solid foundation. Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and production while keeping options open for adaptation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's actions are not yet clear; they could be preparing for a rush, expanding, or teching. No specific threats have been identified.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all in early stages, with no clear commitment to a specific strategy. Army composition is undefined, and you have minimal defensive structures.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and production while keeping options open for adaptation.

Early game uncertainty calls for a flexible approach to avoid being caught off guard by aggressive or economic strategies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame state with a functioning economy and production capacity.
