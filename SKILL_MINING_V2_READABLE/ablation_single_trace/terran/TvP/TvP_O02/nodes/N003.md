# N003 — Midgame Adaptation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, you have a reasonable economy and production, but the opponent's strategy is still not fully known. You need to start making more concrete decisions. Maintain a balanced approach but be ready to adapt based on scouting. Consider adding defensive structures or units to counter potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be preparing a timing attack, expanding, or transitioning to a specific tech. You have not yet identified their primary threat.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light, but you have the potential to expand further or tech up. Your army is still undefined, and you have not committed to a specific composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced approach but be ready to adapt based on scouting. Consider adding defensive structures or units to counter potential threats.

Midgame is a critical point where the opponent's strategy becomes more defined, and you need to be prepared to respond without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition or tech path based on the opponent's actions.
