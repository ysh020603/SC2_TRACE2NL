# N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, you have a basic economy and production, but the opponent's strategy is still not fully scouted. Continue maintaining a balanced approach, but start considering potential transitions based on scouting information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be expanding, teching, or preparing an attack. You have not yet identified their primary threat.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light, but you have the capacity to expand or tech. Your army is still undefined, and you have not committed to a specific composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining a balanced approach, but start considering potential transitions based on scouting information.

Maintaining flexibility allows you to respond to the opponent's tech or army choices without being locked into a disadvantageous position.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare to transition into a more defined strategy once the opponent's plan becomes clearer.
