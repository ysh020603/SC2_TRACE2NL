## Opening Strategy

A balanced macro-oriented opening that focuses on a solid ground-based army composition with a strong economy and defense. The build transitions from early game Marines to a heavier ground force with Marauders and Reapers, while maintaining a heavy defensive and economic posture.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
