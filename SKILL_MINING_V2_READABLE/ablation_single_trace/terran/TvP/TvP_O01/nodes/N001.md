# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and technology foundation while keeping production moderate. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and expansion are heavy, technology is heavy, but production is only moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined, with only a few Marines as representative units. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

Early game is about building a strong foundation. Heavy economy and technology investment will pay off later, but production should be scaled up gradually to avoid overextension.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground-oriented army with increased production and defense.
