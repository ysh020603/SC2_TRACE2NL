# TvP_O01 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvP_O01
- Matchup: Terran vs Protoss
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that develops a heavy economy and technology base while keeping production moderate early, then transitions to a ground-oriented army with heavy production and defense. The opening is flexible and observation-driven, not a fixed build order.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and technology foundation while keeping production moderate.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
As the game progresses, you begin to define your army composition, leaning towards ground forces with Marines and Reapers.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and maintain heavy defense. Keep production moderate but be ready to increase it if needed.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Heavy Production

**Trigger situation:**  
In the midgame, you have a heavy production capacity and a well-rounded ground army, including Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your heavy production and continue to build a balanced ground army. Keep defense strong and be prepared to adapt to opponent moves.

**Read for details:** `N003`
