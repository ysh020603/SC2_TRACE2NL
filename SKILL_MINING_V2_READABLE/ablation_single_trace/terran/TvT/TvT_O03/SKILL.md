## Opening Strategy

A balanced macro-oriented opening in TvT that focuses on establishing a solid economy and production foundation while keeping options open for tech or army composition based on scouting. The early game is characterized by a Marine-heavy ground force with moderate production and tech, and a heavy expansion posture.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
