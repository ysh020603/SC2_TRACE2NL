## Opening Strategy

A ground-oriented macro opening that emphasizes heavy technology and defense while expanding steadily. The plan is to build a solid foundation with Marines and Siege Tanks, then transition into heavier units like Thors in the mid-game.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
