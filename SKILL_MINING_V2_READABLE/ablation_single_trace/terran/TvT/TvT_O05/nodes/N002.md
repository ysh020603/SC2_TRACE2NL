# N002 — Ground Army Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

The army composition becomes ground-oriented with Marines as the representative unit. Production and expansion are now heavy, indicating a commitment to a macro-oriented ground strategy. Continue developing the ground army and expanding, while maintaining a heavy economy and technology investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Still limited information, but the opponent is likely also developing a macro strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early midgame, economy heavy, technology heavy, production heavy, army ground, defense light, expansion heavy, air presence light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing the ground army and expanding, while maintaining a heavy economy and technology investment.

A strong ground army with heavy production and expansion can provide a solid foundation for midgame engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mixed army composition as the game progresses, incorporating air units if needed.
