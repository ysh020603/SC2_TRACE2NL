# N004 — Late Midgame Mixed Army

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

The army composition becomes mixed, with both ground and air units. The economy and production remain heavy, indicating a strong macro position. Maintain the mixed army composition and continue to develop the economy and technology, while keeping an eye on potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is still uncertain, but they are likely also in a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Late midgame, economy heavy, technology heavy, production heavy, army mixed, defense light, expansion heavy, air presence moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the mixed army composition and continue to develop the economy and technology, while keeping an eye on potential threats.

A mixed army provides flexibility to respond to various opponent compositions and strategies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to adapt the army composition based on live observations and prepare for late-game transitions.
