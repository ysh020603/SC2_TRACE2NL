# TvT_O01 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O01
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A balanced macro opening that develops economy and technology while keeping production moderate, then transitions into a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with moderate production and heavy technology investment, economy and expansion heavy.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development path: continue economy and technology investment, keep production moderate, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Ground Army Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with ground army orientation, heavy production and technology, economy and expansion heavy.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your ground army path and continue heavy production and technology. Keep expanding and developing your economy.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy ground army, heavy production and technology, heavy defense and expansion.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your current army composition and production. Continue to expand and tech up, but also consider adding air units or upgrades to counter potential threats.

**Read for details:** `N003`
