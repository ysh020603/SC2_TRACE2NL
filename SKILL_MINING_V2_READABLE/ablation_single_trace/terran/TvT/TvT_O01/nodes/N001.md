# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate production and heavy technology investment, economy and expansion heavy. Maintain current development path: continue economy and technology investment, keep production moderate, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited at this stage. Their army composition, production, and technology are unknown. Be alert for early pressure or cheese.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a developing posture. Production is moderate, technology investment is heavy, and you are expanding economically. Your army composition is not yet defined, but you have Marines as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path: continue economy and technology investment, keep production moderate, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until more information is available.

A balanced macro approach allows you to adapt to opponent actions. Heavy technology investment provides options, while moderate production keeps you safe from early all-ins.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a ground-oriented army with heavier production as you move into the early-midgame.
