# N002 — Ground Army Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with ground army orientation, heavy production and technology, economy and expansion heavy. Maintain your ground army path and continue heavy production and technology. Keep expanding and developing your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is still uncertain, but you may have some scouting information. Be aware of possible pressure or tech plays.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented army. Production is heavy, technology is heavy, and you are expanding. Your army includes Marines, and you may have Thors and Medivacs as you progress.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army path and continue heavy production and technology. Keep expanding and developing your economy.

A ground army with heavy production and technology provides a solid foundation for midgame engagements. Continuing to expand ensures a strong economy for sustained production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame posture with a more defined army composition and defensive structures.
