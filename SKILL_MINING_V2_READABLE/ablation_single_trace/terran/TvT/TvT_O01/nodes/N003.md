# N003 — Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy ground army, heavy production and technology, heavy defense and expansion. Maintain your current army composition and production. Continue to expand and tech up, but also consider adding air units or upgrades to counter potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is still uncertain, but you may have scouted some units. Be prepared for potential tech switches or large engagements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy ground army, including Marines, Thors, and Medivacs. Production and technology are heavy, and you have a strong defensive posture. Your economy is strong with multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army composition and production. Continue to expand and tech up, but also consider adding air units or upgrades to counter potential threats.

A heavy ground army with Thors and Medivacs is strong in TvT, but you need to be flexible to counter air or siege tank compositions. Maintaining a strong economy allows for tech switches if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition with a mix of ground and air units, or a more specialized army based on opponent's composition.
