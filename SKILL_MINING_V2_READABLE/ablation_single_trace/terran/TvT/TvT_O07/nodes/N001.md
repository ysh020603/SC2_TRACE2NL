# N001 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and teching up. Production is moderate, and the army composition is not yet defined. Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until you have more information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's actions are not yet clear. They could be going for an early aggressive push or a macro-oriented build. Maintain awareness of potential early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production is moderate, and your army is not yet formed. You have a light defense and no clear expansion yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until you have more information.

A strong economy and tech lead will allow you to transition into a powerful mid-game army. Keeping production moderate conserves resources for tech and expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent's intentions.
