# N002 — Mid-Game Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, you start to build a heavier defense and increase production. Your army is still undefined, but you are preparing for a ground-based composition. Continue to build up your economy and technology while increasing production. Start to invest in units that will form the core of your army, such as Marines and Siege Tanks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be starting to show their hand. They could be preparing for a push or expanding. Keep scouting to understand their composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology. Your defense is now heavy, and production is increasing. Your army is still not fully formed, but you are leaning towards a ground-based force.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to build up your economy and technology while increasing production. Start to invest in units that will form the core of your army, such as Marines and Siege Tanks.

A heavy defense protects your economy while you tech up. Increasing production allows you to build a larger army for the mid-game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a full ground-based army with Siege Tanks and Marines, and consider adding air support if needed.
