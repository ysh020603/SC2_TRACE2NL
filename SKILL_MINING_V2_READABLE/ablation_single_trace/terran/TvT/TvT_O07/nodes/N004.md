## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to build your army and consider adding Thors for additional anti-air and anti-armor. Push out to take map control or attack the opponent's bases.

A well-rounded army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, Banshees, and Thors is strong in TvT. Taking map control allows you to deny expansions and force engagements on your terms.
