# TvT_O07 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O07
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A macro-oriented Terran opening that prioritizes a strong economy and technology progression while keeping production moderate early on. The build transitions into a heavy ground-based army composition with Siege Tanks and Marines, supplemented by Banshees or Thors for air support. The approach is flexible, relying on live scouting to adapt to the opponent's actions.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on scouting information and opponent behavior.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning with air support

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and teching up. Production is moderate, and the army composition is not yet defined.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production moderate. Avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition until you have more information.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Mid-Game Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
As the game progresses, you start to build a heavier defense and increase production. Your army is still undefined, but you are preparing for a ground-based composition.

**Direction:**  
Continue to build up your economy and technology while increasing production. Start to invest in units that will form the core of your army, such as Marines and Siege Tanks.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Heavy Production and Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
You have reached the mid-game with a heavy economy and production. Your army is now ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders. You are starting to expand.

**Direction:**  
Continue to build your ground army and expand. Consider adding air support, such as Banshees, to harass or defend against air threats.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [TRACE] N004 — Late-Midgame Army Composition

**Trigger situation:**  
In the late-midgame, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion. Your army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Banshees. You are well-positioned for a strong push.

**Direction:**  
Continue to build your army and consider adding Thors for additional anti-air and anti-armor. Push out to take map control or attack the opponent's bases.

**Read for details:** `N004`
