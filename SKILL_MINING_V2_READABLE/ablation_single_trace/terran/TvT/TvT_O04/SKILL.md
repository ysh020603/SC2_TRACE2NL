# TvT_O04 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A balanced macro-oriented Terran opening that prioritizes economy and technology while maintaining a flexible ground army. Early Reaper presence provides scouting and light harassment, transitioning into a heavier ground composition with Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [TRACE] N001 — Early Game Reaper Scout and Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with a Reaper as the representative unit, heavy economy and technology posture, but army composition and pressure unknown.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Use the Reaper for scouting to gather information about the opponent's expansion and tech choices.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [TRACE] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro and Tech Investment

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with a ground army style, moderate production, and heavy technology. Possible pressure from the opponent.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and technology. Maintain scouting to detect any incoming pressure or tech switches. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [TRACE] N003 — Midgame Heavy Ground Army and Tech Advantage

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with a heavy ground army composition including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Heavy production and technology.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your heavy ground army and continue to tech up. Consider adding additional production or upgrades to support a larger army. Use your army to control the map and deny expansions.

**Read for details:** `N003`
