# N003 — Midgame Heavy Ground Army and Tech Advantage

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame phase with a heavy ground army composition including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Heavy production and technology. Maintain your heavy ground army and continue to tech up. Consider adding additional production or upgrades to support a larger army. Use your army to control the map and deny expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is still uncertain, but you should be prepared for a variety of threats, including air units or heavy ground pushes. Your scouting should be ongoing.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Your production is heavy, and your technology is advanced. Your economy is strong, and you have multiple expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your heavy ground army and continue to tech up. Consider adding additional production or upgrades to support a larger army. Use your army to control the map and deny expansions.

A heavy ground army with Siege Tanks provides strong defensive and offensive capabilities. Maintaining tech and production ensures you can adapt to the opponent's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-midgame army with additional upgrades and possibly a more diverse composition, including air support if needed.
