# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro and Tech Investment

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with a ground army style, moderate production, and heavy technology. Possible pressure from the opponent. Continue developing your ground army and technology. Maintain scouting to detect any incoming pressure or tech switches. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be applying pressure or expanding. You have not confirmed their exact composition, but you should be prepared for possible aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have committed to a ground-oriented army style with moderate production. Your economy and technology are heavy, and you have a Reaper for scouting. Your army composition is still developing, with potential for Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology. Maintain scouting to detect any incoming pressure or tech switches. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

A ground macro posture with heavy tech provides a solid foundation for midgame fights. Maintaining scouting helps you adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a heavier ground composition with Siege Tanks and Medivacs, while continuing to expand and tech up.
