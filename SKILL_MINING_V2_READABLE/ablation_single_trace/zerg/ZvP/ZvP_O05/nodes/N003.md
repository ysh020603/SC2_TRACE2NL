# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and expansion, and moderate to heavy technology. Maintain the current ground-oriented army path, continuing to expand economy and production while keeping defense strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may have a mix of gateway units and tech, but their exact composition is still uncertain. Possible pressure or timing attacks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army now includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, with heavy production and a strong economy. Technology is moderate to heavy, and defense remains heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current ground-oriented army path, continuing to expand economy and production while keeping defense strong.

A strong ground army with Roaches provides a solid backbone against many Protoss compositions, while heavy economy supports future tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for late-midgame by adding Infestors and Overseers for spellcasting and detection.
