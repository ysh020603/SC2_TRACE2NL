# N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with moderate to heavy economy and production, still light or uncertain technology. Continue maintaining the current path, focusing on economy and production growth while keeping defense strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may be expanding or teching; their exact build is still unclear. Possible pressure remains.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, with production increasing. Economy is becoming heavier, and expansion is heavy. Defense remains heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining the current path, focusing on economy and production growth while keeping defense strong.

Building a strong economy and production allows for flexibility in responding to Protoss tech choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a solid ground army and the ability to tech into Roaches or other units as needed.
