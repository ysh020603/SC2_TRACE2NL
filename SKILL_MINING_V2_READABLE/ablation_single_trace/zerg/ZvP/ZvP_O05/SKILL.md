## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Protoss opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture, building a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, while keeping technology and air investment light or uncertain. The approach emphasizes steady economy and production growth with a heavy defensive posture, adapting based on live observations.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
