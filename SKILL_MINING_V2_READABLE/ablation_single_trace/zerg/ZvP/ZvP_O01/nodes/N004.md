# N004 — Late-Midgame Preparation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Late-midgame phase, around 10-12 minutes, with a heavy ground army and technology. Maintain your current path, but be prepared to transition to a more specialized army composition if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss is likely to have a developed army and possibly a third base. Expect a major push or tech transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is now heavily ground-oriented with heavy production and technology. Defense is heavy, and you have Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current path, but be prepared to transition to a more specialized army composition if needed.

Your strong economy allows you to tech into higher-tier units or mass produce. Heavy defense protects against timing attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game army composition that counters the Protoss tech path.
