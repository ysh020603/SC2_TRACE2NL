# N003 — Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame phase, around 7-9 minutes, with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Maintain the current army path, but be ready to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may have a mix of gateway units and tech, possibly including air threats. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground posture is now backed by heavy production and moderate technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Overseers provide detection.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current army path, but be ready to adapt based on scouting.

With a strong economy, you can support a larger army and tech. Overseers help against DTs or other cloaked units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for late-midgame by increasing technology and possibly adding more army.
