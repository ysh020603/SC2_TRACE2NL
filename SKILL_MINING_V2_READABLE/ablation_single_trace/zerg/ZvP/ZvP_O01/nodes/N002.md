# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, around 4-6 minutes, with Zerglings and Queens. Continue maintaining the current path, keeping up with economy and production while staying alert.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may be expanding or preparing a tech transition. Possible pressure remains.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground-oriented posture continues with moderate production and light technology. Economy and expansions remain heavy. Zerglings and Queens are present.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining the current path, keeping up with economy and production while staying alert.

Sustain economic growth and build a modest army to deter early aggression. Flexibility is key to respond to Protoss tech choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a solid economy and a larger ground army.
