## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Protoss opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture, prioritizing economy and expansions while maintaining a ground-oriented army with light air presence. The build is flexible and adapts based on scouting information.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate to heavy
- Technology: light to heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning
