# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and early defenses. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and expansions while keeping a light defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the opponent's composition or strategy yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game, with a heavy economy and expansion posture, but light defense and technology. Production is moderate, and the army is ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and expansions while keeping a light defense.

A strong economy is the foundation for Zerg's mid-game power spike. Early expansions allow for more production and tech later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defensive posture with additional Queens and tech as the game progresses.
