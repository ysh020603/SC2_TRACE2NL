# N003 — Midgame Army Development

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, focus on building a strong ground army and maintaining map control. Maintain the current army composition and continue to expand and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the opponent's composition or strategy yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, with a heavy economy, expansions, and defense. Production is heavy, and technology is moderate. The army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current army composition and continue to expand and tech.

A diverse ground army can handle various threats, and a strong economy supports continuous production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame posture with even heavier production and potential tech upgrades.
