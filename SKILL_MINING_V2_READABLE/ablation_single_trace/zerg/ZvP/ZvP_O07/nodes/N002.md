# N002 — Early-Mid Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

After establishing the early economy, begin transitioning to a more defensive and tech-oriented posture. Maintain the current path, continuing to expand and tech up while keeping a strong defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No confirmed information about the opponent's composition or strategy yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-mid game, with a heavy economy and expansions, heavy defense, and moderate production. Technology is light to moderate, and the army includes Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current path, continuing to expand and tech up while keeping a strong defense.

Queens provide defense and creep spread, while tech allows for better army compositions later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and tech posture, adding Roaches and Zerglings.
