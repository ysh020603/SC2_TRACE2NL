# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with continued ground-oriented army and moderate production. Economy and expansion remain heavy, technology still light or uncertain. Maintain current army path and continue economic expansion. Keep production and technology at current levels, but be ready to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may be transitioning to a more defined strategy. Watch for tech choices or army composition shifts.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is still ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens, production is moderate, and you maintain a heavy economy and expansion posture. Technology investment remains light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue economic expansion. Keep production and technology at current levels, but be ready to adapt based on scouting.

Consolidate your economic advantage while staying flexible to counter potential Protoss tech or aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for the midgame by increasing production and technology when appropriate.
