# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, now including Roaches, and heavy production and technology investment. Economy and expansion remain heavy. Maintain current army path and continue heavy production and technology development. Keep expanding and reinforcing your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss likely has a developed army and tech. Be aware of potential air threats or powerful ground compositions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Production and technology are heavy, and you maintain a heavy economy and expansion posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue heavy production and technology development. Keep expanding and reinforcing your economy.

Leverage your economic and production advantage to field a strong ground army and tech up for the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized army composition based on opponent's tech and army composition.
