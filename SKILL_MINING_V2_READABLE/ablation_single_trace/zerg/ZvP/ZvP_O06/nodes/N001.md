# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, you are in the early game phase with a ground-oriented macro posture. Maintain current army path and continue developing economy and expansions, while keeping an eye on potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is Protoss, but their specific actions are not yet known. They may be preparing an early pressure or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and light technology investment. Your army is ground-based, with Zerglings and Queens as representative units. Defense is light, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue developing economy and expansions, while keeping an eye on potential pressure.

A balanced macro start allows you to adapt to the opponent's strategy without committing too early.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame phase while maintaining a solid economy and ground army.
