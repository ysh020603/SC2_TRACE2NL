# N003 — Midgame Tech Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, begin investing in technology while maintaining a strong economy and production. Maintain current army path, continue teching up while keeping production and economy strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss opponent, likely has a tech choice. Possible pressure or timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy and expansion, moderate to heavy production. Ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, continue teching up while keeping production and economy strong.

To prepare for the midgame power spike and be ready to counter the opponent's tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame state with heavy production and technology.
