# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, continue expanding and producing Zerglings and Queens to maintain map presence. Maintain current army path, continue expanding and producing units while keeping an eye on potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss opponent, likely expanding or preparing for a timing. No specific information available.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production. Ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, continue expanding and producing units while keeping an eye on potential pressure.

To secure a strong economy and map control, enabling a smooth transition to midgame tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a solid economy and the ability to tech up.
