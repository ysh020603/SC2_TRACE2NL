# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and early defensive structures. Maintain current macro path, focusing on economy and expansion while keeping defensive options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss opponent, early game. No specific information available yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game, with a heavy economy posture and light or uncertain technology. Ground-oriented with moderate production. Representative units include Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current macro path, focusing on economy and expansion while keeping defensive options open.

To build a strong economic foundation that allows for flexible responses to the opponent's actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a stable economy and the ability to produce Zerglings and Queens.
