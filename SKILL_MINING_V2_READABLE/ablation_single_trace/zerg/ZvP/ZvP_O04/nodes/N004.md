# N004 — Late-Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the late-midgame, consolidate your position with a strong ground army and heavy economy. Maintain current army path, continue producing and teching while ensuring defense is solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss opponent, likely has a strong army composition. Possible late-game tech or all-in.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Late-midgame, heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Ground-oriented with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, continue producing and teching while ensuring defense is solid.

To build a powerful army that can withstand the opponent's attacks and transition to late-game if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to late-game with a versatile army and the ability to adapt to the opponent's composition.
