## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Terran opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture, with a heavy economy and expansion focus, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology investment. The army is ground-oriented with Queens and Zerglings, and later Roaches, while maintaining flexibility for adaptation based on live observations.
