# N003 — Midgame Tech and Army

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, invest in technology and add Roaches to the army composition. Maintain the current macro path, focusing on technology and army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, but their composition and timing are still uncertain. Possible pressure remains a concern.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, with a heavy economy and expansion posture, but still light defense and air presence. Production is moderate, technology is heavy. Representative units include Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current macro path, focusing on technology and army composition.

Roaches provide a durable front-line unit, and heavy technology investment enables upgrades and advanced units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing the army and tech, adapting to the opponent's composition.
