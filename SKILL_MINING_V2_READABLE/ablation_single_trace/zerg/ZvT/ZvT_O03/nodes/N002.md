# N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, begin adding Zerglings for map control and potential pressure. Continue maintaining the current macro path, adding Zerglings for map presence and scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, but their composition and timing are still uncertain. Possible early pressure remains a concern.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, with a heavy economy and expansion posture, but still light defense and air presence. Production is moderate, technology is moderate. Representative units include Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining the current macro path, adding Zerglings for map presence and scouting.

Zerglings provide map control and can scout or apply pressure, while the economy continues to grow.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a stronger ground army and heavier technology investment.
