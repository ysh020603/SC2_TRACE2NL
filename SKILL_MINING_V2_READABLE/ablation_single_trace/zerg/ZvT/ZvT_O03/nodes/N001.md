# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and early defense. Maintain current macro path, focusing on economy and expansion while keeping light defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, but their specific actions are not yet clear. Maintain awareness for possible early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game, with a heavy economy and expansion posture, but light defense and air presence. Production is moderate, and technology is moderate. Representative units include Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current macro path, focusing on economy and expansion while keeping light defense.

A strong economy and early expansion provide a foundation for later tech and army production. Queens provide defense and creep spread.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to early-midgame with a stable economy and a few Zerglings for map presence.
