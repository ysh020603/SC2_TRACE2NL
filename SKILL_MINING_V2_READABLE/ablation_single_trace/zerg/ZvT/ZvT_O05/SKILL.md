# ZvT_O05 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O05
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Terran opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture, with a heavy economy and expansion focus, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology investment. The army is ground-oriented, with Queens as the representative early unit, transitioning to Hydralisks and Overseers in the midgame. The overall tempo is to develop with safety checks, maintaining current directions while remaining flexible to adapt based on live observations.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
