# N004 — Late-Midgame Air Integration

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Late-midgame, air presence is heavy, army includes Mutalisks, still ground-oriented overall. Maintain the current army composition and continue macroing, but be ready to adapt to the opponent's response.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran may be responding to your air presence, possibly building anti-air or preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have added Mutalisks to your army, giving you air presence. Economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current army composition and continue macroing, but be ready to adapt to the opponent's response.

Mutalisks provide harassment and map control, but you need to keep your ground army strong to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a balanced army with both ground and air capabilities, and continue expanding.
