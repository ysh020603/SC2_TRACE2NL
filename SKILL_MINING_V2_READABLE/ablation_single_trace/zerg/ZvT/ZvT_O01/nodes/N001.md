# N001 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early game, own posture is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens, moderate economy, heavy expansion intent. Maintain current army path, keep expanding, and develop economy while staying alert for potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, but their composition and tech are not yet clear. They may be preparing for early pressure or macroing up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and are expanding aggressively. Economy is moderate, technology is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, keep expanding, and develop economy while staying alert for potential early pressure.

Establish a strong economic base and map presence early, while keeping the option to react to the opponent's first moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a solid economy and multiple bases.
