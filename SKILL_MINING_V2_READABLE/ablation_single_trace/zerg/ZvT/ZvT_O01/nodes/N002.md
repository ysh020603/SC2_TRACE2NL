# N002 — Early-Midgame Macro Sustain

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame, economy is heavy, expansion is heavy, still ground-based with Zerglings and Queens. Maintain the current macro path, keep expanding, and continue producing from your existing structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran may be transitioning to a specific composition, but it is not yet clear. They could be preparing for a timing attack or expanding themselves.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, you have multiple bases, and your army is still primarily Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, tech is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain the current macro path, keep expanding, and continue producing from your existing structures.

A heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent later, but you need to stay flexible to defend or counterattack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a strong economy and the ability to tech switch if needed.
