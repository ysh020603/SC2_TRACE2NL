## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture, developing economy and expansions while keeping ground-oriented production and light technology. The approach is flexible, allowing adaptation based on live observations.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning
