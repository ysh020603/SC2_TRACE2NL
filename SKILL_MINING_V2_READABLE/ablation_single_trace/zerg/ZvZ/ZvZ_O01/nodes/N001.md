# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economic and defensive foundation. Maintain current macro path, prioritizing economy and expansions while keeping production moderate and technology light.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; they may be pursuing a similar macro-oriented approach or could apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy posture, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with no significant units yet. Defense is light, and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current macro path, prioritizing economy and expansions while keeping production moderate and technology light.

A balanced macro start allows you to react to opponent moves without committing to a specific tech or army composition early.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to the next phase with a stable economy and a small ground force for safety.
