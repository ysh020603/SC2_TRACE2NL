# N002 — Early-Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As you enter the early-midgame, you have some Zerglings and Queens, and your economy remains strong. Continue maintaining your current path, focusing on economy and expansions while keeping production moderate and technology light.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is still unclear; they might be preparing for aggression or expanding themselves.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansions are heavy, and defense is still light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current path, focusing on economy and expansions while keeping production moderate and technology light.

This approach keeps you flexible to transition into a more specific strategy once you have better scouting information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare to transition into a more defined strategy based on opponent's actions, such as teching up or increasing army production.
