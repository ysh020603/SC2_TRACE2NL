# ZvZ_O06 Balanced Macro

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O06
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: balanced macro opening
- Method: Single Trace

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that focuses on a balanced macro posture, with a ground-oriented army and a heavy economy and expansion focus. Technology investment is initially light or uncertain, but becomes heavier as the game progresses. The opening is designed to be flexible, allowing adaptation based on live observations.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
