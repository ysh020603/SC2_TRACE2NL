# N002 — Early-Midgame Queen-Centric Macro

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, with a ground-oriented macro posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology investment. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, technology, and defense. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is still limited; you only have partial observations. They are Zerg, but their specific composition and intentions are uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Queens as the representative unit, heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and technology. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, technology, and defense. Develop with safety checks.

Continue building a strong economy and expansion lead while using Queens for defense and creep spread. The light technology suggests a focus on macro, but you must remain alert to potential aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a heavier technology investment and a more defined army composition, while maintaining the macro lead.
