# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

In the midgame, you have a solid economy and production, allowing you to expand your army. Maintain your current army path and continue to build up your forces, while keeping an eye on potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is still not fully known, but they may be preparing for a larger engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented posture. Your economy is heavy, and production is now heavy as well. Your army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and your defense is still light. Pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue to build up your forces, while keeping an eye on potential threats.

With a strong economy and production, you can field a larger army and be ready for midgame fights.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to grow your army and consider tech upgrades or additional expansions.
