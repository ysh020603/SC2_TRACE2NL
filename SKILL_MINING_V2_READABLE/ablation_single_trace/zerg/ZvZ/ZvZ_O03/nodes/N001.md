# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and production foundation. Maintain your current path of developing economy and production while keeping an eye on potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition and intentions are not fully known. They may be preparing for an early attack or expanding themselves.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented posture. Your economy is heavy, and you have moderate production and technology. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens, and your defense is light. There is a possibility of pressure from the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current path of developing economy and production while keeping an eye on potential threats.

A balanced macro approach allows you to respond to various opponent strategies without committing too early.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue building your economy and production, and be ready to adapt based on scouting.
