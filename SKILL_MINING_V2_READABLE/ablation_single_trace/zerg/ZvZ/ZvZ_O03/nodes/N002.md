# N002 — Early-Midgame Macro Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, maintain your macro focus while preparing for potential transitions. Continue to maintain your current army path and economy, but start considering tech or production upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's actions are still unclear. They might be building up for an attack or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame, still with a ground-oriented posture. Your economy remains heavy, and production is moderate. Your army still consists of Zerglings and Queens, and your defense is light. Pressure from the opponent is still possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to maintain your current army path and economy, but start considering tech or production upgrades.

Consolidating your macro advantage gives you more options later, while staying safe against early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare to transition to a heavier production and possibly add Roaches to your army.
