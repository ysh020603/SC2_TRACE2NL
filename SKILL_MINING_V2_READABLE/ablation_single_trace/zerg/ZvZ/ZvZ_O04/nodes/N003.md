# N003 — Midgame Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

Entering the midgame, the focus shifts to maintaining a strong ground army and potentially adding Roaches. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Continue developing with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition and strategy are still not fully known; maintain awareness of potential threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame with heavy economy and expansion, ground army of Zerglings and Queens, with Roaches appearing later. Defense is heavy, technology becomes heavier.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Continue developing with safety checks.

A heavy ground army with Roaches provides a solid foundation for midgame engagements while the economy supports further transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more robust army composition and potentially higher technology.
