# N002 — Early-Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game progresses, consolidate the economy and maintain defensive structures. Continue maintaining current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Keep developing with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's strategy is still unclear; possible pressure remains a concern.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame with heavy economy and expansion, still ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense remains heavy, technology still light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Keep developing with safety checks.

Consolidating the economy and defenses prepares for a strong midgame transition while staying adaptable.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for the midgame by ensuring a robust economy and defensive setup.
