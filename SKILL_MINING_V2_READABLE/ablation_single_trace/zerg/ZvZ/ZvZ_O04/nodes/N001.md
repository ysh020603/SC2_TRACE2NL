# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economic and defensive foundation. Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg, but specific actions are not yet clear. Maintain awareness of possible early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game with moderate economy, heavy expansion posture, and a ground-oriented army of Zerglings and Queens. Defense is heavy, and technology investment is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Develop with safety checks.

A balanced macro approach allows for flexibility to respond to opponent's actions while building a strong economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a stable economy and defensive capabilities.
