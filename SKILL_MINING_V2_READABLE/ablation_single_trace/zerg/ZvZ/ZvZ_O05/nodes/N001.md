# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

At the start of the early game, the focus is on establishing a strong economy and expansion while maintaining a light defensive posture. Maintain current macro path: continue expanding and droning while keeping a light defense. Produce Queens for creep spread and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available at this stage; rely on scouting to detect any early pressure or all-in.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Queens as representative units. Your defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current macro path: continue expanding and droning while keeping a light defense. Produce Queens for creep spread and defense.

A heavy economy and expansion posture aims to outgrow the opponent. Queens provide map control and defense against early aggression. Light technology keeps options open for tech switches later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more army-focused posture as you approach the mid-game, adding Zerglings for map presence and potential pressure.
