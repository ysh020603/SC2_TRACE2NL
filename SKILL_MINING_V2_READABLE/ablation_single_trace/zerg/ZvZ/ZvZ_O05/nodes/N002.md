# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Transition

## Node Type

TRACE

## Summary

As the game transitions to the early-midgame, you begin adding Zerglings to your army composition while continuing to expand and tech up. Continue maintaining your current army path: produce Zerglings for map control and potential pressure, while keeping up economy and expansions. Consider adding tech structures if the situation allows.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is still limited; maintain scouting to detect any tech switches or mass army movements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and expansion posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current army path: produce Zerglings for map control and potential pressure, while keeping up economy and expansions. Consider adding tech structures if the situation allows.

Zerglings provide mobility and map presence, complementing Queens for defense. A heavy economy supports a larger army and faster tech later. Maintaining flexibility is key in ZvZ.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition (e.g., Roaches or Mutalisks) based on opponent's build and your tech investments.
