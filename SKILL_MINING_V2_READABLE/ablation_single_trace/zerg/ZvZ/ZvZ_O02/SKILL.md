## Opening Strategy

A flexible Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes a balanced economy and tech progression, allowing for adaptation based on scouting information. The opener avoids committing to a specific all-in or greedy build, instead focusing on a solid foundation with options to transition into various mid-game strategies.

Develop a balanced macro posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
