# N008 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and moderate production, with Marines and Reapers. Enemy has no reliable combat-unit cues, but is likely on a ground macro posture. Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Increase production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and moderate production, but no confirmed combat units. They are likely expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate production, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense and continue developing your economy and technology. Increase production.

With no confirmed enemy units, it's safe to focus on economy and tech. Increasing production prepares you for any aggression.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify completed parent production structures before add-ons and dependent units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined ground army composition.
