# N004 — Late Midgame Ground and Air Flexibility

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Void Rays, while the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Hellions. Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining air support from Void Rays. Increase production and technology, and consider adding more air units if the opponent lacks anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Hellions. Their defense is heavy, and they have a heavy economy and production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology, with a ground-oriented army that includes Void Rays. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining air support from Void Rays. Increase production and technology, and consider adding more air units if the opponent lacks anti-air.

The opponent's ground army is strong, but your Void Rays provide an advantage if they lack anti-air. You can leverage this to gain map control.
