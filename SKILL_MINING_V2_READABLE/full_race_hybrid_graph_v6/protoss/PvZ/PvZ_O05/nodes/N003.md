# N003 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech. Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy remains ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy expansion. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is still undefined, but you have a strong economy and heavy technology. Defense is heavy, and expansion is now solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

As the game progresses, stabilizing your defense and economy while continuing tech development prepares you for a strong midgame transition.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid being too passive; seek map control

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech.

**Mistake:** Tempting to be too passive, missing opportunities to gain map control or punish over-expansion.

**Correction:** Increase defense and economy, strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech. Maintain air and expansion.

**Why:** As the game progresses, stabilizing your defense and economy while continuing tech development prepares you for a strong midgame transition.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when your army composition becomes clear or if the opponent shows signs of tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a defined army and a solid economy.
