# N011 — Early Game Unknown Posture with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and technology. You have a heavy economy and production. Continue developing your economy and technology, while maintaining a flexible army composition. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's posture, but they have a heavy economy and technology. They may have no reliable combat-unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, but your army composition is unknown. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, while maintaining a flexible army composition. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline.

With limited information, it is safe to focus on macro and technology, while preparing for a ground-oriented response.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify completed parent production structures before add-ons and dependent units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's posture becomes clearer.
