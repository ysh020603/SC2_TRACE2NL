## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground with Marines, Ghosts, Hellions, Medivacs, own ground with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, Overseers. Increase air, strengthen air, continue economy, increase upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Marines, Ghosts, Hellions, Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech, ground and air mix.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air, strengthen air, continue economy, increase upgrades.

Use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.
