DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground posture with Marine/Reaper, own Hydralisk/Mutalisk/Queen/Overseer. Own air moderate. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and expansions, increase upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with Marine, Reaper cues. Heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Hydralisks, Mutalisks, Queens, Overseers. Heavy production and tech, heavy economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and expansions, increase upgrades.

Mutalisks provide mobility and harassment. Continue macro to support a strong air force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
