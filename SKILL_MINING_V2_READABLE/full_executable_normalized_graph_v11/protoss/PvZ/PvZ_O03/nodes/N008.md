# N008 — Late-Midgame Ground Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers and Dark Templar, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding Colossi or High Templar. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Hydralisks and Roaches, heavy production and tech. They are macro-ing heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Dark Templar, indicating a tech advantage. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding Colossi or High Templar. Maintain economy and expansion.

Dark Templar can harass and force detection. A tech-heavy ground army can out-muscle Zerg in the late game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with a powerful ground army and tech advantage.
