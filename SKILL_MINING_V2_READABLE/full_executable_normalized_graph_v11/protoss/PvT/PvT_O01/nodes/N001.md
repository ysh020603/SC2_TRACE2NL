# N001 — Early-Midgame Tech/Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240-360 seconds, you have a heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. The opponent shows a ground posture with possible early pressure. Continue developing your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and maintain defensive readiness.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. Your army is ground-oriented with Sentries and Stalkers, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and maintain defensive readiness.

This phase rewards economic and tech growth while staying safe against potential early pressure. A strong ground core with Sentry support provides defensive options.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid neglecting air defense

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 240-360 seconds, with heavy economy and tech investment, moderate production, and opponent showing ground posture with possible early pressure.

**Mistake:** Neglecting air defense, as the opponent may transition to air later, or over-extending your army without proper scouting.

**Correction:** Continue developing your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and maintain defensive readiness.

**Why:** This phase rewards economic and tech growth while staying safe against potential early pressure. A strong ground core with Sentry support provides defensive options.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have a clear read on the opponent's tech or army composition, or when you are ready to transition to a more specialized composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame state with a solid ground army and tech advantage.
