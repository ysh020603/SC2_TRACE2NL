## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 5 minutes, the opponent's posture is unknown, but you have a ground army with Stalkers and a heavy economy. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

With the opponent's posture unknown, building a solid ground army and economy gives you flexibility to adapt.
