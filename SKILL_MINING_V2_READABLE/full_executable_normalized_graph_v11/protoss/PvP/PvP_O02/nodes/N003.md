# N003 — Early Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, you have had time to develop your economy and tech. The opponent's posture is still uncertain, but you have some information. Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep production flexible to adapt to incoming information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and tech are light or uncertain, indicating a possible economic or tech-focused opening.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production and tech are still light or uncertain, but you have a foundation. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep production flexible to adapt to incoming information.

In the early midgame, committing too early to a specific army composition can be punished. A balanced development approach allows you to respond to the opponent's tech or aggression once it becomes visible.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify powered production and prerequisites before adding tech or combat-unit entries.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to a more defined midgame posture, either by transitioning to a ground-heavy army or by continuing to develop your economy and tech.
