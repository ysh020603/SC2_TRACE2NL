# N005 — Early-Mid Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground army with heavy tech, opponent ground with light air. Continue tech development, maintain ground army, increase economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground-oriented with Queen, heavy economy, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Marines and Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue tech development, maintain ground army, increase economy.

Tech advantage allows for stronger army compositions later.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing tech, confirm opponent's tech path and army composition. If opponent shows tech that counters your planned composition, adjust tech choices accordingly and maintain a balanced army.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent tech or army composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a tech-heavy midgame army.
