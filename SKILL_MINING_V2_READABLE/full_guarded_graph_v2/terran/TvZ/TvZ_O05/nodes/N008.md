# N008 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground army, opponent ground with moderate air presence. Maintain current army path, consider air defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground-oriented with Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen, heavy production, moderate tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, consider air defense.

Adapt to opponent air presence while maintaining ground strength.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before considering air defense, verify opponent's air presence and own anti-air capabilities. If opponent's air presence is unclear or own anti-air is insufficient, prioritize scouting and defensive structures.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent air presence becomes clearer.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that handles both ground and air.
