# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground army with heavy production/tech, opponent heavy ground with possible air. Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue tech and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy heavy ground army with Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen, heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Hellions, Banshees.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue tech and production.

Maintain ground dominance while adding air flexibility to counter potential tech switches.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before adding air units, confirm opponent's anti-air capabilities and own economy stability. If opponent has strong anti-air or own economy is unstable, prioritize ground army reinforcement and defensive positioning.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent tech or air presence changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced ground-air composition.
