# N007 — Early Game Reaper Harass

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own moderate production and heavy tech, using Reapers. Opponent ground macro with light defense. Use your Reaper to harass the opponent's economy and scout. Continue to develop your economy and tech, and consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, but their defense is light, making them vulnerable to harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper for harassment, with moderate production and heavy tech. Economy and expansions are heavy, defenses are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use your Reaper to harass the opponent's economy and scout. Continue to develop your economy and tech, and consider expanding.

Reaper harassment can disrupt the opponent's economy and force reactions, while you continue to macro behind it.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before using the Reaper for harassment, confirm that your economy and tech are stable. If the opponent's defense strengthens or they show signs of aggression, prioritize defensive play and recall the Reaper to avoid losses.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's defense strengthens or when you have a clear army lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger army while maintaining pressure on the opponent.
