# N004 — Early Game Heavy Production Alternative

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own heavy production and tech, ground army. Opponent similar to N001 but with lighter defense. Maintain your production and tech, but consider applying light pressure to exploit the opponent's light defense. Continue expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is on a ground macro path with Zerglings and Queens, but their defense is light, suggesting they might be vulnerable to pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy production and tech, with a ground army of Marines. Economy and expansions are heavy, and defenses are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your production and tech, but consider applying light pressure to exploit the opponent's light defense. Continue expanding.

Your heavy production can allow you to field a larger army quickly. Pressuring the opponent can disrupt their macro while you continue to develop.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before applying pressure, confirm that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient. If the opponent's defense strengthens or they show signs of a counterattack, prioritize defensive reinforcement and delay aggressive moves.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's defense strengthens or when you have a clear army lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger army while keeping the opponent off-balance.
