# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own ground-oriented macro posture, heavy economy and tech, moderate production. Opponent appears as a ground macro Zerg with light tech and moderate production. Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Zerg opponent with a ground posture, likely Zerglings and Queens. Their economy is heavy, but tech and production are still light or moderate, suggesting they are also in a macro-oriented phase.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economic and technological foundation, with a ground army centered on Marines. Production is moderate but can be increased. Defenses are heavy, suggesting a safe development path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and consider expanding further.

With both players in a macro phase, the one who can leverage technology and economy effectively will gain an advantage. Your heavy tech investment can provide superior units later, so focus on reaching that point safely.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient to hold early aggression. If scouting reveals an opponent tech switch or early pressure, prioritize defensive production and delay further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have a significant army advantage or when scouting reveals a change in opponent's posture (e.g., tech switch or aggression).

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger ground army composition, possibly adding Siege Tanks or other tech units, while continuing to expand.
