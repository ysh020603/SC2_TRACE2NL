# N009 — Late-Midgame Stabilization and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with own heavy ground army, moderate air, and heavy economy. Opponent has heavy production and tech, with Mutalisks. Stabilize your defenses against potential Mutalisk harassment. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding anti-air units like Thors or Marines with upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has added Mutalisks to their composition, indicating a potential air threat. Their production and tech are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Economy and expansions are heavy, production and tech are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defenses against potential Mutalisk harassment. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding anti-air units like Thors or Marines with upgrades.

Mutalisks can be a threat to your economy, so defensive structures and anti-air are important. Your heavy economy allows you to invest in both defense and army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your anti-air capabilities are sufficient to handle Mutalisk harassment. If the opponent commits heavily to Mutalisks, prioritize adding Thors or upgraded Marines and ensure your defenses cover all bases.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have a clear army advantage or when opponent's composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more balanced army with anti-air capabilities, while maintaining a strong ground core.
