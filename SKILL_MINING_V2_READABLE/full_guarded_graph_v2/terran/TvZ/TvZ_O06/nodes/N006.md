# N006 — Early-Midgame Siege Tank Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 360s) with own heavy production and tech, ground army with Siege Tanks. Opponent has moderate production and light tech, but with Roaches. Maintain your defensive posture and continue to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter Roaches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has added Roaches to their composition, indicating a potential ground push. Their production is moderate, tech light, but they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong defensive position with Siege Tanks and Marines. Economy and expansions are heavy, production and tech are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture and continue to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter Roaches.

Siege Tanks are effective against ground units like Roaches. Your heavy tech allows you to produce them in numbers, giving you a defensive advantage.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your defensive structures and unit composition can handle Roach pushes. If the opponent masses Roaches, prioritize adding Marauders or Siege Tanks and ensure your production is sufficient to reinforce.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have a clear army advantage or when opponent's composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more mobile army once you have secured your defenses, possibly adding Medivacs for drops.
