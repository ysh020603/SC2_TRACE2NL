## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue production and technology.

With both sides committed to ground, it is important to maintain a strong army composition. Marines provide a solid core, but consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your army composition is effective against a mixed ground force (e.g., include units that counter Hydralisks and Roaches) and that your defenses are solid. If not, prioritize adjusting your army composition and reinforcing defenses.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if a major engagement occurs.
