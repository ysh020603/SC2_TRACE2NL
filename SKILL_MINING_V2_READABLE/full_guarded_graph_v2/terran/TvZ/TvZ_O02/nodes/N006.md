## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase defense, economy, and expansion. Continue production and technology.

With both sides committed to ground, it is important to maintain a strong defensive position while expanding economy to support a larger army. Siege tanks and marines provide a solid core.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your army composition is strong against heavy ground (e.g., include Siege Tanks and Marauders) and that your defenses are robust. If not, prioritize reinforcing your ground army and defensive structures before considering further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if a major engagement occurs.
