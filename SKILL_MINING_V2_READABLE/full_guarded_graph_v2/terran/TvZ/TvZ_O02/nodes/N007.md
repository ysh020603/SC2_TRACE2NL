# N007 — Early Midgame Ground Posture with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling. Own economy is moderate with heavy expansion. This is a signal to start leaning towards a ground-oriented response. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue production and technology.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground-based army. Maintaining a flexible development allows you to respond appropriately without overcommitting.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and that you have enough production capacity to support a ground army. If not, prioritize increasing production and ensuring a steady income.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when more information about opponent's posture becomes available, or when a significant event occurs (e.g., opponent attack, expansion).

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing while gathering information. Be ready to adjust based on scouting.
