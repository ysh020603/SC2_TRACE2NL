DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 7 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Enemy has a ground army with Zergling, Hydralisk, and Queen, heavy tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase technology. Maintain economy and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and tech, with a ground army including Hydralisks. They are heavily defended and expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and tech. Your army is ground-oriented with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase technology. Maintain economy and defense.

Your tech advantage allows you to outscale the Zerg. Maintaining defense while teching up prepares you for a strong midgame push.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient to hold potential pressure. If you are not confident in your defensive position, prioritize reinforcing defenses and stabilizing your economy before continuing tech and expansion. Also, ensure you are not over-investing in air without proper scouting; if enemy tech is unclear, prioritize ground upgrades and scouting.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if enemy shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure, and scout enemy tech choices (e.g., Mutalisks, Hydralisks).
