# N002 — Late-Midgame Ground Strength with Siege Tanks

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, you have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions, heavy production and tech. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with Zergling/Queen, heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase technology investment. Maintain economy and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground army of Zergling and Queen. They are heavily defended and expanding. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and tech. Your army is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions. Defense is moderate, and you have some air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase technology investment. Maintain economy and defense.

Your Siege Tank-based army is strong against ground Zerg compositions. Increasing tech (e.g., upgrades) will further solidify your advantage.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient to hold potential pressure. If you are not confident in your defensive position, prioritize reinforcing defenses and stabilizing your economy before continuing tech and expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if enemy shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
