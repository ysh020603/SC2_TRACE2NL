# N003 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and moderate production, with Reaper for scouting. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with Zergling/Queen, moderate production, and light tech. Continue developing your economy and technology, and strengthen your ground army. Maintain current production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy appears to be on a ground macro posture with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and light tech. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are developing technology. Your army composition is not yet clear, but you have a Reaper for scouting. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, and strengthen your ground army. Maintain current production.

Early tech investment sets up a strong midgame. Maintaining economy while scouting with Reaper allows you to adapt to enemy moves.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient to hold potential early pressure. If you are not confident in your defensive position, prioritize reinforcing defenses and stabilizing your economy before continuing tech and expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if enemy shows early pressure or tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Transition into a more defined ground army composition with Marines and Hellions.
