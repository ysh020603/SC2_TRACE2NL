# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 4 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with ground forces (Marine, Reaper) and heavy tech. Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with Zergling/Queen, moderate production, and light tech. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy appears to be on a ground macro posture with Zergling and Queen, moderate production, and light tech. They are expanding heavily and have a heavy economy. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy technology investment. Your army is ground-oriented with Marines and Reapers. Defense is light, but you are expanding and teching up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and production.

Your heavy tech and economy give you a long-term advantage. By continuing to develop, you can outscale the Zerg if they remain on a passive macro path.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient to hold potential pressure. If you are not confident in your defensive position, prioritize reinforcing defenses and stabilizing your economy before continuing tech and expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if enemy shows signs of heavy pressure or tech switch (e.g., Mutalisks).

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger midgame army composition with Siege Tanks and more production.
