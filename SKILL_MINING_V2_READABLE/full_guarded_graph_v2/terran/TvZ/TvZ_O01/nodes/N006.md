# N006 — Late-Midgame Ground with Air Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Banshees. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with Zergling/Queen, heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase technology. Maintain economy and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground army of Zergling and Queen. They are heavily defended and expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and tech. Your army is ground-oriented with some air support (Banshees). Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase technology. Maintain economy and defense.

Your Banshees provide harassment potential and force the Zerg to invest in anti-air. Continuing to tech up will give you a strong midgame army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is stable and your defenses are sufficient to hold potential pressure. If you are not confident in your defensive position, prioritize reinforcing defenses and stabilizing your economy before continuing tech and expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if enemy shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.
