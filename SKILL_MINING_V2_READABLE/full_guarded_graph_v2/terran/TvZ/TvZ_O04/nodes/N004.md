# N004 — Mid-Game Tech and Expansion Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 7 minutes, you have a diverse army with Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Medivacs, and a moderate air presence. The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and heavy tech. Increase your air presence and technology, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Expand and keep your production going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and heavy production and tech. They are likely to be aggressive.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a moderate air presence. Your army is ground-oriented and you are in the mid-game. Your defenses are light or uncertain, so you need to be careful.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Expand and keep your production going.

Your army composition is versatile, and adding air units will give you more options. Expanding will help you maintain your economic lead.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence or expanding, verify that your ground defenses are solid and you have scouted the opponent's tech. If the opponent is aggressive, prioritize defensive units and positioning over tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have scouted the opponent's tech or if you are ready to take a third base.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more advanced army with more Medivacs and possibly Vikings or Liberators.
