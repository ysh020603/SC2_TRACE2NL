# N012 — Early Game Ground with Marauder

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, opponent shows a Marauder, indicating a ground tech path. Your own posture is ground with heavy tech. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative cues may include Marauder. Production moderate, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy technology. Economy is heavy, but expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production. Continue technology development.

Marauders are strong against armored units, so preparing a mix of Marines and Marauders can be effective. Heavy tech investment will pay off later.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting confirms the opponent's ground tech path and that you have a clear read on their composition. If the opponent's composition is unclear or you are vulnerable to early pressure, prioritize scouting and defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have a clear read on the opponent's composition or when you reach a tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable ground posture with a solid economy and tech base.
