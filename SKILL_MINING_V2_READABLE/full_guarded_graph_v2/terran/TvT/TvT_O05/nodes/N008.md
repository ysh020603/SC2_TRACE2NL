# N008 — Late-Midgame Air Transition with Thor Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent shows Siege Tanks, Marines, and Thors, indicating a heavy ground anti-air composition. Your own posture has moderate air presence. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and expansion. Continue technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Thor. Production heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with moderate air presence. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is moderate, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and expansion. Continue technology and upgrades.

Thors are strong anti-air, so you need to be careful with air units. Consider using Battlecruisers or mass Vikings with good positioning.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting confirms the opponent's heavy ground anti-air composition and that you have a composition that can deal with Thors. If the opponent's army composition changes or you are vulnerable to ground attacks, prioritize ground defense and adjust your composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have a composition that can deal with Thors or when the opponent's army composition changes.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
