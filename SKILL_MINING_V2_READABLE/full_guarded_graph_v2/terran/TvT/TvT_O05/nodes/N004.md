# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent shows moderate air presence with Hellions and Medivacs, indicating a possible drop play. Your own posture is ground with moderate air. Increase air presence and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expand further. Continue technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Hellion, Medivac. Production heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is moderate, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expand further. Continue technology and upgrades.

The opponent's Medivacs and Hellions suggest drop harassment. Adding air units like Vikings or Medivacs of your own helps defend and enables counter-drops.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting confirms the opponent's drop play and that you have adequate anti-drop defenses. If the opponent's composition becomes clearer or you are vulnerable to drops, prioritize defensive structures and units.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have sufficient anti-drop defenses or when the opponent's composition becomes clearer.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure your bases against drops while building a strong ground army for a decisive push.
