# N010 — Early Game Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, opponent shows a ground posture with Marines. Your own posture is unknown but with heavy tech investment. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and increase production. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Marine. Production moderate, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy technology. Economy is heavy, but expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and increase production. Continue technology development.

Establishing a solid ground army early sets the foundation for a strong midgame. Heavy tech investment will pay off later.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting confirms the opponent's ground posture and that you have a clear read on their composition. If the opponent's composition is unclear or you are vulnerable to early pressure, prioritize scouting and defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have a clear read on the opponent's composition or when you reach a tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable ground posture with a solid economy and tech base.
