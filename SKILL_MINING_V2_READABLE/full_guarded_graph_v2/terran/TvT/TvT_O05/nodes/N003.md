## Summary

Maintain ground strength, increase economy, and continue technology. Consider adding air units or upgrades to counter potential Battlecruisers.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength, increase economy, and continue technology. Consider adding air units or upgrades to counter potential Battlecruisers.

The opponent may be transitioning to Battlecruisers, so preparing some air defense or Viking support is wise. Maintaining a strong economy allows flexibility.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting confirms the opponent's ground posture and that you have sufficient anti-air capabilities to counter potential Battlecruisers. If the opponent's air transition is confirmed or you are vulnerable to air attacks, prioritize adding anti-air units and upgrades.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you confirm the opponent's air transition or when your army composition is ready.
