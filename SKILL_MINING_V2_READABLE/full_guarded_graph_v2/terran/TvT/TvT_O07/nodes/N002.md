# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both players have an unknown army posture, but with heavy technology investment. The opponent's production is heavy, while your own production is moderate. Focus on increasing your economy and expansion while continuing your technology development. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline, and maintain a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and light expansion, with an unknown army posture and moderate production. Your technology is heavy, and you have a Marine as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your economy and expansion while continuing your technology development. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline, and maintain a defensive posture.

With the opponent's army unknown, it is prudent to develop a solid economy and tech base while preparing a flexible army composition. This allows you to adapt to whatever the opponent reveals.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening your economy and tech, ensure you have adequate scouting to confirm the opponent's army composition. If you lack reliable information, prioritize scouting and defensive readiness over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate once you have more scouting information about the opponent's army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground posture as you gain more information about the opponent's strategy.
