# N008 — Early-Mid Air Transition with Ground Core

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 300-360 seconds, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. The opponent shows a ground army with marines and reapers, while your own forces include siege tanks and medivacs. Increase your air presence and technology, while maintaining a strong ground core. Continue to expand and develop your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a ground army posture and moderate air presence. Your production and technology are heavy, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology, while maintaining a strong ground core. Continue to expand and develop your economy.

Adding air units like medivacs can provide mobility and healing, giving you an advantage in engagements and harassment.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence, confirm that the opponent lacks strong anti-air. If the opponent shows signs of building anti-air, prioritize maintaining a strong ground core and avoid overcommitting to air.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of building anti-air or if your air units are not effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes medivacs and possibly banshees, while maintaining a solid ground army.
