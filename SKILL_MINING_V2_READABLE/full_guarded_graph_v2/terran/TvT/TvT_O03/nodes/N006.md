# N006 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own posture ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, moderate air presence; opponent ground-oriented with heavy defense. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing to expand and tech up.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing to expand and tech up.

Gain an advantage by transitioning to air units that the opponent may not be prepared for.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence, confirm that your ground forces are strong enough to defend against a ground push, and that you have scouted for enemy anti-air. If the opponent has significant anti-air or is preparing a ground assault, prioritize reinforcing ground defenses and maintaining a balanced army.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when enemy anti-air capabilities are assessed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with significant air component.
