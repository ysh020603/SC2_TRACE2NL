# N012 — Late Midgame Air Transition with Ground Core

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) with opponent showing a ground posture with Marines and Medivacs. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, and you have Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Ravens. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue expanding and increasing economy and technology. Maintain production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Medivac. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is heavy, technology is heavy. Representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Ravens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue expanding and increasing economy and technology. Maintain production.

The opponent has Medivacs, indicating a potential for drops or air mobility. Adding air units and Ravens will help counter that and provide detection and utility.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence and strengthening air forces, confirm that the opponent's tech direction is indeed ground-based and that they are not transitioning to a heavy air composition. If you see a significant number of air units or tech structures indicating air, prioritize building anti-air units and adjust your production accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech direction or if they commit to a specific composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a mixed army with air support to handle the opponent's mobility and potential air threats.
