# N005 — Early Midgame Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 5 minutes) with opponent showing a ground posture and possible Reaper cues. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, and you have Marauders. Stabilize your position and then develop. Increase production and economy, maintain defense and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is heavy, technology is heavy. Representative units include Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position and then develop. Increase production and economy, maintain defense and tech.

Marauders are strong against armored units, but you need a stable economy to support them. Stabilizing first ensures you don't overextend, then developing allows you to gain an advantage.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before stabilizing and developing, ensure that your economy and production are not overextended relative to your defensive capabilities. If the opponent shows signs of an early push or your scouting is incomplete, focus on defensive unit production and securing your base before expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have a solid economy and production, or if the opponent forces a reaction.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a stable economy and production to support a strong midgame push.
