# N007 — Midgame Ground Army with Marines and Marauders

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with opponent showing a ground posture with Marines. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, and you have Marines and Marauders. Maintain your ground army and continue increasing economy. Keep production and tech steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is heavy, technology is heavy. Representative units include Marines and Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue increasing economy. Keep production and tech steady.

A balanced Marine/Marauder composition is strong in TvT. Maintaining economy and tech allows you to adapt to opponent's composition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before maintaining your ground army and increasing economy, confirm that the opponent has not transitioned to a composition that counters your current army. If you see a significant number of air units or tech that counters your ground forces, adjust your unit composition and production accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have a clear picture of the opponent's army or if they make a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
