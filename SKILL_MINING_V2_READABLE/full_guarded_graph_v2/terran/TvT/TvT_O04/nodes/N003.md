# N003 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines. Your own army composition is still unknown. Focus on expanding your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines. They may be planning an early push or a fast expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army composition is not yet defined. You have a Reaper for scouting.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

In the early game, a strong economy and technology foundation is crucial for midgame strength. Scouting helps you adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting has identified the opponent's build and that you have a defensive plan against early pressure. If you lack scouting information or are uncertain about the opponent's intentions, prioritize scouting and defensive preparation over expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's build or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting, while maintaining your economic lead.
