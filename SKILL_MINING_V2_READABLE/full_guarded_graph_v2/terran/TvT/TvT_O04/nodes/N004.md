# N004 — Early Game Ground Orientation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Reapers, while the opponent's army composition is unknown but they have a heavy economy. Continue developing your ground army and economy, while scouting to identify the opponent's plan.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, but their army composition is unknown. They may be planning a variety of strategies.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines and Reapers. Your defense is light, and you are ready to expand.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, while scouting to identify the opponent's plan.

A ground-oriented army is flexible and can transition to many compositions. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to adapt quickly.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your scouting has revealed the opponent's army composition and that your expansion is adequately defended. If you lack scouting information or your defense is too light, prioritize scouting and defensive unit production before expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's build or when you are ready to push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized army based on scouting, while continuing to expand.
