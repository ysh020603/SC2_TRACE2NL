# N004 — Early Game with Reaper Cue

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, you spot a Reaper from the opponent, indicating a possible aggressive or tech-oriented opening. Your own economy is moderate, and you have invested in technology. Strengthen your ground forces and increase production. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter Reaper harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Their economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have invested in technology. You have no confirmed combat units yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces and increase production. Consider building a defensive structure or a unit to counter Reaper harassment.

The Reaper suggests the opponent may be planning early aggression or a tech switch. Strengthening your ground forces and increasing production will help you defend and prepare for a counter.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening ground forces, confirm that you have sufficient production capacity and that the opponent's Reaper is not part of a larger early push. If the opponent shows additional combat units or tech, prioritize building a defensive structure or a unit to counter Reaper harassment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's composition or when you have a stronger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army and maintain a strong economy to transition into the midgame.
