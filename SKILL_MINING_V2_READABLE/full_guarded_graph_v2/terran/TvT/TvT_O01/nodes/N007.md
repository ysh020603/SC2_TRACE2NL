# N007 — Early Game Reaper Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, both players have Reapers and a heavy economy. The opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers. Use Reapers for harassment while continuing to expand and tech. Maintain map awareness to avoid losing Reapers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Reapers. Production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with Reapers for harassment. Your expansion is light, but you are teching heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use Reapers for harassment while continuing to expand and tech. Maintain map awareness to avoid losing Reapers.

Reaper harassment can disrupt the opponent's economy and force reactions, giving you a macro advantage.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing Reaper harassment, ensure that the opponent has not invested in early defense, such as mines or marauders. If they have, prioritize macro and transitioning to a stronger army instead of risking your Reapers.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent shows a stronger defense or when you have enough army to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more solid ground army with Marines and Marauders.
