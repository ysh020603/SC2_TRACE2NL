# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 4 minutes, both players have a heavy economy and production, with light air presence. You have Reapers and Hellions, while the opponent shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Continue developing your ground army and economy, maintaining pressure with light harassment while scouting for opponent tech choices.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; no reliable combat-unit cues observed. Production and technology appear heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with Reapers and Hellions for early harassment. Your technology is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, maintaining pressure with light harassment while scouting for opponent tech choices.

A heavy economy and production allow you to out-macro the opponent if they commit to a tech switch. Maintaining a ground presence keeps you safe against early aggression.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing harassment and expansion, verify that the opponent has not committed to an early push by checking for a large ground force moving across the map. If an attack is imminent, prioritize defensive positioning and unit production over further harassment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's first combat units are seen or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger midgame army composition with Siege Tanks and Medivacs, while continuing to expand and tech.
