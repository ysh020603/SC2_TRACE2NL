# N009 — Early Game Marine-Marauder Pressure

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, the opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers. You have Marines and Marauders, with a heavy economy. Apply pressure with your Marine-Marauder army while continuing to expand and tech. Use the army to deny the opponent's expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Reapers. Production appears moderate, technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with Marines and Marauders. Your expansion is heavy, and you are teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Apply pressure with your Marine-Marauder army while continuing to expand and tech. Use the army to deny the opponent's expansion.

A strong early army can punish a greedy opponent and secure map control.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before applying pressure with your Marine-Marauder army, verify that the opponent does not have a strong defensive position, such as bunkers or siege tanks. If they do, focus on macro and teching instead of a risky attack.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent shows a stronger defense or when you have enough army to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more advanced army composition with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.
