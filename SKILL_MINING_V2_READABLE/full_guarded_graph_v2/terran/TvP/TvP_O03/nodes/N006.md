# N006 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, but you are transitioning to an air-oriented army. Your air presence is moderate, and you have Banshees as representative units. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, but you are adding air units like Banshees. Your economy is heavy, and your expansion is heavy. Your defense posture is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

Transitioning to air can give you an advantage if the opponent is heavily ground-based. Banshees can harass and provide mobility.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing your air presence, verify that you have enough ground forces to defend against a potential ground push. If the opponent has strong anti-air capabilities or is likely to transition to air, prioritize building a balanced army that can handle both.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's anti-air capabilities or if they transition to air themselves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a strong air army with Banshees and possibly Battlecruisers, while maintaining a solid ground defense.
