# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

By early-midgame, both you and the opponent have committed to a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Your economy is heavy, and you have a strong expansion. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy and production. Keep your expansion and technology development going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Your economy is heavy, and your expansion is heavy. You have a Marine as a representative unit, and your defense posture is light but you are prepared for possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy and production. Keep your expansion and technology development going.

Both sides are on a similar ground macro path, so maintaining your economy and production will keep you competitive. Your heavy technology investment will pay off in the midgame.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to strengthen your ground army, verify that your army composition is not vulnerable to a timing attack. If you have not scouted the opponent's army composition or if they appear to be preparing a push, prioritize reinforcing your army and maintaining defensive structures.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's midgame tech units or if you face early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a midgame army composition that can handle the opponent's ground forces, possibly adding Siege Tanks or other tech units.
