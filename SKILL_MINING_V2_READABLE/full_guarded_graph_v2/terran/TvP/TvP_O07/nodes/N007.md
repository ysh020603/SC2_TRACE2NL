## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a moderate ground army with Marines, heavy economy, and heavy tech. The opponent's posture is unknown, with a Sentry seen, moderate production. Continue developing your economy and tech. Maintain a defensive posture while scouting to determine the opponent's plan. Expand cautiously.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's army composition is unclear, but they have a Sentry. Their production is moderate, and they have heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a solid economy and tech. Your army is small but you have defensive structures.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech. Maintain a defensive posture while scouting to determine the opponent's plan. Expand cautiously.

With unknown information, it's best to play safe and macro. Your heavy economy will give you an advantage if the game goes late.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before expanding, verify you have sufficient scouting information to determine the opponent's plan. If the opponent's tech is unknown or they could be aggressive, prioritize scouting and defensive structures over expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more scouting information or if the opponent reveals their tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gather more information and transition to a clear game plan.
