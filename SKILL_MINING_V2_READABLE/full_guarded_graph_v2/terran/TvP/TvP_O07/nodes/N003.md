# N003 — Early Game Ground Defense and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a moderate ground army with Marines, heavy economy, and heavy tech. The opponent shows a ground posture with Zealots, moderate production, and heavy tech. Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to expand and tech. Strengthen your ground army with additional Marines and possibly Marauders. Keep scouting for tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is on a ground path with Zealots. Their production is moderate, but they have heavy tech. They are also expanding and have a strong economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a solid economy and tech investment. Your army is small but you have defensive structures. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to expand and tech. Strengthen your ground army with additional Marines and possibly Marauders. Keep scouting for tech switches.

Your heavy economy and tech will pay off in the midgame. By staying defensive, you avoid early losses and can outscale the opponent.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before expanding further, verify that your defensive structures and army can withstand early pressure. If the opponent applies early aggression or your defenses are weak, prioritize army production and defensive upgrades over expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they apply early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Reach the early-midgame with a stronger army and multiple bases.
