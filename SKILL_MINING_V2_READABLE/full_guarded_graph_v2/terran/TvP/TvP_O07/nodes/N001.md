## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech. Strengthen your ground army composition. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army. Maintain defensive positioning while scouting for tech switches.

Your heavy economy and tech investment give you a long-term advantage. By continuing to grow, you can outproduce the opponent in the midgame. Ground army is safe against the opponent's current ground posture.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening economy/tech, verify your army size is sufficient to deter an early push and that your defensive structures are adequate. If the opponent's army is larger or moving out, prioritize army production and defense over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you scout a tech switch (e.g., Phoenix, Oracle, Colossus) or if the opponent's army size becomes threatening.
