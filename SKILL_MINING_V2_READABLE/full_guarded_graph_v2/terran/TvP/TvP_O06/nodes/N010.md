## Summary

Late-midgame, own ground macro with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers, opponent ground posture with Void Rays. Increase air presence and technology, strengthen air forces, and continue expanding.

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Void Rays; production and tech are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with heavy production and tech; representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Air presence is moderate.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and technology, strengthen air forces, and continue expanding.

Void Rays are strong against ground, so transitioning to air with Battlecruisers provides a counter.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence, verify that your ground forces are not vulnerable to Void Ray harassment and that you have scouted the full composition. If opponent's composition is not fully known or shows a ground threat, prioritize anti-air and ground defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-check when opponent's composition is fully known.
