# N009 — Midgame Defense Against Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground macro with Siege Tanks, Marines, Banshees, and Widow Mines, opponent air posture with Phoenix and Oracle. Increase defense, continue production and tech, and maintain ground strength while adding some air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Sentry, Phoenix, and Oracle; production and tech are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with heavy production and tech; representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, Banshees, and Widow Mines. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, continue production and tech, and maintain ground strength while adding some air.

Phoenix and Oracle require anti-air and detection; Banshees and Widow Mines provide harassment and defense.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before adding air units, verify that your ground defense is sufficient to hold against the current air threat and that you have scouted for any transition. If opponent's air threat is not neutralized or they transition to ground, prioritize maintaining a balanced army and defensive positioning.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when opponent's air threat is neutralized or they transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize against air pressure and continue developing.
