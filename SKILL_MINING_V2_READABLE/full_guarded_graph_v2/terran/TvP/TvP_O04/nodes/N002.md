# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, and the opponent is also ground-oriented with Stalkers. Both have heavy economies and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your technology investment. Consider adding air units to complement your ground forces. Maintain your expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground army with Stalkers, and they are expanding and teching heavily. They have heavy production and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines and Reapers. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding. Your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your technology investment. Consider adding air units to complement your ground forces. Maintain your expansion and production.

The opponent is ground-oriented, so a strong ground army is essential. Adding air units can provide flexibility and counter potential air transitions.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing expansion/tech, confirm opponent's army strength and intentions. If opponent has a large army moving out or a timing attack is likely, prioritize reinforcing your army and defending.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have scouted the enemy's army composition and intentions. If you see a large army moving out, prepare to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army that can handle both ground and potential air threats.
