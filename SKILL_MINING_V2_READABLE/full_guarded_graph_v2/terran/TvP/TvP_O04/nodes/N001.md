# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, while the opponent is still unknown but likely teching heavily. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current defensive posture and keep expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and technology investment. They are expanding and have moderate production. No specific threats are detected.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines. Your technology is heavy, and you are expanding. Your defense is light, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current defensive posture and keep expanding.

This approach builds a strong foundation for the midgame, allowing you to transition into a powerful composition while staying flexible to respond to the opponent's tech choices.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening economy/tech, verify opponent's army composition and detect any early pressure. If opponent is aggressive or army is unknown, prioritize defensive units and scouting.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when scouting reveals opponent's army composition or if an aggressive push is detected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a solid ground army and are ready to adapt to the opponent's composition.
