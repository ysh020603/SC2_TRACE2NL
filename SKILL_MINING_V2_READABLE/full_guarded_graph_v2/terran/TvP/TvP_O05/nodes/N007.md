# N007 — Mid-Game Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 9 minutes, the opponent has a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Maintain your defense and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Maintain your defense and technology development.

Both sides are in a macro-oriented phase. Maintaining a strong economy and production will allow you to out-scale the opponent if they commit to a ground composition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to strengthen your ground army and economy, verify that your army size is sufficient to defend against a potential timing attack. If your army is smaller than expected or the opponent shows signs of aggression, prioritize building defensive units and reinforcing your army over further expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of an air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army composition with upgrades and additional production.
