# N006 — Mid-Game Air Transition by Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 5 minutes, the opponent has transitioned to an air posture with Oracles and Zealots. You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders. Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Zealots and Oracles. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air capabilities. Continue developing your economy and technology.

The opponent's air presence requires you to prepare for potential Oracle harassment or a transition to a more air-heavy composition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before maintaining your ground army and considering anti-air, verify that the opponent's air commitment is real and not a feint. If you have not confirmed the air transition, prioritize scouting to confirm before investing heavily in anti-air.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent commits more heavily to air or if they switch back to ground.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air threats, such as adding Marines and possibly Vikings.
