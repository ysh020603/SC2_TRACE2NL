# N004 — Early Game Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a ground army of Marines and Marauders, with a heavy economy. The opponent's posture is unknown but they have a heavy economy. Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's posture, but they have a heavy economy and technology. They may have Zealots and Stalkers.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is light, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

With a ground army already established, it is efficient to continue producing units and expanding your economy to support a strong mid-game.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing production and continuing ground army development, verify that your defense is not too light to withstand an early push. If your defense is weak, prioritize building defensive structures or units before expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of an air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army with additional production and upgrades.
