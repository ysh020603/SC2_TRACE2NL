# N003 — Early-Mid Ground Push with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and heavy tech. Enemy is unknown but has heavy economy and tech, with moderate production. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase production. Maintain expansion and tech progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has heavy economy and tech, moderate production, and moderate expansion. Their army composition is unknown, with only Zealot cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy production and heavy tech, with a ground-oriented army. Your defense is light or uncertain, but you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase production. Maintain expansion and tech progression.

Your heavy production allows you to field a strong ground army. Continuing to expand and tech gives you a long-term advantage.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to strengthen your ground army and expand, verify that your defense is not too light. If your defense is light, prioritize reinforcing your defensive capabilities before further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when enemy tech becomes apparent or when you have a significant army advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
