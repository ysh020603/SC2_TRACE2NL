## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defensive capabilities and continue developing your ground army. Consider tech that counters Colossus, such as Vikings or Liberators.

Colossus are strong against ground units. Adding air units or long-range ground units can help deal with them. Stabilizing your defense is key.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to develop your ground army, verify that you have sufficient anti-Colossus units. If you lack anti-Colossus, prioritize building Vikings or Liberators before further expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have sufficient anti-Colossus units or when enemy tech changes.
