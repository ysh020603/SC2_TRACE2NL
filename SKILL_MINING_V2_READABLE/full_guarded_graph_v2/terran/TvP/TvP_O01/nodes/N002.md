# N002 — Early Game Tech/Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides are in early game with unknown army compositions. You have heavy economy and tech investment, moderate production, and are expanding. Focus on developing your economy and tech while maintaining production. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has heavy economy and tech investment, moderate production, and moderate expansion. Their army composition is unknown, with only Zealot cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and tech investment, moderate production, and are expanding. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and tech while maintaining production. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base. A ground-oriented army provides flexibility against unknown enemy tech.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to develop economy and tech, verify that your defense is adequate to withstand early pressure. If your defense is light, prioritize building defensive units or structures before further expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about enemy composition or when you reach a tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a clear tech direction and a solid economy.
