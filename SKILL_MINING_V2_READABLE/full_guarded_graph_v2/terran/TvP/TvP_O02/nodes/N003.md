# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Entering the midgame, the situation remains unclear. The opponent's posture is still unknown, and your own development is still light or uncertain. Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology remain light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are still light or uncertain. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

The lack of information suggests that both players are still in a macro-oriented phase. Maintaining a balanced approach reduces vulnerability to sudden attacks while allowing you to scale up.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing this balanced development, verify that your army size is adequate to defend against potential early pressure. If you have not scouted enemy units, prioritize producing combat units to ensure you are not vulnerable.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you spot enemy units or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to develop your economy and technology, preparing to transition into a more defined army composition once the opponent's strategy becomes clearer.
