# N010 — Early-Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame: The opponent has a ground posture with Zealots and Sentries, heavy economy, production, and technology. Your own ground forces include Marines. Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders to counter Zealots and Sentries.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Sentries. Production and technology are heavy, and economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your ground posture is developing with moderate production and heavy technology. You have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders to counter Zealots and Sentries.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need to ensure your ground forces can handle them. Marauders are effective against Zealots and can help break Sentry force fields.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening ground forces, ensure you have scouted the opponent's full army composition. If you have not seen the opponent's army, prioritize building a defensive force that includes anti-Zealot units, while maintaining a defensive posture.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or when you are ready to push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain a strong economy to support it.
