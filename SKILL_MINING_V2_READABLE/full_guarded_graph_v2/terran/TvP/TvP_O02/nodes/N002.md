# N002 — Early-Mid Game Development Continuation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-mid game, the situation remains largely unknown. Both sides are still developing, with no significant combat-unit cues. Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology remain light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are still light or uncertain. You have not yet committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current development path. Keep production and technology investment balanced, and avoid unnecessary risks.

The lack of information suggests that both players are still in a macro-oriented phase. Maintaining a balanced approach reduces vulnerability to sudden attacks while allowing you to scale up.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing this balanced development, ensure you have scouted the opponent's base or army composition. If you have no information on enemy units, prioritize building a defensive force to avoid being caught off guard by a timing attack.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you spot enemy units or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue to develop your economy and technology, preparing to transition into a more defined army composition once the opponent's strategy becomes clearer.
