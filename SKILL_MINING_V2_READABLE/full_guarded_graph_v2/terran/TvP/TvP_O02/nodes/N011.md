# N011 — Early-Midgame Unknown vs Unknown

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame: The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your own economy is moderate, and expansion is heavy. Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders to counter Zealots.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Zealots, but the overall posture is unknown. Economy and expansion are heavy, production moderate, and technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, expansion is heavy, and production is moderate. You have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding Marauders to counter Zealots.

The opponent's Zealots suggest a potential ground threat. Strengthening your ground forces can help defend against this while you continue to develop.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening ground forces, verify that you have scouted the opponent's full army composition. If you have not seen the opponent's army, prioritize building a defensive force that includes anti-Zealot units, while maintaining a defensive posture.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or when your own production and technology reach a more defined state.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a ground-oriented army composition that can handle Zealot-based attacks.
