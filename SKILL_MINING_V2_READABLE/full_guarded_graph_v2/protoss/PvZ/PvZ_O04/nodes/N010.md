# N010 — Late-Mid Air Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-mid game, own air posture with Phoenixes, opponent ground with Mutalisks, Roaches, Queens, both heavy tech. Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground posture with Mutalisks, Roaches, Queens, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy production and tech, air army with Phoenixes, heavy economy and defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

Maintain air dominance to counter the opponent's mixed ground and air presence.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Ensure your Phoenixes can handle Mutalisks and Roaches before continuing air investment. If not, consider adding ground units or more Phoenixes.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent tech switches to anti-air or when air army is sufficient.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air force with Phoenixes and possibly Void Rays.
