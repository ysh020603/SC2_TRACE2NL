## Summary

Late-mid game, own ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers, opponent ground with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, both heavy tech. Maintain ground strength, continue economy and tech, consider defensive upgrades.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength, continue economy and tech, consider defensive upgrades.

Ensure a stable ground army to withstand potential late-game pushes.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Maintain a defensive posture and ensure your ground forces are sufficient to repel Roach attacks before focusing on economy. If vulnerable, prioritize defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent tech or army composition changes.
