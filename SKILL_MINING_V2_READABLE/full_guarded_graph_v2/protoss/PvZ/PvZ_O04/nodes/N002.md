## Summary

Early-mid game, own ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers, opponent ground with Zerglings and Queens, both heavy economy. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and production, maintain tech development.

- Enemy ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy production and technology, ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and production, maintain tech development.

Build a robust ground army to handle potential pressure while maintaining economic and tech advantage.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Ensure your ground army is sufficient to hold against potential Zergling aggression before continuing tech. If under pressure, prioritize production and defense.

**Recheck when:**
