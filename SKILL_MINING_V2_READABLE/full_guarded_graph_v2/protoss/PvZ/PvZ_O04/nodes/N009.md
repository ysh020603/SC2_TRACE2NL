# N009 — Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground posture with Zealots, opponent ground with Zerglings and Queens, both heavy economy. Stabilize then develop, increase production and tech, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, moderate tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy production and tech, ground army with Zealots, heavy economy and defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize then develop, increase production and tech, maintain defense.

Ensure a stable position before committing to further development.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before developing further, confirm that your Zealot army can withstand Zergling aggression. If not, prioritize stabilizing your defense and production.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when the situation stabilizes or when opponent tech changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition with tech units.
