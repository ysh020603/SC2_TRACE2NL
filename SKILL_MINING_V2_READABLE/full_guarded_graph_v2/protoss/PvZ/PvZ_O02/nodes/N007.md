# N007 — Early-Midgame: Ground Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Zealot and Stalker cues. Strengthen ground forces, continue production and technology development, and maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, continue production and technology development, and maintain economy.

With both sides ground-oriented, building a strong ground army and tech advantage is key to gaining an edge.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent's ground posture is confirmed and that your ground army is sufficient to defend against potential early ling floods or timing attacks. If the opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure, prioritize reinforcing your ground defense and scouting over further tech development.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's tech or army composition becomes clearer, or when your own tech milestones are reached.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful ground army to pressure the opponent or defend against aggression.
