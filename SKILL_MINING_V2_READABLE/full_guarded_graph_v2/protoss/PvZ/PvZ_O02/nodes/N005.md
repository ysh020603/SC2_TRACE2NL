# N005 — Early Game: Ground Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Your own posture is unknown but with heavy technology investment and heavy defense. Strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, and continue technology development.

The opponent's ground posture suggests potential early pressure. Investing in defense and tech while maintaining economy provides a solid foundation.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent's ground posture is confirmed and that your technology investment is not leaving you vulnerable to early pressure. If the opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure, prioritize building a defensive ground force and scouting over further tech development.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's tech or army composition becomes clearer, or when your own tech milestones are reached.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stable ground army and tech advantage to transition into the midgame.
