# N004 — Midgame Ground Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, heavy production and tech. Strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, indicating heavy tech and production. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is now ground-based with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and tech. Economy and defense are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

With a defined ground army, focusing on strengthening it while maintaining a solid economy is key to matching the opponent's heavy production.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before committing further to ground forces, confirm that the opponent has not transitioned to air tech. If the opponent shows signs of air units or your ground army is ineffective, prioritize adding anti-air capabilities or transitioning to a more balanced composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of air tech or if your army composition needs adjustment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army capable of defending and potentially counter-attacking.
