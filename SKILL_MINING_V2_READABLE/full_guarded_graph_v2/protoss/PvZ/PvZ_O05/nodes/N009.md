# N009 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army air-oriented with Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech. Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is air-based with Oracles, heavy production and tech. Economy and defense are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue production and tech, maintain defense and economy.

With an air-oriented army, focusing on air strength and tech can give you an advantage against a ground-based opponent.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air forces, verify that the opponent lacks anti-air capabilities. If the opponent shows signs of anti-air tech or your air army is being countered, prioritize diversifying your army or adding ground support.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of anti-air tech or if your air army is not effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army to pressure the opponent and secure map control.
