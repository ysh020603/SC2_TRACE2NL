# N002 — Ground Army Stabilization

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army is ground-oriented with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Opponent remains ground-based with light air, moderate production. Stabilize your ground army and continue increasing production and technology. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent still shows a ground posture with light air presence, moderate production, and light tech. Queens are representative.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have committed to a ground army with Stalkers as a core unit. Production and technology are heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your ground army and continue increasing production and technology. Maintain economy and expansion.

A ground army with Stalkers provides a solid foundation against Zerg ground forces. Heavy production and tech allow for transitions if needed.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not committed to air or heavy tech. If they have, prioritize adding anti-air or tech units to your ground army. Also ensure your expansion is defended.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame position with a strong ground army and the ability to tech into additional units.
