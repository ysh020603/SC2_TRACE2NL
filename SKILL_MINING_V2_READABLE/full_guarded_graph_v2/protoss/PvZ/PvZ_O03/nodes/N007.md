# N007 — Air Transition with Oracles

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army air-oriented with Oracles, heavy production and tech. Opponent ground-based with light air, moderate production. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue developing technology and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent remains ground-based with light air presence, moderate production, and light tech. Queens are representative.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have committed to an air army with Oracles, indicating a tech-heavy approach. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue developing technology and economy.

Oracles provide harassment and scouting, and can transition into Phoenix or Void Ray compositions. Air can be strong against ground-based Zerg.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not committed to anti-air or heavy tech. If they have, prioritize adding ground support or transitioning to a more balanced army. Also ensure your economy and production can sustain the air investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame position with a strong air army and tech advantage.
