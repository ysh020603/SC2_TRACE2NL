# N009 — Midgame Air Army with Phoenix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own army air-oriented with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Corruptors, and Queens. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Consider adding Phoenix to counter Mutalisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a mixed ground/air army with Mutalisks and Corruptors, indicating a tech switch. They have heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Oracles and some ground support. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Consider adding Phoenix to counter Mutalisks.

Phoenix are strong against Mutalisks, and Oracles provide utility. An air army can control the skies.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not committed to heavy anti-air or a tech switch. If they have, prioritize adding ground support or transitioning to a more balanced army. Also ensure your air forces are not overextended without proper support.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-midgame position with a strong air army and tech advantage.
