# N006 — Midgame Tech Advantage

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers, Warp Prisms, Observers, and Immortals. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and tech. Leverage your tech advantage to pressure the opponent or expand further. Continue increasing technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a moderate ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and tech. They are not as advanced.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Immortals and Observers, indicating a tech advantage. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Leverage your tech advantage to pressure the opponent or expand further. Continue increasing technology.

Immortals are strong against Roaches, and Observers provide vision. A tech advantage can be used to dictate the pace.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not committed to air or heavy tech. If they have, prioritize adding anti-air or tech units to your army. Also ensure your tech advantage is not overextended without proper defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-midgame position with a decisive tech advantage.
