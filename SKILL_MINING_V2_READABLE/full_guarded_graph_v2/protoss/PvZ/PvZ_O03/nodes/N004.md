# N004 — Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own army ground-oriented with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy production and technology, indicating a macro-oriented Zerg.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Production and technology are heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi.

A ground army with Sentries and Warp Prisms provides good utility and mobility. Heavy production allows for constant reinforcement.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not committed to air or heavy tech. If they have, prioritize adding anti-air units or tech. Also ensure your expansion is defended and your army is not overextended.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-midgame position with a powerful ground army and tech advantage.
