# N011 — Late-Midgame Air Army with Phoenix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own army air-oriented with Zealots, Stalkers, Phoenix, and Oracles. Opponent ground-based with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens. Continue strengthening air forces and consider adding Carriers or Void Rays. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a mixed ground/air army with Mutalisks and Roaches, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong air army with Phoenix and Oracles. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening air forces and consider adding Carriers or Void Rays. Maintain economy and expansion.

Phoenix are strong against Mutalisks, and Oracles provide utility. A heavy air army can dominate the skies.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not committed to heavy anti-air or a tech switch. If they have, prioritize adding ground support or transitioning to a more balanced army. Also ensure your economy and production can sustain the air investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choice or if they commit to anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with a powerful air army and tech advantage.
