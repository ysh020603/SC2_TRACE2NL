# N002 — Early-Mid Game Ground Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 4-6 minutes) with heavy economy and technology, and a developing ground army. Opponent is still on a ground macro posture with Zergling/Queen. Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining your economic and technological lead. Increase production to support a larger army and consider expanding further to sustain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and technology investment is heavy. You have started to develop a ground army, with Stalkers and Sentries as representative units. Your production is heavy, and you are expanding well. Your army composition is still somewhat undefined but leaning towards ground.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining your economic and technological lead. Increase production to support a larger army and consider expanding further to sustain your economy.

With a heavy economy and technology, you can afford to invest in a strong ground army. Stalkers and Sentries provide a versatile core that can handle early Zergling pressure and set up for future tech choices.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your ground army is sufficient to defend against potential aggression and that your economy can sustain continued production. If your army is too small or your expansion is threatened, prioritize reinforcing your army and securing your expansion over further tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's tech path or when you are ready to commit to a specific army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more specialized ground composition, such as adding Immortals or Colossi, based on the opponent's army composition. Continue to expand and upgrade.
