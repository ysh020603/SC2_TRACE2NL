# N009 — Midgame Ground Army with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with a strong ground army and heavy defense. Opponent has a heavy ground presence with Roaches and Overseers. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economic and technological advantage. Consider adding Immortals or Colossi to counter the opponent's Roach-heavy composition. Use your Phoenixes for harassment and to control the map.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and technology investment is heavy. You have a strong ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Phoenixes. Your defense posture is heavy, and you are expanding well. Your army is ground-oriented with some air support.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economic and technological advantage. Consider adding Immortals or Colossi to counter the opponent's Roach-heavy composition. Use your Phoenixes for harassment and to control the map.

The opponent's army is ground-heavy with Roaches, which are vulnerable to Immortals. Your heavy economy and technology allow you to tech into these counters. Your heavy defense posture ensures you can withstand any early aggression.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your ground army is capable of countering the opponent's heavy ground presence and that your defense is not overcommitted at the expense of map control. If your army lacks anti-armor or your expansion is threatened, prioritize adding Immortals or Colossi and securing your expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have information about the opponent's tech transitions or when you are ready to launch a major attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more specialized ground composition with Immortals or Colossi to counter the opponent's Roaches. Continue to expand and upgrade to maintain your advantage.
