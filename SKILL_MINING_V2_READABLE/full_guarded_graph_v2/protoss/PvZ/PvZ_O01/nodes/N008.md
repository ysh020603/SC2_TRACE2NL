# N008 — Early-Mid Game Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 5-6 minutes) with a transition to an air-oriented army. Opponent has a ground posture with Queens and Overseers. Continue strengthening your air army and maintain your economic and technological advantage. Use Oracles for harassment and scouting, and consider adding Phoenixes or Void Rays for more air power. Keep a ground core for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen, Overseer. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and technology investment is heavy. You have transitioned to an air-oriented army with Oracles, supported by Zealots. Your production is heavy, and you are expanding well. Your army is air-oriented and capable of harassment and scouting.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and maintain your economic and technological advantage. Use Oracles for harassment and scouting, and consider adding Phoenixes or Void Rays for more air power. Keep a ground core for defense.

The opponent's army is ground-heavy, and air units like Oracles can provide excellent harassment and scouting. Your heavy economy and technology allow you to invest in a strong air force.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your air army is not overextended without ground support and that you have scouted the opponent's anti-air capabilities. If the opponent has anti-air or your ground defense is weak, prioritize reinforcing your ground core and adding detection before further air investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have information about the opponent's tech transitions or when you are ready to launch a major attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more specialized air composition, such as adding Phoenixes or Void Rays, based on the opponent's response. Continue to expand and upgrade.
