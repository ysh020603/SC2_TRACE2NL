# N009 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 9 minutes, the opponent has a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Oracles, while you have an air army with Phoenixes. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Sentry, Observer, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Stalkers, Sentries, Phoenixes, and Observers, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue economy and technology development.

The opponent is ground-focused, so your air army can provide mobility and harassment advantages.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence, verify that your ground defense is sufficient to hold against a potential ground attack while you invest in air. If your ground defense is weak or the opponent shows signs of transitioning to air, prioritize ground defense or adjust your air investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if your air investment is not effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army that can control the skies and support ground operations.
