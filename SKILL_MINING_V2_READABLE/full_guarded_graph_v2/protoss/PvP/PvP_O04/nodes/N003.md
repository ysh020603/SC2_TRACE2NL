# N003 — Late-Mid Ground Stability

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, the opponent remains ground-focused with a moderate defense, and you have a heavy economy and expansion. Maintain ground strength and economy, with a focus on defense and continued technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms, and Observers, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength and economy, with a focus on defense and continued technology development.

The opponent's ground army is still strong, so maintaining a solid defense and economy will allow you to outlast or out-tech them.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before maintaining ground strength and economy, verify that your expansion is safe and that you have enough army to defend against a potential ground push. If you detect an incoming attack or your army is insufficient, prioritize defense and army production over further economy.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's posture changes or when you reach a critical tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable late-midgame position with a strong economy and army, ready for late-game decisions.
