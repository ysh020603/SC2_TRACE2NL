# N005 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, both players are in early game with unknown army postures, but you have Zealots and Stalkers. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a developing ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and technology. Maintain defense and continue production.

With the opponent's posture unknown, building a solid ground foundation and economy gives you flexibility to adapt.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening ground forces and expanding, verify that you have scouted the opponent's army composition and that your expansion is safe. If the opponent's posture is still unknown or your expansion is vulnerable, prioritize scouting and defense over expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you gain more information about the opponent's army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong ground army and economy, ready to transition based on scouting information.
