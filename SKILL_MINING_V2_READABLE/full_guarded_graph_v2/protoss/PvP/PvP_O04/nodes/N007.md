# N007 — Early Game Defensive Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the opponent has a heavy defense posture and moderate economy, while you have a Stalker and heavy economy. Stabilize your economy and defense while increasing production and technology. Maintain ground strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a developing ground army with Stalkers, and a heavy economy but light expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your economy and defense while increasing production and technology. Maintain ground strength.

The opponent's heavy defense suggests a defensive posture, so focusing on economy and tech will let you outscale them.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before stabilizing economy and defense, verify that you have scouted the opponent's posture and that your expansion is safe. If the opponent's posture is unclear or your expansion is vulnerable, prioritize scouting and defense over further economy.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's posture becomes clearer or when you have a tech advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and defense, ready to transition into a stronger army.
