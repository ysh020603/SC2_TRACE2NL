# N010 — Late-Mid Ground Defense vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, the opponent has a heavy air army with Carriers and Void Rays, while you have a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Void Rays, and Observers. Increase defense and strengthen ground forces while adding anti-air. Continue economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Carrier, VoidRay, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, Sentries, Void Rays, and Observers, and a heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and strengthen ground forces while adding anti-air. Continue economy and technology development.

The opponent's air army is a significant threat, so you need to bolster your defenses and ensure you can counter air units.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing defense and strengthening ground with anti-air, verify that your economy can support the transition and that your ground army is not overcommitted. If the opponent's air composition is strong or your anti-air is insufficient, prioritize building more anti-air or adjusting your composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when the opponent's air composition changes or when you have sufficient anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid defense with anti-air capabilities, ready for late-game.
