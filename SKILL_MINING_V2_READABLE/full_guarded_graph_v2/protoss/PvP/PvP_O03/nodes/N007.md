# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, Oracles). Your own army is ground-oriented with Zealots. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture and consider adding more tech (e.g., High Templar) for spell support.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Zealot, Stalker, Immortal, and Oracle. Their production appears heavy, technology investment heavy, and economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, defense is heavy, and technology investment is heavy. Production is heavy, and your army is ground-oriented with Zealots.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture and consider adding more tech (e.g., High Templar) for spell support.

A ground army with Zealots can be effective, but adding tech like High Templar can provide spell support and counter enemy compositions. Maintaining a solid economy allows for further tech and expansion.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before adding more tech (e.g., High Templar), ensure you have sufficient army to defend against potential pressure. If opponent shows air units, prioritize anti-air or transition.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if you need to adapt your composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful ground army with Zealots and potentially add spellcasters for late-game strength.
