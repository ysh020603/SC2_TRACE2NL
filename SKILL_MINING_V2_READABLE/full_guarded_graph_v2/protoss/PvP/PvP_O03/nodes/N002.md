- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Zealot. Their production appears heavy, technology investment heavy, and economy heavy. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.

### Own cues

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's ground-oriented composition. Continue increasing your economy and technology, and maintain a defensive posture.

The opponent's ground commitment suggests a ground-based confrontation. Building a strong ground army with supporting technology (e.g., Immortals) can give you an advantage in a direct engagement.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before committing to ground army, confirm opponent remains ground-based (no air units or tech). If opponent shows air units or tech, prioritize adding anti-air or transitioning to air.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if you gain information about their tech path.
