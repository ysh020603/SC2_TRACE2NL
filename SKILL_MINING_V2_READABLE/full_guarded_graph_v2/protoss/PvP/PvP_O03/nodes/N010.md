# N010 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with the opponent showing a ground posture (Zealots). Your own army has transitioned to an air-oriented composition with Stalkers and Oracles. Continue strengthening your air army and increasing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture and consider adding more tech (e.g., Void Rays) for air superiority.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Zealot. Their production appears heavy, technology investment heavy, and economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, defense is heavy, and technology investment is heavy. Production is heavy, and your army is air-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and increasing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture and consider adding more tech (e.g., Void Rays) for air superiority.

An air transition can be powerful against a ground-oriented opponent, especially if they lack anti-air. Oracles provide utility and can harass the opponent's economy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing air investment, verify opponent lacks significant anti-air (e.g., Stalkers, Archons). If opponent builds anti-air, consider transitioning to ground or adding support.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent builds significant anti-air or if you need to transition back to ground.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air superiority with a strong air army and potentially add Void Rays for late-game.
