# N012 — Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers, and their economy is heavy. You have a heavy economy and tech, with a heavy expansion. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Their economy is heavy, and they have expanded heavily. Their production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have expanded heavily. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

The opponent's mixed posture suggests they may be building a versatile army. By strengthening your ground forces and maintaining your tech advantage, you can counter their composition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before expanding further, scout for enemy aggression and ensure you have enough army to defend. If enemy units are seen or your army is insufficient, prioritize defensive unit production over expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when more of enemy army is revealed or you are ready to push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition, possibly with Stalkers or Immortals, to counter the opponent's Zealots and Stalkers.
