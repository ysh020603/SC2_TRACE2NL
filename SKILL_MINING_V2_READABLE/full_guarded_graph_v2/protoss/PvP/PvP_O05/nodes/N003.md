# N003 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with both sides having heavy economy and technology, but no clear army composition yet. No reliable combat-unit cues from the opponent. Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition. Continue expanding and increasing production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are heavy, and you are expanding well. Production is moderate, and your army composition is still undefined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition. Continue expanding and increasing production.

In the early game, investing in tech and economy sets up a strong midgame. Keeping options open allows you to react to the opponent's first moves.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before focusing on economy and tech, ensure you have sufficient scouting to detect early pressure. If opponent's army composition becomes clear and indicates aggression, prioritize building a defensive army before continuing expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more scouting information or when you hit a tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a solid economic and tech base, ready to transition into a ground or air army based on scouting.
