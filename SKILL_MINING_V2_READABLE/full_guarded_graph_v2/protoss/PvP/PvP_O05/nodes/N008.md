# N008 — Midgame Air Army vs Mixed Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have an air army with Phoenix and Oracle, while the opponent shows a mixed posture with moderate air presence. Continue strengthening your air army and consider adding some ground units for defense. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a mixed posture with moderate air presence. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy air army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your economy, production, and technology are all heavy, and you have strong defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and consider adding some ground units for defense. Maintain your economy and expansion.

The opponent's mixed posture means you need to be prepared for both ground and air threats. Your air army can control the skies, but you need ground support to defend bases.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to strengthen air army, verify opponent's commitment to air. If opponent commits heavily to ground, consider adding ground units for defense and adjust composition accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent commits more heavily to air or ground.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army with a strong air core and sufficient ground defense.
