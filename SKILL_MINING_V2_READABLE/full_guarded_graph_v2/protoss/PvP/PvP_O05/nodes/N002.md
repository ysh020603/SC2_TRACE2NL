# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Immortal Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where both sides have heavy ground armies and heavy tech. Own army includes Stalkers and Immortals, while opponent shows Zealot cues. Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Zealot cues. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Stalkers and Immortals, backed by heavy production and technology. Your economy and expansion are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

Immortals are strong against the Zealot-heavy ground compositions common in PvP. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before adding more Immortals, confirm opponent remains ground-focused. If opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy air presence, prioritize building anti-air units and adjust composition accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of transitioning to air or if you face a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful ground force with Immortal support and prepare for potential tech switches.
