# N010 — Early-Mid Ground Development with Zealot Cues

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where both sides have heavy economy and technology, but own army composition is still unclear. Opponent shows Zealot cues. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your current expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an unknown posture with Zealot cues. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they have heavy defenses.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are heavy, but your army composition is not yet defined. You have moderate production and are expanding well. Representative units include Stalker.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your current expansion and production.

With both sides investing heavily in tech and economy, a solid ground core provides safety while you transition to a more defined composition. This approach keeps options open for either a ground or air switch later.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening ground army, verify opponent's army composition and tech path. If opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy air presence, prioritize adding anti-air units and consider a more flexible composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a robust ground force with Stalkers and prepare to add supporting units or transition to air based on scouting.
