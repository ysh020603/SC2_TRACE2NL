# N007 — Early-Mid Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, the opponent shows an air posture with Void Rays, while you have a ground army with Zealots. Your economy is strong. Consider transitioning to include anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Continue developing your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air posture with Void Rays, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy. They may be preparing an air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy with multiple expansions. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider transitioning to include anti-air units such as Stalkers or Phoenixes. Continue developing your economy and tech.

Void Rays are strong against ground units. Adding anti-air will help defend against an air attack and maintain map control.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, confirm that you have sufficient anti-air capabilities to defend against Void Rays. If your anti-air is insufficient, prioritize building Stalkers or Phoenixes and consider a transition to air.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions back to ground or if you need to commit to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with ground and anti-air capabilities, while maintaining a strong economy.
