# N003 — Stabilize and Develop Ground

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, the opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers, while you have Zealots. Your expansion count is uncertain, and you may be slightly behind. Focus on stabilizing your economy and increasing production. Continue tech development but prioritize army safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and a moderate expansion count.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, but your expansion count is uncertain. You may need to catch up economically.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your economy and increasing production. Continue tech development but prioritize army safety.

Given the uncertain expansion count, securing your economy is crucial. A stable economy will allow you to outproduce the opponent later.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, ensure that your economy is stable and that you have scouted the opponent's next move. If your economy is unstable or the opponent is preparing an attack, prioritize army production and defense over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate once your economy is secure and you have scouting information on the opponent's next move.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy with a solid ground army and tech advantage.
