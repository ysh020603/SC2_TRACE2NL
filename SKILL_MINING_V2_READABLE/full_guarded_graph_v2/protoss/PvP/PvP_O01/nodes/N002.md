# N002 — Ground Macro with Stalker Presence

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, while you have Zealots. Both have heavy production and tech, but your expansion count is uncertain. Increase your economy and continue ground army production. Consider adding Stalkers for anti-air and mobility.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and a moderate expansion count. They may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy. Your expansion count is uncertain, so you may need to secure additional bases.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and continue ground army production. Consider adding Stalkers for anti-air and mobility.

Stalkers provide flexibility against potential air transitions and are strong in PvP. Expanding will support a larger army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, confirm that the opponent is not preparing a timing attack and that your expansion count is sufficient. If a timing attack is likely or your economy is behind, prioritize defensive units and secure additional bases.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-check if the opponent shows signs of air tech or if you need to defend a push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
