# N012 — Late-Mid Air Superiority

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the opponent has an air posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Void Rays. You have an air army with Stalkers and Carriers. Continue building your air army, focusing on Carriers and supporting units. Maintain a strong economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Void Rays, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air army with Stalkers and Carriers, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy with multiple expansions. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building your air army, focusing on Carriers and supporting units. Maintain a strong economy and tech.

Carriers are powerful in the late game. Your air army can outmaneuver the opponent's air forces.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, confirm that the opponent has not transitioned to ground and that your air army is well-supported. If the opponent transitions to ground, prioritize building ground units or defensive structures.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to ground or if you need to defend against a specific threat.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a formidable air army with Carriers and support, while maintaining a strong economy.
