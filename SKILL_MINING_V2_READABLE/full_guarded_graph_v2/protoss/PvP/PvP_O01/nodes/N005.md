# N005 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides have an unknown army composition, but you are investing heavily in technology. Production is moderate. Continue developing your technology and economy while increasing production. Maintain flexibility to adapt to scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Their expansion count is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an unknown army composition, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Economy is strong but expansion count is uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while increasing production. Maintain flexibility to adapt to scouting.

Heavy tech investment gives you options for either ground or air transitions. A strong economy will support your tech.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, scout the opponent's army composition to confirm that your technology investment is appropriate. If the opponent's composition counters your tech, adjust your tech path and production accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate once you have scouted the opponent's army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a point where you can choose a tech path based on opponent's actions.
