# N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both you and the opponent have unknown army compositions, but you have a heavy economy and technology investment. The opponent shows possible Reapers and Marines. Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Consider scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but possible Marine and Reaper cues suggest early aggression. Their economy is heavy, technology is heavy, and production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, but you have a heavy economy and technology. Your production is moderate, and your expansion is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Consider scouting to clarify the opponent's intentions.

Early game is about establishing a strong foundation. Heavy technology investment can pay off later, but you need to be aware of potential early pressure.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, ensure you have scouted the opponent's army composition and have adequate defense against early aggression. If the opponent's composition is unclear or you face early pressure, prioritize scouting and defensive measures over further economic or technological investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or if you face early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a clearer army composition based on scouting, while continuing to grow your economy and technology.
