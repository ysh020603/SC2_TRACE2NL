# N004 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. The opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Continue developing your economy and tech, and prepare to build a ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Marine, Marauder. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech, and prepare to build a ground army.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base to support future production.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your early defense is sufficient to hold off Marine/Marauder pressure, especially if your army composition is undefined. If your defense is light, prioritize building a defensive core (e.g., Zealots, Sentries) and securing your natural expansion before committing to heavy tech.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's build or when you are ready to expand.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach an early-midgame state with a solid economy and tech foundation.
