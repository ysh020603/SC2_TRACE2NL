# N011 — Early-Midgame Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, you have a heavy defense and moderate economy, while the opponent's posture is unknown. You need to stabilize and develop. Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology appear light or uncertain. The opponent's posture is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is moderate, technology is heavy, and defense is heavy. Your economy is moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

With limited information, it is safe to focus on a balanced development that keeps options open. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid base for any transition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and tech, confirm no hidden enemy tech or expansion. If enemy production or expansion is spotted, prioritize scouting and defense over further economy.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when more of the opponent's army composition or production structures are observed, or upon reaching a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a clearer read on the opponent's intentions and can adapt your composition accordingly.
