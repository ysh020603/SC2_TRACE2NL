# N009 — Midgame Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420 seconds, you have a heavy defense and moderate economy, while the opponent shows a ground posture with Marine and Marauder. You need to stabilize and develop. Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marine and Marauder cues. Production and technology appear heavy. The opponent may be preparing a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is moderate, technology is heavy, and defense is heavy. Your economy is moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Increase defense. Maintain expansion. Continue production.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground attack. You need to stabilize your defense while developing your own economy and tech.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and tech, verify enemy ground push is not imminent. If enemy army or production cues indicate an attack, prioritize defensive units and sim-city over further economy.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when more of the opponent's army composition is seen or if they apply pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable state where you can transition to a more aggressive or macro-oriented plan.
