# N007 — Midgame Ground Army and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420-540 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, while the opponent shows a ground posture with Marine and Marauder. You need to strengthen your ground forces and maintain defense. Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marine and Marauder cues. Production and technology appear heavy. The opponent may be preparing a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is heavy, technology is heavy, and economy is heavy. You have a moderate defense. Your army is ground-oriented with Stalker cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground attack. Strengthening your ground forces and maintaining defense is prudent while you continue to develop.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening ground forces, verify enemy is not adding air or siege units. If enemy tech or production suggests a composition shift, adapt your army composition accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when more of the opponent's army composition is seen or if they expand further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army that can hold against a potential push while you continue to tech up.
