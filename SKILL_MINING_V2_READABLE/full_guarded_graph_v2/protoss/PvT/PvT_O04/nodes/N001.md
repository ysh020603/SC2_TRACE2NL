# N001 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with heavy technology investment and moderate production. Opponent shows a ground posture with possible Reaper pressure. Focus on stabilizing your defense while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture to deter early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-oriented Terran with possible early Reaper harassment. Their production is moderate, but their technology investment is heavy, indicating a potential tech switch later.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, but your army composition is not yet defined. Defensive structures are in place, and you are investing heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your defense while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture to deter early aggression.

A stable early game allows you to reach your powerful mid-game tech without falling behind. The opponent's possible Reaper pressure requires careful defensive positioning.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your defensive structures are sufficient to deter Reaper harassment and that your economy is not overextended. If you detect early Reaper pressure or your defense is insufficient, prioritize reinforcing your defense and maintaining a safe economy over further tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or if you detect a significant tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined army composition (ground or air) once you have scouted the opponent's tech path.
