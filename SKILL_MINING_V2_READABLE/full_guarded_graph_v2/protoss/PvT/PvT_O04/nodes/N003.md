# N003 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with heavy production and tech. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. Maintain your defensive posture while increasing your production and tech. Continue to expand to support your growing army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Terran ground army with Marines and Reapers. Their production is heavy, and they are also investing in technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-oriented, and you are investing heavily in production and technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture while increasing your production and tech. Continue to expand to support your growing army.

A strong economy and tech advantage will allow you to outproduce the opponent in the mid-game. Your defensive structures buy you time.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your expansion is adequately defended against Reaper harassment and that your production is sufficient to support your tech investment. If you scout a timing attack or your defense is weak, prioritize reinforcing your defense and maintaining a safe economy over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's tech path or if they attempt a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive stance once you have a critical mass of units and upgrades.
