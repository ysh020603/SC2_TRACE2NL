# N004 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 4-5 minutes, with heavy tech investment and moderate production. Opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers. Continue to invest in technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Terran ground posture with Reapers. Their production is moderate, but their tech investment is heavy, indicating a possible tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, but your army composition is not yet defined. You are heavily investing in technology, and your defense is solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to invest in technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

Heavy tech investment gives you flexibility to counter the opponent's eventual composition. A defensive posture prevents early game losses.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your defense is solid against Reaper harassment and that your economy is not overextended. If you detect early pressure or your defense is insufficient, prioritize reinforcing your defense and maintaining a safe economy over further tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army or if they apply pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have identified the opponent's tech path.
