# N006 — Early Game Tech and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with heavy tech investment and moderate production. Opponent's posture is unknown, but a Marine is seen. Focus on stabilizing your defense and continuing your tech investment. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but a Marine has been spotted. Their production is moderate, and their tech investment is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a Zealot. You are investing heavily in technology, and your defense is solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your defense and continuing your tech investment. Keep your options open for either a ground or air transition.

A solid defense and strong tech give you a stable foundation to adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your defense is solid against possible early harassment, especially from Reapers, and that your economy is not overextended. If you spot a Marine or Reaper and your defense is insufficient, prioritize reinforcing your defense and maintaining a safe economy over further tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have more information about the opponent's army or if they apply pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent's plan.
