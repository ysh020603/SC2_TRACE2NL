# N004 — Early Game Tech and Economy Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, with a heavy economy and moderate production, facing a Terran with unknown posture but heavy tech. Focus on increasing your economy and production, while continuing to develop your technology. Maintain your current expansion and prepare for the midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with a Marauder, and their production is moderate but tech is heavy. Their defense and expansion are light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and you have a Zealot. Your expansion is light or uncertain, but you have the potential to expand.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your economy and production, while continuing to develop your technology. Maintain your current expansion and prepare for the midgame.

Building a strong economy and tech foundation early will pay off in the midgame. The Terran's heavy tech suggests they will have strong units, so you need to be prepared.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before focusing on economy and production, verify that you have a clear read on the Terran's composition and that your defense is sufficient to handle early pressure. If the opponent's posture is unknown or your defense is light, prioritize scouting and defensive preparations before expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you have a clear read on the Terran's composition or when you are ready to expand.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a solid early-midgame position with a strong economy and tech base.
