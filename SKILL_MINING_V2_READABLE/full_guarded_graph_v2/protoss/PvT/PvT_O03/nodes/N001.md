# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, both sides have a heavy economy and production, with the opponent showing a ground posture and possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and technology, strengthen your ground army, and maintain a defensive posture while scouting for pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Hellions, heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army of Zealots and Stalkers. Your defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be careful about potential early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, strengthen your ground army, and maintain a defensive posture while scouting for pressure.

Your heavy economy and technology investment will pay off in the midgame, but you need to survive any early pressure. Building a solid ground core with Zealots and Stalkers gives you flexibility to transition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening economy and tech, verify that your defensive structure can handle a potential early ground push. If defense is insufficient, prioritize building defensive units and static defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent shows signs of a heavy push or if you scout a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame position with a strong economy and a versatile ground army, ready to transition to tech units like Immortals or Colossi.
