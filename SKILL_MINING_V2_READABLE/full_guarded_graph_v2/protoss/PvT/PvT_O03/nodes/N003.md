# N003 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with heavy economy and technology, but the opponent's posture is unknown. Continue to develop your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions. Build a solid foundation for your midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with a Marine, moderate production, and heavy technology. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but your production is moderate and your army is unknown. You have a Zealot, but need to decide on your tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions. Build a solid foundation for your midgame.

The early game is about setting up your economy and tech for the midgame. Scouting will help you adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before committing to a tech path, scout to determine the opponent's intentions. If the opponent's posture is unknown, prioritize scouting and building a flexible army composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's composition or if you scout an aggressive move.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a strong economy and a clear tech direction, ready to build a suitable army.
