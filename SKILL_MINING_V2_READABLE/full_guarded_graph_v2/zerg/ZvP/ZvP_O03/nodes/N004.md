## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current production and economy, and continue developing your technology. Keep your ground army strong.

The opponent's heavy tech and production require you to keep pace. Maintaining a solid economy and production will allow you to tech up without falling behind.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not transitioned to air and that your army size is sufficient to defend against a potential ground push. If the opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure, prioritize building anti-air units and defensive structures over further technology development.

**Recheck when:**
