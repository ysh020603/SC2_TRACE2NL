# N008 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and a heavy economy. The opponent's army is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and expansions. Increase your economy and expansions, and continue developing your ground army and technology. Increase production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's army, but they have a heavy economy and expansions. They may have Zealots.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground army, heavy economy, and moderate production. Your technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansions, and continue developing your ground army and technology. Increase production.

With the opponent's army unknown, focusing on economy and expansion will give you a strong foundation to adapt.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent is not preparing an early aggression (e.g., Zealot rush). If the opponent's army composition remains unknown or shows signs of early aggression, prioritize scouting and defensive unit production over further economic expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you gain more information about the opponent's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger economy and a flexible army composition.
