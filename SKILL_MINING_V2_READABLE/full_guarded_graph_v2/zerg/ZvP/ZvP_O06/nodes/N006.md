# N006 — Mid-Game Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 8 minutes, opponent has a ground army with Observers and Immortals. You have a heavy defense and are producing heavily. Consolidate your army and continue producing. Consider adding Hydralisks or Lurkers for anti-ground damage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. They are likely to attack soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a heavy defense. You are producing heavily and have moderate tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consolidate your army and continue producing. Consider adding Hydralisks or Lurkers for anti-ground damage.

The opponent's army is strong, so you need a well-rounded composition. Your heavy production will allow you to outnumber them.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before consolidating your army, ensure you have enough anti-ground units to counter the opponent's composition. If the opponent attacks or you lack sufficient army size, prioritize producing more units and defensive structures.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have a large army or when the opponent attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a large, well-upgraded army to counter the opponent's composition.
