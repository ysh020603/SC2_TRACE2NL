# N002 — Mid-Game Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 5 minutes, opponent shows a ground-based army with Zealots and Stalkers. Your economy is strong, and you are expanding. Increase your ground army production and continue teching. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks to counter the ground composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and heavy production. They are likely to apply pressure soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and are expanding. Your army is still ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, but you need to strengthen it to match the opponent's production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your ground army production and continue teching. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks to counter the ground composition.

The opponent's heavy ground army requires a stronger defensive force. Your economy can support a larger army, so it's time to invest in production.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing army production, ensure you have sufficient economy to support it and that you are not neglecting scouting. If the opponent shows signs of a tech switch or a timing attack, prioritize scouting and defensive positioning over pure production.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have a comfortable army size or when the opponent shows a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a sufficient ground army to defend or pressure, while continuing to expand and tech.
