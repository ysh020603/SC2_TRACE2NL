# N001 — Early Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, own ground army with Zerglings, opponent unknown but heavy tech. Continue developing economy and ground forces, increase production and expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture, heavy tech investment, moderate production, no reliable combat units seen.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with Zerglings, heavy economy and defense, moderate production, light tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and ground forces, increase production and expansions.

Maintain economic lead while preparing for potential tech switches.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening economy and ground forces, verify that you have sufficient defensive units to survive early aggression. If scouting reveals a large Zergling force or early pressure, prioritize defense over economy.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent's first combat units appear or when tech advantage is established.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger ground army composition with more tech.
