# N004 — Early Midgame Queen Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, own Queen, opponent unknown but heavy tech. Maintain economy and defense, continue ground development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture, heavy tech, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with Queen, heavy economy and defense, light tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and defense, continue ground development.

Queens provide defense and creep, supporting a safe expansion.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before maintaining economy and defense, ensure you are not neglecting army production. If opponent shows signs of aggression, prioritize building units over expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent's army composition is clearer.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid foundation for midgame.
