# N006 — Early Game Zergling Pressure

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own Zerglings, opponent unknown but heavy tech. Increase economy and expansions, maintain ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture, heavy tech, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with Zerglings, heavy economy and defense, light tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansions, maintain ground forces.

Zerglings provide early map control and potential pressure.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and expansions, ensure you have enough defensive units to survive early aggression. If scouting reveals a large Zergling force or early pressure, prioritize defense over economy.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when more information about opponent's plan is available.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong economy and map presence.
