DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, own Zergling/Infestor/Queen/Overseer, opponent ground with Zealots/Stalkers. Maintain ground army, continue economy, consider upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings, Infestors, Queens, Overseers, heavy economy and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, continue economy, consider upgrades.

Infestors provide strong spellcasting against ground-based Protoss armies.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing economy and upgrades, verify that your army size is sufficient to defend against a potential attack. If opponent shows signs of a large push or air units, prioritize army production and anti-air.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent shows air units or heavy tech.
