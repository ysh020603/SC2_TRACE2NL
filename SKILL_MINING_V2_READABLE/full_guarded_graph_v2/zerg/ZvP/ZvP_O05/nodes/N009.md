# N009 — Early Midgame Ground Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, own Zergling/Queen, opponent ground with Zealots/Stalkers. Increase economy and production, continue ground development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy and defense, light tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and production, continue ground development.

Zealots and Stalkers can pressure, so need to maintain army and economy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and production, verify that you have sufficient army size to defend against a potential timing attack. If opponent shows signs of aggression, prioritize army production over tech.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent shows tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stronger ground army and tech.
