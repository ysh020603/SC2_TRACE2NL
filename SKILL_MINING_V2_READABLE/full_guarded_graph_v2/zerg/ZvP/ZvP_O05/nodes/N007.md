# N007 — Midgame Roach Queen Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own Roach/Queen, opponent ground with Stalkers and Void Rays. Maintain ground army, consider adding anti-air if Void Rays become a threat.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Stalkers and Void Rays, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground army with Roaches and Queens, heavy economy and defense, moderate tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, consider adding anti-air if Void Rays become a threat.

Roaches are cost-effective against ground, but Void Rays require anti-air.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before maintaining ground army and considering anti-air, verify that you have enough army size to defend against a potential push. If opponent's air presence increases, prioritize anti-air production.

Recheck if opponent's air presence increases.
