# N012 — Early Game Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game around 180 seconds, with the opponent showing a Zealot. You have a light army and are expanding. Continue to expand and build your economy. Consider adding Queens for defense and scouting to identify the opponent's tech choice.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and is expanding moderately. Their production is moderate and technology is heavy. A Zealot has been observed, indicating a ground-oriented start.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and you are expanding. You have a ground army with light production and technology. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to expand and build your economy. Consider adding Queens for defense and scouting to identify the opponent's tech choice.

The opponent's heavy tech investment suggests they may be teching to a specific unit. By expanding and building a strong economy, you can adapt to whatever they produce.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before expanding further, ensure you have scouting information to confirm the opponent's tech choice. If scouting is lacking, prioritize scouting and building defensive units (e.g., Queens) over expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when the opponent's first combat units are observed or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's tech direction becomes clearer.
