# N011 — Late-Midgame Air and Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame around 600 seconds, with the opponent having a ground army including Void Rays. You have a strong ground army with air support. Continue to strengthen your air and ground forces. Consider adding Corruptors to counter Void Rays and maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Void Rays. Their production is heavy and technology is heavy, indicating a tech-heavy composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and you are expanding. You have a ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air and ground forces. Consider adding Corruptors to counter Void Rays and maintain your economy.

Void Rays are strong against armored units. Corruptors are effective against Void Rays, and your ground army can handle the rest.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before adding Corruptors, ensure your ground army is sufficient to defend against ground pushes. If your ground defense is weak, prioritize ground units before investing in anti-air.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent adds more anti-air or if you need to adjust your composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both air and ground threats, such as Corruptor-Mutalisk with a strong ground army.
