# N004 — Early-Midgame Economy and Tech Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, but light production and tech. The opponent's posture is unknown, but they have heavy economy and tech. Increase your economy and tech, while maintaining your production and expansion. Keep a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but they have heavy economy and tech, with moderate production. They may be teching up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, but light production and tech. Your army is small, with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech, while maintaining your production and expansion. Keep a defensive posture.

Your economy is strong, so you can invest in tech. The opponent's unknown posture means you should be cautious.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and tech, ensure you have enough defensive units to hold against early pressure. If your army is too small or your defense is weak, prioritize building defensive units and scouting for early aggression.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more scouting information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more tech-heavy army while maintaining your economy.
