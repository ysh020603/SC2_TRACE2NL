# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 480 seconds, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with moderate tech. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and tech. Maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with moderate tech. Your army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and tech. Maintain your defense.

The opponent's ground army is strong, so you need a solid ground composition. Your economy supports further investment.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before strengthening your ground army, verify that your economy and production are stable enough to support a large army. If your army size is insufficient to defend against a potential push, prioritize building more units and scouting for tech switches.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if you see air units or a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground army with better tech, while keeping an eye on potential air threats.
