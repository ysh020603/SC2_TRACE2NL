# N012 — Early-Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate tech. The opponent has an air posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Oracles, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate tech. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and you have a moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy, production, and expansion, while continuing to develop your ground army. Consider adding anti-air.

The opponent's air threat requires a response, so you need to prepare anti-air while maintaining your economy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy, production, and expansion, ensure you have enough anti-air units to counter the opponent's air threat. If your anti-air is insufficient, prioritize building Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers and scout for the air composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's air composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air, while maintaining your economy.
