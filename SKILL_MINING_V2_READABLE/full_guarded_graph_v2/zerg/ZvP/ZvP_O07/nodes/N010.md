# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 540 seconds, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with moderate tech. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your air presence and tech, while continuing to develop your economy and expansion. Strengthen your air army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers and Void Rays, heavy production and tech, and heavy defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with moderate tech. Your army includes Corruptors, Queens, and Overseers, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and tech, while continuing to develop your economy and expansion. Strengthen your air army.

The opponent has air units, so you need to respond with anti-air. Your economy supports this transition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence and tech, ensure you have enough ground defense to hold against a ground push. If your ground defense is weak, prioritize building ground units and scouting for the opponent's composition.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more air-heavy composition, while maintaining your economy.
