# N005 — Midgame Tech and Army Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 480 seconds, you have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. The opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Increase your economy, production, and tech, while continuing to expand. Strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and expansion, with heavy tech. Your army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy, production, and tech, while continuing to expand. Strengthen your ground army.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to invest in tech and army. The opponent's ground posture requires a strong ground response.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy, production, and tech, verify that your army size is sufficient to defend against a potential push. If your army is too small, prioritize building more units and scouting for tech switches.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if you see air units or a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army with better tech, while maintaining your economy.
