# N010 — Early Game Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, enemy shows a heavy tech investment with moderate economy and light production. Your own economy is moderate with a heavy expansion. Increase your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and keep scouting to identify the enemy's plan.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is investing heavily in tech, possibly preparing for a tech-heavy composition or a fast tech rush.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and a heavy expansion. You can tech up and build a ground army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and keep scouting to identify the enemy's plan.

Matching the enemy's tech investment is important to avoid falling behind, while maintaining a defensive ground army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before investing further in tech, verify that you have a sufficient army size to defend against a potential timing attack. If your army is small, prioritize building units before teching.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you see the enemy's first combat units or tech structures.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Expand, tech up, and build a balanced ground army to defend and transition.
