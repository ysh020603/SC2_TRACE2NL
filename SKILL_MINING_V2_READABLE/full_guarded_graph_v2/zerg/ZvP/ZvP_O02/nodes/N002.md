# N002 — Early-Midgame Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240-360 seconds, both sides are still in early-midgame with limited information. Enemy posture is unknown, and your own development is flexible. Maintain current development path, keep scouting, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues; production and tech are uncertain. They could be teching or expanding quietly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and tech are still developing; no strong army composition has been committed. You have the flexibility to choose a direction.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, keep scouting, and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

With limited information, committing early could be punished. Maintaining flexibility allows you to react to the enemy's actual composition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to develop, verify that you have at least one scouting unit or structure providing vision of the enemy's main and natural. If scouting is insufficient, prioritize sending an overlord or zergling to gain information before committing further.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate when you gain more scouting information about the enemy's army composition or tech path.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and tech while gathering more information to transition into a counter composition.
