# N004 — Early-Mid Expansion and Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 5-6 minutes, with enemy showing a ground army and you are expanding. Continue expanding and increasing production. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and possibly Roaches. Maintain scouting to detect any tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and is producing heavily. They may be planning an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding further. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing production. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and possibly Roaches. Maintain scouting to detect any tech switches.

Expanding gives you a long-term economic advantage. A strong ground army deters aggression and allows you to take map control.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your expansion is safe and that you have enough units to defend it. If the enemy is producing heavily and may attack, prioritize building defensive units and scouting over expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have secured the expansion or when enemy army movements are detected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base and have a solid army to defend it.
