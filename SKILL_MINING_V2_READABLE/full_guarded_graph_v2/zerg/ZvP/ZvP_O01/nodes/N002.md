# N002 — Early-Mid Transition to Ground Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 4-5 minutes, with enemy showing Zealots and Stalkers, indicating a ground-based army. Strengthen your ground army by adding more Zerglings and possibly Roaches. Continue expanding and increasing production. Maintain defense while scouting for tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and is likely teching heavily. They may be preparing for a timing attack or expanding further.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and you are starting to invest in tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army by adding more Zerglings and possibly Roaches. Continue expanding and increasing production. Maintain defense while scouting for tech switches.

Matching the enemy's ground army with cost-effective Zerg units allows you to defend and potentially counter-attack. Expanding keeps you ahead economically.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, confirm that your army size is adequate to defend against a potential timing attack. If you have not scouted enemy tech buildings or if your army is too small, prioritize producing more Zerglings and Roaches over expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you see enemy tech buildings or a large army movement.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army capable of defending or attacking, while keeping your economy ahead.
