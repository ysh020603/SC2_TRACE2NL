# N001 — Early Economy and Queen Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 3 minutes, with limited enemy information and your own economy expanding. Continue expanding and increasing economy while maintaining a defensive ground posture. Keep producing Queens for defense and creep spread.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is likely expanding and teching, but their army composition is unclear. They may have Zealots and Stalkers, but no confirmed heavy commitment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy with Queens and possibly Zerglings. Your production is moderate and tech is light. You are vulnerable to early pressure but have the foundation for growth.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy while maintaining a defensive ground posture. Keep producing Queens for defense and creep spread.

A strong economy allows you to outproduce the Protoss later. Queens provide defense against early harass and support creep for faster expansion.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your defensive units are sufficient to hold against early pressure. If you have not scouted the enemy's army composition or if your army is too small, prioritize building defensive units (e.g., Zerglings, Queens) and spreading creep over expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you have scouted the enemy's tech or army composition, or when you have secured a third base.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy with multiple bases and a solid Queen count, ready to transition into a more aggressive or tech-heavy build.
