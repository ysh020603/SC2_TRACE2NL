# N010 — Early-Mid Air Defense and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 6 minutes, with enemy showing Phoenixes and Oracles, and you have a strong economy. Maintain your ground army while preparing anti-air. Consider adding Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers for defense. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a moderate air presence with Phoenixes and Oracles, and is producing heavily. They may be planning harass or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Your production is moderate, and you are teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while preparing anti-air. Consider adding Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers for defense. Continue expanding and teching.

Phoenix and Oracle can harass workers and pick off units. Having anti-air options protects your economy and army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that you have adequate anti-air defenses. If the enemy's air presence is growing or if you lack Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers, prioritize building anti-air units and defenses over expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you see more air units or a ground transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a defense against air while maintaining a strong economy.
