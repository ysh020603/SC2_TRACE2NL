# N006 — Early-Mid Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, around 5 minutes, with enemy hidden and you are focusing on economy. Continue expanding and increasing production. Invest in defensive tech such as Lair for Overseers and possibly Roach Warren. Maintain scouting to detect enemy intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is teching heavily but their army composition is unknown. They may be preparing a surprise attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding. Your army is light, with Zerglings and Queens, and you are investing in tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing production. Invest in defensive tech such as Lair for Overseers and possibly Roach Warren. Maintain scouting to detect enemy intentions.

With unknown enemy plans, a defensive posture with tech allows you to react to whatever comes. Expanding gives you the resources to do so.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, ensure that you have adequate defense against a surprise attack. If you have not scouted the enemy's army composition or if your army is too light, prioritize building defensive units and scouting over expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you scout enemy tech or army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a solid economy and tech base to adapt to enemy strategies.
