# N009 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, consider transitioning to a Mutalisk-based army to harass and control the map. Increase your air presence and use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with moderate technology. Your army includes zerglings, Mutalisks, and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and use Mutalisks for harassment and map control.

Mutalisks are effective against Terran ground armies and can force the opponent to invest in anti-air.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing air presence with Mutalisks, verify that your ground defense is sufficient to handle the opponent's ground army. If the opponent has strong anti-air or your ground army is weak, prioritize ground defense over air harassment.

**Recheck when:**  
Reassess when you have a strong air army and the opponent's anti-air is insufficient.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a Mutalisk-based army that can harass and control the map.
