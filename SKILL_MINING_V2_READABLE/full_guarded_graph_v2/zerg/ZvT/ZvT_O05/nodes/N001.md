# N001 — Early Game Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a solid economy and early defense. Maintain your economy and expansion rate while producing queens for defense. Continue teching towards a midgame composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Terran ground posture, possibly with Marines and Reapers. Their production is moderate, but technology investment appears heavy, indicating potential tech builds.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and light technology. Queens are your primary defensive unit, and you are expanding aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion rate while producing queens for defense. Continue teching towards a midgame composition.

A strong economy allows you to outproduce the opponent in the midgame. Queens provide cost-effective defense against early pressure and creep spread.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your economy is solid and your expansion is safe. If the opponent shows early aggression or your defense is insufficient, prioritize producing additional Queens and defensive units over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Reassess when you have a solid economy and the opponent's tech direction becomes clearer.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a healthy economy and a defensive army.
