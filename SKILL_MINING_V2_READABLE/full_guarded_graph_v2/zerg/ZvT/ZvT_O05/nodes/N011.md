# N011 — Early-Midgame Tech Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, focus on expanding and teching up to prepare for the midgame. Increase your expansion and production while investing in technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate production, with light technology. Your army consists of zerglings and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion and production while investing in technology.

A strong economy and tech base will allow you to transition into a powerful midgame army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before expanding and teching, verify that your army size is sufficient to defend against potential aggression. If the opponent's production is heavy and your army is small, prioritize building defensive units over expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Reassess when you have a strong economy and the opponent's tech direction is clear.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame with a solid economy and tech advantage.
