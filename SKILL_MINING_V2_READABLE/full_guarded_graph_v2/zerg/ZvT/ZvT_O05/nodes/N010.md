# N010 — Late-Midgame Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, continue to use Mutalisks for harassment and map control. Continue to strengthen your air army and use Mutalisks to harass expansions and force the opponent to react.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army includes zerglings, Mutalisks, and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air army and use Mutalisks to harass expansions and force the opponent to react.

Mutalisks can keep the opponent off balance and create opportunities for expansions or tech.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before continuing to harass with Mutalisks, ensure that your economy and production are not neglected. If the opponent's anti-air becomes too strong, prioritize transitioning to a ground-based army or expanding.

**Recheck when:**  
Reassess when the opponent's anti-air becomes too strong or you have a decisive advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air superiority and force the opponent to invest in anti-air.
