# N009 — Early Game Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180-240 game time, you have a heavy economy and are expanding. Enemy intelligence is uncertain, with only a Reaper seen, indicating possible early harassment. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture against potential Reaper harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, with a Reaper seen. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, and exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture against potential Reaper harassment.

A Reaper can harass workers, so having Zerglings and Queens helps defend. Continuing tech will give you more options.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that you have enough units to defend against Reaper harassment and that more enemy information is available. If enemy information is still uncertain, prioritize scouting and building defensive units over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck as more enemy information becomes available.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy and tech base for the midgame.
