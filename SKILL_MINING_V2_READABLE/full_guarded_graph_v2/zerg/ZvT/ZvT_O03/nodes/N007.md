# N007 — Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 480-540 game time, you have a heavy economy and are expanding. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Marines and Hellions, indicating potential harassment. Increase your production and expansion to maintain economic dominance. Continue strengthening your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Marines and Hellions. Exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and your production is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and expansion to maintain economic dominance. Continue strengthening your ground army.

A production advantage will allow you to overwhelm the Terran with numbers. Expanding secures your economy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your production is keeping up with your expansion and that the opponent has not transitioned to air or shown signs of a large push. If the opponent shows air units or a large push, prioritize building anti-air and defensive units over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent transitions to air or shows signs of a large push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong economy and production to support a large army.
