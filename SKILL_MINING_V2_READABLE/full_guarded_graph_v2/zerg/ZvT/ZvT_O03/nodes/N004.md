# N004 — Midgame Ground Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420-540 game time, you have a heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Marines and Hellions, indicating potential harassment or early pressure. Maintain your ground army and continue developing your technology. Be prepared to defend against Hellion run-bys or Marine pushes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Marines and Hellions. Exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, giving you a solid ground defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue developing your technology. Be prepared to defend against Hellion run-bys or Marine pushes.

Hellions can be dangerous to workers, so having Roaches and Queens helps defend. Continuing tech will give you more options.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your defenses are prepared against Hellion run-bys or Marine pushes and that the opponent has not transitioned to air or shown signs of a large push. If the opponent shows air units or a large push, prioritize building anti-air and defensive units over further technology development.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent transitions to air or shows signs of a large push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure your bases and tech up for the midgame.
