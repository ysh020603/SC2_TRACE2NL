# N006 — Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420 game time, you have a heavy economy and are investing in technology. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs, indicating potential multi-pronged pressure. Continue investing in technology while maintaining a defensive ground army. Be prepared for drops or harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs. Air presence is light.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are investing heavily in technology. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue investing in technology while maintaining a defensive ground army. Be prepared for drops or harassment.

Technology will give you access to stronger units and upgrades. A defensive posture is needed to handle potential multi-pronged attacks.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your army is sufficient to defend against drops or harassment and that the opponent has not shown signs of a timing attack or air transition. If the opponent shows a timing attack or air units, prioritize building defensive units and anti-air over further technology investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent shows signs of a timing attack or air transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a tech advantage and prepare for the midgame.
