# N008 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 game time, you have a heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Battlecruisers, indicating a potential air transition. Increase your air presence and continue developing your technology. Maintain a strong ground army for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Battlecruisers. Air presence is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, production, and technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, and you have a heavy air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue developing your technology. Maintain a strong ground army for defense.

The opponent's Battlecruisers require anti-air. Your Mutalisks can harass and counter, but you may need Corruptors for stronger anti-air.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your air presence is sufficient to counter the opponent's moderate air and that the opponent has not committed heavily to air or shown signs of a ground push. If the opponent commits heavily to air, prioritize building more anti-air; if they show a ground push, prioritize building ground defenses.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent commits heavily to air or shows signs of a ground push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army with strong anti-air capabilities.
