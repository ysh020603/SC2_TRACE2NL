# N002 — Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300-360 game time, you have a strong economy and are expanding. Enemy intelligence shows a Terran ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines, indicating a potential defensive or siege-oriented approach. Increase your production and expansion to match the Terran's heavy economy. Continue strengthening your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Terran ground posture with heavy production and technology, including Siege Tanks and Marines. Exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens, and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and expansion to match the Terran's heavy economy. Continue strengthening your ground army.

Matching the Terran's production and expansion will prevent falling behind. A strong ground army is needed to break siege lines or defend.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your production is sufficient to support your expansion and that the opponent has not transitioned to air or shown signs of all-in pressure. If the opponent shows air units or an all-in, prioritize building anti-air and defensive units over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if the opponent transitions to air or shows signs of all-in pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a production advantage and prepare for midgame engagements.
