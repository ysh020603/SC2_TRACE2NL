# N005 — Early Game Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180-240 game time, you have a heavy economy and are expanding. Enemy intelligence is uncertain, with no clear combat units observed, but production and technology appear heavy. Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue developing your technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, with no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology appear heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army is minimal, with only Queens for defense, and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue developing your technology.

A strong economy early will pay off later. Since the opponent's intentions are unclear, it's safe to focus on macro.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that you have enough defensive units to protect your expansion and that more enemy information is available. If enemy information is still uncertain, prioritize scouting and building defensive units over further expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck as soon as more enemy information is available.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economic lead and prepare for the midgame.
