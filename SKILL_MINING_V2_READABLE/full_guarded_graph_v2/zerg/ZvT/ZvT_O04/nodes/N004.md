# N004 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame to midgame, opponent ground with Marines, own ground with Zerglings and Queens. Maintain economy and production, strengthen ground, continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Marines, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame to midgame, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech, ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and production, strengthen ground, continue tech.

Sustain macro while preparing for potential engagements.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent remains on a ground posture with Marines and that your economy is not overextended. If the opponent tech switches to air or shows signs of a timing attack, prioritize army production and scouting over continued tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck at midgame timings or if opponent tech changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a solid ground army and tech.
