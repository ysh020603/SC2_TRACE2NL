- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Thors, Ravens, heavy production and tech.

- Late-midgame, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech, ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

Maintain ground strength, continue economy, consider tech counters.

Handle heavy Terran ground composition.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your ground army can handle the opponent's heavy ground composition and that your tech counters are appropriate. If the opponent adds air or your army is insufficient, prioritize army production and scouting over further tech.
