# N012 — Late-Midgame Air Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent ground with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, Medivacs, own ground with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, Overseers. Increase air, strengthen air, continue economy, increase upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Late-midgame, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech, ground and air mix.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air, strengthen air, continue economy, increase upgrades.

Maintain air superiority and harass Terran economy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your air army is sufficient to counter the opponent's ground forces and that your economy can support continued upgrades. If the opponent tech switches to heavy anti-air or your army is insufficient, prioritize army production and scouting over further upgrades.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent tech or army composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach late game with a strong air force.
