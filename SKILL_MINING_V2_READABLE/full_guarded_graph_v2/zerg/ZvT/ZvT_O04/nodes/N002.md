# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Focus

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent ground posture with Marines and Ghosts, own ground with Zerglings and Queens. Strengthen ground army, maintain economy, continue tech, develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture, possible Marines and Ghosts, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech, ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, maintain economy, continue tech, develop with safety checks.

Match opponent's ground focus while maintaining economic lead.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent's ground posture is confirmed and that you have sufficient army size to defend against potential timing attacks. If the opponent shows signs of air tech or heavy pressure, prioritize army production and scouting over further tech investment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent shows air or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
