- Opponent ground posture with SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper, Marauder cues. Heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Queens, Overseers. Heavy production and tech, heavy economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue expansions and production, increase tech.

Hydralisks provide anti-ground and anti-air, making your army more versatile. Continue macro to support a strong push.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not shown a tech switch or air presence. If such a switch is detected, prioritize adding anti-air and adjust composition accordingly.
