# N003 — Midgame Ground Push Preparation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent ground posture with heavy production, own army has Queens. Economy and expansions heavy. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue tech, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent ground posture with Marine/Reaper cues, heavy production, heavy tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Queens, moderate production, light tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue tech, maintain defense.

As the game progresses, you need to prepare for potential pushes. Maintaining economy while building army is key.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that the opponent has not shown air units or a tech switch. If such a switch is detected, prioritize adding anti-air and adjust composition accordingly.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when opponent shows air or tech switch, or when you reach a new tech tier.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with more tech, possibly adding Hydralisks or other units.
