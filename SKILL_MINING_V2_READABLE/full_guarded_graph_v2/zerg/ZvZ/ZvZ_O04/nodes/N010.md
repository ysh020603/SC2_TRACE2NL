# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, you have air presence with Mutalisk cues, while the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy tech. Increase your air presence and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your air army. Use Mutalisks for harassment while building a ground defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen, and Overseer cues. Their production is heavy, tech heavy, and they are likely to have a strong ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and moderate tech. Your army includes Mutalisks, giving you air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your air army. Use Mutalisks for harassment while building a ground defense.

Mutalisks can harass the opponent's economy and force them to react. Your heavy economy supports this playstyle, and you can transition to a more powerful air composition later.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your ground defense is sufficient to hold against the opponent's heavy-tech ground army. If the opponent gets anti-air or shows signs of a ground push, prioritize ground defense and army production over further air harassment.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when the opponent gets anti-air or if you plan to transition to a different composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air superiority while building a solid ground army to defend.
