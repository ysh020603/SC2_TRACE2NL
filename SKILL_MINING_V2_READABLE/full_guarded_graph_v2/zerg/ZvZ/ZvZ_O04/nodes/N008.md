# N008 — Early Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, you have a heavy economy and heavy expansion, while the opponent shows heavy production and moderate tech. Increase your tech and production, continue your expansion, and strengthen your ground army. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their production is heavy, tech moderate, and they are likely to have a larger army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy expansion, and heavy defense. Your ground army is strong and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your tech and production, continue your expansion, and strengthen your ground army. Develop with safety checks.

The opponent has heavy production, so you need to tech up to get a better army composition. Your heavy economy allows you to invest in tech while maintaining your army.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your army size is not too small relative to the opponent's heavy production. If the opponent's army appears larger or they show signs of attacking, prioritize army production over tech.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when you see the opponent's army composition or if you plan to engage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where your tech and army can match the opponent's production.
