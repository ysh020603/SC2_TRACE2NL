# N012 — Early Game Production and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, you have a heavy economy but a moderate expansion, while the opponent shows a ground posture. Increase your production and expansion, maintain your defense, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize and develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their economy is heavy, production moderate, and tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, but your expansion is moderate. You have a heavy defense and a ground army with moderate production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and expansion, maintain your defense, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize and develop.

You have a good economy but need to expand to support more production. Increasing production and expansion will allow you to outproduce the opponent.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening this direction, verify that your expansion is safe and your defense is adequate. If you scout an attack or your opponent's army is larger, prioritize defense and army production over expansion.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck when your expansion is complete or if you scout an attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a heavy expansion and production while maintaining a solid ground army.
