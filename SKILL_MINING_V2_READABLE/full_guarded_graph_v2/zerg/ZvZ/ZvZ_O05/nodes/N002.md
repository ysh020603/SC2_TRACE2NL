# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 7 minutes, the game has progressed to the midgame. Enemy intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, but their production has increased to heavy and technology is moderate. Your own army includes Roaches, indicating a transition to a more robust ground composition. Strengthen your ground army by adding more Roaches and possibly tech upgrades. Continue to expand your economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate technology. Their army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and they may be preparing for a push or tech advancement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. You are in a good position to strengthen your forces.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army by adding more Roaches and possibly tech upgrades. Continue to expand your economy and production.

The enemy's heavy production suggests they may be building a large army. Matching their production and strengthening your ground forces will help you defend or counter-attack.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before adding more Roaches, ensure you have sufficient anti-air or scouting to detect an air transition. If enemy shows signs of air tech or a timing attack, prioritize building anti-air defenses and scouting.

**Recheck when:**  
Re-evaluate if the enemy shows signs of an air transition or a timing attack.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
