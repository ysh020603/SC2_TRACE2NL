# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Economy Focus

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Opponent shows Queen cues, pressure possible. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue tech, maintain defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Queen cues. Production moderate, tech light or uncertain. Economy heavy, expansion heavy. Pressure possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Defense light, expansion heavy. Pressure possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue tech, maintain defense and expansion.

With both sides on similar ground macro paths, maintaining economy and ground strength while keeping tech options open allows you to respond to opponent's next move without overcommitting.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before deepening ground army and economy, verify opponent has not transitioned to air or committed to heavy pressure. If air units or a large attack are detected, prioritize building anti-air and defensive structures.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with increased economy and tech, ready for early-midgame.
