# N003 — Aggressive Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Opponent Queen cues, pressure possible. Increase economy, expansion, and production, continue tech, strengthen ground.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Queen cues, ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Defense light, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, expansion, and production, continue tech, strengthen ground.

With both sides on similar paths, pushing economy and expansion can give you a macro lead if you can defend against potential pressure.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and production, verify you have sufficient army size to defend against potential pressure. If army is too small, prioritize building units before expanding further.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent pressures or techs up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy and larger ground army.
