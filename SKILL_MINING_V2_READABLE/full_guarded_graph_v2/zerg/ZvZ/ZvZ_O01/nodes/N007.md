# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent ground with heavy air presence, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech. Zergling, Mutalisk, Roach, Queen cues. Increase defense, economy, continue production and tech, strengthen ground.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Zergling, Mutalisk, Roach, Queen cues, ground posture with heavy air presence, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in late-midgame with ground macro posture, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech. Defense heavy, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, economy, continue production and tech, strengthen ground.

With opponent having air presence, you need to bolster defenses and possibly tech to anti-air while maintaining economy.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing defense, verify opponent's commitment to air or ground. If they commit to air, prioritize anti-air; if ground, prioritize ground defense.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent commits to air or ground.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defensive posture with anti-air capabilities.
