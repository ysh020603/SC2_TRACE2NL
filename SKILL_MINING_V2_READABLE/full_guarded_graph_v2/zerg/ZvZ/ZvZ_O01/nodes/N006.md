# N006 — Expansion and Production Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Zergling and Queen cues. Increase economy, expansion, production, continue tech, strengthen ground.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Zergling and Queen cues, ground posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early-midgame with ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Defense light, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, expansion, production, continue tech, strengthen ground.

With both sides on similar paths, pushing economy and production can give you a lead if you can defend against potential pressure.

## Conditional Safety Guardrail

Before increasing economy and production, verify opponent has not started a pressure or teched up. If they have, prioritize building units and defensive structures.

**Recheck when:**  
Recheck if opponent pressures or techs up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy and larger ground army.
