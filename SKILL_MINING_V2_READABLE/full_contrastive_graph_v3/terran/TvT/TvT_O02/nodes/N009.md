# N009 — Early Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, the opponent has a ground army with Reapers, and a heavy economy. You have a ground army with Marines. Strengthen your ground forces and increase your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Reapers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Reapers, heavy production and technology, and a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, and a moderate economy. Your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces and increase your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Reapers.

Both players are on a similar ground macro path. Strengthening your army and economy will help you gain an advantage in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong midgame position with a powerful ground army and a robust economy.
