# N002 — Early-Midgame Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, the opponent's posture remains unknown, but you have had time to develop your economy and technology. Continue maintaining your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep your options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues, and their production and technology remain light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are still light or uncertain, but you have had time to invest in infrastructure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep your options open.

The opponent's lack of visible units could indicate a greedy economy or a hidden tech switch. Maintaining a balanced development allows you to respond to either.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a strong economy and technology base, ready to produce a suitable army.
