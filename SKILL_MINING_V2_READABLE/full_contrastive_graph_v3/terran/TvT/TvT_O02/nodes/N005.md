# N005 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, the opponent has a ground-based army with Marines and Reapers, and a heavy economy. You have a similar ground composition. Continue strengthening your ground forces and increasing your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and technology, and a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Marines, and a heavy economy and production. Your technology is also heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground forces and increasing your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

Both players are on a similar ground macro path. Strengthening your army and economy will help you gain an advantage in the midgame.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Midgame Ground Macro Mirror

**When this applies:** Midgame, when both you and the opponent have ground-based armies with Marines and Reapers, and heavy economies.

**Mistake:** Neglecting your economy while building an army, or overcommitting to a single unit composition without adaptability.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening your ground forces and increasing your economy. Consider adding supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

**Why:** Both players are on a similar ground macro path; strengthening army and economy helps gain an advantage in the midgame.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choices or when you are ready to push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong midgame position with a powerful ground army and a robust economy.
