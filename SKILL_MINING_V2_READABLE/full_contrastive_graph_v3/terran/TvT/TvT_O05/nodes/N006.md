- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Marine, Reaper, Medivac, Banshee. Production heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology. Strengthen ground forces and consider adding anti-air units.

The opponent's Banshees and Medivacs suggest air harassment. Strengthening defense and adding turrets or Vikings protects your economy while you build a ground army.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
