## Summary

By early-midgame, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Your economy is heavy, and you have a strong expansion. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but they have Zealots and Stalkers. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy and production. Keep your expansion and technology development going.

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy and production. Keep your expansion and technology development going.

Both sides are on a similar macro path, so maintaining your economy and production will keep you competitive. Your heavy technology investment will pay off in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a midgame army composition that can handle the opponent's ground forces, possibly adding Siege Tanks or other tech units.
