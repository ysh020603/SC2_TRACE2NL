# N002 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Moderate Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 4 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, but your defense is moderate. The opponent is ground-oriented with Zealots and Stalkers. Maintain your current production and economy, while slightly increasing your defense. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current production and economy, while slightly increasing your defense. Continue technology development.

A moderate defense provides safety against potential pressure while you continue to develop your economy and technology.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army with a mix of Marines, Marauders, and possibly Siege Tanks.
