# N012 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 6 minutes, the opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers. You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders. Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

With a ground army already established, it is efficient to continue producing units and expanding your economy to support a strong mid-game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army with additional production and upgrades.
