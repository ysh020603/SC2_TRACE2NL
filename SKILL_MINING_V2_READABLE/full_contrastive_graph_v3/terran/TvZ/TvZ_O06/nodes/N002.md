# N002 — Midgame Ground Strength and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-300s) with own heavy production and tech, ground army. Opponent still ground macro with moderate production and light tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and expanding. Maintain production and tech progression. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other tech units to your composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent remains on a ground macro path with Zerglings and Queens, but their production is still moderate and tech light. They are likely expanding and teching up as well.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is now heavy, and you have a strong ground army. Economy and expansions are heavy, providing a solid resource base. Tech is heavy, giving you access to advanced units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and expanding. Maintain production and tech progression. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other tech units to your composition.

Your heavy production and tech give you a potential army quality advantage. Expanding further will secure your economy for the long game.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid overextending economy without defense

**When this applies:** Early-midgame (around 240-300s) with own heavy production and tech, ground army. Opponent still ground macro with moderate production and light tech.

**Mistake:** Overextending your economy without sufficient army to defend, or neglecting scouting for tech switches or all-ins.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening your ground army and expanding. Maintain production and tech progression. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other tech units to your composition.

**Why:** Your heavy production and tech give you a potential army quality advantage. Expanding further will secure your economy for the long game.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you have a clear army lead or when opponent's tech or production changes significantly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more powerful midgame army, possibly including Siege Tanks, and prepare for potential engagements.
