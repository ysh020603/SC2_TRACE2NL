# N006 — Early-Mid Game Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 5-6 minutes, you have Marines and Reapers, with heavy production and tech. The opponent has Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and tech. Continue to increase your economy and expansion, while maintaining your production and tech. Keep your army strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and moderate tech. They are likely to be expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early-mid game. Your defenses are light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your economy and expansion, while maintaining your production and tech. Keep your army strong.

Your heavy tech and production give you an advantage. Expanding will help you maintain your lead.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Early Midgame: Avoid Overcommitting Without Scouting

**When this applies:** Around 5-6 minutes, with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and tech, against Zerglings and Queens with moderate production and tech.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to a single tech path without scouting, potentially missing tech switches.

**Correction:** Continue to increase economy and expansion while maintaining production and tech. Keep the army strong.

**Why:** Heavy tech and production provide an advantage; expanding secures the lead.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have scouted the opponent's tech or if you are ready to take a third base.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger mid-game army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.
