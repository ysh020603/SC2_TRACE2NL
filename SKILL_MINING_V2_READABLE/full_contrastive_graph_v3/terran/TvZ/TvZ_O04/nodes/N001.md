# N001 — Early-Mid Game Tech and Production Ramp

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 4 minutes, you have a solid ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers, and your economy and production are heavy. The opponent is likely on a ground-focused macro build with Queens and light tech. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing your economy. Maintain your production and technology development, and keep your defenses up.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a ground macro posture with Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. They are likely expanding heavily and may apply pressure soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early-mid game. Your army is ground-oriented and you are looking to expand your advantage.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing your economy. Maintain your production and technology development, and keep your defenses up.

Your heavy tech and production give you a long-term advantage. By continuing to grow your economy and army, you can outscale the Zerg, who is still in a moderate production phase.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger mid-game army composition with more Siege Tanks and Medivacs, while maintaining a heavy economy.
