# N007 — Late-Midgame Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own heavy ground army, opponent heavy ground with possible air. Increase defense, continue tech, strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy heavy ground army with Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Late-midgame, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, continue tech, strengthen ground army.

Prepare for potential late-game engagements with solid defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game army with strong defensive positioning.
