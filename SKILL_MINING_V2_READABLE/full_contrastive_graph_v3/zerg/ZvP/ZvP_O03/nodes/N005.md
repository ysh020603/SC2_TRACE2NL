# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600-720 seconds, you have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and technology. The opponent shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Increase your economy and technology, continue expanding and producing. Strengthen your ground army further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. They have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a strong ground army, heavy economy, and heavy production and technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and technology, continue expanding and producing. Strengthen your ground army further.

The opponent's composition is strong on the ground, so you need to maintain a powerful ground force and tech to counter their upgrades.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Balance economy, tech, and ground strength in late midgame

**When this applies:** Late midgame, around 600-720 seconds, when you have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and technology, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries.

**Mistake:** Neglecting economy or upgrades, or overcommitting to a single attack if the opponent is defending well, while only focusing on strengthening ground forces.

**Correction:** Increase your economy and technology, continue expanding and producing. Strengthen your ground army further.

**Why:** The opponent's composition is strong on the ground, so you need to maintain a powerful ground force and tech to counter their upgrades.

**Recheck when:** Recheck if the opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more advanced tech composition, such as Lurkers or Brood Lords, while maintaining economy.
