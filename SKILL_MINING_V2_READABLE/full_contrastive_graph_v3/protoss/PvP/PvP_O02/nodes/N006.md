# N006 — Early Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has shown a ground posture with Zealots, and their economy is heavy. You have a moderate economy and heavy tech. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Their economy is heavy, and their production is moderate. They may be planning a ground-based attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

The opponent's ground posture suggests they may be building a Zealot-heavy army. By strengthening your ground forces and maintaining your tech advantage, you can counter their composition.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Ground Defense Priority

**When this applies:** Early midgame, opponent has ground posture with Zealots, heavy economy, moderate production. You have moderate economy and heavy tech.

**Mistake:** Overcommitting to air units or neglecting scouting, assuming the opponent lacks anti-air.

**Correction:** Strengthen your ground army and continue developing economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

**Why:** The opponent's ground posture suggests a Zealot-heavy army. Ground forces and tech advantage counter their composition.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or when ready to push out. If passive, consider expanding or teching further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition, possibly with Stalkers or Immortals, to counter the opponent's Zealots.
