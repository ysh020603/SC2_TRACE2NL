# N008 — Late Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both you and the opponent have a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. Both economies and production are heavy. Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential large-scale engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. Their economy and production are heavy, and they have expanded heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army consisting of Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. Your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential large-scale engagement.

Both sides have similar ground compositions. By continuing to develop your economy and tech, you can gain an advantage in upgrades or army size.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Balanced Ground and Tech

**When this applies:** Late midgame, both you and opponent have ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals. Both economies and production are heavy.

**Mistake:** Neglecting air defense or overextending economy without adequate defense.

**Correction:** Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential large-scale engagement.

**Why:** Both sides have similar ground compositions. Continuing economy and tech development gives an advantage in upgrades or army size.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's main army composition or when ready to push out. If passive, consider expanding or teching further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground army composition, possibly with Colossi or Archons, to gain an edge in the late game.
