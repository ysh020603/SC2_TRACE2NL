## Summary

At around 5 minutes, the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, but you have a mixed army with Oracles and a heavy economy. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and technology development.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and technology development.

The opponent is ground-focused, so adding air units like Oracles can provide harassment and scouting advantages while you build a mixed composition.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Air Harassment Against Ground

**When this applies:** Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) when the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, but you have a mixed army with Oracles and a heavy economy.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to air without sufficient ground support, or neglecting economy.

**Correction:** Increase air presence and strengthen air forces while continuing economy and technology development.

**Why:** The opponent is ground-focused, so adding air units like Oracles can provide harassment and scouting advantages while you build a mixed composition.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions to air or if your air investment is not paying off.
