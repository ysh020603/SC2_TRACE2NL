# N011 — Midgame: Ground Army and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, the opponent shows a ground posture with Queen cues. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Zealot cues. Strengthen ground forces, increase production and technology, and maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase production and technology, and maintain economy.

The opponent's ground posture suggests potential pressure. Investing in economy and tech while maintaining production provides a solid foundation.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Ground vs Ground: Avoid Neglecting Economy

**When this applies:** Midgame, opponent shows ground posture with Queen cues; your own ground posture with Zealot cues.

**Mistake:** Tempting to over-invest in army and tech while neglecting economy, risking being caught off guard by a tech switch or large attack.

**Correction:** Strengthen ground forces, increase production and technology, and maintain economy.

**Why:** Opponent's ground posture suggests potential pressure. Investing in economy and tech while maintaining production provides a solid foundation.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when opponent's tech or army composition becomes clearer, or when your own tech milestones are reached.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stable ground army and tech advantage to transition into the midgame.
