## Summary

At around 360 seconds, the opponent has an air-oriented army with Phoenixes and Oracles, while you have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. ### Own cues

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider adding anti-air units such as Marines, Thors, or Vikings to counter the opponent's air presence. Maintain your ground army and continue expanding.

The opponent's air units can be a threat, so you need to have a response. Adding anti-air units will help defend against harass and potential air attacks.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Ground vs Air: Don't Ignore Anti-Air

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, opponent air-oriented with Phoenixes and Oracles, you have ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, around 360 seconds.

**Mistake:** Neglecting your ground army entirely or over-investing in anti-air without a clear threat.

**Correction:** Add anti-air units such as Marines, Thors, or Vikings to counter the opponent's air presence. Maintain your ground army and continue expanding.

**Why:** The opponent's air units can be a threat, so you need a response. Adding anti-air units helps defend against harass and potential air attacks.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent transitions back to ground or if you face a large push.
