## Summary

Entering the midgame, focus on increasing technology and production to match the opponent's heavy investment. Increase your technology and production to match the opponent. Continue expanding and strengthening your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their production and technology are heavy, indicating a strong midgame push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have moderate production with heavy technology investment. Your army consists of zerglings and queens, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your technology and production to match the opponent. Continue expanding and strengthening your ground army.

Investing in technology now allows you to unlock key units like Hydralisks or Roaches, which are essential against a Terran ground army.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid over-investing in tech at the expense of army

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, when you have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy tech, and the opponent is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech.

**Mistake:** Over-investing in technology and upgrades while neglecting army size and scouting, leaving you unprepared for an enemy attack.

**Correction:** Increase your technology and production to match the opponent. Continue expanding and strengthening your ground army.

**Why:** Investing in technology now allows you to unlock key units like Hydralisks or Roaches, which are essential against a Terran ground army, but you must also maintain a sufficient army to defend.

**Recheck when:** Reassess when you have a solid army composition and the opponent's tech direction is clear.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
