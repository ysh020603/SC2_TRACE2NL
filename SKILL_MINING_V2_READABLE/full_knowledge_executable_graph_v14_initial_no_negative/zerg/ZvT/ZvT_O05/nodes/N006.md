# N006 — Midgame Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, consider transitioning to a more advanced army composition based on the opponent's tech. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air units if the opponent transitions to Battlecruisers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army includes Hydralisks, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a tech investment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding air units if the opponent transitions to Battlecruisers.

The opponent's Battlecruiser presence suggests a potential air transition, so preparing anti-air is wise.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile army that can handle both ground and air threats.

### Human-Trajectory Interpretation

The trajectory action cues include Mutalisk, Drone, Overlord, CreepTumor, Zergling, Corruptor, Queen, Hatchery, Extractor, and SporeCrawler. This indicates a focus on economy (Drones, Hatchery, Extractor), production (Zerglings, Queens), and air transition (Mutalisks, Corruptors), with defensive structures (SporeCrawlers) and creep spread (CreepTumor). The presence of Corruptors suggests preparation against air threats like Battlecruisers.

### Applicability Checks

- Check that the opponent's army is indeed ground-oriented and that air presence is moderate, as per Enemy Intelligence.
- Verify that the own economy and production are heavy enough to support an air transition.
- Confirm that the technology path to Mutalisks and Corruptors is available or in progress.
- Ensure that defensive structures (SporeCrawlers) are in place to protect against potential air harassment.

### Stop / Repair / Recheck

If the opponent's air presence increases significantly, recheck the composition and consider adding more Corruptors or SporeCrawlers. If the economy is insufficient to support the air transition, recheck the expansion and drone count. If the ground defense is weak, recheck the defensive structures and army composition. If supply blocks occur, recheck the Overlord count and production.
