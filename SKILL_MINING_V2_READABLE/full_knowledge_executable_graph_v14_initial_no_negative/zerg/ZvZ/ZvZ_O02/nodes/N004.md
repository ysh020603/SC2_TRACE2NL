# N004 — Late Midgame Preparation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, you have a stable economy, but the opponent's strategy is still unknown. You need to prepare for the late game while maintaining flexibility. Continue to develop your economy and production while increasing your army size. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture remains unknown, with no reliable combat-unit cues. They could be building a large army or expanding further.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you have a solid foundation. You have not committed to a specific army composition, but you are ready to adapt.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and production while increasing your army size. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

As the game progresses, the importance of a strong army increases. By maintaining a balanced approach and scouting, you can prepare for potential conflicts.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized strategy once you have a better understanding of the opponent's intentions. If the opponent is passive, you can focus on tech and upgrades; if they are aggressive, you need to build a stronger army.
