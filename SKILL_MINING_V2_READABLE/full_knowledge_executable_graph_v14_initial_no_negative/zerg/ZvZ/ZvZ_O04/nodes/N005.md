## Summary

Midgame, you have a heavy economy and heavy production, but the opponent shows heavy tech and a ground posture with Roach cues. Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize and prepare for a potential engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, and Queen cues. Their production is heavy, tech heavy, and they are likely to have a strong army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your ground army is strong and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, continue your production and tech, and strengthen your ground army. Stabilize and prepare for a potential engagement.

The opponent has heavy tech, so you need to ensure your army composition can handle theirs. Increasing defense and economy gives you the resources to tech up and outproduce them.
