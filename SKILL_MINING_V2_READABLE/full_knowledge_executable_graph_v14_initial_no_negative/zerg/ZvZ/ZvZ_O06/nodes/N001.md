## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Keep your defense light but be ready to react to early pressure. Continue producing Queens and Zerglings to maintain safety.

In ZvZ, early economy is crucial, but you need some army to defend against early Zergling rushes. Queens provide defense and creep spread, while Zerglings offer map presence and scouting. Maintaining a heavy economy sets up a strong midgame.
