- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and ground forces. Maintain production and consider increasing technology to keep pace with the opponent's heavy tech investment.

The opponent's heavy tech investment suggests they may be teching to a specific composition. By maintaining a strong economy and ground army, you can adapt to whatever they produce while staying ahead economically.
