### L03 — Stabilize Before Expanding

**When this applies:** Midgame, own ground posture with Zealots; opponent ground with Zerglings and Queens; both heavy economy.

**Mistake:** Pushing out aggressively with your tech advantage without ensuring a stable defensive position.

**Correction:** Stabilize then develop, increase production and tech, maintain defense.

**Why:** Ensuring a stable position before committing to further development prevents overextension and allows safe growth.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when the situation stabilizes or when opponent tech changes.
