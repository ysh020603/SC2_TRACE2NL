# N002 — Early Game Heavy Production with Moderate Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with opponent showing a ground posture and possible Reaper cues. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and heavy technology, but economy is moderate. Increase your economy to catch up to your heavy production and technology. Maintain production and continue technology upgrades. Keep defense solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy defense, moderate economy, and moderate expansion. Production is heavy, technology is heavy. Representative units include Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy to catch up to your heavy production and technology. Maintain production and continue technology upgrades. Keep defense solid.

Your production is ahead, but economy lags. Boosting economy will sustain your production and tech investments, ensuring you don't fall behind in the long run.
