# N003 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines. Your own army composition is still unknown. Focus on expanding your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines. They may be planning an early push or a fast expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army composition is not yet defined. You have a Reaper for scouting.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

In the early game, a strong economy and technology foundation is crucial for midgame strength. Scouting helps you adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## V4 Execution Recheck

Before acting on this node:

- Keep the queue short, executable, and prerequisite-ordered.
- Count completed, pending, and queued supply; never repeat three supply providers in one decision.
- At bank ≥750 or army supply <10 near 05:00, convert resources into production and combat units before optional greed.
- At army supply <15 near 06:00 or under OverwhelmingDisadvantage, override economy/tech with army, counters, detection, and production.
- Verify completed parent production structures before add-ons and dependent units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting, while maintaining your economic lead.
