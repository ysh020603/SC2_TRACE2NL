# N006 — Early Game Heavy Economy and Technology

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game: The opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your own economy is moderate, and technology is heavy. Increase your economy and production to match the opponent's heavy economy. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues, but economy and expansion are heavy, production moderate, and technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, technology is heavy, but production is light or uncertain. You have no reliable combat-unit cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production to match the opponent's heavy economy. Continue technology development.

The opponent is investing heavily in economy and technology, suggesting a macro-oriented game. Matching their economy and production will keep you competitive.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Match Opponent's Economy

**When this applies:** Early game when opponent has heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and your own economy is moderate with heavy technology.

**Mistake:** Focusing on technology and economy while neglecting army production, leaving you vulnerable to an attack.

**Correction:** Increase your economy and production to match the opponent's heavy economy, while continuing technology development.

**Why:** The opponent's heavy economy and technology indicate a macro-oriented game; matching their economy and production keeps you competitive.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see enemy units or when your own production and technology become more defined.
