# N006 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, but you are transitioning to an air-oriented army. Your air presence is moderate, and you have Banshees as representative units. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology, but you are adding air units like Banshees. Your economy is heavy, and your expansion is heavy. Your defense posture is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

Transitioning to air can give you an advantage if the opponent is heavily ground-based. Banshees can harass and provide mobility.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid neglecting ground defense during air transition

**When this applies:** Midgame with a ground-oriented army transitioning to air, with moderate air presence and Banshees.

**Mistake:** Neglecting ground defense while focusing on air, as the opponent could still push with a strong ground army.

**Correction:** Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology to support the transition.

**Why:** Transitioning to air can give you an advantage if the opponent is heavily ground-based. Banshees can harass and provide mobility.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's anti-air capabilities or if they transition to air themselves.
