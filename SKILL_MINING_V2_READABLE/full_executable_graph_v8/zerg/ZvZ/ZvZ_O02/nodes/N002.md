# N002 — Early-Mid Game Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-mid game, you still have limited information about the opponent, but your own economy and production are starting to take shape. Continue to develop your economy and production while keeping your army composition flexible. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture remains unknown, with no reliable combat-unit cues. They could be building up economically or preparing an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you are developing steadily. You have not committed to a specific army composition, but you are maintaining a balanced approach.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and production while keeping your army composition flexible. Focus on scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

Maintaining a balanced development allows you to respond to a variety of threats. By not overcommitting, you can quickly adapt to the opponent's actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized strategy once you have a better understanding of the opponent's intentions. If the opponent is passive, you can focus on economy; if they are aggressive, you need to build a defensive army.
