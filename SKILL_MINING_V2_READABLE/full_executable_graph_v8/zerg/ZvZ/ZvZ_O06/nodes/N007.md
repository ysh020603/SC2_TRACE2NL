# N007 — Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. You have a moderate ground army with Roaches and Queens, and moderate production and technology. Increase your defense and continue developing your economy and production. Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's. Consider increasing your technology to get better units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and technology. They may be preparing to attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and moderate production. You have Roaches and Queens, and your technology is moderate. Your defense is heavy, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and continue developing your economy and production. Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's. Consider increasing your technology to get better units.

With the opponent having a heavy army, you need to focus on defense to survive. Roaches are durable and good against Zerglings. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to sustain a defensive war.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late-midgame with a strong defense and economy, ready to counter-attack or transition to air if needed.
