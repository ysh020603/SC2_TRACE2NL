## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have an air army with Phoenix and Oracle, while the opponent shows an air posture with Oracle cues. Maintain your air army and consider adding more Phoenixes or Oracles. Continue developing your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air posture with Zealot, Stalker, and Oracle cues. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate air army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your economy, production, and technology are all heavy, and you have moderate defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your air army and consider adding more Phoenixes or Oracles. Continue developing your economy and technology.

Both sides are investing in air, so you need to maintain air superiority. Phoenixes are good for harassing, while Oracles provide utility.
