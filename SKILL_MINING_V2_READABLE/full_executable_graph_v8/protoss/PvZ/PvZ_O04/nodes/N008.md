- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- Heavy production and tech, mixed army with Void Rays, heavy economy, moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

Leverage early air tech to gain an advantage over the ground-focused opponent.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Leverage Early Air Tech

**When this applies:** Early-mid game, own mixed posture with Void Rays; opponent ground with Zerglings and Queens; both heavy economy.

**Mistake:** Overcommitting to air forces and neglecting ground defense, leaving bases exposed to ground harassment.

**Correction:** Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech. Keep some ground units to protect bases.

**Why:** Early air tech provides an advantage over a ground-focused opponent, but ground defense is essential to secure your economy.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when opponent tech switches to anti-air or when air army is sufficient.
