## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, while the opponent shows a ground posture with SiegeTank, Reaper, Thor, and Medivac. You need to strengthen your ground forces and maintain defense. Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with SiegeTank, Reaper, Thor, and Medivac cues. Production and technology appear heavy. The opponent may be preparing a strong ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your production is heavy, technology is heavy, and economy is heavy. You have a heavy defense. Your army is ground-oriented with Stalker, Sentry, Observer, and Immortal cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground attack. Strengthening your ground forces and maintaining defense is prudent while you continue to develop.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Maintain ground defense against drops

**When this applies:** Late-midgame, around 600 seconds, with heavy economy and production, while the opponent shows a ground posture with SiegeTank, Reaper, Thor, and Medivac.

**Mistake:** Neglecting ground defense, leaving you vulnerable to drops or flanking maneuvers.

**Correction:** Increase economy, production, and technology. Strengthen ground forces. Maintain defense and expansion. Continue air development.

**Why:** The opponent's ground posture suggests a potential ground attack. Strengthening ground forces and maintaining defense is prudent while continuing development.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when you see more of the opponent's army composition or if they expand further.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
