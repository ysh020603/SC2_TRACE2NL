# N007 — Midgame Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with heavy technology investment and moderate production. Strengthen ground army and increase defense. Continue production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about opponent's composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Technology is heavy, production is moderate. Defense is heavy, economy is moderate, expansion is heavy. Army composition is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and increase defense. Continue production and technology development.

Heavy tech investment suggests a tech-based army; need to build a ground force to defend while tech completes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced posture with production and tech.
