# N012 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late midgame with a ground-oriented army and heavy production/technology, transitioning to air. Strengthen air army and increase economy. Continue production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about opponent's composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Void Rays. Production and technology are heavy. Defense and economy are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen air army and increase economy. Continue production and technology development.

Heavy tech investment suggests a tech-based army; transitioning to air can provide a strategic advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture, often with heavy tech and moderate production.
