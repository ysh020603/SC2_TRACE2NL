# N003 — Early Game Tech/Eco Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and no clear army composition yet. Defense is light or uncertain. Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a defensive core. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but keep options open for air transitions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's strategy is unknown; they could be preparing an early attack or also developing economically.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game, focusing on economy and technology. Production is moderate, and you have not yet built a significant army. Your defense is light, so you are vulnerable to early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a defensive core. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but keep options open for air transitions.

A strong economy and technology foundation is crucial for midgame power, but you need some units to defend against early pressure. Moderate production allows you to respond without over-committing.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a stable economy and a clearer idea of your army direction.
