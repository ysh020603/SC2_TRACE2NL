# N006 — Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Zealots and Sentries. Defense is heavy, and pressure is possible. Stabilize your position and continue developing. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding defensive structures if needed. Maintain your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be applying pressure, but their exact composition is not fully known. Be prepared for ground or air threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with a ground-based army including Zealots and Sentries. Your defense is heavy, and you are prepared for potential pressure. You are in the midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position and continue developing. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding defensive structures if needed. Maintain your economy and technology.

A heavy defense with a ground army can withstand early pressure, allowing you to develop economically and technologically. This sets up a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame position with a strong ground army and the ability to transition to air if necessary.
