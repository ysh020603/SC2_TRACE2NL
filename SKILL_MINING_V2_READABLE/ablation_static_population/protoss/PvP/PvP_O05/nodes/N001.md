# N001 — Early-Mid Tech/Eco Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Representative unit cues may include Stalkers. Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production steady. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but remain flexible to transition to air if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; their composition and intentions are not yet clear. Maintain awareness of potential early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology focus. Production is moderate, and your army composition is not yet clearly defined. Defense is light or uncertain, and you have not committed to a specific army style.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production steady. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but remain flexible to transition to air if needed.

A heavy economy and technology investment provides long-term advantages, but you need a solid defensive foundation to survive potential early aggression. Maintaining production ensures you can respond to threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame position with a clear army direction, either ground or air, based on scouting and pressure.
