# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Stalkers and possibly Immortals. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep production going and consider adding defensive structures if pressure increases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent may be applying pressure, but their exact composition is not fully known. Be prepared for ground or air threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with a ground-based army composition. Your technology is heavy, and you have some defensive capability. You are in the midgame and may face pressure from the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep production going and consider adding defensive structures if pressure increases.

A ground-based army with heavy support is solid in PvP, but you need to be ready to adapt if the opponent goes air. Maintaining economy and production ensures you can tech switch if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-midgame position with a strong ground army and the ability to transition to air if necessary.
