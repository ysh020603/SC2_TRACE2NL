# PvP_O05 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss versus Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology investment, a strong economy, and early expansion while keeping production moderate. The build is flexible, allowing transitions into either ground-based or air-based compositions depending on live observations.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Mid Tech/Eco Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Representative unit cues may include Stalkers.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production steady. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but remain flexible to transition to air if needed.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [COMMON] N002 — Midgame Ground Macro

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Stalkers and possibly Immortals. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep production going and consider adding defensive structures if pressure increases.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [COMMON] N003 — Early Game Tech/Eco Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and no clear army composition yet. Defense is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a defensive core. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but keep options open for air transitions.

**Read for details:** `N003`

### [COMMON] N004 — Midgame Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy air presence, including Phoenixes and Oracles. Economy and production are heavy, and technology is heavy. Defense is moderate.

**Direction:**  
Continue increasing your air army and technology. Maintain your economy and production to support the air transition. Consider adding defensive structures if needed.

**Read for details:** `N004`

### [COMMON] N005 — Late-Midgame Air Dominance
