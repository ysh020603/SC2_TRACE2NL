# N001 — Early Game Tech and Defense Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy defense and technology posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain expansion. Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a defensive posture. Focus on establishing a solid foundation without overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited at this stage; their composition and intentions are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy investment in defense and technology. Your production is moderate, and your expansion is light or uncertain. Your army composition is not yet defined, and you have limited air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a defensive posture. Focus on establishing a solid foundation without overcommitting to a specific army composition.

This opening prioritizes long-term strength through technology and economy. Early defense prevents all-ins while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition (ground or air) as scouting reveals opponent intentions.
