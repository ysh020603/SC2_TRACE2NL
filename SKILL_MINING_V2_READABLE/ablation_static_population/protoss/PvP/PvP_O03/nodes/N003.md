# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy defense, economy, and production. Army is ground-oriented with Zealots and Immortals. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Maintain defense while preparing for potential engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is partial; you may have scouted some of their units, but their full composition is not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is ground-based, featuring Zealots and Immortals. You have a heavy technology investment and a defensive posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Maintain defense while preparing for potential engagements.

A ground army with Immortals is strong against enemy ground forces and can be supported by your heavy economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mixed composition if the opponent shows heavy air, or continue to upgrade your ground forces.
