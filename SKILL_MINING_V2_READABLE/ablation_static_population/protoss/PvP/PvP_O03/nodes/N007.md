# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Army with Upgrades

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy defense, economy, and production. Army is ground-oriented with Zealots. Continue to strengthen your ground army and upgrades. Maintain defense while preparing for potential engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is partial; you may have scouted some of their units, but their full composition is not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a heavy economy and production. Your army is ground-based, featuring Zealots. You have a heavy technology investment and are increasing upgrades.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and upgrades. Maintain defense while preparing for potential engagements.

A well-upgraded ground army can be very cost-effective and powerful in the late-midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mixed composition if the opponent shows heavy air, or continue to upgrade your ground forces.
