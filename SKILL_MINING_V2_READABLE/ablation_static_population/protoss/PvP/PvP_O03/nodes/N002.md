# N002 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Consolidation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy defense and technology, heavy economy, and moderate production. Expansion is heavy. Continue to strengthen your economy and technology while maintaining defense. Begin to lean towards a ground or air composition based on scouting and your own preferences.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is still limited; their army composition and pressure potential are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your defense is strong, and you have expanded. Your army composition is still undefined, but you have moderate production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your economy and technology while maintaining defense. Begin to lean towards a ground or air composition based on scouting and your own preferences.

A heavy economy supports further tech and production. Maintaining defense deters aggression while you scale.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame army composition, either ground-based with Zealots and Immortals or air-based with Void Rays.
