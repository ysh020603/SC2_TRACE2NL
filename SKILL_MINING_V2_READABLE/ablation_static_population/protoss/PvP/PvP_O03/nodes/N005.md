# N005 — Midgame Air Army Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy defense, economy, and production. Army is air-oriented with Void Rays and Stalkers. Continue strengthening your air army and economy. Maintain defense while preparing for potential engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is partial; you may have scouted some of their units, but their full composition is not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is air-based, featuring Void Rays and Stalkers. You have a heavy technology investment and a defensive posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and economy. Maintain defense while preparing for potential engagements.

An air army with Void Rays can be very powerful, especially if the opponent lacks anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mixed composition if the opponent shows heavy anti-air, or continue to upgrade your air forces.
