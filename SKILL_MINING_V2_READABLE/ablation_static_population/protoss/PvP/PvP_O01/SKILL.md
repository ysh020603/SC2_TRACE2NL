# PvP_O01 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while keeping production moderate early, then scaling up. The army can develop along either a ground or air path depending on live observations.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase, ground-oriented posture, heavy production and technology, economy heavy, expansion light or uncertain, possible pressure.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army while increasing economy and expansion. Continue production and technology development, maintain defense, and keep an eye on possible pressure.
