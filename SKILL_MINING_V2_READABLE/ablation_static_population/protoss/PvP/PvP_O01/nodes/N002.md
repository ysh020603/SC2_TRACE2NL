# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase, ground-oriented posture, heavy production and technology, economy and expansion heavy, possible pressure. Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain expansion and production, keep economy increasing, and maintain defense. Consider adding tech or upgrades as needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy economy, production, and expansion. Technology is heavy, defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain expansion and production, keep economy increasing, and maintain defense. Consider adding tech or upgrades as needed.

With a strong economy and expansion, you can sustain a larger army and tech advantage. Maintaining pressure on the opponent while defending is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-midgame composition with a mix of ground and possibly air units.
