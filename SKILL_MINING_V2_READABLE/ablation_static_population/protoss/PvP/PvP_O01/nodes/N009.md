# N009 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase, army domain unknown, production moderate, technology heavy, economy heavy, expansion light, heavy defense. Focus on stabilizing your defense while developing economy and tech. Start building a ground army for safety, and consider scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a defensive posture with heavy tech and economy, but your army is not yet defined. Expansion is light, and pressure is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your defense while developing economy and tech. Start building a ground army for safety, and consider scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

A defensive posture is safe against early pressure, but you need to transition to a more active game plan once you have scouted.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a defined army posture and begin expanding.
