# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase, army domain unknown, production moderate, technology heavy, economy heavy, expansion light or uncertain. Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining economy. Start building a ground-oriented army as a safe default, and keep options open for air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined; you have heavy tech and economy, moderate production, and light expansion. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining economy. Start building a ground-oriented army as a safe default, and keep options open for air transition.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base. A ground army is a flexible starting point that can be supplemented with air later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a defined army posture (ground or air) by early-midgame.
