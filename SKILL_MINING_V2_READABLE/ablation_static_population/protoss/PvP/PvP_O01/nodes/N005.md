# N005 — Early-Mid Ground with Heavy Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, ground-oriented posture, heavy production and tech, economy heavy, expansion light or uncertain, possible pressure. Increase production and technology while expanding. Strengthen ground forces and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, but expansion is light. Defense is light, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology while expanding. Strengthen ground forces and maintain defense.

Heavy production allows you to build a larger army quickly, but you need a solid economy to sustain it. Expanding is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a natural expansion and increase army size.
