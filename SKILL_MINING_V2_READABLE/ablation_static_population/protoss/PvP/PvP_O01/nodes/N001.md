# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, ground-oriented posture, heavy production and technology, economy heavy, expansion light or uncertain, possible pressure. Strengthen your ground army while increasing economy and expansion. Continue production and technology development, maintain defense, and keep an eye on possible pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots as a representative unit. Your economy and production are heavy, technology is heavy, but your expansion is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while increasing economy and expansion. Continue production and technology development, maintain defense, and keep an eye on possible pressure.

With a heavy economy and tech, you can afford to expand and produce more. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid core while you transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base and increase army size, while maintaining tech progress.
