# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with ground-oriented army, heavy production and technology, light defense and expansion uncertainty. Continue developing your ground army and technology while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection; rely on live scouting to assess their composition and pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-focused army with Stalkers as a core unit, heavy production and technology investment, but your defense and expansion posture are light or uncertain. Economy is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and production.

A heavy economy and technology base supports a strong midgame transition. Ground units like Stalkers provide a solid core that can be supplemented based on scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army composition, either ground-based with Sentries and Immortals or mixed with air support, based on opponent's tech choices.
