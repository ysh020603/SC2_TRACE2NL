# N003 — Late-Mid Ground Tech with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with ground-oriented army, heavy production, technology, and expansion, light defense. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy and expansion. Keep production and technology development steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; expect possible pressure and scout for tech switches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms, and Observers, indicating a mobile and tech-heavy force. Economy and expansion are heavy, production is heavy, but defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy and expansion. Keep production and technology development steady.

Warp Prisms add mobility and harassment potential. Heavy economy supports a large army and tech upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition with high-tech units like Colossus or Archons, or add air support if needed.
