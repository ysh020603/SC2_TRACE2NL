# N006 — Midgame Air with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with air-oriented army, heavy production, technology, and expansion, light defense. Increase air presence and strengthen your air army while continuing economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; expect possible pressure and scout for ground-based threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Phoenixes, indicating a strong air commitment. Economy and expansion are heavy, production is heavy, but defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen your air army while continuing economy and technology development.

Phoenixes provide air control and harassment. Heavy economy supports a large air force and tech upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame air composition with Void Rays or a mixed army for ground support.
