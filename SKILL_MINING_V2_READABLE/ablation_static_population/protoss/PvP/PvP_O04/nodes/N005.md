# N005 — Early Game Tech with Unknown Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with unknown army composition, heavy technology, moderate production, heavy expansion. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase economy and technology. Maintain production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; early scouting is crucial.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zealots and Stalkers, but your army composition is still undefined. Economy and expansion are heavy, technology is heavy, but production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase economy and technology. Maintain production.

A solid early game with heavy economy and tech sets up a strong midgame. Zealots and Stalkers provide a flexible core.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting, either ground, air, or mixed.
