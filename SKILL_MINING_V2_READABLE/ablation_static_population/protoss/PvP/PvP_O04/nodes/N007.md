# N007 — Early Game Tech with Light Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with unknown army composition, heavy technology, moderate production, light expansion. Stabilize your economy and production before expanding further. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; early scouting is crucial.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Stalkers as a core unit, but your army composition is still undefined. Economy is heavy, technology is heavy, but production is moderate and expansion is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your economy and production before expanding further. Continue technology development.

A light expansion suggests a need to secure your economy before expanding. Stalkers provide a defensive core.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more stable early-midgame state with a defined army direction and heavier expansion.
