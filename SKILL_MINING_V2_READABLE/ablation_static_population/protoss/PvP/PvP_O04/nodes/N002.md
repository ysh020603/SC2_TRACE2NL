# N002 — Midgame Ground Tech with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with ground-oriented army, heavy production, technology, and expansion, light defense. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy and expansion. Keep production and technology development steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; use live scouting to detect tech switches or pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals, indicating a strong tech-based ground force. Economy and expansion are heavy, production is heavy, but defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy and expansion. Keep production and technology development steady.

A robust ground composition with support units provides a solid midgame army. Heavy economy supports further tech and army growth.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame army with Warp Prisms for mobility or add air support if opponent goes heavy air.
