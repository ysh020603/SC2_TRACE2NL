# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Army composition is unknown or minimal. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army direction. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline, but keep options open for air transitions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this node.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment. Production is moderate, and your army is not yet defined. Defense is light or uncertain, and you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army direction. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline, but keep options open for air transitions.

This opening emphasizes technology and economy, so early investment in these areas sets up for a strong midgame. Maintaining flexibility allows adaptation to opponent actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition (ground or air) as the game progresses, based on scouting and opponent pressure.
