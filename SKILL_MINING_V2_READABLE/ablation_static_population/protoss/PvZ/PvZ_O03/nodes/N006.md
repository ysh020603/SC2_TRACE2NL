# N006 — Early-Mid Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Air presence is moderate, with Oracles as representative units. Defense is light or uncertain, pressure possible. Increase your air presence, strengthen your air army, and continue technology development. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this node.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have transitioned to an air-oriented army with Oracles. Production and technology are heavy, and you are expanding. Defense is light, but you are aware of possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence, strengthen your air army, and continue technology development. Maintain economy and expansion.

Oracles provide harassment and detection. An air transition can be strong against certain Zerg compositions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
