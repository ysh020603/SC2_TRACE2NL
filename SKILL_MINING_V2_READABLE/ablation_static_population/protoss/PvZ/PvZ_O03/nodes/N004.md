# N004 — Early Game Tech/Eco with Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Expansion posture is light or uncertain, and army is unknown. Focus on securing your expansion and increasing production. Continue technology development and strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this node.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment, but your expansion is not yet secured. Production is moderate, and your army is undefined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on securing your expansion and increasing production. Continue technology development and strengthen ground forces as a baseline.

Securing an early expansion is crucial for a heavy economy. Technology investment will pay off later, but you need production to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state with a secured expansion and a more defined army.
