# N008 — Midgame Air Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Air presence is heavy, with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles. Defense is light or uncertain, pressure possible. Increase your air presence further, strengthen your air army, and continue technology development. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this node.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong air army with Oracles, supported by ground units. Production and technology are heavy, and you are expanding. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence further, strengthen your air army, and continue technology development. Maintain economy and expansion.

A heavy air army can be very strong against Zerg if they lack anti-air. Oracles provide harassment and detection.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame air composition, possibly adding Phoenixes or Void Rays.
