# N002 — Early-Mid Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Stalkers as representative units. Defense is light or uncertain, but pressure is possible. Strengthen your ground army further, increase production, and continue technology development. Maintain economy and expansion, and keep defense in mind.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this node.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have transitioned to a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, and your production is heavy. Economy and technology remain heavy. Defense is still light, but you are aware of possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army further, increase production, and continue technology development. Maintain economy and expansion, and keep defense in mind.

With a ground army established, reinforcing it and continuing tech will prepare for midgame engagements. Heavy production supports a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more complete midgame army composition, possibly adding Sentries or other tech units.
