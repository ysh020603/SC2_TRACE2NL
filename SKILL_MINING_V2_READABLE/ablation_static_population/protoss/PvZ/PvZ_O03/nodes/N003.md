# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army includes Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Defense is light or uncertain, pressure possible. Continue strengthening your ground army, maintain production and technology, and keep expanding. Consider adding more tech units or transitioning to air if needed.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, maintain production and technology, and keep expanding. Consider adding more tech units or transitioning to air if needed.

A diverse ground army with support units like Sentries and Warp Prisms provides tactical flexibility. Maintaining economy and tech ensures long-term strength.
