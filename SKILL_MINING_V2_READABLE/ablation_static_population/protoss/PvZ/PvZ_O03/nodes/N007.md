## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army includes Stalkers and Warp Prisms. Defense is light or uncertain, pressure possible. Continue strengthening your ground army, maintain production and technology, and use Warp Prisms for harassment or defense.
