# N001 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy technology investment, moderate production, and defensive posture. Economy is heavy but expansion is light or uncertain. Continue teching up while maintaining a defensive posture. Increase economy and defense, and keep production steady. Focus on stabilizing before expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy technology focus. Your production is moderate, and you have a strong defensive posture. Your economy is strong, but your expansion is limited or uncertain. Your army composition is not yet defined, and air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching up while maintaining a defensive posture. Increase economy and defense, and keep production steady. Focus on stabilizing before expanding further.

Heavy technology investment requires time to pay off. A defensive posture protects your tech investments. Strengthening the economy supports future production and tech. Avoiding early expansion reduces vulnerability to early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition as tech completes, while maintaining a strong economy and defense.
