# N006 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy technology, moderate production, moderate economy, and heavy expansion. Defense is heavy. Increase production and technology while maintaining defense. Continue expanding and growing your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy technology focus. Your production is moderate, and your economy is moderate but expanding. Your defense is heavy. Your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology while maintaining defense. Continue expanding and growing your economy.

Expanding early gives a long-term economic advantage. Heavy technology provides a tech lead. Maintaining defense protects your investments.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition as tech completes.
