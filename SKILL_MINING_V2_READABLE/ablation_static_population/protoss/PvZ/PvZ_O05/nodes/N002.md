# N002 — Early-Midgame Tech Consolidation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy technology, moderate production, and heavy economy. Expansion is heavy, but army composition is still undefined. Continue teching and increasing economy. Maintain defense and production. Consider starting to define your army composition based on your tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this stage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy technology investment. Your economy is strong, and you have expanded. Production is moderate, and your army is still undefined. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and increasing economy. Maintain defense and production. Consider starting to define your army composition based on your tech path.

With a strong economy and tech, you can afford to invest in a more defined army. Maintaining defense is crucial as you transition. Increasing production supports a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition, either ground or air, while continuing to grow your economy.
