# PvZ_O04 Technology/Economy/Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O04
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: Technology/Economy/Production
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy economy and technology investment while keeping production moderate early, transitioning to heavy production as the game progresses. The army composition is flexible, leaning ground but with options to transition to air.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while maintaining flexibility to adapt to the opponent's actions. Aim to reach a powerful midgame army composition with either ground or air dominance.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations and scouting information.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate early, heavy later
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning with air transition options

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180 seconds) with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light defense. Army composition is unknown or just Zealots.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing economy and technology while starting to build a defensive core. Increase production to prepare for midgame.
