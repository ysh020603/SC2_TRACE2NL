# N006 — Early Game Ground Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Ground army with Zealots. Defense is light. Continue teching and increase production. Consider expanding to support your economy. Maintain a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent. No specific info, but they may be expanding or teching.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a focus on tech and economy. Production is moderate, and you have a small ground army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and increase production. Consider expanding to support your economy. Maintain a defensive posture.

A tech and economy focus sets up a strong midgame. Moderate production is enough to defend early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition as tech completes.
