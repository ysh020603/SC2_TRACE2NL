# N004 — Early-Mid Tech Heavy Ground

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-360 seconds) with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Stalkers and Sentries. Defense is light. Continue teching and increase production. Consider expanding to support your economy. Use sentries for defensive capabilities.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent. No specific info, but they may be teching or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a tech-heavy ground army with sentries for utility. Economy is heavy, but expansion is light, indicating a focus on tech rather than bases.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and increase production. Consider expanding to support your economy. Use sentries for defensive capabilities.

Tech-heavy armies can be powerful, but need a solid economy to sustain. Expanding now can secure long-term resources.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
