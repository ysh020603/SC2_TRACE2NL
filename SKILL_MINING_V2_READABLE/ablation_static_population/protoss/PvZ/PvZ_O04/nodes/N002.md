COMMON

## Summary

Midgame (around 540 seconds) with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. Defense is moderate. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy growth. Consider adding upgrades and tech to support your composition.

- Zerg opponent. No specific info, but likely they have a large economy and are producing units. Possible tech switches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy growth. Consider adding upgrades and tech to support your composition.

A strong ground army with immortals is effective against Zerg ground. Maintaining economy ensures you can sustain a long game.
