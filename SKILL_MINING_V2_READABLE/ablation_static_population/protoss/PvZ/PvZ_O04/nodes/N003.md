# N003 — Early-Mid Ground Push Prep

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300-360 seconds) with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Zealots and Stalkers. Defense is light. Increase production and continue teching. Consider adding sentries or other support units. Maintain map awareness to detect pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent. No specific info, but they may be expanding or preparing an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are transitioning from early to midgame. Your economy and tech are strong, and you have a core ground army. Production is heavy, allowing for rapid reinforcement.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and continue teching. Consider adding sentries or other support units. Maintain map awareness to detect pressure.

A strong economy and tech advantage can be leveraged into a timing attack or a solid defense. Heavy production ensures you can respond to threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition, either ground or air, based on your tech choices.
