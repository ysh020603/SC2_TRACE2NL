# N004 — Late-Midgame - Unknown Posture

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame, own posture remains unknown with light or uncertain production and technology. No representative units observed. Continue to maintain your current path, but start leaning towards a ground-based army if you have not already. Keep developing economy and technology while staying safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's strategy is still not fully visible. They may be preparing for a timing attack or expanding. Keep scouting to understand their tech and expansion choices.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame and still have not committed to a specific strategy. Production and technology are light or uncertain, and your army composition is unclear. Economy is also light or uncertain, so you have not expanded much.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to maintain your current path, but start leaning towards a ground-based army if you have not already. Keep developing economy and technology while staying safe.

At this stage, you need to start defining your army composition to be ready for midgame fights. A ground-based army is a common choice against Zerg, but you should still adapt based on scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more concrete army composition and economy plan by the midgame. Aim to have a clear tech path and production setup.
