# N006 — Early Game - Expanding Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, own posture is unknown-oriented but with heavy technology investment and moderate economy. Expansion is heavy, production is light or uncertain. Strengthen your ground army and increase production. Continue developing economy and technology, but be ready to defend your expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's strategy is not yet visible, but your heavy expansion suggests you are aiming for a long game. Keep scouting to see if the Zerg is trying to punish your expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy investment in technology and a moderate economy. You have expanded heavily, indicating a macro-oriented approach. Production is still light or uncertain, so you have not yet ramped up unit production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase production. Continue developing economy and technology, but be ready to defend your expansions.

Heavy expansion gives you a strong economy, but you need to protect it. A ground army is a solid choice to defend against early Zerg aggression while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more offensive posture once you have a solid tech advantage and a sufficient army.
