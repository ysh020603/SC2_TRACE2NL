# N001 — Early Game - Unknown Posture

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, own posture is broadly unknown with light or uncertain production and technology. No representative units observed yet. Maintain current development path while keeping options open. Focus on economy and technology, but be ready to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg, but their current strategy is not yet visible. They could be going for early pressure, fast expansion, or tech. Maintain scouting to detect their intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with an unclear development path. Production and technology are light or uncertain, and you have not yet committed to a specific army composition. Economy is also light or uncertain, so you have room to grow in multiple directions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping options open. Focus on economy and technology, but be ready to adapt based on scouting.

Early game is about establishing a solid foundation. Without clear information, it's safer to develop economically and technologically while staying flexible. This allows you to respond to whatever the Zerg does.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture as scouting reveals the opponent's plan. Aim to have a clear direction by the early-midgame.
