# N005 — Early Game - Defensive Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, own posture is unknown-oriented but with heavy technology investment and moderate economy. Defense is heavy, production is light or uncertain. Strengthen your ground army and increase defense. Continue developing economy and technology, but be ready to produce units if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's strategy is not yet visible, but your heavy defense suggests you are anticipating early pressure. Keep scouting to confirm.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy investment in technology and a moderate economy. Your defense is heavy, suggesting you are preparing for potential aggression. Production is still light or uncertain, so you have not yet ramped up unit production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase defense. Continue developing economy and technology, but be ready to produce units if needed.

Heavy technology investment gives you access to powerful units later, but you need to survive the early game. A defensive posture helps you weather any early aggression while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more offensive posture once you have a solid tech advantage and a sufficient army.
