# N008 — Early-Midgame - Heavy Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture is unknown-oriented but with heavy economy and moderate production. Technology is heavy. Strengthen your ground army and continue developing economy and technology. Consider adding more production to support your army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's strategy is still not fully visible, but you have a possible pressure posture. Keep scouting to understand their tech and expansion choices.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and moderate production. Your technology is heavy, so you are teching up while building a solid economy. Your army composition is not yet defined, but you have the resources to support a strong army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue developing economy and technology. Consider adding more production to support your army.

A heavy economy gives you the resources to tech up and build a strong army. You need to start defining your army composition to be ready for midgame fights.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
