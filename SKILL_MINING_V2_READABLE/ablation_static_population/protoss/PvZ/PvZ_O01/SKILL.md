# PvZ_O01 Technology / Economy / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / upgrade opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology and economy development while keeping production moderate in the early game. The strategic goal is to build a strong tech and economic foundation, then adapt to the opponent's actions with live observation.

Develop a technology / economy / upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early Tech and Economy Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense and expansion.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, and consider expanding to support your heavy economy.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [COMMON] N002 — Early-Midgame Production Ramp

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army composition is still undefined but starting to show Stalker and Sentry presence.

**Direction:**  
Continue increasing production and technology. Strengthen your ground army, and consider adding more gateways or tech structures to support your composition.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [COMMON] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms, and Observers.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech like Immortals or Colossus to deal with Zerg ground units. Keep producing Observers for vision.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [COMMON] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Tech Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Zealots, Void Rays, Observers, and Immortals.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding more tech units like Colossus or High Templar. Maintain your economy and production, and keep your Observer count up for vision.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [COMMON] N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Focus with Light Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented, but expansion is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Consider expanding to secure your economy, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Keep production and tech heavy.

**Read for details:** `N005`

---

### [COMMON] N006 — Midgame Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is air-oriented with Phoenixes, supported by Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries.

**Direction:**  
Continue increasing your air army and technology. Consider adding Void Rays or Oracles for additional air power. Maintain your economy and production.

**Read for details:** `N006`

---

### [COMMON] N007 — Early Game Tech Focus with Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Expansion is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Consider expanding to support your heavy economy, while continuing tech development. Increase production to defend against possible early pressure.

**Read for details:** `N007`

### [COMMON] N008 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is air-oriented with Phoenixes, supported by Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries.
