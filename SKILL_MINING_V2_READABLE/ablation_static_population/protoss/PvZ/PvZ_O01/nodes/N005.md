# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Focus with Light Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented, but expansion is light or uncertain. Consider expanding to secure your economy, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Keep production and tech heavy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited, but possible pressure from Zerg is a concern.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and tech, but your expansion is light or uncertain. This means you may be relying on fewer bases, which could limit your economy in the long run.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider expanding to secure your economy, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Keep production and tech heavy.

A heavy economy requires multiple bases. If your expansion is light, you may fall behind in income. Secure a third base if possible.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state with a more solid expansion and a stronger ground army.
