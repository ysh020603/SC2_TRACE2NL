# N007 — Early Game Tech Focus with Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Expansion is light or uncertain. Consider expanding to support your heavy economy, while continuing tech development. Increase production to defend against possible early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited. You need to scout to determine if the Zerg is going for early pressure or a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a focus on tech and economy, but your expansion is light. This means you may be on one base or a fast expand that hasn't been secured yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider expanding to support your heavy economy, while continuing tech development. Increase production to defend against possible early pressure.

A heavy economy requires multiple bases. Expanding early can give you a long-term advantage, but you need to defend it.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state with a more solid expansion and a clearer army direction.
