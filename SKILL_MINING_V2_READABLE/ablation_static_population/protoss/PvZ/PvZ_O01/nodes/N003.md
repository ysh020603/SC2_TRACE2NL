# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms, and Observers. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech like Immortals or Colossus to deal with Zerg ground units. Keep producing Observers for vision.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- You may have some information about the Zerg's composition, but it's not fully confirmed. Possible pressure from Zerg is a concern.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, and support units like Warp Prisms and Observers. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have good map vision with Observers. Defense is still light, so you need to be careful about counterattacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech like Immortals or Colossus to deal with Zerg ground units. Keep producing Observers for vision.

A ground-based army with good vision and support can handle most Zerg compositions, but you need to be ready to adapt if the Zerg goes for air or mass mutalisks.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame army with more advanced tech units like Immortals or Colossus, or transition to air if needed.
