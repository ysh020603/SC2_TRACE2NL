# N001 — Early Tech and Economy Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense and expansion. Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, and consider expanding to support your heavy economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited at this stage. You have not confirmed their build or pressure intentions, so maintain scouting and be ready to adapt.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy focus on economy and technology. Production is moderate, and your army composition is not yet defined. Defense and expansion are light or uncertain, so you need to balance tech progress with safety.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, and consider expanding to support your heavy economy.

A heavy tech and economy investment sets up a strong midgame, but you need production to convert that into army. Early expansion supports the economy, but keep an eye on defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and heavier production as you move into the early-midgame.
