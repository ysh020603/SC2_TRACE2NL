# N009 — Midgame Ground Defense and Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Zealots, Stalkers, and Phoenixes, and defense is heavy. Continue strengthening your ground army and technology. Consider adding more tech units like Immortals or Colossus. Maintain your defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- You may have some information about the Zerg's composition, but it's not fully confirmed. Possible pressure from Zerg is a concern, but your defense is strong.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with some Phoenixes for support, and you have invested in defense. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a solid base defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and technology. Consider adding more tech units like Immortals or Colossus. Maintain your defense and economy.

A strong defense allows you to play a macro game and transition to a powerful late-game army. Heavy production and tech support this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame army with a clear composition and possibly a third base.
