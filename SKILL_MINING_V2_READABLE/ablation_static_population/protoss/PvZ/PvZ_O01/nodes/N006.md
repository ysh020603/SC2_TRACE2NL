# N006 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army is air-oriented with Phoenixes, supported by Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Continue increasing your air army and technology. Consider adding Void Rays or Oracles for additional air power. Maintain your economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- You may have some information about the Zerg's composition, but it's not fully confirmed. Possible pressure from Zerg is a concern.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have transitioned to an air-based army with Phoenixes as a core unit. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a mix of ground units for support. Defense is light, so you need to be careful.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing your air army and technology. Consider adding Void Rays or Oracles for additional air power. Maintain your economy and production.

Phoenixes are strong against mutalisks and overlords, giving you map control. However, they are fragile, so you need a solid ground support.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame air army with more advanced air units like Void Rays or Carriers.
