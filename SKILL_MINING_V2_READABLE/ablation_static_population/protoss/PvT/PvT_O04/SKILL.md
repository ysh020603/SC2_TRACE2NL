# PvT_O04 Technology / Defense / Economy

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O04
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / defense / economy
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology and defense while building a solid economy. It is flexible, allowing transitions into either ground or air compositions depending on scouting and game state.

Develop a technology / defense / economy posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: moderate
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early Game Tech and Defense Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy defense and technology investment, moderate production, and uncertain expansion. Army composition is unknown.

**Direction:**  
Continue teching and defending while scouting to determine the opponent's intent. Maintain a stable economy and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.
