# N001 — Early Game Tech and Defense Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy defense and technology investment, moderate production, and uncertain expansion. Army composition is unknown. Continue teching and defending while scouting to determine the opponent's intent. Maintain a stable economy and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited at this stage. Expect standard Terran early game possibilities such as Reaper harassment or fast expand, but nothing is confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy focus on defense and technology. Your economy is strong, but your expansion is uncertain. Production is moderate, and your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and defending while scouting to determine the opponent's intent. Maintain a stable economy and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

Heavy technology investment requires time to pay off. A defensive posture protects your tech structures and economy while you gather information. Moderate production keeps options open for either ground or air transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent and your tech is established.
