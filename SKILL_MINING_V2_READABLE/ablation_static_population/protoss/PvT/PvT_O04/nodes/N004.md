# N004 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy defense, economy, and production. Ground army composition is developing. Technology is heavy. Continue developing your ground army and increasing production. Maintain defense and economy while scouting for the opponent's tech choices.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing for a midgame push or expanding. Expect Terran bio or mech, but no confirmed information.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is ground-based and developing. Defense is heavy, and technology is heavy. Expansion is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and increasing production. Maintain defense and economy while scouting for the opponent's tech choices.

Heavy production and economy allow you to build a strong army quickly. A ground-based army is versatile and can be adapted with tech units like Colossus or High Templar later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame army with more advanced units and upgrades, possibly adding air support if needed.
