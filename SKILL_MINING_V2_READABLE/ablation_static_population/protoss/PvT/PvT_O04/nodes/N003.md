# N003 — Early-Midgame Tech and Defense Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy defense and technology, moderate production, and uncertain expansion. Army composition is unknown. Continue teching and defending while increasing production and scouting. Decide on a clear army direction based on the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be starting to apply pressure or expand themselves. Expect standard Terran midgame options like bio or mech, but nothing is confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are transitioning from early to midgame. Your defense and technology are heavy, but your production is only moderate. Expansion is uncertain, and your army is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and defending while increasing production and scouting. Decide on a clear army direction based on the opponent's composition.

This is a critical timing to solidify your tech advantage and prepare for the midgame. Maintaining defense while scouting allows you to react appropriately to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition, either ground or air, and increase production to support it.
