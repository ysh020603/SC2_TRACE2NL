## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production, aiming for a flexible midgame posture that can adapt to live observations.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment; ground-oriented army with Zealot and Stalker presence; expansion posture heavy; defense light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion rate and keep developing your economy. Ensure you have some defensive capability to handle potential pressure.

### [COMMON] N004 — Early Game Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, but production is moderate and army composition is unknown; expansion is light or uncertain; defense light or uncertain.

**Direction:**
