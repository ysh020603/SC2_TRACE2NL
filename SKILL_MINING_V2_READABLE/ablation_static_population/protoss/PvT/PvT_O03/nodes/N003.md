COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology; ground-oriented army with Zealots, Stalkers, Warp Prisms, and Immortals; expansion heavy; defense light or uncertain. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production and tech. Use Warp Prisms for harassment and mobility. Consider adding more advanced units like High Templar or Colossus depending on the opponent's composition. Maintain your expansion and keep defense in mind.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production and tech. Use Warp Prisms for harassment and mobility. Consider adding more advanced units like High Templar or Colossus depending on the opponent's composition. Maintain your expansion and keep defense in mind.

Your heavy tech and economy allow you to field a powerful, versatile army. Warp Prisms provide tactical flexibility. Immortals are strong against armored units, which Terran often fields.
