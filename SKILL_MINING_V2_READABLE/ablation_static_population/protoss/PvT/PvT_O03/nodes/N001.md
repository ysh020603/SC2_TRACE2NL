# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment; ground-oriented army with Zealot and Stalker presence; expansion posture heavy; defense light or uncertain. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion rate and keep developing your economy. Ensure you have some defensive capability to handle potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; you only know they are Terran. Their current composition and intentions are not fully scouted, so you must prepare for possible aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production base, with heavy technology investment. Your army is ground-focused, featuring Zealots and Stalkers. You are expanding aggressively, but your defensive setup is light or uncertain, and you may face pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion rate and keep developing your economy. Ensure you have some defensive capability to handle potential pressure.

Your heavy economy and tech investment require time to pay off. By continuing to build up your ground forces and tech, you can reach a strong midgame composition. Maintaining expansions secures your long-term resource advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a solid ground army, continued tech progression, and a secure economy.
