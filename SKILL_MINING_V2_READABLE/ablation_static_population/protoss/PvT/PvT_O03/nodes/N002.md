# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology; ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers; expansion heavy; defense light or uncertain; possible pressure. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production and tech. Maintain your expansion lead and keep an eye on defense. Consider adding support units like Sentries or Immortals to enhance your army's effectiveness.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production and tech. Maintain your expansion lead and keep an eye on defense. Consider adding support units like Sentries or Immortals to enhance your army's effectiveness.

Your heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent if you keep investing. Advancing tech gives you access to powerful midgame units. Maintaining expansions ensures you can sustain a prolonged game.
