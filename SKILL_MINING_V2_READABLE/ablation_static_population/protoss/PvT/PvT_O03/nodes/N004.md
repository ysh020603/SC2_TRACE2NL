# N004 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, but production is moderate and army composition is unknown; expansion is light or uncertain; defense light or uncertain. Continue developing your economy and technology, but start to build a more defined army. Increase production to ensure you can defend against early pressure. Consider scouting to gain information about the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent's early game plan is unknown. They could be going for an early expand, a tech rush, or an aggressive push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game, focusing heavily on economy and technology. Your production is moderate, and you have not yet committed to a specific army composition. You have a Zealot or two for early defense, but your expansion is not yet heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, but start to build a more defined army. Increase production to ensure you can defend against early pressure. Consider scouting to gain information about the opponent's intentions.

Your heavy tech investment will pay off in the midgame, but you need a sufficient army to survive the early game. Balancing tech with production is key to avoiding vulnerability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a solid economy, tech advantage, and a developing army that can respond to the opponent's strategy.
