# PvT_O06 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O06
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy economy and technology investment while maintaining flexible production. Early game focuses on establishing a strong economic base and teching up, with production ramping up as the game progresses. The army composition is flexible, leaning ground but with potential for air transitions.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 6 minutes into the game, with a heavy economy and production posture, and a ground-oriented army starting to form.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy and technology development. Increase production and technology, and keep expanding.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [COMMON] N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At the 3-minute mark, with a heavy economy and technology posture, but production is only moderate and army composition is unknown.

**Direction:**  
Focus on increasing production and expanding, while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a flexible army composition.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [COMMON] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 7-9 minutes, with a heavy economy, production, and technology, and a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army, increasing production and technology. Maintain expansion and economy growth.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [COMMON] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Diversification

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 10 minutes, with a heavy economy, production, and technology, and a ground army that includes Zealots, Stalkers, High Templars, and Sentries.

**Direction:**  
Continue increasing production and technology, and consider adding upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion, and be ready to adapt to the opponent's composition.

**Read for details:** `N004`
