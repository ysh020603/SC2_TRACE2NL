# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Around 6 minutes into the game, with a heavy economy and production posture, and a ground-oriented army starting to form. Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy and technology development. Increase production and technology, and keep expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available at this point.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with technology investment ongoing. Your army is ground-oriented with Stalkers as a representative unit. Defense is light or uncertain, and you have a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy and technology development. Increase production and technology, and keep expanding.

This opening aims to build a strong economic and technological foundation, allowing for a powerful midgame army. Maintaining a heavy economy and production ensures you can outscale the opponent if the game goes late.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a solid ground army and the ability to adapt to the opponent's composition.
