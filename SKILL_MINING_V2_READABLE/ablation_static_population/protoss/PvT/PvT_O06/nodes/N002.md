# N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

At the 3-minute mark, with a heavy economy and technology posture, but production is only moderate and army composition is unknown. Focus on increasing production and expanding, while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a flexible army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's strategy is available.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but production is moderate and your army composition is not yet defined. Defense is light or uncertain, and expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing production and expanding, while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a flexible army composition.

In the early game, it is crucial to establish a strong economic and technological base. Increasing production and expansion now will pay off later, while keeping options open for army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the early-midgame with a solid economy, increased production, and a clear direction for army composition.
