# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Around 7-9 minutes, with a heavy economy, production, and technology, and a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers. Continue strengthening your ground army, increasing production and technology. Maintain expansion and economy growth.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increasing production and technology. Maintain expansion and economy growth.

With a strong economy and production, you can afford to build a larger army and tech up. This positions you well for the midgame, where you can apply pressure or defend against aggression.
