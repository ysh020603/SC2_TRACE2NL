# N001 — Early-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Occurs in the early-midgame phase (around 240-360 game time) when your posture is broadly unknown-oriented with light or uncertain production and technology investment. Increase economy and production while maintaining defense and expansion. Continue technology development and upgrades. Strengthen ground army capabilities.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available. Treat the opponent as potentially aggressive or greedy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition or expansion plan. Confidence in your own state is medium.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and production while maintaining defense and expansion. Continue technology development and upgrades. Strengthen ground army capabilities.

In the early-midgame, it is crucial to establish a solid economic and technological foundation to support future army production. Maintaining flexibility allows you to adapt to the opponent's actions once they are revealed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture with moderate production and technology, or a heavier defensive posture if pressure is detected.
