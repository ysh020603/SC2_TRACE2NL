# N007 — Midgame Defensive Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Occurs in the midgame phase (around 420 game time) when your posture is unknown-oriented with moderate production and heavy technology investment, and a heavy defense posture. Increase economy and defense while continuing technology development. Maintain production and upgrades. Strengthen ground army capabilities.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or strategy is available. Treat the opponent as potentially aggressive or greedy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, production is moderate, and technology is heavy. You have invested in defense, indicating a cautious approach. Confidence in your own state is medium.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and defense while continuing technology development. Maintain production and upgrades. Strengthen ground army capabilities.

A heavy technology investment with moderate production suggests a tech-focused opening. Increasing defense helps mitigate early pressure while you develop your tech advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced posture with moderate production and technology, or continue heavy tech if the opponent is passive.
