# PvT_O09 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O09
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production. The army is initially ground-oriented, with potential to branch into air or mixed compositions later. The opening is flexible and designed to support a macro-oriented midgame.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Reached midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, heavy technology, and heavy economy. Expansion posture is heavy, defense is light or uncertain, and air presence is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy and tech development. Increase production and technology further. Keep expanding to support your heavy economy.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [COMMON] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
In early-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, heavy technology, and heavy economy. Expansion posture is light or uncertain, defense is light or uncertain, and air presence is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production. Maintain your economy and tech development. Consider securing a third expansion soon to support your heavy economy.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [COMMON] N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
In late-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, heavy technology, heavy economy, and heavy defense. Expansion is heavy, and air presence is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy and tech. Consider adding more air units or transitioning to a mixed composition to counter potential Terran air threats. Keep your defense strong.

**Read for details:** `N003`

### [COMMON] N004 — Early Game Tech/Economy Opening

**Trigger situation:**  
In early game phase with unknown army domain, moderate production, heavy technology, and heavy economy. Expansion is light or uncertain, defense is light or uncertain, and air presence is light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue to develop your economy and technology. Increase production as you expand. Keep a small defensive force to protect against early pressure.
