# N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Reached midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, heavy technology, and heavy economy. Expansion posture is heavy, defense is light or uncertain, and air presence is light or uncertain. Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy and tech development. Increase production and technology further. Keep expanding to support your heavy economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Terran. Their composition and tech are not fully scouted; they may be preparing a variety of threats including bio, mech, or air. Maintain awareness of potential drops or timing attacks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army core (Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, Immortals) and are investing heavily in technology and economy. Your production is heavy, and you have multiple expansions. Defense is light, so you may be vulnerable to harassment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining economy and tech development. Increase production and technology further. Keep expanding to support your heavy economy.

Your heavy tech and economy investment gives you a long-term advantage if you can survive the midgame. A strong ground army with Observer support can defend against most Terran ground pushes and provide map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more solid midgame army composition, possibly adding Colossus or transitioning to air if the opponent goes heavy on air. Ensure your economy is secure and your tech is on track.
