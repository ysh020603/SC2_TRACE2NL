# N004 — Early Game Tech/Economy Opening

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

In early game phase with unknown army domain, moderate production, heavy technology, and heavy economy. Expansion is light or uncertain, defense is light or uncertain, and air presence is light or uncertain. Continue to develop your economy and technology. Increase production as you expand. Keep a small defensive force to protect against early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Terran. Their early game strategy is unknown; they could be going for a fast expand, a marine rush, or a tech build. Keep scouting to detect any early aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game, focusing on technology and economy. You have a Zealot or two for defense, but your army is not yet defined. Production is moderate, and you are investing heavily in tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology. Increase production as you expand. Keep a small defensive force to protect against early pressure.

Your heavy tech and economy investment will give you a strong midgame, but you need to survive the early game. A few Zealots can hold off early rushes while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to an early-midgame state with a solid ground army and a secure economy. Start producing Stalkers and get an Observer for detection.
