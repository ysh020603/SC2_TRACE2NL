# N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

In late-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, heavy technology, heavy economy, and heavy defense. Expansion is heavy, and air presence is light or uncertain. Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy and tech. Consider adding more air units or transitioning to a mixed composition to counter potential Terran air threats. Keep your defense strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Terran. They may be transitioning to a late-game composition such as battlecruisers, ghosts, or mass marauder. Your scouting is limited, so you need to prepare for multiple possibilities.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army (Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, Observers) and a well-defended economy. Production and tech are heavy, and you have multiple expansions. Your defense is solid, but you need to be aware of potential late-game transitions by the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy and tech. Consider adding more air units or transitioning to a mixed composition to counter potential Terran air threats. Keep your defense strong.

Your heavy tech and economy allow you to transition to a powerful late-game army. A ground army with Void Ray support can handle most Terran compositions, but you need to be flexible.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition that can counter the Terran's final army. This may involve adding more air units, getting Colossus, or investing in upgrades.
