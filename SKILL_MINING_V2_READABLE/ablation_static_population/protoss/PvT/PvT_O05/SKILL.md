# PvT_O05 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology and economy while expanding aggressively. Early production is moderate, transitioning to heavy production as the game progresses. The army composition is flexible, leaning ground but with options for air or mixed compositions depending on scouting and game state.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Army composition is unknown or just starting with Zealots.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Maintain a defensive posture and strengthen your ground army as you transition into the early-midgame.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [COMMON] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army (Stalkers, Sentries), heavy economy and tech, moderate-to-heavy production, and possible pressure from the opponent.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding Observers for vision. Be ready to defend against potential pressure.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [COMMON] N003 — Midgame Ground Tech Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with a ground-oriented army including Colossus, Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prism. Heavy production and tech, with possible pressure.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding more Colossus or transitioning to air if needed. Use Warp Prism for mobility.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [COMMON] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army including Colossus, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Heavy production and tech, with moderate defense.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding upgrades or transitioning to a more advanced composition (e.g., adding Archons or Disruptors).

**Read for details:** `N004`
