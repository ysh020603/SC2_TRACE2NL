# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Army composition is unknown or just starting with Zealots. Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Maintain a defensive posture and strengthen your ground army as you transition into the early-midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection. You should rely on scouting to determine Terran's opening and adapt accordingly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology focus. Production is moderate, and your army is not yet defined, possibly just Zealots. Defense is light or uncertain, and you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Maintain a defensive posture and strengthen your ground army as you transition into the early-midgame.

This opening prioritizes a strong economy and tech lead. By increasing production and maintaining defense, you can safely reach the midgame with a solid foundation to choose a composition based on scouting.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
