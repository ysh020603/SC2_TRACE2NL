# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army (Stalkers, Sentries), heavy economy and tech, moderate-to-heavy production, and possible pressure from the opponent. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding Observers for vision. Be ready to defend against potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection. You should be aware of possible Terran pressure and scout for tech choices (e.g., bio, mech, or air).
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-based army with Stalkers and Sentries, and your economy and technology are heavy. Production is increasing, and you may be facing some pressure. Defense is light or uncertain, but you have the capacity to handle threats.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and tech lead, and consider adding Observers for vision. Be ready to defend against potential pressure.

A strong ground army with Sentries and Stalkers provides a solid core that can transition into Colossus or other tech later. Heavy economy supports this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier ground composition with Colossus and Warp Prism, or adapt to air if the opponent goes heavy air.
