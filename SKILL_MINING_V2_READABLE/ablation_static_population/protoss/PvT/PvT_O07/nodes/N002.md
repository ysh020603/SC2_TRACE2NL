## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with a ground-oriented macro posture, heavy production and technology, and moderate defense. No specific unit cues. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and production, maintain defense, and keep technology advancing.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is moderate. Pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and production, maintain defense, and keep technology advancing.

Maintaining a strong economy and tech while building a robust ground army positions you well for midgame engagements and transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
