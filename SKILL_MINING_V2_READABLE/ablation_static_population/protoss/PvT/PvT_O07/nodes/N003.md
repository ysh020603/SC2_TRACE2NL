# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with unknown army orientation, moderate production, heavy technology, and light or uncertain defense. No unit cues. Focus on developing technology and economy while establishing a defensive foundation. Begin to define your army direction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details on their composition or strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy technology investment and moderate production. Economy is heavy, expansion is moderate, and defense is light or uncertain. Army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing technology and economy while establishing a defensive foundation. Begin to define your army direction.

Early technology investment sets up strong midgame options. Maintaining economy and production ensures you can support tech choices.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a defined army composition by early-midgame.
