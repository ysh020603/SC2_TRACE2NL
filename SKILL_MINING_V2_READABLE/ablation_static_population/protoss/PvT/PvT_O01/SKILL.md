# PvT_O01 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O01

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology investment and economic expansion while keeping production moderate early on. The posture is flexible, allowing transitions into either a ground-based army with Sentries and Stalkers or an air-oriented composition with Phoenix and Oracle, depending on live observations.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
