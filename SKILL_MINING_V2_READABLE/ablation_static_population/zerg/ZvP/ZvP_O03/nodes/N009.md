# N009 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production, moderate technology, and moderate air presence. Increase air presence and technology, strengthen air army, and continue economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; expect possible pressure or tech transitions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and moderate technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, indicating a transition to air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and technology, strengthen air army, and continue economy and expansion.

Mutalisks provide harassment and map control. Increasing air presence can put pressure on the opponent and force reactions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a heavier air composition (more Mutalisks) or add ground support if needed.
