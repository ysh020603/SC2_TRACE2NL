# N008 — Midgame Moderate Production Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and technology, and possible pressure. Increase economy, expansion, production, and technology, and continue defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; expect possible pressure or tech transitions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, expansion, production, and technology, and continue defense.

With moderate production and tech, you have room to grow. Increasing all aspects will prepare you for the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a heavier production and tech state (e.g., Roaches or Mutalisks) to complement your economy.
