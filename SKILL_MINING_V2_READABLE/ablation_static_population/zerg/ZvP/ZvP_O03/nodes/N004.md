# N004 — Late-Midgame Heavy Ground Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Continue increasing economy and technology, strengthen ground army, and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; expect possible pressure or tech transitions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, indicating a strong ground force.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing economy and technology, strengthen ground army, and maintain defense.

With a heavy economy and tech, you can support a larger army and transition into advanced units. Roaches provide a durable frontline.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-game army with more Roaches or add Mutalisks for air control.
