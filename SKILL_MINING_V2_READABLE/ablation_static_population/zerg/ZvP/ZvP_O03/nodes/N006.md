# N006 — Late-Midgame Heavy Production Moderate Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production, but moderate technology. Maintain production and economy, continue technology development, and keep defense heavy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; expect possible pressure or tech transitions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production but only moderate technology. Your army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, indicating a strong ground force.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain production and economy, continue technology development, and keep defense heavy.

Heavy production allows you to field a large army, but moderate tech means you may lack key upgrades or units. Continuing tech will unlock stronger options.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a heavier tech state (e.g., Roaches or Mutalisks) to complement your production.
