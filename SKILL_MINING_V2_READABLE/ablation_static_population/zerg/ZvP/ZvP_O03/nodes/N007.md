# N007 — Early-Midgame Moderate Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, moderate technology, and possible pressure. Increase economy and technology, continue expansion and production, and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; expect possible early pressure or standard macro play.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansion lead, with Zergling and Queen production. Your army is ground-oriented and your defense is heavy. You have moderate technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology, continue expansion and production, and maintain defense.

With moderate tech, you have some flexibility to transition into stronger units. Increasing economy and tech will set up a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a heavier ground composition (Roach) or tech up to Mutalisks if the situation allows.
