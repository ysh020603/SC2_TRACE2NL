# N005 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and possible pressure. Maintain economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and continue technology development while keeping safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; expect possible early pressure or standard macro play.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansion lead, with Zergling and Queen production. Your army is ground-oriented and your defense is light or uncertain. You may face early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and continue technology development while keeping safety checks.

A heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent in the midgame. Maintaining a ground core with Zerglings and Queens provides defense and map presence while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a heavier ground composition (Roach) or tech up to Mutalisks if the situation allows.
