# ZvP_O04 Expansion / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O04
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on rapid expansion and economic growth, with moderate production and light technology investment in the early game. The posture is ground-oriented, relying on Zerglings and Queens for defense while establishing a strong economy. As the game progresses, production and technology increase, with options to transition into heavier ground forces or tech into air units like Mutalisks.

Develop a strong economy and expansion lead while maintaining a flexible ground-based army, then adapt to the opponent's actions by either reinforcing ground forces or transitioning to air tech.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. The exact actions should be adjusted based on live observations of the opponent's composition and pressure.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early Game Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion posture, light or uncertain defense and technology, moderate production.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your economic and expansion focus while strengthening your ground army. Continue technology development at a moderate pace.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [COMMON] N002 — Early-Mid Game Ground Reinforcement

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game phase with heavy economy and expansion, light or uncertain defense, moderate production, and representative units Zergling and Queen.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Zerglings and possibly Roaches if you anticipate ground pressure. Maintain your economic lead and expand further if safe.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [COMMON] N003 — Midgame Heavy Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology, heavy defense, representative units Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Roaches and possibly transitioning to a tech like Hydralisks or Lurkers if the Protoss goes for heavy ground. Maintain your economy and consider further expansions.
