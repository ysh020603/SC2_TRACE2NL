# N006 — Early-Mid Game Tech Investment

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-mid game phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense and production, heavy technology, representative units Zergling and Queen. Continue to develop your technology while maintaining your economy. Strengthen your ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and prepare to transition to your tech choice, such as Roaches or Mutalisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. You should be aware that Protoss may be teching as well, so you need to scout to see if they are going for a timing attack or a tech advantage.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-mid game with a heavy economy and are investing heavily in technology. Your defense and production are moderate, and you have Zerglings and Queens. Your technology is heavy, indicating you may be teching towards a specific unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your technology while maintaining your economy. Strengthen your ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and prepare to transition to your tech choice, such as Roaches or Mutalisks.

Investing in technology early can give you a mid-game advantage, but you need to ensure you have enough defense to survive until your tech pays off.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to your tech unit as soon as it is available, while continuing to expand and maintain a defensive army.
