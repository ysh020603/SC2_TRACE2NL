COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, defense, production, and technology, representative units Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer. Maintain your ground army and continue to upgrade. Consider adding more Roaches or transitioning to a more advanced ground unit like Lurkers or Ultralisks if the game goes late.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue to upgrade. Consider adding more Roaches or transitioning to a more advanced ground unit like Lurkers or Ultralisks if the game goes late.

A heavy ground army with Roaches and Overseers is strong against most Protoss ground compositions, but you need to be ready to counter air threats.
