# N002 — Early-Mid Game Ground Reinforcement

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-mid game phase with heavy economy and expansion, light or uncertain defense, moderate production, and representative units Zergling and Queen. Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Zerglings and possibly Roaches if you anticipate ground pressure. Maintain your economic lead and expand further if safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. You should be aware that Protoss may start to apply pressure with units like Adepts or Stalkers, or tech towards Stargate or Robo.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-mid game with a strong economy and multiple expansions. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens, with light or uncertain defense. Production is moderate, and technology is still light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Zerglings and possibly Roaches if you anticipate ground pressure. Maintain your economic lead and expand further if safe.

A larger ground army will deter early aggression and allow you to take map control. Expanding further increases your economic advantage, which is crucial for the mid-game transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground composition with Roaches and possibly an evolution chamber for upgrades, while continuing to expand.
