COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, defense, and production, moderate technology, representative units Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen. Continue to strengthen your air force, adding more Mutalisks and possibly upgrading them. Maintain your ground army for defense and continue to expand.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air force, adding more Mutalisks and possibly upgrading them. Maintain your ground army for defense and continue to expand.

Mutalisks provide harassment and map control, but they are fragile. You need to use them hit-and-run and avoid engaging into heavy anti-air.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
