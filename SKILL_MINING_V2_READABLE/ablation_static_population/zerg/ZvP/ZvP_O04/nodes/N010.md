# N010 — Early Game Fast Expand

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, light or uncertain defense, production, and technology, no representative units. Continue to expand and build up your economy. Start producing Queens for defense and Zerglings for scouting and early defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. You should scout to see if the Protoss is also expanding or planning an early attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy focus on economy and expansion. Your defense, production, and technology are all light or uncertain, and you have no significant army yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to expand and build up your economy. Start producing Queens for defense and Zerglings for scouting and early defense.

A fast expand gives you an economic lead, but you need to be careful of early pressure. Queens and Zerglings provide a basic defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid defense with additional Queens and Zerglings, and consider a Roach Warren if you scout aggression.
