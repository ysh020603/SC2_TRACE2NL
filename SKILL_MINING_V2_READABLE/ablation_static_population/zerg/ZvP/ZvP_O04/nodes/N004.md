# N004 — Early-Mid Game Aggressive Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-mid game phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, moderate production and technology, representative units Zergling and Queen. Continue to expand aggressively while increasing your production and defense. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding a Spore Crawler or two for defense against oracle or void ray harass.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to expand aggressively while increasing your production and defense. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding a Spore Crawler or two for defense against oracle or void ray harass.

Aggressive expansion puts you ahead economically, but you need to ensure you can defend your bases. Increasing production allows you to rebuild quickly if you take losses.
