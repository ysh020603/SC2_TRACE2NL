# N003 — Midgame Heavy Ground Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology, heavy defense, representative units Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer. Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Roaches and possibly transitioning to a tech like Hydralisks or Lurkers if the Protoss goes for heavy ground. Maintain your economy and consider further expansions.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, adding more Roaches and possibly transitioning to a tech like Hydralisks or Lurkers if the Protoss goes for heavy ground. Maintain your economy and consider further expansions.

A heavy ground army with Roaches and Overseers can handle most Protoss ground compositions. Overseers provide detection for DTs and allow you to see tech choices.
