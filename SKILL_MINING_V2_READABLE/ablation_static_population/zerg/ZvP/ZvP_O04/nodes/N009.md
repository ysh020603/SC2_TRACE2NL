# N009 — Late-Midgame Air Superiority

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, defense, production, and technology, representative units Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen. Continue to increase your air force and upgrades. Maintain your ground army for defense and consider adding Corruptors to deal with heavy air threats like Void Rays or Phoenixes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Protoss may have a mix of anti-air and ground units. You need to scout to see if they are transitioning to counter your air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a heavy economy and a strong air force of Mutalisks. Your defense, production, and technology are all heavy, and you have Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your air force and upgrades. Maintain your ground army for defense and consider adding Corruptors to deal with heavy air threats like Void Rays or Phoenixes.

A strong Mutalisk force can control the map and harass, but you need to be ready to transition if the Protoss builds a lot of anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced composition, possibly with Brood Lords or a ground switch, based on scouting.
