# N001 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion posture, light or uncertain defense and technology, moderate production. Maintain your economic and expansion focus while strengthening your ground army. Continue technology development at a moderate pace.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection. You should scout to determine if the Protoss is going for a fast expand, tech, or aggressive pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy focus on economy and expansion. Your army is ground-oriented with light or uncertain defense, and you have moderate production. Technology investment is light or uncertain, and you have no significant air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economic and expansion focus while strengthening your ground army. Continue technology development at a moderate pace.

A strong economy allows you to outproduce the Protoss in the mid-game. Maintaining a ground army with Zerglings and Queens provides safety against early pressure while you expand.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground army composition with additional Queens and Zerglings, and consider adding a Roach Warren if you scout early pressure.
