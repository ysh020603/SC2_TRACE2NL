# N007 — Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, production, and technology, representative units Zergling and Queen. Continue to expand and increase your production. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding a Roach Warren if you anticipate ground pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Protoss may be preparing a timing attack or expanding themselves. You need to scout to determine their intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy and are actively expanding. Your defense, production, and technology are moderate, and you have Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to expand and increase your production. Strengthen your ground army with more Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding a Roach Warren if you anticipate ground pressure.

Expanding and increasing production allows you to out-macro the Protoss, but you need to ensure you can defend your bases.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid army composition with Roaches and possibly an evolution chamber for upgrades.
