# N005 — Early Game Unknown Posture

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

You are in the early game with an unknown or uncertain posture, light production, and light technology. Your army composition and pressure are unknown. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Focus on gathering information and building a solid foundation.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions is available.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Focus on gathering information and building a solid foundation.

With limited information, it is prudent to avoid risky commitments and instead focus on a stable economy and production that can adapt to whatever the opponent reveals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once you have better information about the opponent's strategy.
