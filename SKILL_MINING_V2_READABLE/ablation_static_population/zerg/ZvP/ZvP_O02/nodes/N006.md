# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

You are in the early-midgame with a ground-based army, moderate economy, and heavy expansion. Your production is moderate and technology is light. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to expand your economy. Maintain defensive structures to protect your bases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions is available.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to expand your economy. Maintain defensive structures to protect your bases.

With a solid economy and expansion, you can afford to invest in a stronger ground army to apply pressure or defend against potential attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture or tech up to higher-tier units once you have a sufficient army.
