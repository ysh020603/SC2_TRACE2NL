# N007 — Early-Midgame Aggressive Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame, ground-oriented posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy and expansion, light technology. Continue expanding and strengthening your ground army. Increase production and technology. Consider adding defensive structures if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may be expanding or teching. Their army composition is unknown. Watch for early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Economy and expansion are heavy. Technology is light. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and strengthening your ground army. Increase production and technology. Consider adding defensive structures if needed.

Aggressive expansion gives you a long-term economic advantage. Ground units are cost-effective. Light tech allows for a fast economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame by increasing tech and production. Maintain economy and expansion.
