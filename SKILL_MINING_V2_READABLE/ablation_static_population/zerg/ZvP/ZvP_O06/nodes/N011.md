## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame, ground-oriented posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Increase production and technology. Consider adding upgrades or units to counter Protoss.

- Protoss likely has a powerful army. Your ground army is strong, but be aware of air threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a strong ground army. Economy, expansion, production, and technology are all heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Increase production and technology. Consider adding upgrades or units to counter Protoss.

A strong ground army can push and defend. Heavy economy supports a large army. Technology allows for upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
