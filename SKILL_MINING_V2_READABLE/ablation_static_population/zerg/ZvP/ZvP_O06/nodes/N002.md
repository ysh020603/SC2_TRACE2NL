# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame, ground-oriented posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy and expansion, light technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Maintain expansion and production. Consider adding tech structures like a Lair or Evolution Chamber to prepare for midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may be transitioning to midgame tech. Watch for Stalkers, Sentries, or tech structures like Twilight Council or Robo. Their army composition is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Economy and expansion remain heavy, but technology is still light. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and economy. Maintain expansion and production. Consider adding tech structures like a Lair or Evolution Chamber to prepare for midgame.

Maintaining a strong economy allows for a smooth transition. Ground units are still effective, but tech will be needed to counter Protoss units like Colossi or Void Rays.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame by adding tech and increasing production. Keep economy and expansion steady.
