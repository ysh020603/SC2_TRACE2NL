# N005 — Early-Midgame Tech Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame, ground-oriented posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy and expansion, moderate technology. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Increase technology and production. Consider adding tech units like Roaches or Hydralisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss may be teching as well. Watch for tech structures or units. Their army composition is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Economy and expansion are heavy. Technology is moderate, indicating some tech investment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy. Increase technology and production. Consider adding tech units like Roaches or Hydralisks.

Moderate tech allows for a smoother transition to midgame. Heavy economy supports tech and army. Ground units are still effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame by increasing tech and production. Maintain economy and expansion.
