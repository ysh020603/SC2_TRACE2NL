# N001 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, ground-oriented posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy and expansion, light technology. Continue developing your economy and expansion. Strengthen your ground army while maintaining production. Consider adding defensive structures or units if pressure is detected.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Protoss is likely in early game as well, but their exact build is unknown. They may be teching or expanding. Keep scouting to detect early pressure or tech choices.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Your economy and expansion are heavy, but your defense and technology are light or uncertain. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion. Strengthen your ground army while maintaining production. Consider adding defensive structures or units if pressure is detected.

A strong economy and expansion will support a larger army later. Ground units like Zerglings and Queens are cost-effective and provide map control. Early tech investment is light to prioritize economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame by maintaining economy and expansion, while starting to add more production or tech as needed.
