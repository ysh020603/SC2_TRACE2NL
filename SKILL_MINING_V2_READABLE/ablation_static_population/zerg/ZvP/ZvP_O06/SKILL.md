# ZvP_O06 Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O06
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening that prioritizes economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game focuses on Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and light technology. The posture is heavy on economy and expansion, with light or uncertain defense and air presence.

Develop a strong economy and expand aggressively while building a ground-based army. Maintain flexibility to adapt to Protoss tech choices, especially air threats, as the game progresses.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of Protoss tech and army composition.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
