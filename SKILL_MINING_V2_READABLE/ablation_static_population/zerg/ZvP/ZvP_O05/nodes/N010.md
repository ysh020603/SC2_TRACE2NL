# N010 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, defense, and tech, moderate production, and possible pressure. Increase economy and expansions, continue defense and technology, increase production and ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Protoss, but specific information is limited. Expect potential tech or army pushes.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy, defense, and technology, moderate production. Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens are representative. Pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansions, continue defense and technology, increase production and ground forces.

Heavy tech provides access to powerful units. Increasing economy and production supports a strong midgame army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized composition based on scouting.
