COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and defense, moderate production and tech, and possible pressure. Maintain ground strength, continue economy and defense, keep production and tech stable.

## When This Applies

- Opponent is Protoss, but specific information is limited. Expect potential tech or army pushes.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and defense, moderate production and technology. Zerglings and Queens are representative. Pressure is possible.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength, continue economy and defense, keep production and tech stable.

Midgame stability allows for adaptation to opponent's composition. Maintaining current posture avoids overcommitment.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
