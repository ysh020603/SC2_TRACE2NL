# N006 — Midgame Heavy Production and Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, defense, production, and tech, and possible pressure. Increase economy and technology, continue production and expansion, strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Protoss, but specific information is limited. Expect potential tech or army pushes.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy, defense, production, and technology. Zerglings, Infestors, Queens, and Overseers are representative. Pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and technology, continue production and expansion, strengthen ground forces.

Heavy production and tech allow for a powerful midgame army. Increasing economy supports continued production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame army with a strong tech advantage.
