## Summary

Early-midgame phase with own posture ground-oriented, production moderate, economy heavy, and expansion heavy. Strengthen ground forces, maintain defense, economy, expansion, production, and technology.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

Strengthen ground forces, maintain defense, economy, expansion, production, and technology.

With a heavy economy and expansion, maintaining a strong ground army is essential to protect investments and apply pressure.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
