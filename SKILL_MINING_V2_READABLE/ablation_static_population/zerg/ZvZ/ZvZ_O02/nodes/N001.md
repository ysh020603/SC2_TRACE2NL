# N001 — Early Game Unknown Posture

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with own posture broadly unknown, production and technology light or uncertain. Maintain current development path with safety checks; keep production, economy, expansion, and technology directions stable.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable opponent information is available at this stage; treat opponent as unknown and avoid assumptions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks; keep production, economy, expansion, and technology directions stable.

With limited information, committing to a specific direction is risky. Maintaining flexibility allows adaptation once more information is gathered.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress toward a more defined posture while keeping options open.
