# N005 — Early Game Ground Macro Posture

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with own posture ground-oriented, production moderate, and possible pressure. Strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, continue expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable opponent information is available; opponent remains unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase defense and economy, continue expansion and production.

With a ground-oriented posture and possible pressure, building a solid ground army and economy provides a stable foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize the ground army and economy to enable further development.
