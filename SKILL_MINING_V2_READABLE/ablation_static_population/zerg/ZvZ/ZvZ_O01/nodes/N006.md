# N006 — Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Defense is moderate, and army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Increase defense and economy to prepare for potential aggression. Continue production and technology to strengthen your army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing an attack or transitioning. Your moderate defense suggests you need to be ready to react.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a developing ground army. Defense is moderate, and you are preparing for potential pressure. Technology is heavy, giving you flexibility.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy to prepare for potential aggression. Continue production and technology to strengthen your army.

By bolstering your defense, you can survive early pressure and then use your economic advantage to outproduce the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame state with heavier defense or a more aggressive posture if the opponent is passive.
