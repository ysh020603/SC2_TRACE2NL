# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Defense is heavy, and army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Increase defense and economy further to weather any attack. Continue production and technology to maintain your advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing a large attack or transitioning to a different composition. Your heavy defense suggests you are anticipating aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a large ground army. Defense is heavy, indicating you are prepared for a potential attack. Technology is heavy, giving you options.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy further to weather any attack. Continue production and technology to maintain your advantage.

A heavy defense can deter attacks and allow you to transition to a stronger army. Your economy allows you to replenish losses quickly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if the opponent is passive, or continue defending if they are attacking.
