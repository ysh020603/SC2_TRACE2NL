# N009 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Defense is heavy, and air presence is heavy, with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens. Increase air presence and economy. Continue production and technology to support your Mutalisks and ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be struggling to deal with your air units. Your Mutalisks can harass and control the map.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a mixed army with Mutalisks and ground units. Defense is heavy, and you are in a strong position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and economy. Continue production and technology to support your Mutalisks and ground army.

A mixed army with Mutalisks and ground units is versatile and can handle various opponent compositions. Your heavy economy sustains this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if the opponent is passive, or continue defending if they are attacking.
