# N008 — Early Game Standard

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and no representative units yet. Maintain your economy and expansion while developing your ground army. Continue production and technology, but be ready to adjust if you detect aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's actions are unknown. They could be going for an early pool or expanding. Early scouting is crucial.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game, focusing on economy and expansion. Your army is not yet formed, and defense is light. Pressure is possible, so you need to stabilize.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion while developing your ground army. Continue production and technology, but be ready to adjust if you detect aggression.

A balanced approach allows you to react to the opponent's strategy while building a strong foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition, either continuing with ground units (Roaches) or transitioning to air (Mutalisks) if the situation calls for it.
