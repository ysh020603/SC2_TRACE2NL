# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens. Continue developing your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and technology, but be ready to adjust if you detect aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited. They may be preparing an aggressive push or expanding themselves. Maintain scouting to detect early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and expansion lead, but your army is still developing. Production is moderate, and technology is not yet committed. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and technology, but be ready to adjust if you detect aggression.

A strong economy allows you to outproduce the opponent in the midgame. By keeping options open, you can transition to a more defensive or aggressive posture based on the opponent's actions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition, either continuing with ground units (Roaches) or transitioning to air (Mutalisks) if the situation calls for it.
