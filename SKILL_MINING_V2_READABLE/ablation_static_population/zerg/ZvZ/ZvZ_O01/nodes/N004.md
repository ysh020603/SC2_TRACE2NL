# N004 — Early-Midgame Expansion Push

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and Zerglings and Queens. Increase production and expansion to leverage your economic lead. Continue developing your ground army and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be trying to catch up economically or preparing an attack. Your expansion lead gives you an advantage if you can defend.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in a strong economic position with multiple expansions. Your army is moderate, and you are looking to increase production and expansion further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion to leverage your economic lead. Continue developing your ground army and technology.

By expanding aggressively, you can secure a long-term advantage. However, you need to ensure you have enough army to defend your bases.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame state with heavier production and technology, possibly adding Roaches or Mutalisks.
