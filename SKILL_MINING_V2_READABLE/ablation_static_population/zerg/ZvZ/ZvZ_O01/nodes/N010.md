# N010 — Early-Midgame Defense Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and Zerglings only. Increase defense and economy simultaneously. Continue production and expansion, but prioritize safety against early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing an aggressive push. Your lack of army makes you vulnerable, so you need to react quickly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy but a small army. Defense is light, and you are vulnerable to early pressure. You need to increase defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy simultaneously. Continue production and expansion, but prioritize safety against early aggression.

In ZvZ, early game can be volatile. A stable economy and a small defensive force can deter early Zergling rushes while you build up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more developed early-midgame state with a stronger army and clearer tech direction.
