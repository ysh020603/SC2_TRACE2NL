# N007 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production. Defense is heavy, and air presence is heavy, with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Increase air presence and economy. Continue production and technology to support your Mutalisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be unprepared for air units. Your Mutalisks can harass and control the map.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have transitioned to a Mutalisk-based composition. Your economy is strong, and you have heavy production. Defense is heavy, indicating you are prepared for counterattacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and economy. Continue production and technology to support your Mutalisks.

Mutalisks are strong in ZvZ if the opponent lacks anti-air. Your heavy economy allows you to sustain a Mutalisk army and expand.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame state with a larger Mutalisk army or add ground units for support.
