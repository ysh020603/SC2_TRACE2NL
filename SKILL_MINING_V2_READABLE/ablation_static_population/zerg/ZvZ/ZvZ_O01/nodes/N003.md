# N003 — Midgame Ground Heavy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Defense is moderate, and army consists of Zerglings and Queens. Maintain your ground army and economy. Continue production and technology, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows a strong air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be transitioning to a different composition. Keep scouting to see if they are going for Roaches, Mutalisks, or a large ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy technology investment. Your army is still ground-based but growing. Defense is moderate, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy. Continue production and technology, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows a strong air presence.

A heavy ground army with good economy can overwhelm opponents who are not prepared. However, you need to be flexible to counter air threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized composition, either adding Roaches for durability or transitioning to Mutalisks if the opponent is weak to air.
