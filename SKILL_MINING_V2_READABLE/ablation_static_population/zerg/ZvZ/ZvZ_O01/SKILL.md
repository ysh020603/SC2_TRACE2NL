# ZvZ Economy/Expansion Ground Opening

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O01
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: Economy / Expansion / Ground
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game focuses on heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light or uncertain technology. The army is primarily Zerglings and Queens, with a possible transition to Roaches or Mutalisks in the midgame.

Develop a strong economy and expansion lead while preserving flexibility to adapt to the opponent's composition. Maintain a ground-based army with the option to transition to air if needed.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of the opponent's actions and your own scouting information.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
