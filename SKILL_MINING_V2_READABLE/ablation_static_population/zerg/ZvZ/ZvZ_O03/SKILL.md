- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The posture is heavy on economy and expansion, with moderate production and technology in the early game, transitioning to heavy production and technology in the midgame. The army is primarily ground-based, with Zerglings, Queens, and later Roaches and Hydralisks. The overall tempo is to develop with safety checks, ensuring defensive readiness while expanding and teching up.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while expanding aggressively, maintaining a flexible ground army to respond to threats. The goal is to reach a late-midgame position with heavy production and technology, ready to transition into a decisive army composition based on live observations.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. The exact actions should be adapted based on live observations of the opponent's actions and your own resource and supply status.
