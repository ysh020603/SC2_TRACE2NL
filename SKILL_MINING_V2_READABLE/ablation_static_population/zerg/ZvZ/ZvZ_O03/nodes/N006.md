# N006 — Early Game Heavy Defense and Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase, ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, expansion, and defense, moderate production and technology, possible pressure. Increase defense and economy to solidify your position. Continue production and technology to support a larger army. Consider expanding further if safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited, but you are prepared for early pressure. Continue scouting to identify any early aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy, expansion, and defense. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and production and technology are moderate. You are in a stabilizing posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy to solidify your position. Continue production and technology to support a larger army. Consider expanding further if safe.

With heavy defense, you can safely develop your economy and tech. Increasing production ensures you can replace losses and build a stronger army. This stabilizing approach prepares you for a midgame push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once your economy and army are strong enough.
