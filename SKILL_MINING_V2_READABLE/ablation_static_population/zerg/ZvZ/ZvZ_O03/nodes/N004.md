## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy to solidify your position. Continue production and technology to support a larger army. Consider expanding further if safe.

With heavy defense, you can safely develop your economy and tech. Increasing production ensures you can replace losses and build a stronger army. This stabilizing approach prepares you for a midgame push.
