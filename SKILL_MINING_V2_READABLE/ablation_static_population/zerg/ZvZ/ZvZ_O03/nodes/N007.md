# N007 — Early-Midgame Heavy Tech and Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy economy, expansion, and technology, moderate production, heavy defense, possible pressure. Increase production and economy to support your technology investments. Continue expanding and teching up, while maintaining a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited, but you are well-defended. Continue scouting to identify the opponent's tech path.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy, expansion, and technology. Your army includes Zerglings and Queens, and production is moderate. Defense is heavy, indicating a focus on safety while teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and economy to support your technology investments. Continue expanding and teching up, while maintaining a defensive posture.

Heavy technology gives you an advantage in unit quality. Increasing production ensures you can field those units. The heavy defense protects your tech investments while you build up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame posture with heavier production and a more diverse army.
