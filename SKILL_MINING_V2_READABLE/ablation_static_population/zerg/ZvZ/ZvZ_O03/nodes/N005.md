# N005 — Early Game Queen-Centric Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase, ground-oriented army with Queens, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and technology, light defense, possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and expansion. Maintain production and technology at moderate levels, and consider adding Zerglings for defense if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited. Early pressure is possible, so maintain scouting to detect any early aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion. Your army consists mainly of Queens, indicating a focus on economy and defense. Production and technology are moderate, and defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion. Maintain production and technology at moderate levels, and consider adding Zerglings for defense if needed.

Queens provide defense and creep spread, supporting a heavy economy. This opening aims to secure a strong economic lead before transitioning to a more military-focused posture.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to an early-midgame posture with a more balanced army, including Zerglings.
