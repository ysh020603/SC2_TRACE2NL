# N002 — Early Game Ground Macro with Moderate Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate economy and light or uncertain expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and light or uncertain defense. Representative units include Zerglings and Queens. Continue developing your economy and ground forces, but prioritize safety. Strengthen your ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding a defensive structure or two if you feel threatened.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent situation is unknown; maintain scouting to detect potential aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy and limited expansion. Your production is moderate, and you have Zerglings and Queens. Your defense is light, so you need to be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and ground forces, but prioritize safety. Strengthen your ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding a defensive structure or two if you feel threatened.

A moderate economy means you need to balance between expanding and building an army. Zerglings and Queens are cost-effective and provide a good foundation for either a Roach transition or a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier economy and expansion posture, or start teching towards Roaches or other tech.
