# N008 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and light or uncertain defense. Representative units include Zerglings and Queens. Continue developing your economy and ground forces while maintaining a safety check for possible pressure. Strengthen your ground army composition with Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding defensive structures if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent situation is unknown; maintain scouting to detect potential aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding, but your army is still light and ground-focused. Production is moderate, and you have not invested heavily in technology. Your defense is light, so you are vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and ground forces while maintaining a safety check for possible pressure. Strengthen your ground army composition with Zerglings and Queens, and consider adding defensive structures if needed.

A heavy economy allows for faster tech and production later, but you need to avoid being caught off guard. Maintaining a moderate production of Zerglings and Queens provides a solid foundation for either transitioning to Roaches or teching up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground army (e.g., Roaches) or begin teching towards air if the situation allows.
