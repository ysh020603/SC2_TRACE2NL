# N009 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Production and Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and defense, heavy technology, and light or uncertain air presence. Representative units include Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. Stabilize your position and continue developing. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding upgrades. Increase your economy and expansion to support a larger army. Maintain a strong defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent situation is unknown; maintain scouting to detect potential tech switches or aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are producing heavily, with a ground-based army of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your technology is heavy, and your defense is heavy, indicating you have invested in defensive structures.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position and continue developing. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding upgrades. Increase your economy and expansion to support a larger army. Maintain a strong defense.

A heavy defense suggests you are preparing for a longer game. With heavy technology, you can access advanced units and upgrades. Focus on building a strong, well-rounded army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition with Hive tech or a more balanced army.
