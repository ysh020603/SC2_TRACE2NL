# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production, moderate technology, and moderate defense. Representative units include Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding upgrades. Increase your economy and expansion to support a larger army. Maintain a solid defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent situation is unknown; maintain scouting to detect potential tech switches or aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are producing heavily, with a ground-based army of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your technology is moderate, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding upgrades. Increase your economy and expansion to support a larger army. Maintain a solid defense.

A heavy ground army with Roaches is strong in ZvZ, and with a heavy economy you can outproduce the opponent. Upgrades will make your army more efficient.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced tech (e.g., Hive) or incorporate air units if the situation calls for it.
