# N006 — Midgame Air Transition with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and defense, moderate technology, and heavy air presence. Representative units include Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and production, and consider adding upgrades for your Mutalisks. Maintain a strong defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent situation is unknown; maintain scouting to detect potential counters to your air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are producing heavily, with a mix of ground and air units. Your air presence is heavy, and you have invested in moderate technology. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and production, and consider adding upgrades for your Mutalisks. Maintain a strong defense.

Mutalisks provide mobility and harassment potential, and with a heavy economy you can sustain a large air force. This can put pressure on the opponent and force them to react.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army or continue with a heavy air composition, depending on the opponent's response.
