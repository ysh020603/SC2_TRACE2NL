# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy and Roaches

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and moderate defense. Representative units include Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Continue strengthening your ground army with Roaches and Zerglings, and consider adding upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows signs of air or heavy tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent situation is unknown; maintain scouting to detect potential aggression or tech switches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding, with a moderate production of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your defense is moderate, and you have not invested heavily in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army with Roaches and Zerglings, and consider adding upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be ready to adapt if the opponent shows signs of air or heavy tech.

Roaches provide a durable frontline and are strong in ZvZ ground fights. With a heavy economy, you can afford to produce a large army and also tech up if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and possibly tech up to Lair for upgrades or Mutalisks.
