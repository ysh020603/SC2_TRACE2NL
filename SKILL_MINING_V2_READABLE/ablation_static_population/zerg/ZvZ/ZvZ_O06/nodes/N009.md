# N009 — Late-Midgame Roach Heavy Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology, heavy defense, and a ground army with roaches and queens. Continue to strengthen your ground army and tech towards late-game units. Increase defense if needed, but also consider taking map control.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing a large attack or transitioning to a late-game composition. Maintain scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, heavy production, and a ground army with roaches and queens. Technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and tech towards late-game units. Increase defense if needed, but also consider taking map control.

Heavy tech and production allow you to field a powerful army. Roaches are a solid core, but you may need to add other units for composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game army with ultralisks or brood lords, or a mix of units to counter the opponent.
