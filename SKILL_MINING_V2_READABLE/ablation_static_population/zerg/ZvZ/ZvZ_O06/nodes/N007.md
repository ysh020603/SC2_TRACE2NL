# N007 — Midgame Roach Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, moderate production, and a ground army with roaches and queens. Strengthen your ground army with roaches and maintain a defensive posture. Continue to grow your economy and tech towards upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing a roach push or transitioning to mutalisks. Scout to determine.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, moderate production, and a ground army with roaches and queens. Technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army with roaches and maintain a defensive posture. Continue to grow your economy and tech towards upgrades.

Roaches are a durable and cost-effective unit for defending and pushing. Heavy defense allows you to safely expand and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame with a powerful roach army or a tech switch to mutalisks if needed.
