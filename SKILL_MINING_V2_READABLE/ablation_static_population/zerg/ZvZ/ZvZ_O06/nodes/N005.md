# N005 — Early-Midgame Defense and Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, moderate production, and possible pressure. Stabilize your defense while continuing to grow your economy. Increase production and technology gradually, but prioritize safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be preparing an aggressive push or expanding. Maintain scouting to detect threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, moderate production, and a ground army with zerglings and queens. Technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense while continuing to grow your economy. Increase production and technology gradually, but prioritize safety.

Heavy defense ensures you can hold off early aggression while your economy grows. This is a safe approach that allows for flexible transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame posture with a stronger army and tech, ready to take map control.
