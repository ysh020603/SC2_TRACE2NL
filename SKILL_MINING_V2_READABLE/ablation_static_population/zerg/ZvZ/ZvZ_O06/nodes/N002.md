# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Hydralisk Option

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and technology, ground army including hydralisks, and possible pressure. Continue strengthening your ground army, possibly adding more hydralisks for anti-air and ranged damage. Maintain economy and expansion, and consider increasing production if you plan to be more aggressive.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be transitioning to a different composition or preparing an attack. Keep scouting to identify their tech path.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, and a ground army with zerglings, hydralisks, and queens. Production and tech are moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, possibly adding more hydralisks for anti-air and ranged damage. Maintain economy and expansion, and consider increasing production if you plan to be more aggressive.

Hydralisks provide a versatile ground unit that can defend against both ground and air threats. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to support a larger army and tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame posture with a stronger army, either by massing roaches or transitioning to mutalisks if the situation favors air.
