# N010 — Late-Midgame Mutalisk Harass

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production, heavy defense, and a ground army with mutalisks and overseers. Continue to increase your air presence and use mutalisks for harassment. Maintain your economy and production, and consider adding upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be transitioning to a ground army with anti-air. Use mutalisks to harass and force reactions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, heavy production, and a ground army with zerglings, mutalisks, queens, and overseers. Technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your air presence and use mutalisks for harassment. Maintain your economy and production, and consider adding upgrades.

Mutalisks can be very effective in the late-midgame if the opponent is not prepared. They can also transition into a larger air army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game with a strong air army or a mixed composition.
