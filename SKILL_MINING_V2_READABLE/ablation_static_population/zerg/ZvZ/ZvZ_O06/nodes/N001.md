# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain air presence, and possible pressure from opponent. Continue developing your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and technology at current levels, but be ready to adjust if pressure increases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; assume they may be preparing an aggressive response or expanding themselves. Maintain scouting to detect early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with zerglings and queens as representative units. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and technology at current levels, but be ready to adjust if pressure increases.

A heavy economy provides long-term advantage, but you need a sufficient defensive force to deter or hold off early aggression. Queens and zerglings are cost-effective for defense and map presence.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a solid economy and a defensive ground army, ready to tech into roaches or mutalisks based on scouting.
