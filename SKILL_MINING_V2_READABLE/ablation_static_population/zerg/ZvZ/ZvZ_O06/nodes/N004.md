# N004 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light or uncertain technology, and possible pressure. Focus on increasing your economy and maintaining a defensive posture. Continue producing queens and zerglings for defense and creep spread.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is likely also in the early game, possibly expanding or preparing an early pool. Scout to determine their build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion posture. Production is moderate, technology is light or uncertain, and your army consists of zerglings and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your economy and maintaining a defensive posture. Continue producing queens and zerglings for defense and creep spread.

A strong economy early sets the foundation for a powerful midgame. Queens are essential for defense and injects.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a solid economy and a defensive army, ready to expand further or tech up.
