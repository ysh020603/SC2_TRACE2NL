# N006 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, light or uncertain defense, moderate production, and possible pressure. Increase your economy and expansion further, and ramp up production. Maintain a light defensive force but focus on macro.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be aggressive or expanding. Use overlords and speedlings to scout.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, light defense, moderate production, and a ground army with queens. Technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion further, and ramp up production. Maintain a light defensive force but focus on macro.

This is a greedy approach that aims to out-economy the opponent. It is viable if you can defend with minimal units and rely on early warning.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame with a strong economy and production, ready to mass an army.
