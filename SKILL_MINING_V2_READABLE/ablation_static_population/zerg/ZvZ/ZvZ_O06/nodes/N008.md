# N008 — Midgame Mutalisk Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, heavy production, and a ground army with mutalisks and overseers. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to grow your economy and maintain production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent may be unprepared for air. Use mutalisks to harass and gain map control.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, heavy production, and a ground army with zerglings, mutalisks, queens, and overseers. Technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue to grow your economy and maintain production.

Mutalisks provide mobility and harassment potential, forcing the opponent to react. This can give you a tempo advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame with a strong mutalisk force, or switch back to ground if the opponent adapts.
