# ZvZ_O06 Expansion / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O06
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening focused on heavy economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and technology. The early game emphasizes queens and zerglings, transitioning into a flexible midgame that can support either roach-based ground armies or a mutalisk switch depending on live observations.

Develop a strong economic and expansion lead while preserving flexibility to adapt to opponent actions. Prioritize macro consistency, creep spread, and queen production to secure map control and tech options.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent posture.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
