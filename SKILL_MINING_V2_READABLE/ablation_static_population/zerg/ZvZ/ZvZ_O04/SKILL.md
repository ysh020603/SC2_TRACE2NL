# ZvZ_O04 Economy / Defense / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O04
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: economy / defense / ground opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that emphasizes a heavy economy and defense posture with a ground-oriented army. The opening is flexible, allowing for transitions into heavier ground compositions or air support depending on live observations.

Develop a robust economy and defensive structure while maintaining a ground army, preserving flexibility for adaptation based on scouting and pressure.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live information about opponent actions and your own resource/army status.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
