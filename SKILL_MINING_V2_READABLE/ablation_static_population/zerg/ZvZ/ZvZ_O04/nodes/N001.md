# N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and defense; ground army with Zergling and Queen presence; technology moderate. Maintain current heavy economy and production, continue strengthening ground army, and keep defense solid. Avoid overcommitting to tech or air without clear information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; maintain awareness of potential pressure or tech switches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong economy and production, heavy defensive structures, and a ground-oriented army. Technology is moderate, and you have Zerglings and Queens as representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current heavy economy and production, continue strengthening ground army, and keep defense solid. Avoid overcommitting to tech or air without clear information.

A heavy economy and defense posture provides a stable base to outproduce opponents. Ground army with Zergling/Queen is cost-effective and supports expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and army, with safety checks for potential threats.
