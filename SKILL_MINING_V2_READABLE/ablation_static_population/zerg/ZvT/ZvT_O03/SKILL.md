# ZvT_O03 Economy / Technology / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O03
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. It aims to build a strong macro foundation in the early and midgame, with flexibility to adapt to the opponent's actions.

Develop a robust economy and technology base while expanding, keeping a ground army core, and preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on live scouting and game state.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase, heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology; heavy defense posture; ground army with Zergling, Roach, Queen; air presence light or uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansions, maintain defense, and continue technology upgrades.
