# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Light Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and technology, light defense, ground army with Zergling and Queen. Increase economy and production, strengthen ground forces, and maintain light defense while scouting for opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, but their strategy is not fully scouted.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding, with moderate production and tech. Your army is small but ground-based, with Zerglings and Queens. Defenses are light, and air presence is uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and production, strengthen ground forces, and maintain light defense while scouting for opponent's intentions.

Early-midgame is a critical window to solidify your economy and tech lead. Maintaining light defense allows for faster macro, but you need to be ready to react to pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid defense and stronger army as you approach the midgame.
