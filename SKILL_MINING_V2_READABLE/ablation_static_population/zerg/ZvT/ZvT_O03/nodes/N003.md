# N003 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Expansion Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and tech, light defense, ground army with Zergling and Queen. Continue expanding and increasing economy, while strengthening ground forces and maintaining light defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, unknown strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are focusing on economy and expansion, with moderate production and tech. Your army is small, ground-based, and defenses are light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy, while strengthening ground forces and maintaining light defense.

Expanding early can give a long-term economic advantage. Maintaining a light defense allows for faster expansion, but you must be cautious of early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced economy and defense as you approach the midgame.
