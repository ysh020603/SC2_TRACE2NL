# N009 — Early Game Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and tech, heavy defense, ground army with Zergling and Queen. Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground forces, and maintain heavy defense while scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, early game, unknown strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a strong economy and expansion, but your army is small, consisting of Zerglings and Queens. Defenses are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, strengthen ground forces, and maintain heavy defense while scouting.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base. Heavy defense provides safety while you expand and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust army composition as you enter the midgame.
