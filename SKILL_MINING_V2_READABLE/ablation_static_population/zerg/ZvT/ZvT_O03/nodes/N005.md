# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase, heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology; heavy defense; ground army with Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen; air presence light or uncertain. Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and defense, and consider increasing air presence if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, but their composition is not fully known.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and tech, with heavy production and defense. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, indicating some air presence, but it's still light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and defense, and consider increasing air presence if needed.

In the late-midgame, you need to solidify your army and prepare for the late game. Heavy defense protects your economy while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air capabilities.
