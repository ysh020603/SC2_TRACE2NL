# N008 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase, heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology; heavy defense; ground army with Zergling, Roach, Queen; air presence light or uncertain. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansion, and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, unknown strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and tech, with heavy production and defense. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansion, and maintain defense.

In the late-midgame, you need to solidify your ground army and prepare for the late game. Heavy defense protects your economy while you scale.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition, possibly with more tech units.
