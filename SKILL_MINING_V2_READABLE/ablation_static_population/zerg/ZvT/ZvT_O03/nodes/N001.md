## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase, heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology; heavy defense posture; ground army with Zergling, Roach, Queen; air presence light or uncertain. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansions, maintain defense, and continue technology upgrades.

- Opponent is Terran, but specific composition and strategy are unknown due to limited scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and multiple expansions, with heavy production and technology. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Defenses are heavy, but air presence is uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy and expansions, maintain defense, and continue technology upgrades.

With a solid economy and tech, reinforcing your ground army and expanding further can secure a midgame advantage. Heavy defense protects against potential aggression while you scale.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
