# N006 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase, heavy economy, expansion, and production; moderate technology; heavy defense; ground army with Zergling, Roach, Queen. Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and defense, and consider increasing technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran opponent, unknown strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and heavy production, but technology is moderate. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Defenses are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and defense, and consider increasing technology.

With heavy production, you can field a large ground army. Increasing technology will give you access to better units and upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more tech-heavy army composition.
