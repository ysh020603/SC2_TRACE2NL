# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion posture, light or uncertain defense and technology, moderate production, and possible pressure. Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and expansion. Continue technology development with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this node. Maintain awareness of potential early pressure from Terran.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion focus. Your army is ground-oriented with light or uncertain air presence. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and expansion. Continue technology development with safety checks.

Establish a solid economic foundation and a modest ground army to deter early aggression while teching up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early midgame with a stronger ground army and more tech.
