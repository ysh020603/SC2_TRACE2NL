# N008 — Midgame Expansion and Production Push

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, production, and technology. Army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Increase expansion and production while continuing to strengthen ground army. Maintain tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Terran may be expanding or preparing an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Production and technology are moderate. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and production while continuing to strengthen ground army. Maintain tech development.

Take advantage of your economic lead to outproduce the opponent and secure more bases.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
