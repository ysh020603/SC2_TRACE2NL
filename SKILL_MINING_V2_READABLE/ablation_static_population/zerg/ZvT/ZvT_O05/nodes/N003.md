# N003 — Early Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Increase technology further while continuing to expand and produce. Maintain ground army strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Expect possible pressure or tech-based play from Terran.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, but technology is heavy. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology further while continuing to expand and produce. Maintain ground army strength.

Invest in tech to unlock better units and upgrades, giving you an edge in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame army with better tech and production.
