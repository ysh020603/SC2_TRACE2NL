# N002 — Early Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, light or uncertain defense and tech, moderate production, and possible pressure. Continue strengthening ground army and increasing economy. Maintain expansion and production. Keep tech development going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Be alert for Terran tech switches or timing attacks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, tech is light or uncertain, and defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army and increasing economy. Maintain expansion and production. Keep tech development going.

Build a larger ground force to secure map control and prepare for midgame transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid midgame position with heavier production and tech.
