# N011 — Early Midgame Expansion Push

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, light or uncertain defense and tech, moderate production, and possible pressure. Increase expansion and production while continuing to strengthen ground army. Maintain tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Be alert for Terran tech switches or timing attacks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a heavy economy and expansion. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, tech is light or uncertain, and defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and production while continuing to strengthen ground army. Maintain tech development.

Take advantage of your economic lead to outproduce the opponent and secure more bases.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and tech state.
