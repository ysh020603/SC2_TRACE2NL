# N001 — Early Game Flexible Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with light or uncertain production, technology, and economy. No clear army composition or pressure posture. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Keep production, economy, and technology balanced while preserving flexibility.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Treat as unknown and avoid assumptions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Keep production, economy, and technology balanced while preserving flexibility.

Early game information is limited; committing to a specific direction too early can be punished. A flexible posture allows adaptation to whatever the opponent reveals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to early-midgame while maintaining a stable economy and production base.
