# N006 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and possible pressure posture. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansion, increase production, continue technology development. Maintain safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Treat as unknown and avoid assumptions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansion, increase production, continue technology development. Maintain safety checks.

A heavy economy and expansion support a larger army and faster tech. Increasing production allows you to spend resources efficiently.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to late-midgame with a strong ground army and a solid economy.
