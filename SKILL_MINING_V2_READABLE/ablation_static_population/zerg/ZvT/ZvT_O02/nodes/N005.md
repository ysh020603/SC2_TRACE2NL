# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, moderate production, heavy expansion, and possible pressure posture. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansion, continue technology development. Maintain safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Treat as unknown and avoid assumptions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansion, continue technology development. Maintain safety checks.

A ground-oriented macro posture with heavy expansion aims to outproduce the opponent. Increasing economy and expansion supports a larger army and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress to midgame with a strong ground army and a solid economy.
