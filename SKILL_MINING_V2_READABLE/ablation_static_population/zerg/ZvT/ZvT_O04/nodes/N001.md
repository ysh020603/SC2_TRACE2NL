# N001 — Early Game Economy and Expansion Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion posture, moderate production, light technology, and light defense. Representative units include Queens. Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining production. Keep technology progression light but steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection. Maintain scouting to detect early pressure or tech choices.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion posture. Production is moderate, technology is light, and defense is light. You have Queens as representative units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and expansions while maintaining production. Keep technology progression light but steady.

A heavy economy and expansion posture sets up a strong mid-game. Maintaining production ensures you can defend against early pressure while you grow.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to early-midgame with a stronger ground army and continued economy growth.
