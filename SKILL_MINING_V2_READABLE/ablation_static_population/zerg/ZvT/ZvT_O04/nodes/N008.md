# N008 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Defense is moderate. Air presence is moderate. Representative units include Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers. Increase air presence and strengthen air army. Continue economy and technology growth.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Be prepared for potential air counters.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in midgame with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Defense is moderate. You have a mix of ground and air units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air army. Continue economy and technology growth.

Adding air units gives you map control and harassment options. Heavy economy supports this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced air-ground army with heavy tech.
