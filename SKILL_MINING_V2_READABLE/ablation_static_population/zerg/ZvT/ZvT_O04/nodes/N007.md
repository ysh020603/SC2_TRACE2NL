## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light technology, and light defense. Representative units include Zerglings and Queens. Increase production to support a larger army while continuing economy growth.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increasing production allows you to build a larger army to defend or pressure. Economy is strong enough to support it.
