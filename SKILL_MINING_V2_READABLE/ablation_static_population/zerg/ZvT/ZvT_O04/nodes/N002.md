# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light technology, and light defense. Representative units include Zerglings and Queens. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase economy and expansions. Maintain production and continue technology progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Be aware of potential pressure or tech switches.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early-midgame with a heavy economy and expansion posture. Production is moderate, technology is light, and defense is light. You have Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase economy and expansions. Maintain production and continue technology progression.

As the game progresses, you need a stronger army to defend and potentially pressure. Economy still grows to support future tech and production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground army with moderate technology investment.
