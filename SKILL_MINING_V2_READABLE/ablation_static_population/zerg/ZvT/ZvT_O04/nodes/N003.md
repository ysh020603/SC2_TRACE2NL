# N003 — Early-Midgame Technology Investment

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, moderate technology, and light defense. Representative units include Zerglings and Queens. Increase technology investment while continuing to strengthen ground army. Maintain economy and expansion growth.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Continue scouting to anticipate tech choices.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early-midgame with heavy economy and expansion. Production is moderate, technology is moderate, and defense is light. You have Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology investment while continuing to strengthen ground army. Maintain economy and expansion growth.

Moderate technology allows for better upgrades and unit options. This sets up a stronger mid-game army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with heavy production and technology.
