# N001 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with light or uncertain air presence, ground-oriented army, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Economy and expansion posture are heavy. Maintain your ground army and economy, continue expanding, and keep production steady. Consider adding defensive structures if you suspect early pressure.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy, continue expanding, and keep production steady. Consider adding defensive structures if you suspect early pressure.

A heavy economy and expansion posture in the early game sets up a strong midgame. Maintaining a ground army with Zerglings and Queens provides map presence and defense while you tech up.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
