# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with moderate air presence, ground-oriented army, heavy production, and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy. Increase your air army, continue strengthening your ground forces, and maintain your economy. Consider adding upgrades for your air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Terran may be transitioning to air or have a strong ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground army that includes Hydralisks, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers. Your air presence is moderate, and you have heavy production and technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, and defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air army, continue strengthening your ground forces, and maintain your economy. Consider adding upgrades for your air units.

Adding air units like Mutalisks gives you mobility and harassment options, while your ground army provides a solid defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late-midgame with a mixed army of ground and air units.
