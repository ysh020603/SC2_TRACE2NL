# N006 — Early-Midgame Production Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with light or uncertain air presence, ground-oriented army, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Economy and expansion are heavy. Increase your production to support a larger army, continue expanding, and maintain your economy. Consider adding a few defensive structures if you feel threatened.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Terran may be preparing a timing attack or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground army, likely Zerglings and Queens. Your economy and expansion are heavy, but technology is light or uncertain. Production is moderate, and defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production to support a larger army, continue expanding, and maintain your economy. Consider adding a few defensive structures if you feel threatened.

Focusing on production in the early-midgame allows you to field a larger army quickly, which can deter aggression and secure your expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more technology-focused build once your production is solid.
