# N007 — Midgame Heavy Production Ground

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with light or uncertain air presence, ground-oriented army, heavy production, and moderate technology. Economy and expansion are heavy. Maintain your heavy production, continue strengthening your ground army, and consider increasing your technology to access better units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available. Terran may have a strong midgame army composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground army, likely Zerglings and Queens, and you have heavy production but only moderate technology. Your economy and expansion are heavy, and defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your heavy production, continue strengthening your ground army, and consider increasing your technology to access better units.

Heavy production allows you to overwhelm the Terran with numbers, but you need technology to counter their advanced units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more technology-heavy build to complement your production.
