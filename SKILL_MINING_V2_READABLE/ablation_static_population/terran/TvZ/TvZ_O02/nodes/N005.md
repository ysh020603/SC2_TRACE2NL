# N005 — Late Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late midgame phase with a ground-oriented army, heavy production, technology, economy, and expansion. Possible pressure posture. Strengthen ground army and increase defenses. Continue production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Maintain scouting and be prepared for multiple possibilities.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and technology investment. Representative units include Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Marauder.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and increase defenses. Continue production and technology development.

With a solid ground army, it's important to reinforce it and prepare for potential engagements. Heavy production and technology support a strong midgame push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture or prepare for late game by expanding and upgrading.
