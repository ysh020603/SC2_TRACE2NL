# N008 — Early Game Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate economy, heavy technology, and light or uncertain production and expansion. No clear army composition or pressure posture. Increase production and continue technology development. Strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Maintain scouting and be prepared for multiple possibilities.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and continue technology development. Strengthen ground army.

With heavy technology, focus on getting production up to support a ground army. Maintain flexibility in composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition as information becomes available.
