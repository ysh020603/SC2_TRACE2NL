# N012 — Early Game Ground Development

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with a ground-oriented army, moderate production, heavy technology, and light or uncertain economy and expansion. Possible pressure posture. Strengthen ground army and increase economy. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Maintain scouting and be prepared for multiple possibilities.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Representative unit cues include Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army and increase economy. Continue technology development.

With a ground-oriented army, focus on reinforcing it and expanding your economy to support a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture or prepare for late game by expanding and upgrading.
