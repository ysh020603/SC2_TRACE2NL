# N009 — Early Game Expansion and Technology Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate economy, heavy technology, and heavy expansion. No clear army composition or pressure posture. Increase expansion and continue technology development. Maintain production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- No reliable information about the opponent's composition or intentions. Maintain scouting and be prepared for multiple possibilities.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and continue technology development. Maintain production.

With heavy expansion and technology, focus on economic growth to support a strong midgame. Maintain flexibility in army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition as information becomes available.
