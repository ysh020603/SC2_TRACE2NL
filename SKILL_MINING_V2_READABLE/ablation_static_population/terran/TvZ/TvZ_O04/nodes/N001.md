# N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with moderate air presence, heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Medivacs. Defense light or uncertain, pressure possible. Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue production and technology, expand economy and bases. Maintain defense with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is Zerg. Their composition and tech are not fully scouted. They may be preparing a ground swarm or tech to air. Expect possible pressure or harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong economy and production. Your army is ground-focused with some air units. Your defense is light, so you may be vulnerable to harassment. You have the resources to expand your air capabilities and strengthen your ground forces.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen ground army, continue production and technology, expand economy and bases. Maintain defense with safety checks.

With a heavy economy and production, you can afford to diversify into air while keeping your ground army strong. This gives you flexibility against Zerg tech switches. Maintaining defense is important because your defense is light.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with air support and a stronger defensive posture.
