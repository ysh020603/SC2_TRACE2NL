# N005 — Early-Midgame Marine-Reaper Expand

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with light or uncertain air presence, heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Marines and Reapers. Defense light or uncertain, pressure possible. Maintain air presence, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, and continue economy and production. Keep technology development going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent is likely to be expanding or teching. They may have a small army or be vulnerable to harassment. Expect possible pressure from them as well.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a strong economy and production. Your army consists of Marines and Reapers, which can apply early pressure. Your defense is light, so you need to be careful. You have the resources to expand and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain air presence, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, and continue economy and production. Keep technology development going.

With a heavy economy, you can afford to keep your ground army strong while maintaining a light air presence for scouting or harassment. Maintaining defense is crucial due to your light defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with a stronger defensive posture and possibly more air units.
