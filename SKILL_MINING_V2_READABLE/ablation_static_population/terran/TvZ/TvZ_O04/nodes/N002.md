# N002 — Late-Midgame Siege Tank Core

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with moderate air presence, heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Defense moderate, pressure possible. Continue to increase air presence, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, and keep expanding. Continue production and technology upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent may be transitioning to a late-game composition. They could have a large ground army or be teching to brood lords or vipers. Expect possible pressure or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a solid ground army centered around Siege Tanks. Your economy and production are strong. Your defense is moderate, but you have the potential to push out. You have some air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase air presence, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, and keep expanding. Continue production and technology upgrades.

Siege Tanks provide a strong defensive backbone and zoning. Adding air units gives you flexibility against Zerg tech. Maintaining economy and production ensures you can sustain a long game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mobile army with medivac drops or a push to take map control.
