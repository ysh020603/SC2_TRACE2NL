# N009 — Midgame Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with light or uncertain air presence, heavy economy, production and technology. Ground army with Marines and Reapers. Defense heavy, expansion heavy, pressure possible. Maintain air presence, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, and continue economy and production. Keep technology development going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent is likely to have a large ground army or be teching. They may attempt to overwhelm you with numbers or tech to air. Expect possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong economy and production. Your army is ground-focused with Marines and Reapers. Your defense is heavy, so you are well-protected. You have the resources to expand and tech further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain air presence, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, and continue economy and production. Keep technology development going.

With a heavy economy, you can afford to keep your ground army strong while maintaining a light air presence for scouting or harassment. Maintaining defense is crucial due to your light defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with a stronger defensive posture and possibly more air units.
