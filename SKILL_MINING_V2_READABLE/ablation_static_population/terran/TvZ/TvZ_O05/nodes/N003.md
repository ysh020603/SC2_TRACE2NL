## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Marines and Reapers. Light defense and possible pressure. Maintain your economy and production while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding Siege Tanks for defense and pushing power. Keep expanding to support your tech.

- The Zerg opponent is likely expanding and teching. They may be preparing a timing attack or a tech switch. Their exact composition is not fully known.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are transitioning from early to midgame. Your economy and production are heavy, and you have a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Defense is light, and you may be starting to apply pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and production while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Consider adding Siege Tanks for defense and pushing power. Keep expanding to support your tech.

A strong economy and production allow you to transition into a powerful midgame army. Marines and Reapers provide early map control, and adding Siege Tanks gives you a solid defensive and offensive backbone.
