# N005 — Late-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. Heavy defense and possible pressure. Stabilize your position by reinforcing your defense and continuing to expand. Maintain your ground army and consider adding air units for support. Keep tech upgrades progressing.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent is likely to have a large army and may be preparing a timing attack or a tech switch. Their exact composition is not fully known, but they are a significant threat.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks and Marines. Defense is heavy, and you are likely preparing for a major engagement.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by reinforcing your defense and continuing to expand. Maintain your ground army and consider adding air units for support. Keep tech upgrades progressing.

A heavy defense and strong economy allow you to weather Zerg attacks and transition into a powerful late-game army. Siege Tanks and Marines provide a solid core.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
