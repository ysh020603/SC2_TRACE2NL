# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and possibly Battlecruisers. Moderate defense and possible pressure. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your air presence. Maintain defense and keep expanding. Consider increasing technology for upgrades or additional air units.

## When This Applies

- The Zerg opponent is likely to have a large economy and may be transitioning to a tech-heavy composition. They could be preparing a timing attack or expanding further. Their exact composition is not fully known.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks and Marines, and you have started to add air units like Battlecruisers. Defense is moderate, and you may be applying pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your air presence. Maintain defense and keep expanding. Consider increasing technology for upgrades or additional air units.

A solid ground core with Siege Tanks provides a strong backbone. Adding air units gives flexibility against Zerg tech switches. Heavy economy supports continued production and tech.
