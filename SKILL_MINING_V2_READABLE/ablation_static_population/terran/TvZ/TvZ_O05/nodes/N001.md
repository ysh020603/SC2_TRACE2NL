## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, light defense, and Reaper as representative unit. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Increase production and expansion to support your tech-heavy path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg; their early game intentions are not yet clear. They may be expanding, teching, or preparing an early pressure. Maintain scouting to detect their direction.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology focus. Production is moderate, and you have a Reaper for scouting and harassment. Defense is light, and your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Increase production and expansion to support your tech-heavy path.

A heavy economy and technology investment sets up a strong midgame. Reaper provides early map presence and scouting. Increasing production and expansion ensures you can support the tech transition.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
