# N008 — Early Game Tech/Eco with Low Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate economy and technology, moderate production, light defense, and no representative unit cues. Expansion is light or uncertain. Maintain your current path and focus on developing your economy and technology. Consider expanding to support your tech-heavy build. Keep production steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg; their early game intentions are not yet clear. They may be expanding, teching, or preparing an early pressure. Maintain scouting to detect their direction.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy and technology focus. Production is moderate, and you have no clear army composition yet. Expansion is light, and defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current path and focus on developing your economy and technology. Consider expanding to support your tech-heavy build. Keep production steady.

A moderate economy and technology investment sets up a strong midgame. Maintaining your path allows you to adapt to the opponent's moves without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
