# N007 — Early Game Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase, unknown army domain, moderate production, heavy tech, heavy economy, light expansion and defense. Stabilize your position by building a few units for defense, then continue to develop your economy and tech. Consider expanding soon.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent, but no specific information. Be alert for early aggression or a fast expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with an unknown army composition. Your production is moderate, but your tech is heavy. Your economy is strong, but your expansion and defense are light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by building a few units for defense, then continue to develop your economy and tech. Consider expanding soon.

Your heavy tech investment will pay off, but you need to survive the early game. Building a small army will deter early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to an early-midgame posture with a ground army and stronger defense.
