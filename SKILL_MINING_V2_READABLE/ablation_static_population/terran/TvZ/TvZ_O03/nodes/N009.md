# N009 — Midgame Maintain Current Path

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase, ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech, heavy economy and expansion, moderate defense. Maintain your current army path, continue to expand and tech, but do not over-commit to a new direction. Keep your defense moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent, but no specific information. Be ready for any tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines. Your production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path, continue to expand and tech, but do not over-commit to a new direction. Keep your defense moderate.

Your current path is working, so it's safe to continue. Maintaining economy and tech will allow you to adapt later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame posture with a more defined army composition.
