## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army composition, continue to expand and tech, but do not over-commit to air. Keep your ground strength.

Your composition is balanced and strong. Maintaining it while expanding gives you a solid midgame presence. Battlecruisers provide a powerful late-game option.
