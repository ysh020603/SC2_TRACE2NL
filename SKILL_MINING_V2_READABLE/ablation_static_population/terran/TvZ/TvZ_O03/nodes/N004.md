# N004 — Late-Midgame Heavy Production and Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase, ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech, heavy economy and expansion, heavy defense, possible pressure. Continue to strengthen your ground army, increase air presence, and keep expanding. Maintain production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg opponent, but no specific information. Expect possible late-game tech or mass army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with heavy production and tech. Your economy and expansions are solid, and your defense is heavy. Your army is primarily Marines, but you have the infrastructure for more.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, increase air presence, and keep expanding. Maintain production and tech.

With a heavy economy and tech, you can afford to diversify and prepare for the late game. Increasing air presence gives you flexibility against Zerg tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game posture with a more specialized army composition, possibly including more air units.
