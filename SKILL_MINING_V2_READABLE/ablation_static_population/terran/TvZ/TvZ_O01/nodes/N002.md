# N002 — Early-Midgame Tech and Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology, and a ground army including Hellions. Continue increasing technology while maintaining production. Keep expanding at a measured pace.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's tech path is unknown; they may be teching to air or massing ground units.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are heavy, technology is increasing. Army is ground-oriented with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing technology while maintaining production. Keep expanding at a measured pace.

Tech upgrades will give you an edge in the midgame. Maintaining production ensures you can field a strong army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs for a stronger push or defense.
