# N007 — Early Game Production Increase

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, and a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Increase production to support your economy and tech. Maintain expansion and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's early game is unknown; they may be aggressive or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Production is moderate, and you may be under pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production to support your economy and tech. Maintain expansion and continue technology development.

Increasing production will allow you to field a larger army to defend against early pressure or apply pressure yourself.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production state with a more defined army composition.
