# N006 — Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology, and a ground army with Siege Tanks and Hellions. Stabilize your position by increasing defense and expanding. Continue production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg may be preparing a large attack or teching to a specific composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks and Hellions. Defense is heavy, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing defense and expanding. Continue production and technology development.

A stable economy and defense will allow you to weather Zerg's attacks and build a stronger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a solid economy and army.
