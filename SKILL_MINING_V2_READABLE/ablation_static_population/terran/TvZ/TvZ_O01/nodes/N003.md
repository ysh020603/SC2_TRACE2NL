# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, but army composition is unknown. Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech base while producing Reapers for scouting and map control.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's early game is unknown; they could be going for a quick expand or an aggressive pool.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army composition is not yet defined. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech base while producing Reapers for scouting and map control.

A strong economy and tech will pay off later. Reapers provide early map presence and scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once scouting reveals Zerg's plan.
