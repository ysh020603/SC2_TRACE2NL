# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and production, light defense, and possible pressure from Zerg. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and maintaining production. Keep technology development ongoing.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg's composition is not fully scouted; they may be preparing a timing attack or expanding themselves.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Marines, Reapers). Technology is heavy. Defense is light, and you may be under pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and maintaining production. Keep technology development ongoing.

A heavy economy and tech advantage can be leveraged into a strong midgame army. Maintaining production ensures you can respond to pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid defensive posture if pressure is confirmed, or continue expanding if Zerg is passive.
