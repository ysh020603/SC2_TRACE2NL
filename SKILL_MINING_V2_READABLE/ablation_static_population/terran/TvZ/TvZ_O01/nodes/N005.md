# N005 — Late-Midgame Defense and Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology, and a ground army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs. Increase defense and continue expanding. Maintain production and technology to support a late-game army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg may be transitioning to a late-game composition with brood lords or ultralisks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a strong ground army including Siege Tanks and Medivacs. Defense is heavy, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and continue expanding. Maintain production and technology to support a late-game army.

A heavy defense and economy will allow you to outlast Zerg's midgame pressure and transition to a powerful late-game army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition with more air support (e.g., Vikings) if Zerg goes for brood lords.
