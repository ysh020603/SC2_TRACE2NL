# TvZ_O01 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O01
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that prioritizes rapid expansion, heavy economy, and technology investment while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented posture. Early game focuses on Reaper and Marine production for map presence and scouting, transitioning into heavier production and tech in the midgame.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while preserving flexibility to adapt to Zerg's live actions. Aim for a midgame army composition that can handle both ground and potential air threats.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of Zerg's tech and army composition.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
