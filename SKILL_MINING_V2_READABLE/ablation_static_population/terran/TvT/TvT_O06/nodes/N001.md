# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, ground-oriented posture, heavy economy and tech, moderate production, possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and be ready for possible early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this ablation; rely on scouting to infer their posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented macro posture. Economy and technology are heavy, production is moderate, and you have a defensive setup. Representative units include Marines. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and be ready for possible early pressure.

A heavy economy and tech investment sets up a strong midgame, but you need a solid ground core to defend against early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a stronger ground army and continued tech progression.
