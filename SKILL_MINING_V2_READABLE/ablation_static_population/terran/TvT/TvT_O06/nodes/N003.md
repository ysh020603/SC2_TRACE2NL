# N003 — Early Reaper Pressure with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, ground-oriented, heavy production and tech, moderate economy, possible pressure. Use Reapers for harassment while continuing to build up your economy and tech. Maintain production and defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this ablation; rely on scouting to infer their posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a Reaper as a representative unit. Production and tech are heavy, but economy is moderate. You have a defensive posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use Reapers for harassment while continuing to build up your economy and tech. Maintain production and defensive structures.

Reapers provide early map control and scouting, buying time for your heavy tech investment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger ground army as your economy improves.
