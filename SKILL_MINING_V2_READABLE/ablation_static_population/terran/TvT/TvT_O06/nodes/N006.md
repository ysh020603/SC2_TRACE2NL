# N006 — Midgame Ground with Air Support

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame, ground-oriented, heavy economy, tech, and production, possible pressure. Continue strengthening your ground army while adding air support. Increase defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this ablation; rely on scouting to infer their posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a strong ground army (Marines, Marauders, Banshees). Economy, tech, and production are heavy. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while adding air support. Increase defense and expansion.

A mix of ground and air gives you flexibility and pressure options in TvT.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile army capable of both defense and aggression.
