# N009 — Midgame Air Transition with Ground Core

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame, ground-oriented with moderate air presence, heavy economy, tech, and production. Increase air capabilities while maintaining your ground core. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this ablation; rely on scouting to infer their posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, Hellions) and moderate air presence. Economy, tech, and production are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air capabilities while maintaining your ground core. Continue expanding and teching.

Transitioning to air gives you an advantage in TvT if the opponent is ground-heavy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced air-ground army to handle various threats.
