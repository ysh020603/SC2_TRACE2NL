# TvT_O01 Economy / Technology / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O01
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / technology / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that prioritizes a heavy economy, heavy technology, and heavy production posture while remaining ground-oriented. The early game focuses on establishing a strong economic and technological base, with production ramping up through the midgame. The army composition leans toward ground units, with Siege Tanks and Marines as core components, supplemented by Hellions and Medivacs as the game progresses. Air presence remains light or uncertain in the early stages, with moderate air support appearing later.

Develop a robust economy and technology infrastructure while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented army. The primary objective is to outscale the opponent through superior macro and tech, while staying prepared for potential pressure or aggression. The opening is designed to transition smoothly into a midgame army composition that can adapt to the opponent's observed choices.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. The exact build order should be adapted based on live scouting and the opponent's actions. The heavy economy and technology focus provides a strong foundation, but the player must be ready to adjust production and army composition in response to enemy pressure or tech choices.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase (around 360 seconds) with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology investment. Air presence is light or uncertain, and defense posture is light or uncertain. Representative units include Siege Tanks and Marines.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology, while strengthening your ground army. Increase air presence if you anticipate air threats, but maintain a ground-focused core. Keep production active and consider adding defensive structures if you expect pressure.
