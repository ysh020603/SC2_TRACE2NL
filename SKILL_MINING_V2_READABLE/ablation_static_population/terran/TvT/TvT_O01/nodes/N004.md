# N004 — Early-Midgame Heavy Production with Light Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (around 300 seconds) with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, and light defense. Air presence is light or uncertain. Representative unit is Marine. Maintain your production and technology, but consider adding defensive structures or units to protect your bases. Continue expanding to support your heavy production. Strengthen your ground army to deter aggression.

- The opponent's situation is not directly observed. They may be preparing an aggressive push or expanding themselves. You should scout to determine their army composition and expansion count.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a strong economy and heavy production, but your defense is light. Your army is ground-based, primarily Marines, and you have a possible pressure posture. You are vulnerable to harassment or early timing attacks.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your production and technology, but consider adding defensive structures or units to protect your bases. Continue expanding to support your heavy production. Strengthen your ground army to deter aggression.

Your heavy production and tech give you a strong midgame potential, but you need to survive the early-midgame. Adding defense helps you withstand early pressure. Expanding ensures you have the resources to sustain your production.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the midgame with a more robust defense and a stronger army. Aim to have a mix of Marines and possibly Siege Tanks or Marauders, with defensive structures at your bases.
