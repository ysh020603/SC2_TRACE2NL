# N006 — Late-Midgame Heavy Defense and Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase (around 600 seconds) with a ground-oriented army, moderate air presence, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, and heavy defense. Representative units include Siege Tank, Marine, Hellion, and Medivac. Continue to increase your economy and expansion, while strengthening your ground army. Consider adding more air units or upgrades to counter potential air threats. Maintain your heavy defense to protect your bases.

## When This Applies

- The opponent's situation is not directly observed. They may be preparing a large attack or transitioning to a late-game composition. You should scout to determine their army composition and tech level.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a strong economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-focused with moderate air support, and your defense is heavy, indicating you have invested in defensive structures or units. You have a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your economy and expansion, while strengthening your ground army. Consider adding more air units or upgrades to counter potential air threats. Maintain your heavy defense to protect your bases.

Your heavy economy and tech give you a strong late-game potential. Expanding further increases your income, allowing for a larger army and more tech. Strengthening your army ensures you can defend against large attacks and potentially take map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late game with a powerful army and a strong economy. Aim to have a maxed-out army with upgrades, and consider adding more air units like Vikings or Liberators for map control.
