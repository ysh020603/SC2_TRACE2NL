# N007 — Early Game Moderate Economy with Heavy Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase (around 180 seconds) with a ground-oriented army, moderate economy, moderate expansion, heavy production, heavy technology, and light defense. Air presence is light or uncertain. Representative unit is Marine. Increase your economy and expansion to support your heavy production. Continue developing your technology. Strengthen your ground army to defend against early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's situation is not directly observed. They may be going for a fast expand or an early attack. You should scout to determine their build order.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy and expansion, but heavy production and technology. Your army is small and ground-based, primarily Marines. Your defense is light, and you have a possible pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion to support your heavy production. Continue developing your technology. Strengthen your ground army to defend against early aggression.

Your heavy production and tech give you a strong midgame potential, but you need to expand to sustain it. Increasing your economy ensures you have the resources to produce units and tech. Strengthening your army helps you defend against early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a stronger economy and a larger army. Aim to have a solid Marine count and possibly add Reapers or Marauders for map control.
