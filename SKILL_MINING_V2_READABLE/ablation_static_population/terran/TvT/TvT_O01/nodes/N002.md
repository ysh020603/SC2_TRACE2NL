# N002 — Early Game Heavy Tech with Moderate Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase (around 180 seconds) with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, heavy technology, and moderate production. Air presence is light or uncertain, and defense is light or uncertain. Representative unit is Marine. Increase your economy and expansion, while continuing to develop your technology. Strengthen your ground army by adding more production structures and units. Maintain a defensive posture to protect your tech and economy investments.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's situation is not directly observed. In the early game, they could be going for a fast expand, a tech rush, or an early attack. You should scout to identify their build order and adapt accordingly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a strong economic and technological foundation, but your production is only moderate. Your army is small and ground-based, primarily consisting of Marines. You have a possible pressure posture, so you should be alert for early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion, while continuing to develop your technology. Strengthen your ground army by adding more production structures and units. Maintain a defensive posture to protect your tech and economy investments.

Your heavy tech investment will pay off in the midgame, but you need to survive the early game. Increasing production and army strength helps you defend against early pressure. Expanding and growing your economy ensures you can sustain your tech-heavy build.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a stronger army, more production, and continued tech development. Aim to have a solid Marine count and possibly add Reapers or Marauders for early map control.
