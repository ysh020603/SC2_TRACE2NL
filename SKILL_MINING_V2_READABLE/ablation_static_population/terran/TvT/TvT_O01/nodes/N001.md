# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (around 360 seconds) with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology investment. Air presence is light or uncertain, and defense posture is light or uncertain. Representative units include Siege Tanks and Marines. Continue developing your economy and technology, while strengthening your ground army. Increase air presence if you anticipate air threats, but maintain a ground-focused core. Keep production active and consider adding defensive structures if you expect pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's situation is not directly observed. In a Terran mirror, they may be pursuing a similar macro-oriented build, or they could be preparing an aggressive timing. You should scout to determine their expansion count, tech structures, and army composition to gauge their intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economic and technological base, with heavy production capacity. Your army is ground-focused, with Siege Tanks and Marines as core units. You are in the early-midgame, with a possible pressure posture, meaning you should be prepared for potential enemy aggression. Your defense is light, so you may need to reinforce if you detect incoming attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, while strengthening your ground army. Increase air presence if you anticipate air threats, but maintain a ground-focused core. Keep production active and consider adding defensive structures if you expect pressure.

Your heavy economy and tech investment give you a long-term advantage if you can survive the early-midgame. Strengthening your ground army ensures you can defend against common Terran aggression, such as Marine-Tank pushes or Hellion runbys. Maintaining production and tech keeps you on track to outscale the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a solid ground army, a strong economy, and advanced technology. Aim to have a mix of Siege Tanks, Marines, and possibly Medivacs for mobility. Continue expanding and upgrading to maintain your advantage.
