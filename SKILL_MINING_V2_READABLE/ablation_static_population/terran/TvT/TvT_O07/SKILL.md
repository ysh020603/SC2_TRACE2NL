# TvT_O07 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O07
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that prioritizes heavy economy and technology investment while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented posture. Production is moderate early, scaling to heavy as the game progresses. The approach emphasizes macro development and safety checks rather than early aggression.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment. Ground-oriented army with light or uncertain air presence. Defense posture light or uncertain, pressure possible.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and air presence, but be ready to adapt if pressure increases.
