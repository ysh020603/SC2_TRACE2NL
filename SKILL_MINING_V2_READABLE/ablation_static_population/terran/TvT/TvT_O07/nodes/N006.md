# N006 — Midgame Ground with Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Defense posture heavy, pressure possible. Policy suggests increasing air presence. Increase your air presence while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your heavy defense and continue expanding your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited, but you have not detected any immediate threats. They may be building up for a large engagement or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a heavy economy and production. Your army includes Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, with a heavy defense. Your technology is advanced, and you are considering adding air units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your heavy defense and continue expanding your economy.

Adding air units provides flexibility and can counter potential air threats or provide drop opportunities. Your heavy economy supports this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army composition with both ground and air capabilities.
