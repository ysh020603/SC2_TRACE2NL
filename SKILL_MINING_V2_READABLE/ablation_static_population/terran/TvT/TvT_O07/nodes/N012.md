# N012 — Early Game Tech Focus with Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology investment. Army domain unknown, production moderate. Defense posture light or uncertain, pressure unknown. Expansion posture heavy. Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a ground army. Maintain a flexible posture and be ready to adapt to scouting information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is very limited; you have not scouted their composition or intentions. They may be going for an early aggressive build or a fast expand.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment. Your army composition is not yet defined, and your production is moderate. Your defense is light or uncertain, but you have expanded aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to build a ground army. Maintain a flexible posture and be ready to adapt to scouting information.

Your heavy technology investment will pay off in the midgame, but you need to ensure you have enough defense to survive early pressure. Building a ground army provides a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and a solid defensive posture as you gather information.
