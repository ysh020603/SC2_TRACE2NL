# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment. Ground-oriented army with light or uncertain air presence. Defense posture light or uncertain, pressure possible. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and air presence, but be ready to adapt if pressure increases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; you have not confirmed their composition or intentions. They may be preparing an aggressive push or expanding themselves.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a heavy economy and production base. Your technology is advanced, and your army is primarily ground-based with light or uncertain air support. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you may face possible pressure from the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and air presence, but be ready to adapt if pressure increases.

Your heavy economy and technology investment provide a long-term advantage if you can survive early pressure. Strengthening your ground army ensures you can defend against potential attacks while you continue to scale.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid defensive posture with a stronger ground army and clearer scouting information.
