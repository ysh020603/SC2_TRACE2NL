# N012 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Phase early_game, own posture unknown-oriented, moderate production, heavy technology, economy heavy, expansion heavy, defense light or uncertain, air presence light or uncertain, no representative units. Continue developing your economy and tech, and start producing units to build a ground army. Maintain production and consider adding defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; early scouting is essential.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and tech investment, but your army is not yet formed. Production is moderate, and you have no significant units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech, and start producing units to build a ground army. Maintain production and consider adding defensive structures.

With a tech and economy focus, you need to start building an army to defend and transition into the midgame. Producing Marines is a safe start.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a basic army while continuing your tech and economy development.
