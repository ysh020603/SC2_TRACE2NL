# N011 — Early Game Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Phase early_game, own posture unknown-oriented, moderate production, heavy technology, economy heavy, expansion heavy, defense light or uncertain, air presence light or uncertain, representative unit Marine. Stabilize your position by producing a few more units for defense, then continue developing your economy and tech. Increase production to support future army growth.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available; early scouting is crucial to determine their strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and tech investment, but your army composition is not yet defined. Production is moderate, and you have a Marine as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by producing a few more units for defense, then continue developing your economy and tech. Increase production to support future army growth.

With an undefined army, it's wise to stabilize first to avoid being caught off guard. Once you have a safety net, you can focus on your tech and economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a stable early game position and transition into a defined strategy.
