# N003 — Early Game Economy Focus with Unknown Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, but unknown army composition and defense. Moderate production and technology. No clear pressure posture. Continue developing your economy and expansion, while starting to build a more defined army composition. Maintain production and technology development, but be ready to adapt based on scouting information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Use your Reaper to scout and gather information.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion focus. Your army composition is unknown, but you have a Reaper for scouting. Production and technology are moderate, indicating you are still developing your infrastructure. Defense is light or uncertain, so you are vulnerable.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion, while starting to build a more defined army composition. Maintain production and technology development, but be ready to adapt based on scouting information.

A strong economy is the foundation for a powerful midgame. By focusing on economy now, you can outpace your opponent in the later stages, provided you survive the early game.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
