# N005 — Late-Midgame Balanced Ground Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and technology. Ground army with moderate air presence and moderate defense. Possible pressure from opponent. Maintain your current army composition and production levels, while continuing to increase your economy and technology. Keep your defense at a moderate level and be ready to respond to pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Continue to scout and adapt based on live observations.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and a balanced ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. Air presence is moderate, and your defense is moderate. Production is heavy, but you are maintaining rather than increasing it, suggesting a focus on consolidation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army composition and production levels, while continuing to increase your economy and technology. Keep your defense at a moderate level and be ready to respond to pressure.

With a balanced army and strong economy, you are well-positioned for the late game. Maintaining your current posture allows you to react to the opponent's moves without over-committing.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a late-game army composition, possibly adding more air units or specialized units based on the opponent's composition.
