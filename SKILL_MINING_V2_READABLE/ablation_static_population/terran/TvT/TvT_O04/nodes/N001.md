# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology posture. Ground-oriented army with light or uncertain air presence and defense. Possible pressure from opponent. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current production and expansion pace, but be ready to adjust if you detect early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Maintain awareness of potential pressure given your light defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding heavily. Production and technology are both heavy, indicating a commitment to a macro-oriented ground army. Your army is primarily ground-based with Reapers as a representative unit, and your defense is light or uncertain, suggesting you may be vulnerable to early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current production and expansion pace, but be ready to adjust if you detect early pressure.

Your heavy economy and technology investment position you well for a strong midgame. Continuing to expand and tech up will give you a significant advantage if you can survive any early threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into a midgame army composition with Siege Tanks and Marines, while maintaining your economic and technological lead.
