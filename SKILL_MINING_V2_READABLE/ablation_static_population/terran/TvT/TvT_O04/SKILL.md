# TvT_O04 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that prioritizes a heavy economy and technology investment while maintaining a flexible, ground-leaning posture. Early game focuses on Reaper presence and expansion, transitioning into a heavier ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions as the game progresses.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology posture. Ground-oriented army with light or uncertain air presence and defense. Possible pressure from opponent.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current production and expansion pace, but be ready to adjust if you detect early pressure.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [COMMON] N002 — Midgame Ground Army with Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Ground army with moderate air presence and moderate defense. Possible pressure from opponent.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and expansion, and continue technology upgrades. Be prepared to defend against possible pressure.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [COMMON] N003 — Early Game Economy Focus with Unknown Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, but unknown army composition and defense. Moderate production and technology. No clear pressure posture.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and expansion, while starting to build a more defined army composition. Maintain production and technology development, but be ready to adapt based on scouting information.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [COMMON] N004 — Late-Midgame Heavy Defense and Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Ground army with moderate air presence and heavy defense. Possible pressure from opponent.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing your air presence. Maintain your heavy defense and continue expanding your economy and technology. Be prepared for possible pressure.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [COMMON] N005 — Late-Midgame Balanced Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and technology. Ground army with moderate air presence and moderate defense. Possible pressure from opponent.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your current army composition and production levels, while continuing to increase your economy and technology. Keep your defense at a moderate level and be ready to respond to pressure.

**Read for details:** `N005`
