# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with an unknown army composition, moderate production, heavy technology, and a heavy economy. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is unknown. Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your current production and expansion, but keep safety checks due to unknown pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Use live scouting to determine their opening and potential aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army composition is not yet defined. Production is moderate, and you have Marines as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while starting to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your current production and expansion, but keep safety checks due to unknown pressure.

A heavy tech and economy investment sets up a strong midgame, but you need a basic army to defend against early aggression. Strengthening ground forces gives you a safe foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to an early-midgame state with a defined ground army and a clearer strategic direction.
