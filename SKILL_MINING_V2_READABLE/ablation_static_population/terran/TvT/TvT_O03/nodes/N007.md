# N007 — Early Game Tech with Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with an unknown army composition (Marauders), moderate production, heavy technology and economy, and a heavy expansion posture. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is unknown. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Increase your expansion and production, and keep your upgrade investment going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Use live scouting to determine their opening and potential aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment, and you are expanding aggressively. Your army is not yet defined, but you have Marauders as a representative unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Increase your expansion and production, and keep your upgrade investment going.

A heavy economy and expansion posture sets up a strong midgame, but you need a basic army to defend against early aggression. Strengthening ground forces gives you a safe foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
