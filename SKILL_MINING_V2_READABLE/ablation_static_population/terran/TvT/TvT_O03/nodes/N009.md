# N009 — Midgame Ground with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame phase with a ground-oriented army including Widow Mines, heavy production and technology, and a heavy economy. Defense is heavy, and air presence is light or uncertain. Stabilize your position and then continue developing. Increase your production and economy, but maintain your defense and technology investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Use live scouting to assess their composition and potential threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground army (Marines, Widow Mines), heavy production and technology, and a robust economy. Your defense is heavy, and you have a heavy expansion posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position and then continue developing. Increase your production and economy, but maintain your defense and technology investment.

With heavy defense, you are well-protected. Stabilizing first ensures you can safely expand your economy and production without overextending.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once your economy and production are stable, or adapt based on opponent actions.
