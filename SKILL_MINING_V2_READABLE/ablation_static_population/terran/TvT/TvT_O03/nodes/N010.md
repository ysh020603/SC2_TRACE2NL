# N010 — Early Game Tech with Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with an unknown army composition (Marines, Marauders), moderate production, heavy technology and economy, and a moderate expansion posture. Defense is heavy, and pressure is unknown. Stabilize your position and then continue developing. Increase your production and economy, but maintain your defense and technology investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this static population projection. Use live scouting to determine their opening and potential aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army composition is not yet defined. Production is moderate, and you have Marines and Marauders as representative units. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position and then continue developing. Increase your production and economy, but maintain your defense and technology investment.

With heavy defense, you are well-protected. Stabilizing first ensures you can safely expand your economy and production without overextending.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to an early-midgame state with a defined ground army and a clearer strategic direction.
