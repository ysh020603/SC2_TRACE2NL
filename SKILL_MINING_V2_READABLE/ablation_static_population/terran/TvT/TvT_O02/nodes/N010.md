# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with moderate air presence, heavy economy, production, and tech, ground-oriented but with air units. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, and continue economy and tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is unknown; maintain scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with a ground army and some air units. Consider increasing air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, and continue economy and tech development.

Adding air units can give you an advantage in TvT, especially if the opponent is ground-heavy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced or air-heavy composition.
