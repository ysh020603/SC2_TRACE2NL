# N008 — Early Game Ground Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game with moderate production and heavy tech, ground-oriented. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's actions are unknown; maintain awareness.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have moderate production and heavy tech, with a ground army. Focus on development.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue tech.

Early tech investment can pay off later, but you need production to support it.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a strong early-midgame position.
