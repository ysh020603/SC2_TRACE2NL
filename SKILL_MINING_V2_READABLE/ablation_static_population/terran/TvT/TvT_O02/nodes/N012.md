# N012 — Early-Midgame Heavy Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame with heavy production and tech, moderate economy, and unknown army composition. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's strategy is unclear; maintain scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy production and tech, but your army composition is not yet defined. Focus on strengthening ground army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue production and tech.

Heavy production allows you to field a large army quickly. Ground is a safe default.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition.
