# N011 — Midgame Defensive Ground

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy and tech, moderate production, and a defensive ground posture. Increase defense, strengthen ground army, and continue economy and tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is unknown; maintain scouting and be ready for aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and tech, but moderate production. Focus on defense and strengthening ground army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, strengthen ground army, and continue economy and tech development.

A defensive posture can be effective if you are teching up. Strengthening ground army provides safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a tech advantage.
