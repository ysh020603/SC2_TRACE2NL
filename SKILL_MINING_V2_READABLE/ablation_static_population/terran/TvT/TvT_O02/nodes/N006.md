# N006 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech, and a ground-oriented army. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's composition is unknown; maintain scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with a ground army. Continue to expand and tech up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue tech development.

A strong economy supports a large army and tech. Ground army is reliable in TvT.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized composition or add air units if needed.
