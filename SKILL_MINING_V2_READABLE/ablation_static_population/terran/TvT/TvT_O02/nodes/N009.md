# N009 — Early-Midgame Heavy Ground

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech, ground-oriented. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's strategy is unclear; maintain scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with a ground army. Continue to expand and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, and continue production and tech.

A strong economy supports a large army and tech. Ground is reliable.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized composition.
