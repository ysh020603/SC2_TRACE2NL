# N004 — Late-Midgame Stability

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Late-midgame phase, with light or uncertain production and tech, but with a stable economy. Maintain current development, but start to consider late-game tech and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's strategy is still unclear; keep scouting and be ready for late-game transitions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is strong, and you have a solid foundation. Maintain production and tech while preparing for late game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development, but start to consider late-game tech and upgrades.

Late-midgame is about preparing for the late game. A stable economy allows you to tech up or expand your army as needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition with advanced tech and upgrades.
