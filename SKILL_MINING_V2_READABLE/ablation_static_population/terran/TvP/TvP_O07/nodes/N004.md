# N004 — Early Game Marine Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with light or uncertain air presence, heavy defense, economy, expansion, and technology, but moderate production. Representative unit is Marine. Pressure posture is possible. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your heavy defense and expansion posture. Increase your production to support a larger army, and continue technology upgrades.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your heavy defense and expansion posture. Increase your production to support a larger army, and continue technology upgrades.

This node represents an early development phase where you have a solid economy and tech foundation but need to ramp up production. Increasing production will allow you to field a larger army and defend against potential pressure.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
