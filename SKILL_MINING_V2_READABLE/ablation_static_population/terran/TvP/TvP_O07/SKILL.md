# TvP_O07 Economy / Technology / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvP_O07
- Matchup: Terran vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that prioritizes a heavy economy, heavy technology, and heavy expansion posture while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The early game focuses on Marines and Reapers, transitioning to a heavier ground composition with Siege Tanks, Hellions, and Medivacs in the midgame. The overall tempo is development with safety checks, keeping defense heavy and production heavy.

Develop a strong economy, technology, and expansion base while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation. Maintain a heavy ground army and defense to deter aggression while scaling up.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of the opponent's composition and pressure.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [COMMON] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with light or uncertain air presence, heavy defense, economy, expansion, production, and technology. Representative units include Reaper or Marine. Pressure posture is possible.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your heavy defense and expansion posture. Increase your economy and expansion further, and continue production and technology upgrades.
