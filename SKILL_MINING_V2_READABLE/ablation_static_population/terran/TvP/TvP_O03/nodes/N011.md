# N011 — Early Marine Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame with a ground army of Marines, heavy production and economy, light defense. Strengthen your ground army and continue to expand and tech. Increase production to support your army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is not fully known, but you are prepared for possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, heavy production and economy, and light defense. You are expanding and teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to expand and tech. Increase production to support your army.

Marines are a versatile core unit, and heavy production allows you to quickly build an army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more diverse army composition as your tech completes.
