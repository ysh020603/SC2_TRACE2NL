# N007 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with a ground army and moderate air presence (Banshees), heavy production and economy, moderate defense. Increase your air presence and continue to expand and tech. Maintain production and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is not fully known, but you are preparing for possible engagements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Banshees, indicating a transition to air. Heavy production and economy, moderate defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue to expand and tech. Maintain production and defense.

Adding air units like Banshees gives you harassment and anti-ground options, diversifying your army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with strong air support to pressure the opponent.
