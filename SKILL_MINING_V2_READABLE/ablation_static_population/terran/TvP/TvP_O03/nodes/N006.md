# N006 — Midgame Siege Tank Core

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with a ground army including Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and economy, moderate defense. Continue strengthening your ground army with Siege Tanks for defense and pushing. Maintain economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is not fully known, but you are prepared for possible engagements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and economy, and moderate defenses. You are expanding and teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army with Siege Tanks for defense and pushing. Maintain economy and tech.

Siege Tanks provide strong defensive and siege capabilities, allowing you to control the map and defend your expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more mobile army composition as the game progresses.
