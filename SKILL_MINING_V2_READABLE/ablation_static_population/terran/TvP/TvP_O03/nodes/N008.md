COMMON

## Summary

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is not fully known, but you are prepared for possible engagements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a diverse army with ground and air units, heavy production and economy, and moderate defenses. You are in the late midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue to tech and expand. Keep production and defense steady.

A mixed army gives you flexibility to respond to various threats, and your heavy economy supports continued tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
