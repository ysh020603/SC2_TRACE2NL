# N005 — Early Reaper Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game with a Reaper as a representative unit, heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and light defense. Use the Reaper for scouting and harassment while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Increase production to support future army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; you are using the Reaper to scout and harass.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper for early harassment, with a heavy economy and tech focus. Production is moderate, and your defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use the Reaper for scouting and harassment while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Increase production to support future army.

Reaper provides early map control and information, allowing you to adapt your build while maintaining a strong economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition from Reaper harassment into a solid army composition as your tech completes.
