# N001 — Early Tech/Economy Foundation

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production steady. Maintain a defensive posture and be ready to adapt.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited at this stage; you have not confirmed their build or pressure intentions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology focus, but your army composition and defensive posture are not yet clear. Production is moderate, and you have some Marines as a basic unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while keeping production steady. Maintain a defensive posture and be ready to adapt.

A heavy tech/economy investment sets up strong midgame options, but you need to avoid over-committing before scouting confirms the opponent's plan.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into a more defined army composition as your tech completes and scouting reveals the opponent's direction.
