# N003 — Defensive Ground Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy defense, economy, and technology, but moderate production. Maintain a strong defense while continuing to tech and expand. Increase production as needed to support your army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Possible pressure from the opponent, but their exact plan is not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defensive posture with a ground army, strong economy and tech, but production is only moderate. You are expanding and teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a strong defense while continuing to tech and expand. Increase production as needed to support your army.

A defensive posture buys time for your tech to pay off, but you need to ensure you can field enough units to hold.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use your tech advantage to transition into a more powerful army composition.
