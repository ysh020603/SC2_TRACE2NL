# N004 — Midgame Ground Strength

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production and economy, and moderate defense. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep production and tech going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's composition is not fully known, but you are prepared for possible engagements.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Marines and Marauders, heavy production and economy, and moderate defenses. You are expanding and teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep production and tech going.

A strong ground force with heavy economy gives you options to pressure or defend, and you can tech into air or upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for late-game transitions by maintaining a strong economy and tech base.
