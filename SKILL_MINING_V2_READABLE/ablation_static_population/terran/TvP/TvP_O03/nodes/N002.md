# N002 — Ground Macro Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy production and economy, and moderate defense. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to expand and tech. Maintain production and keep defenses solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- You have not confirmed the opponent's exact composition, but you are aware of possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-focused army with Marines, heavy production and economy, and a moderate defensive setup. You are expanding and teching up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to expand and tech. Maintain production and keep defenses solid.

A strong ground army with heavy economy supports a robust midgame, allowing you to transition to tech units or air as needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground force that can either push or defend, and prepare for tech transitions.
