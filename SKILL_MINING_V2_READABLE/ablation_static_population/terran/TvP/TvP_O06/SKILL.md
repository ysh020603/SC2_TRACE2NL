# TvP_O06 Technology/Economy/Production Opening

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvP_O06
- Matchup: Terran vs Protoss
- Opening Family: Technology / Economy / Production
- Method: Static Population

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that emphasizes heavy production, economy, and technology investment while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The early game focuses on establishing a strong economic and production base, with technology upgrades supporting a transition into a versatile midgame composition.

Develop a robust economy and production infrastructure while advancing technology, keeping options open for ground or air transitions based on live observations.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and game state.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
