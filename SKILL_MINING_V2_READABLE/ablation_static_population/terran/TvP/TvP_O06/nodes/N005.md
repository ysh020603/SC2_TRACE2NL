# N005 — Early Game Marine with Heavy Expansion

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with moderate economy, heavy production and expansion. Ground army with Marine cue. Defense light, technology heavy. Continue expanding and producing Marines while maintaining technology progression. Be ready to defend against early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Protoss, but no specific information. Expect possible early pressure or tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy but heavy production and expansion. You have Marines as your main unit, and your defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and producing Marines while maintaining technology progression. Be ready to defend against early aggression.

Heavy expansion gives you a long-term economic advantage. Marines are cost-effective and can be supported by tech later.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
