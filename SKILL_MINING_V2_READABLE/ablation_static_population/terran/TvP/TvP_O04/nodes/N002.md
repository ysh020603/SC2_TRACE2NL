# N002 — Early Game Economy and Tech Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Own posture is unknown-oriented with moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansions, light defense, and unknown pressure. Phase is early game. Prioritize economy and tech development while maintaining a small defensive force. Use Reapers for scouting and harassment to gain information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's intentions are unclear. They could be going for an early expand, tech, or aggression. Keep scouting to gather information.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game, focusing on economy and expansions. Your army composition is not yet defined, and you have light defense. Production and tech are moderate, allowing for flexibility.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Prioritize economy and tech development while maintaining a small defensive force. Use Reapers for scouting and harassment to gain information.

A strong economy and tech foundation will enable a powerful mid-game. Early scouting helps you adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and posture as the game progresses.
