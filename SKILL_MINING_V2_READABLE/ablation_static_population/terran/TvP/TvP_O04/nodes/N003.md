## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding air support if needed. Maintain your economy and tech lead while preparing for potential engagements.

A strong ground army with tech support can handle most mid-game threats. Keeping production and tech heavy ensures you can adapt to the opponent's composition.
