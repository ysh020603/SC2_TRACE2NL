# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, heavy economy and expansions, light defense, and possible pressure. Phase is early-midgame. Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Keep production active and consider adding defensive structures if pressure seems imminent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent's status is unknown; they may be preparing an attack or expanding themselves. Maintain scouting to detect any early aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding aggressively. Your production and tech are heavy, but your army is still developing and defense is light. You may be vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Keep production active and consider adding defensive structures if pressure seems imminent.

Your heavy economy and tech investment will pay off in the mid-game, but you need to survive potential early pressure. A balanced approach ensures you can transition smoothly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger defensive posture with a more substantial army, while maintaining your economic lead.
