# N002 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Moderate Defense

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Ground-oriented army with Marines. Defense posture moderate, pressure possible. Maintain your current economy, production, and technology development. Continue strengthening your ground army and keep defense at a moderate level.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details. Be prepared for possible pressure or tech plays.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army is ground-focused, primarily Marines. Defense is moderate, indicating some preparation for potential attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current economy, production, and technology development. Continue strengthening your ground army and keep defense at a moderate level.

A moderate defense provides safety while you continue to build your economy and tech. Maintaining production ensures you can respond to threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture if you gain an advantage, or to a heavier defense if pressure increases.
