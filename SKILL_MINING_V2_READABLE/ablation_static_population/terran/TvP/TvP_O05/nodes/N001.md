# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology investment. Ground-oriented army with Marines and Marauders. Defense posture light or uncertain, but pressure possible. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and expansion posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details. Be aware of potential early pressure or tech plays.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy technology investment. Your army is ground-focused, primarily Marines and Marauders. Defense is light or uncertain, and you may face pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defense and expansion posture.

Your heavy economy and tech investment will pay off if you can stabilize and reach midgame. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid core for potential pushes or defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid defensive posture if pressure is detected, or continue expanding if safe.
