# N004 — Early Game Heavy Production with Unknown Army

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Army composition unknown, defense light or uncertain, pressure unknown. Maintain your heavy production and technology development. Focus on scouting to determine the opponent's plan and define your army composition accordingly.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details. Be prepared for any possible opening.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy, production, and technology, but your army composition is not yet defined. Defense is light or uncertain, and you have no clear indication of pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your heavy production and technology development. Focus on scouting to determine the opponent's plan and define your army composition accordingly.

With heavy production and tech, you have flexibility to adapt to the opponent. Scouting is crucial to make informed decisions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting information, and establish a defensive posture if needed.
