# N003 — Early Game Ground Macro with Moderate Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, but moderate production. Ground-oriented army with Marines and Marauders. Defense light or uncertain, pressure possible. Increase production to support your heavy economy and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your expansion posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details. Be alert for early aggression or tech rushes.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and technology, but production is moderate. Your army is ground-focused, with Marines and Marauders. Defense is light or uncertain, and you may face early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production to support your heavy economy and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your expansion posture.

With a strong economy, you can afford to increase production to field a larger army. This helps you defend against pressure and sets up for midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state with heavier production and a more solid defense if pressure is detected.
