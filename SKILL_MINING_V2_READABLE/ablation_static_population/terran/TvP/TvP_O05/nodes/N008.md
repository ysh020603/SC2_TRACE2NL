# N008 — Early Game Unknown Army with Moderate Production

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Army composition unknown, defense light or uncertain, pressure unknown. Increase production and continue developing your economy and technology. Focus on scouting to define your army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is limited; no confirmed details. Be prepared for any possible opening.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and technology, but production is moderate. Your army composition is not yet defined, with some Marauders. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and continue developing your economy and technology. Focus on scouting to define your army composition.

With a strong economy, you can afford to increase production. Scouting is essential to make informed decisions about army composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting information, and establish a defensive posture if needed.
