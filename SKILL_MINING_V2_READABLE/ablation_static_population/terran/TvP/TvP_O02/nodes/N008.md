# N008 — Early Game Expansion Focus

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, own posture is unknown-oriented with moderate economy and heavy expansion. Production is light or uncertain, technology is moderate. Strengthen ground forces and continue technology development. Maintain expansion and increase economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy and heavy expansion. Production is light or uncertain, and technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces and continue technology development. Maintain expansion and increase economy.

Heavy expansion suggests a macro-oriented plan. You need to protect your expansions and build a strong economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army and increase production to support your expansions.
