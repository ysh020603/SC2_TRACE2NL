# N001 — Early Game Baseline

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early game, own posture is broadly unknown with light or uncertain production and technology. No clear army composition or pressure posture. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Avoid committing to a specific army composition or tech path until more information is available.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with an unclear economic and production posture. Technology investment is light or uncertain. No representative units are confirmed yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Avoid committing to a specific army composition or tech path until more information is available.

Preserving flexibility early allows adaptation to opponent actions and avoids costly mistakes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress toward a clearer economic and production posture while keeping options open.
