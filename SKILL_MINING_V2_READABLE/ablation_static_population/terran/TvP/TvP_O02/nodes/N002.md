# N002 — Early-Midgame Baseline

## Node Type

COMMON

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture remains broadly unknown with light or uncertain production and technology. No clear army composition or pressure posture. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Continue to avoid committing to a specific army composition or tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent information is not available in this projection.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with an unclear economic and production posture. Technology investment is still light or uncertain. No representative units are confirmed.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Continue to avoid committing to a specific army composition or tech path.

Maintaining flexibility is still valuable, but you should start preparing to commit to a direction as the game progresses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Begin transitioning toward a more concrete army composition or tech path based on your own strengths and potential threats.
