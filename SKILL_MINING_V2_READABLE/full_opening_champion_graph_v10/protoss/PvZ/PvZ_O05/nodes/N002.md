# N002 — Aggressive Tech Development

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light tech. Increase production and technology, strengthen ground forces, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy expansion. Possible early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is undefined, but you have a strong economy and heavy technology investment. Defense is solid, and production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology, strengthen ground forces, maintain defense and economy. Do not over-expand.

This path emphasizes faster tech and production to gain an advantage, while maintaining a defensive posture to handle potential early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a tech lead and a stronger army composition by the early-midgame.
