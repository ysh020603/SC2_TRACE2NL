# N006 — Late-Midgame Ground to Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 600 seconds, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. The opponent shows a ground army with marines and marauders, while your own forces are also ground-based. Begin transitioning to an air-oriented composition, increasing your air presence and technology. Continue to strengthen your ground forces as a base.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Begin transitioning to an air-oriented composition, increasing your air presence and technology. Continue to strengthen your ground forces as a base.

Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage if the opponent is heavily invested in ground forces. Air units can provide mobility and harassment options.
