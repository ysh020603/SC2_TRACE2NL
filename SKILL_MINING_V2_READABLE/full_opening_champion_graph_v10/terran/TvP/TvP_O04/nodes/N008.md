# N008 — Late Midgame Air and Ground Balance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the opponent has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and Sentries, while you have a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Increase your air presence and add anti-air units such as Vikings or Thors. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy air army with Phoenixes and Sentries, indicating a strong air composition. They have heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Your defense is heavy, and you have moderate air presence. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and add anti-air units such as Vikings or Thors. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your defensive posture.

The opponent's air army is a significant threat, so you need to invest in anti-air capabilities. Vikings can provide good air superiority, while Thors can also help.
