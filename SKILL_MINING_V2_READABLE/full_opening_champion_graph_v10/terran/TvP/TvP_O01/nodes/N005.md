# N005 — Early-Mid Ground Defense with Siege Tanks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks and Marines. Enemy is ground-oriented with Zealot/Stalker/Observer cues. Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to develop your ground army. Increase economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with heavy production and tech, including Zealot, Stalker, and Observer cues. Their defense is light or uncertain, but they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks and Marines. Defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture while continuing to develop your ground army. Increase economy and tech.

Siege Tanks provide strong defensive capabilities against ground pushes. Maintaining defense while expanding gives you a safe economy.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Maintain defense while developing

**When this applies:** Early-midgame, around 360 seconds, with ground-oriented posture and enemy ground cues.

**Mistake:** Continuing to expand and tech without reinforcing the army, assuming the enemy is passive.

**Correction:** Maintain defensive posture while continuing to develop ground army. Increase economy and tech.

**Why:** Siege Tanks provide strong defensive capabilities against ground pushes. Maintaining defense while expanding gives a safe economy.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when enemy tech direction becomes clearer or when you have a sufficient army to push.
