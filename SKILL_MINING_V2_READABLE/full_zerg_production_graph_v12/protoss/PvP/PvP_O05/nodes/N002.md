Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

Immortals are strong against the Zealot-heavy ground compositions common in PvP. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid neglecting detection or anti-air when both sides are heavy ground

**When this applies:** Midgame, both sides have heavy ground armies and heavy tech. Own army includes Stalkers and Immortals, opponent shows Zealot cues.

**Mistake:** Continuing to strengthen ground and expand without ensuring detection or anti-air, risking a surprise transition to air units like Oracles or Void Rays.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening your ground army, adding more Immortals and supporting units. Keep expanding and increasing your economy.

**Why:** Immortals are strong against Zealot-heavy ground compositions common in PvP. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of transitioning to air or if you face a timing attack.
