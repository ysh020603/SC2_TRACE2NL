# N003 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with both sides having heavy economy and technology, but no clear army composition yet. No reliable combat-unit cues from the opponent. Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition. Continue expanding and increasing production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are heavy, and you are expanding well. Production is moderate, and your army composition is still undefined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition. Continue expanding and increasing production.

In the early game, investing in tech and economy sets up a strong midgame. Keeping options open allows you to react to the opponent's first moves.
