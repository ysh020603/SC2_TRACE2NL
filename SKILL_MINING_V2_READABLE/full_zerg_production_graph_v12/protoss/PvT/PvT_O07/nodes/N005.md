# N005 — Early Game Ground Posture with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have an unknown army composition, but the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Your economy is heavy, and you are expanding. Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Consider scouting to confirm the opponent's build.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Their economy is heavy, technology is heavy, and production is moderate. They may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, but you have a heavy economy and technology. Your production is moderate, and your expansion is heavy. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Consider scouting to confirm the opponent's build.

The opponent's ground posture with Marines and Marauders suggests a bio-based army. You need to prepare for that with appropriate units and upgrades.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Prepare for bio-based army while expanding

**When this applies:** Early game around 180 seconds, when you have an unknown army composition, heavy economy, and are expanding, while the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to defense at the expense of economy and neglecting scouting for tech switches or all-ins.

**Correction:** Focus on increasing production and technology while maintaining a safe economy. Scout to confirm the opponent's build.

**Why:** The opponent's ground posture with Marines and Marauders suggests a bio-based army. You need to prepare for that with appropriate units and upgrades.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have more information about the opponent's army composition or if you face early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a clearer army composition based on scouting, while continuing to grow your economy and technology.
