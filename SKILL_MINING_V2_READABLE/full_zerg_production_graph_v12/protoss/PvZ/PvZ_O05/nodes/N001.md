# N001 — Early Tech and Defense Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army composition unknown, heavy technology investment, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light technology. Strengthen ground forces, increase defense, continue economy growth, and increase technology. Maintain air and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. They are expanding heavily and may apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined, but you have invested heavily in technology and defense. Economy is strong, but expansion is uncertain. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase defense, continue economy growth, and increase technology. Maintain air and expansion.

With an unknown own army and a possible early Zergling pressure, prioritizing defense and ground strength while teching up is a safe way to transition into a stronger midgame.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Avoid over-committing to air or neglecting defense

**When this applies:** Early game, own army composition unknown, heavy technology investment, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, light technology.

**Mistake:** Tempting to over-commit to air or neglect defense, or expand too greedily without scouting.

**Correction:** Strengthen ground forces, increase defense, continue economy growth, and increase technology. Maintain air and expansion.

**Why:** With an unknown own army and possible early Zergling pressure, prioritizing defense and ground strength while teching up is a safe way to transition into a stronger midgame.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when your army composition becomes clearer or if the opponent shows signs of tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a defined army composition and a solid economy.
