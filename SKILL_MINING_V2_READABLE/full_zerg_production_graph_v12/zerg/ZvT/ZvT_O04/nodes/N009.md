# N009 — Midgame Ground and Air Mix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground with Marines, Hellions, Medivacs, own ground with Zerglings, Queens, moderate tech. Maintain ground strength, continue economy, consider air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Marines, Hellions, Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech, ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength, continue economy, consider air.

Prepare for Medivac drops and ground pushes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to late-midgame with a balanced army.
