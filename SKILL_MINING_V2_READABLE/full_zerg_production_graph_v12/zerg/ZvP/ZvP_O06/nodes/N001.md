# N001 — Early Economy and Expansion Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, around 3-4 minutes, with a heavy economy and expansion posture. Opponent is unknown or light, with heavy tech investment. Continue developing your economy and expansion. Maintain a ground army core and keep scouting to detect tech or air transitions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but they appear to be investing heavily in technology. No clear army composition yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, but production is moderate and tech is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion. Maintain a ground army core and keep scouting to detect tech or air transitions.

A strong economy allows you to outproduce the opponent later. Maintaining a ground army provides safety against early pressure while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy with multiple bases, then transition into a tech choice based on opponent's composition.

## Possible Next Situations

### N002 — Mid-Game Ground Army Strengthening

**Situation:** Around 5 minutes, opponent shows a ground-based army with Zealots and Stalkers. Your economy is strong, and you are expanding.

**Direction:** Increase your ground army production and continue teching. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks to counter the ground composition.
