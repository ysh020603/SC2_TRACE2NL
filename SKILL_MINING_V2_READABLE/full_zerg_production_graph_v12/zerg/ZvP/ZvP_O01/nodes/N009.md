# N009 — Late-Mid Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with enemy having a heavy air presence including Phoenixes and Oracles. Consider transitioning to air units like Mutalisks or Corruptors to counter the enemy's air. Continue expanding and teching. Maintain ground defense as well.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy air army with Phoenixes and Oracles, and is producing heavily. They may be planning a mass air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Your tech is heavy, and you are considering air options.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider transitioning to air units like Mutalisks or Corruptors to counter the enemy's air. Continue expanding and teching. Maintain ground defense as well.

Air units can counter the enemy's air and provide harassment. A mixed army with anti-air capabilities is essential.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Transition to Air

**When this applies:** Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, when enemy has heavy air presence including Phoenixes and Oracles.

**Mistake:** Staying pure ground against heavy air.

**Correction:** Transition to air units like Mutalisks or Corruptors to counter enemy air. Continue expanding and teching. Maintain ground defense.

**Why:** Air units can counter enemy air and provide harassment. Mixed army with anti-air is essential.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have sufficient air force or enemy switches back to ground.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a balanced army with strong anti-air capabilities.
