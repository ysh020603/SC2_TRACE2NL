# N002 — Early Game Queen Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own Queen, opponent unknown but heavy tech. Increase economy and expansions, continue ground development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture, heavy tech investment, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with Queen, moderate economy, heavy expansion, light tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansions, continue ground development.

Queens provide defense and creep spread, supporting a macro-oriented start.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Avoid neglecting scouting and over-investing in tech

**When this applies:** Early game, own Queen, opponent unknown but heavy tech.

**Mistake:** Neglecting scouting and over-investing in tech before confirming the opponent's plan.

**Correction:** Increase economy and expansions, continue ground development.

**Why:** Queens provide defense and creep spread, supporting a macro-oriented start.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when more information about opponent's army composition is available.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy and map presence.
