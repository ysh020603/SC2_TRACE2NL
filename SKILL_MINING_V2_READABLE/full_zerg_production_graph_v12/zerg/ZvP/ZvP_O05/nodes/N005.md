Maintain ground army, continue economy, consider upgrades.

Infestors provide strong spellcasting against ground-based Protoss armies.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Avoid over-committing to Infestors without scouting for air

**When this applies:** Late midgame, own Zergling/Infestor/Queen/Overseer, opponent ground with Zealots/Stalkers.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to Infestors if the opponent transitions to air, and neglecting scouting to detect tech switches.

**Correction:** Maintain ground army, continue economy, consider upgrades.

**Why:** Infestors provide strong spellcasting against ground-based Protoss armies.

**Recheck when:** Recheck if opponent shows air units or heavy tech.
