# N005 — Early Game Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, enemy shows a heavy economy posture with moderate production and heavy tech investment, but no confirmed combat units. Maintain your development, focus on economy and scouting to determine the enemy's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is investing heavily in economy and tech, possibly preparing for a late-game composition or a fast tech rush.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own economy and tech are still light, but you have room to expand and develop.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your development, focus on economy and scouting to determine the enemy's tech path.

With the enemy teching, you need to keep up economically while preparing a response to whatever they are building.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Expand and tech up while scouting to identify the enemy's plan.
