# N007 — Late-Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, the game is in the late-midgame. Enemy intelligence shows an air presence with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Corruptors, and Queens, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your own army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. Increase your defensive capabilities, particularly against air. Continue to develop your economy and production, but prioritize defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and heavy technology. Their army includes air units like Mutalisks and Corruptors, indicating a potential air threat.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. You are in a defensive posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defensive capabilities, particularly against air. Continue to develop your economy and production, but prioritize defense.

The enemy's air presence poses a threat to your ground army. Strengthening your defenses and preparing anti-air measures will help you withstand an attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defensive posture with anti-air capabilities, while maintaining your economy.
