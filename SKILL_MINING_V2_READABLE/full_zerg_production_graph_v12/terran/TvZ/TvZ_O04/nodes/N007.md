# N007 — Early Game Marine and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a Marine and heavy production, with a moderate economy. The opponent has Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and light tech. Increase your economy and maintain your production and tech. Keep your army strong and be ready to defend.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. They are likely to be expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and heavy production, with a Marine. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early game. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and maintain your production and tech. Keep your army strong and be ready to defend.

Your heavy production and tech will give you an advantage. Maintaining your economy will allow you to outscale the Zerg.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Early Game: Avoid Delaying Expansion

**When this applies:** Around 3 minutes, with a Marine and heavy production, moderate economy, against Zerglings and Queens with moderate production and light tech.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to early pressure if the Zerg has a strong defense, delaying expansion or production.

**Correction:** Increase economy and maintain production and tech. Keep the army strong and be ready to defend.

**Why:** Heavy production and tech give an advantage; maintaining economy allows outscaling the Zerg.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have scouted the opponent's expansion or if you see signs of an early attack.
