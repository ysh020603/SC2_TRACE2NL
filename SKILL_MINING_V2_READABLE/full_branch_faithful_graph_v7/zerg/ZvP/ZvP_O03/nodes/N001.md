## Summary

Maintain your current expansion and production, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots and Sentries. Their production and technology appear heavy, and they have a heavy economy and expansions.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while increasing your economy. Maintain your current expansion and production, and continue technology development.

The opponent's ground-heavy composition with Sentries suggests a potential for force-field-based engagements. Strengthening your ground forces and economy will help you sustain a longer game and tech into counters.
