# N004 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame around 540 seconds, with the opponent showing a heavy air presence including Phoenixes. You have a strong ground army and are considering air. Increase your air presence to counter the Phoenixes. Consider adding Mutalisks or Corruptors, while maintaining your ground army for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes, supported by Zealots and Stalkers. Their production is heavy and technology is heavy, indicating a committed air strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your ground army is strong with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. Production and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence to counter the Phoenixes. Consider adding Mutalisks or Corruptors, while maintaining your ground army for defense.

Phoenixes can harass and pick off workers. By adding air units, you can contest the air and protect your economy. Your strong ground army can still defend against ground pushes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army with both ground and air capabilities to handle the opponent's composition.
