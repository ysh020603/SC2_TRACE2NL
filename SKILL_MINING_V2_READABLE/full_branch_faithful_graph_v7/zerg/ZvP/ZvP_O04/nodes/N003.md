## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Detection and Composition vs Dark Templar

**When this applies:** Midgame around 480 seconds, with both players having ground armies. Opponent shows Stalkers, Dark Templars, Warp Prisms, and Observers.

**Mistake:** Neglecting detection and overcommitting to a single unit type, leaving your army vulnerable to Dark Templar harassment and lacking counters to Stalkers.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain economy. Add detection (e.g., Overseers) and mix in units that counter Stalkers and Dark Templars (e.g., Banelings).

**Why:** Dark Templars require detection to be fought effectively; a mixed composition with detection prevents devastating losses and handles multiple threats.

**Recheck when:** Recheck if the opponent adds air units or if you need to adjust your composition.
