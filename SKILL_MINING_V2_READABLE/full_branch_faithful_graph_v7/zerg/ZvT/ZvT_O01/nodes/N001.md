# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Expansion

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, both sides have heavy economy and expansion. Opponent shows ground posture with possible Marine/Reaper cues. Own army is Zergling/Queen. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue technology, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Marine and Reaper cues. Production is moderate, technology heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Economy and expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue technology, maintain defense.

This node represents a positive outcome where your macro is on track. Continuing to expand and tech while maintaining a ground army sets up a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with more tech, while keeping economy heavy.
