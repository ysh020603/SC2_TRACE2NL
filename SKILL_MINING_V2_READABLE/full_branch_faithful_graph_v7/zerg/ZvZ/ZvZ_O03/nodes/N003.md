# N003 — Early Game Economy and Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the game is in early game. Both sides have heavy economy and expansion, but defense is light or uncertain. Your army is minimal (Queen only), while the opponent shows Zergling and Queen. Focus on economy and tech development while preparing to defend. Maintain production and expansion, but be ready to build defensive units if pressure comes.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and tech, and a small ground army with Queen. Defense is light, so you are vulnerable to early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and tech development while preparing to defend. Maintain production and expansion, but be ready to build defensive units if pressure comes.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech lead. Investing now will pay off, but you must be prepared to defend against early Zergling pressure.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Early Economy vs. Defense

**When this applies:** Early game, around 180 seconds, with both sides expanding and minimal army (Queen only), opponent shows Zergling and Queen.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to economy and tech while neglecting defense, assuming the opponent is passive.

**Correction:** Focus on economy and tech development while preparing to defend. Maintain production and expansion, but be ready to build defensive units if pressure comes.

**Why:** Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech lead. Investing now will pay off, but you must be prepared to defend against early Zergling pressure.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate if the opponent shows signs of early aggression, or if you need to adjust your tech path based on scouting.
