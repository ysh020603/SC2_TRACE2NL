# N004 — Late Midgame Ground Power

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, both players have heavy economies and heavy production. Opponent shows moderate tech and ground posture with Roach cues. Maintain your ground army and economy, continue to strengthen your ground forces, and consider adding upgrades or tech to gain an edge.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, and Queen cues. Their production is heavy, tech moderate, and they are likely to continue a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your ground army is strong with Zergling and Queen, and your defense is solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy, continue to strengthen your ground forces, and consider adding upgrades or tech to gain an edge.

With heavy tech, you can transition to a more powerful composition like Roach or Hydralisk. Maintaining a strong ground army keeps you safe while you tech up.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Late Midgame Ground: Avoid Tech Overcommitment

**When this applies:** Late midgame, both players have heavy economies and heavy production. Opponent shows moderate tech and ground posture with Roach cues.

**Mistake:** Tempting to over-invest in technology without sufficient army, or neglect scouting for tech switches.

**Correction:** Maintain your ground army and economy, continue to strengthen your ground forces, and consider adding upgrades or tech to gain an edge.

**Why:** With heavy tech, you can transition to a more powerful composition like Roach or Hydralisk. Maintaining a strong ground army keeps you safe while you tech up.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you see the opponent's tech or army composition change, or if you plan to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for a late-game transition, possibly to a more advanced ground composition or to air if needed.
