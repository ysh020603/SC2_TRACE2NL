- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Marines and Widow Mines. Their economy and production are heavy, and they are expanding. Their defense is moderate, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Stalkers. Your technology is heavy, and you are expanding. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you may face possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your economy and expansion pace, and keep defense at a moderate level.

Your heavy economy and technology investment support a strong ground composition. The opponent's ground posture with Widow Mines suggests a need for detection and careful engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
