# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Enemy intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealot/Stalker cues, heavy production, and heavy tech. Continue developing your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain defense and tech progression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy appears ground-oriented with Zealot/Stalker cues, heavy production, and heavy tech. Their defense is light or uncertain, but they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground macro posture with moderate production, heavy economy, and heavy tech. Defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army while increasing economy and expansions. Maintain defense and tech progression.

Both sides are investing heavily in tech and economy. Strengthening your ground forces while expanding gives you a solid foundation to respond to enemy tech choices.
