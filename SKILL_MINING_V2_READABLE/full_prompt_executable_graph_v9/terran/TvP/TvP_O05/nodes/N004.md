# N004 — Early Game Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a ground army of Marines and Marauders, with a heavy economy. The opponent's posture is unknown but they have a heavy economy. Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's posture, but they have a heavy economy and technology. They may have Zealots and Stalkers.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is light, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

With a ground army already established, it is efficient to continue producing units and expanding your economy to support a strong mid-game.

**Correction:** Increase your production and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.
