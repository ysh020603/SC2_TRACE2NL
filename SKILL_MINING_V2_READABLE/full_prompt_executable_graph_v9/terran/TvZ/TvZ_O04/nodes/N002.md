# N002 — Early Game Reaper Pressure and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a Reaper and heavy production, while the opponent is still in the early game with no reliable combat units seen. Your economy is heavy but your expansion is uncertain. Increase your economy and expansion, while continuing to produce units and tech. Use the Reaper for scouting and harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the early game with a ground macro posture, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. No reliable combat units have been observed, so they are likely still building up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper and heavy production, with a heavy economy. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early game. You are looking to expand and apply early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion, while continuing to produce units and tech. Use the Reaper for scouting and harassment.

Your early Reaper and heavy production allow you to pressure the Zerg while you expand. This can delay their economy and give you a lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
