# N007 — Early Economy and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, heavy defense. Opponent ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech. Increase economy and production, strengthen ground forces, maintain defense and tech. Do not over-expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is undefined, but you have a moderate economy and heavy technology. Defense is heavy, and expansion is solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and production, strengthen ground forces, maintain defense and tech. Do not over-expand.

With a moderate economy, focusing on economic growth and production while maintaining defense is a safe way to transition into a stronger midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a stronger economy and army.
