# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 4 minutes, both players are in early-midgame with heavy economy and technology, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalker cues. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology, but your expansion is light or uncertain. You have Stalkers and are developing.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and continue production.

The opponent is ground-focused, so reinforcing your ground army and expanding your economy will set up a strong midgame. Heavy technology investment supports future transitions.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L01 — Ground Commitment vs. Balanced Expansion

**When this applies:** Early-midgame (around 4 minutes) when both players have heavy economy and tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers.

**Mistake:** Over-committing to a single ground tech path without scouting, potentially neglecting defense against possible pressure.

**Correction:** Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and continue production.

**Why:** The opponent is ground-focused, so reinforcing your ground army and expanding your economy will set up a strong midgame. Heavy technology investment supports future transitions.

**Recheck when:** Re-evaluate when the opponent's army composition or posture changes, or when you reach a new tech tier.
