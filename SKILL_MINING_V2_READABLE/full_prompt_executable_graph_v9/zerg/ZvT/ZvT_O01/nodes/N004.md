# N004 — Early Macro with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, opponent posture unknown (no reliable combat-unit cue), but economy heavy. Own Zergling/Queen. Strengthen ground, increase economy and expansions, continue tech, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, no reliable combat-unit cue. Production moderate, tech heavy, pressure unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, heavy economy and expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy and expansions, continue tech, maintain defense.

When opponent is unknown, it's safe to continue macro while keeping scouting to detect any tech or all-in.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a standard ground macro once opponent's posture is revealed.
