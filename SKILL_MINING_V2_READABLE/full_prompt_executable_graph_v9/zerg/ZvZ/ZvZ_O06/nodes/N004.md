# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and moderate technology. You have a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and heavy production and moderate technology. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your heavy production. Consider increasing your technology to get better units like Roaches or Hydralisks. Keep your defense strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy production and moderate technology. They may be preparing to attack or expand further.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the late-midgame with a ground-oriented posture, heavy economy, and heavy production. You have Zerglings and Queens, and your technology is moderate. Your defense is moderate, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your heavy production. Consider increasing your technology to get better units like Roaches or Hydralisks. Keep your defense strong.

With both players having heavy ground armies, you need to ensure your composition is efficient. Adding Roaches can give you more durability, while Hydralisks provide anti-air and damage. Maintaining a heavy economy allows you to sustain a large army.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L03 — Maintain ground strength and tech for efficiency

**When this applies:** Late-midgame, both have heavy ground armies (Zergling, Roach, Queen) with heavy production and moderate tech.

**Mistake:** Falling into a pure ground vs ground fight without considering composition advantage, risking inefficiency.

**Correction:** Continue strengthening your ground army, maintain heavy production, and consider increasing technology to get Roaches or Hydralisks. Keep defense strong.

**Why:** Adding Roaches gives durability, Hydralisks provide anti-air and damage. Heavy economy sustains a large army.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when you have a strong army and economy. If opponent transitions to air, add anti-air or switch to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the late game with a powerful ground army and heavy production, ready to either finish the game or transition to air if the opponent does.
