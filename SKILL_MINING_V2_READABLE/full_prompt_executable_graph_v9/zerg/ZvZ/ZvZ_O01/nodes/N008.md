# N008 — Midgame Ground with Air Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground with heavy air presence, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech. Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen, Overseer cues. Increase defense, economy, continue production and tech, strengthen ground.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy shows Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen, Overseer cues, ground posture with heavy air presence, heavy economy, heavy production, moderate tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in midgame with ground macro posture, heavy economy, heavy production, heavy tech. Defense moderate, expansion heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, economy, continue production and tech, strengthen ground.

With opponent having air presence, you need to bolster defenses and possibly tech to anti-air while maintaining economy.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
