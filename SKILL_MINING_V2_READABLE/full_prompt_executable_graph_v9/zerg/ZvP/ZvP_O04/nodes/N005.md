# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame around 300 seconds, with the opponent having a ground posture but no combat units observed. You have Zerglings and Queens. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain production and consider increasing technology to match the opponent's heavy investment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and is expanding. Their production is moderate, technology heavy, but army composition is unknown. No combat units have been observed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and you are expanding. You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain production and consider increasing technology to match the opponent's heavy investment.

The opponent's heavy tech suggests they may be teching to a specific unit. By building a strong economy and ground army, you can adapt to whatever they produce.

## Contrastive Mistake → Correction

### L02 — Scouting and Tech Adaptation

**When this applies:** Early-midgame around 300 seconds, with the opponent having a ground posture but no combat units observed. You have Zerglings and Queens.

**Mistake:** Overcommitting to a single unit composition and neglecting scouting, leaving you unprepared for the opponent's heavy tech investment.

**Correction:** Continue developing your ground army and economy. Maintain production and consider increasing technology to match the opponent's heavy investment.

**Why:** The opponent's heavy tech suggests they may be teching to a specific unit; a strong economy and ground army allow you to adapt to whatever they produce.

**Recheck when:** Recheck when the opponent's first combat units are observed or when you reach a new tech milestone.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once the opponent's tech direction becomes clearer.
