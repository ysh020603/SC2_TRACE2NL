## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, enemy has a heavy air army with Carriers, Void Rays, and Oracles, while you have a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Void Rays, and Observers. Maintain ground forces, increase defense, increase economy, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with an air army including Stalkers, Carriers, Void Rays, and Oracles. Their defense is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Stalkers, Sentries, Void Rays, and Observers. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground forces, increase defense, increase economy, continue production and technology.

The enemy has a strong air army, so you need to build a robust defense and possibly transition to more anti-air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong defense and transition to a composition that can counter the enemy's air.
