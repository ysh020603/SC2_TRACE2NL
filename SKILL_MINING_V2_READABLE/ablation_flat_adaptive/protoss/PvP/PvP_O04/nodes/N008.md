# N008 — Midgame Ground vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, enemy has a heavy air army with Carriers, Void Rays, and Oracles, while you have a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Maintain ground forces, increase economy, maintain expansion and production, continue technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with an air army including Stalkers, Carriers, Void Rays, and Oracles. Their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground forces, increase economy, maintain expansion and production, continue technology.

The enemy has a strong air army, so you need to build anti-air units and possibly transition to air yourself.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a composition that can counter the enemy's air army, such as adding Stalkers and possibly transitioning to Phoenixes or Void Rays.
