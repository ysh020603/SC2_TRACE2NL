# N003 — Late-Mid Ground Stability

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, both players have heavy economy and production, with ground armies. Enemy has moderate defense and possible pressure. Maintain ground forces, increase economy, maintain expansion and production, continue technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Their defense is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms, and Observers. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground forces, increase economy, maintain expansion and production, continue technology.

The game is in a stable macro state. Continuing to build economy and army will set up for a decisive engagement.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong late-game composition while maintaining economy.
