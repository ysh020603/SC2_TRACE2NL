# N004 — Early-Mid Mixed Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, enemy has a ground army with Stalkers, while you have a mixed army with Stalkers and Oracles. Your air presence is moderate. Increase air presence, strengthen air forces, continue production and technology, maintain expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Stalkers. Their expansion is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a mixed army including Stalkers and Oracles. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air forces, continue production and technology, maintain expansion.

Adding Oracles gives you harassment and detection options. Increasing air presence can put pressure on the enemy's ground army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air superiority or use Oracles for harassment while maintaining a solid ground base.
