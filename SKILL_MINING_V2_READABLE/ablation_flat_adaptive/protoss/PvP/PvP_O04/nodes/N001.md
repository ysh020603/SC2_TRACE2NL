# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, both players have heavy economy and production, with ground-oriented armies. Enemy has light air presence and possible pressure. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and expansion, continue production and technology, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Stalkers. Their expansion is heavy, and they have heavy technology investment. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Stalkers. Your expansion is light or uncertain, and you have heavy technology investment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and expansion, continue production and technology, maintain defense.

Both players are on similar ground macro paths. Increasing economy and expansion will provide a long-term advantage, while maintaining a solid ground army deters early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and expand to secure a macro lead.
