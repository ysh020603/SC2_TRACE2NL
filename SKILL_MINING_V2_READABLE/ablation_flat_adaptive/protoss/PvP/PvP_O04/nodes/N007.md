# N007 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, enemy has an unknown army composition with heavy defense, while you have a small army with Stalkers. Your economy is heavy. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and production, continue technology, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a moderate economy and heavy defense, with an unknown army composition. Their expansion is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with a small army including Stalkers. Your expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and production, continue technology, maintain defense.

The enemy is defensive, so you can focus on building your economy and army to prepare for a midgame push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong economy and army to break the enemy's defense.
