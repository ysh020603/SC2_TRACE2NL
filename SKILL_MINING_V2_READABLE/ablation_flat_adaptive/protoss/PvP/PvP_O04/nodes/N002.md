# N002 — Midgame Ground Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economy and production, with ground armies including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Enemy has light air presence. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, maintain expansion and production, continue technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Stalkers, Sentries, Observers, and Immortals. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase economy, maintain expansion and production, continue technology.

The enemy is likely to continue with ground forces. Maintaining a strong ground army and economy will keep you competitive.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army and economy while preparing for potential tech switches.
