# N005 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players have unknown army compositions, with heavy economy and technology investment. Enemy has light air presence. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and technology investment, with an unknown army composition. Their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with a small army including Zealots and Stalkers. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, continue production and technology.

With both players on similar tech paths, building a solid ground army and economy will provide a stable foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a strong economy and tech base while scouting for enemy intentions.
