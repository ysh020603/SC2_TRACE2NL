# PvP_O04 Technology / Economy / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O04
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / economy / upgrade opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss mirror opening that prioritizes heavy economy, technology, and upgrades while keeping production moderate early on. The goal is to establish a strong macro foundation and tech lead, then adapt to the opponent's composition.

Develop a technology / economy / upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, both players have heavy economy and production, with ground-oriented armies. Enemy has light air presence and possible pressure.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen ground forces, increase economy and expansion, continue production and technology, maintain defense.
