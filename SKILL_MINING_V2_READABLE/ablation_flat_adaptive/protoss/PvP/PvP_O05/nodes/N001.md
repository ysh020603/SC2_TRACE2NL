# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and light defensive posture. Enemy intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Stalker presence, but exact composition is uncertain. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defensive and expansion posture, but be ready to adapt if scouting reveals a different enemy composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and you have invested in technology. Production is moderate, and your army composition is not yet clearly defined, though Stalkers may be present. Defenses are light, and you have room to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current defensive and expansion posture, but be ready to adapt if scouting reveals a different enemy composition.

With a heavy economy and technology lead, you can afford to invest in a strong ground army to secure map control and prepare for a midgame transition. Maintaining flexibility is key because the enemy's exact composition is still uncertain.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting information, while continuing to expand and tech up.
