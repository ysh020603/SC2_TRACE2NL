# N010 — Early-Midgame Tech Focus with Unknown Enemy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and no clear army composition. Enemy posture is unknown, but they have heavy defenses and economy. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Scout to determine the enemy's direction and prepare to adapt your army composition accordingly.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are focusing on economy and technology, with moderate production. Your army is not yet defined, and you have not committed to a specific composition. Defenses are light, and you have expanded aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Scout to determine the enemy's direction and prepare to adapt your army composition accordingly.

In the early-midgame, it is important to establish a strong economic and technological foundation. By staying flexible, you can react to the enemy's composition without wasting resources.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting information, while continuing to expand and tech up.
