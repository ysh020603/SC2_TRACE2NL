# N001 — Early Game Uncertainty

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. Enemy posture is unknown, and your own build is still developing. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on economy and technology while keeping options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy intelligence is limited; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Their production and technology are also uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on economy and technology while keeping options open.

Early game is about establishing a solid foundation. Overcommitting to a specific strategy without information can be punished.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and technology base to enable flexible mid-game transitions.
