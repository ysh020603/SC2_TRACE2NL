# N005 — Early Game Tech Advantage

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, you have a technology advantage over the enemy. Your economy is moderate, and you have invested heavily in tech. Strengthen your ground army and increase defense. Continue to develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy economy is heavy, but their production is moderate and technology is heavy. They have no reliable combat-unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have heavy technology investment. Your defense is heavy, but production is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase defense. Continue to develop your economy and technology.

With a tech advantage, you can aim for a powerful mid-game army. Strengthening ground forces and defense helps you survive early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to support a tech-heavy composition.
