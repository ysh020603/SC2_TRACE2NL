# N002 — Late Midgame Uncertainty

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, the situation remains unclear. Both sides have not revealed their full hand. Continue to maintain your current path with safety checks. Avoid overcommitting to a single strategy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy posture is still unknown, with no reliable combat-unit cues. They may be hiding their tech or army composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you have maintained flexibility. Technology is also uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to maintain your current path with safety checks. Avoid overcommitting to a single strategy.

In the late midgame, information is crucial. Staying flexible allows you to react to the enemy's eventual composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare to transition into a specific composition once the enemy's intentions become clearer.
