# N003 — Early Midgame Flexibility

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, you have multiple possible paths. The enemy is still unknown, but you have some flexibility. Maintain your current development path with safety checks. Keep your options open for a potential transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy posture is unknown, with no reliable combat-unit cues. They could be going for any strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain, but you have the ability to transition into different compositions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path with safety checks. Keep your options open for a potential transition.

The early midgame is a critical point where you can still adapt to the enemy's strategy. Overcommitting now could be costly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a point where you can commit to a specific composition based on scouting.
