# N008 — Late Midgame Ground Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both you and the enemy have strong ground armies with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. Their economy is heavy, and they have heavy technology investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have heavy technology investment. Your production is heavy, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your defense.

Both sides have similar ground armies. You need to maintain your tech advantage and possibly transition to a more advanced composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a superior ground army or transition to a mixed composition to gain an edge.
