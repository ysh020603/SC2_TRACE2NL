# N012 — Midgame Ground Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the enemy has a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers. You have a tech advantage and heavy economy. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Their economy is heavy, and they have heavy technology investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your defense.

The enemy's mixed posture suggests they may be vulnerable to ground attacks. Strengthening your ground army can give you an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army to exploit the enemy's mixed composition.
