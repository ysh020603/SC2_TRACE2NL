# N009 — Early Midgame Tech Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, you have a tech advantage and a moderate economy. The enemy has a heavy economy and heavy tech investment. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and heavy technology investment. Their production is moderate, and they have a moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have a moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain your defense.

The enemy is investing heavily in tech and economy. You need to keep up while building a strong army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to support your tech advantage.
