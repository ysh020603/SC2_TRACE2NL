# N007 — Midgame Ground Confrontation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the enemy has a ground posture with Zealots. You have a tech advantage and moderate economy, but your defense is heavy. Strengthen your ground army and increase defense. Continue to develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Zealots. Their economy is heavy, and they have heavy technology investment. Production is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase defense. Continue to develop your economy and technology.

The enemy is investing heavily in ground forces. You need to match their army strength while maintaining your tech advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and defense to withstand the enemy's push.
