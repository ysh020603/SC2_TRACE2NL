# N010 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent has a ground army with Zealots, own army is air-oriented with Stalkers and Oracles. Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your air army, while maintaining defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and an air army with Stalkers and Oracles. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your air army, while maintaining defense and economy.

Your air army can harass the opponent's ground forces and economy, giving you an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air superiority and use it to pressure the opponent.
