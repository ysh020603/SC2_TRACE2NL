# N009 — Late-Midgame Air Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has an air army with Void Rays and Oracles, own army is air-heavy with Void Rays. Continue to increase your air presence and maintain pressure on the opponent, while keeping your economy strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and a strong air army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Void Rays. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to increase your air presence and maintain pressure on the opponent, while keeping your economy strong.

With a superior air army, you can force the opponent to react and potentially make mistakes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use your air dominance to secure expansions and deny the opponent's.
