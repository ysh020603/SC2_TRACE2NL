## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, own army is ground-oriented with Immortals. Continue to strengthen your ground army, focusing on units that counter the opponent's composition, such as Immortals against Stalkers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and a strong ground army with Zealots and Immortals. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, focusing on units that counter the opponent's composition, such as Immortals against Stalkers.

A ground army with Immortals is effective against Stalker-heavy compositions, and you can maintain a strong defensive position.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
