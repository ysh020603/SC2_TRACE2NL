# N003 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles, own army is air-oriented with Void Rays. Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your air army, while maintaining defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and a strong air army with Void Rays. Production is heavy, and you are investing in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your air army, while maintaining defense and economy.

Your air army can exploit the opponent's lack of anti-air, and Void Rays are strong against ground units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air superiority and use it to pressure the opponent's economy or force a tech switch.
