# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Adaptation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent shows ground posture with Zealots, own posture still unknown but economy and defense are strong. Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's ground presence, while continuing to increase economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Zealots, heavy production, and heavy technology investment. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, defense is heavy, and you have a strong technology base. Production is moderate, and you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's ground presence, while continuing to increase economy and technology.

Since the opponent is ground-oriented, having a solid ground force prevents being caught off guard and allows you to defend or counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile ground army that can hold against early pressure and transition to a stronger composition.
