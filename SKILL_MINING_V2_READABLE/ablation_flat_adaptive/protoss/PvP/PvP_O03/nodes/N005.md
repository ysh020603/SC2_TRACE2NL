# N005 — Early-Midgame Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent posture unknown, own posture unknown but economy is moderate and defense is heavy. Focus on increasing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. No reliable combat-unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, defense is heavy, and you are investing in technology. Production is moderate, and you have not expanded much.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture.

With limited information, building a strong economy and technology base gives you flexibility to adapt later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger economic position to support a more defined army composition.
