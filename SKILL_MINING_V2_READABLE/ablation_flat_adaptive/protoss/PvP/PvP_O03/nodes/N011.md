# N011 — Midgame Ground vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has an air army with Void Rays and Oracles, own army is ground-oriented with Immortals and Oracles. Maintain your ground army and consider adding anti-air units to deal with the opponent's air threat.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and a ground army with Zealots, Warp Prisms, Immortals, and Oracles. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and consider adding anti-air units to deal with the opponent's air threat.

Your ground army is strong, but you need to be able to defend against Void Rays. Adding Stalkers or Archons can help.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Create a balanced army that can handle both ground and air threats.
