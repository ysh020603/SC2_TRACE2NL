# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, own army is ground-only. Maintain and strengthen your ground army, focusing on defensive positioning and countering the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and a ground army with Zealots. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain and strengthen your ground army, focusing on defensive positioning and countering the opponent's composition.

A strong ground defense can hold against the opponent's ground push, and you can look for opportunities to counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Hold your ground and potentially transition to a more aggressive posture if the opportunity arises.
