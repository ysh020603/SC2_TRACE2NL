# N008 — Midgame Air Mirror

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has an air army with Void Rays and Oracles, own army is air-oriented with Void Rays. Increase your air presence and focus on out-producing the opponent in air units, while maintaining defense and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and an air army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Void Rays. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and focus on out-producing the opponent in air units, while maintaining defense and economy.

In an air mirror, the player with more Void Rays and better upgrades will likely win engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve air superiority and use it to control the map.
