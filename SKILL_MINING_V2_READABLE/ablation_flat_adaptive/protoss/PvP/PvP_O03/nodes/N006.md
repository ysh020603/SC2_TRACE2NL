# N006 — Late-Midgame Mixed Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, own army is ground with some air support. Increase your air presence to complement your ground army, focusing on Void Rays to deal with the opponent's ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles. Heavy production and technology, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy defense, and a mixed army with Zealots, Void Rays, and Immortals. Production is heavy, technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence to complement your ground army, focusing on Void Rays to deal with the opponent's ground forces.

Adding air units like Void Rays gives you an advantage against ground-heavy compositions, as they can attack from a safe distance.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Create a versatile army that can handle both ground and potential air threats.
