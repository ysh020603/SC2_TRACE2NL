# PvP_O01 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss versus Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while maintaining moderate production. The early game is flexible, with the option to transition into either a ground or air composition based on scouting information.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. Opponent expansion is heavy, and pressure is possible.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy. Maintain your current expansion and production. Consider adding defensive structures if pressure seems imminent.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [POSITIVE] N002 — Ground Macro with Stalker Presence

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers, and your own army is ground-oriented with Zealots. Both have heavy production and technology.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army by adding Stalkers or other tech units. Increase your economy and consider expanding to match the opponent's economy. Maintain production and technology development.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [NEGATIVE] N003 — Ground Macro with Expansion Risk

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, both players have a ground macro posture with heavy tech. Your expansion is light, while the opponent is expanding heavily.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid expanding too greedily without defensive measures, as the opponent may punish you. Do not neglect army production while expanding.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [POSITIVE] N004 — Stabilize and Develop Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers, and you have a ground army with Zealots. Your expansion is light, but you have heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Stabilize your position by adding defensive structures and tech units like Stalkers. Increase production to support a larger army. Once stable, consider expanding.

**Read for details:** `N004`
