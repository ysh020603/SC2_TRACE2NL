# N008 — Early-Mid Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, opponent has an air posture with Void Rays, while you have a ground army with Zealots. Your expansion is heavy. Consider transitioning to an air army to counter the opponent's Void Rays. Add Stalkers or other anti-air units to defend. Continue developing your economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Void Rays, indicating an air transition. They are expanding heavily and have heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is ground-based, but you have the infrastructure to transition to air. Your expansion is heavy, giving you a solid economic base.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider transitioning to an air army to counter the opponent's Void Rays. Add Stalkers or other anti-air units to defend. Continue developing your economy and production.

The opponent's Void Rays are a threat to your ground army. By transitioning to air or adding anti-air, you can counter their composition and maintain map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a composition that can handle the opponent's air threat while maintaining a strong economy.
