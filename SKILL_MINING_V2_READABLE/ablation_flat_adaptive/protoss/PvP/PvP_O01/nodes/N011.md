# N011 — Midgame Air vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. You have an air army with Stalkers and Carriers. Continue building your air army and add more Carriers. Maintain your economy and production. Consider adding more Stalkers for anti-air defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals, indicating a strong ground composition. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is air-based with Carriers, which are powerful but require time to build. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building your air army and add more Carriers. Maintain your economy and production. Consider adding more Stalkers for anti-air defense.

Your Carriers are a powerful late-game unit, but they need protection. By building a strong air army, you can overpower the opponent's ground forces.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful air army that can crush the opponent's ground composition.
