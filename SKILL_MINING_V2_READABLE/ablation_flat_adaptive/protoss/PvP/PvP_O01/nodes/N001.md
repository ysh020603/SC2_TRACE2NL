# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, both players have a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy production and technology. Opponent expansion is heavy, and pressure is possible. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy. Maintain your current expansion and production. Consider adding defensive structures if pressure seems imminent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots. They are expanding heavily and investing in technology. Their production is heavy, but exact unit counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are developing a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Your economy is strong, and you have a solid base of Zealots. Defense is light, so be cautious of early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy. Maintain your current expansion and production. Consider adding defensive structures if pressure seems imminent.

Both players are in a similar macro-oriented state. Maintaining a strong economy and production will allow you to outscale if the opponent makes a mistake. Heavy technology investment suggests a potential tech switch later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable mid-game position with a strong economy and a versatile army composition.
