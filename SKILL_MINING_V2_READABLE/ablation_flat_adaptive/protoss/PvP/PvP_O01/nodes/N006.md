# N006 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players have an unknown army posture with heavy technology investment. Production is moderate, and expansion is light. Continue developing your technology and economy. Increase production to prepare for the mid-game. Maintain scouting to determine the opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but they are investing heavily in technology. Their production is moderate, and they have not shown any combat units yet.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a focus on technology. Your economy is strong, but your army is not yet defined. You have the flexibility to transition into any composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy. Increase production to prepare for the mid-game. Maintain scouting to determine the opponent's tech path.

With both players focusing on tech, the game will likely be decided by who can leverage their technology advantage. By maintaining a strong economy, you can support a tech switch if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the mid-game with a clear tech direction and a solid economy.
