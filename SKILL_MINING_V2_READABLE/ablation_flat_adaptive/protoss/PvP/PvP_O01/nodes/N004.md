# N004 — Stabilize and Develop Ground Army

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-mid game, opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers, and you have a ground army with Zealots. Your expansion is light, but you have heavy production and tech. Stabilize your position by adding defensive structures and tech units like Stalkers. Increase production to support a larger army. Once stable, consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Stalkers, indicating a possible aggressive or tech-heavy ground composition. They are expanding moderately.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and production, but your expansion is light. Your army is Zealot-heavy, and you need to stabilize before expanding further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by adding defensive structures and tech units like Stalkers. Increase production to support a larger army. Once stable, consider expanding.

The opponent's Stalker presence suggests they may be preparing for a push. By stabilizing first, you can defend against aggression and then transition to a stronger economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable mid-game position with a solid army and economy, ready to expand.
