# N009 — Midgame Air Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has an air posture with Void Rays, while you have a ground army with Zealots. Your expansion is heavy. Strengthen your ground army with anti-air units like Stalkers or Archons. Consider transitioning to air if you have the tech. Maintain your economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Void Rays, indicating a committed air composition. They are expanding heavily and have heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is ground-based, but you have the resources to add anti-air or transition to air. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army with anti-air units like Stalkers or Archons. Consider transitioning to air if you have the tech. Maintain your economy and production.

The opponent's Void Rays are a significant threat. By adding anti-air, you can defend against their air force and potentially counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army that can handle the opponent's air composition while maintaining a strong economy.
