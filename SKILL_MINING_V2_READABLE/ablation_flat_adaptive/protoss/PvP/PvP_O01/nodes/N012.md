# N012 — Early-Mid Air Mirror

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, both players have an air posture with Void Rays. Your army includes Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles. Continue developing your air army and add more Void Rays. Maintain your economy and production. Consider using Oracles for scouting and harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Void Rays, indicating an air composition. They are expanding heavily and have heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is a mix of ground and air units, with Oracles providing utility. Your expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your air army and add more Void Rays. Maintain your economy and production. Consider using Oracles for scouting and harassment.

In an air mirror, the player with more Void Rays and better positioning will win. By maintaining a strong economy, you can out-produce the opponent.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
