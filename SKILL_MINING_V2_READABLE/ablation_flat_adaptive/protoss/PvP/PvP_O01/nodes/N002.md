# N002 — Ground Macro with Stalker Presence

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-mid game, opponent has a ground posture with Stalkers, and your own army is ground-oriented with Zealots. Both have heavy production and technology. Strengthen your ground army by adding Stalkers or other tech units. Increase your economy and consider expanding to match the opponent's economy. Maintain production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Stalkers, indicating a possible transition to a more mobile or tech-heavy ground army. They are expanding moderately and have heavy production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and heavy production. Your army is primarily Zealots, but you have the infrastructure to add Stalkers or other tech units. Your expansion is light, so consider expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army by adding Stalkers or other tech units. Increase your economy and consider expanding to match the opponent's economy. Maintain production and technology development.

The opponent's Stalker presence suggests they may be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch. By adding Stalkers, you can counter their mobility and maintain map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile ground army that can handle both gateway units and potential tech switches.
