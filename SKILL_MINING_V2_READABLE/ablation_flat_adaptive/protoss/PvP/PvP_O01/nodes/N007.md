# N007 — Early-Mid Tech vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, opponent has an unknown posture with heavy tech, while you have a ground-oriented army with Zealots. Your expansion is light. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your expansion to secure your economy. Maintain scouting to detect the opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but they are investing heavily in technology. They have not shown any combat units, so their composition is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots and heavy production. Your economy is strong, but your expansion is light. You are vulnerable to tech switches.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your expansion to secure your economy. Maintain scouting to detect the opponent's tech path.

The opponent's unknown tech path is a threat. By expanding and maintaining a strong ground army, you can defend against a variety of compositions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure your economy and build a versatile army that can adapt to the opponent's tech.
