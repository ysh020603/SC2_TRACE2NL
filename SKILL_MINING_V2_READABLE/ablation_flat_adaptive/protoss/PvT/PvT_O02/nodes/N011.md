# N011 — Early-Midgame Defense vs Unknown

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, the opponent shows a light or uncertain posture, with no reliable combat-unit cues. Your own posture is defensive with moderate production and heavy tech. Increase your defenses to prepare for potential attacks, while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Maintain your current production and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defenses to prepare for potential attacks, while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Maintain your current production and upgrades.

With the opponent unknown, it is wise to be defensive and prepared for anything. Your tech investment will give you long-term options, but you need to be safe in the short term.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the midgame with a stable defense and a tech advantage.
