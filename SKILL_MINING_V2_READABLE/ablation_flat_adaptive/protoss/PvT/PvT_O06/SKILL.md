# PvT_O06 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O06
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and production while maintaining a strong economy, with a ground-leaning army composition that can adapt to air if needed.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, own ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, opponent shows ground cues (Marine, Reaper) with heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion and economy, and add defensive structures if you suspect early pressure.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Opponent

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game, own posture unknown but heavy tech, opponent unknown with possible Reaper scout, moderate production.

**Direction:**  
Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech foundation. Scout to determine the opponent's direction. Keep production flexible to adapt to what you see.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army with Siege Potential

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame, own ground army with heavy tech, opponent ground with SiegeTank and Medivac, heavy production.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground forces and consider adding units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or air support. Maintain map presence and watch for drops.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [DEFAULT] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground with Templar Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame, own ground army with High Templar, opponent ground with Medivac and heavy production.

**Direction:**  
Leverage your High Templar for storm and feedback. Continue to increase production and tech, and consider adding Colossus or air units for additional splash damage.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [DEFAULT] N005 — Early Game Ground Scout with Marine

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game, own unknown posture, opponent shows Marine with heavy tech, moderate production.

**Direction:**  
Continue to develop your economy and tech. Scout to confirm the opponent's build. Keep your options open for ground or air.

**Read for details:** `N005`
