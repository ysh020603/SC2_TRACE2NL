## Summary

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground army with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. They have heavy production and tech, and a strong economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, High Templar, and Sentries. Defense is moderate, and you have heavy production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Leverage your High Templar for storm and feedback. Continue to increase production and tech, and consider adding Colossus or air units for additional splash damage.

High Templar provide strong area denial and can shut down Medivac healing. Your tech advantage allows for powerful late-game compositions.
