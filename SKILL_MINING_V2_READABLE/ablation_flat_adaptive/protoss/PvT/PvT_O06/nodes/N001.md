# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, own ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, opponent shows ground cues (Marine, Reaper) with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion and economy, and add defensive structures if you suspect early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible early pressure. Representative cues include Marine and Reaper. Their production and technology appear heavy, and they have a heavy economy and expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology investment. Economy and expansion are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, but you have the capacity to strengthen it.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your expansion and economy, and add defensive structures if you suspect early pressure.

Your heavy tech and production advantage can be leveraged to out-scale the opponent if you survive the early game. Maintaining a strong economy supports this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a strong ground army, key tech upgrades, and a solid economy.
