# N003 — Midgame Ground Army with Siege Potential

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground army with heavy tech, opponent ground with SiegeTank and Medivac, heavy production. Strengthen your ground forces and consider adding units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or air support. Maintain map presence and watch for drops.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground army with Marines, Marauders, Medivacs, and possibly Siege Tanks. They have heavy production and tech, and a strong economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces and consider adding units that counter Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or air support. Maintain map presence and watch for drops.

The opponent's composition is ground-heavy, so a strong ground army with proper support can hold. Your tech advantage allows for flexible counters.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a composition that can break a siege line or force the opponent to engage on your terms.
