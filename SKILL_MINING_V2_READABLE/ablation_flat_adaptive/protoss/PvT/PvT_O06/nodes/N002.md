# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown but heavy tech, opponent unknown with possible Reaper scout, moderate production. Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech foundation. Scout to determine the opponent's direction. Keep production flexible to adapt to what you see.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but a Reaper may have been spotted. Their production is moderate, technology heavy, and they have a heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined, but you are investing heavily in technology and have a moderate production. Economy is heavy, but expansion is uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech foundation. Scout to determine the opponent's direction. Keep production flexible to adapt to what you see.

Early game is about information and infrastructure. Heavy tech investment gives you options, but you need to know the opponent's plan to choose the right response.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gather enough information to choose a clear strategic direction, whether ground, air, or a mix.
