# N004 — Early-Midgame Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture unknown, heavy defense and economy, moderate production, heavy tech. Opponent ground-leaning with Reaper, possible pressure. Increase defense, continue economy and technology development, strengthen ground forces, and maintain expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is light or uncertain. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense, continue economy and technology development, strengthen ground forces, and maintain expansion.

Uncertainty and possible early pressure require defensive investment. Heavy tech investment suggests a long-term plan, but early stability is needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once opponent intentions become clearer.
