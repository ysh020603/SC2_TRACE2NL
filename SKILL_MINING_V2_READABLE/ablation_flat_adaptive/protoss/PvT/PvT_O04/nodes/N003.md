# N003 — Early-Midgame Ground Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, heavy defense, moderate expansion. Opponent ground-heavy with Marine, Reaper. Increase production and technology, maintain defense and economy, and keep expanding. Strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is moderate. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology, maintain defense and economy, and keep expanding. Strengthen ground forces.

Opponent's heavy production and tech suggest a strong midgame. Maintaining a solid ground core while expanding tech gives flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined midgame posture with either stronger ground or air presence.
