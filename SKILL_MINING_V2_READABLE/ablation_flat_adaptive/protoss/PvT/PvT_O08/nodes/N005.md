# N005 — Early Game Unknown Opponent Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) where the opponent's posture is unknown, with only a Marine seen; your own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain; only a Marine has been observed. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, but the opponent's army domain and style are unknown. Pressure posture is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and production, with a ground army (Zealots) and heavy technology. Your expansion is heavy, indicating a focus on economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

With an unknown opponent, it's crucial to gather information while building a solid foundation. A heavy economy allows you to adapt quickly once you know the opponent's plan.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined strategy once you have scouted the opponent's base and army composition.
