## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600 seconds) with both sides having heavy economies and production; opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production. Maintain your economy and consider adding tech upgrades to counter the opponent's composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with a mix of units, including Siege Tanks and Hellions. They have heavy production and technology, and their defense is heavy, indicating a well-established position.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Your technology is heavy, and you have a solid defensive posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production. Maintain your economy and consider adding tech upgrades to counter the opponent's composition.

With a diverse ground army, you can handle various threats. Immortals are strong against Siege Tanks, and Sentries provide utility. Increasing production ensures you can sustain a long fight.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the late game with a powerful ground army and the ability to transition to air if needed.
