# N004 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 540 seconds) with both sides having heavy economies and production; opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Maintain your ground army strength and increase production. Consider adding upgrades to improve your army's effectiveness.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Marines and Marauders, heavy production and technology. Their defense is moderate, and they may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. Your technology is heavy, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army strength and increase production. Consider adding upgrades to improve your army's effectiveness.

Marauders are strong against armored units, so you need a mix of units to counter them. Sentries can provide shields, and Immortals can deal with Marauders effectively.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the late game with a well-rounded army and the ability to adapt to the opponent's composition.
