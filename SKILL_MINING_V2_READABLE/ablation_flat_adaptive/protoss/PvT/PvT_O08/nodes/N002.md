# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300-360 seconds) where both sides have expanded their economies and production; opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines. Increase production and technology while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your expansion and keep an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and technology. Possible pressure is still a concern, so maintain defensive awareness.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Zealots and Stalkers. Your technology is heavy, and you are looking to increase production further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your expansion and keep an eye on defense.

With a heavy economy, you can afford to ramp up production and tech to gain an advantage in the midgame. Siege Tanks require careful positioning, so your army composition should include units that can break or bypass them.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the midgame with a robust ground army and the ability to adapt to the opponent's composition.
