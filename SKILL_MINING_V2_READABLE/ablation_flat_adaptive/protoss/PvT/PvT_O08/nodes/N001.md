# N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with both sides developing; opponent shows a ground posture with possible pressure, while you have a heavy economy and technology focus. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain a defensive posture to handle possible early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible early pressure. Production appears heavy and technology investment heavy, but exact hidden counts are unknown. Be prepared for a potential early push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented army (Zealots), moderate production, heavy technology investment, and a heavy economy. Your expansion is light or uncertain, so you need to balance tech with base development.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain a defensive posture to handle possible early pressure.

A heavy tech/economy opening requires safety checks to avoid being caught off guard. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid foundation for transitioning into a midgame composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a stronger ground army and additional production.
