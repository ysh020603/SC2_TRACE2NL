# PvT_O08 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O08
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while maintaining a moderate production base, aiming for a flexible transition into a ground or mixed composition.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180 seconds) with both sides developing; opponent shows a ground posture with possible pressure, while you have a heavy economy and technology focus.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain a defensive posture to handle possible early pressure.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Strengthening

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame (around 300-360 seconds) where both sides have expanded their economies and production; opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines.

**Direction:**  
Increase production and technology while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain your expansion and keep an eye on defense.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Diversification

**Trigger situation:**  
Late-midgame (around 600 seconds) with both sides having heavy economies and production; opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase production. Maintain your economy and consider adding tech upgrades to counter the opponent's composition.

**Read for details:** `N003`
