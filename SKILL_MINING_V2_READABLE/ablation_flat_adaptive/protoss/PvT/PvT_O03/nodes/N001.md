# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, both sides have heavy economy and production, with ground-oriented armies. Enemy shows possible Marine/Hellion cues. Strengthen ground forces while increasing production and technology. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech. Possible Marine/Hellion cues suggest early pressure or a bio opener.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Zealot/Stalker). Defense is light, so you need to be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing production and technology. Maintain economy and expansion.

Matching the opponent's heavy macro while investing in tech gives you a strong mid-game. Ground army is safe against unknown air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army with tech support (e.g., Immortal/Sentry) to handle bio and mech.
