# N005 — Late-Mid Ground vs Siege Tank Composition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-mid game, enemy has a heavy ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. You have a ground army with tech. Strengthen ground forces with tech units like Immortal and Sentry. Increase production and tech. Maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Siege Tanks, indicating a mech or bio-tank composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Stalker, Sentry, Observer, and Immortal. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces with tech units like Immortal and Sentry. Increase production and tech. Maintain economy.

Immortals are strong against Siege Tanks. Sentries provide utility with Force Field.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a composition that can break siege lines, possibly adding Colossus or High Templar.
