# N004 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Enemy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, enemy posture is unknown but shows Marine cues. You have heavy tech investment. Continue teching while increasing production. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has heavy tech and moderate production, with Marine cues. Their army composition is unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and tech, but production is moderate. Army is unknown, but you have Zealot.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching while increasing production. Maintain economy and expansion.

Heavy tech gives you options. Increasing production ensures you can field an army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid tech base (e.g., Robotics or Stargate) and a production backbone.
