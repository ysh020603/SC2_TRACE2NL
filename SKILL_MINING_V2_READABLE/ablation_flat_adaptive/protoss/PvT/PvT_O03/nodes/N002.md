# N002 — Early-Mid Ground with Light Expansion

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early-mid game, enemy shows Reaper cues, indicating possible early harassment. Your expansion is light, so you are vulnerable. Avoid neglecting defense; Reapers can punish a greedy expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has heavy production and tech, with Reaper cues suggesting early aggression or scouting.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy economy and production but light expansion, meaning you are on fewer bases. Ground army (Stalker) is present.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid neglecting defense; Reapers can punish a greedy expansion.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck if enemy commits to a timing attack or transitions to air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base and build a defensive ground force to deter harassment.
