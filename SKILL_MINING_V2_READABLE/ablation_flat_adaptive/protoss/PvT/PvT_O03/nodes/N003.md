# N003 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, enemy has heavy defense and ground army (Marine/Medivac). You have heavy economy and production. Strengthen ground forces, increase production and tech, and maintain economy. Consider adding defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is heavily defended with a ground army, possibly preparing for a push or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army (Zealot/Stalker). Defense is light, so you need to be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase production and tech, and maintain economy. Consider adding defensive structures.

Enemy's heavy defense suggests a macro game. Matching their economy while teching gives you an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army with tech (e.g., Colossus or High Templar) to break defenses.
