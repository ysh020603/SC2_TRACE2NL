# N001 — Early-Mid Game Tech and Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game phase (around 240-360 seconds) where both players are developing their economies and tech. Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-oriented Terran with heavy production and tech, possibly with Reaper harassment. Your own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech investment. Continue developing your economy and technology, strengthen your ground army, and increase production. Maintain your current expansion rate and keep a watchful eye on potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Reaper. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Their defense is moderate, and they may apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Your army is ground-oriented with Sentry and Stalker presence, but defense is light or uncertain. You are in a development phase, aiming to increase production and tech further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, strengthen your ground army, and increase production. Maintain your current expansion rate and keep a watchful eye on potential early pressure.

Your heavy tech and economy investment will pay off in the mid-game if you can survive early pressure. Increasing production now will allow you to field a larger army when your tech completes. Maintaining expansion ensures a strong economy for future tech and upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the mid-game with a solid economy, increased production, and a tech advantage. Be ready to adapt your army composition based on the opponent's observed units.
