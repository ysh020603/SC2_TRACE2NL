# PvT_O01 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology investment and economic expansion while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented posture. The build aims to establish a strong economy and tech lead in the early-mid game, with production ramping up as the game progresses. The opponent is Terran, and the strategy is to outscale them through superior tech and economy, while staying alert to potential early pressure.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Mid Game Tech and Economy Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game phase (around 240-360 seconds) where both players are developing their economies and tech. Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-oriented Terran with heavy production and tech, possibly with Reaper harassment. Your own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech investment.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology, strengthen your ground army, and increase production. Maintain your current expansion rate and keep a watchful eye on potential early pressure.
