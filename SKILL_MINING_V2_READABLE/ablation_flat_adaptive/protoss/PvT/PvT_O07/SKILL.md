# PvT_O07 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O07
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment with moderate production, aiming to develop a strong macro posture while remaining flexible to adapt based on scouting information.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 300 seconds, both sides have developed heavy economies and production, with the opponent showing a ground-oriented posture and possible pressure. Your own forces are ground-focused with Stalkers, and you have heavy technology investment.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture, but be ready to reinforce if the opponent applies pressure.

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Reinforcement

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 480 seconds, the game has reached the midgame. Both sides have heavy economies and production, with the opponent showing a ground posture including Siege Tanks and Marines. Your own army is ground-oriented, and you have heavy tech.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's composition. Continue to increase your production and technology, and consider adding units that can deal with Siege Tanks, such as Immortals or Void Rays. Maintain your expansion and defense.

**Read for details:** `N003`

### [DEFAULT] N004 — Late-Midgame Ground and Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 600 seconds, the game is in the late-midgame. The opponent has a heavy ground posture with Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. Your own army is ground-oriented but you have started to include Void Rays, indicating a possible air transition.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army while transitioning to a more air-heavy composition if needed. Your Void Rays can provide good damage against ground units, but you need to be careful of anti-air. Maintain your economy and tech lead.
