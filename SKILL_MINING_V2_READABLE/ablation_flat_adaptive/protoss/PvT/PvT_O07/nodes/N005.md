## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Marauders. Their production is moderate, and they have heavy technology investment. They may be planning a bio-based composition or a tech-heavy build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army composition is not yet defined. Your production is moderate, and you have a light defense. You are in the early game, and you need to decide on your tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while scouting to determine the opponent's exact build. Start producing a core army to defend against possible early pressure, and consider teching into units that can counter bio, such as Colossi or High Templar.

The opponent's composition is likely to be bio-based, which is strong against light units. You need to tech into units that can deal with massed bio, such as Colossi or Psionic Storm. Your heavy economy allows you to afford this tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a tech-based army that can counter the opponent's bio composition, while continuing to expand and tech up. Aim to have a solid defensive core to survive the early game.
