# N002 — Early Game Tech and Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, both sides are in the early game with heavy economies and technology investment, but the opponent's posture is unknown, and your own army composition is not yet defined. Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions, and start producing a core army to defend against possible early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about the opponent's posture, but they have been seen with Marines and Reapers. Their production is moderate, and they have heavy technology investment. They may be planning a tech-based build or early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army composition is still unknown. Your production is moderate, and you have not yet committed to a specific army style. Your defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be careful about early harassment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions, and start producing a core army to defend against possible early aggression.

In the early game, it's important to establish a strong economic and tech foundation. Since the opponent's posture is unknown, you need to be flexible and ready to adapt your army composition based on scouting information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a defined army composition based on scouting, while continuing to expand your economy and tech. Aim to have a solid defensive core to survive the early game.
