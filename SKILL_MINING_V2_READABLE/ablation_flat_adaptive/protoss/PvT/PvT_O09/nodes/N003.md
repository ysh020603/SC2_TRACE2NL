## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with both sides having heavy production and technology, opponent showing ground posture with possible Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, Medivac cues. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase production and technology, maintain economy and expansion, and keep defense solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues may include Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Medivac. Their defense is heavy, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Observers. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase production and technology, maintain economy and expansion, and keep defense solid.

With both sides at high tech and economy, maintaining a strong ground army with some air support can provide flexibility against potential tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for a potential late-game transition by diversifying tech and maintaining a strong economy.
