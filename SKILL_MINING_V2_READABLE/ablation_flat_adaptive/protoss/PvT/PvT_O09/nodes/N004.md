# N004 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with opponent posture unknown, but heavy technology investment, possible Marauder cues. Increase production and technology, strengthen ground forces, maintain economy, and consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but technology investment appears heavy. Representative cues may include Marauder. Their production is moderate, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and moderate production, with a developing army including Zealots. Your defense is light or uncertain, and expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology, strengthen ground forces, maintain economy, and consider expanding.

Early game is crucial for establishing a tech lead and economy, while staying flexible to adapt to opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid foundation for a tech-heavy midgame.
