# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both sides having heavy production and technology, opponent showing ground posture with possible Marine cues. Increase production and technology, strengthen ground forces, maintain economy, and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues may include Marine. Their defense is moderate, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Zealots and Stalkers. Your defense is light or uncertain, and expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology, strengthen ground forces, maintain economy, and consider expanding further.

Early-midgame is a critical window to secure a tech lead and expand safely, setting up for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid ground army core and tech infrastructure to transition into midgame.
