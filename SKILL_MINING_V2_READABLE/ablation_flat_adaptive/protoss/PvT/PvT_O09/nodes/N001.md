# N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with both sides having heavy production and technology, opponent showing ground posture with possible Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, Medivac cues. Strengthen ground forces, increase production and technology, maintain economy and expansion, and keep defense at a safe level.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues may include Siege Tank, Marine, Reaper, and Medivac. Their defense is heavy, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and possibly Immortals. Your defense is light or uncertain, but you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase production and technology, maintain economy and expansion, and keep defense at a safe level.

With both sides investing heavily in tech and economy, maintaining a strong ground army and tech advantage can set up a decisive midgame push or transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a robust ground composition with support from Observers and Immortals to handle Siege Tanks and bio.
