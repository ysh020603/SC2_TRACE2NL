# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both sides have heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. Opponent posture is unknown, possibly with Reaper presence. Continue developing your economy and technology, while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. Their army posture is unknown, but a Reaper may be present. They are likely expanding and teching up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and tech focus, with moderate production. Your army is light or uncertain, but you have Zealots as a core unit. You are expanding and investing in technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

This opening aims to secure a strong economic and tech lead. By investing in economy and tech, you set up for a powerful mid-game. The moderate production suggests you should not overcommit to early aggression but focus on macro.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition, likely with Stalkers and Sentries, while continuing to expand and tech.
