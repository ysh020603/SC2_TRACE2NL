# N003 — Mid-Game Ground Tech Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Mid-game, both sides have heavy ground armies and tech. Opponent has a ground macro posture with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and maintain your tech advantage. Keep expanding and defending.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. They have heavy production and tech, and are likely to push with a bio army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Colossus, Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Your production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and maintain your tech advantage. Keep expanding and defending.

The opponent's bio army is strong in the mid-game. Colossus and Sentries provide good area damage and utility. Increasing production ensures you can replace losses and maintain army size.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-mid game army with more Colossus or possibly transition to air if the opponent goes heavy on ground.
