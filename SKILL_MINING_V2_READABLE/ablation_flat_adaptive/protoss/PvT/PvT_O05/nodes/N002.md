# N002 — Early-Mid Ground Army Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, both sides have a ground-oriented army with heavy economy and tech. Opponent has a ground macro posture with Marines and possibly Reapers. Strengthen your ground army, increase production, and continue teching. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be ready to defend against possible pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and possibly Reapers. They have heavy production and tech, and are likely expanding. Their defense is moderate, and they may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers and Zealots, moderate production, and heavy tech. Your economy is strong, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase production, and continue teching. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be ready to defend against possible pressure.

The opponent is likely to build a bio-based army. By strengthening your ground forces and teching, you prepare for the mid-game. Increasing production ensures you can sustain your army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground composition, possibly including Sentries and Immortals, while continuing to tech towards Colossus or other tech units.
