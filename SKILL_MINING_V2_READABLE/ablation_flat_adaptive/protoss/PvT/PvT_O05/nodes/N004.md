# N004 — Late-Mid Game Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-mid game, both sides have heavy ground armies and tech. Opponent has a ground macro posture with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and maintain your tech advantage. Keep expanding and defending.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. They have heavy production and tech, and are likely to have a large bio army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Colossus, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries. Your production and tech are heavy, and your economy is strong. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and maintain your tech advantage. Keep expanding and defending.

The opponent's bio army is strong, but your Colossus and Zealots can counter it. Increasing production ensures you can sustain a long fight. Maintaining tech allows you to transition if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game army with more Colossus or possibly add High Templar for storm, or transition to air if the opponent goes heavy on ground.
