# PvT_O05 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology and economy while expanding, with moderate production. It aims to build a strong macro foundation and tech advantage, adapting to the opponent's actions.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game, both sides have heavy economy and tech investment, with moderate production. Opponent posture is unknown, possibly with Reaper presence.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology, while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and defense posture.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Mid Ground Army Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-mid game, both sides have a ground-oriented army with heavy economy and tech. Opponent has a ground macro posture with Marines and possibly Reapers.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army, increase production, and continue teching. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be ready to defend against possible pressure.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Mid-Game Ground Tech Army

**Trigger situation:**

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and maintain your tech advantage. Keep expanding and defending.
