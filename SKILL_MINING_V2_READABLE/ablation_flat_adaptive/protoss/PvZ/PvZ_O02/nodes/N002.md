# N002 — Early-Midgame Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game progresses into the early-midgame. The opponent remains largely unknown, but you have had time to develop your economy and technology. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to adapt to the opponent's eventual reveal.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is still consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are still uncertain, but you have had time to invest in infrastructure. You may have some units or buildings completed.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep your army composition flexible to adapt to the opponent's eventual reveal.

With limited information, it is still too early to commit to a specific strategy. Maintaining a balanced development path allows you to respond effectively to a variety of opponent strategies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a solid economy and tech base, ready to transition into a specific army composition.
