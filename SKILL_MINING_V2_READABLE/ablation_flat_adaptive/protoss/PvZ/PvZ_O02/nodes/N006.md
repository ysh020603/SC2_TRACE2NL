# N006 — Early Game Tech Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, the opponent shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Your own posture is economy-focused with heavy technology investment. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues include Zergling and Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have invested heavily in technology, with moderate economy and heavy expansion. Your defense is light or uncertain, indicating a focus on economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential pressure.

The opponent's ground posture suggests that you need a ground army to defend. Your economy and tech advantage can be leveraged later, but you must survive the early game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a strong economy and tech base, ready to transition into a specific army composition.
