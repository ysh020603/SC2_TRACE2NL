# N008 — Early Ground Army with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army is ground-oriented with Zealots, heavy defense, and moderate production. Opponent is Zerg with Queens, heavy economy. Increase expansion and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, likely having Queens. Their economy is heavy and they are expanding heavily, but their technology is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy defense, and moderate production. Your economy is heavy but your expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and technology.

With heavy defense, you are safe from early pressure. Expanding secures your economy, while continuing tech and production keeps you ahead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined composition based on scouting.
