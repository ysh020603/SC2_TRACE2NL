DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army is mixed with Void Rays, heavy production and tech. Opponent is Zerg with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and heavy tech. Increase air forces and continue production and technology. Maintain expansion and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, having Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, economy heavy, and technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a mixed army with Void Rays, heavy production and technology. Your economy is strong and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces and continue production and technology. Maintain expansion and defense.

With Void Rays, you have a powerful air unit. Continuing tech and production ensures you can transition to heavier air units if needed.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
