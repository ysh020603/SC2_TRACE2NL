# N001 — Early Ground Commitment with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game, own army is ground-oriented with Zealots, heavy tech investment, and moderate production. Opponent is likely Zerg with Queens and a heavy economy. Avoid overcommitting to a single tech path without scouting. Do not neglect expansion or defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, likely having Queens. Their economy is heavy and they are expanding heavily, but their technology is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy technology investment, and moderate production. Your economy is heavy but your expansion is light or uncertain, indicating a need to secure your economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid overcommitting to a single tech path without scouting. Do not neglect expansion or defense.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you have more information about opponent's tech or army composition, or when your expansion is secured.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting, while maintaining economic growth.
