# N002 — Early Tech Focus with Unknown Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army composition is unknown but you have heavy tech and moderate production. Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture and heavy economy. Focus on developing your technology and economy while keeping production moderate. Maintain current expansion and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, likely having Queens. Their economy is heavy and they are expanding heavily, but their technology is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, but you have heavy technology investment and moderate production. Your economy is heavy and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your technology and economy while keeping production moderate. Maintain current expansion and defense.

With an unknown army, it's wise to invest in tech and economy to enable flexibility. This allows you to adapt to opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition once you have more information about the opponent.
