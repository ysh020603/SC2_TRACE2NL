# N010 — Midgame Ground Army with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own army is ground-oriented with Zealots, heavy defense, and heavy production. Opponent is Zerg with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and tech. Stabilize your position and then develop further. Continue strengthening ground forces and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, having Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, economy heavy, and technology moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy defense, and heavy production. Your economy is strong and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position and then develop further. Continue strengthening ground forces and production.

With heavy defense, you are safe. Stabilizing allows you to develop your economy and tech without immediate threat.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a tech advantage.
