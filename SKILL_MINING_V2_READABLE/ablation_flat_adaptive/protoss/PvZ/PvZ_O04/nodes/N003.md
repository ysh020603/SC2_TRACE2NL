# N003 — Early-Mid Ground Army with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, own army is ground-oriented with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Opponent is Zerg with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production. Continue strengthening ground forces and increasing production and technology. Maintain expansion and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, having Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, economy heavy, and technology light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and technology. Your economy is heavy and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces and increasing production and technology. Maintain expansion and defense.

With a solid ground army and heavy production, you can apply pressure while continuing to develop. Maintaining economy and tech ensures long-term strength.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized composition (e.g., Immortal-based or air) based on opponent's tech.
