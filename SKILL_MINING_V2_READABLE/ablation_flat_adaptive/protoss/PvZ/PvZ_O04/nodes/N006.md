# N006 — Early-Mid Ground Army with Sentry and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army is ground-oriented with Stalkers and Sentries, heavy production and tech. Opponent is Zerg with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production. Increase expansion and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, having Zerglings and Queens. Their production is moderate, economy heavy, and technology light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers and Sentries, heavy production and technology. Your economy is heavy but your expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and continue strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and technology.

With Sentries, you have defensive capabilities. Expanding secures your economy, while continuing tech and production keeps you ahead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined composition (e.g., Immortal or air) based on opponent's tech.
