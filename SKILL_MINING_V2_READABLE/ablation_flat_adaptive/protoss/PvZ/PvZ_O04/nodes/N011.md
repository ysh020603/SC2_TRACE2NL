# N011 — Late-Mid Air Army with Phoenix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own army is air-oriented with Phoenixes, heavy production and tech. Opponent is Zerg with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech. Increase air forces and continue production and technology. Maintain expansion and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture but has Mutalisks, indicating air presence. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are well defended.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Phoenixes, heavy production and technology. Your economy is strong and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces and continue production and technology. Maintain expansion and defense.

With Phoenixes, you can counter Mutalisks and harass. Continuing tech and production ensures you can transition to heavier air units if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
