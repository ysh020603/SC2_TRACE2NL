# N011 — Midgame Ground Army with Air Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480s) with own ground army (Zealots, Void Rays) and heavy production/tech. Opponent ground-oriented with moderate air presence, heavy production, moderate tech, possible Mutalisks and Corruptors. Continue strengthening ground forces and increasing production. Maintain economy and technology. Consider adding more tech units like Colossi or Archons to counter ground threats, while using Void Rays for anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Zergling, Mutalisk, Corruptor, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Zealots and Void Rays, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your economy is strong, and you are in a good position to apply pressure or expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces and increasing production. Maintain economy and technology. Consider adding more tech units like Colossi or Archons to counter ground threats, while using Void Rays for anti-air.

A ground army with air support can be effective against a Zerg that has both ground and air units. Your tech lead allows for this hybrid composition, and Void Rays can deal with Mutalisks and Corruptors.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army composition, possibly adding more tech units or transitioning to air if the opponent goes for Mutalisks. Continue expanding and teching.
