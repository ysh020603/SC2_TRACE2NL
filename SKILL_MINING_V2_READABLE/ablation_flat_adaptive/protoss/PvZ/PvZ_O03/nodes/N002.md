# N002 — Early Game Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own army unknown, heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Opponent ground-oriented with light air, moderate production, light tech. Increase production and technology while strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion. Consider adding more gateways to support a ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined, but you are investing heavily in economy and technology. Production is moderate, so you need to ramp up unit production to support your expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology while strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion. Consider adding more gateways to support a ground army.

A ground-oriented army is a safe default against a Zerg opponent who appears to be going ground. Heavy technology investment allows for tech switches later, but you need a solid ground core to defend and potentially apply pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with tech support (e.g., Stalkers, Sentries, Immortals) as you move into the midgame. Continue expanding and teching.
