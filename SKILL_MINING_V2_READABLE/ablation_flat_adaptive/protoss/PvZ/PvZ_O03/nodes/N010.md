# N010 — Midgame Air Army with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480-540s) with own air army (Zealots, Stalkers, Oracles) and heavy production/tech. Opponent ground-oriented with moderate air presence, heavy production, moderate tech, possible Mutalisks and Corruptors. Continue strengthening air forces and increasing production. Maintain economy and technology. Consider adding more Phoenixes or Void Rays to counter Mutalisks and Corruptors.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Zergling, Mutalisk, Corruptor, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong air army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your economy is strong, and you are in a good position to apply pressure or expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening air forces and increasing production. Maintain economy and technology. Consider adding more Phoenixes or Void Rays to counter Mutalisks and Corruptors.

An air-oriented army can be effective against a ground-focused Zerg, especially if they lack anti-air. Your tech lead allows for this transition, and Oracles can harass workers while Phoenixes control the skies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger air army with tech support (e.g., Void Rays, Carriers) as you move into the midgame. Continue expanding and teching.
