# N005 — Midgame Ground Army with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480-540s) with own ground army (Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms) and heavy production/tech. Opponent ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, possible Roaches. Continue strengthening ground forces and maintaining production. Maintain economy and technology. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi to counter Roaches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Zergling, Roach, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your economy is strong, and you are in a good position to apply pressure or expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces and maintaining production. Maintain economy and technology. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi to counter Roaches.

With a tech and production advantage, you can aim to stabilize and then develop. Your ground army is capable of defending against most midgame Zerg compositions, and your tech lead allows for transitions if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army composition, possibly adding more tech units or transitioning to air if the opponent goes for Mutalisks. Continue expanding and teching.
