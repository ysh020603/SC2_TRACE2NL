# N006 — Early Game Tech Focus with Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own army unknown, heavy economy and technology, moderate production, expansion posture light or uncertain. Opponent ground-oriented with light air, moderate production, light tech. Maintain economy and technology development while strengthening ground forces. Increase production and consider expanding to secure your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined; you have a heavy economy and technology investment, but production is only moderate. You are vulnerable to early pressure due to lack of scouting information.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and technology development while strengthening ground forces. Increase production and consider expanding to secure your economy.

With an unknown army and potential early pressure, it is prudent to build a solid ground core (e.g., Zealots, Stalkers) to defend while continuing to tech. This balances safety with your economic and technological goals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting and opponent's tech choices. Aim to have a solid defensive force while expanding your economy.
