# PvZ_O03 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O03
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening focused on heavy technology investment and economic expansion, with moderate production. The early game is characterized by an unknown army composition, allowing flexibility to transition into either ground or air based on scouting and opponent actions.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [NEGATIVE] N001 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Army

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180s) with own army composition unknown, heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Opponent appears ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and light technology.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid over-committing to a specific army composition (air or ground) before scouting confirms the opponent's intentions. Also avoid neglecting production, as you may need units quickly.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early Game Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180s) with own army unknown, heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Opponent ground-oriented with light air, moderate production, light tech.

**Direction:**  
Increase production and technology while strengthening ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion. Consider adding more gateways to support a ground army.
