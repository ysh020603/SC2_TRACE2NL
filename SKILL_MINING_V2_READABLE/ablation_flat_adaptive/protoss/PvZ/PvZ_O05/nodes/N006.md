# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Army and Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army is ground-oriented with Stalkers. Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, moderate defense, and heavy economy. Strengthen your ground army and increase your production. Continue your technology development and maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their defense is moderate, and their economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your defense and economy are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your production. Continue your technology development and maintain your defense.

With a solid ground army and heavy tech, you can continue to develop while maintaining safety. Increasing production will help you field a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful army composition, possibly adding air units or higher-tier ground units.
