# N003 — Early-Midgame Tech and Defense Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own army still unknown, but heavy tech and economy. Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, moderate defense, and heavy economy. Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense. Continue your economy and technology development while maintaining your expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their defense is moderate, and their economy and expansion are heavy. They may apply pressure, but their tech is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is still not defined, but you have a heavy technology investment and a strong economy. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense. Continue your economy and technology development while maintaining your expansion.

As the game progresses, you need to solidify your position. Strengthening your ground army and defense prepares you for potential midgame engagements, while your tech continues to develop.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and potentially take a third base if safe.
