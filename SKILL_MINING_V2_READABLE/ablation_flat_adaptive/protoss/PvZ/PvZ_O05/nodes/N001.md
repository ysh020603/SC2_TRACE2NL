# N001 — Early Game Stabilization and Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army composition unknown, but heavy technology investment is underway. Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, light defense, and heavy economy. Strengthen your ground army while increasing your defense and economy. Continue your technology development and maintain your current expansion rate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Zerg ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their defense appears light, but their economy and expansion are heavy. They may apply early pressure, but their technology is uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet clear, but you have a heavy technology investment and moderate production. Your defense is heavy, and your economy is strong. You are in the early game, so your army is still developing.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while increasing your defense and economy. Continue your technology development and maintain your current expansion rate.

With a heavy technology investment, you need to survive the early game while your tech pays off. Strengthening your ground army and defense protects you from potential Zergling pressure, while continuing to develop your economy sets up a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have more information about the opponent's tech and your own tech completes.
