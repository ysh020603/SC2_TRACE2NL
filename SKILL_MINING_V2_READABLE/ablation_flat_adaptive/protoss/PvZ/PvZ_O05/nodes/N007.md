# N007 — Early Game Economy and Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, but heavy tech and moderate economy. Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture, light defense, and heavy economy. Increase your economy and production while maintaining your defense. Continue your technology development and strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their defense is light, but their economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet clear, but you have a heavy technology investment and moderate production. Your defense is heavy, and your economy is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production while maintaining your defense. Continue your technology development and strengthen your ground army.

With a heavy tech investment, you need to build a strong economy to support it. Increasing production will help you field a larger army once your tech completes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have more information.
