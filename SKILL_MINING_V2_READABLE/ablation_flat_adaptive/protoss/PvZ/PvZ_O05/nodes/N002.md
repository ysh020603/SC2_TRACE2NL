# N002 — Safe Tech Development with Increased Production

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, but heavy tech and economy. Opponent is Zerg with a ground posture and light defense, but possible pressure. Increase your production and economy while maintaining your defense. Continue your technology development and strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their defense is light, but their economy is heavy. They may apply early pressure, so you need to be prepared.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is still undefined, but you have a strong economy and heavy technology. Your defense is heavy, and you are increasing production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and economy while maintaining your defense. Continue your technology development and strengthen your ground army.

This is a positive situation where you can safely develop your economy and production because your defense is solid. Increasing production will help you field a larger army once your tech completes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a solid army and know the opponent's tech path.
