# N008 — Early Midgame Air Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, own posture air-oriented with heavy production, opponent ground macro with light tech. Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen, Overseer. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly air-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air forces, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech development.

Opponent's light tech suggests limited anti-air, making air units like Oracle effective for harassment and scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build an air force to gain map control and harass while maintaining economy.
