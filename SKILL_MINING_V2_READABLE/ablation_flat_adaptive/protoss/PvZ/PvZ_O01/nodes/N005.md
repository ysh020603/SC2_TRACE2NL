# N005 — Early Game Tech/Economy Alternative

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown-oriented with heavy tech, opponent ground macro with no reliable combat unit cues. Focus on economy and tech development, increase production, and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and tech development, increase production, and maintain defense.

Lack of visible combat units suggests opponent is also developing, so you can safely invest in economy and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong economic and tech foundation to support future army production.
