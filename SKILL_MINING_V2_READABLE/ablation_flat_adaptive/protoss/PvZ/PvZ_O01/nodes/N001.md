## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown-oriented with heavy tech investment, opponent appears ground macro with light tech and moderate production. Continue heavy technology and economy development, strengthen ground forces, and maintain defense while expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue heavy technology and economy development, strengthen ground forces, and maintain defense while expanding.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base to support later transitions. Opponent's light tech suggests low immediate threat, allowing safe development.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach early midgame with a solid economy, tech infrastructure, and a modest ground army ready to adapt.
