# N003 — Midgame Ground Tech Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own posture ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and defense, and continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain economy and defense, and continue tech development.

Opponent has heavy tech and production, so you need to match their army strength while maintaining your tech advantage. Ground forces are the current focus.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army and tech lead to be ready for late midgame engagements.
