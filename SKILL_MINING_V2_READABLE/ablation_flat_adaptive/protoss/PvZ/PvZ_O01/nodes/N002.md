# N002 — Early Midgame Tech/Economy Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, own posture still unknown-oriented but with heavy production and tech, opponent remains ground macro with moderate defense. Increase production and technology, maintain economy growth, and strengthen ground forces while keeping defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology, maintain economy growth, and strengthen ground forces while keeping defense.

With opponent still on light tech, you can safely invest in production and tech to gain an advantage. Heavy economy supports this expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong production base and tech lead to transition into midgame with a clear army direction.
