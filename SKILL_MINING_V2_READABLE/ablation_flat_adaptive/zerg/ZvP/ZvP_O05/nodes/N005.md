# N005 — Late-Mid Ground Army and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-mid game with heavy economy and production. Opponent has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers. Maintain your ground army and continue teching. Consider adding more spellcasters or transitioning to a different composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and is teching heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Zerglings, Infestors, Queens, and Overseers. Tech is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue teching. Consider adding more spellcasters or transitioning to a different composition.

With the opponent on ground, your Infestors can be very effective. However, they might transition to air, so keep an eye on that.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army while preparing for potential air threats.
