# N002 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with moderate economy and heavy defense, but expansion is light. Opponent is unknown with heavy tech. Increase economy and expand further. Continue teching and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has no visible combat units, but is teching heavily and has moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Queen and are focusing on economy, but your expansion is not yet heavy. Defense is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expand further. Continue teching and maintain defense.

Early game, it's important to secure a strong economy before the opponent's tech kicks in. Heavy tech investment suggests they might be going for a fast tech unit, so you need to be ready.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy and start producing more units.
