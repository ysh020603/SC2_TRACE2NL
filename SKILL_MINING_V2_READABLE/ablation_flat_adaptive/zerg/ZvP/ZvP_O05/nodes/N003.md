# N003 — Early-Mid Ground Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game with both sides having heavy economy and defense. Opponent has a ground army with Stalkers. Maintain your ground army and continue developing. Keep defense strong and consider adding more tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Stalkers, indicating a gateway-based composition. They are teching heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a mix of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, with moderate production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue developing. Keep defense strong and consider adding more tech.

With the opponent showing Stalkers, they might be going for a blink or void ray transition. Your ground army with Roaches can hold, but you need to be ready for air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced ground army and prepare for potential air threats.
