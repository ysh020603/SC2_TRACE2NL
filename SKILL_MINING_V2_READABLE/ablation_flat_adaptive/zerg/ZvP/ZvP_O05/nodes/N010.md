# N010 — Midgame Air and Ground Mix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy and production. Opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Increase your air presence and tech. Consider adding more Mutalisks or Corruptors.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers, and is teching heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a mix of Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, with heavy air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and tech. Consider adding more Mutalisks or Corruptors.

With Mutalisks, you can harass and control the map. Your ground army is strong, but you need to maintain air superiority.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a balanced army with air and ground capabilities.
