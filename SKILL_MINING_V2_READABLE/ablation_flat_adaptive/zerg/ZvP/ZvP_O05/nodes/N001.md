# N001 — Early-Mid Economy and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game with heavy economy and defense, moderate production, and light tech. Opponent is unknown but likely teching heavily. Continue expanding and increasing economy while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and consider adding tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy intelligence is unclear; no combat units observed, but they appear to be investing heavily in technology and have moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economic base and defensive structures, with Zerglings as your main combat unit. Production is moderate, and tech is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and consider adding tech.

With an unknown opponent, it's safe to focus on economy and defense. Heavy tech investment by the opponent suggests they may be aiming for a timing attack or late-game composition, so you need a strong economy to respond.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong economy with multiple bases and a solid defensive perimeter, while starting to tech up.
