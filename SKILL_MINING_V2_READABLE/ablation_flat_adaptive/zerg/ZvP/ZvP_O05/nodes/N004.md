# N004 — Early-Mid Defense and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game with heavy economy and defense, but opponent is unknown. You have Queens and are teching lightly. Maintain defense and economy, continue teching, and prepare to scout.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is unknown, but teching heavily and has moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and defense, with Queens as your main unit. Production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense and economy, continue teching, and prepare to scout.

With an unknown opponent, it's important to stay flexible. Heavy tech investment suggests they might be going for a specific strategy, so you need to be ready to adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Get scouting information and start building a versatile army.
