# N012 — Early Game Ground Army and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and defense. Opponent has a ground army with Zealots. Increase your economy and production, and continue teching. Strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots, and is teching heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings and are focusing on economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and production, and continue teching. Strengthen your ground army.

With the opponent on ground, you need a strong ground army. Your economy is good, so you can afford to produce more units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and continue teching.
