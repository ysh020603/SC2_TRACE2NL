# N011 — Midgame Ground Army and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy and production. Opponent has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers. Increase your economy and tech, and continue producing ground units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and is teching heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a mix of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, with moderate tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech, and continue producing ground units.

With the opponent on ground, you need a strong ground army. Your economy is good, so you can afford to produce more units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and continue teching.
