# N006 — Early Game Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and defense, but opponent is unknown. You have Zerglings. Continue expanding and increasing economy. Maintain defense and start teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is unknown, but has heavy economy and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding, with Zerglings as your main unit.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy. Maintain defense and start teching.

Early game, it's important to secure a strong economy before the opponent's tech kicks in. Heavy tech investment suggests they might be going for a fast tech unit, so you need to be ready.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy and start producing more units.
