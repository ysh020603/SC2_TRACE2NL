# N007 — Midgame Ground and Air Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy and production. Opponent has a ground army with Stalkers and Void Rays. Maintain your ground army but start preparing for air defense. Consider adding Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a mix of Stalkers and Void Rays, indicating a potential air transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Roaches and Queens, and moderate tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start preparing for air defense. Consider adding Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers.

With Void Rays, you need anti-air. Your ground army can hold, but you need to be ready for air attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army with anti-air capabilities.
