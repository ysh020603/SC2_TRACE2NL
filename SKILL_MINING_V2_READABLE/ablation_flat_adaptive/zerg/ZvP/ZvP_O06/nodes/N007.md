# N007 — Midgame Air Transition Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with enemy air presence heavy (Phoenix, WarpPrism, Immortal). Own army is ground-based with heavy production and technology. Defense is heavy. Increase technology to get anti-air units (e.g., Hydralisk, Mutalisk) and consider adding spores for defense. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy air presence with Phoenix, WarpPrism, and Immortals, heavy production and technology. They may be transitioning to an air-based composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is heavy, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology to get anti-air units (e.g., Hydralisk, Mutalisk) and consider adding spores for defense. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army.

Enemy air presence requires you to prepare anti-air. Investing in tech now allows you to counter their air while maintaining ground strength.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with anti-air (e.g., Hydra/Muta) while keeping ground forces. Continue expanding.
