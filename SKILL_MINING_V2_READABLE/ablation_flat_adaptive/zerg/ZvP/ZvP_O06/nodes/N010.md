## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with enemy ground confirmed including Carriers. Own army is ground-based with heavy production and technology. Defense is heavy. Increase technology to get anti-air units (e.g., Hydralisk, Corruptor) and consider adding spores for defense. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Carriers, heavy production and technology. They are transitioning to a Carrier-based composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Defense is heavy, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology to get anti-air units (e.g., Hydralisk, Corruptor) and consider adding spores for defense. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army.

Carriers are a major threat. Preparing anti-air is essential to counter them.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with anti-air (e.g., Hydra/Corruptor) while keeping ground forces. Continue expanding.
