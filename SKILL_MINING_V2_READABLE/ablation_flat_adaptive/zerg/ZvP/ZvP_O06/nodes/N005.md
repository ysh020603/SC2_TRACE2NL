## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with enemy ground confirmed including Immortals. Own army is ground-based with heavy technology and moderate production. Defense is heavy. Increase technology and production to match enemy's heavy investment. Strengthen ground army and continue expanding. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals, heavy production and technology. They are likely to push with a strong gateway army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is heavy, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology and production to match enemy's heavy investment. Strengthen ground army and continue expanding. Maintain defense.

With heavy tech, you can produce advanced units like Hydralisks or Lurkers to counter Protoss ground. Increasing production ensures you have enough army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
