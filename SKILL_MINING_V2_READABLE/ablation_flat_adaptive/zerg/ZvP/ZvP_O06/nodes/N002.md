# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Confirmation with Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where enemy ground posture is confirmed with Zealots and Stalkers. Both sides have heavy economy and expansion. Own army is ground-based with moderate production. Increase production and expansion to match enemy's heavy economy. Strengthen ground army and continue technology development. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is confirmed ground with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and technology. They have a heavy economy and expansion, and may be preparing a timing attack or tech transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light but you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion to match enemy's heavy economy. Strengthen ground army and continue technology development. Maintain defense.

Enemy's ground composition suggests a gateway-based army. Increasing your production and economy allows you to field a larger army and potentially counter with Roaches or Hydralisks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground composition (e.g., Roach/Hydra) while expanding. Keep scouting for tech switches.
