# N003 — Midgame Ground Tech Increase

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with enemy ground confirmed (Zealots, Stalkers). Own army is ground-based with moderate production and technology. Defense is moderate. Increase technology to unlock better units (e.g., Roach, Hydralisk) and upgrades. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and technology. They are likely to push with a gateway army or tech into Immortals/Colossus.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is moderate, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology to unlock better units (e.g., Roach, Hydralisk) and upgrades. Continue expanding and strengthening ground army. Maintain defense.

As the game progresses, you need better tech to deal with Protoss ground units. Increasing tech now prepares you for midgame fights.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a Roach/Hydra composition with upgrades. Keep expanding and teching.
