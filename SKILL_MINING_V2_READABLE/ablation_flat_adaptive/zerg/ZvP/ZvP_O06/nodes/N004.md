# N004 — Midgame Ground Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with enemy ground confirmed. Own army is ground-based with moderate production and light technology. Defense is moderate. Maintain current production and economy, focus on stabilizing your position. Keep technology development steady. Strengthen ground army as needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and technology. They may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is moderate, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current production and economy, focus on stabilizing your position. Keep technology development steady. Strengthen ground army as needed.

If you are not ready to tech up, it's safer to stabilize and build up your army to defend against potential aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your economy and army, then transition to tech when safe.
