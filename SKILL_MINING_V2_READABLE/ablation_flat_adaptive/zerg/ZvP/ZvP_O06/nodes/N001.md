Increase economy further if safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy intelligence is uncertain but suggests a heavy economy and expansion posture. They have heavy production and technology, with possible Zealot sightings. Their army composition is unknown, but they may be preparing a tech-heavy ground or air transition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion lead, with moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you have light defense. You are in a developing posture with possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion lead while strengthening your ground army. Continue technology development and keep production steady. Increase economy further if safe.

Your heavy economy allows you to outproduce the Protoss if you can survive their tech timing. Strengthening ground forces helps defend against early pressure and sets up for a midgame army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition (e.g., Roach or Hydralisk) while maintaining economy. Be ready to adapt if enemy air is detected.
