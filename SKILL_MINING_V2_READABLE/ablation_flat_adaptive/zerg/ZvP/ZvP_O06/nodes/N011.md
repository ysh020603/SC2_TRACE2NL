# N011 — Early-Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with enemy air presence moderate (Phoenix, Oracle). Own army is ground-based with moderate production and technology. Defense is light. Increase defense to protect against air harassment. Continue technology development and strengthen ground army. Maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a moderate air presence with Phoenix and Oracle, heavy production and technology. They may be harassing or transitioning to air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense to protect against air harassment. Continue technology development and strengthen ground army. Maintain economy.

Phoenix and Oracle can harass workers and bases. Adding spores and queens helps defend while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with anti-air (e.g., Hydralisk) while maintaining economy. Continue expanding.
