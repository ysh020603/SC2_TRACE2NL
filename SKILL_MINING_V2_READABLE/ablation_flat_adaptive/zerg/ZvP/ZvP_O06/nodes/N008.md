# N008 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with enemy intelligence uncertain. Own army is ground-based with moderate production and light technology. Economy is heavy. Focus on economy and expansion, while maintaining a basic ground army. Continue technology development and keep production steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy intelligence is uncertain but suggests heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. They may be teching quickly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and expansion, while maintaining a basic ground army. Continue technology development and keep production steady.

Early game, economy is key. Building a strong economy allows you to outproduce the Protoss later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army once economy is established. Be ready to adapt to enemy tech.
