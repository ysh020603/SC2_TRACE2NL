# N012 — Early-Midgame Expansion with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with enemy intelligence uncertain but heavy production. Own army is ground-based with moderate production and light technology. Defense is heavy. Increase production and expansion to match enemy's heavy economy. Strengthen ground army and continue technology development. Maintain heavy defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy intelligence is uncertain but suggests heavy production and technology, with possible Zealot sightings. They may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is heavy, and you have possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion to match enemy's heavy economy. Strengthen ground army and continue technology development. Maintain heavy defense.

With heavy defense, you can safely expand and build up your economy. Increasing production ensures you have enough army to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition while maintaining economy. Be ready to adapt to enemy tech.
