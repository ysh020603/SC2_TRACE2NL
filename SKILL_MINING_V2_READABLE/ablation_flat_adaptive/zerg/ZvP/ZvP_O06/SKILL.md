# ZvP_O06 Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O06
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on heavy economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game emphasizes Zerglings and Queens for defense and creep spread, with moderate production and light technology. The goal is to outgrow the Protoss economically while staying flexible to adapt to their tech choices.

Develop a strong economy and expansion lead while building a ground-based army. Preserve flexibility to respond to Protoss tech transitions, especially air or heavy ground compositions.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and enemy intelligence.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
