# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent ground posture with heavy defense, own ground macro. Continue strengthening ground, increase economy, maintain production, continue tech, keep defense light.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground posture, heavy economy and defense, moderate production, heavy tech. No reliable combat unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production, light tech. Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground, increase economy, maintain production, continue tech, keep defense light.

Opponent's heavy defense suggests a macro game, so investing in tech and economy is safe. Maintaining a ground core allows flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a strong economy and tech advantage.
