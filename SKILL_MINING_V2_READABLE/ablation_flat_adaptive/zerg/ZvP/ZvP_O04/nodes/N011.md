# N011 — Late-Midgame Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent ground posture with Void Rays, own ground with Mutalisks. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and production, increase tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground posture, heavy production and tech, possible pressure. Units include Zealot, Stalker, VoidRay.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens. Heavy production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and production, increase tech.

Mutalisks can harass and force a reaction, while Void Rays are dangerous but can be countered with corruptors or focus fire. Maintaining economy supports tech transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air harassment while preparing for a potential ground engagement.
