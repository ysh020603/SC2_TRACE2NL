# N009 — Midgame Ground Tech Advantage

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground posture with Immortals and Observers, own ground with heavy tech. Strengthen ground, increase economy, continue production and tech, maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground posture, heavy production and tech, possible pressure. Units include Zealot, Stalker, Observer, Immortal.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, Overseers. Heavy production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy, continue production and tech, maintain defense.

Opponent's composition with Immortals requires Roaches or other units to counter. Heavy tech allows for upgrades and transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a tech advantage and adapt to opponent's composition.
