# N008 — Midgame Ground Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground posture with Warp Prisms and Immortals, own ground macro. Strengthen ground, increase economy and expansion, increase production, continue tech, improve defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground posture, heavy production and tech, possible pressure. Units include WarpPrism, Immortal.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production and tech. Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy and expansion, increase production, continue tech, improve defense.

Opponent's Warp Prism and Immortal composition suggests a timing attack, so need to expand and produce army. Increasing production and defense prepares for the attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a strong economy and army to defend against timing attacks.
