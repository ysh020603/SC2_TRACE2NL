# N007 — Early-Midgame Tech-Heavy Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent air posture, own ground with heavy tech investment. Strengthen ground, increase economy and production, continue tech, consider air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Air posture, heavy production and tech, possible pressure. Units include Stalker, Phoenix, Oracle.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture, heavy economy, moderate production, heavy tech. Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground, increase economy and production, continue tech, consider air transition.

Heavy tech investment allows for quick transition to air or upgrades. Maintaining economy supports this flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Be ready to transition to air or tech-based ground units.
