# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, opponent posture unknown, own ground macro developing. Continue developing economy and production, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent posture unknown, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, heavy tech investment. No reliable combat unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech. Possible early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and production, strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase tech.

Establish a strong economic lead while staying safe against potential early pressure. Investing in tech now prepares for midgame transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a solid economy and a few defensive units.
