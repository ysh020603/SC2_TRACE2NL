# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent ground posture with Dark Templars, own ground with Mutalisks. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and production, increase tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Ground posture, heavy production and tech, possible pressure. Units include Zealot, Stalker, DarkTemplar, WarpPrism.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens. Heavy production, moderate tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and production, increase tech.

Mutalisks provide mobility and harass, while opponent's ground composition lacks strong anti-air. This can force a reaction and create opportunities.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air control and force opponent to react.
