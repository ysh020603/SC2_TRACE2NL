# N004 — Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent air posture with Phoenixes and ground support. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and production, continue tech, consider air defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Air posture, heavy production and tech, possible pressure. Units include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Phoenix.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground macro posture with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, Overseers. Heavy production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and production, continue tech, consider air defense.

Phoenixes threaten Overlords and harass, so need anti-air or spore crawlers. Maintaining economy allows transition to air if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure anti-air capabilities and maintain economy for potential air transition.
