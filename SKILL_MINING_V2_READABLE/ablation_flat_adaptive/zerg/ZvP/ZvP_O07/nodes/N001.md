# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light technology. Opponent shows ground posture with heavy production and technology, possible pressure. Maintain your economy and expansion, strengthen your ground army, and continue production. Keep your technology development steady. Focus on developing with safety checks to avoid being caught off guard.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They have a heavy economy and expansion, so they are likely to be macroing as well.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be cautious about potential early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion, strengthen your ground army, and continue production. Keep your technology development steady. Focus on developing with safety checks to avoid being caught off guard.

Your heavy economy allows you to outproduce the opponent if you can survive the early game. Maintaining a strong ground army and production will help you defend against potential pressure while you continue to grow.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with more production and technology, while maintaining your economic lead.
