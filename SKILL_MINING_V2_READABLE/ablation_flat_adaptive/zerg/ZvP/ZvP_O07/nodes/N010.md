# N010 — Midgame Air Transition with Ground Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and moderate technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Stalkers and Void Rays, heavy production and technology. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue your economy and expansion. Maintain your production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers and Void Rays. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They are likely to be preparing a mixed attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, production, and moderate technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Corruptors, Queens, and Overseers. Defense is heavy, so you are well-protected.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue your economy and expansion. Maintain your production and technology development.

The opponent's mix of ground and air units requires you to have a balanced army. Increasing your air presence will help you deal with the Void Rays, while your ground army can handle the Stalkers.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with strong air and ground components, while maintaining your economy.
