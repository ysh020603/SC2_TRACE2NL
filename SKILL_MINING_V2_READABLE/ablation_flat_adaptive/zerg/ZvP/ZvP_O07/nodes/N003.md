DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and moderate technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, heavy production and technology. Maintain your economy and production, and strengthen your ground army. Continue your technology development. Keep your defense strong and be ready for potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They are likely to be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, and production, with moderate technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens. Defense is heavy, so you are well-protected.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and production, and strengthen your ground army. Continue your technology development. Keep your defense strong and be ready for potential pressure.

Your heavy production and economy allow you to field a large army. Maintaining a strong ground force and defense will help you withstand any timing attack and potentially counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground army with better technology and upgrades, while maintaining your economic and production advantage.
