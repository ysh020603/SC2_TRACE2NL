# N002 — Early Game Development with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light technology. Opponent's army domain is unknown, with heavy defense and technology, but moderate economy. Continue developing your economy and production, and strengthen your ground army. Maintain your expansion and technology development. Use safety checks to ensure you are not vulnerable to early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear about their army composition, but they have heavy defense and technology, and a moderate economy. They may be teching up or preparing for a specific strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented but you have no combat units yet. Defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be careful.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production, and strengthen your ground army. Maintain your expansion and technology development. Use safety checks to ensure you are not vulnerable to early aggression.

In the early game, it is important to establish a strong economic base while staying flexible. Since the opponent's army is unknown, you need to be prepared for various possibilities.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground army and improve your defense, while continuing to grow your economy.
