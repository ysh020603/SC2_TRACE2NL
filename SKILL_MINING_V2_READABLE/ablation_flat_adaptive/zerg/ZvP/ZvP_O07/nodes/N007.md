# N007 — Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production, moderate technology. Opponent shows air posture with Void Rays, heavy production and technology. Increase your defense and economy. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army, but also consider adding anti-air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Void Rays. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They are likely to be preparing an air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, and production, with moderate technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Defense is moderate, so you need to improve it.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army, but also consider adding anti-air units.

The opponent's air threat requires you to improve your defense and possibly add anti-air capabilities. Increasing your economy will help you afford the necessary units and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with anti-air capabilities, while maintaining your economy and production.
