# N009 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Stalkers and Immortals, heavy production and technology. Increase your economy and expansion. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army and keep your defense strong.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers and Immortals. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They are likely to be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. Defense is heavy, so you are well-protected.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army and keep your defense strong.

Your heavy tech and production allow you to field a powerful army. Continuing to increase your economy will give you a long-term advantage, while your strong army can defend against pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground army with better upgrades and technology, while maintaining your economic lead.
