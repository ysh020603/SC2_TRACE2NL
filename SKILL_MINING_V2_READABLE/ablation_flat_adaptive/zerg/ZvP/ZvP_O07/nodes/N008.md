# N008 — Midgame Heavy Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Opponent shows heavy air presence with Stalkers and Oracles, heavy production and technology. Increase your economy and expansion. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army, but also consider adding anti-air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a heavy air posture with Stalkers and Oracles. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They are likely to be preparing an air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, production, and technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. Defense is heavy, but you need to address the air threat.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army, but also consider adding anti-air units.

The opponent's heavy air threat requires you to improve your anti-air capabilities. Increasing your economy will help you afford the necessary units and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with anti-air capabilities, while maintaining your economy and production.
