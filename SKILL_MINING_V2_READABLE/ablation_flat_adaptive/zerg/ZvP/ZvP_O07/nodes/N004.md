# N004 — Early-Midgame Economy Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light technology. Opponent's army domain is unknown, with heavy economy and expansion, and heavy technology. Increase your economy and continue your technology development. Maintain your production and expansion. Strengthen your ground army and keep an eye on the opponent's tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear about their army composition, but they have a heavy economy and expansion, and heavy technology. They may be teching up to a specific strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens. Defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and continue your technology development. Maintain your production and expansion. Strengthen your ground army and keep an eye on the opponent's tech.

With an unknown opponent, it is important to focus on your own economy and tech while staying flexible. Increasing your economy will give you more options later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy and technology, while building a solid ground army for defense.
