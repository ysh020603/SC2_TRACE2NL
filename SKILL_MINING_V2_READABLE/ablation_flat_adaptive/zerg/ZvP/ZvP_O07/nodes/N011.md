# N011 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment with Ground Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with heavy economy, expansion, and production, heavy technology. Opponent shows ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and technology. Increase your economy and continue your expansion. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army and improve your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture. They are likely to be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, expansion, and production, with heavy technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Roaches and Queens. Defense is moderate, so you need to improve it.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and continue your expansion. Continue your production and technology development. Strengthen your ground army and improve your defense.

Your heavy tech investment gives you an advantage in unit quality. Continuing to increase your economy will help you afford more units and tech, while your strong army can defend against pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground army with better technology and upgrades, while maintaining your economic lead.
