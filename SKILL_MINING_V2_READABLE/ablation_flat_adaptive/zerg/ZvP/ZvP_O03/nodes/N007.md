# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground with Air Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy production and moderate tech. Opponent shows ground cues (Zealot, Carrier) with heavy production and tech. Maintain your ground army but start teching up to deal with the air threat. Consider adding Hydralisks or Corruptors to counter Carriers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground-based Protoss with Zealots and Carriers, indicating a transition to air with a ground support army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, but your tech is only moderate. Production is heavy, and you have a solid economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start teching up to deal with the air threat. Consider adding Hydralisks or Corruptors to counter Carriers.

Carriers are a significant threat to your ground army. Teching to anti-air units is essential to avoid being overwhelmed. Your heavy production can support a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air units (e.g., Hydralisks, Corruptors) while maintaining your ground core.
