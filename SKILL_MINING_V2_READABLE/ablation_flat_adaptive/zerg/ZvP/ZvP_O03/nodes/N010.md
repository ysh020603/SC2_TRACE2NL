# N010 — Midgame Ground vs Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground macro posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Opponent shows air cues (VoidRay, Oracle) with heavy production and tech. Maintain your ground army but start teching to anti-air units like Hydralisks or Corruptors. Continue expanding to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air-based Protoss with Void Rays and Oracles, suggesting a transition to air with heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and your tech is heavy. Production is moderate, and you have a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start teching to anti-air units like Hydralisks or Corruptors. Continue expanding to support a larger army.

Void Rays and Oracles are a significant threat to your ground army. Teching to anti-air is essential to counter the opponent's air composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air units while maintaining your ground core.
