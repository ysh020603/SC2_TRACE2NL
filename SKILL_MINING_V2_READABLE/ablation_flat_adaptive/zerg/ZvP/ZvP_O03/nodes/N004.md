# N004 — Midgame Ground with Moderate Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy production and moderate tech. Opponent shows ground cues (Stalker, Sentry, WarpPrism) with heavy production and tech. Maintain your ground army and production, but consider teching up to match the opponent's tech advantage. Continue expanding to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground-based Protoss with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms, indicating a gateway-heavy army with potential for harass.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Zerglings and Queens, but your tech is only moderate. Production is heavy, and you have a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and production, but consider teching up to match the opponent's tech advantage. Continue expanding to support a larger army.

Your heavy production allows you to field a large army, but you need tech to break through Sentry force fields and Stalker mobility. Investing in tech will improve your army's effectiveness.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more tech-heavy ground army, such as adding Roaches or Hydralisks, and start upgrades.
