# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro posture with moderate production, heavy economy, and light tech. Opponent shows ground cues (Zealot, Sentry) with heavy production and tech. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy. Maintain your current expansion and production, but be ready to add more defensive units if pressure comes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Protoss with Zealots and Sentries, heavy production and tech investment. Their exact army composition is uncertain, but they are likely to pressure with ground forces.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground-oriented economy with Zerglings and Queens, but your tech is still light. Production is moderate, and you have room to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy. Maintain your current expansion and production, but be ready to add more defensive units if pressure comes.

With a heavy economy, you can afford to invest in both army and tech. Strengthening ground forces helps counter the likely Zealot/Sentry composition, while maintaining economy ensures long-term stability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more solid ground army with Roaches or other tech units, while keeping your economy ahead.
