# N003 — Midgame Ground Tech with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy production and heavy tech. Opponent shows ground cues (Stalker, Sentry, WarpPrism) with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding tech upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be prepared to defend against warp prism drops.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground-based Protoss with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms, suggesting a gateway-heavy army with potential for warp prism harass.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and your tech is heavy. Production is high, and you are well-positioned to take engagements.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding tech upgrades. Maintain your economy and expansion, but be prepared to defend against warp prism drops.

With heavy production and tech, you can out-produce the opponent. Upgrades will give you an edge in engagements, and maintaining economy ensures you can sustain a long fight.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful ground army with upgrades (e.g., +1/+1) and possibly add tech units like Infestors or Ultralisks if the game goes late.
