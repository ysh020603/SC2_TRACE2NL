# N006 — Early Game Economy Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground macro posture with moderate production and heavy economy. Opponent's army domain is unknown, but they show Zealot cues with heavy tech. Focus on expanding and developing your economy while maintaining a defensive ground army. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear about the opponent's army, but they have Zealots and heavy tech investment. They could be going for a tech rush or a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and you are expanding aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding and developing your economy while maintaining a defensive ground army. Scout to determine the opponent's intentions.

With a heavy economy, you can out-macro the opponent if they are teching. Maintaining a defensive army protects your expansions while you gather information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid army once you have a clear picture of the opponent's composition.
