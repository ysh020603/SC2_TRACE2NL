# N012 — Early-Mid Ground vs Air Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro posture with moderate production and heavy economy. Opponent shows air cues (Oracle) with heavy production and tech. Maintain your ground army but start teching to anti-air units like Hydralisks or Spores. Continue expanding to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air-based Protoss with Oracles, suggesting a transition to air with heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and light tech. Your economy is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start teching to anti-air units like Hydralisks or Spores. Continue expanding to support a larger army.

Oracles can harass your economy and army. Teching to anti-air is essential to counter the air threat and protect your expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air units while maintaining your ground core.
