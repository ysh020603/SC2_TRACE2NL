# N002 — Early-Mid Flexible Ground with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro posture with moderate production and heavy economy. Opponent's army domain is unknown, but they show Stalker cues with heavy tech. Maintain your ground army and economy while increasing scouting to determine the opponent's direction. Keep production flexible to adapt to either ground or air threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear about the opponent's army composition, but they have Stalkers and heavy tech investment. They could be transitioning to air or staying ground.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and ground-oriented army with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and you have not committed to a specific tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy while increasing scouting to determine the opponent's direction. Keep production flexible to adapt to either ground or air threats.

With an unknown opponent, it's wise to keep options open. Your heavy economy allows you to tech switch later, but you need information to make the right call.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gather information on the opponent's composition and transition to a countering army, whether ground or air.
