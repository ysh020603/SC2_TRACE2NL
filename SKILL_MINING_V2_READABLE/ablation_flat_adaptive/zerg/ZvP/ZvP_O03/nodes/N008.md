# N008 — Early-Mid Aggressive Expansion with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro posture with moderate production and heavy economy. Opponent's army domain is unknown, but they show Zealot cues with heavy tech. Continue expanding and increasing production to support a larger army. Scout to determine the opponent's tech path and prepare a counter.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear about the opponent's army, but they have Zealots and heavy tech investment. They could be teching to a specific composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding aggressively. Your ground army is moderate with Zerglings and Queens, and you have light tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing production to support a larger army. Scout to determine the opponent's tech path and prepare a counter.

Your heavy economy allows you to expand aggressively and out-produce the opponent. Increasing production ensures you can defend your expansions and transition to a countering army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid army once you have scouting information, while maintaining your economic lead.
