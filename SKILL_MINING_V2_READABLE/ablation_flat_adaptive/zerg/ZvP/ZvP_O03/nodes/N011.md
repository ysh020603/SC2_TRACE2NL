# N011 — Early-Mid Ground with Moderate Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro posture with moderate production and moderate tech. Opponent shows ground cues (Zealot, Sentry) with heavy production and tech. Increase your production and tech to match the opponent's strength. Continue expanding to support a larger army and consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground-based Protoss with Zealots and Sentries, heavy production and tech. They are likely to pressure with a gateway army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and tech. Your economy is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and tech to match the opponent's strength. Continue expanding to support a larger army and consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks.

Your heavy economy can support a larger army and tech investment. Increasing production and tech will help you counter the opponent's gateway army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more tech-heavy ground army with upgrades and additional units.
