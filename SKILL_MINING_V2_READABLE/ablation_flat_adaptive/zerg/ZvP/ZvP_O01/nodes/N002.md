## Summary

- Opponent shows Zealot and Stalker, heavy economy and tech, moderate production, unknown army style.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, ground army with Zergling and Queen.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, continue economy growth, keep production steady, and continue tech development.

Opponent's ground units suggest a gateway-based army; maintaining a ground core with Zerglings and Queens provides a solid base to tech into appropriate counters.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a strong economy and a versatile ground army, ready to tech into Roaches or other units based on opponent's composition.
