# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, opponent unknown, own ground-oriented with Queens. Continue developing economy and expansion, maintain ground army, keep production moderate, and invest in tech gradually.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent unknown, heavy economy and tech, moderate production, no reliable combat unit cues.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, ground army with Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and expansion, maintain ground army, keep production moderate, and invest in tech gradually.

Establish a strong economic base to support future tech and army choices, while staying flexible to opponent's unknown strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach early-midgame with a solid economy and a small ground force, ready to adapt to opponent's first tech choices.
