# N007 — Early-Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent shows Phoenix/Oracle, own ground with Zergling/Queen. Maintain ground army, increase economy and tech, and consider adding anti-air capabilities.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows Phoenix and Oracle, heavy production and tech, air posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and tech, ground army with Zergling and Queen.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, increase economy and tech, and consider adding anti-air capabilities.

Phoenix and Oracle indicate an air threat; maintaining ground economy while preparing anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks or Spore Crawlers) is essential.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a ground economy and some anti-air defense, ready to transition to a more air-capable army.
