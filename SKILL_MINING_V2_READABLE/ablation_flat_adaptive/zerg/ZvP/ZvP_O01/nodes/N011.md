# N011 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has ground army, own has Mutalisks. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has ground army with Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, moderate tech, ground army with Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

Mutalisks provide harassment and map control; transitioning to a more air-focused army can exploit opponent's ground-heavy composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach late-midgame with a strong air army and economy, ready to counter opponent's ground.
