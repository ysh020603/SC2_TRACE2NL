# N005 — Midgame Ground Stability

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has ground army with Sentry, own ground with Zergling/Queen. Maintain ground army, continue economy, keep production and tech steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has ground army with Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Midgame, heavy economy and expansion, moderate production and tech, ground army with Zergling and Queen.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, continue economy, keep production and tech steady.

Opponent's composition is balanced ground; maintaining a stable ground army and economy allows for flexible responses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach late-midgame with a stable economy and ground army, ready to adapt to opponent's tech.
