# N009 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has air with Phoenix/Oracle, own ground with Zergling/Queen/Overseer. Increase economy and tech, continue production, and consider transitioning to anti-air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has air with Zealot, Stalker, Phoenix, Oracle, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Late-midgame, heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech, ground army with Zergling, Queen, Overseer.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and tech, continue production, and consider transitioning to anti-air units.

Opponent's air presence requires a response; investing in anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks, Mutalisks, or Corruptors) while maintaining economy is necessary.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach late game with a mixed army capable of handling air threats.
