# N012 — Late-Midgame Air Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has ground army, own has Mutalisks. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has ground army with Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Observer, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Late-midgame, heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech, ground army with Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech.

Mutalisks are strong against ground armies; continuing to build air power can give a decisive advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach late game with a dominant air army and strong economy, ready for final battles.
