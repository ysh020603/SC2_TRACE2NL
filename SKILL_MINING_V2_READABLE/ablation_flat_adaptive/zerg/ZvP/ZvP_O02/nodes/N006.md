# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At early-midgame, opponent posture is unknown with heavy defense and economy. Own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue technology development. Maintain defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue technology development. Maintain defense and expansion.

Opponent is investing heavily in defense and economy. Strengthening your ground army and economy will help you match their production and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to counter opponent's heavy defense.
