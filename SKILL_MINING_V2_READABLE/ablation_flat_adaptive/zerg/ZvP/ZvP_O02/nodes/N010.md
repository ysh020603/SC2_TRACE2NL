# N010 — Early Game Ground Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At early game, opponent posture is unknown with heavy defense and moderate economy. Own posture is ground-oriented with light production. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, increase expansion, increase production, continue technology development. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears light_or_uncertain, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with light_or_uncertain production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, increase expansion, increase production, continue technology development. Maintain defense.

Opponent is investing in defense and tech. Expanding and increasing production will help you build a strong economy and army to match their tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to counter opponent's heavy defense.
