# N012 — Late-Midgame Ground Stalemate

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At late-midgame, opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and moderate tech. Maintain current army path, economy, production, technology, and expansion. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and moderate technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, economy, production, technology, and expansion. Develop with safety checks.

Both sides have strong ground armies. Maintaining a balanced approach allows you to respond to opponent's moves without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing and look for opportunities to gain an advantage.
