# N007 — Early-Midgame Ground Confrontation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At early-midgame, opponent has a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue technology development. Maintain defense and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with moderate production and light_or_uncertain technology investment. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue technology development. Maintain defense and expansion.

Opponent is fielding a ground army with heavy production. Strengthening your ground forces and economy will help you match their army size and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to counter opponent's ground composition.
