# N008 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture and possible air presence. You have heavy economy and production, but light air. Increase your defense and economy, while continuing production and tech. Consider adding anti-air capabilities if opponent's air presence becomes confirmed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture and possible air presence. They show Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens, indicating a mixed army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy tech. Your ground army is strong, but you have no air presence. You are in a defensive position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, while continuing production and tech. Consider adding anti-air capabilities if opponent's air presence becomes confirmed.

Opponent's possible air presence threatens your ground army. Strengthening defense and economy prepares you for a longer game, while tech can provide answers.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with anti-air capabilities, while maintaining a strong economy.
