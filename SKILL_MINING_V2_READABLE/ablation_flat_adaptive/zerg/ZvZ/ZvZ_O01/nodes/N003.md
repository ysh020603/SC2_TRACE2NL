# N003 — Midgame Ground Strength

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, and heavy tech. Ground posture with possible pressure. Avoid over-committing to air or tech that could weaken your ground army. Do not neglect defense while maintaining economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent also has heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture. They show Zerglings and Queens, but no confirmed air or special threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansion, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your ground army includes Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, making you strong on the ground.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-committing to air or tech that could weaken your ground army. Do not neglect defense while maintaining economy.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate if opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure. Adjust your army composition accordingly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition or consider tech switches if opponent shows weaknesses.
