# N001 — Early Economy Focus with Safety

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, both players have heavy economy and expansion, light tech, and moderate production. Opponent shows ground posture with possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Increase production and tech gradually, but keep safety checks for potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent mirrors your economy and expansion, with moderate production and light tech. They show a ground posture with possible early pressure, but no confirmed threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Your army is ground-oriented but not yet strong. You are vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansion while strengthening your ground army. Increase production and tech gradually, but keep safety checks for potential pressure.

A heavy economy and expansion provide long-term advantage. Strengthening ground forces ensures you can defend against early aggression while maintaining economic lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with more production and tech, while maintaining economic lead.
