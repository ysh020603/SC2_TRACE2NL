# N006 — Defensive Early Game

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Ground posture with possible pressure. Focus on stabilizing your defense before continuing expansion. Increase defense and economy, while maintaining production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a similar economy and expansion, but shows light defense and possible pressure. They may be planning an early attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Your ground army is developing, but you feel the need to stabilize before expanding further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your defense before continuing expansion. Increase defense and economy, while maintaining production and tech.

If opponent is planning early pressure, stabilizing your defense prevents losses. Once safe, you can resume expansion and development.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stable economy and army, then resume expansion and development.
