# N002 — Stable Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Ground posture with possible pressure. Maintain your economy and expansion, continue strengthening ground forces, and keep production and tech at current levels. Focus on safety and stability.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is in a similar stable position, with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. They show a ground posture with possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Your ground army is developing but not yet strong. You are in a stable position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion, continue strengthening ground forces, and keep production and tech at current levels. Focus on safety and stability.

Both players are in a similar economic and military position. Maintaining stability avoids unnecessary risks while allowing you to react to opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army with more production and tech when safe, or adapt if opponent applies pressure.
