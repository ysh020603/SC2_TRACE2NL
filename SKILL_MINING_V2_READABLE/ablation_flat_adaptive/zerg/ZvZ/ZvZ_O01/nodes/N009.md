# N009 — Midgame Air Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture and heavy air presence. You have heavy economy and production, but light air. Increase your defense and economy, and consider adding anti-air units or tech. Continue production and tech to support a balanced army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture and heavy air presence. They show Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a strong air force.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy tech. Your ground army is strong, but you have no air presence. You are vulnerable to air attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, and consider adding anti-air units or tech. Continue production and tech to support a balanced army.

Opponent's heavy air presence threatens your ground army. Adding anti-air capabilities is essential to defend against Mutalisks and other air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with anti-air capabilities, while maintaining a strong economy.
