# N007 — Early-Mid Expansion and Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Ground posture with possible pressure. Increase your economy and expansion, and ramp up production and tech. Focus on growing your advantage while maintaining some defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a similar economy and expansion, but shows light defense and possible pressure. They have not committed to heavy production or tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Your ground army is developing, and you have the resources to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion, and ramp up production and tech. Focus on growing your advantage while maintaining some defense.

Your opponent's light defense and moderate production suggest they are not applying heavy pressure. Expanding and increasing production can give you a significant lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy and army, with increased production and tech, to solidify your advantage.
