# N005 — Midgame Ground Stability

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, and moderate tech. Ground posture with possible pressure. Maintain your economy, production, and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and keep defense solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a similar economy and production, with moderate tech. They show a ground posture with possible pressure, but no confirmed threats.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansion, heavy production, and moderate tech. Your ground army includes Zerglings and Queens, and you are in a stable position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy, production, and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and keep defense solid.

Both players are in a similar position, so maintaining stability avoids unnecessary risks. You can react to opponent's moves without over-committing.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition or consider tech switches if opponent shows weaknesses.
