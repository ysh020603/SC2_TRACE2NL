# N011 — Late-Midgame Air Advantage

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture and possible air presence. You have heavy economy and production, and have a strong air presence. Continue increasing your air presence and tech, while maintaining your economy and production. Use your air advantage to pressure the opponent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy economy, production, and tech, with a ground posture and possible air presence. They show Zerglings and Queens, indicating a ground-heavy army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy tech. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens, giving you a balanced force with air superiority.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing your air presence and tech, while maintaining your economy and production. Use your air advantage to pressure the opponent.

Your air superiority allows you to harass and control the map. Maintaining a strong economy ensures you can sustain your army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a dominant air army, with a strong ground defense.
