# N006 — Early-Midgame Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production and light tech. You have a ground posture with Queens, moderate production and light tech. Increase your economy and expansion, and boost production. Continue developing your ground army and technology. Be ready to defend against possible early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts are unknown. They may pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented posture. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and expansion, and boost production. Continue developing your ground army and technology. Be ready to defend against possible early aggression.

Expanding and increasing production gives you a long-term advantage. Maintaining a defensive core allows you to survive early pressure while you grow.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a strong economic lead and a solid production base, ready to transition into the midgame.
