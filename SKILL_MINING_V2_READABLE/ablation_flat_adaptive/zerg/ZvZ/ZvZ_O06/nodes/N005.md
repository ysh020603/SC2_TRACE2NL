# N005 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Queens only, moderate production and light tech. You have a ground posture with Queens and Overseers, moderate production and moderate tech. Increase your economy and technology investment while continuing to expand. Maintain production and strengthen your ground army. Use Overseers for scouting to gain information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Queens. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts are unknown. They may pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented posture. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is moderate. Defense is moderate, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and technology investment while continuing to expand. Maintain production and strengthen your ground army. Use Overseers for scouting to gain information.

Investing in technology gives you more options for the midgame. Overseers provide valuable scouting to detect the opponent's tech direction, allowing you to adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a tech advantage and good scouting information, ready to choose an effective composition.
