# N007 — Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and heavy tech. You have a ground posture with Roaches and Queens, moderate production and moderate tech. Stabilize your position by increasing defense and continuing to develop your economy and army. Maintain production and technology, and be ready to counter the opponent's heavy ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Production appears heavy, technology heavy, and exact hidden counts are unknown. They may pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented army including Roaches. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is moderate. Defense is heavy, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by increasing defense and continuing to develop your economy and army. Maintain production and technology, and be ready to counter the opponent's heavy ground forces.

The opponent has a strong production and tech advantage, so you need to focus on defense and catching up. Roaches provide a solid frontline, but you may need to tech into counters.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your defense and catch up in production and tech, ready to transition to a stronger composition.
