# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, both sides still have a ground macro posture with light or uncertain air. Economy and expansion are heavy, production moderate, and technology is now moderate on your side. Continue your economic and ground army development. Maintain production and technology, and keep your expansion going. Be ready to defend against potential aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts are unknown. They may pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a ground-oriented posture. Your economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is moderate. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue your economic and ground army development. Maintain production and technology, and keep your expansion going. Be ready to defend against potential aggression.

Maintaining a steady macro game while keeping a defensive core allows you to reach a stronger midgame position. Moderate tech gives you options without committing too heavily.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a strong economy and a versatile ground army, ready to transition to a specific composition based on the opponent's tech.
