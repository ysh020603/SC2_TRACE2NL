# N001 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both sides have a ground macro posture with light or uncertain air presence. Economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Maintain your current development path: continue expanding and building economy, strengthen your ground army, and keep production and technology steady. Ensure you have safety checks against possible early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, showing Zerglings and Queens. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts are unknown. They may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented macro posture. You have moderate production and light or uncertain technology. Your economy and expansion are heavy, but your defense is light or uncertain. You may face possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path: continue expanding and building economy, strengthen your ground army, and keep production and technology steady. Ensure you have safety checks against possible early pressure.

With both sides on a similar macro path, the one who executes more efficiently gains an advantage. Maintaining a strong economy while keeping a defensive core allows you to respond to pressure without sacrificing long-term growth.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Progress smoothly into the early-midgame with a solid economic base and a modest ground army, ready to adapt to the opponent's tech choices.
