# N008 — Early Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 300 seconds) with opponent on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, heavy production, and moderate technology. Your own economy and expansions are heavy, but production is moderate. Maintain economy and defense, strengthen ground army, continue expansions and production, and increase technology. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansions, heavy defense, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zergling and Queen cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and defense, strengthen ground army, continue expansions and production, and increase technology. Develop with safety checks.

With the opponent having heavy production, you need to increase your own production and technology to keep up. Maintaining economy and defense while developing ensures you can match their army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with increased production and technology, ready to match opponent's army.
