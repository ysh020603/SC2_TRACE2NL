# N005 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480 seconds) with opponent on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your own economy and expansions are heavy, with heavy defense and production. Increase defense and economy, continue expansions and production, and continue technology development. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Roach, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansions, heavy defense, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zergling and Queen cues, but you have invested heavily in tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue expansions and production, and continue technology development. Stabilize then develop.

With both players on heavy tech, focusing on defense and economy while maintaining production is key. Stabilizing your position allows you to develop a tech advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your position and transition to a more aggressive tech development phase.
