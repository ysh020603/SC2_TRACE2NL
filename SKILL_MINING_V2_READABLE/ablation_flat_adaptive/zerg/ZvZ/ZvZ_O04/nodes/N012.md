# N012 — Early Game Ground Macro with Moderate Expansions

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with opponent on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, moderate production, and light technology. Your own economy is heavy but expansions are moderate. Maintain economy and defense, strengthen ground army, continue expansions and increase production. Stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy but moderate expansions, heavy defense, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zergling and Queen cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and defense, strengthen ground army, continue expansions and increase production. Stabilize then develop.

With a heavy economy but fewer expansions, you need to expand to secure your economy. Increasing production while maintaining defense ensures you can protect new expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to early midgame with more expansions and increased production, ready to develop further.
