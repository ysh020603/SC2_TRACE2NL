# N003 — Early Stabilization with Defense Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with opponent on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, moderate production, and light technology. Your own economy and expansions are moderate, and you have a defensive posture. Increase defense and economy, continue expansions and production, and continue technology development. Stabilize before developing further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and expansions, heavy defense, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zergling and Queen cues, but you are slightly behind in economy compared to the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue expansions and production, and continue technology development. Stabilize before developing further.

When your economy is slightly behind, focusing on defense and catching up economically is crucial. Building defensive structures and units while expanding ensures you can withstand early pressure and grow.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your economy and defense, then transition to a more aggressive development phase.
