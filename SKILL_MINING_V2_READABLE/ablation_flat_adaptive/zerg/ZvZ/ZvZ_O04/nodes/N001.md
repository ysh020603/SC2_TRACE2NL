# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with both players on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, moderate production, and light technology. Opponent shows Zergling and Queen cues, with possible pressure. Maintain economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, keep defense and production steady, and continue technology development with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansions, heavy defense, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with no confirmed combat units yet, but you are developing a macro posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, keep defense and production steady, and continue technology development with safety checks.

In ZvZ, a heavy economy with defensive structures and a growing ground army provides a solid foundation. Maintaining production and technology while scouting for early aggression allows you to respond without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a strong economy and a reinforced ground army, ready to adapt to opponent's tech choices.
