# N010 — Midgame Air Transition with Ground Base

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 540 seconds) with opponent on a ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your own army has air presence (Mutalisk cues) but is still ground-oriented. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and expansions, and continue technology development. Develop with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansions, heavy defense, heavy production, and moderate technology. Your army includes Zergling, Mutalisk, and Queen cues, indicating an air transition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and expansions, and continue technology development. Develop with safety checks.

Transitioning to air (Mutalisks) can give you a mobility advantage against a ground-based opponent. Continuing economy and technology while building air units allows you to harass and control the map.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a strong air army while maintaining a solid ground base, ready to harass or engage.
