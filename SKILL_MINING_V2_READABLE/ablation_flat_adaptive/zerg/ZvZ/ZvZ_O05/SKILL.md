# ZvZ_O05 Economy / Ground / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O05
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: economy / ground / upgrade
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy and ground army development, with a focus on upgrades. The early game is characterized by heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. The army is ground-oriented, typically consisting of Zerglings and Queens, with potential for Roaches and Mutalisks later.

Develop a strong economy and ground army while maintaining flexibility to adapt to opponent's tech and army composition. Aim for a smooth transition into mid-game with a solid upgrade advantage.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of opponent's army composition, tech, and pressure.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [NEGATIVE] N001 — Early Game Queen Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180 seconds) with heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and no combat units yet. Opponent is also in early game with similar posture.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid building too many Zerglings early, as this can hurt economy. Avoid teching too fast without a solid ground army.
