# N004 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with moderate economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Both sides have Zerglings and Queens. Focus on expanding and increasing economy. Maintain Zergling production for defense and scouting. Keep defense light but be ready to react.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings and Queens. They have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and expansion, with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding and increasing economy. Maintain Zergling production for defense and scouting. Keep defense light but be ready to react.

In ZvZ, economy is key. Expanding early gives a long-term advantage. Zerglings provide map control and scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a standard early-midgame ground posture with a third base and more Zerglings.
