# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 360 seconds) with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Both sides have Zerglings and Queens. Continue developing economy and ground army. Maintain Zergling production, add Roaches if needed, and consider starting a Lair for tech. Keep defense light but be ready to react.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings and Queens. They have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and ground army. Maintain Zergling production, add Roaches if needed, and consider starting a Lair for tech. Keep defense light but be ready to react.

This is a standard macro-oriented ZvZ. Maintaining a strong economy while producing a mix of Zerglings and Roaches gives flexibility. Starting Lair tech opens up options for Mutalisks or upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame composition with Roaches and possibly Mutalisks, and start upgrade research.
