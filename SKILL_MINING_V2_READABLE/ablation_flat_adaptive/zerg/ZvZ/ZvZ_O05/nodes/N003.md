# N003 — Midgame Ground Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420 seconds) with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. You have Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, while opponent has Zerglings and Queens. Continue strengthening ground army, add more Roaches, and consider starting upgrades. Maintain economy and expansion. Keep defense moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings and Queens. They have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, and moderate technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army, add more Roaches, and consider starting upgrades. Maintain economy and expansion. Keep defense moderate.

Roaches are strong in ZvZ and provide a solid core. Upgrades become important as the game progresses. Maintaining economy allows for tech switches later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame composition with Roaches and possibly Mutalisks, and start +1/+1 upgrades.
