# N007 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 540 seconds) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, and moderate technology. You have Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers, while opponent has Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Increase air presence and strengthen air army. Continue economy and expansion. Maintain defense and production. Consider adding Corruptors if opponent has many Mutalisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, and heavy technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers. Production is heavy, and technology is moderate. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air army. Continue economy and expansion. Maintain defense and production. Consider adding Corruptors if opponent has many Mutalisks.

Mutalisks provide map control and harassment. They are strong against ground armies. Adding Corruptors can counter enemy Mutalisks.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Transition to a late-game composition with Mutalisks, Corruptors, and ground support, and start air upgrades.
