# N009 — Early-Midgame Defense Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240 seconds) with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. Both sides have Zerglings and Queens. Increase defense and continue economy. Maintain ground army and production. Consider adding Spine Crawlers for defense. Continue technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, with Zerglings and Queens. They have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and continue economy. Maintain ground army and production. Consider adding Spine Crawlers for defense. Continue technology and upgrades.

The opponent may be preparing an attack. Increasing defense helps survive early pressure. Spine Crawlers are cost-effective defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a standard early-midgame ground posture with a third base and more Zerglings.
