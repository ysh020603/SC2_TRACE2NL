# N001 — Early Game Queen Focus

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and no combat units yet. Opponent is also in early game with similar posture. Avoid building too many Zerglings early, as this can hurt economy. Avoid teching too fast without a solid ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture, but no reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. They appear to have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light or uncertain technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, but light defense and no combat units. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. You have at least one Queen.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid building too many Zerglings early, as this can hurt economy. Avoid teching too fast without a solid ground army.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have a solid economy and a few Zerglings, or if you scout an early pool or aggressive opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more standard Zergling/Queen composition with a third base, and start researching metabolic boost (speed) for Zerglings.
