# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both sides on ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and tech. Opponent shows Zergling and Queen cues, possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and tech while maintaining ground army strength. Keep an eye on defense and be ready to respond to potential pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and tech. Your army is primarily Zergling and Queen, with light defense. You have the capacity to continue developing.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while maintaining ground army strength. Keep an eye on defense and be ready to respond to potential pressure.

Both sides are in a similar macro-oriented state. Continuing to develop while maintaining a safety net allows you to stay ahead economically without overcommitting to army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a solid economy and tech base, ready to adapt to the opponent's next moves.
