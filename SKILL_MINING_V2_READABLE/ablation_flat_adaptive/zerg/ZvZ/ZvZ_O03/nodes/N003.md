# N003 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with both sides on ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and tech. Opponent shows Zergling and Queen cues, possible pressure. Continue focusing on economy and expansion while maintaining a minimal ground army for safety. Be ready to produce Zerglings if pressure comes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and tech. Your army is primarily Queen, with light defense. You are focusing on economic development.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue focusing on economy and expansion while maintaining a minimal ground army for safety. Be ready to produce Zerglings if pressure comes.

In the early game, a strong economy sets the foundation for the midgame. Keeping a light army allows you to invest more in workers and expansions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong economic lead that you can leverage in the midgame.
