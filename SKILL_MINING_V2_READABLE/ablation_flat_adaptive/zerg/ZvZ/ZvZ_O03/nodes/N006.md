## Summary

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in a ground-oriented macro posture with heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and tech. Your army includes Zergling and Queen, with heavy defense. You are prioritizing safety.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on defense and economy. Build a few extra Zerglings for safety while continuing to expand and tech.

Early game defense is crucial to avoid falling behind to early pressure. A strong economy will pay off later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
