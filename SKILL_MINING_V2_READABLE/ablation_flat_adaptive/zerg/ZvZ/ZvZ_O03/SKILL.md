# ZvZ_O03 Economy / Technology / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O03
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The strategy is flexible and observation-driven, adapting to the opponent's posture.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while expanding, keeping ground army strength as a safety net, and adapting to the opponent's observed posture.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations of the opponent's army composition, expansions, and tech.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Safety Checks

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with both sides on ground macro posture, heavy economy and expansions, moderate production and tech. Opponent shows Zergling and Queen cues, possible pressure.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech while maintaining ground army strength. Keep an eye on defense and be ready to respond to potential pressure.
