# ZvT_O03 Economy / Technology / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O03
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / technology / expansion opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The opponent is Terran, and the early game is characterized by heavy economy and technology investment from both sides.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while expanding, and maintain flexibility to adapt to the opponent's composition and pressure.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
