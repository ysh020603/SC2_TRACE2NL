# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Production Increase

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro, opponent ground with Siege Tank and Marine cues. Increase production and expand further to support a larger army. Continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy likely has Siege Tanks and Marines, indicating a defensive or timing-oriented ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro with moderate production and tech. Economy and expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expand further to support a larger army. Continue tech development.

Siege Tanks require a strong ground force to break, so building a larger army and economy is necessary.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a Roach or Zergling-based army with upgrades to challenge the Terran ground composition.
