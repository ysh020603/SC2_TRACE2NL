# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase, own ground-oriented macro posture, opponent likely ground with possible pressure. Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and technology development. Keep defense light but be ready to respond to pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Marine and Reaper cues. Production and technology appear heavy, but exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro posture with moderate production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Army consists of Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and technology development. Keep defense light but be ready to respond to pressure.

A balanced approach allows for economic growth while building a sufficient army to deter or respond to early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground composition (e.g., Roaches) or tech up for midgame options.
