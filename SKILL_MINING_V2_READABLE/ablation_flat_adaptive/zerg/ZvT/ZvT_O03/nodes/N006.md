# N006 — Midgame Tech Investment and Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own moderate production with heavy tech, opponent ground with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs. Continue tech development and increase army strength. Consider adding anti-air or detection for drops.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a mix of ground units including Medivacs, indicating potential for drops or bio pushes.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with moderate production but heavy tech investment. Economy and expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue tech development and increase army strength. Consider adding anti-air or detection for drops.

Medivacs enable drops, so having detection and anti-air is important. Tech can provide better units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Tech to units like Hydralisks or Infestors to counter bio and drops.
