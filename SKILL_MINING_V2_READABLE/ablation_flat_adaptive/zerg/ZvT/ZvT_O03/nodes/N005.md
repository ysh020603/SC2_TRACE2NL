# N005 — Early Game Economy and Expansion Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground macro, opponent unknown posture with heavy production and tech. Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has no reliable combat-unit cues, but production and tech appear heavy, suggesting a macro-oriented opening.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro with moderate production and tech. Economy is heavy, expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and expansion while maintaining a defensive posture. Continue tech development.

With no immediate threat, it's safe to prioritize economy and tech to gain a long-term advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong economy and tech base to support a midgame army.
