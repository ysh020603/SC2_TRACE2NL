# N009 — Early Game Tech and Expansion Balance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground macro, opponent unknown with Reaper cue. Maintain economy and tech development while being prepared for Reaper harassment. Consider adding a spine crawler or Queen for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a Reaper, indicating early harassment potential, but overall posture is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented macro with moderate production and tech. Economy is heavy, expansions are moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and tech development while being prepared for Reaper harassment. Consider adding a spine crawler or Queen for defense.

Reapers can harass workers, so a defensive measure is needed. Economy and tech remain priorities.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue expanding and teching while keeping a safe defense.
