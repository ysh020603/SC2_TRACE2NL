# ZvT_O01 Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O01
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on heavy economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game emphasizes Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and light technology investment. The strategy aims to outgrow the Terran opponent through superior economy and map presence, while staying flexible to adapt to observed Terran tech choices.

Develop a strong economy and expansion lead while building a ground-based army core. Maintain map presence and scouting to detect Terran tech transitions, and adapt your composition accordingly. Avoid overcommitting to a single tech path early, keeping options open for either ground or air responses.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on live scouting and enemy intelligence.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [POSITIVE] N001 — Early Game Economy Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180s) with both players on heavy economy and expansion. Terran shows ground posture with Marines/Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, light tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue expanding and increasing economy. Strengthen your ground army with additional Zerglings and Queens for defense. Maintain scouting to detect Terran tech transitions. Consider adding a Spawning Pool and starting Lair tech soon to unlock more options.
