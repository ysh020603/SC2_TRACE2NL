# N003 — Early Game Standard Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with Marines/Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, light tech. Continue developing your economy and expansions. Maintain a balanced approach: produce a few more Zerglings for safety, start Lair tech to unlock more options, and keep scouting. Do not overcommit to any single strategy yet.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran is expanding and teching, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Their production is moderate, but they are investing heavily in technology. Their exact tech direction is unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and expansions. Your army is small but sufficient for defense. Production is moderate, and you have not yet committed to a tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and expansions. Maintain a balanced approach: produce a few more Zerglings for safety, start Lair tech to unlock more options, and keep scouting. Do not overcommit to any single strategy yet.

This is a standard macro game. By maintaining a balanced economy and tech development, you keep your options open. Terran's heavy tech investment means they may be going for a specific composition, but you can adapt once you know what it is.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong economy with 3+ bases, then transition to a tech choice based on scouting. If Terran is going bio, prepare for a ground army with Banelings or Roaches. If they are going mech, consider tech to Brood Lords or Swarm Hosts later.
