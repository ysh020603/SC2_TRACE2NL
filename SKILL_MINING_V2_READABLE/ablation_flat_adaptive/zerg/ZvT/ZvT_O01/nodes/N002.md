# N002 — Early Game Expansion Stall

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with Marines/Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, light tech. Avoid cutting corners on defense. Do not ignore scouting, as Terran could be hiding a fast tech unit like Banshees or Cloaked Ghosts. Avoid expanding too greedily without army support.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran is also expanding, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Their production is moderate, but they are teching heavily. They could be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions, but your army is minimal. Production is moderate, and you have not invested in technology. You are vulnerable to early pressure if Terran decides to attack.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid cutting corners on defense. Do not ignore scouting, as Terran could be hiding a fast tech unit like Banshees or Cloaked Ghosts. Avoid expanding too greedily without army support.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck if Terran moves out with a large army or if you detect a tech building (e.g., Starport, Factory). Also recheck if you are supply blocked or have idle larvae.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure your economy with defensive structures and a small army, then transition to a tech choice. If Terran shows no aggression, continue droning and prepare for a mid-game tech switch.
