# N011 — Late-Mid Game Ground Army Stall

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Late-mid game (around 600s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with SiegeTanks, Marines, Reapers, Hellions, heavy production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, heavy production, heavy tech. Avoid sticking to a single unit type that is countered by Terran's composition. Do not neglect anti-air, as Terran could transition to air later. Avoid taking unfavorable engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their production and tech are heavy. They are likely to push with a mech or bio composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you have heavy production and tech. However, you are not using your tech advantage effectively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid sticking to a single unit type that is countered by Terran's composition. Do not neglect anti-air, as Terran could transition to air later. Avoid taking unfavorable engagements.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck if Terran transitions to air or if you see a large army moving out. Also recheck if you are supply blocked or have idle larvae.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a counter army and push to break Terran's defenses. Continue expanding and upgrading to maintain your advantage.
