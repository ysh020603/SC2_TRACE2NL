# N012 — Mid-Game Ground Army Stall

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Mid-game (around 420s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with SiegeTanks, Marines, Reapers, Hellions, heavy production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings, Hydralisks, Queens, moderate production, heavy tech. Avoid neglecting anti-air, as Terran could transition to air later. Do not overcommit to a single unit type that is countered by Terran's composition. Avoid taking unfavorable engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their production and tech are heavy. They are likely to push with a mech or bio composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens. Production is moderate, but you have heavy tech. You are at a point to increase production and improve composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid neglecting anti-air, as Terran could transition to air later. Do not overcommit to a single unit type that is countered by Terran's composition. Avoid taking unfavorable engagements.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck if Terran transitions to air or if you see a large army moving out. Also recheck if you are supply blocked or have idle larvae.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Increase production and build a counter army to push Terran. Continue expanding and upgrading to maintain your advantage.
