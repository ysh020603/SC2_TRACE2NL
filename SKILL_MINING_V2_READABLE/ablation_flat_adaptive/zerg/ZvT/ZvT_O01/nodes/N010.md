# N010 — Early-Mid Game Production Increase

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 300s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with Marines/Reapers, heavy production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, light tech. Increase your production by adding more Hatcheries and tech structures. Start Lair tech to unlock more options. Continue expanding and droning to support your army. Maintain scouting to determine Terran's tech direction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a ground army with Marines and Reapers. Their production is heavy, and they are teching heavily. They could be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is small, mostly Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and you have not invested in technology. You are at a point to increase production and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production by adding more Hatcheries and tech structures. Start Lair tech to unlock more options. Continue expanding and droning to support your army. Maintain scouting to determine Terran's tech direction.

You need to increase production to match Terran's heavy production. By adding more Hatcheries and tech, you can produce a larger army and have more options. Terran's heavy tech investment means they may be vulnerable to a timing attack if you can mass a sufficient force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Increase production and tech to support a larger army. Aim for a mid-game timing attack or a strong defensive position.
