# N001 — Early Game Economy Expansion

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with both players on heavy economy and expansion. Terran shows ground posture with Marines/Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, light tech. Continue expanding and increasing economy. Strengthen your ground army with additional Zerglings and Queens for defense. Maintain scouting to detect Terran tech transitions. Consider adding a Spawning Pool and starting Lair tech soon to unlock more options.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran is also expanding heavily, with a ground-oriented army of Marines and Reapers. Their production is moderate, but they are investing heavily in technology, suggesting a potential tech transition soon. Their exact composition and tech choices are uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economic base with multiple expansions. Your army is small but sufficient for defense, consisting of Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and you have not yet committed to a specific tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy. Strengthen your ground army with additional Zerglings and Queens for defense. Maintain scouting to detect Terran tech transitions. Consider adding a Spawning Pool and starting Lair tech soon to unlock more options.

Your economic lead is your main advantage. By continuing to expand and drone up, you can afford a larger army and faster tech later. Terran's heavy tech investment means they may be vulnerable to a timing attack if you can mass a sufficient ground force before their tech completes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong economy with 3+ bases, then transition to a tech choice based on scouting. If Terran is going bio, prepare for a ground army with Banelings or Roaches. If they are going mech, consider tech to Brood Lords or Swarm Hosts later.
