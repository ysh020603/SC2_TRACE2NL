# N005 — Early Game Tech Advantage

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran posture unknown, but production moderate and tech heavy. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, light tech. Take advantage of your economic lead by expanding further and droning up. Start Lair tech to unlock more options. Maintain scouting to determine Terran's tech direction. Consider a quick tech to Mutalisks or Roaches to pressure Terran before their tech completes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran's army composition is unknown, but they are investing heavily in technology. Their production is moderate, and they are expanding. This suggests they may be going for a tech-heavy composition, possibly air or mech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is small but sufficient for defense. Production is moderate, and you have not yet invested in technology. You have a good economic foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Take advantage of your economic lead by expanding further and droning up. Start Lair tech to unlock more options. Maintain scouting to determine Terran's tech direction. Consider a quick tech to Mutalisks or Roaches to pressure Terran before their tech completes.

Your economy is strong, and Terran's tech investment means they may be vulnerable to a timing attack. By teching quickly, you can hit them before their tech pays off. Alternatively, you can use your economic lead to outproduce them in a macro game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use your economic lead to tech quickly and either pressure Terran or outmacro them. Aim for a strong mid-game army composition.
