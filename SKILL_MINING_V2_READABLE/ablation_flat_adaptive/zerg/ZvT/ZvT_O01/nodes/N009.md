# N009 — Early-Mid Game Tech Stall

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 360s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with Marines/Reapers, heavy production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, heavy tech. Avoid sitting on your tech without producing units. Do not neglect scouting, as Terran could be hiding a tech switch. Avoid overexpanding without army support.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a ground army with Marines and Reapers. Their production is heavy, and they are teching heavily. They could be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is small, mostly Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, but you have invested heavily in technology. You are at a point where you need to use your tech advantage.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid sitting on your tech without producing units. Do not neglect scouting, as Terran could be hiding a tech switch. Avoid overexpanding without army support.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck if Terran moves out with a large army or if you are supply blocked. Also recheck if you have idle larvae or resources.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Produce a tech-based army and use it to pressure Terran or defend against their push. Continue expanding and upgrading to maintain your advantage.
