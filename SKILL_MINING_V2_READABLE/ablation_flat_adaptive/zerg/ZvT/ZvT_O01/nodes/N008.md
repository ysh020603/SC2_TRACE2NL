# N008 — Early-Mid Game Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 300s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, heavy production, heavy tech. Zerg has Zerglings/Queens, moderate production, moderate tech. Invest in technology to unlock more units. Based on scouting, choose a tech path: if Terran is going bio, get Baneling Nest and Roach Warren; if they are going mech, consider getting Lair for Mutalisks or Swarm Hosts. Continue expanding and droning to support your tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their production is heavy, and they are teching heavily. They could be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is small, mostly Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, and you have invested some in technology. You are at a point to decide your tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Invest in technology to unlock more units. Based on scouting, choose a tech path: if Terran is going bio, get Baneling Nest and Roach Warren; if they are going mech, consider getting Lair for Mutalisks or Swarm Hosts. Continue expanding and droning to support your tech.

At this point, you need to commit to a tech path to stay competitive. Terran's heavy production and tech investment means they will have a strong army soon. By choosing the right tech, you can counter their composition and maintain your economic lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a tech-based army composition that counters Terran's. Continue expanding and droning to support your army. Aim for a mid-game timing attack or a strong defensive position.
