# N004 — Early-Mid Game Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 240s) with heavy economy and expansion for both. Terran ground posture with Marines/Reapers, heavy production, heavy tech. Zerg has Queens, moderate production, light tech. Start your tech transition. Based on scouting, choose a tech path: if Terran is going bio, get Baneling Nest and Roach Warren; if they are going mech, consider getting Lair for Mutalisks or Swarm Hosts. Continue expanding and droning to support your tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran is heavily producing and teching, with a ground army of Marines and Reapers. Their production is heavy, and they are investing heavily in technology. They could be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch to air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and expansions. Your army is small, mostly Queens, and you have not yet invested in technology. Production is moderate, and you are at a critical point to choose a tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Start your tech transition. Based on scouting, choose a tech path: if Terran is going bio, get Baneling Nest and Roach Warren; if they are going mech, consider getting Lair for Mutalisks or Swarm Hosts. Continue expanding and droning to support your tech.

At this point, you need to commit to a tech path to stay competitive. Terran's heavy production and tech investment means they will have a strong army soon. By choosing the right tech, you can counter their composition and maintain your economic lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a tech-based army composition that counters Terran's. Continue expanding and droning to support your army. Aim for a mid-game timing attack or a strong defensive position.
