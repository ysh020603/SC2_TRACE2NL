# ZvT_O02 Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O02
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on establishing a strong economy and expanding while keeping production and technology light or uncertain. It is flexible and can adapt to either a macro-oriented or aggressive ground-based approach depending on scouting information.

Develop a economy / expansion / ground posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: light_or_uncertain
- Production: light_or_uncertain
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, both sides are in early game with limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own posture is also uncertain.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Prioritize economy and expansion, but be ready to adjust if scouting reveals aggression.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

**Trigger situation:**  
The game progresses to early-midgame. The opponent's posture remains unknown, but you have had time to expand and develop.

**Direction:**  
Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for potential aggression. Consider adding a few defensive units or scouting overlords to gain information.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

**Trigger situation:**  
The game reaches the midgame. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have had time to build a strong economy.

**Direction:**

**Read for details:** `N003`

**Trigger situation:**  
The game enters the late-midgame. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have a strong economy and army.
