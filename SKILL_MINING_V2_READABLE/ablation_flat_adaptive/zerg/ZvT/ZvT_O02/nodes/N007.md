# N007 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Own Ground Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The opponent shows a ground-based macro posture with heavy production and technology, possibly with Marines. Your own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production. Strengthen your ground army and continue to increase your economy. Consider teching to units that counter Marines, such as Banelings or Roaches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with representative cue Marine. Production and technology are heavy, and they may be planning a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with moderate production and heavy expansion. You are in a good position to match the opponent's ground game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to increase your economy. Consider teching to units that counter Marines, such as Banelings or Roaches.

You are both going for a ground-based macro game. You need to out-produce and out-tech your opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a strong ground army and a solid economy to support it.
