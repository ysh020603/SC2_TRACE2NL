# N002 — Early-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game progresses to early-midgame. The opponent's posture remains unknown, but you have had time to expand and develop. Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for potential aggression. Consider adding a few defensive units or scouting overlords to gain information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent could be teching, expanding, or preparing a timing attack. Their production and technology are light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you may have a second base and some basic units. Continue to focus on macro.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for potential aggression. Consider adding a few defensive units or scouting overlords to gain information.

As the game enters the midgame, the opponent's plan becomes more critical. Maintaining economy while preparing for defense is a safe approach.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid economy with at least three bases and a modest army to defend against early pushes.
