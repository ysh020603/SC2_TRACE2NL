# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game reaches the midgame. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have had time to build a strong economy. Maintain current development path, but start to transition into a more specific strategy based on your scouting. Consider teching to a midgame composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent could be hiding a tech switch or preparing a large army. Their production and technology are light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you likely have multiple bases and a decent army. You need to decide whether to tech up or mass units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start to transition into a more specific strategy based on your scouting. Consider teching to a midgame composition.

In the midgame, you need to have a plan. Without information, it's best to stay flexible and avoid committing to a composition that could be countered.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a solid economy and a midgame army composition that can handle common Terran strategies.
