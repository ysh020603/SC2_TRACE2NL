# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides are in early game with limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own posture is also uncertain. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Prioritize economy and expansion, but be ready to adjust if scouting reveals aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production and technology are light or uncertain. They could be going for an early expand, a tech rush, or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units yet. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks active. Prioritize economy and expansion, but be ready to adjust if scouting reveals aggression.

Early game is about maximizing economy and flexibility. Overcommitting to a specific plan without information can be punished. Maintaining a balanced approach allows adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy with at least two bases and a basic defensive structure (e.g., spine crawlers) before transitioning to more specific strategies.
