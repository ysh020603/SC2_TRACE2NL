## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The opponent shows a ground-based macro posture with heavy production and technology, possibly with Marines and Reapers. Your own posture is still unknown. Maintain current development path, but start to strengthen your ground army. Consider teching to units that counter Marines and Reapers, such as Banelings or Roaches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with representative cues like Marine and Reaper. Production and technology are heavy, and they may be planning a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain. You need to respond to the opponent's ground-heavy approach.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start to strengthen your ground army. Consider teching to units that counter Marines and Reapers, such as Banelings or Roaches.

The opponent is committing to a ground army. You need to prepare a ground defense and potentially a counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a solid ground army and defensive structures to hold off any early pushes.
