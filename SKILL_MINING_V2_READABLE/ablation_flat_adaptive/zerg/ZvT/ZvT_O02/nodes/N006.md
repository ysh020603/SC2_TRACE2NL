## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The opponent shows a ground-based macro posture with heavy production and technology, possibly with Reapers and Battlecruisers. Your own posture is still unknown. Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for both ground and air threats. Consider teching to units like Hydralisks or Mutalisks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with representative cues like Reaper and Battlecruiser. Production and technology are heavy, and they may be transitioning to air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain. You need to respond to the opponent's ground-heavy approach, but also be aware of potential air threats.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for both ground and air threats. Consider teching to units like Hydralisks or Mutalisks.

The opponent has a mix of ground and air units. You need a balanced army to counter both.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a versatile army that can handle both ground and air threats.
