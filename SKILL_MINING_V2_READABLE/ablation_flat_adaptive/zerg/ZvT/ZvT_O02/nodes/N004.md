# N004 — Late-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game enters the late-midgame. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have a strong economy and army. Maintain current development path, but start to consider aggressive options. If you have a supply advantage, you might want to push.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent could be preparing a massive army or a tech switch. Their production and technology are light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you likely have a large army and multiple bases. You need to decide whether to push or continue teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start to consider aggressive options. If you have a supply advantage, you might want to push.

In the late-midgame, you need to start applying pressure or you will fall behind. Without information, it's best to keep your army mobile and ready to respond.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Have a strong army and be ready to transition into a late-game composition or a timing attack.
