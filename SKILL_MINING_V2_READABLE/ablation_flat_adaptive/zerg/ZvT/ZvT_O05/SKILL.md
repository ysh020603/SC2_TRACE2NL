# ZvT_O05 Expansion / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O05
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on heavy economy and expansion while maintaining moderate production and light technology. The goal is to develop a strong macro foundation and adapt to the Terran's ground-oriented posture.

Develop a expansion / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy, and light or uncertain technology. Opponent shows possible pressure with Marines and Reapers.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and expansion. Continue technology development with safety checks.
