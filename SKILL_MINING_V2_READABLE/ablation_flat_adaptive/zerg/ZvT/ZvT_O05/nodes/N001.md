# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy, and light or uncertain technology. Opponent shows possible pressure with Marines and Reapers. Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and expansion. Continue technology development with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Queens as the main unit. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army while maintaining economy and expansion. Continue technology development with safety checks.

Early game is about establishing a solid economy and tech base. Since the opponent is ground-oriented with possible early pressure, having a defensive ground army is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground composition with more production and technology as the game progresses.
