# N001 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, but limited army information. Enemy scouting is uncertain, possibly Terran with Marines only. Maintain economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and continue technology development. Keep defense light but be ready to react if pressure comes.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is likely on a heavy economy and expansion too, with moderate production and heavy tech investment. Only Marines have been spotted, so their army composition is unclear. They could be preparing for a tech switch or early aggression.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. Army is ground-oriented with Queens and possibly Zerglings. Defense is light, so you are vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and expansion, strengthen ground army, and continue technology development. Keep defense light but be ready to react if pressure comes.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to invest in tech and army. Since enemy is unknown, it's safer to develop a solid foundation before committing to a specific strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined strategy once you have better scouting information, either continuing economy or shifting to army production.
