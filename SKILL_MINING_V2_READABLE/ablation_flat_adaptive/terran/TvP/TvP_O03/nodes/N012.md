# N012 — Midgame Ground vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 9 minutes) with opponent showing air presence (Carriers), your posture is ground macro with Siege Tanks and Marines. Maintain ground army strength and continue production. Consider adding Vikings or Thors to counter Carriers. Keep upgrades and scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Stalker, Carrier. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength and continue production. Consider adding Vikings or Thors to counter Carriers. Keep upgrades and scouting.

Carriers are powerful air units, so you need anti-air support. Siege Tanks provide ground defense, and Medivacs keep your army alive.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
