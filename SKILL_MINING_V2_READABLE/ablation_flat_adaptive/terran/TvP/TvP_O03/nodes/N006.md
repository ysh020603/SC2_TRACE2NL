# N006 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with opponent showing ground army, but you have moderate air presence and are transitioning to air. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue production and technology, and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army but also moderate air presence (Banshees). Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces. Continue production and technology, and consider expanding further.

Transitioning to air can give you an advantage if the opponent is heavily ground-based. Banshees can harass, and later you can add Battlecruisers.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a strong air army with Battlecruisers and support.
