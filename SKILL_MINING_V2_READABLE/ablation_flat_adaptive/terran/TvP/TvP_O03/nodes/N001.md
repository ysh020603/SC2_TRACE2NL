# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and technology, production moderate, opponent posture unknown. Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Strengthen ground forces gradually and consider scouting to clarify opponent intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are developing a heavy economy and technology base with moderate production. Your army is light and ground-oriented, with Marines as a representative unit. Expansion is heavy, indicating a macro-oriented start.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Strengthen ground forces gradually and consider scouting to clarify opponent intentions.

This opening aims to leverage a strong economy and tech advantage. Early safety checks are needed to avoid being caught off guard by aggressive plays.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined army composition based on scouting, either continuing ground macro or branching into air if opponent commits to air.
