# N010 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with opponent unknown but defensive posture, your posture is tech-heavy with a Reaper for scouting. Continue developing economy and technology while using the Reaper for scouting. Increase production and maintain a light defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production. You have a Reaper for scouting and harassment. Your defense is light, and you are expanding moderately.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while using the Reaper for scouting. Increase production and maintain a light defense.

This is a safe opening that focuses on tech and economy. The Reaper provides valuable information about the opponent's plans.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined army composition based on scouting.
