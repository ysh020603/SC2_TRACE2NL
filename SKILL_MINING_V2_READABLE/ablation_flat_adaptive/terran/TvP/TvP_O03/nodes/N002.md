# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 4 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy, production, and technology, opponent confirmed ground posture. Continue strengthening ground forces and maintain production. Keep expanding and upgrading, but stay alert for possible pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground macro posture with heavy production and technology. Your army is ground-based with Marines, and you have a strong economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces and maintain production. Keep expanding and upgrading, but stay alert for possible pressure.

With both sides on heavy macro, the game is likely to be decided by army composition and upgrades. Maintaining a strong economy and tech will allow you to outproduce.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a midgame army with a mix of bio and mechanical units, such as Siege Tanks and Medivacs.
