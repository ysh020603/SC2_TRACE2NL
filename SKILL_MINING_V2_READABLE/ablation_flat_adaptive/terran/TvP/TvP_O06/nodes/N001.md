# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy own economy and tech, but opponent posture is unknown with light defense and expansion. Own army is ground-oriented with Marines. Avoid over-committing to economy without army, as early pressure could punish you.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Production appears moderate, tech investment heavy, but expansion and defense are light or uncertain. This could indicate a tech rush or hidden expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a heavy economy and tech investment, expanding aggressively. Your army is ground-based, currently with Marines, and you have heavy production. Defense is light, and you are vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-committing to economy without army, as early pressure could punish you.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when opponent's first combat units are revealed or when you have a sufficient defensive force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a solid ground army composition with Siege Tanks and Marines, while maintaining economic lead.
