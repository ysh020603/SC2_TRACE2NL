## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with own ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, Battlecruiser) and opponent showing Void Ray. Increase air presence and tech, consider adding more Battlecruisers or Vikings to counter Void Rays.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Void Ray, indicating a possible air transition. Their production and tech are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and a Battlecruiser, heavy economy and tech. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and tech, consider adding more Battlecruisers or Vikings to counter Void Rays.

Void Rays are strong against ground, so you need air superiority or strong anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
