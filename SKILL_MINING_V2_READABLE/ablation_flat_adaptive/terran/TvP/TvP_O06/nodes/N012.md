# N012 — Early-Midgame vs Protoss Air with Tempest

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 360s) with own ground army (Marines, Reapers, Hellions, Battlecruiser) and opponent air posture with Tempest. Increase air presence and tech, consider adding more Battlecruisers or Vikings to counter Tempests.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with Zealot, Stalker, Tempest. They have a mix of ground and air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and a Battlecruiser, heavy economy and tech. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and tech, consider adding more Battlecruisers or Vikings to counter Tempests.

Tempests are long-range siege units, so you need to close the distance or have your own air superiority.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
