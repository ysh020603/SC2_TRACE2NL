# N009 — Early Game with Zealot Cue and Moderate Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own ground army (Marines) and opponent showing Zealot, but economy moderate. Strengthen ground forces, continue economy and tech, but be ready for early Zealot pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Zealot, indicating a possible early ground pressure. Their economy is heavy, but expansion is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, heavy production and tech, but economy is moderate. You are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, continue economy and tech, but be ready for early Zealot pressure.

Zealots can be dangerous early, so you need enough Marines and possibly a bunker to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a solid ground army with Siege Tanks and Marauders to handle Zealots.
