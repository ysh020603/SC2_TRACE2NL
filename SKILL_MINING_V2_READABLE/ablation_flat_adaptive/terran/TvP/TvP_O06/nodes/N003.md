# N003 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Protoss Ground Cues

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300s) with own ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines) and opponent showing ground posture with Warp Prism. Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and tech, but be ready to defend against warp prism plays.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Warp Prism, heavy production and tech. They are likely preparing for warp prism-based harassment or a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy economy and tech. Your expansion is heavy, and you are in a good position to continue macro.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and tech, but be ready to defend against warp prism plays.

Warp Prism indicates potential for harassment or all-in. Keeping a strong ground force and defensive positioning is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more mobile army with Medivacs and possibly Widow Mines for defense.
