# N005 — Early-Midgame Defensive Posture with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240s) with own ground army (Siege Tanks, Reapers) and opponent unknown but with heavy defense. Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and tech, but be cautious about attacking into a fortified position.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with no reliable combat-unit cues, but heavy defense. They may be turtling or preparing a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Reapers, heavy economy and tech, and are expanding. Your defense is light, but you have the tools to defend.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and tech, but be cautious about attacking into a fortified position.

Heavy defense suggests the opponent is not planning early aggression, so you can focus on macro and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame army with Medivacs and possibly air support to break a turtle.
