DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480s) with own ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, Battlecruiser) and opponent ground posture with Stalkers, Immortals. Maintain ground strength, continue tech to support Battlecruiser, and consider adding more air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalkers, Warp Prism, Observer, Immortal. They have a heavy ground army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and a Battlecruiser, heavy economy and tech. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength, continue tech to support Battlecruiser, and consider adding more air units.

Battlecruiser provides a strong late-game option, but you need to protect it from Stalkers. Continue building a balanced army.
