## Summary

## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Warp Prism, heavy production and tech. They may be preparing for a timing attack or harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Widow Mines, heavy economy and tech, and are expanding aggressively. Production is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and strengthening ground forces, but maintain defensive structures to counter warp prism plays.

Widow Mines are good against ground harassment, but you need to ensure your economy is protected.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
