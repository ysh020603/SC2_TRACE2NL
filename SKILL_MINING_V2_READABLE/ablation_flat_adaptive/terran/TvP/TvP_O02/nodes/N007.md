# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Confrontation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers. You have a strong ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy. Consider adding more production to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers. Their economy is heavy, and they are likely preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a strong ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Widow Mines. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy. Consider adding more production to support a larger army.

The enemy's heavy ground army requires a strong response. You need to match their army size and composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a larger ground army to counter the enemy's push.
