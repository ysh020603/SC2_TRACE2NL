## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. Their economy is heavy, and they are likely expanding. They may be preparing for a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units yet. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path. Keep scouting to gather information. Consider adding production structures if you plan to transition.

Maintaining flexibility allows you to react to the enemy's first real move.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gather enough information to choose a specific army composition or tech path.
