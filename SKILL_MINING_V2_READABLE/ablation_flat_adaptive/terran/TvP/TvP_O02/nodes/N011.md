# N011 — Early-Midgame Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Enemy has a heavy economy and production, but no confirmed combat units. You have a moderate economy and heavy tech investment. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Consider adding production structures to support a ground composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy economy and production, but no reliable combat-unit cues. They could be teching or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have heavy tech investment. You have a few Marines. Focus on developing your tech and army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Consider adding production structures to support a ground composition.

The enemy's heavy economy suggests they are preparing for a long game. You need to be ready to defend or attack with a solid ground army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army to match the enemy's economy.
