# N001 — Early Game Uncertainty

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. Enemy posture is unknown, and your own production and tech are still developing. Maintain current development path. Keep building workers, supply, and basic infrastructure. Avoid overcommitting to a specific strategy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production and tech investment are light or uncertain. They could be going for any opening.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light and uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units yet. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path. Keep building workers, supply, and basic infrastructure. Avoid overcommitting to a specific strategy.

Early game is about information gathering and economic stability. Overcommitting without knowledge can be punished.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and production base to support future tech or army choices.
