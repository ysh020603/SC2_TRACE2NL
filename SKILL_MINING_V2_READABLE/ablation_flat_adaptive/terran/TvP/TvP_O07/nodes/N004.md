# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense and Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) when you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and tech, including Immortals. Increase defense while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Maintain your ground army and consider upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Representative cues may include Stalker, Sentry, Observer, Immortal. Defense is moderate, expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include SiegeTank, Marine, Medivac. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Maintain your ground army and consider upgrades.

As the game progresses, the opponent's army becomes more powerful. Strengthening defense and maintaining a strong economy ensures you can withstand potential pushes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more stable late-game composition with higher tech and upgrades.
