# N002 — Midgame Ground vs Ground with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 540s) when you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and tech, including units like Immortal. Increase your air presence and technology while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain economy and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Representative cues may include Stalker, Sentry, Observer, Immortal. Defense is moderate, expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper, Hellion. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain economy and defense.

The opponent's heavy ground composition with Immortals requires a mix of anti-armor and mobility. Adding air units can provide flexibility and counter potential threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with air support and higher tech.
