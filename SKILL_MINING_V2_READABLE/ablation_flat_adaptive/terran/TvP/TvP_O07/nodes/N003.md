# N003 — Early Game Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) when you have a ground army with moderate production and heavy tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain expansion and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Representative cues may include Zealot. Expansion is light or uncertain, defense is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with moderate production and heavy tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include Marine. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain expansion and defense.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base. Keeping a solid ground core allows you to respond to any early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier production and tech state as you approach midgame.
