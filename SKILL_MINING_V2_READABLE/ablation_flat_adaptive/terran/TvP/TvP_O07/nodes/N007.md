# N007 — Early Game Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) when you have a ground army with moderate production and heavy tech, and the opponent's posture is unknown with moderate production and heavy tech. Continue developing your economy and tech while maintaining a flexible army. Increase expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain; representative cues may include Sentry. Production is moderate, tech is heavy, expansion is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with moderate production and heavy tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include Marine. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while maintaining a flexible army. Increase expansion and production.

With limited information, it's important to build a solid foundation that can adapt to any opponent strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once the opponent's strategy becomes clearer.
