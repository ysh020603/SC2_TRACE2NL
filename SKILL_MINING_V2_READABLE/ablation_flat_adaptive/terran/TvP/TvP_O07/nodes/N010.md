# N010 — Late-Midgame vs Air with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) when you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows an air posture with heavy production and tech, including Phoenix. Maintain your ground army and continue developing economy and tech. Consider increasing your air presence to counter the opponent's air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with heavy production and tech. Representative cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Phoenix, Observer. Defense is heavy, expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper, Hellion. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue developing economy and tech. Consider increasing your air presence to counter the opponent's air.

The opponent's air-heavy composition requires you to have anti-air capabilities. Increasing your own air presence can provide flexibility and counter their air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air capabilities.
