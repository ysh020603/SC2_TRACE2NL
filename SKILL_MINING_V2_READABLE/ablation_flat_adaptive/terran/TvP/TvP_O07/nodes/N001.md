# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game (around 240s) when you have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defense and consider increasing expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Representative cues may include Stalker. Expansion is heavy, defense is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include Reaper, Marine. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defense and consider increasing expansion.

Your heavy economy and tech advantage can be leveraged to outscale the opponent. Maintaining a strong ground core provides safety while you expand.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army composition with more production and tech.
