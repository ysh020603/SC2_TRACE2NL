# N006 — Early-Mid Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300s) when you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows an air posture with heavy production and tech, including Oracle. Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air units. Continue developing economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with heavy production and tech. Representative cues may include Zealot, Oracle. Defense is light or uncertain, expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army while considering adding anti-air units. Continue developing economy and tech.

The opponent's air presence requires you to prepare anti-air defenses. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to transition if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle air threats while maintaining a strong ground core.
