# N005 — Midgame Ground vs Ground with VoidRay

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420s) when you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and tech, including VoidRay. Maintain your ground army and continue developing economy and tech. Consider adding anti-air if the VoidRay presence becomes significant.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Representative cues may include Zealot, VoidRay. Defense is heavy, expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include SiegeTank, Marine, Hellion, Medivac.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue developing economy and tech. Consider adding anti-air if the VoidRay presence becomes significant.

The opponent's composition includes a potential air threat. Maintaining a strong ground core while preparing anti-air ensures you can handle both ground and air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air threats.
