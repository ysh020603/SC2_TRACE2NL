# N011 — Midgame Ground vs Ground with VoidRay (Maintain)

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420s) when you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and the opponent shows a ground posture with heavy production and tech, including VoidRay. Maintain your current army path and continue developing economy and tech. Avoid unnecessary risks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and tech. Representative cues may include Zealot, VoidRay. Defense is heavy, expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with heavy production and tech, economy and expansion are heavy. Representative units may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue developing economy and tech. Avoid unnecessary risks.

The opponent's composition is similar to yours, so maintaining your current path and focusing on macro can give you an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing and look for opportunities to gain an advantage.
