# N002 — Early Game Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and technology, opponent posture unknown with Zealot cue, own posture unknown with Reaper cue. Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue production and technology. Maintain expansions and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture with Zealot cue. Production moderate, technology heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Unknown army orientation with moderate production and heavy technology. Economy heavy, expansions heavy. Defense light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy, continue production and technology. Maintain expansions and defense.

Early game uncertainty calls for safe development. Building a ground core while expanding economy and tech keeps options open.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined ground posture as more scouting information is gathered.
