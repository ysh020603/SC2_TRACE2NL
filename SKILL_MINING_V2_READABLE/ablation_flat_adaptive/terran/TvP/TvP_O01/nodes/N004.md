# N004 — Late Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with both sides having heavy economy and technology, opponent ground posture with Zealot/Stalker/Sentry/Immortal, own ground posture with Marine/Reaper/Marauder and moderate air. Strengthen ground army, increase air, increase economy, continue production and technology. Maintain defense and expansions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Zealot, Stalker, Sentry, Immortal cues. Production and technology heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions heavy. Defense heavy. Moderate air presence. Possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase air, increase economy, continue production and technology. Maintain defense and expansions.

With opponent fielding a strong ground army including Immortals, adding air units provides a counter. Increasing economy and tech supports a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army with air support to counter the opponent's ground composition.
