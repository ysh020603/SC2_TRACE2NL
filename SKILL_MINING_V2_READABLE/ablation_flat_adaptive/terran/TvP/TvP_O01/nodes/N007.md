- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Colossus, Zealot, Stalker, Observer cues. Production and technology heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions heavy. Defense heavy. Possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, maintain defense, increase economy, maintain expansions and production. Continue technology.

WidowMines provide anti-ground splash and can help against Zealots. Maintaining a strong ground force while growing economy is key.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
