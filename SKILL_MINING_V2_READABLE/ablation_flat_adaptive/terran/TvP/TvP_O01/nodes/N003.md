# N003 — Early Midgame Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame with opponent unknown posture but heavy defense, own ground posture with heavy production and Reaper cue. Strengthen ground army, continue defense, increase economy and expansions, continue production and technology, increase upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows unknown posture with Zealot cue. Production moderate, technology heavy. Defense heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions heavy. Defense light or uncertain. Possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, continue defense, increase economy and expansions, continue production and technology, increase upgrades.

Heavy production allows for a strong army, while increasing upgrades and economy prepares for a midgame push. Defense is maintained to avoid vulnerability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once upgrades and army strength are sufficient.
