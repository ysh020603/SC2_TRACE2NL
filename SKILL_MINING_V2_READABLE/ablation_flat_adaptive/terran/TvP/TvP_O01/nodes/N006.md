# N006 — Midgame Ground vs Colossus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with opponent ground posture including Colossus, own ground posture with Marine/Reaper/Marauder. Strengthen ground army, increase defense, increase economy, maintain expansions, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Colossus, Zealot, Stalker, Observer cues. Production and technology heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions heavy. Defense heavy. Possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase defense, increase economy, maintain expansions, continue production and technology.

Colossus requires careful positioning and possibly air support. Strengthening ground forces and defense helps mitigate splash damage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle Colossus, possibly with Vikings or more mobile units.
