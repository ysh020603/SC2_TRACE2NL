# N008 — Midgame Air vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with opponent air posture including VoidRay, own ground posture with SiegeTank/Marine/WidowMine. Strengthen ground army, increase defense, increase economy, maintain expansions, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows air posture with Stalker, VoidRay cues. Production and technology heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions heavy. Defense heavy. Possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase defense, increase economy, maintain expansions, continue production and technology.

VoidRays are strong against ground, so increasing defense and possibly adding anti-air units is necessary. Maintaining economy supports a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with anti-air capabilities, such as Marines with upgrades or Vikings.
