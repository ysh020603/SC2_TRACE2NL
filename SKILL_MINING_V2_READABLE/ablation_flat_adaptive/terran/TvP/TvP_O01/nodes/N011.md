# N011 — Late Midgame Air vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with opponent air posture including Sentry/VoidRay, own ground posture with Marine. Strengthen ground army, increase defense, increase economy, maintain expansions, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows air posture with Stalker, Sentry, VoidRay cues. Production and technology heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions heavy. Defense heavy. Possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase defense, increase economy, maintain expansions, continue production and technology.

Late game air composition requires a robust anti-air response. Increasing defense and economy supports a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with strong anti-air, such as Marines with upgrades and Vikings.
