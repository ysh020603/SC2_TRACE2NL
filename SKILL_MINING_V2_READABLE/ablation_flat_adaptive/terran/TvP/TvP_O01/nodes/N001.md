## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame with both sides having heavy economy and technology, opponent showing ground posture with Zealot/Stalker, own ground posture with Marine/Reaper. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue production and technology. Maintain air and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealot and Stalker cues. Production and technology appear heavy. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented army with moderate production and heavy technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is moderate. Potential pressure possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and expansions, continue production and technology. Maintain air and defense.

With both sides investing heavily, maintaining a strong economy and tech while building a solid ground force provides a stable foundation. Increasing expansions supports long-term production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army with more production and tech, while keeping economy growing.
