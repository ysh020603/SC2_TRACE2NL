# TvP_O04 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvP_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Protoss
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that prioritizes economic and technological development while maintaining a flexible, ground-oriented posture. Early scouting with Reaper is common, and the build aims to transition into a heavy production and tech-heavy midgame.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while preserving flexibility to adapt to Protoss tech choices and pressure.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and enemy intelligence.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase (~4 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy scouting is uncertain but may show Stalker. Own army is ground-oriented with Marines, production and tech are heavy.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current air and defense posture, but be ready to increase defense if scouting shows aggression.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [NEGATIVE] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Defense

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase (~5 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy is ground-oriented with Stalkers, production and tech heavy. Own army includes Marines and Reapers, defense is moderate.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid neglecting defense entirely, as enemy may push with Stalkers. Also avoid over-investing in defense at the cost of economy.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Early-Midgame Tech and Air Investment
