# N003 — Early-Midgame Tech and Air Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (~5 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy is ground-oriented with Stalkers, production and tech heavy. Own army includes Marines and Reapers, defense moderate. Increase your technology investment and consider adding air units or upgrades. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain defense. Keep expanding your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a heavy economy and tech, with heavy production. They are ground-oriented with Stalkers, and may be teching towards air or other advanced units. Their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and tech. Your army is ground-focused with Marines and Reapers. Defense is moderate, and you have the resources to invest further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your technology investment and consider adding air units or upgrades. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain defense. Keep expanding your economy.

With enemy teching heavily, investing in your own tech and possibly air units gives you flexibility to counter their composition. Maintaining a strong ground army ensures you are not vulnerable to ground pushes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a tech-heavy midgame army with both ground and air capabilities, ready to adapt to enemy tech choices.
