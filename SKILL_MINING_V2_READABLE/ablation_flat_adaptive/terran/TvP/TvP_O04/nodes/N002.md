# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Defense

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (~5 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy is ground-oriented with Stalkers, production and tech heavy. Own army includes Marines and Reapers, defense is moderate. Avoid neglecting defense entirely, as enemy may push with Stalkers. Also avoid over-investing in defense at the cost of economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a heavy economy and tech, with heavy production. They are ground-oriented with Stalkers, and may be preparing pressure. Their expansion is heavy, so they are likely macroing.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and tech. Your army is ground-focused with Marines and Reapers. Defense is moderate, so you have some safety but should not be complacent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid neglecting defense entirely, as enemy may push with Stalkers. Also avoid over-investing in defense at the cost of economy.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have better scouting on enemy army size or if you see signs of an attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger midgame army while maintaining a safe defense, ready to punish any overextension by the enemy.
