# N008 — Midgame vs Heavy Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase (~9 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy has heavy air presence with Stalkers, Sentries, Phoenixes, and Immortals. Own army is ground-focused with Marines and Marauders, defense heavy. Increase your air presence by adding Vikings or other anti-air units. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain defense. Increase tech and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a heavy economy and tech, with heavy production. They have a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and possibly other air units, indicating a strong air composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and tech. Your army is ground-focused with Marines and Marauders. Defense is heavy, but you need more anti-air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence by adding Vikings or other anti-air units. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain defense. Increase tech and upgrades.

With enemy heavy air, you need a robust anti-air response. Vikings are effective against Phoenixes and can also harass. Upgrading your army keeps you competitive.
