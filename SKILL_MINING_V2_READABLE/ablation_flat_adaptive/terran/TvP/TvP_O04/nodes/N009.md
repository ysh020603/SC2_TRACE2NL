# N009 — Late-Midgame vs Air and Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase (~10 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy has heavy air presence with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Phoenixes. Own army includes Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, and Widow Mines, defense heavy. Increase your air presence with Vikings or other anti-air units. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain defense. Increase tech and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a heavy economy and tech, with heavy production. They have a mixed army with air and ground units, including Phoenixes and Stalkers, indicating a flexible composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and tech. Your army is ground-focused with Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Defense is heavy, but you need more anti-air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence with Vikings or other anti-air units. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain defense. Increase tech and upgrades.

With enemy mixed composition, you need a balanced army. Vikings can handle Phoenixes, while your ground army deals with ground units. Upgrades are crucial.
