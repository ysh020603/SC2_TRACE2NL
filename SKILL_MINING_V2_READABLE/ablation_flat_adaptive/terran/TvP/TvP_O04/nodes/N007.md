## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (~6 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy has moderate air presence with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Own army is ground-focused with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers, defense moderate. Maintain your ground army but start investing in anti-air, such as Marines with upgrades or adding a Starport for Vikings. Continue economy and tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a heavy economy and tech, with heavy production. They have a moderate air presence with Phoenixes and Oracles, indicating an air tech transition. Their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and tech. Your army is ground-focused with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Defense is moderate, but you lack anti-air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start investing in anti-air, such as Marines with upgrades or adding a Starport for Vikings. Continue economy and tech development.

Enemy air presence requires a response to avoid being vulnerable. Adding anti-air units or upgrades ensures you can defend against Phoenix harass and Oracles.
