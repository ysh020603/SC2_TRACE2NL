## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (~4 minutes) with both sides on heavy economy and tech. Enemy scouting is uncertain but may show Stalker. Own army is ground-oriented with Marines, production and tech are heavy. Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current air and defense posture, but be ready to increase defense if scouting shows aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a heavy economy and tech, with moderate production. Their army composition is unknown but may include Stalkers. They are likely expanding and teching up, but their pressure potential is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with heavy production and tech. Your army is ground-focused, likely with Marines and possibly Reapers. Defense is light or uncertain, so be cautious.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech while strengthening your ground army. Maintain current air and defense posture, but be ready to increase defense if scouting shows aggression.

Both sides are in a macro-oriented phase. Investing in economy and tech now sets up a stronger midgame. Maintaining a ground army is safe against unknown compositions, and heavy tech allows flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into a midgame composition with a solid economy and tech lead, ready to respond to enemy tech choices.
