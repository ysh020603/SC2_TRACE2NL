# N008 — Midgame Air Threat with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480s) with heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence suggests a heavy air posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your own forces are ground-oriented with Marines, Marauders, and Widow Mines, and you have heavy defense. Continue producing ground forces and consider adding more anti-air capabilities. Maintain your current expansion rate and technology development, but be ready to adjust your composition to counter the air threat.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue producing ground forces and consider adding more anti-air capabilities. Maintain your current expansion rate and technology development, but be ready to adjust your composition to counter the air threat.

With the opponent having a heavy air presence, you need to ensure you have enough anti-air to defend. Continuing production ensures you have a strong army, and adding anti-air (e.g., more Marines, turrets, or Thors) will help counter Phoenixes and Oracles. Maintaining your economy and tech keeps you competitive.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air threats. Aim to have a solid defense against air harass while continuing to grow your economy.
