# N003 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Enemy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and technology investment on both sides. Enemy intelligence is uncertain, with only a Zealot observed. Your own forces are not yet fully defined, but you have heavy production and tech. Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a flexible army composition. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline, and be ready to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent's intentions are unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology investment. Your army composition is not yet clear, but you are investing heavily in tech. Your defense is light, so you need to be careful about early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while maintaining a flexible army composition. Strengthen your ground forces as a baseline, and be ready to adapt based on scouting.

With both sides investing heavily in tech, the early game is about establishing a strong foundation. Maintaining a flexible approach allows you to respond to whatever the opponent does. Strengthening ground forces provides a safe baseline.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have more information about the opponent. Aim to have a solid economy and tech base to support your midgame.
