## Summary

Your own forces are ground-oriented with Marines, Marauders, Medivacs, and Widow Mines, and you have heavy defense. Increase your air presence and continue developing your ground forces. Maintain your current expansion rate and technology development, but be ready to adjust your composition based on scouting.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue developing your ground forces. Maintain your current expansion rate and technology development, but be ready to adjust your composition based on scouting.

With the opponent having a strong ground army, having air support (Medivacs) gives you mobility and healing. Increasing your air presence can also provide scouting and flexibility. Continuing to develop your economy and tech keeps you competitive.
