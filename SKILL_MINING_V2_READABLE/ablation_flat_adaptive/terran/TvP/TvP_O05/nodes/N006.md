# N006 — Early-Mid Air Threat with Continued Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300s) with heavy economy and production. Enemy intelligence suggests an air posture with Zealots and Oracles. Your own forces are ground-oriented with Marines and Marauders, and you have moderate defense. Continue producing ground forces and consider adding anti-air capabilities. Maintain your current expansion rate and technology development, but be ready to adjust your composition to counter the air threat.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown. The opponent may be planning harass with Oracles or transitioning to air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology investment. Your army is ground-based with Marines and Marauders, and your defense is moderate. You need to be aware of the air threat from Oracles.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue producing ground forces and consider adding anti-air capabilities. Maintain your current expansion rate and technology development, but be ready to adjust your composition to counter the air threat.

With the opponent showing air units, you need to ensure you have a way to deal with them. Continuing production ensures you have a strong army, and adding some anti-air (e.g., Marines with upgrades or turrets) will help defend against Oracle harass. Maintaining your economy and tech keeps you competitive.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air threats. Aim to have a solid defense against Oracle harass while continuing to grow your economy.
