# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7-9 minutes) with both players having heavy economies and technology. You have a ground army with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your air presence slightly to support your army. Maintain your economy and expansion rate, and continue technology upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with possible pressure. They have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and moderate defense. Representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a strong ground army with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Production is heavy, and you are expanding. Defense is moderate, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your air presence slightly to support your army. Maintain your economy and expansion rate, and continue technology upgrades.

A strong ground army with medivac support can be very effective in TvT. Adding some air presence can help with map control and scouting, and prepare for potential air transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army while preparing for potential air transitions or late-game compositions.
