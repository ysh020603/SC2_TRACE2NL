# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Stability

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 6 minutes) with both players having heavy economies and technology. You have a Marauder, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. Maintain your current army path and continue developing your economy and technology. Avoid unnecessary risks and keep your defenses solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with possible pressure. They have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and moderate defense. Representative units include Marines and Reapers. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a ground-oriented army with a Marauder. Production is moderate, and you have a moderate number of expansions. Defense is moderate, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue developing your economy and technology. Avoid unnecessary risks and keep your defenses solid.

With a moderate army and economy, it is important to stabilize and avoid overextending. Maintaining a balanced development allows you to react to the opponent's moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a stable economy and army, ready to transition to a more aggressive or defensive posture based on the opponent's actions.
