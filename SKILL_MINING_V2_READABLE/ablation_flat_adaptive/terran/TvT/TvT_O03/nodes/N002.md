# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 4-6 minutes) with both players having heavy economies and technology. You have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Marauders, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your economy. Maintain your current expansion rate and keep your technology development on track.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with possible pressure. They have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and moderate defense. Representative units include Reapers. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a solid ground army composition with Marines and Marauders. Production is heavy, and you are expanding. Your defense is light, but you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your economy. Maintain your current expansion rate and keep your technology development on track.

A strong ground army and economy will allow you to either pressure the opponent or transition to a more advanced composition later. Maintaining a balanced development is crucial.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a strong economy, a capable ground army, and the flexibility to transition to air or other tech if needed.
