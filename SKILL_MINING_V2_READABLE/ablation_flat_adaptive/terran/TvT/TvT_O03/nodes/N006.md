# N006 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 10 minutes) with both players having heavy economies and technology. You have a ground army with Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology, and consider adding more air units to support your ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with possible pressure. They have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and heavy defense. Representative units include Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a strong ground army with Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Production is heavy, and you are expanding. Defense is moderate, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology, and consider adding more air units to support your ground forces.

Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage in TvT, especially if the opponent is heavily ground-based. Air units can provide mobility and harassment opportunities.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air force to complement your ground army, giving you more options for attack and defense.
