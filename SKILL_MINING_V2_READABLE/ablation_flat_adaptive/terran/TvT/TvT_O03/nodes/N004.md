# N004 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation (Variant)

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and limited information about the opponent. You have a Marine, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Marines. Maintain your economic and technological development while strengthening your ground army. Increase production slightly to ensure you can defend against potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible early pressure. They have a heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and heavy defense. Representative units include Marines. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you are investing heavily in technology. Production is moderate, and you have a Marine. You have a heavy expansion rate. Defense is light, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economic and technological development while strengthening your ground army. Increase production slightly to ensure you can defend against potential early pressure.

A heavy economy and tech lead can be leveraged later, but you need a sufficient army to avoid being punished by early aggression. Balancing development with safety is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a solid economy, tech, and a modest ground force to deter or survive early pressure.
