# TvT_O03 Technology / Economy / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O03
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / upgrade opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that prioritizes heavy economy and technology investment while keeping production moderate early on. The goal is to establish a strong macro foundation and tech lead, then adapt to the opponent's observed posture.

Develop a technology / economy / upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 3 minutes) with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and limited information about the opponent's army composition.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your current economic and technological development while strengthening your ground army. Increase production slightly to ensure you can defend against potential early pressure.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame (around 4-6 minutes) with both players having heavy economies and technology. You have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Marauders, and the opponent shows a ground posture with Reapers.
