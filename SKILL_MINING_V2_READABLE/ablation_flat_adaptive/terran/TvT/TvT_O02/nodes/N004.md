# N004 — Early Game Tech-Heavy Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with opponent showing heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology; possible Reaper presence. Strengthen ground forces while increasing production and continuing technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and heavy technology. A Reaper may be present, indicating possible early harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is moderate in production and heavy in technology, but you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing production and continuing technology development.

The opponent's heavy economy and tech could lead to a strong midgame. You need to build a solid ground army to defend against potential pressure and prepare for a midgame confrontation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid ground army and continue tech progression to match the opponent's economy.
