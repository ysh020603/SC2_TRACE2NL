# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with both sides still showing light or uncertain production and technology; no clear opponent army composition. Continue developing with safety checks, maintaining a balanced economy and technology path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent remains hidden; no reliable combat-unit cues. They could be preparing for a timing attack or expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are still developing; you have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing with safety checks, maintaining a balanced economy and technology path.

The lack of information means you should not overcommit. A balanced approach keeps you safe against various opponent strategies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and tech base to support a more committed army composition.
