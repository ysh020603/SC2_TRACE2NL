# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with limited information about opponent and own posture; both sides appear light or uncertain in production and technology. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks for potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues; their production and technology are also light or uncertain. They may be teching or expanding quietly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is still developing; production and technology are light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping safety checks for potential early pressure.

With both sides unknown, overcommitting to a specific plan is risky. Maintaining a balanced development allows you to react to whatever the opponent reveals.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing your economy and technology while scouting for opponent's intentions.
