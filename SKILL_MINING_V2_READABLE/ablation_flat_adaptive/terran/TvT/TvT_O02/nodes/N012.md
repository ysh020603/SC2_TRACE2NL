# N012 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with opponent showing a ground-based army with heavy production and technology; possible Marine and Reaper presence. Increase air presence and continue developing economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Marines and Reapers are likely, suggesting a bio-heavy composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own army is ground-based with heavy production and technology, and you have a moderate air presence. You have Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and continue developing economy and technology.

The opponent is likely to field a strong bio army. Adding air units can give you a tactical advantage, especially with Medivacs for mobility and healing.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with air support to gain an edge over the opponent's ground composition.
