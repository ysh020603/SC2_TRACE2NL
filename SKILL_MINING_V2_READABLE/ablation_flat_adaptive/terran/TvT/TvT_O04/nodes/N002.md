# N002 — Midgame Ground Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with both players having heavy ground armies including Siege Tanks and Marines, and moderate air presence. Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground army. Maintain expansion and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a similar ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines. They also have heavy economy and technology, and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Siege Tanks and Marines. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground army. Maintain expansion and production.

As the game progresses, adding air units (e.g., Medivacs, Vikings) provides mobility and support. Continuing tech upgrades keeps you ahead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced army composition with both ground and air components.
