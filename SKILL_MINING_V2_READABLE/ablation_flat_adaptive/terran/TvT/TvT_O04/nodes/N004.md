# N004 — Early Game Scouting and Adaptation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with your army including Marines and Reapers, while opponent's army is unknown but has a Reaper. Continue scouting to determine opponent's build. Maintain your ground army and economy development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper, but their overall army composition is unknown. They have a heavy economy and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Reapers. Your economy is heavy, and technology investment is high. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue scouting to determine opponent's build. Maintain your ground army and economy development.

Information is key in the early game. Knowing the opponent's tech path allows you to adapt your build accordingly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gain enough information to choose an appropriate midgame strategy.
