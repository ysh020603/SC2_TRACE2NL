## Summary

Early game phase with your army composition unknown, but you have a Reaper. Opponent shows a Marine, indicating a ground posture. Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech base. Use the Reaper for scouting to gather information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Marine, suggesting a ground-oriented build. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they may be preparing for early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet clear, but you have a Reaper. Your economy is heavy, and technology investment is high. Defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech base. Use the Reaper for scouting to gather information.

Early game is about setting up for the midgame. Scouting with Reaper helps identify opponent's intentions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
