# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both players on heavy economy and technology, ground-oriented armies, and possible early pressure. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive readiness.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Reapers and Hellions. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they may apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with a ground-oriented army including Reapers and Hellions. Defense is moderate, and you are prepared for possible early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive readiness.

This opening aims to outscale the opponent economically and technologically. A solid ground core provides safety against early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a strong economy and tech advantage.
