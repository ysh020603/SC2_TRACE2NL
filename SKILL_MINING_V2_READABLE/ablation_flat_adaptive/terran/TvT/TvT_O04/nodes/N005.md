# N005 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with both players having heavy ground armies including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, and moderate air presence. Continue increasing your air presence and strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a similar heavy ground army. They also have heavy economy and technology, and may be preparing for a decisive engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a strong ground army. Your defense is heavy, and you are well-established.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing your air presence and strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and expansion.

As the game reaches late-midgame, having a balanced army with air support is crucial for map control and engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for the late game by ensuring a strong economy and versatile army.
