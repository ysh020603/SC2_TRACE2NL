# TvT_O04 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that prioritizes economic and technological development over early aggression, aiming for a strong midgame position.

Develop a robust economy and technology base while maintaining flexibility to adapt to opponent's actions.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with both players on heavy economy and technology, ground-oriented armies, and possible early pressure.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive readiness.
