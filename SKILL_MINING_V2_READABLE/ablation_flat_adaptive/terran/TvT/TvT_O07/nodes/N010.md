# N010 — Early Game Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180) with both players having heavy economy and technology. Opponent shows heavy defense and moderate production, with Marines. Stabilize your position by maintaining your defense and increasing your production. Continue developing your economy and consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a unknown posture with heavy defense and moderate production, with Marines. They have heavy technology investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with a moderate production. Your defense is heavy, and you have Marines and Reapers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by maintaining your defense and increasing your production. Continue developing your economy and consider expanding.

With the opponent having heavy defense, it's important to stabilize and build up your forces before making any aggressive moves. This ensures you can defend against potential pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger early-midgame position with a larger army and more expansions.
