Maintain your current defense posture and consider increasing your expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology. They have moderate defense and possible pressure, with representative units like Marines. Exact counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you are in a developing phase with possible pressure from the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current defense posture and consider increasing your expansion.

With both sides investing heavily in economy and tech, the game is likely to be decided by macro efficiency and army composition. Strengthening your ground forces while expanding ensures you can defend against potential pressure and set up for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a solid midgame position with a strong economy, multiple expansions, and a versatile ground army that can adapt to the opponent's composition.
