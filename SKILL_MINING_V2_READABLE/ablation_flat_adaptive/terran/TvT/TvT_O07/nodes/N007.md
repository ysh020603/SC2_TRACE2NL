# N007 — Early-Mid Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300-360) with both players having heavy economy and production. Opponent shows heavy defense and possible pressure, with Marines. Stabilize your position by maintaining your defense and increasing your production. Continue developing your economy and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with heavy defense and possible pressure, with Marines. They have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Marines and Reapers, and your defense is moderate. Your economy and production are strong, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by maintaining your defense and increasing your production. Continue developing your economy and consider expanding further.

With the opponent having heavy defense, it's important to stabilize and build up your forces before making any aggressive moves. This ensures you can defend against potential pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger midgame position with a larger army and more expansions.
