# N002 — Early Game Tech/Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180) with both players having heavy economy and technology, but army composition is unknown. Production is moderate, and defense is light or uncertain. Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech infrastructure while preparing to transition into a ground army. Maintain your current production and consider expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about their army posture, but they have heavy production and technology. Their defense is light, and they are likely focusing on economy and tech as well.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army composition is not yet defined. Your production is moderate, and you have light defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech infrastructure while preparing to transition into a ground army. Maintain your current production and consider expanding.

In the early game, it's crucial to secure your economy and tech advantages. Since the opponent's army is unknown, it's wise to build a flexible foundation that can adapt to any threat.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a defined army composition (likely ground) while maintaining your economic and tech lead.
