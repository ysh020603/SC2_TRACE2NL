# N003 — Midgame Ground Siege with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420-540) with both players having heavy economy, production, and ground armies. Opponent shows moderate defense and possible pressure, with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders. Increase your defense and continue strengthening your ground army. Maintain your economy and expansion, and consider adding more production to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders. They have moderate defense and possible pressure, with heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Marines and Hellions, and your defense is heavy. Your economy and production are strong, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and continue strengthening your ground army. Maintain your economy and expansion, and consider adding more production to support a larger army.

With both sides having heavy ground forces, the game may revolve around siege lines and positioning. Strengthening your defense and army ensures you can hold your ground and potentially push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-midgame position with a strong, well-rounded ground army and a solid economy to support continued production.
