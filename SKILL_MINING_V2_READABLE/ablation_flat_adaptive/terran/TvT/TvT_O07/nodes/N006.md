# N006 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600) with both players having heavy economy and ground armies. Opponent shows light air presence and moderate defense, with Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and production, and consider adding air units like Medivacs or Vikings.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with light air presence. They have moderate defense and possible pressure, with heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Marines and Marauders, and your defense is heavy. Your economy and production are strong, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and production, and consider adding air units like Medivacs or Vikings.

Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage, as the opponent has limited air presence. Air units provide mobility and can help you control the map.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a balanced army with a strong air component, while maintaining a solid ground defense.
