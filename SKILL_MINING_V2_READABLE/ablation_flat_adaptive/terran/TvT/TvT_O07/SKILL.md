# TvT_O07 Technology/Economy/Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O07
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production. The early game is characterized by uncertainty about the opponent's army composition, with a focus on establishing a strong macro foundation and tech infrastructure. As the game progresses, the opening transitions into a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and expansion, while keeping options open for air transitions if the opponent shows signs of air play.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame (around 240-360 game time) with both players having heavy economy and production, and ground-oriented armies. Opponent shows moderate defense and possible pressure, with representative units like Marines.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and production while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current defense posture and consider increasing your expansion.

**Read for details:** `N001`
