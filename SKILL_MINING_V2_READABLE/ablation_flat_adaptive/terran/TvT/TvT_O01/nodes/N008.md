# N008 — Early Game Reaper Harass with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 180 game time when both players have a ground posture with Reapers, and you have a heavy production. Your economy is heavy, but your expansion is light, indicating a focus on harassment. Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining your expansion. Consider using Reapers for harassment to disrupt the opponent's economy. Maintain production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Reapers, indicating they may be planning harassment as well. They have a heavy economy and production, so they may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Reapers. Your technology investment is heavy, and you are in the early game. Your defense is light, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining your expansion. Consider using Reapers for harassment to disrupt the opponent's economy. Maintain production and technology.

With both players having Reapers, the early game may be decided by harassment. Using your Reapers to harass the opponent's workers can give you an economic advantage, while your heavy production allows you to maintain pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a strong economy and a flexible ground army, ready to respond to the opponent's next move.
