# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Defense with Siege Tanks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 300 game time when the opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marauders, and a moderate defense. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Marines, and you are focusing on defense and economy. Strengthen your ground forces and consider adding Siege Tanks or other defensive units. Maintain production and technology, and increase your economy. Avoid overextending without scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marauders, indicating a potential siege push. They have a moderate defense and heavy economy, so they may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Marines. Your defense is light, and you are in the early-midgame. You are not under immediate pressure but may be vulnerable to a siege tank push.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces and consider adding Siege Tanks or other defensive units. Maintain production and technology, and increase your economy. Avoid overextending without scouting.

The opponent's Siege Tanks and Marauders suggest a ground push. By adding your own Siege Tanks and defensive structures, you can hold your position and potentially counterattack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more secure midgame position with a stronger defense and a larger army, ready to respond to the opponent's push.
