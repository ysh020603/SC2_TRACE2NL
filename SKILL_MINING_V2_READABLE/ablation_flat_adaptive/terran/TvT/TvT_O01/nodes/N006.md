# N006 — Midgame Ground Stalemate with Balanced Forces

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 480-540 game time when both players have a ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. The opponent has moderate air presence, and you are in a balanced midgame position. Maintain your ground army and economy, and consider adding more air units or tech upgrades. Continue production and technology, and keep a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. They have moderate air presence and heavy production and tech. They are likely preparing for a midgame engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Your air presence is moderate, and you are in the midgame. Your defense is moderate, and you have a strong army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy, and consider adding more air units or tech upgrades. Continue production and technology, and keep a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

With both players having strong ground armies, the game may become a stalemate. Maintaining a balanced army and economy allows you to outlast the opponent or transition to a different composition if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced midgame or late-midgame posture, possibly with heavier air support or tech upgrades.
