# N002 — Early-Midgame Expansion Pressure with Ground Focus

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Occurs around 300 game time when the opponent has a moderate defense and heavy economy, but your own posture is similar to N001. This node is marked as negative, indicating a potential risk or suboptimal situation. Avoid being caught off guard by a sudden attack. Do not neglect scouting or defensive positioning, as the opponent may be hiding a large army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with moderate defense and heavy economy. They have no confirmed combat units, but their production and tech are heavy. They may be preparing a push or expanding aggressively.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Reapers. Your defense is light, and you are in the early-midgame. You are not under immediate pressure but may be vulnerable to a timing attack.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid being caught off guard by a sudden attack. Do not neglect scouting or defensive positioning, as the opponent may be hiding a large army.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have better scouting information or when the opponent's intentions become clearer. Also recheck if you feel threatened or if you have an opportunity to punish an overextension.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more secure midgame position with a stronger defense and a larger economy, ready to respond to the opponent's next move.
