# N010 — Early Game Ground Army with Reaper Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 180 game time when the opponent has a ground posture with Reapers and a heavy defense. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Marines and Marauders, and you have a heavy economy. Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining your expansion. Consider adding defensive structures to protect against Reaper harassment. Maintain production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Reapers, indicating they may be planning harassment. They have a heavy defense and economy, so they may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Marines and Marauders. Your technology investment is heavy, and you are in the early game. Your defense is light, but you have a strong army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining your expansion. Consider adding defensive structures to protect against Reaper harassment. Maintain production and technology.

With the opponent having Reapers, they may attempt to harass your workers. By adding defensive structures and having a strong ground army, you can deter harassment and maintain your economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a strong economy and a flexible ground army, ready to respond to the opponent's next move.
