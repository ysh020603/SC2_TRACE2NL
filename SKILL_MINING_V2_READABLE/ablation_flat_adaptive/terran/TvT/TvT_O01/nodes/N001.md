## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology, but no confirmed combat units. Their economy and expansion are heavy, and they may be preparing a ground push or tech transition. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Reapers and Hellions. Your technology investment is heavy, and you are in the early-midgame phase. Defense is light, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy while maintaining a safety check. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and continue production and technology. Avoid overextending without scouting information.

With both players investing heavily in economy and tech, the game is likely to be decided by midgame army composition and positioning. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to transition to counter whatever the opponent commits to, while your ground forces provide a solid base.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a balanced ground army, possibly adding air units or tech upgrades as the opponent's strategy becomes clearer.
