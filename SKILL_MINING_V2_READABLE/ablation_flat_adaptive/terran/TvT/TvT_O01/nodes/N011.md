DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 600 game time when the opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Banshees, and Ravens, indicating a mixed composition. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, and you have moderate air presence. Maintain your ground army and economy, and consider adding more air units or tech upgrades to counter the opponent's air support. Continue production and technology, and keep a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Banshees, and Ravens, indicating a mixed composition with air support. They have a heavy defense and economy, so they may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Your air presence is moderate, and you are in the late-midgame. Your defense is moderate, and you have a strong army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy, and consider adding more air units or tech upgrades to counter the opponent's air support. Continue production and technology, and keep a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

With the opponent having Banshees and Ravens, you need to prepare for air threats. Adding air units or tech upgrades (like turrets or Vikings) can help you defend against air harassment and maintain a strong position.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
