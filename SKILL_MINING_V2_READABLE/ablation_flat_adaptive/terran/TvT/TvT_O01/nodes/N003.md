# N003 — Midgame Ground Army with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 480-540 game time when both players have a ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. The opponent has moderate air presence, and you are transitioning to include more air units. Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground army. Continue production and technology, and consider adding more Medivacs or air units to support your ground forces. Maintain a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. They have moderate air presence and heavy production and tech. They are likely preparing for a midgame engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs. Your air presence is moderate, and you are in the midgame. Your defense is light, but you have a strong army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground army. Continue production and technology, and consider adding more Medivacs or air units to support your ground forces. Maintain a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

With both players having strong ground armies, adding air units (like Medivacs for healing and mobility) gives you an edge in engagements. Increasing air presence also prepares you for potential air transitions by the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with both ground and air components, ready for a decisive midgame fight or further tech progression.
