# N007 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 600 game time when the opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marauders, and a moderate defense. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, and you have a heavy defense. Increase your defense and continue strengthening your ground army. Continue production and technology, and consider adding more air units or tech upgrades. Maintain a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marauders, indicating a potential siege push. They have a moderate defense and heavy economy, so they may be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs. Your air presence is moderate, and you are in the late-midgame. Your defense is heavy, and you have a strong army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and continue strengthening your ground army. Continue production and technology, and consider adding more air units or tech upgrades. Maintain a safety check to avoid overcommitting.

With the opponent likely preparing a siege push, a heavy defense and a strong ground army allow you to hold your position and potentially counterattack. Increasing your economy also gives you a long-term advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more secure late-game position with a stronger defense and a larger army, ready to respond to the opponent's push.
