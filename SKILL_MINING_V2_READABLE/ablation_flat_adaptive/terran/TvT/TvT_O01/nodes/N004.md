# N004 — Early Game Economy Focus with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Occurs around 180 game time when the opponent's posture is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and tech. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Marines, and you are focusing on economy and expansion. Continue developing your economy and ground army, while increasing your expansion. Maintain production and technology, and consider scouting to gain information about the opponent's strategy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, with no confirmed combat units. They have a heavy economy and tech, but their production is moderate. They may be going for a fast tech build or a hidden expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Marines. Your technology investment is heavy, and you are in the early game. Your defense is light, and you are not under immediate pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and ground army, while increasing your expansion. Maintain production and technology, and consider scouting to gain information about the opponent's strategy.

With the opponent's posture unknown, it is important to build a solid economy and army that can adapt to any threat. A heavy economy allows you to transition quickly if needed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to the early-midgame with a strong economy and a flexible ground army, ready to respond to the opponent's first move.
