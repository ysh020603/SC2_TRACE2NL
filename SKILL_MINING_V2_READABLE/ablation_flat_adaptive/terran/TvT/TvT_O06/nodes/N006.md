# N006 — Early-Midgame Siege Tank Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, you have Siege Tanks and Marines, with heavy production and tech. The opponent has a Marine/Reaper ground army with heavy production and tech. Increase your defense and expansion, while continuing to strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding more Siege Tanks for positional advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marine and Reaper cues, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and expansion, while continuing to strengthen your ground forces. Consider adding more Siege Tanks for positional advantage.

With a defensive posture, you can safely expand and build up a strong economy. Siege Tanks provide excellent defense against ground aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong defensive position and prepare for a midgame push with a superior economy.
