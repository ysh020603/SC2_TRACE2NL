# N002 — Early Reaper Pressure with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, you have a Reaper-based army with heavy production, while the opponent is similar to N001 but with moderate production. Your economy is moderate, and you have a defensive posture. Maintain your production and technology development, but consider using your Reapers for harassment to disrupt the opponent's economy. Keep your defense solid.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Reaper cues, heavy economy and tech, but moderate production and light defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Reapers, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your economy is moderate, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your production and technology development, but consider using your Reapers for harassment to disrupt the opponent's economy. Keep your defense solid.

With heavy production, you can apply early pressure while still developing your economy. Reaper harassment can force the opponent to invest in defense, slowing their tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use the early pressure to gain a slight advantage, then transition into a more standard macro game.
