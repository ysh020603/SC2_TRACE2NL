# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players have a ground-oriented posture with heavy economy and technology. Opponent may have Reaper presence, and your own forces are Marine-based with heavy defense. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground forces. Maintain your defensive posture and be prepared for possible early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Reaper activity. Their economy and technology are heavy, but their production is only moderate, and their defense appears light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with a ground army centered on Marines. Your defense is heavy, and you are in a stable early-game position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground forces. Maintain your defensive posture and be prepared for possible early pressure.

With both sides investing heavily in economy and tech, the game is likely to be decided by superior macro and tech advantages. Maintaining a solid defense while expanding your own economy sets up a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the midgame with a robust economy and tech lead, ready to produce a more diverse army composition.
