# N007 — Midgame Marine/Marauder Composition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, you have Marines and Marauders, with heavy production and tech. The opponent has a Marine-based ground army with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground forces and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech units like Medivacs or Siege Tanks for support.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marine cues, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground forces and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech units like Medivacs or Siege Tanks for support.

A Marine/Marauder composition is versatile and can be supported by various tech units. Maintaining a strong economy allows you to tech up and adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more advanced composition with medivacs and tanks, ready for a midgame engagement.
