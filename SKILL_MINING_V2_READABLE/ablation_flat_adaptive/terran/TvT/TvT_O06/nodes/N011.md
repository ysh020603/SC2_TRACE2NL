# N011 — Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, you have Siege Tanks and Marines, with heavy production and tech. The opponent has a ground army with Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Widow Mines. Maintain your current army path and continue to develop your economy. Consider adding detection or tech units to counter Widow Mines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with a mix of units, heavy production and tech, and moderate defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue to develop your economy. Consider adding detection or tech units to counter Widow Mines.

With a stable position, you can focus on macro and tech. Widow Mines can be dangerous, so having detection is important.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your position and prepare for a midgame push with a strong economy.
