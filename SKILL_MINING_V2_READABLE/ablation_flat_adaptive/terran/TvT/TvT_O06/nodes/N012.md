# N012 — Late-Midgame Air Superiority

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Ravens, and heavy air presence. The opponent has a ground army with Marines and Medivacs, and heavy air presence. Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground forces. Consider adding more Vikings or Ravens to gain air superiority.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Medivacs, heavy production and tech, and heavy air presence.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with a mix of units, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground forces. Consider adding more Vikings or Ravens to gain air superiority.

With both sides having heavy air presence, controlling the air is crucial. Ravens provide detection and offensive capabilities, while Vikings can contest air space.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more balanced army with air superiority, ready for a decisive engagement.
