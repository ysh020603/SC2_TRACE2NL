# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Hellions, and moderate air presence. The opponent has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground forces. Consider adding Vikings or Ravens to counter the opponent's Battlecruisers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Reapers, and Battlecruisers, heavy production and tech, and moderate air presence.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with a mix of units, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue strengthening your ground forces. Consider adding Vikings or Ravens to counter the opponent's Battlecruisers.

The opponent's Battlecruisers pose a significant threat. Investing in air support and detection can give you an edge in engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more balanced army with air support, ready to take map control and push.
