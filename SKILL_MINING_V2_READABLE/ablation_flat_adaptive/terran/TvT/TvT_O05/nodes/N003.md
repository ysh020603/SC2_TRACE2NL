## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy and tech, heavy production. Opponent shows a mix of ground units including Battlecruiser, indicating possible late-game tech. Maintain ground strength and economy, continue technology development, and keep defense stable. Consider increasing air presence if opponent's Battlecruiser becomes a threat.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Marauder, Battlecruiser. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology investment. Defense is moderate, economy and expansions are heavy. Pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground strength and economy, continue technology development, and keep defense stable. Consider increasing air presence if opponent's Battlecruiser becomes a threat.

Opponent's Battlecruiser cue suggests a potential transition to air or late-game tech; maintaining a balanced economy and tech allows for adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for potential late-game transitions by maintaining tech flexibility and a strong economy.
