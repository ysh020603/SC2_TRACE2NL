## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy and tech, heavy production. Opponent shows SiegeTank and Marauder cues, indicating possible tech investment. Continue strengthening ground forces and economy, while maintaining production and technology development. Keep defense posture stable.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marauder. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology investment. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces and economy, while maintaining production and technology development. Keep defense posture stable.

Opponent's SiegeTank presence suggests a potential defensive or siege-oriented approach; maintaining a strong economy and tech allows for flexible responses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a robust ground army with supporting tech to handle midgame engagements.
