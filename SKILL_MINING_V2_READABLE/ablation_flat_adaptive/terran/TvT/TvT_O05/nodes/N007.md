# N007 — Midgame Air Transition with Ground Core

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy and tech, heavy production. Opponent shows Marine and Marauder, indicating a standard ground composition. Increase air presence and technology, continue strengthening ground forces and economy, and expand further. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Marauder. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology investment. Defense is moderate, economy and expansions are heavy. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and technology, continue strengthening ground forces and economy, and expand further. Maintain defense.

With a standard ground opponent, transitioning to air can provide a strategic advantage, especially if opponent lacks anti-air. Maintaining a ground core ensures flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with air superiority potential while maintaining a solid ground foundation.
