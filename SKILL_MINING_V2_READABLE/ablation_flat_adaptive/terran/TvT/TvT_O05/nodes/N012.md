# N012 — Early Game Ground Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with opponent posture unknown, heavy economy and tech, moderate production. Own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech. Strengthen ground forces and continue technology development, while maintaining economy and production. Keep expansion stable.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marauder. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Economy is heavy, expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces and continue technology development, while maintaining economy and production. Keep expansion stable.

Establishing a solid ground foundation early allows for flexible responses to opponent's unknown strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach an early-midgame state with a clear ground-oriented posture and a secure economy.
