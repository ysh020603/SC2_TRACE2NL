# N006 — Midgame Ground with Defense Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy and tech, heavy production. Opponent shows air presence with Banshee, indicating potential air harassment. Increase defense and economy, continue ground strength and production, and expand further. Maintain technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper, Medivac, Banshee. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy and expansions are heavy. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue ground strength and production, and expand further. Maintain technology development.

Opponent's Banshee and Medivac suggest potential air harassment or drops; strengthening defense and economy provides resilience while maintaining a strong ground core.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a secure economy and a robust defense to withstand harassment while preparing for midgame engagements.
