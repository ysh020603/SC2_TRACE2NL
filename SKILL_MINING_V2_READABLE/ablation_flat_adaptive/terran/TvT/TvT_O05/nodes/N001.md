# N001 — Early-Mid Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy and tech, moderate-to-heavy production. Opponent shows light air presence and possible pressure. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and continuing technology development. Maintain current air and defense posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology investment. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and continuing technology development. Maintain current air and defense posture.

With both sides investing heavily in tech and economy, maintaining a solid ground core and expanding economy provides a stable foundation for midgame transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger ground army composition and a more secure economy before midgame.
