# N011 — Midgame Balanced Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with both sides ground-oriented, heavy economy and tech, heavy production. Opponent shows a mix of ground units including Battlecruiser, indicating potential late-game tech. Maintain current army path and economy, continue technology development, and keep defense stable. Avoid unnecessary risks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Marauder, Battlecruiser. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology investment. Defense is moderate, economy and expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and economy, continue technology development, and keep defense stable. Avoid unnecessary risks.

With both sides heavily invested, maintaining a balanced approach allows for flexibility and avoids overcommitment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Preserve a strong position while preparing for potential late-game transitions.
