# N004 — Midgame Tech and Expansion Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies and expansions. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, heavy production, and heavy tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Medivacs. Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your ground army. Expand further to secure more resources, and continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens. They have heavy production and tech, indicating a strong mid-game army. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy tech. Your army includes Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Medivacs, giving you a versatile ground force. Defenses are light, so you may need to build up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your ground army. Expand further to secure more resources, and continue tech development.

Adding air units like Medivacs or possibly Vikings can give you an edge. Expanding ensures you have the resources to support a larger army and tech. The enemy's ground army can be countered with a mix of Marauders and Hellions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more balanced composition with air support, and consider adding Siege Tanks for defensive strength.
