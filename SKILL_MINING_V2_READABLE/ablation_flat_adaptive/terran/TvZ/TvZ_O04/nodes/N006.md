# N006 — Early-Midgame Transition and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies and expansions. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and moderate tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Marines and Reapers. Continue to expand and increase your production. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding Siege Tanks for defense. Maintain tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. They have moderate production and tech, indicating a developing army. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy tech. Your army includes Marines and Reapers, which can be used for harassment. Defenses are light, so you may need to build up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to expand and increase your production. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding Siege Tanks for defense. Maintain tech development.

Expanding secures your economy, while adding Siege Tanks gives you a strong defensive backbone. The enemy's moderate tech suggests they are not rushing, so you have time to build up.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more defensive posture with Siege Tanks, and prepare for a mid-game push.
