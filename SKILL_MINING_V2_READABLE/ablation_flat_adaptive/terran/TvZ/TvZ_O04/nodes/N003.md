- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy production and tech, indicating a strong mid-game army. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy tech. Your army includes Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, giving you a solid ground force. Defenses are moderate.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army further, possibly adding more Siege Tanks and Marines. Maintain your economy and production, and consider adding upgrades.

The enemy's heavy ground army requires a strong response. Your Siege Tanks and Marines are effective against Zerglings and Roaches. Medivacs provide mobility and healing, allowing you to sustain engagements.
