# N007 — Early Game Marine and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies and expansions. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and light/uncertain tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Marines. Increase your economy and continue to build Marines. Maintain your production and tech development. Consider adding Reapers for scouting and harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. They have moderate production and light/uncertain tech. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy but heavy production and tech. Your army includes Marines, which are versatile. Your expansion is heavy, so you have a solid economic base.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and continue to build Marines. Maintain your production and tech development. Consider adding Reapers for scouting and harassment.

Marines are a solid core unit that can be used in many compositions. Increasing your economy ensures you can support a larger army later. The enemy's light tech suggests they are not a major threat yet.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more diverse army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs, and consider expanding further.
