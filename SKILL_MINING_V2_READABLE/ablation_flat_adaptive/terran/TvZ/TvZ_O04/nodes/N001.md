# N001 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase (around 4 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies and expansions. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and light/uncertain tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and expansion, but be ready to adapt if the enemy shows signs of aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture, with heavy economy and expansions. They have moderate production and light/uncertain tech. Representative units include Queens. Air presence is light or uncertain, so no immediate air threat is visible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy technology investment. Your army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Defenses are light, but you have the capacity to build up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and expansion, but be ready to adapt if the enemy shows signs of aggression.

Your heavy tech and production advantage can be leveraged to outscale the opponent. By maintaining a strong economy and tech, you can transition into a more powerful composition later. The enemy's light tech suggests they are not rushing to a specific threat, so you have time to build up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a stronger mid-game army composition, possibly adding Medivacs for mobility and healing, and consider adding more production structures to support a larger army.
