## Summary

Late-midgame (around 10 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies and expansions. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, heavy production, and heavy tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Continue to strengthen your ground army and tech. Consider adding more air units for mobility and to counter potential air threats. Maintain your economy and production.

- Enemy has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy production and tech, indicating a strong late-midgame army. Air presence is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with heavy tech. Your army includes Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines, giving you a powerful ground force. Defenses are moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and tech. Consider adding more air units for mobility and to counter potential air threats. Maintain your economy and production.

Your composition is strong against ground, but you need to be prepared for possible air transitions. Widow Mines are effective against large numbers of Zerglings. Medivacs provide sustain.
