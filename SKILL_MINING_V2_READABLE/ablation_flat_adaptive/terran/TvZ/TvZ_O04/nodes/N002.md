# N002 — Early Game Reaper Pressure and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and light/uncertain tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Reapers. Use Reapers for harassment to disrupt the enemy's economy while you expand and increase production. Continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a ground macro posture with heavy economy and expansions. They have moderate production and light/uncertain tech. No reliable combat units have been observed, so they may be focusing on economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy tech investment. Your army is small but includes Reapers, which can be used for harassment. Your expansion is light or uncertain, so you may need to expand soon.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use Reapers for harassment to disrupt the enemy's economy while you expand and increase production. Continue tech development.

Reapers can exploit the enemy's lack of early defense to deal economic damage. This buys time for you to expand and build up your army. Your heavy tech investment will pay off later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Expand to secure a third base, and transition towards a more robust ground army with Marines and Siege Tanks.
