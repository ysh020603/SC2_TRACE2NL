# TvZ_O04 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that emphasizes heavy production and technology investment while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The strategy is to develop a strong economy and tech base, then leverage that advantage to transition into a powerful mid-game composition.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase (around 4 minutes) with both sides having heavy economies and expansions. Enemy is ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and light/uncertain tech. Own forces are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain production and expansion, but be ready to adapt if the enemy shows signs of aggression.
