# N009 — Midgame Stable Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480 seconds) with both sides on heavy economy. Enemy shows a ground posture with Zergling, Queen, heavy production, and moderate tech. Your own posture is ground-oriented with Marines, heavy production, and heavy tech. Maintain your current army path and production. Keep your economy stable and avoid unnecessary risks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Marines, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your defense is moderate, and you have a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and production. Keep your economy stable and avoid unnecessary risks.

With a stable position, you can afford to play defensively and wait for tech advantages to pay off.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a tech advantage.
