# TvZ_O03 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O03
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that emphasizes heavy technology investment and a strong economy while maintaining moderate production. The army is ground-leaning, with early Marines and later tech units like Ghosts, Siege Tanks, and Battlecruisers. The opening is flexible, allowing adaptation based on scouting and opponent behavior.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame (around 240-360 seconds) with both sides on heavy economy and expansions. Enemy appears ground-oriented with light or uncertain air, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy tech and moderate production.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining your tech advantage. Increase production and expansion to support your heavy tech investments.
