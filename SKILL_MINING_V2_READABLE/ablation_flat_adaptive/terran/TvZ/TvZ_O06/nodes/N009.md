## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows Zergling, Mutalisk, and Queen, indicating a Mutalisk transition. Production and tech are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Air presence is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense against air, consider adding Thors or Marines with upgrades. Maintain economy and expansion, and continue tech development.

Mutalisks can harass and threaten your economy. Your heavy tech allows you to add anti-air units like Thors or upgrade Marines.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
