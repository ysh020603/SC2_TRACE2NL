# N006 — Early-Midgame Roach Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks. Opponent Zerg with Roach cues, moderate production, heavy expansion. Maintain your ground army and defense, continue tech development, and consider adding more Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter Roaches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows Zergling, Roach, and Queen, indicating a potential Roach push. Production is moderate, but they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and tech. Economy is heavy, and you are maintaining expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and defense, continue tech development, and consider adding more Siege Tanks or Marauders to counter Roaches.

Siege Tanks are effective against Roaches. Your heavy tech and production allow you to field a strong defensive force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Hold off any Roach pressure and transition to midgame with a strong economy.
