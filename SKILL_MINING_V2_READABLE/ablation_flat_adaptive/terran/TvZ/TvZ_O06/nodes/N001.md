# N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground macro posture with heavy economy and tech, moderate production. Opponent Zerg with ground posture, light or uncertain air, moderate production, heavy economy and expansion. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and keep scouting for early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Zerg ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Production is moderate, tech is light or uncertain, and they are expanding heavily. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with a ground-oriented army. Production is moderate, and you are expanding. Defense is heavy, suggesting you are preparing for potential pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and keep scouting for early pressure.

Your heavy tech and economy investment will pay off if you can survive the early game. Maintaining a strong ground army and defense deters early Zergling aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into the early-midgame with a solid economy and tech base, ready to adapt to opponent's tech choices.
