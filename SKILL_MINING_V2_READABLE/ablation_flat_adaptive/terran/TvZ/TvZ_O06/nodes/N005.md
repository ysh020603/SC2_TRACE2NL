# N005 — Midgame Air Transition Option

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, but air presence is moderate. Opponent Zerg with heavy production and tech, ground posture. Increase air presence and continue strengthening ground army. Maintain economy and expansion, and consider adding more air production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has heavy production and tech, with Zergling and Queen cues. They are expanding heavily and may be preparing for a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Reapers, Hellions, and Battlecruisers, indicating a transition to air. Economy and production are heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and continue strengthening ground army. Maintain economy and expansion, and consider adding more air production.

Adding air units like Battlecruisers gives you a powerful late-game option. Your heavy economy can support this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with air support to counter opponent's tech choices.
