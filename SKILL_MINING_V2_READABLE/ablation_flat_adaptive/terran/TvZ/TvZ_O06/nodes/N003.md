# N003 — Midgame Ground Tech Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, including Siege Tanks and Marines. Opponent Zerg with heavy production and tech, ground posture. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase tech investment, and maintain economy. Consider adding more production or tech structures to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has heavy production and tech, with Zergling and Queen cues. They are expanding heavily and may be preparing for a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and tech. Economy is heavy, and you are maintaining expansions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase tech investment, and maintain economy. Consider adding more production or tech structures to support a larger army.

Your Siege Tank and Marine composition is strong against ground Zerg. Heavy tech gives you options for dealing with tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain a strong ground army while preparing for potential air threats.
