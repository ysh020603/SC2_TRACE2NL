# N008 — Early-Midgame Marine-Reaper Composition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, including Marines and Reapers. Opponent Zerg with ground posture, moderate production, heavy expansion. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and expand. Consider adding tech structures for midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows Zergling and Queen, moderate production, heavy expansion. Tech is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Marine-Reaper composition, heavy production and tech. Economy is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase production, and expand. Consider adding tech structures for midgame.

Marine-Reaper is mobile and can apply pressure. Your heavy economy and production allow you to maintain a strong army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a strong ground army and tech base.
