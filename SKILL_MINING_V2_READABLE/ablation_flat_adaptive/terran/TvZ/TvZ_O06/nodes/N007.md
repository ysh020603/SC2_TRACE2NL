# N007 — Early Game Reaper Harass

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground posture with moderate production and tech, including Reapers. Opponent Zerg with ground posture, moderate production, heavy expansion. Use Reapers for harassment while continuing to develop economy and tech. Increase production and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows Zergling and Queen, moderate production, heavy expansion. Tech is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Reaper for harassment, with moderate production and heavy tech. Economy is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use Reapers for harassment while continuing to develop economy and tech. Increase production and consider expanding further.

Reaper harassment can disrupt Zerg's economy and force reactions. Your heavy tech and economy will benefit from the time gained.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to early-midgame with a strong economy and tech, having dealt some damage.
