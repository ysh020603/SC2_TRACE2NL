# N002 — Early-Midgame Macro Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground macro posture with heavy production and tech. Opponent still ground posture, moderate production, heavy economy and expansion. Continue increasing economy and production, while maintaining a strong ground army. Keep expanding and consider adding tech structures to prepare for midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent still shows Zergling and Queen, with moderate production and heavy expansion. Tech is still light or uncertain, but pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and tech are heavy, and production has increased. You are expanding and have a strong ground army. Defense remains heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing economy and production, while maintaining a strong ground army. Keep expanding and consider adding tech structures to prepare for midgame.

Your heavy economy and production will allow you to out-macro the Zerg if you can avoid early damage. Tech investment gives you options for midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with a strong economy, production, and tech base, ready to respond to opponent's composition.
