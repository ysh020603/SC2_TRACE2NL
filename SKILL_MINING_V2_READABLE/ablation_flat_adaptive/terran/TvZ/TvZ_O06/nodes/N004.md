# N004 — Early Game Heavy Production Variant

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own ground posture with heavy production and tech, but economy and expansion are heavy. Opponent similar to N001. Maintain production and tech, but consider increasing economy to support a larger army. Keep expanding and scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent shows Zergling and Queen, moderate production, heavy expansion. Tech is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have heavy production and tech, with a strong ground army. Economy is heavy, but expansion is also heavy, suggesting you are prioritizing production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain production and tech, but consider increasing economy to support a larger army. Keep expanding and scouting.

Heavy production allows you to field a larger army quickly, but you need a solid economy to sustain it. Balancing production and economy is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to early-midgame with a strong production base and sustainable economy.
