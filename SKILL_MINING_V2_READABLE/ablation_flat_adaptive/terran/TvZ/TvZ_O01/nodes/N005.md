# N005 — Midgame Tech and Army Response

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Marauders, but you may be facing a Zerg tech switch. Opponent has heavy production and tech. Avoid being caught off guard by a tech switch. Do not overcommit to a single unit composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, but their tech is heavy. They may be transitioning to a different composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Siege Tanks and Marauders, and heavy tech. Your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid being caught off guard by a tech switch. Do not overcommit to a single unit composition.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you scout a specific Zerg tech or when you are ready to push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more flexible army that can handle multiple threats, while maintaining your tech advantage.
