# N004 — Early Game Scouting and Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with heavy economy and expansion, but your army composition is still unknown. Opponent is Zerg with moderate production and light tech. Use your Reaper to scout for early aggression or tech choices. Continue developing your economy and decide on a tech path based on what you see.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg is on a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, but their tech is light. They are likely expanding as well.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding, but your army is not yet defined. You have a Reaper for scouting.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use your Reaper to scout for early aggression or tech choices. Continue developing your economy and decide on a tech path based on what you see.

Early scouting is crucial to adapt to Zerg's build. A heavy economy gives you flexibility, but you need information to choose the right units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting, while maintaining your economic lead.
