# N012 — Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have a ground army and heavy tech, but you may be facing pressure. Opponent is Zerg with heavy production and moderate tech. Focus on stabilizing your defenses and building a sufficient army to repel any pressure. Delay further expansion until you are safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their production is heavy. They may be preparing an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, and heavy tech. Your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your defenses and building a sufficient army to repel any pressure. Delay further expansion until you are safe.

If you die to pressure, your economy advantage is meaningless. Prioritize survival and then resume your macro plan.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Once you have stabilized, transition back to your macro development plan.
