# N003 — Late-Midgame Tech and Army Advantage

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Late-midgame phase where you have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and other units, and heavy technology. Opponent is Zerg with heavy production and tech, but you have an advantage. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider pushing to deal damage. Maintain your economy and tech advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and their tech is heavy. They are likely well-defended but you have an edge.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a powerful ground army with Siege Tanks and a heavy economy. Your technology is strong, and you are in a good position to push.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider pushing to deal damage. Maintain your economy and tech advantage.

With a superior army composition and tech, you can force engagements and gain map control. Use your Siege Tanks to break defensive positions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture, aiming to take map control and force the Zerg to react.
