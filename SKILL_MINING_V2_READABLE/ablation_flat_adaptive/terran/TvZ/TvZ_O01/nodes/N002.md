# N002 — Stabilize Against Early Pressure

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame phase where you are facing possible pressure from Zerg. Your economy is heavy, but you may need to stabilize before continuing to develop. Avoid over-investing in economy at the expense of army. Do not take a risky expansion without proper defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their production is moderate. They may be preparing an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, but your army is still developing. You may be vulnerable to early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-investing in economy at the expense of army. Do not take a risky expansion without proper defense.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you have a sufficient army to defend or when the pressure subsides.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Once you have stabilized, transition back to your macro development plan.
