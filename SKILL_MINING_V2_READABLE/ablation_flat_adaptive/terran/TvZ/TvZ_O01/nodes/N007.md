# N007 — Midgame Army and Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with a strong ground army and heavy tech. Opponent is Zerg with heavy production and tech, but you are in a good position. Continue developing your army and tech, and consider pushing to gain map control. Maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and their tech is heavy. They are likely well-defended.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions, and heavy tech. Your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your army and tech, and consider pushing to gain map control. Maintain your economy.

With a strong army and tech, you can pressure the Zerg and force them to react. Use your Siege Tanks to control the map.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture, aiming to take map control and force engagements.
