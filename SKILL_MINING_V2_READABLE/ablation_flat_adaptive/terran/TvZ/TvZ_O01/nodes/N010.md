# N010 — Early-Midgame Tech and Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with a ground army and heavy tech. Opponent is Zerg with moderate production and tech, possibly transitioning. Continue developing your army and tech, and consider adding units to counter potential threats. Maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their tech is moderate. They may be transitioning to a different composition.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, and heavy tech. Your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your army and tech, and consider adding units to counter potential threats. Maintain your economy.

Zerg's tech is moderate, so they may be going for a specific composition. You need to be flexible to counter it.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specialized army based on scouting, while maintaining your tech advantage.
