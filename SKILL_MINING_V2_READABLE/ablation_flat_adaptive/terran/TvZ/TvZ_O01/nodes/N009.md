## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase where you have a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Banshees, and heavy tech. Opponent is Zerg with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your army and tech, and consider using your Banshees for harassment or to support a push. Maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and their tech is heavy. They are likely well-defended.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a diverse army with air support from Banshees, and heavy tech. Your economy is strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your army and tech, and consider using your Banshees for harassment or to support a push. Maintain your economy.

With a diverse army, you can apply pressure in multiple ways. Banshees can harass workers or support your ground army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture, using your tech advantage to force engagements.
