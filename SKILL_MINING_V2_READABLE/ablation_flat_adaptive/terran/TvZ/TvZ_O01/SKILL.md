# TvZ_O01 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O01
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Flat Adaptive

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that prioritizes a heavy economy and technology investment while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The early game focuses on establishing a strong economic base and teching up, with production ramping up as the game progresses. The opponent is Zerg, and the opening is designed to be flexible, allowing adaptation based on scouting information.

Develop a strong economic and technological foundation while maintaining a flexible ground army, ready to respond to Zerg's tech choices and pressure.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and game state.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Macro Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame phase with heavy economy and production, ground-oriented army, and heavy technology. Opponent is Zerg with moderate production and light/uncertain tech, likely on a ground macro posture.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and production, strengthen your ground army, and maintain your technology advantage. Consider expanding further to solidify your lead.
