# N003 — Early Heavy Production and Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is unknown but has Reaper. Opponent is Zerg with light/uncertain air, ground posture, moderate production, light/uncertain tech, possible pressure. Increase your production capacity and continue tech development. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential early aggression. Maintain scouting to detect any threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Zerg ground posture with light or uncertain air. They have moderate production and light or uncertain tech. Their economy and expansion are heavy. They may be preparing for early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding aggressively. Your production is heavy, and you are investing heavily in technology. Your army composition is not yet clear, but you have a Reaper for scouting. Your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production capacity and continue tech development. Strengthen your ground army to prepare for potential early aggression. Maintain scouting to detect any threats.

Heavy production allows you to quickly build an army if needed, while heavy tech gives you an advantage later. Strengthening ground forces provides immediate defense against Zergling rushes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition and establish map control.
