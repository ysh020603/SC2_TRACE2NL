# N004 — Early-Mid Stabilization and Ground Army Build-up

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 4 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is ground-based with Marines and Reapers. Opponent has Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light/uncertain tech, possible pressure. Stabilize your position by strengthening your ground army and maintaining your economy. Continue tech development and consider adding defensive structures if needed. Keep scouting to anticipate attacks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Zerglings and Queens, indicating a ground posture. They have moderate production and light or uncertain tech. Their economy is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your production is heavy, and you are investing in tech. Your army consists of Marines and Reapers, giving you a ground presence. Your defense is light or uncertain, and you need to stabilize.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by strengthening your ground army and maintaining your economy. Continue tech development and consider adding defensive structures if needed. Keep scouting to anticipate attacks.

With Zerglings and Queens, the opponent may attempt early aggression. Building a solid ground army with Marines and Reapers provides a strong defense and allows you to transition to a midgame composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition, such as Marine/Tank or Marine/Hellion, while maintaining a stable economy.
