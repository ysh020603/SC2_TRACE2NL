# N009 — Early Game Flexible Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) with moderate economy and expansion, moderate production, heavy tech. Own army is unknown with no representative units. Opponent is Zerg with light/uncertain air, ground posture, moderate production, light/uncertain tech, possible pressure. Maintain your current development path and focus on scouting to determine the opponent's intentions. Continue tech development and prepare to build an army based on what you see.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a Zerg ground posture with light or uncertain air. They have moderate production and light or uncertain tech. Their economy is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and are expanding cautiously. Your production is moderate, and you are investing heavily in technology. Your army composition is not yet clear, and you have no representative units. Your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path and focus on scouting to determine the opponent's intentions. Continue tech development and prepare to build an army based on what you see.

With an unknown army composition, it is important to stay flexible. Heavy tech investment gives you options, but you need to scout to decide which units to produce.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent.
