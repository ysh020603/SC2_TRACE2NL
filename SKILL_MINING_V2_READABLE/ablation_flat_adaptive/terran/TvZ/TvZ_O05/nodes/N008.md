# N008 — Midgame Ground Army Maintenance with Air Awareness

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 7 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Opponent has Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, indicating a possible air transition. Maintain your current army path but be prepared to add anti-air units, such as Marines or Thors, to counter Mutalisks. Continue developing your economy and tech. Keep scouting to confirm the air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. This suggests a ground posture with a possible air transition. They have heavy production and moderate tech. Their economy is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your production is heavy, and you are investing in tech. Your army consists of Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers, giving you a strong ground presence. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path but be prepared to add anti-air units, such as Marines or Thors, to counter Mutalisks. Continue developing your economy and tech. Keep scouting to confirm the air transition.

Mutalisks can be very disruptive. By maintaining your ground army and adding anti-air, you can defend against both ground and air threats. Continuing tech will allow you to transition to a more flexible composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle both ground and air, such as Marine/Tank/Medivac with additional anti-air.
