# N007 — Late-Midgame Defense and Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 10 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is ground-based with Siege Tanks and Marines. Opponent has heavy ground forces (Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen) with heavy production and tech. Increase your defensive capabilities and strengthen your ground army. Continue expanding and teching. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or other units to counter the Zerg composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a strong Zerg ground army with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy production and tech, and their economy is strong. They may be preparing to attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your production is heavy, and you are investing in tech. Your army consists of Siege Tanks and Marines, giving you a strong defensive core. Your defense is heavy, but you may need to strengthen it further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defensive capabilities and strengthen your ground army. Continue expanding and teching. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or other units to counter the Zerg composition.

The Zerg's heavy ground army poses a significant threat. Siege Tanks and Marines are effective, but you may need additional support, such as Medivacs or Thors, to hold your position.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition, possibly including Battlecruisers or more air support, while maintaining a strong defense.
