# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening with Tech Increase

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 9 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Hellions, and Banshees. Opponent has heavy ground forces (Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen) with heavy production and tech. Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your air presence for support. Maintain your defensive posture and keep expanding. Continue tech upgrades to gain an advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a strong Zerg ground army with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy production and tech, and their economy is strong. They may be preparing to attack or expand further.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Hellions, and some air support (Banshees). Your economy and production are heavy, and you are continuing to tech up. Your defense is moderate, and you are prepared for a potential engagement.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increase your air presence for support. Maintain your defensive posture and keep expanding. Continue tech upgrades to gain an advantage.

The Zerg's heavy ground army requires a strong response. Siege Tanks and Hellions are effective against Zerglings and Roaches, while Banshees provide harassment and support. Increasing tech will unlock better units and upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition, possibly including Battlecruisers or more Banshees, while maintaining a strong ground core.
