# N006 — Early-Mid Ground Army with Reaper/Hellion Harass

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is ground-based with Reapers and Hellions. Opponent has Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, moderate tech, possible pressure. Use your Reapers and Hellions to harass the Zerg economy and gain map control. Continue developing your economy and tech. Strengthen your ground army for potential engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Zerglings and Queens, indicating a ground posture. They have moderate production and moderate tech. Their economy is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your production is heavy, and you are investing in tech. Your army consists of Reapers and Hellions, which are good for harassment and map control. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use your Reapers and Hellions to harass the Zerg economy and gain map control. Continue developing your economy and tech. Strengthen your ground army for potential engagements.

Reapers and Hellions are effective at harassing workers and forcing reactions. This can slow the Zerg's economy while you build a stronger army and tech advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust army composition, such as Marine/Tank or Mech, while maintaining map control.
