# N005 — Early-Mid Ground Army Development with Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) with heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, heavy tech. Own army is ground-based with Marines and Marauders. Opponent has Queens, moderate production, light/uncertain tech, possible pressure. Continue developing your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep tech upgrades going. Consider adding more production if needed. Maintain scouting to detect any tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows only Queens, suggesting a ground posture with light or uncertain air. They have moderate production and light or uncertain tech. Their economy is heavy, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your production is heavy, and you are investing in tech. Your army consists of Marines and Marauders, giving you a strong ground core. Your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and maintain your economy. Keep tech upgrades going. Consider adding more production if needed. Maintain scouting to detect any tech switches.

Marines and Marauders are versatile and can handle early Zerg units. Continuing tech will unlock better units and upgrades, giving you an advantage in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition, possibly including Siege Tanks or Medivacs, while maintaining a strong economy.
