## Summary

Early midgame, opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen, production moderate. Maintain current development path, but start preparing for ground defense. Consider building a few Marines or Marauders.

## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen. Production appears moderate, tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is unknown-oriented with light production and tech. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start preparing for ground defense. Consider building a few Marines or Marauders.

Zerg's ground posture suggests a possible early pressure. A small defensive force can deter or hold off early Zergling attacks.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
