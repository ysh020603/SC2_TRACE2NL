# N001 — Early Game Development with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, opponent posture unknown, no reliable combat-unit cues observed. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production flexible.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and technology appear light or uncertain. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is also unknown-oriented with light production and technology. You have not committed to a specific army composition yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production flexible.

With no clear threat, it is safe to develop infrastructure and tech. Overcommitting to army early could hurt economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and tech base, ready to adapt to the first signs of Zerg's strategy.
