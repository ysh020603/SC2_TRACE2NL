# N007 — Early Midgame with Ground Zerg and Own Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame, opponent shows ground posture with Zergling, production moderate. Your own posture is unknown-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech. Continue developing your tech while building a small defensive force. Consider adding a few Marines or Marauders.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling. Production appears moderate, tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is unknown-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech investment. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your tech while building a small defensive force. Consider adding a few Marines or Marauders.

Your heavy tech investment will pay off later, but you need some defense against early Zergling pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong tech base while maintaining a minimal defensive army.
