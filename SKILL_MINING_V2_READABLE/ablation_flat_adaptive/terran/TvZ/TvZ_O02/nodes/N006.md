# N006 — Late Midgame with Heavy Ground Zerg

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, opponent shows heavy ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen, production heavy. Strengthen your ground army and increase defenses. Continue expanding and teching up.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a heavy ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen. Production and tech appear heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech. You have Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase defenses. Continue expanding and teching up.

Zerg has a strong ground army. You need a robust composition with Siege Tanks and Marines to hold and push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
