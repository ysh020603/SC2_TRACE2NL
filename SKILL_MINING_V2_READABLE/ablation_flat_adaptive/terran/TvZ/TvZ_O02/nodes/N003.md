# N003 — Midgame with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent unknown, no reliable combat-unit cues. Maintain current development path, but start building a defensive army to protect your bases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Production and tech appear light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is unknown-oriented with light production and tech. You have not committed to a specific army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start building a defensive army to protect your bases.

By midgame, Zerg may have hidden tech or a large army. A defensive force is necessary to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a solid defensive position while continuing to develop economy and tech.
