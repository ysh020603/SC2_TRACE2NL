# N002 — Early Game Queen-Centric Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a Queen and a heavy defensive posture, with moderate economy and expansion. The opponent is unknown but appears to have heavy technology investment. Continue expanding and increasing your economy. Maintain defensive structures and consider adding more Queens for defense and creep spread. Strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues, but they appear to have heavy defense and technology investment. Their production is moderate, and their army composition is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Queen for defense and creep spread, with a moderate economy and heavy expansion. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing your economy. Maintain defensive structures and consider adding more Queens for defense and creep spread. Strengthen your ground army.

Queens provide cost-effective defense and creep spread, which is valuable against early Protoss pressure. A strong economy will allow you to transition into a more powerful army later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive or tech-heavy composition once you have a solid economy and know the opponent's direction.
