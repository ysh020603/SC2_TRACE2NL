# N012 — Early Game Zergling Pressure with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have Zerglings and heavy economy and defense, while the opponent has a ground army with Zealots. Continue expanding and increasing your economy. Maintain defensive structures and consider adding more Zerglings for map control. Strengthen your ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots. They have light or uncertain defense and heavy technology investment, and their production is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Zerglings for early pressure or defense, with heavy economy and expansion. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing your economy. Maintain defensive structures and consider adding more Zerglings for map control. Strengthen your ground army.

With the opponent showing a ground army, Zerglings can be effective for harassment and map control. A strong economy allows you to transition to a more powerful army later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have better intelligence on the opponent's tech.
