# N004 — Early-Midgame Queen Defense with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a Queen and heavy economy and defense, while the opponent is unknown but appears to have heavy tech investment. Maintain your economy and defensive posture. Continue producing Queens and Zerglings, and consider expanding further. Keep scouting for opponent's tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues, but they appear to have heavy defense and technology investment. Their production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a Queen for defense, with heavy economy and expansion. Production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and defensive posture. Continue producing Queens and Zerglings, and consider expanding further. Keep scouting for opponent's tech.

With an unknown opponent, it's safe to focus on economy and defense. Queens provide good defense and creep spread, which helps with map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have better intelligence on the opponent's tech.
