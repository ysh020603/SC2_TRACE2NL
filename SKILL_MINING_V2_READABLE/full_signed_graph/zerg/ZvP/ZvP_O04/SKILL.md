# ZvP_O04 Expansion / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O04
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / production opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on establishing a strong economy and production infrastructure while maintaining a ground-oriented army. It emphasizes heavy expansion and economic growth, with moderate production and light technology investment in the early game. The strategy is flexible, allowing adaptation based on scouting information about the opponent's composition and tech choices.

Develop a robust economy and production base while preserving flexibility to respond to the opponent's tech and army composition. Aim to reach a strong midgame position with multiple bases and a solid ground army, ready to transition into air or tech as needed.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Economy Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on expanding and building a strong economy. The opponent's tech is unknown, so maintain flexibility.

**Direction:**  
Continue expanding and building economy. Maintain production of workers and start producing basic units like Zerglings and Queens for defense. Keep scouting to detect any early pressure.
