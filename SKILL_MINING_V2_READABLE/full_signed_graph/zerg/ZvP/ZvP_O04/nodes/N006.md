# N006 — Early Midgame Air Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has been scouted as an air-based Protoss army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. Transition to an air-based army to counter the opponent's Phoenixes. Build Spire and start producing Mutalisks. Continue expanding and maintain your economy. Use Overseers for detection and to spot incoming attacks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an air-based army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. They have heavy production and technology investment, and their defense is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is moderate, and technology is moderate. You have Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Transition to an air-based army to counter the opponent's Phoenixes. Build Spire and start producing Mutalisks. Continue expanding and maintain your economy. Use Overseers for detection and to spot incoming attacks.

Phoenixes are strong against ground units, so you need air units to counter them. Mutalisks are a good choice as they are mobile and can harass. Your heavy economy allows you to support this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a Mutalisk-based army, with support from ground units like Roaches and Zerglings. Aim to have a strong economy and a versatile army that can handle both air and ground threats.
