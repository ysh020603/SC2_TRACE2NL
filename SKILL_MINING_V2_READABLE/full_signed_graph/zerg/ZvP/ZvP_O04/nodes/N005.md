## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army. Add more Zerglings and Queens, and consider teching to Roaches. Keep expanding and maintain a strong economy. Scout to determine the opponent's tech path.

The opponent's tech investment is heavy, so they may be going for a tech-heavy composition. By building a solid ground army, you can defend against early pressure and be ready to transition to counter their composition.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more versatile ground army with Roaches and possibly Mutalisks if the opponent goes for air. Aim to have a strong economy and a solid army by the midgame.
