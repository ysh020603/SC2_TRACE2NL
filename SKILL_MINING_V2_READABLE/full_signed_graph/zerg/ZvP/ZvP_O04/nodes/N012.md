# N012 — Early Game Expansion Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on expanding and building a strong economy. The opponent has been scouted with a Zealot, indicating a possible early ground push. Continue expanding and building economy. Start producing Zerglings and Queens for defense. Keep scouting to detect any early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the early game with an unknown army composition, but a Zealot has been observed. They have a heavy economy and moderate expansion, moderate production, and heavy technology investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a ground-oriented macro posture, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is light or uncertain, and technology is light or uncertain. You have no significant army yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and building economy. Start producing Zerglings and Queens for defense. Keep scouting to detect any early pressure.

A strong economy is the foundation for all later transitions. The opponent's Zealot suggests they may be planning early aggression, so you need to be ready to defend while still expanding.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground army with Zerglings and Queens, while continuing to expand. Aim to have a strong economy by the time the opponent's tech comes online.
