## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army. Add more Roaches and consider teching to Hydralisks or Lurkers to counter Immortals. Use Overseers to detect Warp Prism harass. Maintain your economy and expansion.

Immortals are strong against armored units, so you need to mix in light units like Zerglings and Hydralisks. Warp Prisms allow for harass, so keep defenses at your bases. Your heavy economy allows you to support a larger army.
