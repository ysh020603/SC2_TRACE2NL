# N009 — Midgame Ground vs Ground with Immortals

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has been scouted as a ground-based Protoss army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. You have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. Continue strengthening your ground army. Add more Roaches and consider teching to Hydralisks or Lurkers to counter Immortals. Use Overseers for detection. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. They have heavy production and technology investment, and their defense is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the midgame with a ground-oriented army, heavy economy, and heavy expansion. Production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army. Add more Roaches and consider teching to Hydralisks or Lurkers to counter Immortals. Use Overseers for detection. Maintain your economy and expansion.

Immortals are strong against armored units, so you need to mix in light units like Zerglings and Hydralisks. Your heavy economy allows you to support a larger army. The opponent's heavy defense suggests a longer game, so focus on macro.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more versatile ground army with Hydralisks or Lurkers, and possibly add Mutalisks for harassment. Aim to have a strong economy and a versatile army.
