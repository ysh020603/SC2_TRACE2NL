# N002 — Early-Midgame Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240-360 seconds, both players have unknown army compositions and light or uncertain production/tech. No reliable combat unit cues from the opponent. Maintain current army path and continue developing with safety checks. Keep scouting to identify the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat unit cues. Production and tech appear light or uncertain. The opponent's strategy is unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, with light or uncertain production and tech. You have not committed to a specific strategy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue developing with safety checks. Keep scouting to identify the opponent's intentions.

With both sides unknown, it is risky to commit to a specific tech or army composition. Maintaining flexibility allows you to react to the opponent's first major move.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined strategy once the opponent's composition becomes clearer. If the opponent is greedy, consider pressuring; if they are aggressive, focus on defense.
