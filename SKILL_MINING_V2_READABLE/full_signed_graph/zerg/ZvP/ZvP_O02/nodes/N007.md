# N007 — Early-Midgame Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, the opponent shows a ground army with Stalkers. They have heavy production and tech. You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and light tech. Strengthen your ground army and continue teching. Increase economy to support more production. Maintain defense and be ready to react to a possible attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground army with Stalkers. They have heavy production and tech, and may be preparing a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. Your economy is heavy, but production and tech are moderate. You are vulnerable to a timing attack.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue teching. Increase economy to support more production. Maintain defense and be ready to react to a possible attack.

The opponent's ground army is strong, but you have the economic advantage. By strengthening your ground forces and teching, you can outlast them in a macro game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground composition, such as adding Roach/Ravager or Hydralisk, and consider getting upgrades. If the opponent attacks, defend and counter.
