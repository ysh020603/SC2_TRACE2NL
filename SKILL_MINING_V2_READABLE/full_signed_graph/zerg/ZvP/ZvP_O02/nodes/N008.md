## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the opponent shows a ground army with Stalkers. They have heavy production and tech. You have a ground army with Queens, with heavy production and moderate tech. Strengthen your ground army and continue teching. Increase economy and expansion. Maintain defense and be ready to react to a possible attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground army with Stalkers. They have heavy production and tech, and may be preparing a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Queens. Your economy and production are heavy, but tech is moderate. You are in a good position to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue teching. Increase economy and expansion. Maintain defense and be ready to react to a possible attack.

The opponent's ground army is strong, but you have the economic and production advantage. By strengthening your ground forces and teching, you can outlast them in a macro game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground composition, such as adding Roach/Ravager or Hydralisk, and consider getting upgrades. If the opponent attacks, defend and counter.
