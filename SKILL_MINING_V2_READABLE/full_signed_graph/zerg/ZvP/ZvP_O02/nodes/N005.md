# N005 — Early Game Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent has unknown army composition but moderate production and heavy tech. You have unknown army composition and light or uncertain production/tech. Maintain current army path and continue developing with safety checks. Keep scouting to identify the opponent's tech choice.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat unit cues. Production appears moderate, tech appears heavy. The opponent may be teching quickly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, with light or uncertain production and tech. You have not committed to a specific strategy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and continue developing with safety checks. Keep scouting to identify the opponent's tech choice.

The opponent's heavy tech investment suggests they may be going for a tech-based strategy. Maintaining flexibility allows you to counter their tech choice.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined strategy once the opponent's tech choice becomes clearer. If they are teching to air, prepare anti-air; if they are teching to ground, prepare ground counters.
