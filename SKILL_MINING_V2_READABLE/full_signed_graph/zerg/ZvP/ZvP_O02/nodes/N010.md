# N010 — Early Game Ground vs Unknown

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the opponent has unknown army composition but heavy defense and moderate economy. You have a ground army with Zerglings, with light production and tech. Strengthen your ground army and continue teching. Increase economy and expansion. Maintain defense and be ready to react to a possible attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat unit cues. Defense appears heavy, economy moderate. The opponent may be playing defensively.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zerglings. Your economy is moderate, but expansion is heavy. You are in a good position to expand further.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue teching. Increase economy and expansion. Maintain defense and be ready to react to a possible attack.

The opponent's heavy defense suggests they may be playing defensively. By strengthening your army and economy, you can either pressure them or outmacro them.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive stance if the opponent is confirmed greedy, or continue macroing if they are defensive. Consider adding Roaches or other tech.
