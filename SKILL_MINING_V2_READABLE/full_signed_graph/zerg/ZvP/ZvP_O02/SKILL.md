## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on economy and expansion, with a ground-oriented army. The early game is flexible, with many possible transitions based on scouting information.

Develop a strong economy and expand while maintaining a ground army capable of defending and pressuring. Adapt to opponent's tech choices with appropriate counters.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on live scouting and game state.
