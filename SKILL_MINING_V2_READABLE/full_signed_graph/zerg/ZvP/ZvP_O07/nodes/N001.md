# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansions, with a moderate ground army (Zerglings, Queens). Opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, heavy production and tech. Continue developing your economy and production while strengthening your ground army. Maintain a defensive posture with safety checks to avoid being caught off guard.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-oriented Protoss with Stalkers, heavy production and tech investment. They may be preparing for a timing attack or expanding further. Exact army composition and counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and multiple bases, but your army is still developing. Your tech is light, and you have moderate production. You are vulnerable to early pressure but have potential for rapid growth.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production while strengthening your ground army. Maintain a defensive posture with safety checks to avoid being caught off guard.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to invest in both army and tech. Keeping a balanced approach allows you to respond to either a ground push or a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more solid midgame army composition, possibly adding Roaches or Hydralisks, while continuing to expand and tech up.
