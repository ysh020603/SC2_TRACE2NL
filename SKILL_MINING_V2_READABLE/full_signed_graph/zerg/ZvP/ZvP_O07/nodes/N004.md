# N004 — Early-Midgame Tech and Economy Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansions, with a moderate ground army. Opponent's posture is unknown, but they have heavy tech investment. Increase your economy and tech development while maintaining a defensive army. Scout to identify the opponent's tech path and adapt accordingly.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear about the opponent's army, but they are investing heavily in tech. They might be going for a specific tech build, such as stargate or twilight.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and multiple bases, but your army is still light. You have moderate production and light tech. You are in a development phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy and tech development while maintaining a defensive army. Scout to identify the opponent's tech path and adapt accordingly.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to invest in tech. However, you need to know what the opponent is doing to choose the right tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more developed state with a clear tech direction, such as Roach or Hydralisk, and a stronger army.
