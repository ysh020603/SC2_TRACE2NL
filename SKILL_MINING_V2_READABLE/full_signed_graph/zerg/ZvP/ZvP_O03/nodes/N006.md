# N006 — Early Game Economy and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and are expanding. The opponent has an unknown army composition but shows a Zealot and is heavily teching. Continue to expand and develop your economy while maintaining a defensive ground army. Consider scouting to determine the opponent's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's army is unknown, but they have a Zealot and are heavily teching. They may be going for a tech rush or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and your tech is light. You are in a safe macro position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to expand and develop your economy while maintaining a defensive ground army. Consider scouting to determine the opponent's tech path.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to expand and tech. The opponent's heavy tech investment suggests they may be going for a timing, so you need to be ready.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state where you have a solid economy and tech, ready to adapt to the opponent's strategy.
