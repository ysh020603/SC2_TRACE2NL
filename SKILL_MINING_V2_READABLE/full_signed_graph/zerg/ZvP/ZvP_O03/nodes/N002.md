# N002 — Early-Midgame Unknown Opponent Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a heavy economy and are producing Zerglings and Queens. The opponent's army composition is unknown, but they have a Stalker and are heavily teching. Maintain your economy and production while sending scouts to determine the opponent's tech path. Be ready to adapt your army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's army is unknown, but they have a Stalker and are heavily teching. They may be going for a tech rush or a timing attack. Their exact plan is unclear.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and are expanding. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and your tech is light. You are in a safe macro position but need to scout.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and production while sending scouts to determine the opponent's tech path. Be ready to adapt your army composition.

With an unknown opponent, it's important to gather information. Your economy is strong, so you can afford to invest in scouting and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a tech that counters the opponent's observed style, whether that be ground or air.
