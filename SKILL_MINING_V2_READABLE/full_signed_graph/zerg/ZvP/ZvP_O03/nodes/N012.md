## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. The opponent has an air army with Oracles, and is heavily teching. Consider adding anti-air units like Hydralisks or Queens to counter the air threat. Continue to develop your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an air army with Oracles, indicating a potential air commitment. They are heavily teching.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and a moderate ground army. Your tech is light, and you are in a good position to expand further. Your defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider adding anti-air units like Hydralisks or Queens to counter the air threat. Continue to develop your economy and tech.

The opponent's Oracles are a threat, so you need to prepare anti-air. Your economy is strong, so you can afford to tech and produce.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air units to counter the opponent's air army.
