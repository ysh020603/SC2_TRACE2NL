# ZvP_O03 Economy / Expansion / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvP_O03
- Matchup: Zerg vs Protoss
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / upgrade opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening that prioritizes economic growth, early expansion, and upgrade investment while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented posture. The strategy aims to outscale the Protoss through superior economy and tech, but requires careful scouting to adapt to potential pressure or tech switches.

Develop a strong economy and tech base while preserving flexibility to respond to Protoss pressure or tech choices. Aim to reach a midgame army composition that can counter the opponent's observed style.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, you have a solid economy and are producing Zerglings and Queens. The opponent shows a ground-oriented composition with Zealots and Sentries, and appears to be investing heavily in tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech while maintaining a defensive ground army. Consider adding Roaches for defense against potential Zealot/Sentry pressure.
