# N011 — Air Harassment Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent has an air army with Phoenixes and Oracles. You need to defend against air harassment. Build Spore Crawlers at your bases and start teching towards anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks). Maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has an air army with Phoenixes and Oracles. They are likely to harass your workers or bases.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your tech is moderate, but you lack anti-air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Build Spore Crawlers at your bases and start teching towards anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks). Maintain your economy.

You need to defend against air harassment to protect your economy. Your economy can support a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air units.
