# N008 — Early Game Flexibility

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, opponent is unknown but teching. You have a strong economy and expansion, and need to stay flexible. Continue macroing and expanding, while scouting to identify the opponent's tech. Maintain a defensive ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is teching heavily, but their army composition is unknown. They may be going for a fast tech or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your tech is light, and you are not committed to a specific build.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue macroing and expanding, while scouting to identify the opponent's tech. Maintain a defensive ground army.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to be greedy. However, you need to know what the opponent is doing to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a specific response once you have scouted the opponent's tech.
