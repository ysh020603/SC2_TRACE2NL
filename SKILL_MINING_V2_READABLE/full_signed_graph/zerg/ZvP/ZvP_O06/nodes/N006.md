# N006 — Midgame Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a ground army with Observers and Immortals. You have a heavy economy and a moderate army, but need to consolidate. Consolidate your army and prepare for a defensive engagement. Continue producing units and maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Observers, and Immortals. They are likely to push soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your production is now heavy, and you have Overseers for detection.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consolidate your army and prepare for a defensive engagement. Continue producing units and maintain your economy.

You need to be ready to defend against the opponent's push. Your army is not yet large enough to take a fight.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defensive posture and wait for the opponent's push.
