# N004 — Stabilize and Scout

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent is ground-based, but you are not sure about their exact tech. You have a moderate army and need to stabilize. Focus on stabilizing your defense and scouting the opponent's tech. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground army of Zealots and Stalkers, with heavy production and tech. They could be going for a variety of tech choices.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your tech is light, and you are not committed to a specific tech path.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your defense and scouting the opponent's tech. Maintain your economy and expansion.

You need more information before committing to a tech path. Staying flexible is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a specific tech response once you have scouted the opponent's tech.
