# N010 — Carrier Threat Response

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame, opponent has a ground army with Carriers. You need to prepare for a potential Carrier transition. Start teching towards anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks, Corruptors, or Spore Crawlers) while maintaining your ground army. Continue expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Carriers. They are likely to transition to a Carrier-based army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a heavy ground army. Your tech is heavy, but you lack anti-air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Start teching towards anti-air (e.g., Hydralisks, Corruptors, or Spore Crawlers) while maintaining your ground army. Continue expanding.

You need to be able to counter Carriers. Your economy can support a tech switch.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that includes anti-air units.
