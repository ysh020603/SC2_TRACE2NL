# N001 — Early Macro Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own economy and expansion are heavy, opponent unknown but likely teching. Focus on macro and scouting. Continue expanding and droning, while maintaining a defensive ground army. Scout to identify opponent tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is teching heavily, possibly going for a fast tech build. Their army composition is unclear, but they have a Zealot or two.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and expansion lead, with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, tech is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and droning, while maintaining a defensive ground army. Scout to identify opponent tech path.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to take a greedy approach. However, you need to know what the opponent is doing to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more specific response once you identify the opponent's tech choice (e.g., ground, air, or tech).

## Possible Next Situations

### N002 — Midgame Ground Transition

**Situation:** Early-midgame, opponent is now confirmed ground-based with Zealots and Stalkers. Your economy is strong, but you need to start building a more substantial army.

**Direction:** Increase production and start teching towards a more robust ground composition (e.g., Roaches or Hydralisks). Continue expanding.
