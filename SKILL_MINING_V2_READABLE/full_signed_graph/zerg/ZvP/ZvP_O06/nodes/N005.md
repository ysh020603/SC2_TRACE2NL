# N005 — Midgame Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has a strong ground army with Immortals. You have a heavy economy and are starting to build a more substantial army. Increase production and strengthen your ground army. Consider adding units that counter Immortals (e.g., Roaches or Hydralisks).

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. They are likely to push soon.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your tech is now heavy, and you are producing more units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and strengthen your ground army. Consider adding units that counter Immortals (e.g., Roaches or Hydralisks).

You need to have a strong enough army to defend against the opponent's push. Your economy can support a larger army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a larger, more powerful ground army.
