# N003 — Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent is still ground-based, but you have started to invest in technology. Your army is still moderate, but you are teching up. Continue teching towards a key unit (e.g., Roach Warren, Hydralisk Den, or Lair) while maintaining a defensive army. Keep expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, and is teching heavily. They may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Your tech is now moderate, and you are starting to invest more.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching towards a key unit (e.g., Roach Warren, Hydralisk Den, or Lair) while maintaining a defensive army. Keep expanding.

You need to get to a tech advantage to deal with the opponent's army. Your economy can support this investment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a tech-based army composition that can counter the opponent's ground forces.
