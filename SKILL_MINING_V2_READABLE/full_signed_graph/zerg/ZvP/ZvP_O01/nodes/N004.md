# N004 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent has ground army, own army is developing, economy heavy. Increase economy and expansion, maintain production, continue tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech, ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy and expansion, maintain production, continue tech.

Opponent's heavy production suggests potential aggression; need to balance economy with defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a strong economy while building a defensive army.
