# N005 — Midgame Ground Army with Moderate Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, opponent has ground army with Sentries, own army is moderate tech. Maintain army and economy, continue tech, keep defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, moderate production and tech, ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain army and economy, continue tech, keep defense.

Opponent's composition is standard; need to match with upgrades and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame position.
