# N009 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, opponent has air army with Phoenixes and Oracles, own army is ground. Increase tech and production, consider air transition, maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zealots, Stalkers, Phoenixes, Oracles, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, production, and tech, ground army with Zerglings, Queens, Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase tech and production, consider air transition, maintain economy.

Opponent's air army requires a response; need to transition to anti-air or air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a mixed army with anti-air capabilities.
