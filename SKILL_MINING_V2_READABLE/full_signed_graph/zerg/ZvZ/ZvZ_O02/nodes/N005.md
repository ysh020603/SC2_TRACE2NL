# N005 — Early Game Ground Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have scouted the opponent and they appear to be pursuing a ground macro posture, with Queens and a heavy economy. You decide to commit to a ground-based army as well. Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense. Continue to expand and develop your economy. Focus on building a solid foundation for the midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Queens. The opponent's economy is heavy, and they are expanding. They may be preparing for a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zerglings and are starting to build Queens. Your economy is light or uncertain, but you are planning to expand. You are in the early game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your defense. Continue to expand and develop your economy. Focus on building a solid foundation for the midgame.

In ZvZ, a ground macro posture is a safe and effective way to play. By matching the opponent's economy and army, you can avoid falling behind and set up for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more developed ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and expand to support it.
