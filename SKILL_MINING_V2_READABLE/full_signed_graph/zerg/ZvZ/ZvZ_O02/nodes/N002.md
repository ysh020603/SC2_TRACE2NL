# N002 — Early-Midgame Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game progresses into the early-midgame while the opponent's posture remains unknown. You have not yet committed to a specific strategy, and the opponent's intentions are still unclear. Continue to develop your economy and infrastructure while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep scouting to detect any sudden changes in the opponent's behavior.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's economy, production, and technology remain light or uncertain. Their army domain is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain. You have not built a significant army. You are in the early-midgame, and the opponent has not shown any clear signs of aggression or expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and infrastructure while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep scouting to detect any sudden changes in the opponent's behavior.

If the opponent is also playing passively, you can safely expand and build a strong economy. However, you must be prepared to defend against a possible timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more concrete strategy once you have a better read on the opponent. This could involve committing to a ground army or teching up.
