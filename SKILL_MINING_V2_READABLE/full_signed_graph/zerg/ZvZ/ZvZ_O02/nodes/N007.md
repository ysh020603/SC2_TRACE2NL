# N007 — Midgame Ground Macro Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have committed to a ground macro posture and are in the early-midgame. You have a strong economy and a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. Continue to strengthen your ground army and expand. Maintain your economy and be ready to transition to a more advanced composition if needed.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings. The opponent's economy is heavy, and they are expanding. They may be preparing for a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and a ground army with Zerglings and Queens. Your defense is light or uncertain, but you are expanding. You are in the early-midgame.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and expand. Maintain your economy and be ready to transition to a more advanced composition if needed.

A strong ground army with a good economy is a solid foundation for the midgame. By continuing to develop, you can set up for a powerful late-game composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition, such as Roaches or Banelings, or consider teching to air if the situation calls for it.
