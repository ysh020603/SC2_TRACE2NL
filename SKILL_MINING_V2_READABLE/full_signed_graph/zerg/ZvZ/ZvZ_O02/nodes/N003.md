# N003 — Midgame Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game reaches the midgame, and the opponent's posture is still unknown. You have maintained a flexible approach, but now you need to start making more concrete decisions. Start committing to a specific strategy. Given the lack of information, it is often safe to focus on a ground-based army with a strong economy. Continue to scout and be ready to adapt.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's economy, production, and technology remain light or uncertain. Their army domain is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain. You have not built a significant army. You are in the midgame, and the opponent has not shown any clear signs of aggression or expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Start committing to a specific strategy. Given the lack of information, it is often safe to focus on a ground-based army with a strong economy. Continue to scout and be ready to adapt.

In the midgame, you need to have a plan to avoid falling behind. A ground macro posture is a solid default in ZvZ, as it allows for both defense and aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a concrete army composition, such as Zerglings and Queens, and start expanding to support it.
