# N006 — Early Game Ground Macro Posture (Defensive)

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have scouted the opponent and they appear to be pursuing a ground macro posture, with Queens and a heavy economy. You decide to commit to a ground-based army, but with a more defensive focus. Maintain your ground army and focus on defense. Continue to develop your economy and expand. Be ready to defend against any early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Queens. The opponent's economy is heavy, and they are expanding. They may be preparing for a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with moderate economy. You are building Queens and Zerglings. Your defense is light or uncertain, but you are planning to expand. You are in the early game.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and focus on defense. Continue to develop your economy and expand. Be ready to defend against any early pressure.

A defensive approach can be safer if you are unsure about the opponent's intentions. By focusing on defense, you can avoid early game losses and transition to a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive ground army once you have a solid economy and defense.
