# N004 — Late-Midgame Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game is in the late-midgame, and the opponent's posture is still unknown. You have maintained a flexible approach, but now you need to make a decisive move. Make a decisive move. If you have been playing passively, you need to either expand aggressively or build a strong army to pressure the opponent. Consider teching up to a more advanced composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's economy, production, and technology remain light or uncertain. Their army domain is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain. You have not built a significant army. You are in the late-midgame, and the opponent has not shown any clear signs of aggression or expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Make a decisive move. If you have been playing passively, you need to either expand aggressively or build a strong army to pressure the opponent. Consider teching up to a more advanced composition.

At this stage, the game cannot remain passive for long. You need to take a proactive stance to secure a win. A ground army with tech support is a common choice.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition, such as Roaches or Mutalisks, and start applying pressure on the opponent.
