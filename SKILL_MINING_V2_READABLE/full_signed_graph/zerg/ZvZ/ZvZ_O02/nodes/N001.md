# N001 — Early Game Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own posture is also uncertain. This node represents the initial phase where you are still gathering information. Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks. Focus on economy and scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. Their army domain is unknown, and they may be pursuing any strategy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition. You are in the early game, with limited scouting information.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping safety checks. Focus on economy and scouting to determine the opponent's intentions.

In the early game, overcommitting to a specific strategy can be punished if the opponent is doing something different. Maintaining flexibility allows you to react to the opponent's first moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture once you have more information about the opponent's strategy. This could be a ground macro posture or a more aggressive build.

## Possible Next Situations

### N002 — Early-Midgame Unknown Posture

**Situation:** The game progresses into the early-midgame while the opponent's posture remains unknown. You have not yet committed to a specific strategy, and the opponent's intentions are still unclear.

**Direction:** Continue to develop your economy and infrastructure while maintaining a defensive posture. Keep scouting to detect any sudden changes in the opponent's behavior.
