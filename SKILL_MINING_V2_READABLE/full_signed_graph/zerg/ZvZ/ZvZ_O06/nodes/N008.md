# N008 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You are in the midgame and have decided to transition to an air-based army with Mutalisks. Strengthen your air force and use it to harass the opponent's economy and force a reaction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production, and heavy technology. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is moderate. You have Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers, indicating a mix of ground and air.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your air force and use it to harass the opponent's economy and force a reaction.

Mutalisks can be very effective in ZvZ if the opponent is unprepared for air. They can also provide map control and harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish air superiority and use it to gain an economic or positional advantage.
