# N001 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players are establishing their economies and initial production. Maintain your expansion and economy focus while ensuring you have enough defensive units to handle early aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a similar ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion posture, with moderate production and light technology. Your army is ground-oriented with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your expansion and economy focus while ensuring you have enough defensive units to handle early aggression.

A strong economy will allow you to outproduce your opponent in the mid-game. Early safety is important to avoid falling behind to a timing attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a solid economy and a modest army to defend or apply pressure.
