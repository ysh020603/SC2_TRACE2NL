# N005 — Early-Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have a strong economy and are investing in technology earlier than usual. Continue increasing your technology and production, while maintaining a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a ground macro path with light technology and moderate production. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and technology is moderate. You have Queens and an Overseer, indicating scouting and tech investment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing your technology and production, while maintaining a defensive posture.

Investing in tech early can give you a mid-game advantage, but you must ensure you have enough army to defend against early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the mid-game with a tech advantage and a stable economy.
