# N002 — Early-Midgame Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You have a solid economy and are moving into the early-midgame, with both players still in a macro-oriented phase. Continue developing your economy and production, while starting to invest in technology to prepare for the mid-game.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a similar path, with a ground posture, moderate production, and light technology. Pressure is still possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and technology is now moderate. Your army remains ground-based with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and production, while starting to invest in technology to prepare for the mid-game.

Maintaining economic growth while teching up will give you more options in the mid-game, whether to transition to a different army composition or to out-macro your opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the mid-game with a strong economy, moderate tech, and a capable ground army.
