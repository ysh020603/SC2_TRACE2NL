# N007 — Midgame Defense and Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You are in the midgame and facing a potentially aggressive opponent with a heavy ground army. Focus on defense and stabilization, while continuing to grow your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production, and heavy technology. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and technology is moderate. Your army includes Roaches and Queens, and you have a heavy defense posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on defense and stabilization, while continuing to grow your economy and tech.

If the opponent is preparing an attack, a solid defense will allow you to survive and then counter with your superior economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your position and prepare to transition to a stronger army composition.
