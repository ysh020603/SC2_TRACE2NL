# N006 — Aggressive Expansion and Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You are in the early-midgame and have decided to expand aggressively and increase production. Increase your expansion and production to out-macro your opponent, while keeping a small defensive force.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is on a similar ground macro path with moderate production and light technology. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light. You have Queens and are expanding quickly.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion and production to out-macro your opponent, while keeping a small defensive force.

A faster economy and more production will allow you to overwhelm the opponent in the mid-game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a significant economic lead by the mid-game.
