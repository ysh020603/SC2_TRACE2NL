# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You are in the midgame, and the opponent has started to build a heavier ground army with Roaches. Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's potential threat, while continuing to expand your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production, and heavy technology. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is moderate, and technology is moderate. Your army includes Zerglings, Hydralisks, and Queens, indicating a mix of ground units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to match the opponent's potential threat, while continuing to expand your economy.

The opponent's heavier production and tech suggest they may be preparing for a timing attack or a tech switch. A stronger army will help you defend or counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile ground army that can handle Roach-based aggression and be ready to adapt to tech switches.
