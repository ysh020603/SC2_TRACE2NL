# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

You are in the late-midgame, with both players having heavy economies and production. Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding tech or air units to gain an advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production, and moderate technology. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is moderate. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, but you may need to diversify.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding tech or air units to gain an advantage.

With both players on heavy economies, the one who can field a more effective army composition will have the edge. Adding tech or air can provide a decisive advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a superior army composition or economy to force an advantage.
