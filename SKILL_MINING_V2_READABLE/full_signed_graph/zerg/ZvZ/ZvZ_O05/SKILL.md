# ZvZ_O05 Economy / Ground / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvZ_O05
- Matchup: Zerg vs Zerg
- Opening Family: economy / ground / upgrade
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy and ground army development with light early technology investment. The strategic posture is flexible, allowing adaptation to either a ground-heavy composition or a transition into air units like Mutalisks depending on scouting information.

Develop a strong economy and ground army while maintaining flexibility to transition into air or tech as needed. Focus on map control and creep spread to support a macro-oriented playstyle.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [NEGATIVE] N001 — Early Game Vulnerability

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, both players are in the early game with light defenses and uncertain technology. The opponent may be preparing an early pressure or rush.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid over-committing to economy at the expense of defense. Do not neglect scouting or assume the opponent is passive.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Midgame Macro

**Trigger situation:**  
The game has progressed to the early-midgame. Both players have expanded and are producing Zerglings and Queens. The opponent's defense is heavy, suggesting a macro-oriented approach.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and army. Add more production structures (Hatcheries) and consider teching up to Roaches or other units. Maintain creep spread and map vision.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army

**Trigger situation:**  
The game has reached the midgame. The opponent has a heavy production and moderate technology, with Zerglings and Queens. You have added Roaches to your composition.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army by adding more Roaches and upgrading their attacks and armor. Consider adding a few Overseers for detection. Maintain map control and watch for flanks.

**Read for details:** `N003`
