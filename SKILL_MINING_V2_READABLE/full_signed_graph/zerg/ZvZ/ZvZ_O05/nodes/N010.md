# N010 — Midgame Air Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a heavy air presence with Mutalisks and Corruptors, while you have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Prepare for air defense by building Spore Crawlers and Hydralisks. Consider adding Corruptors to fight for air control. Maintain a strong ground army to defend against any ground attacks.

## When This Applies

- The opponent has a heavy air presence with Mutalisks and Corruptors, along with Zerglings and Queens. They may be planning an air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your technology is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Prepare for air defense by building Spore Crawlers and Hydralisks. Consider adding Corruptors to fight for air control. Maintain a strong ground army to defend against any ground attacks.

Air units can be devastating if not countered. Building anti-air units is essential to protect your economy and army.
