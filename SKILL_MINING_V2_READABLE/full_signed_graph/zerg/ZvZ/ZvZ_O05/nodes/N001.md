# N001 — Early Game Vulnerability

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

At the start of the game, both players are in the early game with light defenses and uncertain technology. The opponent may be preparing an early pressure or rush. Avoid over-committing to economy at the expense of defense. Do not neglect scouting or assume the opponent is passive.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a similar economy-focused path, but their defensive posture is light or uncertain. They may be planning an early attack or expanding quickly.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy focus with light defense and moderate production. Your army is small, mostly Queens, and you are vulnerable to early aggression.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-committing to economy at the expense of defense. Do not neglect scouting or assume the opponent is passive.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you have a few defensive units and scouting confirms the opponent is not rushing. If you see a large Zergling force, prepare for defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stable early-midgame with a solid economy and a modest army to deter attacks.

## Possible Next Situations

### N009 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

**Situation:** In the early-midgame, you have a heavy economy but light defense. The opponent has a heavy economy and defense, with Zerglings and Queens.

**Direction:** Stabilize your defense by building Spine Crawlers and a few more Zerglings. Continue developing your economy and consider teching up to Roaches for safety.
