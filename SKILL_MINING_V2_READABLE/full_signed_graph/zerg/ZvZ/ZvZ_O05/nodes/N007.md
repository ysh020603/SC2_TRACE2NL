## Summary

In the midgame, you have a heavy air presence with Mutalisks and Overseers, while the opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Use Mutalisks to harass the opponent's workers and expansions. Keep your Mutalisk count high and avoid engaging in unfavorable ground fights. Continue expanding and teching.

Mutalisk harassment can cripple the opponent's economy and force them to make defensive units, giving you a strategic advantage.
