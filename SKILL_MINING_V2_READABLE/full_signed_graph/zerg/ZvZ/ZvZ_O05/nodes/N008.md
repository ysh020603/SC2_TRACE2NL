# N008 — Defensive Stance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has a heavy air presence with Mutalisks and Corruptors, while you have a ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your defense is heavy. Focus on defense by building Spore Crawlers and Hydralisks to counter air threats. Maintain a strong ground army to defend against any ground attacks. Consider adding Corruptors of your own.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy air presence with Mutalisks and Corruptors, along with Zerglings and Queens. They may be planning an air attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Roaches, Queens, and Overseers. Your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on defense by building Spore Crawlers and Hydralisks to counter air threats. Maintain a strong ground army to defend against any ground attacks. Consider adding Corruptors of your own.

Defending against air attacks is crucial. Spore Crawlers and Hydralisks are effective against Mutalisks and Corruptors.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stable late-game state with a balanced army.
