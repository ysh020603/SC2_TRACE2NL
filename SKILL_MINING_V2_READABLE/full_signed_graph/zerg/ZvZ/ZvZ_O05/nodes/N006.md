# N006 — Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, you have transitioned to a heavy air presence with Mutalisks and Overseers, while the opponent remains ground-focused with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Continue building Mutalisks and harass the opponent's economy. Use your air advantage to control the map and force the opponent to react. Consider adding Corruptors if the opponent builds anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. They may not have anti-air units, making them vulnerable to Mutalisks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army includes Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers. Your technology is moderate, and you are investing in air upgrades.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building Mutalisks and harass the opponent's economy. Use your air advantage to control the map and force the opponent to react. Consider adding Corruptors if the opponent builds anti-air.

Mutalisks are highly mobile and can deal significant damage if the opponent lacks anti-air. This can give you a strong economic advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-game state with air dominance and a strong economy.
