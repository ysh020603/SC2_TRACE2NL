# N004 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, you have a moderate economy and light expansion, while the opponent has a heavy economy and defense. This suggests a macro-oriented game. Focus on catching up in economy by expanding and building more drones. Maintain a small defensive force to deter attacks. Consider teching up to Roaches for safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and defense, with Zerglings and Queens. They are likely focusing on macro and may be ahead in workers.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and light expansion. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens. You are slightly behind in economy compared to the opponent.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on catching up in economy by expanding and building more drones. Maintain a small defensive force to deter attacks. Consider teching up to Roaches for safety.

In a macro game, economy is king. Catching up in workers will allow you to compete in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stable early-midgame with a competitive economy.
