## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game has reached the late-midgame. Both players have heavy economies and armies consisting of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. The opponent's technology is moderate. Continue strengthening your ground army. Upgrade Roach attacks and armor. Consider adding a few Infestors or Ultralisks for late-game power. Maintain map control and vision.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and production, with a similar army composition. They may be preparing for a large engagement or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army is strong with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your technology is moderate, and you are in a good position to upgrade.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army. Upgrade Roach attacks and armor. Consider adding a few Infestors or Ultralisks for late-game power. Maintain map control and vision.

In the late-midgame, upgrades and army composition are crucial. A well-upgraded Roach army can be very cost-effective.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into the late game with a powerful army and tech advantage.
