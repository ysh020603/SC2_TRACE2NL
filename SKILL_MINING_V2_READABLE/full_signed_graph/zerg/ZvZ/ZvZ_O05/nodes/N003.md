# N003 — Midgame Ground Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game has reached the midgame. The opponent has a heavy production and moderate technology, with Zerglings and Queens. You have added Roaches to your composition. Strengthen your ground army by adding more Roaches and upgrading their attacks and armor. Consider adding a few Overseers for detection. Maintain map control and watch for flanks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy production and moderate technology, with Zerglings and Queens. They may be preparing for a Roach push or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and a moderate production. Your army consists of Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your technology is light, but you are in a good position to upgrade.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army by adding more Roaches and upgrading their attacks and armor. Consider adding a few Overseers for detection. Maintain map control and watch for flanks.

Roaches are a strong midgame unit in ZvZ. Upgrading them gives you a significant advantage in army trades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-midgame with a powerful ground army and potential for tech upgrades.
