# N009 — Early-Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, you have a heavy economy but light defense. The opponent has a heavy economy and defense, with Zerglings and Queens. Stabilize your defense by building Spine Crawlers and a few more Zerglings. Continue developing your economy and consider teching up to Roaches for safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy economy and defense, with Zerglings and Queens. They may be preparing for an attack or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production. Your army consists of Zerglings and Queens. Your defense is light, making you vulnerable to attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense by building Spine Crawlers and a few more Zerglings. Continue developing your economy and consider teching up to Roaches for safety.

A stable defense allows you to develop your economy without fear of harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stable midgame with a solid defense and economy.
