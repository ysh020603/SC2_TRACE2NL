# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy defense and economy, moderate production, light technology. Opponent appears similar with light defense and heavy economy. Maintain defense and economy, strengthen ground army, continue production and technology development with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their defense is light, economy heavy, production moderate, technology light. They may apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defensive posture with a strong economy and expansions. Production is moderate, technology is light. You are vulnerable to early pressure but have safety checks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense and economy, strengthen ground army, continue production and technology development with safety checks.

A heavy defense and economy foundation allows you to outscale if the opponent commits to early aggression. Maintaining production and technology keeps you flexible.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army and heavier production while maintaining defensive integrity.
