# N009 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Air Presence

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, heavy economy. Opponent has heavy air presence and ground posture. Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology, stabilize then develop.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen cues, heavy production and economy, moderate technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with heavy production and technology, heavy economy and defense. Air presence is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology, stabilize then develop.

With opponent having air presence, you need to prepare anti-air or transition to air yourself.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a posture that can handle air threats, possibly with air units.
