# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own and opponent have heavy ground macro postures with heavy economy, production, and technology. Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and production, keep technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen cues, heavy production and economy, moderate technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with heavy production and technology, heavy economy and defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and production, keep technology development.

With heavy technology, you can leverage upgrades and advanced units to gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game posture with a decisive army composition.
