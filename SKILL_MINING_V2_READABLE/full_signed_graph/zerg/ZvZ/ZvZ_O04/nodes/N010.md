# N010 — Midgame Ground Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and moderate technology, heavy economy. Opponent has heavy ground posture with heavy technology. Increase air presence, continue economy and production, maintain technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen, Overseer cues, heavy production and technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with heavy production and moderate technology, heavy economy and defense. Air presence is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, continue economy and production, maintain technology development.

With heavy air presence, you can leverage air units to gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a posture with a strong air component.
