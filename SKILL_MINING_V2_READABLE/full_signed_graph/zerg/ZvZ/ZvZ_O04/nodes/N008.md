# N008 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy defense and economy, moderate production. Opponent has heavy production and moderate defense. Maintain defense and economy, increase technology, continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, heavy production, moderate defense, moderate technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defense and economy, moderate production, light technology. Expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense and economy, increase technology, continue production.

With a strong economy, you can invest in technology to gain an edge.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army and heavier technology while maintaining defensive integrity.
