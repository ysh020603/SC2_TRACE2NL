# N003 — Early Defense and Economy Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own economy is moderate, defense heavy, production moderate. Opponent has heavy economy and light defense. Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology, stabilize before developing further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, heavy economy, light defense, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defense but moderate economy and expansions. Production is moderate, technology light. You need to stabilize your economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and technology, stabilize before developing further.

With a moderate economy, you need to catch up economically while maintaining defense to avoid being overwhelmed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your economy and defense, then transition to a more developed posture.
