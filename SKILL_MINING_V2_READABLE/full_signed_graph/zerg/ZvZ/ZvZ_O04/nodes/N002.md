# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own and opponent have heavy ground macro postures with heavy economy and production, moderate technology. Continue strengthening ground army, maintain production and economy, keep technology development steady.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, heavy production and economy, moderate technology. They may have a similar army strength.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with heavy production and economy, moderate technology. Defense is heavy, expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army, maintain production and economy, keep technology development steady.

In a mirror matchup, maintaining a strong economy and production allows you to outlast the opponent. Technology upgrades can provide an edge.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame posture with heavier technology and possibly air presence.
