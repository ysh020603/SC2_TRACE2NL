# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy defense and economy, moderate production. Opponent has heavy economy and moderate defense. Maintain defense and economy, strengthen ground army, continue production and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, heavy economy, moderate defense, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defense and economy, moderate production, light technology. Expansions are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defense and economy, strengthen ground army, continue production and technology development.

A strong economy allows you to outscale, but you need to maintain defense to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army and heavier production while maintaining defensive integrity.
