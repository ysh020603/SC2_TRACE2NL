# N011 — Late-Midgame Air Superiority

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Continue increasing air presence and maintain a strong ground army. Keep economy and production high.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Production heavy, tech moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in late-midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens. Air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing air presence and maintain a strong ground army. Keep economy and production high.

With air superiority, you can harass and control the map. Maintaining a strong ground army prevents counterattacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game composition with both air and ground strength.
