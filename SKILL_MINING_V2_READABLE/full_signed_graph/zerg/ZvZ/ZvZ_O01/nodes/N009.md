# N009 — Midgame Air Transition Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production. Opponent shows air presence (Mutalisk cues) and moderate tech. Increase defense and consider transitioning to air or anti-air units. Continue economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen, Overseer cues. Production heavy, tech moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. No air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and consider transitioning to air or anti-air units. Continue economy and production.

Opponent's Mutalisks can be a threat. Preparing anti-air or transitioning to Mutalisks yourself can help counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition that can handle air threats.
