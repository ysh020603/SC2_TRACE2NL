# N010 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production. Opponent shows ground posture with Roach cues, heavy tech. Increase air presence and continue strengthening air forces. Maintain economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Roach, Queen cues. Production and tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in midgame with heavy economy, production, and moderate tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens. Air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and continue strengthening air forces. Maintain economy and production.

Transitioning to Mutalisks can give you mobility and harass potential. Maintaining economy supports this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a strong air-based army with ground support.
