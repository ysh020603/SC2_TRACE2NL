# N006 — Early Defense and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players on heavy economy and expansion, light tech, moderate production. Opponent shows ground posture with Queen cues. Focus on stabilizing defense while continuing economy and expansion. Increase defense posture slightly.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Queen cues. Production moderate, tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech. Ground-oriented army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing defense while continuing economy and expansion. Increase defense posture slightly.

If you feel threatened by early aggression, prioritizing defense can prevent losses while maintaining economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once safe.
