# N008 — Late-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Opponent shows ground posture with air presence (Mutalisk cues). Increase defense posture while continuing economy and production. Consider adding anti-air or air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling, Mutalisk, Roach, Queen cues. Production and tech heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in late-midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. No air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense posture while continuing economy and production. Consider adding anti-air or air units.

With opponent showing air presence, you need to defend against potential Mutalisk harass. Increasing defense and possibly teching to anti-air is wise.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with air defense.
