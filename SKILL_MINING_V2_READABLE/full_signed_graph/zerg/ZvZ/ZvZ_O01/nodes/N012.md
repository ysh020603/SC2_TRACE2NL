# N012 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, both players on heavy economy and expansion, light tech, moderate production. Opponent shows ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Stabilize your position by maintaining economy and production, and consider increasing defense slightly.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Production moderate, tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech. Ground-oriented army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by maintaining economy and production, and consider increasing defense slightly.

In early game, stabilizing your economy and defense can prevent early losses and set up for a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once stable.
