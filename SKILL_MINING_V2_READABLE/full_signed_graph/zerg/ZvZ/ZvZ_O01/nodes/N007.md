# N007 — Early-Midgame Expansion and Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, both players have heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech. Ground armies with Zergling and Queen cues. Increase expansion and production while continuing to strengthen ground forces. Consider increasing tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Production moderate, tech light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early-midgame with heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, light tech. Ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase expansion and production while continuing to strengthen ground forces. Consider increasing tech.

Building a lead in economy and production can set you up for a strong midgame. Ground forces provide safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a stronger economy and army.
