# N001 — Early Economy Focus with Safety

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, both players on heavy economy and expansion, light tech, moderate production. Opponent shows ground posture with possible Queen cues. Continue developing economy and expansion while strengthening ground forces. Maintain defense and consider increasing production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Queen cues. Production moderate, tech light or uncertain. Exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a heavy economy and expansion posture, moderate production, light tech. Ground-oriented army with no air presence yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and expansion while strengthening ground forces. Maintain defense and consider increasing production.

In ZvZ, early economy and expansion advantage can snowball if defended properly. Maintaining a ground army with Queens provides safety against early ling floods.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger ground army composition with more production and tech as the game progresses.
