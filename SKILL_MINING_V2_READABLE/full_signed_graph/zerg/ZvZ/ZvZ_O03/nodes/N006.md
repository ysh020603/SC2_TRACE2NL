# N006 — Early Game Defense Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with opponent on ground posture, moderate production, tech light or uncertain, heavy economy and expansion; own side heavy defense, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansion. Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech, and strengthen ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, tech light or uncertain, heavy economy and expansion, defense light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a ground-oriented army, heavy defense, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, continue production and tech, and strengthen ground army.

Heavy defense early can deter aggression while you build economy and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach early-midgame with a safe economy and a solid defense.
