# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with both sides on ground macro posture, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansion. Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and expansion, keep production and tech steady, and add safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansion. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early-midgame with a ground-oriented army, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansion. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army strength, continue economy and expansion, keep production and tech steady, and add safety checks.

Both sides are developing similarly; maintaining a balanced approach avoids falling behind while preparing for potential pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition smoothly into midgame with a solid ground army and economy.
