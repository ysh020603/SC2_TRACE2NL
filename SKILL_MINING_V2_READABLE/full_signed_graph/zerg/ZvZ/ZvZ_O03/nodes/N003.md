# N003 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game phase with both sides on ground macro posture, moderate production, heavy economy and expansion, tech light or uncertain. Continue economy and expansion, maintain ground army, keep production and tech steady, and add safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, tech light or uncertain, heavy economy and expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in early game with a ground-oriented army, moderate production and tech, heavy economy and expansion, defense light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue economy and expansion, maintain ground army, keep production and tech steady, and add safety checks.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy while staying safe against early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach early-midgame with a healthy economy and a modest army.
