## Opening Strategy

A Zerg versus Zerg opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented posture. The strategy is flexible and adapts based on live observations.

Develop a robust economy and technology base while expanding, keeping ground army strength as a safety net, and remaining adaptable to opponent actions.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adjust based on scouting and opponent behavior.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
