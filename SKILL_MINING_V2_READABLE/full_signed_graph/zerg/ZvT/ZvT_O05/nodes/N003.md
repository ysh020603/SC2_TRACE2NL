## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 360s) with both sides heavy on economy and expansion. Enemy has moderate defense and heavy production, with ground cues including Hellion. Your tech has increased to heavy. Increase technology and continue expanding. Strengthen ground army and maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine, Reaper, Hellion. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy technology. Economy and expansion are heavy, defense is moderate. You have Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase technology and continue expanding. Strengthen ground army and maintain defense.

With heavy tech, you can unlock better units and upgrades to counter the enemy's ground forces.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to midgame with a tech advantage and a solid army.
