# N001 — Early Game Expansion and Ground Defense

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with both players on heavy economy and expansion. Opponent shows ground posture with Marines and Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Own forces are Zerglings and Queens, light tech. Continue expanding and increasing economy while strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and continue tech development. Keep an eye on defense but prioritize macro.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran is on a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, moderate production but heavy tech investment. They may be teching towards something like Siege Tanks or Medivacs. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and expansion lead, with Zerglings and Queens for defense. Production is moderate, tech is light. You are vulnerable to early pressure but have the economic base to recover.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and increasing economy while strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and continue tech development. Keep an eye on defense but prioritize macro.

Your economic lead allows you to outproduce the opponent. By continuing to expand and tech, you can reach a critical mass of units before the Terran's tech pays off. Ground forces are sufficient to defend early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong mid-game position with multiple bases, a solid ground army, and tech ready for a switch if needed.
