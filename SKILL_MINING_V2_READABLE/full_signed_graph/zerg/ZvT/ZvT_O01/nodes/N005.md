# N005 — Early Game Tech Advantage

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and expansion. Opponent's posture is unknown, but production is moderate and tech is heavy. Own forces are Zerglings and Queens, light tech. Continue expanding and teching. Since the opponent is teching, you can safely expand and prepare a counter. Maintain scouting to identify their tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran's army composition is unknown, but they have heavy tech investment. They may be going for a tech-heavy build like cloaked Banshees or Siege Tanks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with Zerglings and Queens. Production is moderate, tech is light. You are in a good economic position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and teching. Since the opponent is teching, you can safely expand and prepare a counter. Maintain scouting to identify their tech.

Your economic lead allows you to tech up and prepare a counter. The opponent's tech investment means they may be vulnerable early.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a mid-game with a tech advantage and a counter to the opponent's composition.
