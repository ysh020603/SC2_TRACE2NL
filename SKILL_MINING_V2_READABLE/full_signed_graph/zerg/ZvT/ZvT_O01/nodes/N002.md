## Node Type

NEGATIVE

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy but expansion is maintained rather than increased. Opponent is on ground posture with Marines and Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Own forces are Zerglings and Queens, light tech. Avoid being too passive and letting the Terran dictate the pace. Do not neglect army production while expanding.

## When This Applies

- Terran is on a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. They may be preparing a timing attack or tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy but are not pushing for additional bases. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, production is moderate, tech is light. You are in a safe but passive position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid being too passive and letting the Terran dictate the pace. Do not neglect army production while expanding.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have secured a new expansion or if the Terran shows signs of aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
