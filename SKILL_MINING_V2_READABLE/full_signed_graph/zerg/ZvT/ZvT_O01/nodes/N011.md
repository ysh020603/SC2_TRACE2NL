# N011 — Late Mid-Game Ground Stagnation

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Late midgame (around 600s) with heavy economy and expansion. Opponent is on ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, Hellions, heavy production, heavy tech. Own forces are Zerglings and Queens, heavy production, heavy tech. Avoid being passive and letting the Terran dictate the pace. Do not neglect upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. They have heavy production and tech, and are likely preparing a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a large ground army of Zerglings and Queens. Production and tech are heavy. You are in a strong position but may be stagnant.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid being passive and letting the Terran dictate the pace. Do not neglect upgrades.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have a critical mass of units or when the Terran moves out.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a composition that can break the Terran's siege lines and win the game.
