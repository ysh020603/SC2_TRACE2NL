# N012 — Mid-Game Ground Vulnerability

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Midgame (around 420s) with heavy economy and expansion. Opponent is on ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, Hellions, heavy production, heavy tech. Own forces are Zerglings, Hydralisks, Queens, moderate production, heavy tech. Avoid over-investing in tech at the expense of army size. Do not neglect scouting to anticipate the attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Terran has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. They have heavy production and tech, and are likely preparing a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with a mixed ground army including Hydralisks. Production is moderate, tech is heavy. You may lack army size.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-investing in tech at the expense of army size. Do not neglect scouting to anticipate the attack.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck when you have a sufficient army to defend or when the Terran attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a sufficient army to defend while maintaining your tech advantage.
