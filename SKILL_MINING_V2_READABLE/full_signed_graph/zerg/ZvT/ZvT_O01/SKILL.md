# Economy / Expansion / Ground

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: ZvT_O01
- Matchup: Zerg vs Terran
- Opening Family: economy / expansion / ground opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Zerg opening focused on heavy economy and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. Early game emphasizes Zerglings and Queens, with moderate production and light technology investment. The strategy aims to outgrow the opponent economically and transition into a strong mid-game ground force.

Develop a robust economy and expansion lead while preserving flexibility to adapt to Terran's tech or pressure. Use map control and creep spread to secure bases and prepare for a tech switch if needed.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent's tech choices.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: light_or_uncertain
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [POSITIVE] N001 — Early Game Expansion and Ground Defense

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180s) with both players on heavy economy and expansion. Opponent shows ground posture with Marines and Reapers, moderate production, heavy tech. Own forces are Zerglings and Queens, light tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue expanding and increasing economy while strengthening ground forces. Maintain production and continue tech development. Keep an eye on defense but prioritize macro.
