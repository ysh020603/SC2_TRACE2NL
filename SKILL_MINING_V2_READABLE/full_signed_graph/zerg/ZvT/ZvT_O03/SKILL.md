## Opening Strategy

This opening emphasizes a heavy economy with moderate production and technology, aiming for a broad ground-oriented posture while keeping flexibility for adaptation based on live observations.

Develop a robust economy and technology base while expanding, maintaining a flexible ground-oriented army that can respond to Terran pressure or tech switches.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on scouting and enemy actions.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: moderate
- Army direction: ground-leaning

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
