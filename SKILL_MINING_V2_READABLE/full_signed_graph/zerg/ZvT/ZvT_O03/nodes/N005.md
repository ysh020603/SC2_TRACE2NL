## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and are expanding. The opponent's posture is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and production, indicating a macro-oriented start. Continue expanding and developing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture with Queens and Zerglings, and scout to determine the opponent's plan.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture, but they have a heavy economy and production. Their tech is heavy, and they may be going for a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, moderate production, and moderate tech. Your army is ground-based with Queens, and you are expanding. Defense is light, but you have potential for pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue expanding and developing your economy. Maintain a defensive posture with Queens and Zerglings, and scout to determine the opponent's plan.

This opening aims to outscale the opponent economically. By focusing on economy and expansion, you can gain an advantage in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger ground army or tech as the game progresses, based on scouting.
