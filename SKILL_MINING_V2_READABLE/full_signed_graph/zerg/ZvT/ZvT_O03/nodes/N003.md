# N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Mutalisks. The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Thors, indicating a strong defensive position. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more tech units or upgrades to break through the opponent's defenses.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Thors. They have a heavy economy and production, and are likely well entrenched.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, and you are well defended.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more tech units or upgrades to break through the opponent's defenses.

The opponent has a strong defensive position, so you need to tech into units that can handle Thors and Siege Tanks, such as Brood Lords or Vipers, or use harassment to pull them apart.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a late-game composition with Brood Lords, Vipers, or other high-tech units to break the opponent's defense.
