# N008 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Mutalisks. The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Battlecruisers, indicating a potential air threat. Increase your air presence and tech to counter the opponent's Battlecruisers. Consider adding Corruptors or other anti-air units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Battlecruisers. They have a heavy economy and production, and may be transitioning to air.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens, and you are well defended.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and tech to counter the opponent's Battlecruisers. Consider adding Corruptors or other anti-air units.

Battlecruisers are a significant threat, so you need to tech into anti-air units to deal with them. By increasing your air presence, you can also harass the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a composition with Corruptors or other anti-air units to counter Battlecruisers, while maintaining a strong ground army.
