# N006 — Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 7 minutes, you have a heavy economy and moderate production, with a ground army of Zerglings and Queens. The opponent has a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs, indicating a potential timing attack. Increase your technology to prepare for the opponent's potential push. Consider adding Roaches or other tech units to counter their composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, and Medivacs. They have a heavy economy and production, and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, moderate production, and heavy tech. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you are moderately defended.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your technology to prepare for the opponent's potential push. Consider adding Roaches or other tech units to counter their composition.

The opponent has a mix of units that can be strong in a timing attack. By investing in tech, you can get units that are more cost-effective in engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a tech-heavy ground army (e.g., Roach/Ravager) to defend and counter.
