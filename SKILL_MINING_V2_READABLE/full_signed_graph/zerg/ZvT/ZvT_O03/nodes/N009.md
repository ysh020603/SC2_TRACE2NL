## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and moderate expansion. The opponent's posture is unknown, but they have a Reaper, indicating early harassment. Maintain your economy and expansion, and prepare to defend against Reaper harassment. Consider adding a Queen or Zerglings to deal with the Reaper.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture, but they have a Reaper. They have a moderate production and heavy tech, and may be planning early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, moderate production, and moderate tech. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you are moderately expanded.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your economy and expansion, and prepare to defend against Reaper harassment. Consider adding a Queen or Zerglings to deal with the Reaper.

Reapers can be annoying, but they are not a major threat. By maintaining your economy, you can outscale the opponent in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a stronger ground army or tech as the game progresses, based on scouting.
