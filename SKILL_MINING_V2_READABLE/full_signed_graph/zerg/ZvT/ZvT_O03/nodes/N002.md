# N002 — Midgame Ground Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 5 minutes, you have a strong economy and are expanding further. The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines, indicating a potential push or defensive posture. Increase your production and tech to prepare for a potential midgame fight. Consider adding Roaches or other tech units to break through Siege Tank lines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marines. They have a heavy economy and production, and are likely expanding. Their tech is heavy, and they may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, moderate production, and moderate tech. Your army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens, and you are expanding. Defense is light, but you have potential for pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and tech to prepare for a potential midgame fight. Consider adding Roaches or other tech units to break through Siege Tank lines.

Siege Tanks are strong defensively, so you need to tech into units that can engage them effectively, such as Roaches with upgrades or Mutalisks for harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a tech-heavy ground army (e.g., Roach/Ravager) or consider air if the opponent is immobile.
