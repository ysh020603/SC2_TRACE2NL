## Opening Strategy

This opening focuses on building a strong economy and teching up while expanding, with a ground-oriented army. It is flexible and adapts based on scouting information.

Develop a robust economy and tech infrastructure while maintaining map presence and flexibility to respond to opponent's actions.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live scouting and opponent's posture.
