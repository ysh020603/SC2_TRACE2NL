# N006 — Midgame Tech Investment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, invest in tech to improve your army's effectiveness. Increase tech and production, maintain army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase tech and production, maintain army.

Tech upgrades like attack and armor are essential to trade efficiently.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a tech advantage to overpower the opponent.
