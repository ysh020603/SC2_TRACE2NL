# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on economy and tech while maintaining a light defense. Continue developing economy and tech, maintain production, and strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is likely Terran with unknown posture, possibly teching up. They have moderate production and heavy tech investment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light tech. Your army is ground-oriented with Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and tech, maintain production, and strengthen ground forces.

Establish a strong economic foundation to support future tech and army production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you can support a larger army and tech upgrades.
