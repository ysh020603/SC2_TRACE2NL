# N007 — Early Game Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, consider expanding to secure a lead. Maintain economy and production, strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Marines, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, moderate expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain economy and production, strengthen ground forces.

Expanding early gives a long-term economic advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base and maintain economy.
