# N010 — Late Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, focus on defending and maintaining a strong army. Maintain production and economy, strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Thors, and Ravens, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain production and economy, strengthen ground forces.

A strong defense is crucial to withstand Terran's late-game pushes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Hold your bases and build a counter-attacking force.
