# N009 — Midgame Ground Army and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, focus on building a strong ground army and tech. Maintain production and economy, continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Marines, Hellions, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy production, moderate tech. Ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain production and economy, continue tech development.

A balanced army and tech are needed to handle Terran's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong army to pressure the opponent.
