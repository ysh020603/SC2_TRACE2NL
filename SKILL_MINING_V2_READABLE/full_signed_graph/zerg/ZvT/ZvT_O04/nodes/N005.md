# N005 — Late Midgame Tech and Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, you have a strong economy and can invest in tech and army. Continue increasing tech and production, maintain army strength.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue increasing tech and production, maintain army strength.

Tech upgrades and a diverse army are crucial to counter Terran's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a powerful army composition to challenge the opponent.
