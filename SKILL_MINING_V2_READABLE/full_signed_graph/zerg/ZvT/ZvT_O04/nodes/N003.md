# N003 — Expansion and Production Increase

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

When you have a stable economy, consider expanding further and increasing production. Increase production and expansion, continue tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Marines, Reapers, and Hellions, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, moderate production, and light tech. Ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and expansion, continue tech development.

More bases and production allow for a larger army and faster tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong production capacity to support a larger army.
