# N012 — Late Midgame Air Superiority

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, leverage your air advantage. Increase air presence and tech, maintain production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and tech, maintain production.

Air superiority allows for effective harassment and map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Use air units to gain an advantage and pressure the opponent.
