# N011 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

When you have a strong economy, consider transitioning to air units. Increase air presence and tech, maintain production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground posture with Marines, Ghosts, Hellions, and Medivacs, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and expansion, heavy production and tech. Ground army with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Queens, and Overseers.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and tech, maintain production.

Air units like Mutalisks can harass and provide map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong air force to complement your ground army.
