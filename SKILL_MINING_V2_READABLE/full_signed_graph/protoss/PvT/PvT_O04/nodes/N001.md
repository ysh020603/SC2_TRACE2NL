# N001 — Early Tech-Defense Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy technology investment and defensive posture, but production is still moderate and army composition is unclear. Opponent shows a ground-leaning posture with possible early pressure (Reaper). Continue teching and strengthen ground forces while increasing defenses. Maintain economy growth and stabilize before expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible early pressure, indicated by Reaper sightings. Their economy is heavy, but production is moderate and defense is light or uncertain, suggesting they may be greedy or teching themselves.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and you are investing heavily in technology, but your production is only moderate and your army composition is not yet defined. Defenses are heavy, but expansion is light or uncertain, suggesting you are prioritizing tech over map presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue teching and strengthen ground forces while increasing defenses. Maintain economy growth and stabilize before expanding further.

With heavy tech investment and moderate production, you are vulnerable to early pressure. Strengthening ground forces and defenses mitigates this risk while your tech advantage develops.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting, while maintaining a solid defensive and economic base.
