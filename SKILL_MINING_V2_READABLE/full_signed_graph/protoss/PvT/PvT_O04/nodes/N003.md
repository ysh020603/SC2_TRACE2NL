# N003 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300-360s) with heavy production and technology on both sides. Opponent shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, indicating possible early pressure. Your own army is ground-oriented with heavy production. Continue developing your ground army and production. Maintain your defenses and economy, and be prepared to defend against potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers, indicating possible early pressure. Their production is heavy, and they have a strong economy, suggesting they may be preparing for a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. Your defenses are heavy, and you are investing heavily in technology. Your expansion is moderate, suggesting you are focusing on solidifying your position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and production. Maintain your defenses and economy, and be prepared to defend against potential early pressure.

With heavy production and tech, you are in a good position to defend and then counter-attack. Maintaining a strong defensive posture while developing your army will allow you to weather any early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting, while maintaining a solid defensive and economic base.
