# PvT_O01 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy economy and technology investment while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented posture. The opening is designed to develop a strong macro foundation with moderate production, allowing adaptation to the opponent's observed posture.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240-300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production. Your army is ground-oriented, possibly with Sentry or Stalker. The opponent shows a ground posture with possible Reaper presence, heavy production and technology.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your expansion and increase production. Keep a safety check for potential pressure.

**Read for details:** `N001`
