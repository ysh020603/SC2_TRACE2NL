# N003 — Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 420-540 seconds, both sides show light or uncertain postures, with no reliable combat-unit cues from the opponent. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and keep production increasing.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliably grounded combat-unit cue has been observed. Production and technology appear light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology. Economy and expansion are light or uncertain, and you have no reliable combat-unit cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and keep production increasing.

With no clear threat detected, it is safe to focus on economic and technological growth. Strengthening ground forces prepares for potential midgame pressure while maintaining flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and technology base, ready to transition into a more defined army composition once opponent intentions become clearer.
