# N008 — Early Midgame Defensive Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, the opponent shows a ground posture with Marine cues, heavy economy and expansion, and moderate production. Increase defensive structures and continue developing your economy and technology. Maintain production and strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Marine. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and heavy technology investment. Economy is moderate, expansion is moderate, and defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defensive structures and continue developing your economy and technology. Maintain production and strengthen ground forces.

The opponent's heavy economy and expansion, combined with Marine cues, suggest a potential economic lead or early pressure. Strengthening defense helps stabilize while you continue your own development.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your defense and economy, ready to transition into a more proactive strategy once you have a clearer picture of the opponent's intentions.
