# N011 — Early Midgame Defensive Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, the opponent shows an unknown posture with no reliable combat-unit cues, and light or uncertain production and technology. Increase defensive structures and continue developing your economy and technology. Maintain production and strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliably grounded combat-unit cue has been observed. Production appears light or uncertain, technology investment appears light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Economy is moderate, expansion is moderate, and defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defensive structures and continue developing your economy and technology. Maintain production and strengthen ground forces.

With no clear threat detected, it is safe to focus on economic and technological growth. Strengthening defense helps stabilize while you continue your own development.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your defense and economy, ready to transition into a more proactive strategy once you have a clearer picture of the opponent's intentions.
