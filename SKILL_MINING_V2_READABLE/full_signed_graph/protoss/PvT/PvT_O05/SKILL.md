# PvT_O05 Technology / Economy / Expansion

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Terran
- Opening Family: technology / economy / expansion opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while expanding, with moderate production early on. The strategy is flexible, allowing for adaptation based on scouting information.

Develop a technology / economy / expansion posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Tech/Eco Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Early game (around 180s) with both players investing heavily in economy and technology. Opponent's posture is unclear, possibly with a Reaper scout.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Increase production slightly to prepare for potential threats.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

**Trigger situation:**  
Early midgame (around 240-360s) where both players have established a ground-based army. Opponent shows a ground macro posture with Marines.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase production and technology. Maintain your expansion and keep an eye on defense.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army with Colossus

**Trigger situation:**  
Midgame (around 540s) where both players have heavy economies and production. Opponent has a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army, focusing on Colossus and support units. Increase production and technology further. Maintain your expansion and consider adding more defensive structures.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [DEFAULT] N004 — Late Midgame Ground Army with High Templar

**Trigger situation:**  
Late midgame (around 600-720s) where both players have heavy economies and production. Opponent maintains a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army, adding High Templar for Psionic Storm. Increase production and technology further. Maintain your expansion and consider adding more defensive structures.

**Read for details:** `N004`
