## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with both players investing heavily in economy and technology. Opponent's posture is unclear, possibly with a Reaper scout. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Increase production slightly to prepare for potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is also heavy on economy and technology, with moderate production. Their army composition is unknown, but a Reaper may have been spotted. Defense is light, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology investment, with moderate production. Your army is light or uncertain, possibly with a Zealot. Defense is light, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a light defense. Increase production slightly to prepare for potential threats.

Both players are in a macro-oriented phase. Investing in tech and economy now will pay off later. Maintaining a light defense is sufficient as no major pressure is expected.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition as the game progresses, while continuing to expand and tech up.
