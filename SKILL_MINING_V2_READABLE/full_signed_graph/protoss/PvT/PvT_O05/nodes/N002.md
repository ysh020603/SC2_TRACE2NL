# N002 — Early Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 240-360s) where both players have established a ground-based army. Opponent shows a ground macro posture with Marines. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase production and technology. Maintain your expansion and keep an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with heavy production. Their army is ground-based, with Marines and possibly Reapers. Defense is moderate, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate to heavy production. Your army is ground-based, possibly with Stalkers and Zealots. Defense is light, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to increase production and technology. Maintain your expansion and keep an eye on defense.

The opponent is likely to push with a ground army. Building a strong ground force will help you hold or counter. Your tech advantage can be leveraged with units like Stalkers and Sentries.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more robust ground composition, possibly including Colossus or Immortals, while maintaining tech and economy.
