DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 600-720s) where both players have heavy economies and production. Opponent maintains a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs. Continue strengthening your ground army, adding High Templar for Psionic Storm. Increase production and technology further. Maintain your expansion and consider adding more defensive structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy economy and technology, with heavy production. Their army is ground-based, with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs. Defense is heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with heavy production. Your army is ground-based, including Colossus, Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and possibly High Templar. Defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, adding High Templar for Psionic Storm. Increase production and technology further. Maintain your expansion and consider adding more defensive structures.

High Templar provide powerful area damage against the opponent's bio army. Your tech advantage allows for a versatile composition. Maintaining a strong economy will support a larger army.
