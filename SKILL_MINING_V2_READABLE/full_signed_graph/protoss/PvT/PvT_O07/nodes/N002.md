# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology, but your army composition is unknown. Enemy is also unknown but may have Marines and Reapers. Focus on developing your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the enemy's plan. Prepare to transition into a ground or air composition based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy's posture is unknown, but they may have Marines and Reapers. Their production is moderate, and they have heavy technology, suggesting a possible tech-oriented build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army is not yet defined. Your expansion is light or uncertain, so you need to focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the enemy's plan. Prepare to transition into a ground or air composition based on scouting.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech lead. By scouting, you can adapt your build to counter the enemy's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a defined army composition by the midgame, with a solid economy and tech base.
