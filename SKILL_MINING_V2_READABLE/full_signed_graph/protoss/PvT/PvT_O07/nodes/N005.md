## Summary

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground-based army with Marines and Marauders, suggesting a bio build. Their production is moderate, and they have heavy technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army is not yet defined. Your expansion is heavy, so you are in a good economic position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology, while scouting to determine the enemy's exact build. Prepare to transition into a ground or air composition based on scouting.

Your heavy economy and tech give you flexibility. By scouting, you can choose the most effective counter to the enemy's bio army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a composition that can handle bio, such as Colossi or Storm, while maintaining a strong economy.
