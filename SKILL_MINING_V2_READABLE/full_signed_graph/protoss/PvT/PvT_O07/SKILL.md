## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while maintaining moderate production, aiming for a flexible midgame transition based on scouting.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Mid Transition with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army and heavy technology investment. Enemy is likely ground-based with heavy production and tech, possibly with Marines and Widow Mines.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology, strengthen your ground army, and maintain defense while scouting for enemy intentions.

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Macro

**Trigger situation:**

Continue strengthening your ground army and economy, while adding defensive structures if needed. Consider transitioning to air if the enemy's ground is too strong.
