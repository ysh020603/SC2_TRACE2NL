# PvT_O09 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvT_O09

- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while maintaining moderate production, aiming for a flexible midgame transition based on scouting.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 7 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army and heavy technology. Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, possibly with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing production and technology. Maintain your economy and expansion, but add defensive structures or units if you suspect an imminent attack.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 4-5 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, possibly with Marines and Reapers.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production and technology. Consider adding an expansion if you feel safe, but keep some units for defense.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Air Potential

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 10 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army that may include Void Rays. Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, possibly with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while maintaining your economy and production. Consider adding more air units if the enemy lacks anti-air, or transition to a more powerful ground composition.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [DEFAULT] N004 — Early Game Tech Development

**Trigger situation:**  
Around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production. Enemy is likely on an unknown posture, possibly with Marauders or Reapers, and heavy technology.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your technology and economy while increasing production. Consider scouting to determine the enemy's tech path.

**Read for details:** `N004`
