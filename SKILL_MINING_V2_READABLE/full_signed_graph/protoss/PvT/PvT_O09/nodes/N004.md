# N004 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, you have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production. Enemy is likely on an unknown posture, possibly with Marauders or Reapers, and heavy technology. Continue developing your technology and economy while increasing production. Consider scouting to determine the enemy's tech path.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Marauders or Reapers. Their defense is light or uncertain, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production. Your army is unknown-oriented, possibly with Zealots. Your defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be careful about early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and economy while increasing production. Consider scouting to determine the enemy's tech path.

Your tech advantage can be leveraged to get key units like Stalkers or Sentries. Increasing production ensures you can defend against early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a solid early-midgame army composition, possibly with Stalkers and Sentries, based on scouting.
