# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 4-5 minutes, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army. Enemy is likely on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, possibly with Marines and Reapers. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production and technology. Consider adding an expansion if you feel safe, but keep some units for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues may include Marines and Reapers. Their defense is moderate, and they are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Zealots and Stalkers. Your defense is light or uncertain, and your expansion is light or uncertain, so you need to balance expansion with safety.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production and technology. Consider adding an expansion if you feel safe, but keep some units for defense.

Your tech advantage can be used to get units like Sentries or Observers for better engagements. Increasing production ensures you can defend against potential pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a solid midgame army composition, possibly adding Immortals or Colossi depending on the enemy composition.
