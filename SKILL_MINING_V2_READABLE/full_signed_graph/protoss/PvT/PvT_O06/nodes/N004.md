# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, the opponent still has a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Medivacs, while you have Zealots, Stalkers, High Templars, and Sentries. Both are heavily invested in economy and technology. Continue developing your ground army and consider adding Colossi or Archons for additional damage and tanking. Maintain your economy and production to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Medivac support, indicating a strong bio composition. They have heavy defense and economy, suggesting they are preparing for a late-game push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with High Templars for spellcasting, heavy economy, and production. Your defense is moderate, so you can hold against most attacks.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and consider adding Colossi or Archons for additional damage and tanking. Maintain your economy and production to support a larger army.

High Templars provide powerful storm damage against bio, but you need additional units to deal with the sheer volume. Colossi or Archons can complement your composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-game army with a mix of ground and possibly air units, and ensure you have enough production to remax after engagements.
