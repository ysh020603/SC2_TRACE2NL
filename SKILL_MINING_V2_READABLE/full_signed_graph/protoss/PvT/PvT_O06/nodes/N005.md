# N005 — Early Game Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the opponent has a Marine, indicating a ground posture, while you have no combat units yet. Both are focusing on economy and technology. Start producing units to defend against potential early aggression while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Strengthen your ground army as you transition to the midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a Marine, suggesting a ground-based opening. They have heavy economy and technology, indicating a macro-oriented approach, but they may still apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment, but no army yet. Your defense is light, so you need to prepare for potential early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Start producing units to defend against potential early aggression while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Strengthen your ground army as you transition to the midgame.

A Marine can be used for early pressure, so having a few units to defend is crucial. Balancing macro with minimal defense will allow you to reach your tech goals safely.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state where you have a basic army composition and scouting information to make more informed decisions.
