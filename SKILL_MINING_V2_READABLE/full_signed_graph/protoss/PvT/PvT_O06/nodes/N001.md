# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 6 minutes, the opponent is likely to have a ground-based army with Marines and Reapers, while you have a Stalker-heavy ground force. Both sides are investing heavily in economy and technology. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and production, but be ready to defend against possible aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a ground macro posture with Marines and Reapers, heavy economy and production, and moderate defense. They may be preparing for pressure or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army including Stalkers. Your defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be cautious about potential pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain your current expansion and production, but be ready to defend against possible aggression.

Your heavy economy and technology investment will pay off in the midgame. By maintaining a strong ground force, you can deter early pressure and set up for a powerful transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid midgame army composition, possibly adding Sentries or Immortals for defense, and consider scouting for the opponent's tech choices.
