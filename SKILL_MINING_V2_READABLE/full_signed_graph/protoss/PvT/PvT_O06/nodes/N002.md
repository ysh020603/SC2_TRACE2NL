# N002 — Early Game Tech/Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the opponent is unknown but may have a Reaper, while you have no combat units yet. Both are focusing on economy and technology. Continue developing your economy and technology, but start producing units to defend against potential early pressure. Strengthen your ground army as you transition to the midgame.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is also in the early game, with an unknown army composition but possibly a Reaper. They have heavy economy and technology, suggesting a macro-oriented approach.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and technology investment, but your army is not yet formed. Your defense is light, so you need to be careful about early harassment.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, but start producing units to defend against potential early pressure. Strengthen your ground army as you transition to the midgame.

A strong economy and tech lead will give you an advantage later, but you need some units to survive early aggression. Balancing macro with minimal defense is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a state where you have a basic army composition and scouting information to make more informed decisions.
