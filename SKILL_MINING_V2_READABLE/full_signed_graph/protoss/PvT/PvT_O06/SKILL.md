## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy while maintaining flexible production. The early game focuses on developing a strong economic and technological base, with production ramping up as the game progresses. The army composition is initially unclear but tends toward ground-based units, with potential transitions to air later.

Develop a robust economy and technology infrastructure while maintaining flexibility to adapt to the opponent's actions. Aim to reach a strong midgame position with a versatile army that can respond to Terran ground or air threats.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations and opponent actions.
