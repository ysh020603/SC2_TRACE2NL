# PvZ_O05 Technology / Defense / Economy

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O05
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: Technology / Defense / Economy
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that prioritizes heavy technology and defense while maintaining a moderate economy and production. It aims to develop a strong tech base and defensive posture, with flexibility to transition into either ground or air compositions based on scouting.

Develop a technology / defense / economy posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: moderate
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.
