# N009 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture air-oriented with heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with moderate defense. Increase air army, strengthen air, maintain defense and economy, continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly air-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air army, strengthen air, maintain defense and economy, continue production and technology.

Transitioning to air early can catch opponent off guard, as they have light tech. Oracles can harass and provide scouting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air force to gain map control and harass.
