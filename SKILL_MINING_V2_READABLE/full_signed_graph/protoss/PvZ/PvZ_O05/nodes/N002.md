# N002 — Proactive Tech and Production Expansion

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown-oriented with heavy tech and defense, opponent ground macro with possible pressure. Positive node indicates a more aggressive development path. Increase production and technology, strengthen ground army, maintain defense and expansion, increase economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is light or uncertain. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and technology, strengthen ground army, maintain defense and expansion, increase economy.

With opponent not showing heavy pressure, it is safe to invest more in production and tech to gain an advantage. Maintaining defense prevents surprises.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a stronger tech and production base to support a midgame transition, either to a ground push or air switch.
