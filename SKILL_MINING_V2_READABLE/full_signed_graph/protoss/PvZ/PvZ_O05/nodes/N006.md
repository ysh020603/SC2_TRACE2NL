# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with moderate defense. Strengthen ground army, increase production and technology, maintain defense and expansion, increase economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase production and technology, maintain defense and expansion, increase economy.

With opponent's defense moderate, there is an opportunity to apply pressure or expand safely. Increasing production and tech supports a midgame push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army to take map control or prepare for a timing attack.
