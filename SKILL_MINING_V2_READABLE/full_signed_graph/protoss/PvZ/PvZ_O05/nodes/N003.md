# N003 — Early-Midgame Tech and Defense Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture unknown-oriented with heavy tech and defense, opponent ground macro with moderate defense and possible pressure. Strengthen ground army, increase defense and economy, continue production, increase technology, maintain air and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase defense and economy, continue production, increase technology, maintain air and expansion.

As the game progresses, opponent's defense is moderate, indicating potential for aggression. Maintaining heavy defense while developing economy and tech is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Consolidate a strong defensive and economic position, then transition to a midgame army composition.
