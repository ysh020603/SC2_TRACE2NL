# N008 — Late-Midgame Air Army Sustain

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own posture air-oriented with heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with heavy production and moderate tech. Increase air army, strengthen air, continue defense and economy, increase technology and upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears moderate, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your completed or developing posture is broadly air-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. Defense is heavy, economy is heavy, expansion is heavy. Check live Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and army strength before choosing exact actions.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air army, strengthen air, continue defense and economy, increase technology and upgrades.

Opponent's tech is moderate, so air superiority can be maintained. Continuing to build air and tech will pressure the opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain air dominance and prepare for a decisive engagement.
