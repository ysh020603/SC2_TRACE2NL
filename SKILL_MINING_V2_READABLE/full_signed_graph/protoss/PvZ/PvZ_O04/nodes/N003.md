# N003 — Mid-Game Ground Army with Heavy Production

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech, opponent ground-macro with Zerglings and Queens. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase production, and maintain tech advantage. Consider adding Sentries for defensive utility.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and heavy economy/expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase production, and maintain tech advantage. Consider adding Sentries for defensive utility.

Your tech and production advantage should allow you to out-macro the opponent, but you need to be ready for potential ling run-bys or baneling busts.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground composition with Immortals or Colossus, while maintaining economy.
