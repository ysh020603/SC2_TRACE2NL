# N011 — Late-Midgame Air Army with Phoenix

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own air army with Phoenix, heavy production and tech, opponent ground-macro with Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens. Continue strengthening air forces, increase production and tech, and consider adding Void Rays or Carriers for late game.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings, Mutalisks, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air army with Phoenix, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries, heavy production and tech, and heavy economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening air forces, increase production and tech, and consider adding Void Rays or Carriers for late game.

Phoenix can control the air and harass, but you need to be careful of corruptors and hydras.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger air composition with Void Rays or Carriers, while maintaining ground support.
