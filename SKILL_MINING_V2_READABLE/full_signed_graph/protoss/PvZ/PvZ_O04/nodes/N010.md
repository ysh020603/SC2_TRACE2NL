# N010 — Midgame Ground Army with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground army with Zealots, heavy defense, heavy production and tech, opponent ground-macro with Zerglings and Queens. Stabilize your defense, then continue developing ground forces and tech. Consider adding Stalkers and Sentries for a more balanced army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, moderate tech, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy defense, heavy production and tech, and heavy economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your defense, then continue developing ground forces and tech. Consider adding Stalkers and Sentries for a more balanced army.

Heavy defense can hold off early pressure, but you need to transition to a more mobile army to take map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive ground army with Stalkers and Sentries, while expanding.
