## Summary

- Opponent has Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

- You have a ground army with Zealots, heavy defense, moderate production, and heavy tech. Economy is heavy but expansion is uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain defensive posture, continue teching and expanding, and consider adding Stalkers for mobility.

Heavy defense can deter early aggression, but you need to expand to keep up economically.
