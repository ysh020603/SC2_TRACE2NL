# N002 — Early Tech Focus with Unknown Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army composition unknown but heavy tech, opponent ground-macro with light tech. Maintain flexibility, continue teching and expanding, but start scouting to determine your army direction.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent appears ground-macro with Queens, moderate production, light tech, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is unknown, but you have heavy technology and moderate production. Economy is heavy and expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain flexibility, continue teching and expanding, but start scouting to determine your army direction.

With unknown army, you need to gather information to decide whether to commit to ground, air, or mixed.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a clear army direction based on scouting, while maintaining tech and economy lead.
