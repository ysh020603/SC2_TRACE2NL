DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech, opponent ground-macro with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain production and tech, and consider adding more splash damage (Colossus or Archons) to deal with roach/ling.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, heavy production and tech, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and heavy economy/defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, maintain production and tech, and consider adding more splash damage (Colossus or Archons) to deal with roach/ling.

Your tech advantage allows for powerful compositions, but you need to be careful of flanking and baneling drops.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
