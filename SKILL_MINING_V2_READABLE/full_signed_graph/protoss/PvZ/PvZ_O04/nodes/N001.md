# N001 — Early Ground Commitment with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game, own ground-oriented posture with heavy technology, opponent appears ground-macro with light tech and heavy economy. Avoid over-committing to tech without sufficient army to defend against potential early ling floods or timing attacks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Queens, moderate production, light or uncertain tech, and heavy economy/expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, moderate production, and heavy technology investment. Your economy is heavy but expansion is uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-committing to tech without sufficient army to defend against potential early ling floods or timing attacks.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Recheck if opponent shows signs of air transition or heavy pressure; adjust accordingly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid ground army with Stalkers and Sentries, while continuing to expand and tech up.
