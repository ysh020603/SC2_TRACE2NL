# PvZ_O01 Technology / Economy / Upgrade

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvZ_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / upgrade opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy development with moderate production, aiming for a flexible midgame transition based on scouting.

Develop a technology / economy / upgrade posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation

**Trigger situation:**  
At the start of the game, focus on establishing a strong economy and technology base while keeping options open.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production to a moderate level. Maintain a defensive posture and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Commitment

**Trigger situation:**  
After the early game, you have a heavy economy and technology, and you are starting to produce ground units like Stalkers and Sentries.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and technology development, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is expected.

**Read for details:** `N002`

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
In the midgame, you have a heavy economy and production, and your ground army is expanding with Stalkers, Sentries, and support units like Warp Prisms and Observers.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and maintaining your economy. Consider adding tech units like Immortals or Colossi to counter Roaches, and use Observers for vision.
