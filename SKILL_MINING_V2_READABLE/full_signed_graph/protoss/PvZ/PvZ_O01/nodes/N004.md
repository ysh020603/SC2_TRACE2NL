## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, you have a heavy economy and production, and your army is a mix of ground and air units like Zealots, Void Rays, Observers, and Immortals. Continue strengthening your army and maintaining your economy. Consider adding more air units or tech to counter the opponent's composition, and ensure you have detection.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, and heavy production and technology. They are expanding heavily and may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, Void Rays, Observers, and Immortals, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your army and maintaining your economy. Consider adding more air units or tech to counter the opponent's composition, and ensure you have detection.

A mix of ground and air units provides flexibility against Zerg's ground-based army, and your heavy economy supports a diverse composition.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
