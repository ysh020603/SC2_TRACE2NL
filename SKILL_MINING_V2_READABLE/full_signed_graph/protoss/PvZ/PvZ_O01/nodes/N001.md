# N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, focus on establishing a strong economy and technology base while keeping options open. Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production to a moderate level. Maintain a defensive posture and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be following a ground macro posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. They are likely expanding heavily and may apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology investment is heavy, but production is only moderate and your army composition is not yet defined. You have not committed to a specific army style.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production to a moderate level. Maintain a defensive posture and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

A heavy economy and technology investment will pay off in the midgame, but you need enough production to defend against early pressure and to transition smoothly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a solid economy, moderate production, and a clear direction for your army (ground or air) based on scouting.

## Possible Next Situations

**Situation:** After the early game, you have a heavy economy and technology, and you are starting to produce ground units like Stalkers and Sentries.

**Direction:** Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and technology development, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is expected.
