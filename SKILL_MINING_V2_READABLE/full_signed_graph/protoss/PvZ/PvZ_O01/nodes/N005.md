# N005 — Early Game Tech/Economy Alternative

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

An alternative early game path where you focus on economy and technology with moderate production, but with a different expansion timing. Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Maintain a defensive posture and be ready to adapt based on scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a ground macro posture with no reliable combat unit cues, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology is heavy, but production is moderate and your army is not yet defined. You have a heavy expansion posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Maintain a defensive posture and be ready to adapt based on scouting.

This path emphasizes economy and tech, but you need to ensure you have enough production to defend against early pressure.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a solid economy, moderate production, and a clear direction for your army.
