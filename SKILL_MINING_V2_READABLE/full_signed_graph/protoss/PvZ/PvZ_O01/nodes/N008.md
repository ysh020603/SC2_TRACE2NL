# N008 — Early-Midgame Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, you have an air-oriented army with Oracles, focusing on harassment and map control. Continue strengthening your air army and increasing your air presence. Use Oracles for harassment and scouting, and maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground macro posture with Queens and Overseers, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have an air army with Zealots and Oracles, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and increasing your air presence. Use Oracles for harassment and scouting, and maintain your economy.

Oracles can provide early harassment and vision, while your heavy economy supports a tech-heavy air composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army and prepare for the midgame.
