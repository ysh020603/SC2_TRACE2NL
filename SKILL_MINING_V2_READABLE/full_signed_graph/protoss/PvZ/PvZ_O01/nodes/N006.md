# N006 — Early-Midgame Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, you have a ground army with Stalkers and a moderate defense posture, while the opponent is still on a ground macro posture. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintaining your defense. Consider adding Sentries for force fields and scouting for tech switches.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground macro posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have a ground army with Stalkers, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintaining your defense. Consider adding Sentries for force fields and scouting for tech switches.

A solid ground army with Stalkers and Sentries can defend against early Zergling pressure and transition into a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and prepare for the midgame.
