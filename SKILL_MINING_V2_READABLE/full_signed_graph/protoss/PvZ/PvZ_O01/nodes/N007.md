# N007 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, you have transitioned to an air-oriented army with Phoenixes and other air units, while the opponent remains on a ground macro posture. Continue strengthening your air army and increasing your air presence. Consider adding Oracles for harassment and scouting, and maintain your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground macro posture with Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers, moderate production, and moderate technology. They are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have an air-oriented army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Phoenixes, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and increasing your air presence. Consider adding Oracles for harassment and scouting, and maintain your economy.

An air army with Phoenixes can harass Zerg workers and provide map control, while your heavy economy supports a tech-heavy composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army and prepare for the late game.
