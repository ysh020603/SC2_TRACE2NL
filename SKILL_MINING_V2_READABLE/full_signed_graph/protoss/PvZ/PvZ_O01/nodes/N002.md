# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Commitment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

After the early game, you have a heavy economy and technology, and you are starting to produce ground units like Stalkers and Sentries. Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and technology development, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is expected.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent still appears to be on a ground macro posture with Zergling and Queen cues, moderate production, and light or uncertain technology. They are expanding heavily and may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have a ground-oriented army with Stalkers and Sentries, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and increasing production. Maintain your economy and technology development, and consider adding defensive structures if pressure is expected.

A ground army with Stalkers and Sentries provides a solid core that can transition into various compositions, and your heavy economy supports a strong midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and prepare for the midgame, either by adding more tech (e.g., Immortals) or transitioning to air if needed.
