# N007 — Early-Mid Game: Ground vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, both you and the opponent have ground armies. You have Zealots and Stalkers, while the opponent has Zerglings and Queens. Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground army with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and heavy economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Zealots and Stalkers, moderate production, and heavy technology.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop your economy and technology.

With both sides on the ground, you need to maintain a strong army to contest the map and prepare for future tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong ground army and economy to support a transition to a more advanced composition.
