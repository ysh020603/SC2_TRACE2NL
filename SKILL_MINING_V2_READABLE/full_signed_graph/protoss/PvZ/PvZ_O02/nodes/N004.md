# N004 — Late-Midgame: Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent is still unknown, but you have had time to build up your forces. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but start to consider a transition to a more aggressive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture is still unknown, with no confirmed combat units.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are still light or uncertain, but you have a moderate army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology, but start to consider a transition to a more aggressive posture.

With no information, it is important to be prepared for any attack, but also to start thinking about how to win the game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong army and economy that can support a transition to a more aggressive strategy.
