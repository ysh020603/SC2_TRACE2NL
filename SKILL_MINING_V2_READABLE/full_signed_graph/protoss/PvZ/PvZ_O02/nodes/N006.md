# N006 — Early Game: Ground Macro Posture (Expansion Focus)

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, the opponent shows a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, and you have a heavy expansion. Increase your production and continue to develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their economy and expansion are heavy, and they may apply early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy technology investment and a heavy expansion, but light production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and continue to develop your economy and technology.

With a heavy expansion, you need to increase production to defend it and take advantage of your economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong economy and production to support a powerful army.
