# N010 — Late-Midgame: Ground Macro Posture (Heavy)

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, the opponent has a heavy ground macro posture with Queens. You have a strong ground army. Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Queens. Their economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Your economy and technology are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and develop your economy and technology.

With both sides having heavy armies, you need to maintain a strong force and look for opportunities to gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful army and economy to support a transition to a more advanced composition.
