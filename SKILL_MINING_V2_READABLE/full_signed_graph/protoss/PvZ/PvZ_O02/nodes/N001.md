# N001 — Early Game: Unknown Posture

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own development is also uncertain. Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production, technology, and expansion are all light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain a balanced development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping production options open.

With no information, it is safe to develop a solid foundation that can adapt to any opponent strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have more information and can make a more informed decision.
