# N002 — Early-Mid Game: Still Unknown

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

As the game progresses, the opponent remains unknown, but you have had time to develop your economy and technology. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but start to prepare for potential threats.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture is still unknown, with no confirmed combat units.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are still light or uncertain, but you have had time to build infrastructure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology, but start to prepare for potential threats.

Maintaining a balanced approach allows you to respond to whatever the opponent does.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a solid foundation that can support a variety of strategies.
