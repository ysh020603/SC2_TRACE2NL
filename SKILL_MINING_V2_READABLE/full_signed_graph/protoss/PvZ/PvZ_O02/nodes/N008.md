# N008 — Early-Mid Game: Ground Macro Posture (Tech Focus)

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-mid game, the opponent shows a ground macro posture, and you have a heavy technology investment. Increase your production and continue to develop your technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy technology investment and moderate production, but your army is unknown.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your production and continue to develop your technology.

With a heavy technology investment, you need to increase production to field a strong army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong army and economy to support your technology.
