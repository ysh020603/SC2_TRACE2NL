A Protoss opening that emphasizes technology and economy while keeping production flexible. Early game is uncertain, but the plan is to develop a strong tech base and expand safely, adapting to Zerg's ground-oriented macro posture.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.
