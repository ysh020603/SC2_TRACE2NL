# N006 — Early Expansion with Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, expansion posture light, opponent ground macro with light defense. Increase your expansion rate while continuing to develop technology. Strengthen your ground army as a default.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is on a ground macro path with light defense and possible pressure. They have a heavy economy and are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is unknown, but you have heavy tech and a strong economy. Your expansion is light, and production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion rate while continuing to develop technology. Strengthen your ground army as a default.

You need to keep up with the opponent's economy. Expanding now will give you a long-term advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state with a solid economy and a defined army.
