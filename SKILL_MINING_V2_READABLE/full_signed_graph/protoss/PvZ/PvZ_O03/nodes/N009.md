- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy defense and production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Dark Templar and Warp Prisms. Your production and tech are heavy, and you have a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and use Dark Templar for harassment. Maintain your economy and tech.

Dark Templar can be very effective if the opponent lacks detection. Warp Prisms allow for mobile harassment.
