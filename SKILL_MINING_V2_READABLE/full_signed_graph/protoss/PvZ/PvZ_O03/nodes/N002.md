# N002 — Standard Tech/Eco Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army unknown, heavy tech, moderate production, opponent ground macro with light defense, possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Strengthen your ground army as a default, but stay flexible.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is on a ground macro path, with light defense and possible early pressure. They have a heavy economy and are expanding, but their tech is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army is not yet defined, but you have a strong economy and heavy tech investment. Production is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while increasing production. Strengthen your ground army as a default, but stay flexible.

This is a balanced approach that keeps your options open. Increasing production will allow you to respond to threats or transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a state where you have a clear army composition and a solid production/tech base.
