# N005 — Midgame Ground Army with Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, Warp Prisms, heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with heavy defense and production. Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech or upgrades to gain an edge.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. They have heavy defense and production, and their tech is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Stalkers, Sentries, and Warp Prisms. Your production and tech are heavy, and you have a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding tech or upgrades to gain an edge.

The opponent is also strong, so you need to maintain your advantage. Continuing to develop your army and tech will keep you competitive.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition, possibly with additional tech units.
