# N008 — Air Transition with Oracles

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own air army with Oracles, heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with light defense. Continue strengthening your air army and increase production. Use Oracles for harassment and scouting.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent remains on a ground macro path with light defense and possible pressure. Their tech is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have transitioned to an air army with Oracles. Your production and tech are heavy, and you have a strong economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your air army and increase production. Use Oracles for harassment and scouting.

Air units can be difficult for a ground-based opponent to handle. Oracles provide harassment and detection.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army and use it to pressure the opponent.
