# N004 — Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own ground army with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, opponent ground macro with light defense, possible pressure. Continue developing your ground army and economy. Increase production and tech, but maintain safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is still on a ground macro path, with light defense and possible pressure. Their tech is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Stalkers, heavy production and tech, and a strong economy. You are expanding and have a moderate defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy. Increase production and tech, but maintain safety checks.

This is a stable development path that keeps you competitive. You are not committing to a risky strategy, but rather building a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame state with a strong army and economy, ready to transition to a specific strategy.
