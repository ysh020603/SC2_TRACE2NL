# N001 — Early Tech Investment with Unknown Army

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game, own army composition unknown, heavy tech investment, moderate production, opponent appears ground-oriented with light defense and possible early pressure. Avoid over-committing to a specific army composition before you have scouted the opponent's tech path. Also avoid neglecting defense while focusing on economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent appears to be on a ground-oriented macro path, with light defense and a possible early pressure. They have a heavy economy and are expanding, but their technology is light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined; you have invested heavily in technology and have a strong economy, but your production is only moderate. You need to decide how to translate your tech lead into a concrete army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-committing to a specific army composition before you have scouted the opponent's tech path. Also avoid neglecting defense while focusing on economy.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you have scouted the opponent's tech path or when you have a clear army composition in mind.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined ground army composition with a solid production base, while keeping an eye on the opponent's tech choices.
