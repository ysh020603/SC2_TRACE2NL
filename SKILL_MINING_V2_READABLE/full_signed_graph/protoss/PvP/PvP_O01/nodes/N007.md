# N007 — Early-Midgame Transition with Unknown Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame state where opponent's army is unknown, but you have a ground-oriented posture. You are increasing expansion and tech. Continue developing your ground army and tech, but keep scouting to detect the opponent's composition. Increase expansion to support your economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Defense is heavy, expansion is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, but expansion is light. You are increasing expansion and tech.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and tech, but keep scouting to detect the opponent's composition. Increase expansion to support your economy.

With the opponent unknown, maintaining a strong ground core while expanding gives you a solid foundation to adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong economy and ground army, ready to transition based on scouting.
