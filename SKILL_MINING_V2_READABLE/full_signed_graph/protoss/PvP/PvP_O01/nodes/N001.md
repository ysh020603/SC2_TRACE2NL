# N001 — Ground Macro Development with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame state where both sides are developing ground-oriented compositions with heavy production and technology. Opponent shows Zealot cues and heavy expansion. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and technology. Maintain current expansion and defense levels.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealot cues, heavy production, technology, and expansion. Pressure is possible but not confirmed.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology, and are expanding. Your army is Zealot-based, with light defense and possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and technology. Maintain current expansion and defense levels.

Both sides are in a macro-oriented ground game. Investing in tech and economy while keeping a solid ground core positions you to transition into a stronger midgame composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger ground composition with upgraded units and a solid economy, ready to respond to opponent's tech choices.
