# N011 — Midgame Air Transition with Ground Opponent

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame state where you have transitioned to an air-oriented composition with Carriers, while opponent remains ground-based with Stalker, Sentry, and Immortal cues. Continue building your air army and tech, but ensure you have enough ground support to defend against potential ground pushes. Maintain economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Stalker, Sentry, and Immortal cues, heavy production and tech, and heavy expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an air-oriented army with Stalkers and Carriers, heavy production and tech, and a solid expansion. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building your air army and tech, but ensure you have enough ground support to defend against potential ground pushes. Maintain economy.

Your air transition gives you a strong late-game composition. Maintaining a mix of ground and air ensures you can defend while building up your air force.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a powerful air army with Carrier support, while maintaining a solid ground defense.
