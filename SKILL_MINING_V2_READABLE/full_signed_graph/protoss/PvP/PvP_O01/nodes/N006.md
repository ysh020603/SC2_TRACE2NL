# N006 — Early Game Tech Investment with Unknown Army

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game state where both sides have unknown army compositions, but heavy technology investment is evident. Production is moderate. Focus on developing your technology and economy while maintaining flexibility. Increase production and tech, but keep options open for ground or air transitions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an unknown posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Pressure is unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have an unknown army posture with moderate production and heavy tech. Economy is heavy, expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your technology and economy while maintaining flexibility. Increase production and tech, but keep options open for ground or air transitions.

With both sides unknown, investing in tech and economy gives you flexibility to adapt to the opponent's composition later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a point where you can choose a tech path based on scouting information.
