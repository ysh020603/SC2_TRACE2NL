# N003 — Ground Macro with Expansion Risk

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame state where you are expanding aggressively while opponent remains ground-heavy with Zealots. Your expansion is light or uncertain, and you are increasing expansion. Avoid expanding without scouting; avoid neglecting army production while focusing on economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, heavy production, tech, and heavy expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, but your expansion is light or uncertain. You are increasing expansion and economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid expanding without scouting; avoid neglecting army production while focusing on economy.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate if you detect an incoming attack or if the opponent's army composition changes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a third base or additional expansion while maintaining a strong ground army and tech advantage.
