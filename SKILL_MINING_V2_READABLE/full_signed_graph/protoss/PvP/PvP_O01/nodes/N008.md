# N008 — Ground vs Air: Early-Midgame Adaptation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame state where opponent shows VoidRay cues, indicating an air transition. You are ground-oriented with heavy production and tech. Consider adding anti-air units such as Stalkers or Archons, and possibly transition to a mixed composition. Maintain your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows an air posture with VoidRay cues, heavy production and tech, and heavy expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, and a solid expansion. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider adding anti-air units such as Stalkers or Archons, and possibly transition to a mixed composition. Maintain your economy and tech.

The opponent's air transition threatens your ground army. Adding anti-air and potentially transitioning to air yourself keeps you competitive.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with anti-air capabilities, ready to counter the opponent's air composition.
