# N004 — Stabilize and Develop Ground Tech

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame state where opponent shows Stalker cues, and you are in a ground macro posture. Your production is increasing and you are stabilizing before further development. Focus on stabilizing your economy and army composition before expanding further. Continue tech and production increases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows Stalker cues, heavy production and tech, moderate expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, but expansion is light. You are increasing production and tech, with a stabilize-then-develop tempo.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your economy and army composition before expanding further. Continue tech and production increases.

A stable foundation allows for safer expansion and tech transitions. Matching the opponent's tech level prevents falling behind.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a stable ground army with tech upgrades, ready to expand or transition based on opponent's moves.
