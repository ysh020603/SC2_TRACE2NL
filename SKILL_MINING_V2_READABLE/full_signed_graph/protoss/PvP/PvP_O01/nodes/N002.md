## Node Type

POSITIVE

Early-midgame state where opponent shows Stalker cues, indicating a ground tech path. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech. Maintain your ground army and tech progression, but consider adding Stalkers or other tech units to counter the opponent's composition. Increase production slightly.

## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence shows Stalker cues, heavy production and tech, with moderate expansion. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots, heavy production and tech, but your expansion is light or uncertain. You are increasing economy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and tech progression, but consider adding Stalkers or other tech units to counter the opponent's composition. Increase production slightly.

The opponent's Stalker presence suggests a tech-heavy ground army. Matching with your own tech units and maintaining economy keeps you competitive.
