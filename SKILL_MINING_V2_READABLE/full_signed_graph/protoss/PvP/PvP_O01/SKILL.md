# PvP_O01 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: PvP_O01
- Matchup: Protoss vs Protoss
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Protoss-versus-Protoss opening that emphasizes heavy technology and economy investment while maintaining moderate production. The early game is flexible, with the potential to transition into either a ground-heavy or air-heavy composition depending on scouting and opponent actions.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Ground Macro Development with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame state where both sides are developing ground-oriented compositions with heavy production and technology. Opponent shows Zealot cues and heavy expansion.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing economy and technology. Maintain current expansion and defense levels.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [POSITIVE] N002 — Ground Tech Advantage with Stalker Cues

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame state where opponent shows Stalker cues, indicating a ground tech path. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and tech.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your ground army and tech progression, but consider adding Stalkers or other tech units to counter the opponent's composition. Increase production slightly.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [NEGATIVE] N003 — Ground Macro with Expansion Risk

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame state where you are expanding aggressively while opponent remains ground-heavy with Zealots. Your expansion is light or uncertain, and you are increasing expansion.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid expanding without scouting; avoid neglecting army production while focusing on economy.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [POSITIVE] N004 — Stabilize and Develop Ground Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
Early-midgame state where opponent shows Stalker cues, and you are in a ground macro posture. Your production is increasing and you are stabilizing before further development.

**Direction:**  
Focus on stabilizing your economy and army composition before expanding further. Continue tech and production increases.
