# N009 — Midgame Air vs Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with own air macro, opponent ground macro, heavy economy and tech, light defense. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech, maintain expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with ground posture, heavy economy and tech, heavy defense, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Air-oriented with heavy production and tech, economy heavy, expansion heavy, heavy air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, continue economy and tech, maintain expansion.

Opponent is ground-heavy, so air superiority can be leveraged, but need to be careful of ground-based anti-air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a strong air army, ready to transition to midgame.
