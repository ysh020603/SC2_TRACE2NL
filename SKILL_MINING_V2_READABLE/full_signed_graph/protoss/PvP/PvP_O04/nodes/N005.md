# N005 — Early Game Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with both players unknown posture, heavy economy and tech, light defense. Strengthen ground army, increase economy and tech, maintain expansion, continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with unknown posture, heavy economy and tech, light defense, unknown pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Unknown-oriented with moderate production and heavy tech, economy heavy, expansion heavy, light defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground army, increase economy and tech, maintain expansion, continue production.

Early game is about establishing a solid economy and tech base while scouting opponent's intentions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable economy and tech, ready to transition to a specific composition based on scouting.
