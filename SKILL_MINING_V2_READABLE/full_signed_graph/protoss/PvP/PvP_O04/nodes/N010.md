# N010 — Late-Mid Ground vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame with own ground macro, opponent air macro, heavy economy and tech, moderate defense. Maintain ground army, increase defense, continue economy and tech, consider adding anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with air posture, heavy economy and tech, moderate defense, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, economy heavy, expansion heavy, moderate air presence, heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, increase defense, continue economy and tech, consider adding anti-air.

Opponent is air-heavy, so need to prepare anti-air while maintaining a strong defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a ground army with sufficient anti-air and defense, ready to transition to late game.
