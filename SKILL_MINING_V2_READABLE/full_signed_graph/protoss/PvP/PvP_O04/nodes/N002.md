# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with both players on ground macro, heavy economy and tech, light defense, possible pressure. Continue strengthening ground army, maintain economy and expansion, continue tech and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with ground posture, heavy economy and tech, light defense, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Ground-oriented with heavy production and tech, economy heavy, expansion heavy, possible pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground army, maintain economy and expansion, continue tech and production.

Both players are still similar, so maintaining a strong economy and tech while building a solid ground army is safe and flexible.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stronger ground composition with better economy and tech, ready to transition to late-midgame.
