# N006 — Early-Mid Air Mirror

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame with both players on air macro, heavy economy and tech, light defense, possible pressure. Maintain air presence, strengthen ground army, continue economy and tech, maintain expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence consistent with air posture, heavy economy and tech, light defense, possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Air-oriented with heavy production and tech, economy heavy, expansion heavy, moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain air presence, strengthen ground army, continue economy and tech, maintain expansion.

In an air mirror, having a ground backbone is important for defense and to counter potential ground switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a balanced air-ground army, ready to transition to midgame.
