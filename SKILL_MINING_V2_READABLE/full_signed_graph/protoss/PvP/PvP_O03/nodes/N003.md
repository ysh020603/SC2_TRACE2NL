# N003 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles, while you have transitioned to an air-heavy composition with Void Rays. Continue to strengthen your air army and consider applying pressure on the opponent's ground-based composition. Maintain your economy and defense while pushing with your air advantage.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, and Oracles, a heavy production and technology investment, and a heavy expansion posture. They may be vulnerable to air attacks.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy air presence with Void Rays, a heavy economy and defense, and a heavy production and technology investment. Your army is air-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air army and consider applying pressure on the opponent's ground-based composition. Maintain your economy and defense while pushing with your air advantage.

Void Rays are strong against ground units, and the opponent's composition lacks significant anti-air. Pressuring them can force them to react and potentially make mistakes.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced composition if the opponent starts building anti-air, or continue to mass Void Rays if they remain ground-heavy.
