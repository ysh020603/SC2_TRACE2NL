## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Immortals, and Oracles, while you have a mixed army with Void Rays and Immortals. Increase your air presence and consider transitioning to a more air-heavy composition to counter the opponent's ground army. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a decisive engagement.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and consider transitioning to a more air-heavy composition to counter the opponent's ground army. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a decisive engagement.

Void Rays are strong against ground units, and the opponent's composition lacks significant anti-air. Transitioning to air can give you a strategic advantage.
