# N004 — Midgame Ground Composition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, both you and the opponent have ground-based armies. The opponent has Zealots and Stalkers, while you have Zealots and Immortals. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding support units like Sentries or Archons to counter the opponent's composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a potential engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army with Zealots and Stalkers, a heavy production and technology investment, and a heavy expansion posture. They may be preparing for a ground engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zealots and Immortals, a heavy economy and defense, and a heavy production and technology investment. Your army is ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and consider adding support units like Sentries or Archons to counter the opponent's composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a potential engagement.

Immortals are strong against Stalkers, and Zealots can tank damage. A well-rounded ground army can hold against the opponent's composition and potentially push.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced composition with anti-air support if the opponent starts building air units, or continue to strengthen your ground army for a push.
