# N011 — Midgame Ground Counter

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has an air-based army with Void Rays and Oracles, while you have a ground-based army with Zealots, Immortals, and Oracles. Strengthen your ground army and consider adding anti-air units like Stalkers or Archons to counter the opponent's air composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a potential engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an air-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles, a heavy production and technology investment, and a heavy expansion posture. They may be preparing for an air engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy ground army with Zealots, Immortals, and Oracles, a heavy economy and defense, and a heavy production and technology investment. Your army is ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and consider adding anti-air units like Stalkers or Archons to counter the opponent's air composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a potential engagement.

Immortals are strong against ground units, and Oracles provide support. Adding anti-air units can help you defend against the opponent's air army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced composition with anti-air support if the opponent continues to build air units, or continue to strengthen your ground army for a push.
