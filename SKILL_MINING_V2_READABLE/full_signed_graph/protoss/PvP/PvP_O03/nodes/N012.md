# N012 — Early-Mid Flexible Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent has an unknown army composition, while you have a heavy economy and defense posture with moderate production. Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition. Consider adding Stalkers for scouting and defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an unknown army composition, a moderate production, and a heavy technology investment. Their defense is moderate, and they have a heavy expansion posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Your army composition is unknown, but you have a moderate expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and technology while maintaining a flexible army composition. Consider adding Stalkers for scouting and defense.

With the opponent's army composition unknown, it is important to build a strong economy and technology base to support future transitions. Stalkers provide mobility and can scout the opponent's base.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent's tech and have a better understanding of their strategy.
