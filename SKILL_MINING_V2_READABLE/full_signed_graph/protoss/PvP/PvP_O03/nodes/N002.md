# N002 — Early-Mid Transition to Ground

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

As the game progresses into the early-midgame, the opponent shows a ground-based army composition with Zealots, while you still have an unknown army composition but a heavy economy and defense. Strengthen your ground army to counter the opponent's Zealot-based composition. Continue to invest in technology and economy while maintaining a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground-based army composition with Zealots, a heavy production and technology investment, and a heavy expansion posture. They may be preparing for a ground push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and defense posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Your army composition is still unknown, but you have a heavy expansion posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army to counter the opponent's Zealot-based composition. Continue to invest in technology and economy while maintaining a defensive posture.

Since the opponent is committing to ground units, you need to ensure your army can hold against a potential push. Investing in ground units and upgrades will help you defend and potentially counter-attack.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a solid ground-based army composition with a mix of Zealots and Stalkers, and consider adding support units like Sentries or Immortals depending on the opponent's tech.
