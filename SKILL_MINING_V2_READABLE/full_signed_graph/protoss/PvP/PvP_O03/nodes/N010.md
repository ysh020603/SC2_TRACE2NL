- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate air presence with Stalkers and Oracles, a heavy economy and defense, and a heavy production and technology investment. Your army is air-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air army and consider adding support units like Void Rays to counter the opponent's ground composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a potential engagement.

Oracles provide support and can harass the opponent's economy, while Stalkers can defend against ground units. Transitioning to Void Rays can give you a strong air advantage.
