# N001 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides are in the early game with unknown army compositions. The opponent shows a heavy technology investment and moderate production, while you have a heavy defense and economy posture. Focus on stabilizing your economy and defense while continuing to invest in technology. Maintain a flexible army composition and avoid overcommitting to a specific unit type.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is in the early game with an unknown army composition, but shows a heavy technology investment and moderate production. Their defense is light or uncertain, and they have a heavy economy posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy defense and economy posture with moderate production and heavy technology investment. Your army composition is unknown, and you have not yet committed to a specific unit composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your economy and defense while continuing to invest in technology. Maintain a flexible army composition and avoid overcommitting to a specific unit type.

In the early game, information is limited, and overcommitting to a specific army composition can be punished. A defensive and economic focus allows you to react to the opponent's tech choices while maintaining a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined army composition once you have scouted the opponent's tech path and have a better understanding of their intentions.
