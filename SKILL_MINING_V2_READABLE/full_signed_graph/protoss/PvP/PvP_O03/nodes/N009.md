# N009 — Late-Mid Air Dominance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late-midgame, the opponent has an air-based army with Void Rays and Oracles, while you have an air-based army with Void Rays and Tempests. Continue to strengthen your air army and consider adding support units like Stalkers or Archons to counter the opponent's air composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a decisive engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has an air-based army with Zealots, Stalkers, Void Rays, and Oracles, a heavy production and technology investment, and a heavy expansion posture. They may be preparing for a final engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy air presence with Void Rays and Tempests, a heavy economy and defense, and a heavy production and technology investment. Your army is air-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your air army and consider adding support units like Stalkers or Archons to counter the opponent's air composition. Maintain your economy and defense while preparing for a decisive engagement.

Tempests provide long-range support and can outrange the opponent's Void Rays. A well-rounded air army can dominate the opponent's composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced composition with ground support if the opponent starts building ground units, or continue to strengthen your air army for a push.
