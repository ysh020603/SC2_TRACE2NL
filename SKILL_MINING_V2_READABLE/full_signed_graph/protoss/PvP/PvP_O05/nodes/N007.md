# N007 — Late-Midgame Air vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, both players have heavy air armies. The opponent has Stalkers and Carriers, while you have Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Continue building your air army, adding more Phoenixes and possibly Void Rays to counter Carriers. Maintain a strong economy to support the air production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent also has a heavy air army with Stalkers and Carriers. They are likely investing heavily in air tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy air army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your economy and tech are heavy, and you have a heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building your air army, adding more Phoenixes and possibly Void Rays to counter Carriers. Maintain a strong economy to support the air production.

In an air vs air matchup, having a mix of Phoenixes for control and Void Rays for damage can be effective. Carriers are strong, so you need to focus them down.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced air composition, such as adding Carriers or Tempests, if the game goes late.
