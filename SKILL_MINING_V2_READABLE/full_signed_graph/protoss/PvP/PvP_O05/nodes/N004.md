Continue building your air army and increase your air presence. Use Oracles for harassment and Phoenixes for control. Maintain a strong economy to support the air transition.

Air units can be very effective against a ground-based opponent, especially if they lack anti-air. Phoenixes can pick off workers and Oracles can provide support.
