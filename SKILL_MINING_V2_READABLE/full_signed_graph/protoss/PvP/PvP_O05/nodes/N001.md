# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 240-300 seconds, both players have a heavy economy and technology investment. The opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, while your own army is still undefined but likely to include Stalkers. Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain defensive structures to guard against potential early pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Stalkers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they have a moderate expansion. They may be preparing for a ground-based push or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are heavy, but your army composition is not yet clear. You have moderate production and are likely to have Stalkers. Your expansion is heavy, indicating a macro-oriented approach.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Maintain defensive structures to guard against potential early pressure.

Since the opponent is ground-oriented, having a solid ground army with Stalkers and possibly Immortals can deter aggression and set up for a midgame advantage. Heavy economy and tech allow for a strong transition later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more defined army composition (ground or air) based on scouting, while maintaining economic and tech growth.
