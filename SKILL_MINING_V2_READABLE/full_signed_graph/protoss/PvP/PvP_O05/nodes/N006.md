## When This Applies

- The opponent has a mixed army with moderate air presence, indicating they may be transitioning to air or have a mix of units. Their defense is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Stalkers, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy and expansions are strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue building your ground army but add some anti-air units like Stalkers or Archons to handle potential air threats. Maintain scouting to confirm the opponent's composition.

Since the opponent has a mixed posture, you need to be prepared for both ground and air. Stalkers are versatile, but you may need additional anti-air.
