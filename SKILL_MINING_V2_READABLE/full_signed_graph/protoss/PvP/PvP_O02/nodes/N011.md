# N011 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has a mixed posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Your own posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy technology. The goal is to transition to an air army to gain an advantage. Increase air presence, strengthen air army, and increase economy. Continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a mixed posture. Representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have a heavy defense posture and moderate economy. You have not expanded aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence, strengthen air army, and increase economy. Continue production and technology.

Transitioning to an air army can give you an advantage if the opponent lacks anti-air. Increasing economy supports the transition. Continuing technology ensures you have access to advanced air units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a strong air army. Aim to pressure the opponent and take map control.
