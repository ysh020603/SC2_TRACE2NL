# N006 — Early Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has been scouted as having a ground posture with Zealots. Your own posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy technology. The goal is to strengthen your ground army to counter the opponent's ground forces. Strengthen your ground army, increase economy, and continue production. Maintain defense and increase technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture. Representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have a moderate defense posture and economy. You have not committed to a specific army composition, but you have the capability to produce ground units.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase economy, and continue production. Maintain defense and increase technology.

Since the opponent is likely to field ground units, strengthening your ground army is a direct counter. Increasing your economy and technology will give you a long-term advantage. Maintaining defense ensures you are not caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a solid ground army. Aim to pressure the opponent and take map control.
