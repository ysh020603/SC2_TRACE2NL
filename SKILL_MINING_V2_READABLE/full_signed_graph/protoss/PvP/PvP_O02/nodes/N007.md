# N007 — Midgame Ground Strengthening and Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has been scouted as having a ground posture with Zealots. Your own posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy technology. The goal is to strengthen your ground army and increase defense to prepare for potential aggression. Strengthen your ground army, increase defense, and increase economy. Continue production and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture. Representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have a heavy defense posture and moderate economy. You have expanded to a heavy expansion posture, indicating you have taken additional bases.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase defense, and increase economy. Continue production and technology.

With the opponent fielding ground units, strengthening your ground army is essential. Increasing defense protects your expansions. Increasing economy supports a larger army and technology upgrades.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a solid ground army and defense. Aim to pressure the opponent and take map control.
