# N002 — Late Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both sides have had time to develop their economies and technologies. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have maintained a flexible approach. The focus is on continuing to develop while keeping safety in mind. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep production flexible and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture. No reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production and technology investment appear light or uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture remains broadly unknown-oriented, with light or uncertain production and technology investment. You have not committed to a specific army composition, and your economy is still light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology. Keep production flexible and avoid overcommitting to a specific army composition.

In the late midgame, the opponent's composition is still unclear. Maintaining flexibility allows you to respond to the opponent's eventual army composition. Continuing to develop your economy and technology ensures you have the resources to adapt.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined posture as scouting information becomes available. Aim to establish a solid economy and technology base while keeping options open.
