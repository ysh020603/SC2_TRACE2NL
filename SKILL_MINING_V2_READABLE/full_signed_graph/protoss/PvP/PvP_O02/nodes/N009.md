# N009 — Early Midgame Ground Strengthening with Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent's posture is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and expansion. Your own posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy technology. The goal is to strengthen your ground army and continue expanding. Strengthen your ground army, increase economy, and continue production and technology. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture. No reliable combat-unit cues have been observed. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly unknown-oriented, with moderate production and heavy technology investment. You have a moderate defense posture and economy. You have expanded to a heavy expansion posture, indicating you have taken additional bases.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase economy, and continue production and technology. Maintain defense.

With the opponent having a heavy economy, you need to keep up economically. Strengthening your ground army prepares you for potential engagements. Continuing technology ensures you have access to advanced units.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a solid ground army and economy. Aim to pressure the opponent and take map control.
