# N008 — Late Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both sides have developed strong ground armies with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. The opponent has a heavy economy and production. The goal is to continue strengthening your ground army and maintain a strong economy. Strengthen your ground army, increase economy, and continue production and technology. Maintain defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture. Representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Stalker, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is broadly ground-oriented, with heavy production and heavy technology investment. You have a heavy defense posture and economy. You have expanded to a heavy expansion posture, indicating you have taken additional bases.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase economy, and continue production and technology. Maintain defense.

With both sides fielding ground armies, strengthening your ground army is key to winning engagements. Increasing economy supports a larger army and technology upgrades. Maintaining defense protects your bases.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a superior army. Aim to pressure the opponent and take map control.
