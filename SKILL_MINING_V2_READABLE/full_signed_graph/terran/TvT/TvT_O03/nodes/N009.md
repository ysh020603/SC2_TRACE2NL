# N009 — Midgame Ground Stability

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 480s) with heavy economy, technology, and production, and a ground army with Marines and Widow Mines. Stabilize your position, increase production, and continue technology development. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and a ground army with Marines and Widow Mines. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position, increase production, and continue technology development. Maintain your economy and expansion.

With a stable economy and defense, you can afford to increase production and tech to prepare for the late game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced composition with Siege Tanks and Medivacs.
