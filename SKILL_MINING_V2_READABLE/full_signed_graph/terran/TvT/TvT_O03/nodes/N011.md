# N011 — Late-Midgame Ground and Air Balance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with heavy economy, technology, and production, and a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Increase your defense and economy, continue strengthening your ground army, and maintain production and technology development. Consider adding more air support.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Your air presence is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and economy, continue strengthening your ground army, and maintain production and technology development. Consider adding more air support.

With a strong ground army, you need to ensure your defense is solid against drops and counterattacks. Increasing your economy and tech will allow you to field a more advanced army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced army with strong air support and utility units like Ravens.
