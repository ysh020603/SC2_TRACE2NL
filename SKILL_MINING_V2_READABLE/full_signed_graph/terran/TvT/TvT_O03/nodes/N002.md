# N002 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-300s) with heavy economy, technology, and production, and a ground-oriented army. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase your economy and expansion, and maintain production and technology development. Keep an eye on defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with heavy production and technology. They have a possible pressure posture and may have Reapers. Their economy and expansion are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and a ground army with Marines and Marauders. Your defense is light, but you are expanding and teching up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase your economy and expansion, and maintain production and technology development. Keep an eye on defense.

With both players investing heavily in economy and tech, the one who can field a stronger ground army while maintaining economy will have an advantage. Continuing to develop with safety checks is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a midgame composition with Siege Tanks and Medivacs, while maintaining a strong economy.
