# N001 — Early Game Tech/Economy Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and technology investment, moderate production, and unknown own army composition. Stabilize your economy and technology foundation while preparing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain current defense and expansion, increase production, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with light or uncertain air presence. They have heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and possible early pressure. Representative cues include Marines.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology investment is heavy, but production is moderate and your army composition is not yet defined. You have limited defensive structures and no clear pressure posture.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your economy and technology foundation while preparing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain current defense and expansion, increase production, and continue technology development.

In the early game, a heavy economy and technology investment sets up a strong midgame. Maintaining a flexible army composition allows you to react to the opponent's first moves without overcommitting.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition while continuing to expand and tech up.
