# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420-540s) with heavy economy, technology, and production, and a solid ground army. Continue strengthening your ground army, increase your air presence, and continue technology and upgrade development. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and a ground army with Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army, increase your air presence, and continue technology and upgrade development. Maintain your economy and expansion.

In the midgame, having a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs is crucial. Adding air support (e.g., Vikings) can give you an edge in map control and drop defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame army with a mix of ground and air, and consider adding Widow Mines or Ravens for utility.
