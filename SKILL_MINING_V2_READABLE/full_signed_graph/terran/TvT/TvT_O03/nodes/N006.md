# N006 — Late-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600s) with heavy economy, technology, and production, and a ground army with some air presence. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology, and maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, heavy production, and a ground army with Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Your air presence is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army. Continue developing your economy and technology, and maintain your defense.

In the late-midgame, having air superiority can be decisive. Adding Vikings or Liberators can give you an advantage in engagements and map control.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a balanced army with strong air support and utility units like Ravens.
