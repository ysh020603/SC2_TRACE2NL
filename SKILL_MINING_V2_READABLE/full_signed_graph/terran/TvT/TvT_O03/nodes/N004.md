# N004 — Early Game Defensive Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and technology, but heavier defense posture and moderate production. Maintain your defensive posture while increasing production and continuing technology development. Strengthen your ground army and expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy defense, economy, and expansion. They have moderate production and heavy technology, with possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology is heavy, but production is moderate. You have a heavier defense posture and are expanding. Your army composition is unknown, but you have some Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture while increasing production and continuing technology development. Strengthen your ground army and expand.

A defensive posture can be safe against early pressure, but you need to transition to a more active game. Increasing production and tech will allow you to take the initiative later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive ground army while maintaining a solid defense.
