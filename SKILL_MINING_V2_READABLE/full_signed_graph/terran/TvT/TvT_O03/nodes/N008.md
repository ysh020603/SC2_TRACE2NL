# N008 — Early Game Expansion and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and an unknown army composition. Increase your expansion and production, continue technology development, and strengthen your ground army. Maintain your defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with light defense, heavy economy, and expansion. They have moderate production and heavy technology, with possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, technology is heavy, but production is moderate. You have a light defense and are expanding. Your army composition is unknown, but you have some Marauders.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your expansion and production, continue technology development, and strengthen your ground army. Maintain your defense.

Expanding early can give you a long-term economic advantage. Continuing tech and production will allow you to field a strong army later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army while maintaining a strong economy.
