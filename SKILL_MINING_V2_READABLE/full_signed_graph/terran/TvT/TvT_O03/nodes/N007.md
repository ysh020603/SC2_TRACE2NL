# N007 — Early-Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240-360s) with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and a ground army with Marines. Strengthen your ground army, increase your economy, and continue technology development. Maintain your production and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. They have heavy production and technology, and a possible pressure posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and a ground army with Marines. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase your economy, and continue technology development. Maintain your production and expansion.

With both players investing in tech, a stronger ground army can give you an edge. Continuing to develop with safety checks is prudent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
