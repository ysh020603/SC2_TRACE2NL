# N001 — Early Macro Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Opponent appears to have a similar heavy economy and technology, with light defense and possible pressure. Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Consider adding production to support a larger army if you plan to apply pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Reaper presence. Their economy and technology are heavy, but their defense is light or uncertain. They may be preparing for early pressure or a fast expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid economy and technology base, with a ground army including Marines. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding. Production is moderate, so you can add more production structures if needed.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Consider adding production to support a larger army if you plan to apply pressure.

With both players on heavy economy, the one who transitions to a stronger army composition first can gain an advantage. Maintaining defense prevents early game losses while you tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame with a solid ground army and tech upgrades, ready to transition to midgame compositions.
