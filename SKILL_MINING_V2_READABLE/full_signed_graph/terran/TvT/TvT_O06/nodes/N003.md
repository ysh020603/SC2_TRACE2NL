## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines. Their defense is moderate, so they may be preparing for a midgame engagement.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production, and heavy economy. Your defense is heavy, and you are ready to move out.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider a timing attack with your Siege Tanks to leverage your army strength. Continue producing and upgrading.

Siege Tanks give you a positional advantage in TvT. A well-timed push can break a moderate defense and secure map control.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
