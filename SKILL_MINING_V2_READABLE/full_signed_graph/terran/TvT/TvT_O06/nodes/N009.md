# N009 — Defensive Tech Up

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers. Opponent has a ground army with Marines, Reapers, Marauders, and Hellions, moderate defense. Increase your defense and continue teching. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or static defense to hold your position.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a diverse ground army. Their defense is moderate, so they may be preparing to attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a strong defensive position. You are teching and expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and continue teching. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or static defense to hold your position.

With a heavy economy, you can afford defensive structures to protect your bases while you tech up to a superior army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Secure a strong economy and transition to a late game army with air support.
