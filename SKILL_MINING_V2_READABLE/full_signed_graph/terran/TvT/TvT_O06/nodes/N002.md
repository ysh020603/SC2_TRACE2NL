# N002 — Aggressive Reaper Opening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, but moderate economy. Opponent has heavy economy and technology, light defense, and possible Reaper presence. Use your Reapers for harassment while expanding to catch up economically. Continue producing from your heavy production structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy appears to be on a heavy economy with light defense, possibly using Reapers for harassment. They may be vulnerable to early pressure if you can exploit their lack of defense.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy production and technology investment, with Reapers as your main unit. Your economy is moderate, so you need to expand soon. Defense is heavy, but you are not yet fully saturated.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use your Reapers for harassment while expanding to catch up economically. Continue producing from your heavy production structures.

Reapers can punish a light defense and delay the opponent's economy, buying you time to expand and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Expand and transition to a more robust ground army, such as Marines and Marauders, while maintaining tech progress.
