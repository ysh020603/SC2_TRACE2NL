# N004 — Midgame Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, but air presence is light. Opponent has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Hellions, and Ravens, and moderate air presence. Increase your air presence to counter potential air threats and support your ground army. Continue teching and expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a diverse ground army including Siege Tanks and Ravens, with moderate air presence. They are also heavy on economy and production, so they are likely preparing for a midgame confrontation.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines. Your defense is heavy, but you lack air units. You are expanding and teching.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence to counter potential air threats and support your ground army. Continue teching and expanding.

Ravens can provide detection and interference; having some air units like Medivacs or Vikings gives flexibility and counters enemy air.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with air support and maintain a strong economy for late game.
