# N011 — Maintain Current Path

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, with Siege Tanks and Marines. Opponent has a ground army with Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Widow Mines, moderate defense. Maintain your current army path and continue producing. Consider adding detection for Widow Mines.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a diverse ground army including Widow Mines. Their defense is moderate, so they may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a solid ground army. Your defense is heavy, and you are in a stable position.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current army path and continue producing. Consider adding detection for Widow Mines.

With a stable economy and army, you can outlast the opponent if you avoid mistakes. Detection is crucial against Widow Mines.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain your advantage and transition to a late game army.
