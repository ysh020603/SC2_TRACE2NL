# N012 — Air Superiority Push

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, but air presence is heavy, including Medivacs and Ravens. Opponent has a ground army with Marines and Medivacs, heavy air presence. Use your air superiority to control the map and prepare for a decisive push. Continue teching and expanding.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army and Medivacs, indicating a bio composition. Their air presence is heavy, so they may be planning a drop or a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a strong ground army and heavy air support. Your defense is heavy, and you are ready to push.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use your air superiority to control the map and prepare for a decisive push. Continue teching and expanding.

With heavy air support, you can drop or engage the opponent's army with mobility. Ravens provide detection and interference.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army with Battlecruisers or other late game units.
