## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, with Marines and Marauders. Opponent has a ground army with Marines, moderate defense. Consider a timing attack with your Marine Marauder composition. Continue producing and upgrading.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines. Their defense is moderate, so they may be vulnerable to a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a solid ground army of Marines and Marauders. Your defense is heavy, and you are ready to engage.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider a timing attack with your Marine Marauder composition. Continue producing and upgrading.

Marine Marauder is a strong midgame composition in TvT, especially against pure Marines. A well-timed attack can break their defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
