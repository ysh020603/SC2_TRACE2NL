- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines. Their defense is heavy, so they are likely preparing for a long game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a diverse ground army. Your defense is heavy, and you are well-positioned for a late game push.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and consider adding air support for mobility. Maintain your economy and tech.

With a diverse ground army, you can adapt to the opponent's composition. Adding Medivacs or Vikings gives you flexibility.
