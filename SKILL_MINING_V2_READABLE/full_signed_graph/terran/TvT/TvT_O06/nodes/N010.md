# N010 — Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, but air presence is moderate. Opponent has a ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, and Battlecruisers, moderate air presence. Increase your air presence to counter Battlecruisers. Consider building Vikings or Ravens, and continue teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a ground army with Siege Tanks and Battlecruisers, indicating a tech switch to air. Their economy and production are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. Your air presence is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence to counter Battlecruisers. Consider building Vikings or Ravens, and continue teching.

Battlecruisers are a powerful late game unit; you need air superiority to deal with them. Vikings are cost-effective, and Ravens provide support.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with strong air support and maintain a heavy economy.
