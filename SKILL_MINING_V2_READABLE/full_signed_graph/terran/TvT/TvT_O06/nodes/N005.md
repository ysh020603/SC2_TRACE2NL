# N005 — Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology, but economy is heavy. Opponent has a ground posture with Reapers and heavy economy, light defense. Focus on stabilizing your economy and army composition. Add production if needed and continue teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and light defense, with Reapers for harassment. They may be trying to disrupt your economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with Marauders as your main unit. Your defense is heavy, but you may need to stabilize your army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your economy and army composition. Add production if needed and continue teching.

With a heavy economy, you can out-produce the opponent if you avoid early losses. Stabilizing prevents harassment from paying off.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame with a solid ground army and tech upgrades.
