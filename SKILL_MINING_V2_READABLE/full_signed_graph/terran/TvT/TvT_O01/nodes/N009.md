# N009 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a moderate economy and production, with Marines as representative units. Enemy intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture, with no reliable combat-unit cues. Focus on increasing your economy and production, while maintaining your ground army. Continue to expand and tech up.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture, with no reliable combat-unit cues. Their production appears moderate, but technology appears heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a moderate economy and production. Your army is ground-oriented with Marines, and you have a light or uncertain defense posture. You are expanding and teching up.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on increasing your economy and production, while maintaining your ground army. Continue to expand and tech up.

A strong economy is crucial for long-term success. Marines provide a basic defense while you build up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more defined composition based on scouting information.
