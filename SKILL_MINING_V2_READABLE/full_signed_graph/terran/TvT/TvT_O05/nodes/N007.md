# N007 — Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both sides have heavy ground posture, production, and technology. Opponent shows a ground army with Marines and Marauders, but you have moderate air presence. Increase air presence and strengthen air forces, while continuing ground development and economy.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues include Marines and Marauders. Exact counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is moderate. Air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and strengthen air forces, while continuing ground development and economy.

Transitioning to air can provide a strategic advantage if the opponent remains ground-focused. Increasing air presence allows for harassment and flexibility.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a mixed army with a significant air component, while maintaining a strong economy.
