# N001 — Early Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, both sides have a ground-oriented posture with heavy economy and technology. Opponent production is moderate, while your production is heavy. Pressure is possible but not confirmed. Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and maintaining production. Continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with moderate production and heavy technology. Representative cues may include Marines. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, and air presence is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and maintaining production. Continue technology development.

Both sides are investing heavily in economy and tech. Strengthening ground forces provides a solid base for potential aggression or defense, while increasing economy ensures long-term sustainability.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a heavier ground army composition with Siege Tanks and Marauders, while maintaining economic growth.
