# N006 — Midgame Ground with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both sides have heavy ground posture, production, and technology. Opponent shows a mix of ground and air units (Medivacs, Banshees), indicating potential harassment. Increase defense and economy, while continuing ground strength and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with heavy production and technology. Representative cues include Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, and Banshees. Exact counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and economy, while continuing ground strength and technology development.

The opponent's air units (Medivacs, Banshees) suggest a harassment or drop playstyle. Increasing defense and economy will help withstand pressure and outscale.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a strong defensive posture with a solid ground army, while expanding economy.
