# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground with Air Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, the enemy has a ground army with Siege Tanks and Medivacs, and you have a similar composition with some air presence. Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding more air units for mobility and support. Maintain heavy defenses and expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Their defense is heavy, and they have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Hellions. Your defense is heavy, and you have some air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and consider adding more air units for mobility and support. Maintain heavy defenses and expand.

The opponent has a strong ground army, so you need to match their strength while also having the flexibility to respond to drops or air threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a late-game position with a balanced army and strong economy.
