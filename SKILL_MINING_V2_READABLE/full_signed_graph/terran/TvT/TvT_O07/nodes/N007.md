# N007 — Early-Midgame Stabilize and Develop

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 5 minutes, both players have heavy economies and ground armies, but you are focusing on stabilizing before expanding further. Focus on stabilizing your position by strengthening defenses and ensuring your economy is secure. Then continue developing your tech and army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines. Their defense is heavy, and they have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines and Reapers. Your defense is moderate, and you are maintaining your expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your position by strengthening defenses and ensuring your economy is secure. Then continue developing your tech and army.

The opponent has a heavy defense, so it is important to avoid unnecessary aggression and instead build a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable midgame position with a strong economy and tech.
