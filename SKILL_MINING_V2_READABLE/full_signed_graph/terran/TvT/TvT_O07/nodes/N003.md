# N003 — Midgame Ground Siege with Heavy Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 7-8 minutes, both players have heavy economies and are fielding ground armies with Siege Tanks and Marines. Strengthen your ground army and increase defenses. Continue expanding and teching, but be prepared for potential siege pushes or drops.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders. Their defense is moderate, and they have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines and Hellions. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase defenses. Continue expanding and teching, but be prepared for potential siege pushes or drops.

The game is in a midgame macro state, and the player who can maintain a strong economy while defending against aggression will be in a better position.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong late-midgame position with a powerful ground army and solid economy.
