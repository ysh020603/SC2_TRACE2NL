# N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 4-5 minutes, both players have a heavy economy and production, with ground-oriented armies and possible early pressure. Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and be ready to respond to potential aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with heavy production and technology, possibly Marines. Their defense is moderate, and they may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines and possibly Reapers. Your technology is heavy, and you are expanding aggressively.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while strengthening your ground army. Maintain defensive structures and be ready to respond to potential aggression.

Both players are in a similar macro-oriented state, so the one who can maintain economy and tech while avoiding losses will gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong midgame position with a solid economy, tech, and a versatile ground army.
