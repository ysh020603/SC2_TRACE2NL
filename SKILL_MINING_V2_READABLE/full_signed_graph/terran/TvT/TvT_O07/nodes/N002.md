# N002 — Early Game Tech Focus with Unknown Enemy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At 3 minutes, the enemy's army composition is unknown, but they have a heavy economy and technology investment. Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech base while scouting to determine the enemy's direction. Prepare to adapt your army composition accordingly.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain about their army composition, but they have heavy production and technology. Their defense is light, and they are expanding moderately.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with moderate production. Your army is not yet defined, but you have Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on establishing a solid economy and tech base while scouting to determine the enemy's direction. Prepare to adapt your army composition accordingly.

With the enemy unknown, it is crucial to maintain flexibility and avoid committing to a specific tech path that could be countered.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gather information and transition into a defined strategy based on scouting.
