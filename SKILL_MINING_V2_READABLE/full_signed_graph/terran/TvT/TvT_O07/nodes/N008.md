# N008 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 5 minutes, you have a moderate air presence and are transitioning to a more air-focused army. Increase your air presence and technology, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain moderate defenses and expand.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Marines and Reapers. Their defense is moderate, and they have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Maintain moderate defenses and expand.

Transitioning to air can give you an advantage in mobility and harassment, especially if the opponent is ground-focused.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a midgame position with a balanced army and strong economy.
