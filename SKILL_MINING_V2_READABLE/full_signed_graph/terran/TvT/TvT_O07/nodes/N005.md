# N005 — Midgame Ground with Moderate Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 7 minutes, both players have heavy economies and ground armies, but your defense is moderate and you are not expanding as aggressively. Maintain your current posture, focusing on strengthening your ground army and tech. Consider increasing defenses if you expect aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Their defense is moderate, and they have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines and Marauders. Your defense is moderate, and you are maintaining your expansion.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current posture, focusing on strengthening your ground army and tech. Consider increasing defenses if you expect aggression.

The opponent has a similar army composition, so it is important to avoid falling behind in tech or economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong midgame position with a solid army and economy.
