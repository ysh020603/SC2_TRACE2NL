# N005 — Late-Midgame Heavy Ground and Air Integration

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, the game has reached the late-midgame. Both players have heavy ground armies with Siege Tanks and Marines, and you have moderate air presence. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing air presence to gain map control and prepare for potential Viking or Medivac play.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they may be preparing for a major push or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Siege Tanks and Marines. Your defense is heavy, and you have some air units for support.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing air presence to gain map control and prepare for potential Viking or Medivac play.

In the late-midgame, Siege Tank positioning and air superiority become critical. Investing in both ground and air allows you to respond to the opponent's moves and take favorable engagements.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Establish a strong late-midgame army composition with Siege Tanks, Marines, and air support, while maintaining a healthy economy.
