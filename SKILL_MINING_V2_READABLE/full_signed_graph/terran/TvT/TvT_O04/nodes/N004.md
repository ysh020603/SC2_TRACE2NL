# N004 — Early Game Ground Orientation with Scouting

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Reapers, while the opponent's posture is unclear. Both have heavy economies and technology. Continue developing your ground army and economy, using your Reaper to scout for any signs of aggression or tech switches. Maintain a flexible approach.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but they have a Reaper and a heavy economy and technology. They may be going for a similar macro-oriented build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Marines and Reapers. Your defense is light, and you are ready to scout or apply light pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and economy, using your Reaper to scout for any signs of aggression or tech switches. Maintain a flexible approach.

With both players investing heavily in economy and technology, the early game is about gathering information and setting up for the midgame. A ground-oriented army provides a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a clear understanding of the opponent's build and a solid economy and tech base.
