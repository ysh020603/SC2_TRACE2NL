# N003 — Early Game Tech and Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, the game is in the early phase. You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army composition is unclear. The opponent shows a possible ground posture with Marines. Focus on economic and technological development while using your Reaper to scout for early aggression. Maintain a defensive posture until your army composition is clearer.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Marines. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they may be preparing for early pressure or a fast expansion.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, but your army is not yet defined. You have a Reaper for scouting, and your production is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economic and technological development while using your Reaper to scout for early aggression. Maintain a defensive posture until your army composition is clearer.

In the early game, it is important to establish a strong economy and tech base without overcommitting to a specific army composition. Scouting helps you react to the opponent's plan.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a solid economy, tech, and a clear direction for your army composition.
