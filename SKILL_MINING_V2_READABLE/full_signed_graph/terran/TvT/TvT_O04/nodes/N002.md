## Summary

At around 540 seconds, the game has progressed to the midgame. Both players have a ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks and Marines, and you have moderate air presence. ## When This Applies

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they may be preparing for a push or a tech switch.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and technology, with a ground army including Siege Tanks and Marines. Your defense is moderate, and you are starting to invest in air capabilities.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while gradually increasing air presence to gain map control and prepare for potential Viking or Medivac play.

In TvT, Siege Tank dominance is crucial, but adding air units provides flexibility against drops and allows for better positioning. Investing in both keeps you adaptable.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Establish a strong midgame army composition with Siege Tanks, Marines, and some air support, while maintaining a healthy economy.
