# TvT_O04 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvT_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Terran
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Terran mirror opening that prioritizes economic and technological development over early aggression. The strategic template emphasizes heavy economy, heavy technology, and a ground-oriented army composition, while maintaining flexibility to adapt based on live observations.

Develop a expansion / economy / technology posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, both players have established a heavy economy and technology base, with ground-oriented armies. The opponent shows a possible ground posture with Reapers and Hellions, while your own forces are similar.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your ground army and economy, while maintaining defensive structures to guard against potential early-midgame aggression.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening with Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 540 seconds, the game has progressed to the midgame. Both players have a ground-oriented army with Siege Tanks and Marines, and you have moderate air presence.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army while gradually increasing air presence to gain map control and prepare for potential Viking or Medivac play.

**Read for details:** `N002`
