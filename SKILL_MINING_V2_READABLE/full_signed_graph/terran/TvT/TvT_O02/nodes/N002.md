# N002 — Early-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

The game has progressed to the early-midgame. The opponent's posture is still unknown, but you have had time to develop your own economy and technology. Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Consider scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their economy and technology are also light or uncertain. They could be teching up or preparing an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are still light or uncertain, but you have had time to build up. You may have some units, but your army composition is not yet defined.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to develop your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Consider scouting to gain information about the opponent's plans.

In the early-midgame, it is important to have a solid economy to support future army production. Maintaining flexibility allows you to choose the right army composition later.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a clear understanding of the opponent's strategy and a solid economy to support your chosen army composition.
