## Recommended Strategic Direction

Given the opponent's ground army, you need to decide on a counter. Consider building Siege Tanks of your own, or transitioning to air units like Banshees or Vikings. Focus on defense while you tech up.

The opponent's composition is strong on the ground, so you need to either match it or find a counter. Air units can be effective against Siege Tanks, but you need to protect them from Marines.
