# N009 — Early-Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, you have scouted a Reaper from the opponent, indicating a possible early pressure or tech-oriented build. Your own army is ground-based with Marines. Strengthen your ground forces to defend against potential Reaper harassment. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize a defensive army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a Reaper, suggesting they might be planning early harassment or a tech rush. Their economy appears heavy, and they have invested in technology.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, and you have invested heavily in technology. You have a ground-based army with Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces to defend against potential Reaper harassment. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but prioritize a defensive army.

A Reaper can be used for early harassment, so having a few Marines or a Reaper of your own can help defend. Investing in ground forces now prepares you for the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a small defensive force and a solid economy to support further development.
