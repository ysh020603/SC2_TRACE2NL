# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own development is also uncertain. Maintain current development path while keeping an eye on safety. Continue building economy and infrastructure, but be ready to adjust if the opponent shows aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. Their economy, production, and technology are also light or uncertain. They could be going for a fast expand, tech rush, or early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units yet. Focus on establishing a solid foundation.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path while keeping an eye on safety. Continue building economy and infrastructure, but be ready to adjust if the opponent shows aggression.

In the early game, information is scarce. Overcommitting to a specific plan can be punished. A balanced approach allows you to react to the opponent's first moves.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a clear direction based on scouting information.
