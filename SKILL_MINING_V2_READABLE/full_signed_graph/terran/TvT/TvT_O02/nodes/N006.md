# N006 — Late-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, the opponent has a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Your own posture is still unknown, and you have not committed to a specific army composition. Given the opponent's strong army, you need to decide on a counter. Consider building Siege Tanks of your own, or transitioning to air units like Banshees or Vikings. Focus on defense while you tech up.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. They are likely to be aggressive and have a solid economy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and technology are light or uncertain, and you have no confirmed combat units. You are still in a development phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Given the opponent's strong army, you need to decide on a counter. Consider building Siege Tanks of your own, or transitioning to air units like Banshees or Vikings. Focus on defense while you tech up.

The opponent's composition is strong on the ground, so you need to either match it or find a counter. Air units can be effective against Siege Tanks, but you need to protect them from Marines.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable late-midgame position with a clear army composition that can counter the opponent's.
