# N011 — Early-Midgame Ground Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, you have scouted a ground-based opponent with Marines and Reapers. Your own army is also ground-based, and you have a strong economy. Strengthen your ground forces to match the opponent's composition. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but focus on building a strong army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground-based army with Marines and Reapers. Their economy and technology are heavy, and they are likely to apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, and you have a ground-based army with Marines. You have invested heavily in technology and production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground forces to match the opponent's composition. Continue to develop your economy and technology, but focus on building a strong army.

In a mirror matchup, having a stronger army composition and economy will give you an advantage. Marines are versatile, and you can add supporting units like Marauders or Siege Tanks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the midgame with a strong ground army and a solid economy to support further production.
