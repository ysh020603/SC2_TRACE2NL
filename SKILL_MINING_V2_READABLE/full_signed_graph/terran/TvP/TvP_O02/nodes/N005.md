## Summary

Early-midgame (around 240 seconds) with opponent showing a ground posture, indicated by Zealot cues. Opponent has heavy economy and expansion. Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for a ground-based opponent.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealot cues. Production appears moderate, technology heavy, and economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is still light or uncertain, with no clear army composition yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, but start to prepare for a ground-based opponent.

The opponent is investing heavily in economy and technology, and has shown ground units. It is wise to continue developing while preparing to counter a ground army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
