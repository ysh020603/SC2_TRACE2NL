# N004 — Late-Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame (around 600 seconds) with both sides still showing light or uncertain postures. No reliable combat-unit cues from the opponent. Continue maintaining current development path, with safety checks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues; production and technology remain light or uncertain.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture remains light or uncertain, but you have had time to develop economy and technology. Still no clear army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining current development path, with safety checks.

The opponent has not revealed their hand, so it is still too early to commit to a specific army composition. Continuing to develop economy and technology is safe and keeps options open.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Keep developing while actively scouting to gain information about the opponent's intentions.
