# N001 — Early Game Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with both sides showing light or uncertain postures. No reliable combat-unit cues from the opponent. Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping an eye on safety.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues; production and technology appear light or uncertain. Exact hidden counts are unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is also light or uncertain, with no clear army composition yet. Economy and production are still developing.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path, focusing on economy and technology while keeping an eye on safety.

With both sides uncertain, it is prudent to develop steadily without overcommitting to a specific army composition. This preserves flexibility to react to scouting information.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and technology while preparing to scout for opponent's army composition.
