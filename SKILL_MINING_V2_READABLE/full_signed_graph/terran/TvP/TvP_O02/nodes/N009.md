# N009 — Early Economy and Expansion Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180 seconds) with opponent showing heavy economy and technology, but no reliable combat-unit cues. Your own economy is moderate and expansion heavy. Increase economy, continue production and technology development, and strengthen ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows heavy economy and technology, but no reliable combat-unit cues. Production appears moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is unknown-oriented, with moderate economy and expansion, and moderate technology. Production is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase economy, continue production and technology development, and strengthen ground forces.

With both sides investing in economy and technology, it is important to keep up economically while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build up a solid economy and technology base while starting to produce a ground army.
