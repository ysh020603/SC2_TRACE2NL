# N008 — Early-Midgame Ground Army Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 300-360 seconds) with opponent showing a ground posture with Zealot and Sentry cues. Your own army is ground-oriented with Marines. Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealot and Sentry cues. Production and technology are heavy, and economy is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production and heavy technology. Army consists of Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and continue technology development.

The opponent is building a ground army with support units. It is important to match their production and technology while maintaining a strong economy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a versatile ground army with upgrades and technology to counter the opponent's composition.
