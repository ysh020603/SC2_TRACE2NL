# N002 — Early Game Tech/Economy Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and tech investment, but your army composition is still unknown. The opponent is also in an early game state with moderate production and heavy tech. Focus on developing your economy and tech while scouting to determine the opponent's direction. Maintain a flexible army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is uncertain, but they have heavy economy and tech, with moderate production. They may be going for a tech-heavy build.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and tech, but your army is not yet defined. You are expanding heavily and have moderate production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on developing your economy and tech while scouting to determine the opponent's direction. Maintain a flexible army composition.

Early game is about establishing a strong economy and tech base. Flexibility allows you to adapt to whatever the opponent is doing.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined army composition based on scouting information.
