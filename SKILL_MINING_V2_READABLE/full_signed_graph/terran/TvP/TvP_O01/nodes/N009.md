# N009 — Early-Midgame Ground Defense vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines. The opponent has an air-heavy composition with Void Rays. Increase your defense and consider adding anti-air units. Continue developing your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests an air posture with Stalkers and Void Rays. They have heavy production and tech, and may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production, heavy economy, and heavy tech. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and consider adding anti-air units. Continue developing your economy and tech.

Void Rays can be very strong early. Adding anti-air units like Marines or Thors is important. Your heavy economy supports this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a composition with anti-air capabilities while maintaining a ground core.
