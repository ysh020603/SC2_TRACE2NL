# N005 — Early-Midgame Ground Defense with Siege Tanks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production and tech. The opponent is ground-oriented with heavy tech. Maintain your defensive posture with Siege Tanks while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Keep production going.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Observers. They have heavy production and tech, and may apply pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy production, heavy economy, and heavy tech. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding heavily.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your defensive posture with Siege Tanks while continuing to develop your economy and tech. Keep production going.

Siege Tanks provide strong defense against ground attacks. Maintaining a defensive posture allows you to safely expand and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a stronger army and better information.
