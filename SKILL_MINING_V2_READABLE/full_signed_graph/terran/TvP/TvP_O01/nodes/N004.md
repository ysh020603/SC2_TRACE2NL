# N004 — Late-Midgame Ground Macro with Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a ground army with heavy production and tech, and you are starting to add air units. The opponent is ground-oriented with heavy tech. Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Immortals. They have heavy production and tech, and are expanding heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines, Reapers, and Marauders, heavy production, heavy economy, and heavy tech. You have moderate air presence and heavy defense.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army while increasing your air presence. Maintain your economy and tech development.

Adding air units gives you flexibility against ground-heavy compositions. Your heavy economy supports this transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a mixed army composition with both ground and air capabilities.
