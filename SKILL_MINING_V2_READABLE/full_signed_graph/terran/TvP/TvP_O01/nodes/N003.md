Continue heavy production and tech development while increasing your defense and expansion. Keep scouting to identify the opponent's plan.

Your heavy production and tech give you an advantage. Increasing defense protects your expansion, and scouting helps you adapt.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
