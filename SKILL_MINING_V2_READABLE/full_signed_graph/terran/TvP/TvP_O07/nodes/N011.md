## Summary

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, heavy defense. Ground posture with Zealot and VoidRay cues. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, heavy defense. Ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers. Light air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path, maintain economy and technology, and consider adding anti-air to counter VoidRays.

VoidRays can be a threat. Maintaining a solid ground army while adding some anti-air (e.g., Thors, Vikings) can handle both ground and air.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal
