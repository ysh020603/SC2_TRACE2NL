## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, continue technology, and consider increasing air presence to counter potential VoidRay threats.

VoidRays can be dangerous to a ground-heavy army. Adding anti-air units or tech (e.g., Thors, Vikings) can mitigate this threat.
