# N006 — Early-Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame phase with opponent showing air posture with Zealot and Oracle cues. Own army is ground-oriented with Marines. Maintain ground army, continue technology, and consider adding anti-air or air units to counter Oracle harassment.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, light defense. Air posture with Zealot and Oracle cues. Possible pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Heavy economy, heavy production, heavy technology, heavy defense. Ground army with Marines. Light air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain ground army, continue technology, and consider adding anti-air or air units to counter Oracle harassment.

Oracle harassment can be disruptive. Having some anti-air (e.g., Marines with Stimpack, or a few Vikings) can deter or punish Oracle attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Develop a response to air harassment while maintaining a strong economy.
