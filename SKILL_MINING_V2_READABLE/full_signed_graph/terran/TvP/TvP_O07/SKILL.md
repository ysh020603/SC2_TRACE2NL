## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that prioritizes economy, technology, and expansion while maintaining a ground-oriented army. The opponent is Protoss, and the opening is designed to be flexible, adapting to observed enemy postures.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while expanding, keeping the army composition flexible to respond to Protoss ground or air threats.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on live observations.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning
