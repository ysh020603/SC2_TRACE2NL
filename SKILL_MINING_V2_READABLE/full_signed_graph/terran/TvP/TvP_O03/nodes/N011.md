# N011 — Early Midgame Tech Uncertainty

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 5 minutes) where the opponent's tech path is unclear, but you have a solid ground army. Continue developing your ground army and technology while keeping an eye on the opponent's tech choices. Consider adding scouting units.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative cues may include Zealot, Stalker. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Marines, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology while keeping an eye on the opponent's tech choices. Consider adding scouting units.

The opponent's tech path is unclear, so maintaining a flexible army composition is important. A strong economy will allow you to adapt quickly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gain clarity on the opponent's tech direction and adapt accordingly.
