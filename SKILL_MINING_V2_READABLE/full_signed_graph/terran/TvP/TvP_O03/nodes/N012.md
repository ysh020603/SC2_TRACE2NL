# N012 — Midgame Ground vs Air

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 9 minutes) where the opponent has a heavy air presence with Carriers, and you have a ground-based army. Maintain your ground army but consider adding anti-air units like Vikings or Thors to deal with the Carrier threat.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an air posture; representative cues may include Stalker, Carrier. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but consider adding anti-air units like Vikings or Thors to deal with the Carrier threat.

Carriers can be very strong against ground armies, so adding anti-air is essential. Your heavy economy should allow you to transition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army that can handle both ground and air threats.
