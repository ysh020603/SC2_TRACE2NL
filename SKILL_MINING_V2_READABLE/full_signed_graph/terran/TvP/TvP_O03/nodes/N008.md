# N008 — Late Midgame Defensive Stance

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) where the opponent has a ground army with Stalkers, Warp Prisms, Observers, and Immortals, and you have a defensive posture. Increase your defense and continue developing. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or static defense to protect your bases.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Stalker, WarpPrism, Observer, Immortal. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and continue developing. Consider adding more Siege Tanks or static defense to protect your bases.

The opponent may be preparing for a warp prism drop or a timing attack. A strong defense will allow you to stabilize and then counter.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Stabilize your defenses and then transition to a stronger army composition.
