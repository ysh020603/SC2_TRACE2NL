# N007 — Late Midgame Ground Army with Colossus Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) where the opponent has a ground army with Colossus, Zealots, Stalkers, and Sentries, and you have Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs. Continue strengthening your ground army and technology. Consider adding Vikings or Liberators to counter the Colossus threat.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Colossus, Zealot, Stalker, Sentry. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Medivacs, and your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and technology. Consider adding Vikings or Liberators to counter the Colossus threat.

Colossus can be devastating against bio, so adding air units that can target them is crucial. Siege Tanks provide support from range.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a midgame army that can handle the opponent's ground composition.
