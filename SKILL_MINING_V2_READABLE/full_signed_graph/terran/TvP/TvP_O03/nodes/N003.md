# N003 — Midgame Ground Tech Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 5 minutes) where the opponent shows a ground posture with Stalkers, and you have a solid ground army with Marines. Continue strengthening your ground army and technology. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other tech units to counter Stalkers.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Stalker. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based with Marines, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and technology. Consider adding Siege Tanks or other tech units to counter Stalkers.

The opponent is likely going for a gateway-heavy composition. Siege Tanks and Marauders can provide a strong backbone against this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a midgame army that can handle both ground and potential air threats.
