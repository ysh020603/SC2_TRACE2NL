# N002 — Ground Macro Consolidation

## Node Type

POSITIVE

## Summary

Early-midgame (around 4 minutes) where both players have committed to a ground-based macro posture with heavy production and technology. Maintain your ground army strength, continue producing, and keep expanding. Consider adding tech upgrades to stay ahead.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Zealot. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army with Marines, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your economy is strong and you have expanded well.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army strength, continue producing, and keep expanding. Consider adding tech upgrades to stay ahead.

Both players are on similar ground-macro paths, so maintaining a strong economy and production will allow you to outlast the opponent in a macro game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Reach a midgame position with a decisive army composition advantage.
