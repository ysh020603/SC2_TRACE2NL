# N009 — Late Midgame Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 10 minutes) where you have a moderate air presence with Banshees and Battlecruisers, and the opponent is on a ground macro path. Increase your air presence and technology. Consider transitioning to a more air-focused army composition to exploit the opponent's lack of anti-air.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include Colossus, Zealot, Stalker, Sentry. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and heavy technology. Your army is ground-based but you have added air units like Banshees and Battlecruisers, and your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology. Consider transitioning to a more air-focused army composition to exploit the opponent's lack of anti-air.

If the opponent is heavily ground-based, air units like Banshees and Battlecruisers can be very effective. This can give you a strategic advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a mixed army with a strong air component to pressure the opponent.
