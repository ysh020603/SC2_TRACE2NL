# N010 — Early Game Reaper Harass

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 3 minutes) where you have a Reaper for harassment, and the opponent has a defensive posture with no clear army composition. Use the Reaper for harassment while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider expanding or adding production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; representative cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, moderate production, and heavy technology. Your army is unknown but includes a Reaper, and your defense is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use the Reaper for harassment while continuing to develop your economy and technology. Consider expanding or adding production.

Reaper harassment can disrupt the opponent's economy and scout their tech. This aligns with the opening's goal of gaining an economic and tech lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Gather information and transition into a standard macro game.
