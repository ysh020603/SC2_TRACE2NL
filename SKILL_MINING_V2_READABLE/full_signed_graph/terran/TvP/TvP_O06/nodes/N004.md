# N004 — Early Midgame: Ground Macro, Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Opponent has a ground-oriented army with Warp Prism, indicating possible pressure. Strengthen your ground army and increase your economy, while being aware of potential warp prism pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground-oriented army with Warp Prism, indicating potential warp prism harass or a timing attack. They have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Marines, Widow Mines) and heavy tech. Your defense is light, but you have a strong army.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase your economy, while being aware of potential warp prism pressure.

Your heavy tech and economy give you a strong mid-game army. Maintaining your ground army and economy will allow you to out-macro your opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army composition while maintaining your economy.
