# N007 — Midgame: Ground Macro, Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Opponent has a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, Immortals, and Observers, indicating a strong ground composition. Maintain your ground army and economy, while continuing to develop your tech and air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground-oriented army with Stalkers, Immortals, and Observers, indicating a strong ground composition. They have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, Battlecruisers) and heavy tech. Your defense is moderate, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and economy, while continuing to develop your tech and air presence.

Your heavy tech and economy give you a strong mid-game army. Maintaining your ground army and economy will allow you to out-macro your opponent.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army composition with air support, while maintaining your economy.
