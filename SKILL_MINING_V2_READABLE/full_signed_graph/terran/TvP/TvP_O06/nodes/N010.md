# N010 — Midgame: Air Opponent, Ground Defense

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Opponent has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and Oracles, indicating an air-oriented composition. Increase your defense and maintain your ground army, while continuing to develop your tech and air presence.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and Oracles, indicating an air-oriented composition. They have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Banshees, Widow Mines) and heavy tech. Your defense is heavy, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your defense and maintain your ground army, while continuing to develop your tech and air presence.

Your ground army is strong, but you need to defend against air harass. Increasing your defense and developing air units will help you counter the opponent's air composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army composition with air support, while maintaining your economy.
