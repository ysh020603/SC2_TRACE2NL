# N001 — Early Game: Heavy Tech, Light Defense

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy and tech investment, but light defense and uncertain opponent army composition. Avoid over-committing to tech at the expense of army production, as you may be vulnerable to a timing attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent is also heavy on economy and tech, but their army composition is unknown. They have moderate production and light defense, suggesting they may be teching up as well.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Marines) and heavy tech investment. Defense is light, and you are vulnerable to early pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-committing to tech at the expense of army production, as you may be vulnerable to a timing attack.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate if you scout an aggressive opponent build or if your defenses are threatened.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger defensive position while continuing to grow your economy and tech.
