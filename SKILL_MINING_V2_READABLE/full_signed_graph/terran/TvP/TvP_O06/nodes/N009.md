# N009 — Early Game: Heavy Tech, Light Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy production and tech, but moderate economy. Opponent has a Zealot, indicating early ground pressure. Increase your economy while maintaining your ground army and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a Zealot, indicating early ground pressure. They have moderate production and heavy tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy production and tech, with a ground-oriented army (Marines) and moderate economy. Your expansion is heavy, but your economy is not as strong as your production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your economy while maintaining your ground army and tech.

Your heavy production and tech will give you a mid-game advantage, but you need to increase your economy to support it.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy while maintaining your ground army and tech.
