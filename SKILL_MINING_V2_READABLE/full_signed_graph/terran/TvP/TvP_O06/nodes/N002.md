# N002 — Early Game: Heavy Tech, Heavy Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game with heavy economy, production, and tech, and a heavy expansion posture. Opponent is also heavy on economy and tech but with unknown army. Continue your macro-oriented development, focusing on economy and tech, while maintaining a defensive ground army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy economy and tech, with moderate production and heavy expansion. Their army composition is unknown, but they are likely also macroing.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Marines) and heavy tech. Your expansion is heavy, indicating a macro-oriented approach.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue your macro-oriented development, focusing on economy and tech, while maintaining a defensive ground army.

Your heavy economy and tech will give you a mid-game advantage. Maintaining a strong economy and tech while keeping a defensive army is a safe and scalable strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army composition while maintaining your economic lead.
