# N011 — Late Midgame: Ground Opponent, Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Opponent has a ground-oriented army with Void Rays, indicating a potential air transition. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army, while maintaining your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a ground-oriented army with Void Rays, indicating a potential air transition. They have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, Battlecruisers) and heavy tech. Your defense is moderate, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army, while maintaining your economy and tech.

Your opponent is transitioning to air, so you need to increase your air presence to counter them. Your heavy tech and economy will allow you to do this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more air-oriented army composition, while maintaining your economy.
