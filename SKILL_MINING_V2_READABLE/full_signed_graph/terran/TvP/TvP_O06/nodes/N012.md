# N012 — Early Midgame: Air Opponent, Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame with heavy economy, production, and tech. Opponent has an air-oriented army with Tempests, indicating a strong air composition. Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army, while maintaining your economy and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has an air-oriented army with Tempests, indicating a strong air composition. They have heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Marines, Reapers, Hellions, Battlecruisers) and heavy tech. Your defense is moderate, and you have moderate air presence.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and strengthen your air army, while maintaining your economy and tech.

Your opponent has a strong air composition, so you need to increase your air presence to counter them. Your heavy tech and economy will allow you to do this.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more air-oriented army composition, while maintaining your economy.
