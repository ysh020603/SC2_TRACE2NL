## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, the opponent has transitioned to an air posture with Zealots and Oracles, while you remain ground-oriented with heavy production and tech. Maintain your ground army and continue developing, but start considering anti-air options. Keep your economy and tech going, and strengthen your ground forces.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a air posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zealot, Oracle. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Marauders, heavy production, and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy and you are expanding. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and continue developing, but start considering anti-air options. Keep your economy and tech going, and strengthen your ground forces.

The opponent's air presence is moderate, so you need to prepare for potential Oracle harassment or Phoenix play. Adding anti-air units like Marines with Stimpack or turrets can help.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a composition that includes anti-air, such as adding Medivacs for mobility and Marines for anti-air, while continuing to tech.
