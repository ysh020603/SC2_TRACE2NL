DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy tech focus with moderate production, but your army composition is unknown. The opponent is also unknown in army composition but has heavy tech and moderate production. Continue developing your economy and technology, and consider increasing your expansion. Maintain production and start defining your army composition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a unknown posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have moderate production and heavy tech, but your army composition is not yet defined. Your economy is heavy and you are expanding moderately. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology, and consider increasing your expansion. Maintain production and start defining your army composition.

With a tech focus, you need to ensure your economy can support it. Expanding moderately is fine, but you may need to increase production soon.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a defined ground army composition, likely Marines and Marauders, while continuing to tech and expand.
