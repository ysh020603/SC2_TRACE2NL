# TvP_O04 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvP_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Protoss
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A macro-oriented Terran opening that prioritizes economy and technology while maintaining a flexible ground-leaning army. The build aims to develop a strong economic and technological foundation before committing to a specific army composition, allowing adaptation based on scouting information.

Develop a expansion / economy / technology posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240-300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, with a ground-oriented army (Marines, possibly Reapers) and heavy technology investment. Enemy intelligence is uncertain but suggests a possible ground or unknown posture with heavy economy and tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and maintain a defensive posture. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

**Read for details:** `N001`

---

### [NEGATIVE] N002 — Risk of Over-Expansion Without Defense

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, but your defense is only moderate. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, and may be preparing a push.

**Risk direction:**  
Avoid continuing to expand and tech without reinforcing your army. Do not assume the enemy is passive.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Tech and Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 300-360 seconds, you have a strong ground army and heavy tech. Enemy is on a ground macro posture, but you are considering increasing your air presence and tech.

**Direction:**  
Consider transitioning to a more versatile composition by adding air units (e.g., Medivacs, Vikings) and increasing your tech. This will give you more options against potential enemy compositions.

**Read for details:** `N003`

---

### [DEFAULT] N004 — Early Game Economy and Tech Focus

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 180 seconds, you are in the early game with a heavy economy and tech investment. Enemy is unknown but likely on a similar path.

**Direction:**  
Continue your economic and tech development, while maintaining scouting to detect any early aggression. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

**Read for details:** `N004`

---

### [DEFAULT] N005 — Midgame Ground Army with Air Support

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 420-480 seconds, you have a strong ground army (Marines, Medivacs) and heavy tech. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with Warp Prisms, indicating possible harassment or drops.

**Direction:**  
Strengthen your ground army and maintain your air support. Consider adding defensive structures or units to counter potential drops. Continue teching up.

**Read for details:** `N005`

---

### [DEFAULT] N006 — Late-Midgame Ground Army with Heavy Tech

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 600 seconds, you have a heavy ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders) and heavy tech. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with High Templars and Sentries, indicating a strong late-game composition.

**Direction:**  
Continue strengthening your ground army and tech. Consider adding Ghosts or other units to counter High Templars. Maintain your defensive posture.

**Read for details:** `N006`

---

### [DEFAULT] N007 — Early-Midgame vs Air Transition

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 360 seconds, you have a ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers) and heavy tech. Enemy has transitioned to an air posture with Phoenixes and Oracles.

**Direction:**  
Consider adding anti-air units (e.g., Marines, Thors, Vikings) and possibly a few Medivacs for mobility. Maintain your ground army but be prepared to defend against air attacks.

**Read for details:** `N007`

---

### [DEFAULT] N008 — Midgame vs Heavy Air

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 540 seconds, you have a ground army (Marines, Marauders) and heavy tech. Enemy has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and Immortals.

**Direction:**  
Increase your anti-air capabilities, such as adding more Marines, Thors, or Vikings. Consider getting upgrades for your air units if you plan to transition.

**Read for details:** `N008`

---

### [DEFAULT] N009 — Late-Midgame vs Air Harassment

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 600 seconds, you have a ground army (Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, Widow Mines) and heavy tech. Enemy has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and Sentries.

**Direction:**  
Maintain your ground army and consider adding more anti-air units. Use Widow Mines to protect your bases from harassment. Continue teching up.

**Read for details:** `N009`
