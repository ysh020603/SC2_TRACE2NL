# N003 — Midgame Tech and Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 300-360 seconds, you have a strong ground army and heavy tech. Enemy is on a ground macro posture, but you are considering increasing your air presence and tech. Consider transitioning to a more versatile composition by adding air units (e.g., Medivacs, Vikings) and increasing your tech. This will give you more options against potential enemy compositions.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech. They have Stalkers and are likely to have a similar army strength.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army (Marines, Reapers) and heavy production and tech. Your economy is strong, and you are looking to expand your options.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider transitioning to a more versatile composition by adding air units (e.g., Medivacs, Vikings) and increasing your tech. This will give you more options against potential enemy compositions.

Adding air units and tech increases your flexibility and can counter certain Protoss compositions (e.g., Colossus, Void Rays). It also allows for better map control and harassment.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a balanced army with both ground and air components, and a tech advantage.
