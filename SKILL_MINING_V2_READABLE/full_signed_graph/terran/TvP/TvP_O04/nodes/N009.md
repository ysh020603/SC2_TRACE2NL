# N009 — Late-Midgame vs Air Harassment

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 600 seconds, you have a ground army (Marines, Reapers, Medivacs, Widow Mines) and heavy tech. Enemy has a heavy air presence with Phoenixes and Sentries. Maintain your ground army and consider adding more anti-air units. Use Widow Mines to protect your bases from harassment. Continue teching up.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with an air army (Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, Phoenixes). They are likely to use Phoenix harassment.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army and moderate air presence. Your defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army and consider adding more anti-air units. Use Widow Mines to protect your bases from harassment. Continue teching up.

Phoenix harassment can be very effective, so you need to have a way to deal with it. Widow Mines can be very good against Phoenixes if placed well.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a strong late-game position with a composition that can handle the enemy's air harassment.
