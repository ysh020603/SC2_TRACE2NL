# N004 — Early Game Economy and Tech Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you are in the early game with a heavy economy and tech investment. Enemy is unknown but likely on a similar path. Continue your economic and tech development, while maintaining scouting to detect any early aggression. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy is also investing heavily in economy and tech, with no clear army composition yet. Their production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and tech, with moderate production. Your army is small (Reapers) and you are focused on development.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue your economic and tech development, while maintaining scouting to detect any early aggression. Consider adding production structures to support a larger army.

A strong economy and tech lead in the early game sets you up for a powerful midgame. Scouting is crucial to avoid being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early-midgame with a solid economy, tech, and a small but capable army.
