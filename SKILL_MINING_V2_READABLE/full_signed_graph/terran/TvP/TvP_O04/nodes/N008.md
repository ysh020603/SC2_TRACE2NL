## Summary

## When This Applies

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with an air army (Stalkers, Sentries, Phoenixes, Immortals). They are likely to have a strong air force.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army and heavy defense. Your air presence is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your anti-air capabilities, such as adding more Marines, Thors, or Vikings. Consider getting upgrades for your air units if you plan to transition.

A heavy air army can be very strong, so you need to have a solid anti-air defense. Marines are cost-effective, and Thors can provide heavy damage.
