# N002 — Risk of Over-Expansion Without Defense

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

At around 300 seconds, you have a heavy economy and production, but your defense is only moderate. Enemy is on a ground macro posture with heavy production and tech, and may be preparing a push. Avoid continuing to expand and tech without reinforcing your army. Do not assume the enemy is passive.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with a ground army (Stalkers). They are likely to have a similar army strength, and could be planning an attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are heavily invested in economy and tech, with a ground army (Marines, Reapers). Your defense is moderate, but you may be vulnerable to a timing attack.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid continuing to expand and tech without reinforcing your army. Do not assume the enemy is passive.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you have scouted the enemy's army composition and intentions. If you see a large army moving out, prepare to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a safer position where you can defend against early aggression while maintaining your macro lead.
