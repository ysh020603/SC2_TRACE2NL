# N007 — Early-Midgame vs Air Transition

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 360 seconds, you have a ground army (Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers) and heavy tech. Enemy has transitioned to an air posture with Phoenixes and Oracles. Consider adding anti-air units (e.g., Marines, Thors, Vikings) and possibly a few Medivacs for mobility. Maintain your ground army but be prepared to defend against air attacks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and production, with an air army (Stalkers, Phoenixes, Oracles). They are likely to use harassment and air control.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army and moderate defense. Your air presence is light.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Consider adding anti-air units (e.g., Marines, Thors, Vikings) and possibly a few Medivacs for mobility. Maintain your ground army but be prepared to defend against air attacks.

Phoenix and Oracle harassment can be very annoying, so you need to have a way to deal with them. Marines are good anti-air, and Thors can provide strong defense.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a balanced composition that can handle both air and ground threats.
