# TvZ_O01 Expansion / Economy / Technology

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O01
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: expansion / economy / technology opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that prioritizes economy and technology development while maintaining a flexible ground-oriented posture. The plan is to expand and tech up, using early Reapers and Marines for map presence and scouting, then transition into a heavier ground army with Siege Tanks and supporting units.

Develop a strong economy and technology base while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation. Aim to reach a mid-game position with a solid ground army and the ability to respond to Zerg tech choices.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order. Adapt based on scouting and enemy intelligence.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: moderate
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Ground Development

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 240 seconds, you have a ground-oriented army with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and technology, and a heavy economy. The opponent is likely on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech.

**Direction:**  
Continue developing your economy and tech, strengthen your ground army, and maintain a defensive posture. Consider adding more production or tech structures to support your mid-game transition.

**Read for details:** `N001`
