# N011 — Early Game Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, moderate production, and heavy tech. The opponent has no reliable combat-unit cues, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Focus on stabilizing your economy and defenses. Increase production to ensure you can defend against potential aggression, and consider adding a defensive structure or two.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has no reliable combat-unit cues, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. They are expanding, so they might be vulnerable to pressure, but they could also be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a small ground army with Marines and Reapers, and you are teching up. Your economy is strong, and you are expanding. Defense is light or uncertain, so you need to be careful about potential pressure.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on stabilizing your economy and defenses. Increase production to ensure you can defend against potential aggression, and consider adding a defensive structure or two.

Your expansion is behind, so you need to catch up economically. Stabilizing first reduces the risk of being caught off guard by an early attack while you are vulnerable.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Catch up on economy and then transition into a standard mid-game development.
