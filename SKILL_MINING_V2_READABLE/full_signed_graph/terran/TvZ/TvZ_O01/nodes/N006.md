# N006 — Early-Midgame Defensive Focus

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

At around 240 seconds, you have a ground army with Marines and Reapers, heavy production and tech, but your expansion is not as aggressive. The opponent is on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Avoid expanding too greedily without proper defenses. Also, avoid committing to an attack before your economy is stable.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is likely on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. They are expanding, so they might be vulnerable to pressure, but they could also be preparing a timing attack.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a decent ground army but your expansion is slower. Your production is heavy, but you may need to focus on stabilizing your economy and defenses before pushing out.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid expanding too greedily without proper defenses. Also, avoid committing to an attack before your economy is stable.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate once your economy is stable and you have a solid defensive position. Then you can resume aggressive expansion and tech.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Catch up on economy and then transition into a standard mid-game development.
