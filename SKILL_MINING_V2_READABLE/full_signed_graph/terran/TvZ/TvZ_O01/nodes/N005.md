# N005 — Midgame Tech Switch

## Node Type

NEGATIVE

## Summary

At around 540 seconds, you have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, and Marauders, heavy production and tech, and moderate air presence. The opponent is on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production and tech, and heavy defense. Avoid over-investing in air without proper scouting. Also, avoid neglecting upgrades.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, heavy production and tech, and heavy defense. They are likely preparing for a mid-game engagement, so you need to be ready.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a strong ground army, but you are considering adding air units. Your economy is heavy, and you are expanding. You have a moderate air presence, so you can transition to air if needed.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Risk Direction

Historical matched contexts associate this broad direction with worse outcomes; the evidence is associative, not causal.

Avoid over-investing in air without proper scouting. Also, avoid neglecting upgrades.

## Safer Re-evaluation

Re-evaluate when you see the opponent's tech choices (e.g., Mutalisks, Hydralisks) or if you plan to attack. Adjust your army composition accordingly.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a mixed army with air support, while maintaining a strong ground core.
