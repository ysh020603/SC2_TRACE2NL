## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a Reaper and are developing your economy and tech. The opponent is on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. Continue developing your economy and tech. Use your Reaper to scout the opponent's base and detect any tech choices. Consider adding more production or tech structures.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is likely on a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light or uncertain tech. They are expanding, so they might be vulnerable to early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a Reaper for scouting. Your economy is heavy, and you are expanding. Your army is not yet defined, so you have flexibility in your tech choices.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and tech. Use your Reaper to scout the opponent's base and detect any tech choices. Consider adding more production or tech structures.

You have a strong economy, so you can afford to tech up and expand. Scouting is crucial to adapt to the opponent's strategy.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a mid-game army composition based on scouting information.
