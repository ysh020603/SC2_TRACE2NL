- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include no reliably grounded combat-unit cue. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy economy and technology, moderate production, and light or uncertain defense. Representative units include Marines. Expansion is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces while increasing economy and expansion. Maintain defense and production, continue technology development.

With a heavy economy and technology lead, you can afford to expand and strengthen your ground army to secure map control and prepare for midgame transitions.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
