## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology. Opponent shows Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen cues. Increase air presence and technology, continue strengthening ground. Maintain defense and economy, continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology, heavy defense, and light or uncertain air presence. Representative units include Marine.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase air presence and technology, continue strengthening ground. Maintain defense and economy, continue production.

With Mutalisks present, you need to add anti-air and possibly transition to a more air-heavy composition.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
