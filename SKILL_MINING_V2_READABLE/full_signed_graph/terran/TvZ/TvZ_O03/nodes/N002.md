# N002 — Midgame Ground Macro with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase with heavy economy, production, and technology on both sides. Opponent shows Zergling, Roach, Queen cues. Continue strengthening ground forces, increase air presence and technology. Maintain defense and economy, continue production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Zergling, Roach, Queen. Production appears heavy, technology investment appears heavy, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology, heavy economy and expansion, and heavy defense. Representative units include Marine, Ghost, Hellion, Medivac.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces, increase air presence and technology. Maintain defense and economy, continue production.

With both sides heavily invested, you need to maintain tech and production advantages while preparing for potential air threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a late-midgame composition with air support and upgraded ground forces.
