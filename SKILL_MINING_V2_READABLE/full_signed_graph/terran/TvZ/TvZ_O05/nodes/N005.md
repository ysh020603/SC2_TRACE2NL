# N005 — Early Midgame Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 300s) with own ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech. Opponent has Queen cues. Continue tech development and strengthen ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Queen, production moderate, tech light/uncertain, economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Marines and Marauders, heavy economy, production, and tech. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue tech development and strengthen ground forces. Maintain economy and expansion.

Tech upgrades will give you an edge in the midgame. Maintaining a strong economy supports continued production.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced army composition with tech upgrades.
