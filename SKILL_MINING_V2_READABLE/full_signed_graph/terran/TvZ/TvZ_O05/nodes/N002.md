# N002 — Midgame Ground Army Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 540s) with own ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech. Opponent has heavy ground forces with Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen cues. Continue strengthening ground forces and increase air presence. Maintain defense and expand further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has a heavy ground army with Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, and Queen. Production and tech are heavy, and defense is heavy. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a solid ground army with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Hellions, and Banshees. Economy and expansions are heavy, production and tech are heavy. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening ground forces and increase air presence. Maintain defense and expand further.

The opponent's heavy ground army requires a strong ground response. Adding air units (Banshees) provides harassment and flexibility. Expanding maintains economic lead.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more balanced army with air support while maintaining ground strength.
