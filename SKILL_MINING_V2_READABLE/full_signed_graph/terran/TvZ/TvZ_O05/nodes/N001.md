DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game (around 180s) with own posture unknown-oriented, heavy economy and technology, moderate production. Opponent appears ground-oriented with light air presence, moderate production, and light/uncertain tech. Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground forces. Maintain current defense and air posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with possible Queen cues. Production appears moderate, technology light or uncertain, and economy heavy. No special threats detected, but pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy and expanding, technology investment is heavy, but army composition is not yet defined. Production is moderate, and you have a Reaper for scouting. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while strengthening ground forces. Maintain current defense and air posture.

The early game is a race to establish a strong economy and tech lead. Since the opponent is likely ground-based, investing in ground forces and tech will pay off later. Maintaining scouting with Reaper helps adapt to any tech switches.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition while continuing to expand and tech up.
