# N004 — Early Midgame Stabilization

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early midgame (around 240s) with own ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech. Opponent has Zergling and Queen cues. Stabilize your position by maintaining defense and expanding. Continue production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zergling and Queen, production moderate, tech light/uncertain, economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground army with Marines and Reapers. Economy and expansions are heavy, production and tech are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Stabilize your position by maintaining defense and expanding. Continue production and tech.

The early midgame is a critical point to solidify your economy and army. Maintaining a defensive posture while expanding ensures safety.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once your army is strong enough.
