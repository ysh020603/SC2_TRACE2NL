# N007 — Late Midgame Defense and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late midgame (around 600s) with own ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech. Opponent has heavy ground forces with Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Queen. Increase defense and continue expanding. Maintain production and tech.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has heavy ground forces, production and tech heavy, defense heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Siege Tanks and Marines, heavy economy, production, and tech. Defense is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase defense and continue expanding. Maintain production and tech.

With a heavy opponent army, defense is crucial. Expanding maintains economic lead for a longer game.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive posture once you have a decisive advantage.
