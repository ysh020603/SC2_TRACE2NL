## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420s) with own ground-oriented army, heavy production and tech. Opponent has Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen cues. Maintain current army path and defense. Continue economy and production.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has Zergling, Mutalisk, Queen, production heavy, tech moderate, economy heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have Siege Tanks, Marines, and Reapers, heavy economy, production, and tech. Defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current army path and defense. Continue economy and production.

The opponent has air presence (Mutalisk), so maintaining a balanced army is important. Continuing production ensures you can respond.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
