# TvZ_O04 Technology / Economy / Production

## Skill Identity

- Skill ID: TvZ_O04
- Matchup: Terran vs Zerg
- Opening Family: technology / economy / production opening
- Method: Full Signed Graph

## Opening Strategy

A Terran opening that emphasizes heavy production and technology while maintaining a strong economy. The army is ground-leaning, with early Reapers for scouting and map control, transitioning into a Marine-Tank core with Medivac support. The plan is to develop a robust macro posture and then adapt to the Zerg's tech choices.

Develop a technology / economy / production posture while preserving flexibility for live observation-driven adaptation.

This is a strategic template, not a fixed build order.

## Strategic Characteristics

- Economy: heavy
- Production: heavy
- Technology: heavy
- Army direction: ground-leaning

## Strategic Priorities

- Preserve the opening's strategic identity without reproducing a fixed sequence.
- Check current Completed, Under Construction, Active Queues, resources, supply, and prerequisites before choosing exact macro actions.
- Match any adaptation against partial Enemy Intelligence and current Threat Flags.
- Re-evaluate economy, production, technology, and army trade-offs at every decision.

## Decision Nodes

### [DEFAULT] N001 — Early-Midgame Macro Consolidation

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 4 minutes, you have a solid ground army (Marine, Reaper, Siege Tank) and heavy production/tech. The Zerg appears to be on a ground macro posture with Queens and light air presence.

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army while maintaining your economy and production. Keep your defensive posture light but be ready to react. Consider adding Medivacs for mobility and to support future pushes.

**Read for details:** `N001`

### [DEFAULT] N002 — Early Game Reaper Pressure

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 3 minutes, you have a Reaper and are looking to apply early pressure while expanding. The Zerg has a ground macro posture with no clear combat units yet.

**Direction:**  
Use the Reaper to scout and harass, while continuing to expand and increase production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

**Read for details:** `N002`

---

### [DEFAULT] N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 8-9 minutes, you have a strong ground army (Marine, Tank, Medivac) and are facing a Zerg with heavy ground units (Zergling, Roach, Queen).

**Direction:**  
Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more Tanks and Medivacs for a strong push. Keep your defense moderate and be ready to repel an attack.

**Read for details:** `N003`

### [DEFAULT] N004 — Midgame Tech and Expansion

**Trigger situation:**  
At around 7 minutes, you have a diverse ground army (Marine, Marauder, Hellion, Medivac) and are looking to expand and tech up. The Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens.
