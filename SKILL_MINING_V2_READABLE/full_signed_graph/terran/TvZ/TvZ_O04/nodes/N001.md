## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army while maintaining your economy and production. Keep your defensive posture light but be ready to react. Consider adding Medivacs for mobility and to support future pushes.

Your heavy tech and production advantage allows you to out-macro the Zerg if you can avoid early damage. Maintaining a strong ground core (Marine-Tank) gives you a solid foundation to transition into whatever composition is needed.
