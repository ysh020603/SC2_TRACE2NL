# N007 — Early Game Marine Pressure

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a Marine and are looking to apply early pressure while expanding. The Zerg has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Use the Marine to apply light pressure while continuing to expand and maintain production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their production is moderate and tech is light or uncertain. They have a heavy economy and expansions, and may be vulnerable to early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and heavy production, with a Marine. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early game. You are looking to expand and maintain your production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use the Marine to apply light pressure while continuing to expand and maintain production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

Early pressure can disrupt the Zerg's economy and force reactions, buying time for your macro. Your heavy production and tech will give you a midgame advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a standard Marine-Tank composition with Medivacs, and secure a third base. Keep scouting to detect any tech switches.
