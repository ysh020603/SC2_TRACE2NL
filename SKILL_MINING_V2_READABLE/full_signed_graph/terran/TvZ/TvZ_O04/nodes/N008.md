# N008 — Early Game Reaper Scout

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a Marine and Reaper and are looking to scout and expand. The Zerg has a ground macro posture with no clear combat units. Use the Reaper to scout and harass, while continuing to expand and increase production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg has a ground macro posture with no reliable combat units, and their production is moderate and tech is light or uncertain. They have a heavy economy and expansions, and may be vulnerable to early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a Marine and Reaper. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early game. You are looking to expand and continue production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use the Reaper to scout and harass, while continuing to expand and increase production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

Early Reaper pressure can disrupt the Zerg's economy and force reactions, buying time for your macro. Your heavy production and tech will give you a midgame advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a standard Marine-Tank composition with Medivacs, and secure a third base. Keep scouting to detect any tech switches.
