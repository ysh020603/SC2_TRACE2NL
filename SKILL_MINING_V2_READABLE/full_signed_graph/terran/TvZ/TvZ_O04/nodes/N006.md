# N006 — Early-Midgame Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 5-6 minutes, you have a ground army (Marine, Reaper) and are looking to expand and tech up. The Zerg has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens. Continue to strengthen your ground army and expand. Keep your technology heavy and consider adding Medivacs for mobility. Maintain a moderate defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg has a ground macro posture with Zerglings and Queens, and their production is moderate and tech is moderate. They have a heavy economy and expansions, and may be preparing for pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines and Reapers. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early-midgame. Your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and expand. Keep your technology heavy and consider adding Medivacs for mobility. Maintain a moderate defense.

Your heavy tech and production advantage allows you to out-macro the Zerg if you can avoid early damage. Maintaining a strong ground core (Marine-Tank) gives you a solid foundation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more mobile army with Medivacs, and prepare to take a third base if you haven't already. Keep your army composition flexible.
