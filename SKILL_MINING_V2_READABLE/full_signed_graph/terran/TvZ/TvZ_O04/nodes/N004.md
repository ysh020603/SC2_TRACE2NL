# N004 — Midgame Tech and Expansion

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 7 minutes, you have a diverse ground army (Marine, Marauder, Hellion, Medivac) and are looking to expand and tech up. The Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens. Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your ground army. Expand to a third base if you haven't already, and continue to tech up. Keep your defense moderate.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings and Queens, and their production and tech are heavy. They have a heavy economy and expansions, and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Marines, Marauders, Hellions, and Medivacs. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the midgame. Your defense is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue to strengthen your ground army. Expand to a third base if you haven't already, and continue to tech up. Keep your defense moderate.

Your diverse army composition gives you flexibility against various Zerg compositions. Expanding and teching will give you a long-term advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more standard Marine-Tank composition with Medivacs, and consider adding Widow Mines or Thors. Prepare for the late game.
