# N005 — Late-Midgame Army and Economy

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, you have a strong ground army (Marine, Tank, Medivac, Widow Mine) and are facing a Zerg with heavy ground units (Zergling, Roach, Queen). Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase your air presence. Consider adding more Medivacs and Widow Mines for a strong push. Keep your economy heavy and expand if possible.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and their production and tech are heavy. They have a heavy economy and expansions, and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the late-midgame. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase your air presence. Consider adding more Medivacs and Widow Mines for a strong push. Keep your economy heavy and expand if possible.

Your Marine-Tank-Widow Mine composition is strong against Zergling-Roach, and your heavy tech allows for upgrades. Maintaining a strong economy will let you out-produce the Zerg.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more mobile army with additional Medivacs, and consider adding Thors or Vikings for air defense. Prepare for the late game.
