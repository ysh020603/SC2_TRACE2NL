# N002 — Early Game Reaper Pressure

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, you have a Reaper and are looking to apply early pressure while expanding. The Zerg has a ground macro posture with no clear combat units yet. Use the Reaper to scout and harass, while continuing to expand and increase production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg is on a ground macro posture with a heavy economy and expansions, but their production is moderate and tech is light or uncertain. They have no reliable combat units visible, so they may be vulnerable to early pressure.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a Reaper for scouting and harassment. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the early game. You are looking to expand and increase your production.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use the Reaper to scout and harass, while continuing to expand and increase production. Keep your army grounded and prepare to transition into a Marine-Tank core.

Early Reaper pressure can disrupt the Zerg's economy and force reactions, buying time for your macro. Your heavy production and tech will give you a midgame advantage if you can avoid losses.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a standard Marine-Tank composition with Medivacs, and secure a third base. Keep scouting to detect any tech switches.
