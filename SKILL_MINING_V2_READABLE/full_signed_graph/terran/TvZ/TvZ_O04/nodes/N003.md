# N003 — Midgame Ground Army Strengthening

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 8-9 minutes, you have a strong ground army (Marine, Tank, Medivac) and are facing a Zerg with heavy ground units (Zergling, Roach, Queen). Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more Tanks and Medivacs for a strong push. Keep your defense moderate and be ready to repel an attack.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The Zerg has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and their production and tech are heavy. They have a heavy economy and expansions, and may be preparing for a push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army including Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Medivacs. Your technology is heavy, and you are in the midgame. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and maintain your economy. Consider adding more Tanks and Medivacs for a strong push. Keep your defense moderate and be ready to repel an attack.

Your Marine-Tank composition is strong against Zergling-Roach, and your heavy tech allows for upgrades. Maintaining a strong economy will let you out-produce the Zerg in the long run.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more mobile army with additional Medivacs, and consider adding Widow Mines or Marauders for extra damage. Prepare to take a fourth base if possible.
