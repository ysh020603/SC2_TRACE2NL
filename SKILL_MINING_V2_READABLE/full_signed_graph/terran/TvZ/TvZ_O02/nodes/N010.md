## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early game, the opponent has shown a ground posture with Zerglings. Your own posture is unknown but you have heavy technology investment and a heavy expansion. Continue developing your technology and expansion. Increase production to defend against potential Zergling aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings. Their economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, expansion is heavy, production is light, and technology is heavy. You have no confirmed combat units. You are in the early game phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your technology and expansion. Increase production to defend against potential Zergling aggression.

Your heavy technology investment suggests you are aiming for a tech-based army. Your heavy expansion indicates a macro-oriented approach. Ensure you have enough production to defend.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a powerful tech-based army composition once your technology completes.
