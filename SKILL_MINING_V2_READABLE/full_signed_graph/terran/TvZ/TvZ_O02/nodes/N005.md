## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has shown a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Your own posture is still unknown, but you are developing. Maintain your current development path while preparing for potential ground pressure. Consider building a defensive structure or units to counter early Zergling aggression.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens. Their economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are light or uncertain. You have no confirmed combat units. You are in the early midgame phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path while preparing for potential ground pressure. Consider building a defensive structure or units to counter early Zergling aggression.

The opponent's heavy economy and expansion suggest they are macroing, but the presence of Zerglings indicates possible early pressure. Staying safe while developing is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more concrete army composition once you have better scouting information, or prepare for a potential timing attack.
