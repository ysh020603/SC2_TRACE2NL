# N012 — Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, the opponent has shown a heavy ground posture with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy production and technology. Strengthen your ground army and continue expanding. Maintain production and technology. Prepare for a potential engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings, Hydralisks, Roaches, and Queens. Their economy, expansion, production, and technology are all heavy. Defense is heavy. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is heavy, expansion is heavy, production is heavy, and technology is heavy. You have a Marine. Defense is moderate. You are in the midgame phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue expanding. Maintain production and technology. Prepare for a potential engagement.

The opponent has a strong ground army. You need to build up your own forces to match. Continue teching and producing to gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful army composition, possibly adding air units or more advanced tech, to break the opponent's defenses.
