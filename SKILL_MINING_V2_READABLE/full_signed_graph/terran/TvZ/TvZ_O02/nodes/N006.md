# N006 — Late Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both sides have committed to a ground macro posture. The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Your own army is also ground-based with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. Strengthen your ground army and increase defenses. Continue expanding and teching up. Stabilize your position before considering a push.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens. Their economy, expansion, production, and technology are all heavy. Defense is heavy. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are heavy. You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Marauders. Defense is heavy. You are in the late midgame phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and increase defenses. Continue expanding and teching up. Stabilize your position before considering a push.

Both sides are heavily invested in ground forces. A strong defensive position with Siege Tanks can hold against Zergling/Roach attacks. Continue to build up your army and tech to gain an advantage.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more powerful army composition, possibly adding air units or more advanced tech, to break the opponent's defenses.
