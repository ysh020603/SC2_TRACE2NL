# N002 — Late Midgame Development with Safety Checks

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the late midgame, both sides still have unknown postures. The opponent has not shown any significant army or tech investment, and your own development is also light. Continue maintaining your current development path with safety checks. Avoid overextending without more information.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence still shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. Their posture remains unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology remain light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition. You are in the late midgame phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue maintaining your current development path with safety checks. Avoid overextending without more information.

The opponent's lack of visible investment could indicate a hidden tech or economy play. Staying flexible allows you to adapt to whatever they reveal.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more concrete army composition once you have better scouting information, or prepare for a potential late-game confrontation.
