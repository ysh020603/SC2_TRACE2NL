# N008 — Early Midgame Ground Development with Heavy Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has shown a ground posture with Zerglings. Your own posture is ground-oriented with heavy technology investment. Continue developing your ground army and technology. Maintain production and expand. Prepare for potential Zergling pressure.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings. Their economy and expansion are heavy, production is moderate, and technology is light or uncertain. Pressure is possible.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, expansion is moderate, production is moderate, and technology is heavy. You have a Marine. You are in the early midgame phase.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your ground army and technology. Maintain production and expand. Prepare for potential Zergling pressure.

Your heavy technology investment suggests you are aiming for a tech-based army. Continue teching up while ensuring you have enough production to defend against early aggression.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a powerful tech-based army composition once your technology completes.
