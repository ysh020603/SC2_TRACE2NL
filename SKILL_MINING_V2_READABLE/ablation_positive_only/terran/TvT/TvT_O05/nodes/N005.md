- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative cues may include SiegeTank, Marine, Reaper. Production heavy, technology heavy, exact hidden counts unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented posture with heavy production and technology. Economy and expansions are heavy. Defense is light or uncertain, and pressure is possible.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen ground forces, increase economy, and maintain production and technology. Consider adding Reapers for map control or scouting.

Reapers can provide early harassment and scouting, helping you react to the opponent's moves. Maintaining a strong economy supports a larger army later.
