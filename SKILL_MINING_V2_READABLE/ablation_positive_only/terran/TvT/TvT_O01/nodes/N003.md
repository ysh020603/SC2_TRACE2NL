# N003 — Early Game Macro Foundation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, the opponent's posture is unknown, but you have a ground-oriented army with Marines. Your economy is heavy, and you are expanding. Focus on establishing a strong macro foundation, continuing to expand and tech while maintaining a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with an unknown posture; no reliable combat-unit cues. Production appears moderate, technology heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and are expanding, with a moderate production and heavy technology investment. Your army consists of Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on establishing a strong macro foundation, continuing to expand and tech while maintaining a defensive posture.

A heavy economy and technology investment provide long-term advantages. Early defense prevents any surprise attacks.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more active midgame with a stronger army composition.
