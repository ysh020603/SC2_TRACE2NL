# N010 — Late Midgame Tech Diversification

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 10 minutes, the opponent shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Banshees, and Ravens. You have a ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Hellions. Diversify your tech to counter the opponent's mix, adding air defense and detection. Continue expanding and teching.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Reapers, Banshees, and Ravens. Production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army. Your defense is moderate, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Diversify your tech to counter the opponent's mix, adding air defense and detection. Continue expanding and teching.

The opponent's mix of air and ground units requires a balanced response. Heavy economy allows for tech diversification.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition into a more versatile army with both ground and air capabilities.
