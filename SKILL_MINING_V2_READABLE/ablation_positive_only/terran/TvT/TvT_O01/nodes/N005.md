## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 8 minutes, both players have a moderate air presence and heavy ground armies. You have Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs, while the opponent shows similar units. Maintain your balanced army composition and continue expanding. Consider adding air units for scouting and drop potential.

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Hellions. Production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a balanced ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Marauders, and Medivacs. Your economy and production are heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your balanced army composition and continue expanding. Consider adding air units for scouting and drop potential.

A balanced army is versatile and can adapt to various opponent compositions. Heavy economy supports continued production.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
