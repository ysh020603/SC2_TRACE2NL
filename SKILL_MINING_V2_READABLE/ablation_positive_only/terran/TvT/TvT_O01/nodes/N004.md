## Summary

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Siege Tanks and Marauders. Production and technology are heavy.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Transition into a Siege Tank-based army to counter the opponent's ground composition, while continuing to expand and tech.

Siege Tanks provide strong defensive and offensive capabilities against ground armies, especially in TvT.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.
