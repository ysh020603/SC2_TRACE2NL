# N010 — Mid-Game Ground Macro with Air Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 9 minutes, the opponent has a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. You have a ground army with Marines, Marauders, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Increase your air presence and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. Their production and technology are heavy, and they are expanding.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Marines, Marauders, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. Your defense is heavy, and you are expanding.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and continue developing your ground army. Maintain your economy and technology development.

Adding Medivacs provides healing and mobility, while Widow Mines offer defensive utility. This composition is strong against ground-based armies.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger mid-game army with additional production and upgrades.
