## Summary

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent is transitioning to an air posture with Oracles. They have heavy production and tech. Their defense is light.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early-midgame with a strong economy and tech. Your army is ground-based with Marines.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your ground army but start preparing anti-air. Consider adding Marines and possibly Thors or Vikings. Continue expanding and teching.

Oracles can harass, but your economy is strong. By adding anti-air, you can defend against air threats while maintaining a ground core.
