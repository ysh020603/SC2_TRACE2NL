## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 10 minutes, you have a strong ground army with Siege Tanks, Marines, Medivacs, and Widow Mines. The opponent has a heavy ground army with Zerglings, Roaches, and Queens, and heavy tech. Increase your air presence and technology, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Expand and keep your production going.

- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and technology, while continuing to strengthen your ground army. Expand and keep your production going.

Your army is strong, but you need to keep up with the Zerg's heavy production. Adding air units and tech will give you an edge in the late game.
