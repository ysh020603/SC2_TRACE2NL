## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame (around 420-480s) with own ground army but moderate air presence, including Battlecruisers. Opponent heavy production and tech, ground-oriented. Continue to strengthen your ground army while also developing your air presence. Increase production and tech, and consider expanding further.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Opponent has heavy production and tech, still fielding Zerglings and Queens, but with heavier investment. They are likely preparing for a stronger army.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Reapers, Hellions, and Battlecruisers, indicating a mix of harassment and heavy units. Production and tech are heavy, economy strong.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army while also developing your air presence. Increase production and tech, and consider expanding further.

Having a mix of ground and air units can give you flexibility against Zerg's ground-based army. Battlecruisers provide a powerful late-game option.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition towards a more balanced army with strong air support, while maintaining a solid ground core.
