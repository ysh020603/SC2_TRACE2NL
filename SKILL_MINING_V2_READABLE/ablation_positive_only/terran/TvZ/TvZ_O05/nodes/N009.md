# N009 — Early Game Flexibility

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own posture unknown with moderate economy, opponent ground macro. Maintain current path, keep options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy ground-oriented with Queen, heavy economy, moderate production.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early game, moderate economy and production, heavy tech, unknown army composition.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current path, keep options open.

Preserve flexibility until more information is available.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a defined strategy once opponent is scouted.
