# N006 — Early Game Tech Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Around 3 minutes, you have an unknown army posture, while the opponent has a Queen and light tech. Increase production and economy, while maintaining defense and continuing tech development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is consistent with a ground posture; representative observed or remembered cues may include Queen. Production appears moderate, technology investment appears light_or_uncertain, and exact hidden counts remain unknown.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army posture is unknown, but you have moderate production and heavy tech. Your economy is heavy, but expansion is light or uncertain.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase production and economy, while maintaining defense and continuing tech development.

Your heavy tech investment gives you a long-term advantage, so you can focus on macro. The opponent's light tech suggests they are not rushing, so you have time to develop.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach the early midgame with a solid economy and tech lead.
