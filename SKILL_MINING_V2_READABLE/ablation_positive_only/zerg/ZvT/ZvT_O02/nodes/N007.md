# N007 — Early-Midgame Ground Macro

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early-midgame, the opponent reveals a ground posture with heavy production and economy. Your own posture is ground-oriented with moderate production. Strengthen your ground army, increase economy and expansion, and continue technology development.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with representative cue Marine. The opponent's production and economy are heavy, and expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy is moderate, expansion is heavy, and production is moderate. You have a ground-oriented army with no specific unit cues.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, increase economy and expansion, and continue technology development.

The opponent is investing heavily in ground forces, so it is wise to strengthen your ground army while continuing to develop your economy and expansion.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and production, and prepare to transition to a more concrete strategy once the opponent's posture is revealed.
