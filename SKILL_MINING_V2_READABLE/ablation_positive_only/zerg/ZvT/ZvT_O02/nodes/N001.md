# N001 — Early Game Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At the start of the game, both sides have limited information. The opponent's posture is unknown, and your own economy and production are still developing. Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on economy and expansion while keeping options open.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues. The opponent's production, technology, and expansion are all light or uncertain, indicating an unknown posture.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy, production, and technology are all light or uncertain. You have not committed to a specific army composition yet.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain current development path with safety checks. Focus on economy and expansion while keeping options open.

With limited information, it is efficient to develop a solid economic foundation before committing to a specific strategy. This allows adaptation based on future observations.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Continue developing economy and production while gathering more information about the opponent.
