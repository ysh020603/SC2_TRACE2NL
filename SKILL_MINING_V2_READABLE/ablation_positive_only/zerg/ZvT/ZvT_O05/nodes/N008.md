# N008 — Midgame Zergling Pressure

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, use zerglings to apply pressure while maintaining your economy. Use zerglings for harassment and map control while teching up to a stronger army.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent has a ground posture with Siege Tanks, Marines, Reapers, and Battlecruisers. Their production and technology are heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with heavy technology. Your army consists of zerglings and queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Use zerglings for harassment and map control while teching up to a stronger army.

Zerglings are fast and can harass expansions, forcing the opponent to split their army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Maintain pressure while building a stronger army.
