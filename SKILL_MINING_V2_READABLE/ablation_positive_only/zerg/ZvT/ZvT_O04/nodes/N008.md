## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early-midgame, opponent ground with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, own ground with Zerglings, Queens, moderate tech. Continue economy, increase tech, strengthen ground.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows ground posture with Marines, Reapers, Hellions, heavy production and tech.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Early-midgame, heavy economy, moderate production, moderate tech, ground-oriented.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue economy, increase tech, strengthen ground.

Tech up to handle Terran ground threats.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach midgame with better tech and army.
