# N003 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 3 minutes, the game is in the early game. Enemy intelligence shows a ground posture with Zerglings and Queens, moderate production, and light technology. Your own economy is moderate, and you are still developing your expansion. Focus on expanding your economy and establishing a solid foundation. Continue producing Zerglings and Queens for defense.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a heavy economy and expansion, with moderate production and light technology. Their army is ground-based with Zerglings and Queens.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and expansion, with a moderate ground army. Production is moderate, and technology is light. You are in the early stages of development.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on expanding your economy and establishing a solid foundation. Continue producing Zerglings and Queens for defense.

A strong economy is crucial for long-term success. Building a solid foundation will allow you to outproduce the enemy in the midgame.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a stronger economy and expansion, while maintaining a defensive army.
