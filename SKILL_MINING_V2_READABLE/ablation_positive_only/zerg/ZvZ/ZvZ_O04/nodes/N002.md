# N002 — Midgame Ground Sustain

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame, both players have heavy economies and heavy production. Opponent shows moderate tech and ground posture. Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and production, and keep your defense up. Consider adding tech when safe.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zergling and Queen cues. Their production is heavy, tech moderate, and they are likely to continue a macro game.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy, heavy production, and moderate tech. Your ground army is strong and your defense is solid.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army, maintain your economy and production, and keep your defense up. Consider adding tech when safe.

With both players on heavy economies, the one who transitions more efficiently to a better composition will gain an advantage. Maintaining a strong ground army prevents being caught off guard.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Prepare for a late-midgame transition, either to a more advanced ground composition or to air if the opponent does.
