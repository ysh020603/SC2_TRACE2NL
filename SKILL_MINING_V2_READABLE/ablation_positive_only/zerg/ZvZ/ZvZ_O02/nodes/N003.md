# N003 — Midgame Consolidation

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, you have established a solid economic base, but the opponent's strategy is still unclear. You need to consolidate your position and prepare for potential conflicts. Maintain your current development path while increasing your scouting efforts. Consider building a defensive army to protect your expansions and prepare for potential attacks.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- The opponent's posture remains unknown, with no reliable combat-unit cues. They could be building a large army or expanding further.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your economy and production are still light or uncertain, but you have a stable foundation. You have not committed to a specific army composition, but you are ready to adapt.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Maintain your current development path while increasing your scouting efforts. Consider building a defensive army to protect your expansions and prepare for potential attacks.

As the game progresses, the risk of aggression increases. By maintaining a balanced approach and scouting, you can detect threats early and respond appropriately.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more aggressive or defensive posture based on the opponent's actions. If the opponent is passive, you can focus on tech and upgrades; if they are aggressive, you need to build a stronger army.
