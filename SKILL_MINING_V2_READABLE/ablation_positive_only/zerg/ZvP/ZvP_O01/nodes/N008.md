# N008 — Late-Mid Ground Army and Tech

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame, around 10-12 minutes, with enemy having a heavy ground army including Observers. Continue strengthening your ground army and tech. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks for anti-armor. Maintain map control and prepare for a decisive battle.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy has a well-rounded ground army with Zealots, Stalkers, Sentries, and Observers. They are likely preparing for a decisive push.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army of Zerglings, Queens, and Overseers. Your tech is heavy, and you are ready for a major engagement.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue strengthening your ground army and tech. Consider adding Roaches or Hydralisks for anti-armor. Maintain map control and prepare for a decisive battle.

With a strong economy, you can afford a larger army and tech upgrades. Matching the enemy's composition with appropriate counters is key.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Achieve a powerful army composition that can win a major engagement or force the enemy to react.
