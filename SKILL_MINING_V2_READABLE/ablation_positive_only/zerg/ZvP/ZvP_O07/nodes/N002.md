# N002 — Early Game Economy Focus

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

At around 180 seconds, you have a heavy economy and expansion, but your production and tech are light. The opponent's posture is unknown, but they have heavy defense and tech. Focus on economy and expansion, while starting to build up your production and army. Maintain a defensive posture.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence is unclear, but they have heavy defense and tech, with moderate production. They may be teching up.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You are in the early game with a heavy economy and expansion, but light production and tech. Your army is small, but you have Zerglings and Queens.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Focus on economy and expansion, while starting to build up your production and army. Maintain a defensive posture.

Your economy is strong, so you can afford to expand and tech. The opponent's unknown posture means you should be cautious.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more solid production and army, while continuing to expand.
