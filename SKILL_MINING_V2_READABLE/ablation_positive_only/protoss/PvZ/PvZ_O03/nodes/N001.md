## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Early game, own army composition unknown, economy heavy, technology heavy, production moderate. Opponent shows ground posture with light air presence, moderate production, light tech. Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline, but keep options open for air transition.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence suggests a ground-based Zerg with light air presence, moderate production, and light tech. Queens are representative, indicating a macro-oriented start.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- Your army composition is not yet defined; you are investing heavily in technology and economy. Production is moderate, and you have not committed to a specific army style.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing economy and technology while maintaining flexibility. Strengthen ground forces as a baseline, but keep options open for air transition.

Early game is about establishing a strong economic and technological foundation. Committing too early to a specific army composition can be punished by scouting and adaptation.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Reach a stable early-midgame position with a clear army direction (ground or air) based on scouting.
