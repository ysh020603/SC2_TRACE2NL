# N010 — Midgame Ground Development with Upgrades

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the midgame, both you and the opponent have a ground posture with Zealots and Stalkers. Both economies and production are heavy. Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase your production and technology. Focus on upgrades to gain an edge in engagements.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows a ground posture with Zealots, Stalkers, and Immortals. Their economy and production are heavy, and they have expanded heavily.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy economy and production, with a ground army consisting of Zealots and Stalkers. Your technology is heavy.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue to strengthen your ground army and increase your production and technology. Focus on upgrades to gain an edge in engagements.

Both sides have similar ground compositions. By increasing your production and tech, you can gain an advantage in upgrades or army size.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more advanced ground army composition, possibly with Colossi or Archons, to gain an edge in the late game.
