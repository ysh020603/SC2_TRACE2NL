# N009 — Early Midgame Expansion Development

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

In the early midgame, the opponent has a heavy economy and expansion, but their production is moderate. You have a moderate economy and heavy tech, with a heavy expansion. Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence shows no reliable combat-unit cues, but their economy is heavy and they have expanded heavily. Their production is moderate.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a moderate economy and heavy technology investment. Your production is moderate, and you have expanded heavily. Your defense is moderate.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army and continue to develop your economy and technology. Maintain a defensive posture while preparing for a potential ground engagement.

The opponent's heavy economy and expansion suggest they are playing a macro-oriented game. By strengthening your ground forces and maintaining your tech advantage, you can counter their eventual army.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Transition to a more defined ground army composition, possibly with Stalkers or Immortals, to counter the opponent's eventual composition.
