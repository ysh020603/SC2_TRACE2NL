# N012 — Midgame Ground Army with Sentry Support

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Midgame phase where you have a ground army with Zealots and Sentries, while the opponent shows a ground posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. Strengthen your ground army, adding more Sentries for force fields and other support. Continue developing your economy and technology.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates a ground posture with no reliable combat-unit cues. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a ground-oriented army with Zealots and Sentries, backed by heavy production and technology. Your economy and expansion are strong, and you have heavy defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Strengthen your ground army, adding more Sentries for force fields and other support. Continue developing your economy and technology.

Sentries are crucial in PvP for controlling engagements. A heavy economy allows you to sustain a large army and tech up.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build a well-rounded ground army with Sentry support and prepare for potential tech switches.
