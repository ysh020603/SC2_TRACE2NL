# N007 — Late-Mid Air vs Air with Carrier Threat

## Node Type

DEFAULT

## Summary

Late-midgame phase where both sides have heavy air armies. Own army includes Phoenix and Oracle, while opponent shows Stalker and Carrier cues. Increase your air presence and consider adding units that can counter Carriers, such as Void Rays or more Phoenixes. Maintain your economy and expansion.

## When This Applies

### Opponent cues

- Enemy Intelligence indicates an air posture with Stalker and Carrier cues. They have heavy production and technology, and their expansion is heavy.
- Treat remembered or observed Enemy Intelligence as partial and uncertain.

### Own cues

- You have a heavy air army with Stalkers, Phoenixes, and Oracles. Your economy, production, and technology are all heavy, and you have strong defenses.
- Use the live observation to check army supply, free supply, resource bank, technology, and current queues.
- These cues are approximate and do not all need to be true.

## Recommended Strategic Direction

Increase your air presence and consider adding units that can counter Carriers, such as Void Rays or more Phoenixes. Maintain your economy and expansion.

Carriers are a late-game threat, so you need to prepare accordingly. Phoenixes can harass, but you may need heavier anti-air units to deal with Carriers.

## What This Does NOT Mean

This node is not an instruction to reproduce a historical action sequence.

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

## Transition Goal

Build an air force capable of dealing with Carriers while maintaining a solid defense.
