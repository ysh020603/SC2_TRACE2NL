## Recommended Strategic Direction

Continue developing your economy and technology while maintaining a defensive posture. Strengthen your ground army as a baseline, but remain flexible to transition to air if scouting suggests.

In the early game, information is limited. A defensive, technology-focused approach minimizes risk while allowing you to adapt to the opponent's composition once it becomes clearer. Heavy economy supports future transitions.

## What This Does NOT Mean

Choose exact macro actions from the current live observation, not merely because a unit or structure appeared in historical evidence.

Reach the early-midgame with a stable economy, a defensive structure, and the technology infrastructure to support either a ground or air transition.
